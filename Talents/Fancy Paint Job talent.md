## Fancy Paint Job

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Modder]], [[Rigger]]

The character can upgrade the ability of all \[their\] [[Charm (Pr)|Charm]], [[Deception (Cun)|Deception]], and [[Negotiation (Pr)|Negotiation]] checks made in the presence of \[their\] [[Signature Vehicle talent|Signature Vehicle]] once.
