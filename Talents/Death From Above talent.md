## Death from Above

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Death Watch Warrior]] (1)

Once per encounter while using a jetpack, the character may take the Death from Above action, inflicting 1 system strain on their jetpack and making a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Piloting - Planetary (Ag)|Piloting (Planetary) check]]**. If the check is successful, for each [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] result, one adversary of silhouette 0 or 1 within short range is knocked prone. The character may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result from the check to stagger one affected adversary until the end of that adversary's next turn.

[^1]: [[Category - Clone Wars End|Collapse of the Republic]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 38)
