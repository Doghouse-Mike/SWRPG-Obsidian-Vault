## Resist Disarm

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Makashi Duelist]]

If the character would be disarmed or \[their\] weapon would be damaged or destroyed, \[they\] may choose to suffer 2 strain to ignore the effect.

[^1]: [[Category - Way of the Force|***Star Wars*** **Force and Destiny**]] Roleplaying Game: *Core Rulebook* (Page 151)
