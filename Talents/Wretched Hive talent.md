## Wretched Hive

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Blockade Runner]]

Once per session, make the Wretched Hive action; may make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Astrogation (Int)|Astrogation]] or [[Underworld (Int)|Knowledge (Underworld)]]** check to identify the path to the nearest Shadowport. Upgrade all checks the character makes to travel to and interact with the Shadowport this session, including astrogation, piloting, and social checks.
