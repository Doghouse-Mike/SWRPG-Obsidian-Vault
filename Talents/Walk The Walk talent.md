## Walk the Walk

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Enforcer]]

The character may spend one Destiny Point to add damage equal to \[their\] ranks in [[Streetwise (Cun)|Streetwise]] to one hit of a successful [[Brawl (Br)|Brawl]] check.
