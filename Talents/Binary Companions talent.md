## Binary Companions

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Droid Tech (reSpec)]] (1)

The Character may choose an additional willing PC or NPC droid as an additional [[Droid Companion talent|Droid Companion]] per rank of Binary Companions. The character may choose different skills to add an [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result to for each [[Droid Companion talent|Droid Companion]].
