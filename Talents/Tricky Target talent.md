## Tricky Target

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Clone Pilot]], [[Driver]], [[Pilot]], [[Squadron Leader]], [[Starfighter Ace]]
- **Items:** [[Overcharged Ion Engines]] (Modification Option)

Any vehicle the character pilots counts as having a silhouette one lower than normal when being attacked to a minimum of 0.
