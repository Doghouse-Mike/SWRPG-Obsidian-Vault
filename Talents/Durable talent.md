## Durable

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Species:** [[Aleena]], [[Chevin]]
- **Trees:** [[Archaeologist]] (1), [[Bodyguard]] (1), [[Colossus]] (3), [[Commando]] (2), [[Cyber Tech]] (2), [[Enforcer]] (1), [[Fringer]] (1), [[Gunner]] (2), [[Heavy]] (1), [[Marshal]] (2), [[Sapper]] (2), [[Shii-Cho Knight]] (2), [[Sleeper Agent]] (1)
- **Items:** [[Alliance Engineer's Helmet and Vest]]; [[Reinforced Gauntlets]] (Modification Option)

The character may reduce a Critical Injury result \[they\] suffer by 10 per rank of Durable, to a minimum of one.
