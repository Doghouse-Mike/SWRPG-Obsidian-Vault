## Skilled Jockey

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Species:** [[Sullustan]]
- **Trees:** [[Clone Pilot]] (1), [[Driver]] (2), [[Fringer]] (1), [[Hotshot]] (1), [[Operator]] (2). [[Pilot]] (2), [[Racer]] (1), [[Starfighter Ace]] (2)

The character removes [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice per rank of Skilled Jockey from \[their\] [[Piloting - Planetary (Ag)|Piloting (Planetary)]] and [[Piloting - Space (Ag)|Piloting (Space)]] checks.
