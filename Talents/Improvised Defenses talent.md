## Improvised Defenses

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Sapper]]

The character may attempt an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Survival (Cun)|Survival check]]** to fashion small defenses using scavenged materials. If the check is successful, the structure can provide cover for up to 4 characters for the rest of the encounter. The character may spend two [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result from the check to increase the ranged defense the structure provides to 2.

[^1]: [[Category - Engineer|Fully Operational]]: *A Sourcebook for Engineers* (Page 30)
