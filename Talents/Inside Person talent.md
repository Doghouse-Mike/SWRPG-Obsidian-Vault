## Inside Person

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Sleeper Agent]]

Once per session, the character may spend a Destiny Point to establish \[they\] have previously been undercover gathering information in an identified enemy base or large vehicle. For the remainder of the session, add automatic two [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results to checks \[they\] or \[their\] allies make that are associated with that locations (such as checks to attack it or social skill checks targeting personnel in it).

[^1]: [[Category - Spy|Cyphers and Masks]]: *A Sourcebook for Spies* (Page 31)
