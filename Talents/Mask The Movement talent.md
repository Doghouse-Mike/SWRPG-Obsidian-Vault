## Mask The Movement

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Agitator (reSpec)]]

The character may take the Mask The Movement action; Make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Deception (Cun)|Deception check]]** to cover the character's or allies' involvement in a crime or dissident activity. On success, authorities are misled, adding a [[Narrative Dice#Failure|failure result]] plus a [[Narrative Dice#Failure|failure result]] for each additional two [[Narrative Dice#Success|success results]] to all checks to investigate that activity or the characters involved with it this session. The character may spend three [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage results]] or a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph result]] to implicate a third party who is both known to both the character and to authorities. This can be accomplished by physical tampering of the crime scene, bearing false witness, spreading rumors, or a combination thereof.
