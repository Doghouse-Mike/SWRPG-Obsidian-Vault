## Hit The Deck

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Demolitionist (reSpec)]]

After determining the character would be hit by an explosive, but before damage is applied, spend a Destiny Point to perform a move maneuver as an incidental to move into cover or out of the blast radius of a weapon or explosion.

### Improved

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Demolitionist (reSpec)]]

When activating Hit The Deck, allies within short range of the character equal to [[Cool (Pr)|Cool]] or [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]] may also perform a Move maneuver as an incidental to move into cover out of a blast radius of a weapon or explosion.
