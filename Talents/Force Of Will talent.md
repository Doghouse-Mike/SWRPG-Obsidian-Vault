## Force of Will

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Force Adherent]], [[Force-Sensitive Emergent]], [[Master]], [[Nightsister]]

Once per game session, the character can choose to make one skill check using Willpower rather than the characteristic linked to that skill. When they do this, they must explain how, in this particular instance, their willpower is helping them overcome this challenge.
