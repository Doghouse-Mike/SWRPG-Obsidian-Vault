## Unearth the Answer

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Archaeologist (reSpec)]]

Once per session, make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Lore (Int)|Knowledge (Lore) check]]** to reveal that an item or place conceals a hidden secret that solves a problem in the current scene. What exactly the revelation is and how the character finds it is per Game Master discretion.
