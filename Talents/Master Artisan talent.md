## Master Artisan

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Artisan]], [[Droid Specialist]], [[Shipwright]]

Once per round, the character may voluntarily suffer 2 strain to decrease the difficulty of \[their\] next [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics]] check (or \[their\] next check to build or mod an item) by one, to a minimum of **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Easy]]**.

[^2]: [[Category - Way of the Force|***Star Wars*** **Force and Destiny**]] Roleplaying Game: *Core Rulebook* (Page 146)
