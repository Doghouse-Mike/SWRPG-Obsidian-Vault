## Subject Matter Expert

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Analyst (reSpec)]]

Once per encounter, the character may spend 1 Destiny point to add three [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost dice]] to any check they make to which their personal knowledge or experience is relevant, per GM discretion.
