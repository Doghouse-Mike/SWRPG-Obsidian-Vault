## No Witnesses

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Assassin (reSpec)]]

Once per encounter, after the character incapacitates an enemy, make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Stealth (Ag)|Stealth check]]**. On success, they are undetected as the attacker. Spend [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results to move one range band away from the crime per [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result spent, or spend [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] to escape to extreme range, or leave the scene completely, per Game Master discretion.
