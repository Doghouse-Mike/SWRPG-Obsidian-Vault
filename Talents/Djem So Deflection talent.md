## Djem So Deflection

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Shien Expert]]

*Force talent.* After using the [[Reflect talent|Reflect]] incidental, the character may spend one Destiny Point to perform a Move maneuver as an out of turn incidental to move closer to or engage an opponent. This incidental may be performed once per round.

[^1]: [[Category - Way of the Force|***Star Wars*** **Force and Destiny**]] Roleplaying Game: *Core Rulebook* (Page 142)
