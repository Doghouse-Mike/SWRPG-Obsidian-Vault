## The Perfect Crime

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Assassin (reSpec)]]

Once per session, make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Skulduggery (Cun)|Skullduggery check]]**. On success, the character (or player) reveals how their previously unknown plan allows them to commit a stealthy or underhanded crime or attack during this scene or encounter. The nature of the revelation is ultimately subject to the GM's discretion, but they should give a significant advantage to the PCs. If the check fails and generates a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result, however, the GM may decide that the character's plan goes seriously awry.
