## Quick Pickup

Source: [^0-1][^2]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Driver (reSpec)]], [[Bodyguard (reSpec)]]

Once per encounter while piloting a starship or vehicle, make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Piloting - Planetary (Ag)|Piloting]] [[Piloting - Space (Ag)|check]]** to have any number of allies within medium range of the starship or vehicle board it as an out-of-turn incidental.
