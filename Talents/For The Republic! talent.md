## For the Republic!

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Clone Officer]]

Once per encounter when an ally is incapacitated or killed, the character may perform the For the Republic! incidental. The character makes a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Daunting difficulty]] [[Leadership (Pr)|Leadership check]]**. If the check succeeds, the ally is not incapacitated or killed until the end of the following round, instead. If the character's wounds or strain are reduced below their threshold before the end of the next round, they are not incapacitated.

[^1]: [[Category - Clone Wars Begin|Rise of the Separatists]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 39)
