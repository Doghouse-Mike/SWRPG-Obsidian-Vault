## Respected Scholar

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Archaeologist]] (2), [[Scholar]] (2), [[Scientist]] (2), [[Teacher]] (1)

The character downgrades the difficulty of checks to interact with institutions of learning by one per rank of Respected Scholar.
