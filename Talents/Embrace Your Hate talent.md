## Embrace Your Hate

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Juyo Berserker]] (2)

After making a successful [[Brawl (Br)|Brawl]], [[Lightsaber (Br)|Lightsaber]], or [[Melee (Br)|Melee]] combat check, the character may spend one Destiny Point and then gain Conflict equal to the number of dark side Destiny Points in the Destiny pool to add an equal amount of damage to one hit of the attack. The character may use this ability once per encounter for each rank of Embrace Your Hate. A character who has purchased one or more ranks of this talent automatically gains 1 Conflict at the beginning of a game session.

[^1]: [[Category - Warrior|Knights of Fate]]: *A Sourcebook for Warriors* (Page 30)
