## Sleight of Mind

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Force-Sensitive Emergent]] (2), [[Padawan Survivor]] (1), [[Pathfinder]] (1), [[Sentry]](2), [[Shadow]] (2)

*Force talent.* The character adds a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die per rank of Sleight of Mind to \[their\] [[Stealth (Ag)|Stealth]] checks unless the being attempting to detect the character is immune to Force Powers.
