## Strategic Form

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Soresu Defender]]

*Force talent.* While wielding a Lightsaber weapon, the character may take the Strategic Form action, making a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Lightsaber (Br)|Lightsaber (Intellect) check]]** and rolling [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] dice no greater than Force rating as part of the check. For each [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] result, one target within short range of the character may only make combat checks if those combat checks target the character until the end of the following round. The character may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]] to cause Strategic Form to affect a target for one additional round. The effects of Strategic Form end if the character is incapacitated.

[^1]: [[Category - Way of the Force|***Star Wars*** **Force and Destiny**]] Roleplaying Game: *Core Rulebook* (Page 152)
