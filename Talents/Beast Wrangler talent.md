## Beast Wrangler

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Beast Rider]] (2)

The character adds a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die per rank of Beast Wrangler to all \[their\] checks made to tame or wrangle creatures.

[^1]: [[Category - Ace|Stay on Target]]: *A Sourcebook for Aces* (Page 32)
