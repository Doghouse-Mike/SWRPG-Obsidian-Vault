## Knowledgeable Healing

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Alchemist]], [[Healer]]

When the character performs a successful [[Medicine (Int)|Medicine]] check to heal an ally, the character may spend on Destiny Point to allow the target to heal additional wounds equal to the character's ranks in [[Xenology (Int)|Knowledge (Xenology)]].

[^1]: [[Category - Way of the Force|***Star Wars*** **Force and Destiny**]] Roleplaying Game: *Core Rulebook* (Page 146)
