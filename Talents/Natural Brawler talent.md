## Natural Brawler

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Infiltrator]], [[Marauder]], [[Martial Artist]]

Once per game session, the character may reroll any one [[Brawl (Br)|Brawl]] or [[Melee (Br)|Melee]] check.
