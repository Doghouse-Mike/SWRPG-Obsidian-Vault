## Alchemical Arts

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Alchemist]] (2)

*Force talent.* After making a crafting check to create a potion or talisman, the character may choose to suffer strain no greater than twice their ranks in Alchemical Arts. For every two strain, they may add a [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|light point]] or [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|dark point]] to the results. If they choose to add a [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|dark point]], they also suffer 1 Conflict per [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|dark point]].

[^1]: [[Category - Mystic|Unlimited Power]]: *A Sourcebook for Mystics* (Page 32)
