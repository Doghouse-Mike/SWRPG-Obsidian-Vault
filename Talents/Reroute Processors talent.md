## Reroute Processors

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Droid Specialist]], [[Droid Tech]]

Once per encounter, the character may perform the Reroute Processors action on a droid \[they\] are engaged with by making an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Computers (Int)|Computers check]]**. If successful, the character decreases on of the droid's characteristics by one (to a minimum of 0) until the end of the encounter and increases another of its characteristics by one (to a maximum of 7) until the end of the encounter. If the character is a droid, it may perform this action on itself.

[^2]: [[Category - Technician|Special Modifications]]: *A Sourcebook for Technicians* (Page 35)
