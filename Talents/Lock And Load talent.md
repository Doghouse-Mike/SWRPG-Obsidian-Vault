## Lock And Load

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Heavy (reSpec)]]

Once per encounter, the character may load or reload a weapon as an incidental, including those with the limited ammo quality. This talent reduces the amount of time to draw or stow a weapon that usually requires more than one maneuver to properly prepare or stow by 1 maneuver, or reduces its [[Item Qualities#Prepare (Passive)|Prepare]] quality by 1.
