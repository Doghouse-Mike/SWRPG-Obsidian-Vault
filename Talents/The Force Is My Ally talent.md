## The Force is my Ally

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Master]], [[Sage]], [[Seer]]

*Force talent.* Once per session, the character may suffer 2 strain to perform a Force power action as a maneuver.
