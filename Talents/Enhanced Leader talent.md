## Enhanced Leader

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[General]], [[Peacekeeper]]

*Force talent.* When making a [[Leadership (Pr)|Leadership]] check, the character may add [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force dice]] no greater than Force rating to the check. The character may spend [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|points]] to add [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] or [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantages]] (character's choice) to the result.

[^2]: [[Category - Way of the Force|***Star Wars*** **Force and Destiny**]] Roleplaying Game: *Core Rulebook* (Page 142)
