## Natural Rogue

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Gambler]], [[Infiltrator]], [[Pirate]], [[Thief]]

Once per game session, the character may reroll any one [[Skulduggery (Cun)|Skulduggery]] or [[Stealth (Ag)|Stealth]] check.
