## Forceful Expulsion

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Slicer (reSpec)]]

Once per session, make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Computers (Int)|Computers check]]** to eject an another character accessing a connected system. Spend two [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results from this check or on a successful Eject User check to inflict strain equal to ranks of [[Education (Int)|Knowledge (Education)]], and may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result to render the chosen character's slicer's gear or access point unusable.
