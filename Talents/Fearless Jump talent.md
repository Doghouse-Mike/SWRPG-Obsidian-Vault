## Fearless Jump

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Fringer (reSpec)]]

Once per session, reroll an [[Astrogation (Int)|Astrogation]] check to make a hyperspace jump, and add a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] and [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] to the result. The [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] added this way cannot be canceled by effects of talents, abilities, or equipment.

[^0-1]: [[Category - ReSpecialized|The reSpecialized Project]]'s Fringer Folio
