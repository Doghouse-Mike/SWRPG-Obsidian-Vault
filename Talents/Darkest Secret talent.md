## Darkest Secret

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Dark Side Cultist]]

Once per encounter, the character may take a Darkest Secret action, making a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Daunting difficulty]] [[Category - Knowledge Skills|Knowledge check]]** (the GM and player should determine which Knowledge skill is most applicable in the given situation). If successful, the character may add a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result to any one check made by an adversary during the encounter. The subsequent check should relate in some way to the facts the character learned, or the player should come up with an explanation for why the information the character learned is instrumental in the disaster of the adversary's check.

[^0-1]: [[Category - Sequels|Fires of Resistance]]: *An Unofficial Era Sourcebook* (Page 11)
