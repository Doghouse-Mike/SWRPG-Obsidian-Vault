## Explosive Surprise

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Demolitionist (reSpec)]]

Once per session, make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]]** **[[Skulduggery (Cun)|Skulduggery check]]** to establish how a previously existing device or explosive the character posesses has been covertly placed somewhere within long range of them. Spend four [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result to allow the character to make an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Easy difficulty]] [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics check]]** as an incidental to activate or detonate the device while they are within extreme range of it. How the device was hidden is at the Game Master's discretion.
