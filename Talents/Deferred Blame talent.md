## Deferred Blame

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Imperial Loyalist]]

Once per session, take the Deferred Blame action: make an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Deception (Cun)|Deception check]]** to shift the blame for a mistake this character made to another person.
