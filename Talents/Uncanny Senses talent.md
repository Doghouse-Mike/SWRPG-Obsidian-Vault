## Uncanny Senses

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Force-Sensitive Emergent]] (2), [[Force Sensitive Exile]] (2), [[Force-Sensitive Outcast]] (1), [[Hunter]] (1), [[Navigator]] (1), [[Warleader]] (2)

*Force talent.* The character adds a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die per rank of Uncanny Senses to \[their\] [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] checks.
