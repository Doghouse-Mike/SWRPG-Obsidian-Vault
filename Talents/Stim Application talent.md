## Stim Application

Source: [^0-1][^1-2][^2-3]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Alchemist]], [[Doctor]], [[Medic]]

The character may take the Stim Application action. To perform this action, they must have access to [[Category - Drugs & Poisons|drugs]], a [[Medpac|medpac]], or [[Stimpack|stimpacks]]. They make an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Medicine (Int)|Medicine check]]**. If successful, one ally they are engaged with (including themselves) increases one characteristic of the character's choice by one for the remainder of the encounter, and suffers four strain. A single character's individual characteristics may each only be increased once by Stim Application during an encounter.

### Improved

Source: [^0-1][^1-2][^2-3]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Alchemist]], [[Doctor]], [[Medic]]

As an incidental, when performing the Stim Application action, the character may increase the difficulty of the [[Medicine (Int)|Medicine]] check to **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard]]**. If they do so, the target of the action suffers one strain instead of four.

### Supreme

Source: [^1-2][^2-3]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Doctor]], [[Medic]]

When performing the Stim Application action, each [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result may be spent to increase an additional characteristic by one.
