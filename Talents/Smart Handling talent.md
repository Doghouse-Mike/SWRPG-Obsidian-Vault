## Smart Handling

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Shipwright]]

Once per session, while aboard a starship of silhouette 4 or higher, the character may take the Smart Handling action; making a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Education (Int)|Knowledge (Education) check]]**. If the check is successful, until the start of the next round, the ship's handling increases by two plus one per [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result scored on the check to a maximum handling of +4. A [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result can be spent to extend the effect until the end of the encounter.

[^1]: [[Category - Engineer|Fully Operational]]: *A Sourcebook for Engineers* (Page 31)
