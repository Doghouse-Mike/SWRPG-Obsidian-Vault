## One with the Universe

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Navigator]], [[Sage]]

*Force talent.* Once per session, the character may spend several minutes meditating, then take the One with the Universe action, making an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Astrogation (Int)|Astrogation check]]**. If successful, during the next encounter \[they\] add a [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Light point]] to all Force power checks. However, if \[they\] succeeded with a [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] result, \[they\] add a [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|Dark point]] to all Force power checks instead.

[^1]: [[Category - Way of the Force|***Star Wars*** **Force and Destiny**]] Roleplaying Game: *Core Rulebook* (Page 149)
