## Sense the Scene

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Investigator]]

*Force talent.* Once per session, the character may perform the Sense the Scene action. \[They\] make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Perception (Cun)|Perception check]]** while present at a single crime scene (or similar location), adding [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] dice no greater than \[their\] Force rating to the check. If \[they\] succeed and generate a [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]], \[they\] may identify the emotional state of one person involved in the crime (such as the perpetrator or victim). \[They\] may spend an additional [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]] to identify the emotional state of one additional person involved in the crime, and may do so multiple times.

[^1]: [[Category - Sentinel|Endless Vigil]]: *A Sourcebook for Sentinels* (Page 31)
