## Improvised Concoction

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Alchemist]]

*Force talent.* Once per encounter, as an action, the character may make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Xenology (Int)|Knowledge (Xenology) check]]**, adding [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] dice no greater than their Force rating. If the character succeeds, they create 1 dose of any [[Alchemical Crafting|potion]], [[Category - Poisons|poison]], or [[Category - Drugs & Poisons|drug]] out of available materials. The rarity of this item must be lower than or equal to the character's Intellect + [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] results + [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|points]] spent.

[^1]: [[Category - Mystic|Unlimited Power]]: *A Sourcebook for Mystics* (Page 32)
