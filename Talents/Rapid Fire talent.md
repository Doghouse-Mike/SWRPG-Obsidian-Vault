## Rapid Fire

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[ARC Trooper]]

Before making a [[Ranged - Light (Ag)|Ranged (Light)]] combat check using a weapon that does not have the [[Item Qualities#Limited Ammo (Passive)|Limited Ammo]] item quality, the character may perform the Rapid Fire incidental to add the [[Item Qualities#Auto-fire (Active)|Auto-fire]] item quality to the attack. After resolving the attack, the weapon runs out of ammo.

[^1]: [[Category - Clone Wars End|Collapse of the Republic]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 42)
