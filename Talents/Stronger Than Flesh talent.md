## Stronger Than Flesh

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Cyber Tech (reSpec)]]

Once per encounter, avoid suffering an [[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average]] or [[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Easy]] severity critical injury by having an installed cybernetic immediately malfunction, chosen by the Game Master. It provides no benefit until the character spends 6 hours and makes an [[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics check]] to repair it.

### Improved

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Cyber Tech (reSpec)]]

The character may use Stronger Than Flesh to avoid [[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard]] or [[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Daunting]] severity critical injuries.

#### Supreme

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Cyber Tech (reSpec)]]

Immediately after being successfully attacked but before suffering damage, spend 1 Destiny Point to increase the character's Soak by half the number of installed cybernetics, rounded up.
