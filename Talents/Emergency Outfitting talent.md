## Emergency Outfitting

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Maneuver)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Quartermaster (reSpec)]]

Once per session as a maneuver, the character may make an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance check]]** to reveal an undisclosed piece of personal gear that is available for immediate use, and may spend three [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage results]] or a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph result]] to produce a number of identical items equal to the character's ranks of [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]].

If the equipment has a rarity higher than the character's contribution rating plus 2, or the character does not have a Contribution Rank, it is considered on loan by the character's organization or an ally specified by the Game Master. Otherwise it may be kept by the character and their allies.
