## Relentless

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Skip Tracer (reSpec)]] (2)

When making check to chase, track, or tail a target, suffer a number of strain to downgrade the difficulty of the check a number of times equal to the strain suffered. This number cannot exceed their ranks in Relentless.
