## Galaxy Mapper

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Commodore]] (3), [[Fringer]] (2), [[Navigator]] (2), [[Operator]] (1), [[Pilot]] (2), [[Republic Navy Officer]] (2), [[Starfighter Ace]] (1)

The character removes a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die per rank of Galaxy Mapper from \[their\] [[Astrogation (Int)|Astrogation]] checks. In addition, [[Astrogation (Int)|Astrogation]] checks take 50% less time (this does not increase with additional ranks of Galaxy Mapper).
