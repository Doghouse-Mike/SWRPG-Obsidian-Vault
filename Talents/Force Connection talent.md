## Force Connection

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Hermit]]

*Force talent.* When the character performs a [[Survival (Cun)|Survival]] or [[Xenology (Int)|Knowledge (Xenology)]] skill check, \[they\] may roll a number of [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] dice no greater than \[their\] Force rating. The character may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]] to add a [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] or [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result (character's choice) to the result.

[^1]: [[Category - Seeker|Savage Spirits]]: *A Sourcebook for Seekers* (Page 30)
