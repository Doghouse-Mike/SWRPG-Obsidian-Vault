## Let's Make It Interesting

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Gambler (reSpec)]]

Once per session after making a successful check to wager or gamble, spend a Destiny Point to have a loser substitute or supplement a narrative or material reward of the character's choice, instead of only credits. The reward is per GM discretion.
