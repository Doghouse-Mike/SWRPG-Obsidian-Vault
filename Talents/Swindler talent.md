## Swindler

Source: [^0-1][^2]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Gambler (reSpec)]] (1), [[Scoundrel (reSpec)]] (1)

The character adds a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die or removes a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die per rank of Swindler from checks to lie, cheat, steal or commit subterfuge. Applicable checks are per GM discretion.
