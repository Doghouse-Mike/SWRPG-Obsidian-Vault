## Marked for Death

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Maneuver)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Executioner]]

*Force talent.* The character may take the Marked for Death maneuver, selecting one target and committing a [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] die. The character adds two automatic [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results to combat checks against that target while a [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] die remains committed, but cannot use the Marked for Death talent again until the character uncommits Force dice, the original target is incapacitated, or the session ends. [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|Advantage]] results gained in this way cannot be used to recover strain.

[^1]: [[Category - Seeker|Savage Spirits]]: *A Sourcebook for Seekers* (Page 30)
