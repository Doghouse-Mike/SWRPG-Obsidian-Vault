## Read The Wind

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Scout (reSpec)]]

Once per encounter, make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Perception (Cun)|Perception check]]**. On success, a moderate environmental effect appears. Spend a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result for a severe environmental effect.

### Improved

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Maneuver)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Archaeologist (reSpec)|Scout (reSpec)]]

The character may suffer 2 strain to perform the Read The Wind action as a maneuver.

#### Supreme

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out-of-Turn)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Archaeologist (reSpec)|Scout (reSpec)]]

Once per round, the character may suffer a number of strain up to their ranks in [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] to remove up to that many [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice imposed due to environmental conditions from an enemy's check, adding an equal number of [[Narrative Dice#Failure|failure]] or [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results to that check. If four or more [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice are removed this way, add a single [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result to the check instead of [[Narrative Dice#Failure|failure]] or [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results.
