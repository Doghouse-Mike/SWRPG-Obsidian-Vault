## Martial Prowess

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Martial Artist (reSpec)]]

Once per round when making a combat check, the character may suffer 2 strain to substitute [[Athletics (Br)|Athletics]] instead of [[Melee (Br)|Melee]] or [[Coordination (Ag)|Coordination]] instead of [[Ranged - Light (Ag)|Ranged (Light)]] or [[Ranged - Heavy (Ag)|(Heavy)]].
