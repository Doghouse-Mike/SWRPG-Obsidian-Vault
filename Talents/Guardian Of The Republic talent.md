## Guardian of the Republic

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Knight]]

*Force talent.* After the character uses [[Parry talent|Parry]] or [[Reflect talent|Reflect]] to reduce damage to an ally, that ally cannot be targeted by combat checks for the remainder of the round.

[^1]: [[Category - Clone Wars Begin|Rise of the Separatists]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 39)
