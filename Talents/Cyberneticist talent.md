## Cyberneticist

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Cyber Tech]] (2)

The character removes a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die per rank of Cyberneticist from \[their\] checks to build, repair, and install cybernetic implants. In addition, cybernetics and any materials used to craft cybernetics cost the character 50% less (this does not decrease with additional ranks of Cyberneticist).

[^1]: [[Category - Technician|Special Modifications]]: *A Sourcebook for Technicians* (Page 34)
