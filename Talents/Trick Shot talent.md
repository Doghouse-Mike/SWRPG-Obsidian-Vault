## Trick Shot

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Gunslinger (reSpec)]]

The character removes two [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice imposed on [[Ranged - Heavy (Ag)|Ranged (Heavy)]] or [[Ranged - Light (Ag)|Ranged (Light)]] checks due to their target's cover or from the aim maneuver. Additionally, the character may make [[Ranged - Heavy (Ag)|Ranged (Heavy)]] or [[Ranged - Light (Ag)|Ranged (Light)]] attacks targeting foes for which they lack line of sight, per GM discretion.
