## Clever Commander

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Clone Commander]], [[Strategist]]

When upgrading the ability of a Mass Combat check in a mass combat in which the character is the commander of the acting force (or when upgrading the difficulty if the character is the commander of the enemy force), the character may use ranks of [[Warfare (Int)|Knowledge (Warfare)]] instead of ranks in [[Leadership (Pr)|Leadership]].

[^1]: [[Category - Clone Wars End|Collapse of the Republic]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 38)
[^2]: [[Category - Commander|Lead by Example]]: *A Sourcebook for Commanders* (Page 34)
