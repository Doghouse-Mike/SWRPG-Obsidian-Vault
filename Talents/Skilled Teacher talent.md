## Skilled Teacher

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Teacher]] (2)

Before an ally at short range makes a skill check, if that ally has fewer ranks in that skill than the character does, the character may choose to immediately perform a Skilled Teacher incidental to suffer a number of strain, then add an equal number of [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] results to the ally's next check. The number of strain suffered cannot exceed the character's ranks in Skilled Teacher.

[^1]: [[Category - Consular|Disciples of Harmony]]: *A Sourcebook for Consulars* (Page 30)
