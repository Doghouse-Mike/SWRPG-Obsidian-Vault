## Disorienting Strike

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Padawan Survivor]]

After missing an opponent with a [[Lightsaber (Br)|Lightsaber]] combat check, the character may choose to spend two [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result to disorient \[their\] foe for three rounds. Disoriented opponents add a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die to all skill checks.

[^1]: [[Category - Early Rebellion|Dawn of Rebellion]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 112)
