## Desperate Scramble

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Fringer (reSpec)]]

Once per session, make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Coordination (Ag)|Coordination check]]** to escape immediate confinement through a previously unknown method. On success, the character is able to escape and may spend three [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result to allow allies to follow. The nature of the escape method is Game Master discretion, but may be highly improbable.
