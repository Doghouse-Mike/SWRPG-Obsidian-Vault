## Showboat

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Hotshot]]

Once per round while piloting a starship or vehicle, the character may suffer 2 strain to showboat during \[their\] next check. If the check succeeds, the character adds a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result to the results. However, if the check fails, \[they\] add a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] instead. The [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] or [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] results added this way do not include the success or failure results that are usually part of them, only the beneficial or negative effects of the symbol.

[^1]: [[Category - Ace|Stay on Target]]: *A Sourcebook for Aces* (Page 33)
