## Knockdown

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[ARC Trooper]], [[Archaeologist]], [[Commando]], [[Fringer]], [[Infiltrator]], [[Marauder]]

After hitting with a melee attack, the character may knock the target prone by spending a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result. If the target is larger than the acting character, it requires one additional [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] for each silhouette larger.

[^1]: [[Category - Clone Wars End|Collapse of the Republic]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 41)
[^2]: [[Category - Rebel Ranks|***Star Wars*** **Age of Rebellion**]] Roleplaying Game: *Core Rulebook* (Page 150)
[^3]: [[Category - On the Fringe|***Star Wars*** **Edge of the Empire**]] Roleplaying Game: *Core Rulebook* (Page 138)
