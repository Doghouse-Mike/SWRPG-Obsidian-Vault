## Stunning Blow

Source: [^0-1][^1-2]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Archaeologist]], [[Enforcer]], [[Gadgeteer]], [[Infiltrator]]

When making [[Melee (Br)|Melee]] checks, the character may choose to deal damage as strain instead of wounds. This does not ignore soak.

### Improved

Source: [^0-1][^1-2]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Gadgeteer]], [[Infiltrator]]

When dealing strain damage with [[Melee (Br)|Melee]] or [[Brawl (Br)|Brawl]] checks, the character may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result to stagger \[their\] target for one round per [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] spent.
