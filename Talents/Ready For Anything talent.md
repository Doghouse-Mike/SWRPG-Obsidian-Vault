## Ready for Anything

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Strategist]] (2)

Remove a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice per rank of Ready for Anything from mass combat made while the character is acting on the side of the acting force and the character's [[Cool (Pr)|Cool]] or [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]] checks to determine Initiative order.

### Improved

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Strategist]]

When making [[Cool (Pr)|Cool]] or [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]] checks to determine Initiative order, the character may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result to add additional [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] results equal to ranks in Ready for Anything.
