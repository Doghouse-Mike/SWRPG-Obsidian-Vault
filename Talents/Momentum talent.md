## Momentum

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Death Watch Warrior]] (1)

When making a melee attack, the character deals additional damage equal to the number of range bands they moved during the current round.

[^1]: [[Category - Clone Wars End|Collapse of the Republic]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 41)
