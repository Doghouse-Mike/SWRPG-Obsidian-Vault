## Uniter Of People

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Ambassador (reSpec)]]

Once per session, the character may choose a faction or group that the character doesn't belong to and make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Charm (Pr)|Charm]]** or **[[Negotiation (Pr)|Negotiation check]]**. On success, allies from the character's faction and the chosen faction within medium range of the character add an [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage result]] to all skill checks this encounter while working towards a shared goal together. The character may spend four [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage results]] or a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph result]] to decrease the difficulty of all affected characters' next check. Which character belong to which group, and what constitutes a shared goal, is determined by the Game Master.

[^1]: [[Category - ReSpecialized|The reSpecialized Project]]'s Ambassador Folio
