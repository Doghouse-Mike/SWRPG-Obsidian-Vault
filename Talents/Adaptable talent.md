## Adaptable

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Padawan]]

*Force talent.* When resolving a check that uses a skill in which they have no ranks, the character may spend one Destiny Point to remove a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result from the check or to remove [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results equal to their ranks in [[Cool (Pr)|Cool]] from the check.

[^1]: [[Category - Clone Wars Begin|Rise of the Separatists]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 37)
