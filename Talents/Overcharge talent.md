## Overcharge

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Cyber Tech]]

Once per encounter, the character may take the Overcharge action by making a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics check]]** and choosing one of \[their\] cybernetic implants that grants \[them\] one or more of the following: +1 to a characteristic rating, +1 rank to a skill, or +1 rank of a ranked talent. If \[they\] succeed, until the end of the encounter, the chosen cybernetic provides an additional +1 to any characteristic ratings (to a maximum of 7), an additional +1 ranks to any skills (to a maximum of 5), and an additional +1 ranks of any ranked talents that it provides.

The GM may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] from the check to have the overcharged cybernetic short out at the end of the encounter; it provides no benefit until the character spends several hours making an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics check]]** to repair it.

#### Improved

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Cyber Tech]]

Once per round when using the Overcharge action, the character may spend two [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result from the [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics]] check to immediately take one additional action.

#### Supreme

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Cyber Tech]]

When performing the Overcharge action, the character may choose any number of cybernetic implants to attempt to overcharge instead of just one. If \[they\] do, upgrade the difficulty of the [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics]] check once for each additional cybernetic beyond the first.

The GM may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result from the check to have any one overcharged cybernetic short out at the end of the encounter; each cybernetic that shorts out provides no benefit until the character spends several hours making an [[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|**Average difficulty**]] **[[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics check]]** to repair it.
