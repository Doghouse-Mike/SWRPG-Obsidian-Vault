## Read The Room

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Advocate (reSpec)]]

Once per encounter, the character may take the Read The Room action; make an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance check]]**. On success, gain social insight into one character per [[Narrative Dice#Success|success result]], and may spend two [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage results]] to add a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost die]] to all social checks made by allies against one of those characters this encounter. For each character, the player learns which social skill will be most effective against them, or which social skill they are most likely to employ, but not both.
