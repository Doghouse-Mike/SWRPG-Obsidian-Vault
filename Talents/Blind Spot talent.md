## Blind Spot

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Trailblazer]], [[Warleader]]

The character and allies within short range add an automatic [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result to combat checks they make while benefiting from cover.
