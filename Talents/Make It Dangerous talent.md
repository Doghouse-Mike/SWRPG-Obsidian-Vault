## Make it Dangerous

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Maneuver)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Outlaw Tech (reSpec)]]

Once per encounter, make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Education (Int)|Knowledge (Education) check]]** to add choice of [[Item Qualities#Burn (Active)|Burn]] 1, [[Item Qualities#Sunder (Active)|Sunder]], or [[Item Qualities#Vicious (Passive)|Vicious]] 1 to a weapon within engaged range until the end of the character's next turn, and may spend two advantage results to extend the effect one round. If that weapon already has that quality, increase its rating by one. The Game Master may spend two [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result from this check or any check made using the weapon before this effect expires to damage it one step.

### Improved

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Cyber Tech (reSpec)|Outlaw Tech (reSpec)]]

Suffer 1 strain to decrease the difficulty of Make it Dangerous to **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]]**. Spend four [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results or [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result so that all critical injuries inflicted by an attack using this weapon result in a Crippled effect, unless the roll would result in a more grievous injury. This effect lasts until Make It Dangerous expires.
