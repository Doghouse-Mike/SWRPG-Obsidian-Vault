## Fly Casual

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Blockade Runner]]

Once per encounter while piloting or aboard a familiar craft, spend a Destiny Point to add [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice equal to the character's Cunning to a check made to identify, scan, or investigate the craft or its contents.
