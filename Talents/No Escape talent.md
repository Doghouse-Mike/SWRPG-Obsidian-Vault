## No Escape

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Warden]]

*Force talent.* When making a [[Coercion (Will)|Coercion]] check, the character may spend two [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results to cause one enemy within short range to lose \[their\] free maneuver during \[their\] next turn. When a foe within short range makes a Fear check, the character may spend two [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results from that check to cause that enemy to lose \[their\] free maneuver during \[their\] next turn.

[^1]: [[Category - Guardian|Keeping the Peace]]: *A Sourcebook for Guardians* (Page 30)
