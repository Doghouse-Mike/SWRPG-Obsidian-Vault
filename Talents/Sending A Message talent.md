## Sending A Message

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Enforcer (reSpec)]]

Once per encounter, after incapacitating a foe or inflicting a critical injury, suffer 3 strain to add [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results to all checks made this encounter by foes equal to ranks Willpower within medium range of the character.
