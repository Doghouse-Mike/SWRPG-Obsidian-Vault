## Well-Supplied

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Quartermaster (reSpec)]]

Once per round when an ally attempts a skill check, the character may spend 25 credits times the difficulty of the check to add a [[Narrative Dice#Success|success result]] to that check. The character (or player) must justify what previous investment they made in that ally's equipment led to their success. This talent may also be used on an allied Mass Combat check by spending 250 credits times the difficulty of the check, as determined by the size of the opposing force.

### Improved

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Quartermaster (reSpec)]]

When activating Well-Supplied, increase the affected ally's strain threshold by 2 until the end of the encounter. Each ally may only benefit from this once per encounter.
