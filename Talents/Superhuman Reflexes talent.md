## Superhuman Reflexes

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Racer]]

*Force talent.* Once per session, when the character generates a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result on a [[Piloting - Planetary (Ag)|Piloting (Planetary)]] or [[Piloting - Space (Ag)|Piloting (Space)]] check, \[they\] may cancel the [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result and add [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] results equal to \[their\] ranks in [[Cool (Pr)|Cool]] to the results.

[^1]: [[Category - Sentinel|Endless Vigil]]: *A Sourcebook for Sentinels* (Page 31)
