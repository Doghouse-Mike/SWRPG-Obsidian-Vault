## Holistic Navigation

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Navigator]]

*Force talent.* When making an [[Astrogation (Int)|Astrogation]] skill check, the character may spend one Destiny Point to remove a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result, or remove [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results equal to \[their\] ranks in [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]].

[^1]: [[Category - Seeker|Savage Spirits]]: *A Sourcebook for Seekers* (Page 30)
