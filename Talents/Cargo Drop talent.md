## Cargo Drop

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Blockade Runner]]

Once per encounter, make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Skulduggery (Cun)|Skulduggery check]]** to dump the ship's cargo. Foes within short range equal to [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] results must drop to speed 1 or suffer a major collision. Spend two [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result to trigger any additional effects, based on the nature of the cargo dropped, and Game Master determination.
