## Well-Connected

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Politico (reSpec)]]

Once per session, the character reveals a contact who can provide access to a person, location, or resource and how the character knows them. The contact should be open to helping the character, bu the Game Master determines their potential cost.
