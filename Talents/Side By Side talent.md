## Side by Side

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Knight]]

*Force talent.* While the character is wielding a lightsaber and engaged with one or more allies also wielding a lightsaber, add a [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] result to all combat checks that target the character or those allies. (If multiple allied characters in the engagement possess this talent, the effects are cumulative.)

[^1]: [[Category - Clone Wars Begin|Rise of the Separatists]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 42)
