## Gatherer

Source: [^0-1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Big Game Hunter (reSpec)]] (1), [[Survivalist (reSpec)]] (1)

When foraging for food, water, or shelter, choose to add a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die or remove a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die from the check per rank of Gatherer.
