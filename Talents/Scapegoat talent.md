## Scapegoat

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[First Order Loyalist]]

Once per session, may take the Scapegoat action; Make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Deception (Cun)|Deception check]]** to ensure that the character is not blamed for a decision or action they were responsible for, and that someone else is.
