## Cartel License

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Cartel Dealer]]

While on a civilized world the character does not suffer legal repercussions from being in the possession of restricted equipment. They can however still be arrested or otherwise persecuted for using any such equipment in an unlawful manner.

[^0-1]: [[Category - Old Republic|The Old Republic]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 26)
