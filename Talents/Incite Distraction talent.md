## Incite Distraction

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Courier]]

While in a crowd or populated area, the character may perform the Incite Distraction action to make an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Deception (Cun)|Deception check]]**. If successful, enemies treat the area as difficult terrain for the remainder of the encounter. The character may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result to make the location impassable terrain to them instead.

[^1]: [[Category - Spy|Cyphers and Masks]]: *A Sourcebook for Spies* (Page 30)
