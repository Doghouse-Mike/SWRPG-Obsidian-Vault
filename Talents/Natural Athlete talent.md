## Natural Athlete

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Courier]], [[Performer]]

Once per game session, the character may reroll any one [[Athletics (Br)|Athletics]] or [[Coordination (Ag)|Coordination]] check.
