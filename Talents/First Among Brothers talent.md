## First Among Brothers

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Clone Commander]] (2), [[Clone Officer]] (2)

When the character makes a check that does not result in any uncancelled [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results, add [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantages]] equal to the character's ranks in First Among Brothers to the check results. The character may only spend these additional [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantages]] to affect allied clones.

[^1]: [[Category - Clone Wars End|Collapse of the Republic]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 39)
[^2]: [[Category - Clone Wars Begin|Rise of the Separatists]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 39)
