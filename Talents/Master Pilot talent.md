## Master Pilot

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Clone Pilot]], [[Pilot]]

Once per round when piloting a starship (a ship piloted using the [[Piloting - Space (Ag)|Piloting (Space)]] skill), the character may voluntarily suffer two strain to perform any action as a maneuver instead.
