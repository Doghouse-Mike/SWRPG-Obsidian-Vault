## Temple Training

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Padawan]], [[Padawan Survivor]]

*Force talent.* Before making a [[Lightsaber (Br)|Lightsaber]] skill check the character may spend one Destiny Point to add damage to the attack equal to their ranks in [[Lore (Int)|Knowledge (Lore)]].
