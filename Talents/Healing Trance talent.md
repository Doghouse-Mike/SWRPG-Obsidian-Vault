## Healing Trance

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Healer]] (3), [[Magus]] (2)

*Force talent.* The character may commit a [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] die. For every full encounter a [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] die remains committed, the character heals 1 wound \[they\] are suffering per rank of Healing Trance. This is in addition to wounds healed due to natural rest or other abilities. If in a situation without defined encounters, the GM can have Healing Trance take effect every 12 hours.

#### Improved

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Healer]], [[Magus]]

*Force talent.* Whenever the character heals wounds as a result of Healing Trance, if the character is suffering from a Critical Injury, \[they\] make a [[Resilience (Br)|Resilience]] check with the difficulty equal to the Critical Injury's severity rating. On a successful check, the character recovers from the Critical Injury. If the character is suffering from multiple Critical Injuries, \[they\] choose which one to attempt to recover from.
