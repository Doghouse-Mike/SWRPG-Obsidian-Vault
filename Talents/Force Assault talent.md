## Force Assault

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Niman Disciple]]

*Force talent.* Upon missing an opponent with a [[Lightsaber (Br)|Lightsaber]] (Willpower) combat check, the character may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result or three [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results to perform a [[Move]] Force power action as a maneuver this turn (the character must still be able to perform maneuvers and still may not perform more than two maneuvers in a turn).

The character must have already purchased the [[Move]] Force power to use it as part of this ability.

[^1]: [[Category - Way of the Force|***Star Wars*** **Force and Destiny**]] Roleplaying Game: *Core Rulebook* (Page 143)
