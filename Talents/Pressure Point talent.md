## Pressure Point

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Doctor]], [[Interrogator]]

When making a [[Brawl (Br)|Brawl]] check against a living opponent, the character may choose to forgo dealing damage as wounds, instead dealing the equivalent damage as strain, plus additional strain equal to \[their\] ranks in [[Medicine (Int)|Medicine]]. These checks cannot be made with any weapons, but this strain damage is not reduced by soak.

[^2]: [[Category - On the Fringe|***Star Wars*** **Edge of the Empire**]] Roleplaying Game: *Core Rulebook* (Page 141)
