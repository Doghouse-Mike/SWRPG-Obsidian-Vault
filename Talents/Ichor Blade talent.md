## Ichor Blade

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Nightsister]]

When the character purchases this talent, choose one Melee or Brawl weapon they possess that does not feature electronic or other high-tech parts. (For example, they could choose a sword or staff, but not a vibroknife or electrostaff.) The weapon gains the [[Item Qualities#Cortosis (Passive)|Cortosis]] and [[Item Qualities#Pierce (Passive)|Pierce]] 2 item qualities and reduces its critical rating by one to a minimum of 1. In addition, any other character adds [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice to checks they make to use this weapon. If the weapon is ever lost or destroyed, the character can apply Ichor Blade to a new weapon. If the weapon leaves the character's possession for longer than a single scene or encounter, it loses all effects of this talent until the character reacquires it.

### Improved

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Nightsister]]

The weapon affected by the character's Ichor Blade talent also gains the [[Item Qualities#Sunder (Active)|Sunder]] and [[Narrative Dice#Defensive (Passive)|Defensive]] 1 item qualities, and increases its damage by 2.
