## Follow My Lead

Source: [^1-1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Mercenary Soldier (reSpec)]]

Once per round, spend two [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result from a check to allow an ally within short range count as having the same number of ranks of that skill as the character for the next check they make using that skill.
