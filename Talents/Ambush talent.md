## Ambush

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Maneuver)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Trailblazer]]

Once per round while benefiting from cover, the character may perform the Ambush maneuver. The character may add additional damage equal to \[their\] ranks in the [[Stealth (Ag)|Stealth]] skill to one hit of the next successful combat check with a non-starship/vehicle weapon \[they\] make against a target within short range before the end of \[their\] turn.

[^1]: [[Category - Soldier|Forged in Battle]]: *A Sourcebook for Soldiers* (Page 34)
