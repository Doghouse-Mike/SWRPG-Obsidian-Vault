## Disruptive Strike

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Shien Expert]]

*Force talent.* The character may take a Disruptive Strike action, making a [[Lightsaber (Br)|Lightsaber]] (Cunning) combat check against one engaged target and adding [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] dice no greater than Force rating to the check. The character may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|points]] to add [[Narrative Dice#Failure|failure]] results to the target's next combat check made during this encounter.

[^1]: [[Category - Way of the Force|***Star Wars*** **Force and Destiny**]] Roleplaying Game: *Core Rulebook* (Page 142)
