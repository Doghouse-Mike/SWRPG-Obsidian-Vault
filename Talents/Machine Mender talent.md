## Machine Mender

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Droid Specialist]] (2), [[Droid Tech]] (3)

When this character makes a [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics]] check to help a character heal wounds, the target heals one additional wound per rank of Machine Mender. (Typically, only droids can be healed with [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics]] check.)

```
The following is a homebrew expansion of this talent from a community-produced homebrew sourcebook!
```

### Improved

Source: [^3]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Droid Tech (reSpec)]]

Once per droid per session, when making a check to repair it, the character may spend two [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results to increase the droid's Melee or Ranged defense by 1 per two [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results spent until the end of the session. A [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result may be spent to increase its Soak rating by 1 until the end of the session.
