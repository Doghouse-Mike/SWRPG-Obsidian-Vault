## Disruptive

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Slicer (reSpec)]] (2)

Suffer a number of strain to downgrade the difficulty of a [[Computers (Int)|Computers]] check, or upgrade the difficulty when opposing a [[Computers (Int)|Computers]] check, by an equal number. Strain suffered this way cannot exceed ranks in Disruptive.

### Improved

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Slicer (reSpec)]]

Spend two [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results from a [[Computers (Int)|Computers]] Check or two [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results when opposing a foe's computers check to add a [[Narrative Dice#Failure|failure]] result equal to the character's ranks of Disruptive to a foe's next [[Computers (Int)|Computers]] check in that system.
