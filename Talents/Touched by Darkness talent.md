## Touched by Darkness

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Dark Side Cultist]] (2)

Whenever the GM spends a Destiny Point, the character recovers 1 strain and 1 wound for each rank of Touched by Darkness. This happens before the GM enacts whatever effect the Destiny Point would have.

[^0-1]: [[Category - Sequels|Fires of Resistance]]: *An Unofficial Era Sourcebook* (Page 12)
