## Consider Our Options

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Republic Representative]]

The character may take the Consider Our Options action, making a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Negotiation (Pr)|Negotiation check]]**. If the check succeeds, until the start of the character's next turn, any enemy who attacks the character suffers 2 strain, plus 1 strain per additional two [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] results on the character's check, before resolving the attack. (If this strain causes an adversary to become incapacitated, the GM may determine that adversary simply bows out of the fight.) The effect end immediately if the character inflicts damage on an enemy.

### Improved

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Republic Representative]]

When the character takes the Consider Our Options action, the character may choose to have the effects also apply to attacks that target the character's allies who are within short range. The effects end for all characters if the character or an affected ally inflict damage on an enemy.
