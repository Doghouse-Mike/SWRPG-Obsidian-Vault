## Something More Real

Source: [^0-1][^2]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Trader (reSpec)]], [[Quartermaster (reSpec)]]

Spend a Destiny Point when negotiating the cost of goods or services to introduce physical goods to be bartered, counting the value of those goods at 125% of their current value, instead of or in addition to credits. Modify the check based on legality and the item's level of damage.
