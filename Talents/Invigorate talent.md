## Invigorate

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Force-Sensitive Emergent]]

*Force talent.* Once per encounter, when an ally within short range attempts a skill check using the Brawn or Agility characteristic, the character may roll a [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force die]]. Each [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Light point]] adds a [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] result to the target's check; however, each [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|Dark point]] adds a [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] result to the target's check.

```
While the Age of Rebellion Roleplaying Game: Core Rulebook shows this as "Activation: Active (Incidental)", the Errata (Page 1) corrects this to "Activation: Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)".
```
