## Frenzied Attack

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Infiltrator]] (2), [[Marauder]] (3)

When making a Melee or Brawl combat check, the character may suffer a number of strain, then upgrade the ability of \[their\] combat check by that number. This number cannot exceed \[their\] ranks in Frenzied Attack.
