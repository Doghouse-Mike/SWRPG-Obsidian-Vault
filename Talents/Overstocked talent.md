## Overstocked

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Quartermaster (reSpec)]]

When purchasing personal equipment, the character may suffer 2 strain to gain an additional unit of that kind of equipment at 50% of its final unit price. The GM may determine what is personal gear, but it could range from weapons and explosives to small speeder bikes.
