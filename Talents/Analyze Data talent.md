## Analyze Data

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Sleeper Agent]]

Once per session, after capturing or stealing data about an opponent, the character may perform the Analyze Data action to make an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Warfare (Int)|Knowledge (Warfare) check]]** to determine its value. If successful, once before the end of the session the character may add an automatic [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] results equal to their ranks in [[Warfare (Int)|Knowledge (Warfare)]] to one check related to that captured data. The GM has the final say on what checks are related to the data.

[^1]: [[Category - Spy|Cyphers and Masks]]: *A Sourcebook for Spies* (Page 30)
