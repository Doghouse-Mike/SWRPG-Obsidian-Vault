## Natural Hunter

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Big-Game Hunter]], [[Hunter]], [[Scout]]

Once per game session, the character may reroll any one [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] or [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]] check.
