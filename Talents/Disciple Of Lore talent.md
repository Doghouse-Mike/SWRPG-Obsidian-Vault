## Disciple of Lore

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Force Adherent]] (2)

Once per round, the character may suffer one strain to decrease the difficulty of \[their\] next [[Lore (Int)|Knowledge (Lore)]] check a number of times equal to ranks in Disciple of Lore, to a minimum of **Simple (—)**.
