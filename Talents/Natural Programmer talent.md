## Natural Programmer

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Analyst]], [[Slicer]]

Once per game session, the character may reroll any one [[Computers (Int)|Computers]] or [[Astrogation (Int)|Astrogation]] check.
