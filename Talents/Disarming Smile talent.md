## Disarming Smile

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Charmer]] (2)

The character can take the Disarming Smile action to make an opposed [[Charm (Pr)|Charm]] check against one target within short range. If \[they\] succeed, decrease the target's defense (melee and ranged) by a number equal to the character's ranks in Disarming Smile (to a minimum of 0) until the end of the encounter.

[^1]: [[Category - Smuggler|Fly Casual]]: *A Sourcebook for Smugglers* (Page 32)
