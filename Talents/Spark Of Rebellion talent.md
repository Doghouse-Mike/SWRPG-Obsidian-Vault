## Spark of Rebellion

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Agitator (reSpec)]]

Once per session, the character may take the Spark of Rebellion action to make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Coercion (Will)|Coercion check]]**. If successful, cause a number of beings equal to ranks of [[Coercion (Will)|Coercion]] plus Willpower to rebel against an organization or authority with power over them until the end of the encounter, and may spend two [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage results]] to guide their general goal. This could be due to something the character did or said, or just because the beings were already unhappy with their position.

### Improved

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Agitator (reSpec)]]

Double the number of characters affected by Spark of Rebellion. Spend four [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage results]] or a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph result]] to affect all rebel-sympathetic characters instead. The GM may determine that the rebelling characters can occupy or overwhelm local authority.
