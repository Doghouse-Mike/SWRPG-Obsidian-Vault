## Know Your Prey

Source: [^0-1][^2]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Big Game Hunter (reSpec)]], [[Survivalist (reSpec)]]

Once per encounter, make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Xenology (Int)|Knowledge (Xenology) check]]** to add two [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice to all checks that target or are opposed by the character for the remainder of this encounter. Spend an [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result to extend that benefit to one ally per [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result spent, and a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result to upgrade the difficulty of those checks once.
