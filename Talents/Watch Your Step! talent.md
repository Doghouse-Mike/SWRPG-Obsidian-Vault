## Watch Your Step!

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Archaeologist (reSpec)]]

Once per session, make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Athletics (Br)|Athletics check]]** to have all other characters within short range suffer 1 wound plus 1 wound per each [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] result. Spend an [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result to exclude an ally from this effect per each [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result. Spend three [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result to Immobilize one affected enemy for one round.
