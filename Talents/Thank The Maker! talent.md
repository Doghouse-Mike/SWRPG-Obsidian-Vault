## Thank The Maker!

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Droid Tech (reSpec)]]

Once per session per Droid Companion, when a Droid Companion would be incapacitated or destroyed, spend a Destiny Point. It is not defeated until the end of the character's next turn. If the droid is mechanically stabilized before the effect ends, it is not incapacitated or destroyed.

[^0-1]: [[Category - ReSpecialized|The reSpecialized Project]]'s Droid Tech Folio
