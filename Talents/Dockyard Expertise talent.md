## Dockyard Expertise

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Shipwright]] (2)

The character may make an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Education (Int)|Knowledge (Education) check]]** when at a dry dock with suitable personnel and equipment to conduct repairs to a starship, or to add attachments or modifications to one. If successful, the cost and time for repairs is reduced by 20% for each rank of Dockyard Expertise to a minimum of 100 credits and one day.

[^1]: [[Category - Engineer|Fully Operational]]: *A Sourcebook for Engineers* (Page 30)
