## Hold Together

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Commodore]], [[Mechanic]]

The character may spend on Destiny Point to make a Hold Together incidental immediately after a vehicle or starship \[they\] have repaired this encounter takes damage resulting in hull trauma. The character explains why the apparently serious damage was in fact superficial, and the damage from the attack becomes system strain instead.
