## Secrets of the Jedi

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Padawan Survivor]]

[[Lightsaber (Br)|Lightsaber]] becomes a career skill. The character may also decrease the difficulty of checks made to construct or repair lightsaber hilts a number of times equal to half \[their\] ranks in [[Lore (Int)|Knowledge (Lore)]] (rounded up), to a minimum of **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Easy]]**. This does not include checks made to install mods.

### Improved

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Padawan Survivor]]

[[Discipline (Will)|Discipline]] becomes a career skill. Secrets of the Jedi can also be used when installing mods to lightsabers.
