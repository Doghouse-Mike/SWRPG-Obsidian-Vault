## Droid Commander

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Separatist Commander]]

Once per encounter, the character may perform Field Commander as a maneuver instead of an action. All affected allies must be droids.

[^1]: [[Category - Clone Wars End|Collapse of the Republic]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 38)
