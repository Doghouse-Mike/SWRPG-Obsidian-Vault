## Respected Delegate

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Republic Representative]] (2), [[Senator]] (1)

The character downgrades the difficulty of social skill checks made to interact with government institutions outside of their homeworld a number of times equal to their ranks in Respected Delegate.

[^2]: [[Category - Clone Wars Begin|Rise of the Separatists]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 42)
