## For A Good Cause

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Quartermaster (reSpec)]]

The legality of an item does not affect the cost when buying or selling restricted items, and the character may use Negotiation to buy and sell illegal goods. The details of the transaction are subject to the GM's discretion, as always, and a buyer might not be immediately (or easily) available.
