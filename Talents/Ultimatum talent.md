## Ultimatum

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Skip Tracer (reSpec)]]

Once per encounter, make an opposed [[Negotiation (Pr)|Negotiations]] vs. [[Cool (Pr)|Cool check]] to add a [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] result to each check made to attack or subdue the target this encounter. Spend three [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result to affect an additional target.
