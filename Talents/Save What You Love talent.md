## Save What You Love

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[First Order Defector]]

Once per session, when an allied character within Short Range would suffer a hit that would incapacitate them, the character may prevent that hit. The character then immediately suffers 5 wounds and a critical injury with +20 added to the result.

[^0-1]: [[Category - Sequels|Fires of Resistance]]: *An Unofficial Era Sourcebook* (Page 12)
