## Fire Support

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Clone Pilot]] (2)

After the character makes a successful combat check using a vehicle weapon, the next ally to make a check adds [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] dice equal to the character's ranks of Fire Support.

[^1]: [[Category - Clone Wars Begin|Rise of the Separatists]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 39)
