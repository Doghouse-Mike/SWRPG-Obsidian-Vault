## Savage Response

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Marauder (reSpec)]]

When the character suffers a critical injury if they aren't incapacitated, suffer 3 strain to inflict a hit with an equipped [[Melee (Br)|Melee]] or [[Brawl (Br)|Brawl]] weapon on an engaged foe. This hit deals the weapon's base damage plus any damage from applicable talents or abilities.
