## Inspiring Rhetoric

Source: [^0-1][^1-2][^3][^2-4][^3-5]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Ambassador]], [[Charmer]], [[Figurehead]], [[Politico]], [[Prophet]], [[Senator]], [[Ship Captain]]

The character may take the Inspiring Rhetoric action; making an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Leadership (Pr)|Leadership check]]**. For each [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] \[result\], one ally within close range recovers one strain. For each [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] \[result\], one ally benefiting from Inspiring Rhetoric recovers one additional strain.

### Improved

Source: [^0-1][^1-2][^2-4][^3-5]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Ambassador]], [[Charmer]], [[Figurehead]], [[Politico]], [[Prophet]], [[Senator]]

Each ally affected by Inspiring Rhetoric gains a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] dice on all skill checks for a number of rounds equal to the character's ranks in [[Leadership (Pr)|Leadership]]; this does not stack with itself.

### Supreme

Source: [^2-4][^3-5]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Ambassador]], [[Politico]]

The character may voluntarily suffer 1 strain to perform Inspiring Rhetoric as a maneuver instead of an action.
