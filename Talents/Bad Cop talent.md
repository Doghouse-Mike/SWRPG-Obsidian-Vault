## Bad Cop

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Interrogator]] (2), [[Marshal]] (2), [[Warden]] (2)

The character may spend two [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results from a [[Deception (Cun)|Deception]] or [[Coercion (Will)|Coercion]] check to upgrade the ability of a single ally's subsequent social interaction skill check against the same target in the same encounter. Upgrade the ability a number of times equal to the character's ranks in Bad Cop. A single check may only benefit from one use of Bad Cop.
