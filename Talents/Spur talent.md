## Spur

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Beast Rider]]

The character may push an animal past its normal comfort level. When riding a beast, \[they\] may perform the spur action, attempting a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Survival (Cun)|Survival check]]**. With success, the beast's top speed increases by 1. The character can choose to end the effects of this at the start of any of \[their\] turns. However, if \[they\] choose to maintain the effects for that turn, the beast suffers 2 strain. The beast still cannot perform actions or maneuvers it could not perform normally (e.g., actions that have a minimum speed requirement).

### Improved

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Maneuver)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Beast Rider]]

The character may voluntarily suffer 1 strain to attempt Spur as a maneuver. In addition, the difficulty of Spur is reduced to **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average]]**.

### Supreme

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Beast Rider]]

The beast the character is riding suffers 1 strain to maintain Spur's effects instead of the normal 2.
