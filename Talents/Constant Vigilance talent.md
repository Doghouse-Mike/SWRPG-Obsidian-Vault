Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Padawan Survivor]], [[Sentry]]

The character may always choose to use the [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]] skill when making checks to determine Initiative.
