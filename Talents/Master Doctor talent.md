## Master Doctor

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Doctor]], [[Medic]]

Once per round, the character may suffer two strain to decrease the difficulty of \[their\] next [[Medicine (Int)|Medicine]] check by one to a minimum of **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Easy difficulty]]**.
