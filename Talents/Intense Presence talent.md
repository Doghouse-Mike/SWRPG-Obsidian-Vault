## Intense Presence

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Ambassador]], [[Charmer]], [[Figurehead]], [[Hotshot]], [[Makashi Duelist]], [[Performer]], [[Politico]], [[Republic Representative]]

The character may spend one Destiny Point to recover strain equal to their Presence rating.
