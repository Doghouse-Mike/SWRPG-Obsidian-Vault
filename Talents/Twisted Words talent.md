## Twisted Words

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Advocate]], [[Prophet]]

When the character is targeted by a social check, the character may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result or two [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results and suffer 1 strain as an incidental to inflict a number of strain equal to their ranks in [[Coercion (Will)|Coercion]] on the character who made the check.

[^2]: [[Category - Diplomat|Desperate Allies]]: *A Sourcebook for Diplomats* (Page 34)
