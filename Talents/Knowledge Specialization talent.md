## Knowledge Specialization

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Species:** [[Chagrian]]
- **Trees:** [[Analyst]] (4), [[Archaeologist]] (2), [[Sage]] (3), [[Scholar]] (2), [[Scientist]] (2), [[Strategist]] (2)

When the character first acquires this talent, \[they\] may choose one [[Category - Knowledge Skills|Knowledge skill]]. When making checks with that skill, \[they\] may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result to gain additional [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] results equal to \[their\] ranks in Knowledge Specialization.
