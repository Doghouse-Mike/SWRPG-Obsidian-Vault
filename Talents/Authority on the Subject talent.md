## Authority on the Subject

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Scholar (reSpec)]]

Once per encounter, when a foe would make a check, spend 1 Destiny Point to establish a relevant fact. Treat the check as opposed by the character's most appropriate [[Category - Knowledge Skills|Knowledge Skill]], replacing the original difficulty.
