## Natural Camouflage

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Survivalist (reSpec)]]

When making a [[Stealth (Ag)|stealth]] check in natural environments, may suffer 2 strain to substitute [[Survival (Cun)|Survival]] for the required skill. Natural environments should not be limited to dense forests or jungles, but any environment out in the wild.
