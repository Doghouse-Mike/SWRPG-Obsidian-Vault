## Parry

Source: [^1][^2][^0-3][^4][^5][^1-6]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Arbiter]] (1), [[Ataru Striker]] (3), [[Force-Sensitive Outcast]] (2), [[General]] (1), [[Juyo Berserker]] (3), [[Knight]] (2), [[Makashi Duelist]] (5), [[Martial Artist]] (2), [[Niman Disciple]] (3) [[Padawan]] (2), [[Padawan Survivor]] (2), [[Protector]] (1), [[Shien Expert]] (2), [[Shii-Cho Knight]] (4), [[Soresu Defender]] (4), [[Steel Hand Adept]] (1)
- **Items:** [[Etaan Crystal]], [[Sorian Crystal]] (Lightsaber Mod)

When the character suffers a hit from a [[Brawl (Br)|Brawl]], [[Melee (Br)|Melee]], or [[Lightsaber (Br)|Lightsaber]] combat check, after damage is calculated (but before soak is applied, so immediately after step 3 of **Perform a Combat Check**) the character may take a Parry incidental. They suffer 3 strain and reduces the damage dealt by that hit by a number equal to 2 plus their ranks in Parry. This talent may only be used once per hit and when the character is wielding a Lightsaber or Melee weapon.

### Improved

Source: [^0-3][^1-6]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Ataru Striker]], [[Knight]], [[Makashi Duelist]], [[Shii-Cho Knight]], [[Soresu Defender]]

When the character suffers a hit from a [[Brawl (Br)|Brawl]], [[Melee (Br)|Melee]], or [[Lightsaber (Br)|Lightsaber]] combat check and uses the Parry incidental to reduce the damage from that hit, after the attack is resolved, the character may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] \[result\] or three [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] \[results\] to automatically hit the attacker once with a wielded Brawl, Melee, or Lightsaber weapon. This hit deals the weapon's base damage plus any damage from applicable talents or abilities. This talent may not be used if the original attack incapacitates the character.

### Supreme

Source: [^1-6]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Soresu Defender]]

*Force talent.* If the user did not make a combat check during \[their\] previous turn, \[they\] suffer 1 strain when taking the Parry incidental, instead of 3.
