## Animal Empathy

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Pathfinder]]

*Force talent.* When making checks to handle, tame, or control animals, the character may add a number of [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] dice no greater than \[their\] Force rating to the check. The character may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]] to add a [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] or [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] (character's choice) to the result.

[^1]: [[Category - Way of the Force|***Star Wars*** **Force and Destiny**]] Roleplaying Game: *Core Rulebook* (Page 139)
