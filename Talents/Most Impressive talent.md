## Most Impressive

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Ship Captain]]

The character may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result from any skill check to allow a number of allies not exceeding \[their\] rating in Presence within short range to add an automatic [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result to their next skill check.

[^1]: [[Category - Early Rebellion|Dawn of Rebellion]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 114)
