## Cunning Snare

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Trailblazer]]

Once per encounter, the character may take the Cunning Snare action to use materials in \[their\] environment to assemble a trap within short range. Each time another character moves to engaged range of the trap, \[they\] must make an **Opposed [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]] vs. [[Survival (Cun)|Survival]] check** against the character who placed the trap. If the character who triggers the trap fails, \[they\] suffer wounds (these ignore soak) equal to the trapper's Cunning characteristic, plus 1 additional wound per [[Narrative Dice#Failure|failure]] result. The trapper may spend [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results generated to disorient the character for 1 round per [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] result spent, three [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result to immobilize the character for 1 round, and a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result to stagger the character for 1 round.

Once a character springs the trap, it is expended; at the GM's discretion, the trapper may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result from the check to have it remain functional for one extra use.

The trapper may disarm the trap with an action. Any other character who is aware of the trap may spend several minutes to attempt to disarm it with a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Survival (Cun)|Survival check]]**. If \[they\] fail, \[they\] suffer the damage and negative effects in the same manner as if \[they\] had triggered it.

[^1]: [[Category - Soldier|Forged in Battle]]: *A Sourcebook for Soldiers* (Page 34-35)
