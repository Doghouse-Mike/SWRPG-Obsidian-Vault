## Center of Attention

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Performer (reSpec)]]

Once per round while performing before a gathering or audience,suffer 2 strain to become the center of attention during their next check. If the check succeeds, the character adds a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result to the results. However, if the check fails, they add a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result instead. The [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] or [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] results added this way do not include the [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] or [[Narrative Dice#Failure|failure]] results that are usually part of them.
