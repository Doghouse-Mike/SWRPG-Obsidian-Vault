## Sense Danger

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Force-Sensitive Emergent]], [[Force Sensitive Exile]], [[Padawan]], [[Seer]], [[Teacher]], [[Warleader]]

*Force talent.* Once per game session, the character may remove two [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice from any one skill check.
