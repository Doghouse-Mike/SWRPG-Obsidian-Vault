## Opportunist

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Scoundrel (reSpec)]] (2)

The character adds a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die per rank of Opportunist to combat checks made against enemies who have attacked an ally this round, or are otherwise distracted, per GM discretion.
