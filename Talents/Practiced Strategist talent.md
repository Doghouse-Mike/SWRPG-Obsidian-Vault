## Practiced Strategist

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Clone Commander]]

Once per session during a mass combat or combat encounter, the character may take the Practiced Strategist action, making an **opposed [[Warfare (Int)|Knowledge (Warfare)]] versus [[Warfare (Int)|Knowledge (Warfare) check]]** targeting the leader of the enemy forces. If the check succeeds, the character (or player) reveals a narrative advantage provided by their strategic planning, such as the timely arrival of reinforcements, sabotage of the enemy's equipment, or a spy planted within the enemy ranks. The exact nature of the revelation is ultimately at the GM's discretion, but it should provide the character and their allies with a significant advantage in the encounter or session.

[^1]: [[Category - Clone Wars End|Collapse of the Republic]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 42)
