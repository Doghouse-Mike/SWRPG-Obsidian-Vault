## Touch of Fate

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Force-Sensitive Emergent]], [[Force Sensitive Exile]], [[Starfighter Ace]]

*Force talent.* Once per game session, the character may add two [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] dice to any one skill check.
