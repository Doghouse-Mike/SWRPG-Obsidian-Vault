## Surgeon

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Cyber Tech]] (2), [[Doctor]] (3), [[Healer]] (2), [[Interrogator]] (2), [[Medic]] (2)

When this character makes a [[Medicine (Int)|Medicine]] check to help a character heal wounds, the target heals one additional wound per rank of Surgeon.

```
The following is a homebrew expansion of this talent from a community-produced homebrew sourcebook!
```

### Improved

Source: [^4]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Doctor (reSpec)]]

When performing a [[Medicine (Int)|Medicine]] check, suffer strain up to their ranks of Surgeon, then choose either to remove that many [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice or add that many [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] dice to the check.
