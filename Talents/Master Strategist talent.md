## Master Strategist

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Strategist]]

Once per phase during a mass combat, the character may suffer 2 strain to decrease the difficulty of the next Mass Combat check once.
