## Overwhelm Defenses

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Gunner]] (2), [[Operator]] (2)

Upon making an unsuccessful attack with a starship or vehicle weapon, the character may spend two [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results per rank of Overwhelm Defenses. Reduce the defense rating in the defense zone targeted by the attack of the remainder of the encounter by 1 for every two [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results spent.

[^2]: [[Category - Rebel Ranks|***Star Wars*** **Age of Rebellion**]] Roleplaying Game: *Core Rulebook* (Page 154)
