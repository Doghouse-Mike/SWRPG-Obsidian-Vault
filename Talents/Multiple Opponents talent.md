## Multiple Opponents

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Clone Veteran]], [[Juyo Berserker]], [[Shii-Cho Knight]]

The character adds a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] dice to their [[Brawl (Br)|Brawl]], [[Melee (Br)|Melee]], and [[Lightsaber (Br)|Lightsaber]] combat checks when engaged with multiple opponents. This includes single groups of multiple minions.
