## Double-Cross

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Scoundrel (reSpec)]]

Once per session, make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Deception (Cun)|Deception check]]**. On success, the character (or player) reveals how their previously unknown scheming has a meaningful impact on the current scene or encounter. The nature of the revelation and its impact on the scene are ultimately subject to the GM's discretion, but should inflict significant danger or disadvantage to an adversarial character. If the check fails and generates a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result, however, the GM may decide that the character is actually the one being double-crossed.
