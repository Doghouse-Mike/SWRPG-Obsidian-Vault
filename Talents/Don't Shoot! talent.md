## Don't Shoot!

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Charmer]]

Once per session, the character can take the Don't Shoot! action to make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Charm (Pr)|Charm check]]**. If \[they\] succeed, \[they\] cannot be the target of a combat check until the end of the encounter or until \[they\] make a combat check. This player must explain how \[they\] talk \[their\] way out of being associated with \[their\] party's combatants, and the GM has final say on whether \[their\] ruse succeeds.

[^1]: [[Category - Smuggler|Fly Casual]]: *A Sourcebook for Smugglers* (Page 32)
