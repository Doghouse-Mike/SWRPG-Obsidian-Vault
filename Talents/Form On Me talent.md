## Form on Me

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Squadron Leader]]

When the character performs the Gain the Advantage action, a number of allied vehicles equal to \[their\] ranks in [[Leadership (Pr)|Leadership]] and within close range also gain the benefits of the action on the target. These allied vehicles maintain the benefits until they leave close range with the character or the character loses the benefits of Gain the Advantage.

[^1]: [[Category - Rebel Ranks|***Star Wars*** **Age of Rebellion**]] Roleplaying Game: *Core Rulebook* (Page 147)
