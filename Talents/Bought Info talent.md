## Bought Info

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Entrepreneur]], [[Quartermaster]], [[Shipwright]], [[Skip Tracer]]

When required to make a [[Category - Knowledge Skills|Knowledge skill]] check, the character can instead make a Bought Info action. \[They\] spend a number of credits equal to 50 times the difficulty of the check and counts as succeeding on the check with one uncancelled [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]]. At the GM's discretion, the character may not be able to use this ability if the information sought is particularly hard to find, or if the character is in a situation where \[they\] could not purchase information (such as marooned on a planet with no access to the HoloNet).

[^2]: [[Category - Colonist|Far Horizons]]: *A Sourcebook for Colonists* (Page 32)
