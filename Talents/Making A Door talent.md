## Making A Door

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Demolitionist (reSpec)]]

Once per encounter, make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Skulduggery (Cun)|Skulduggery check]]** to treat an explosive as if it had the [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] 1 quality when breaching structures or vehicles this encounter. Spend three [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result to treat it as if it had [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] 2 instead.
