## Combat Veteran

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Clone Veteran]] (2), [[Retired Clone Trooper]] (2)

The character adds a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] dice to their [[Brawl (Br)|Brawl]] and [[Discipline (Will)|Discipline]] checks equal to their ranks in Combat Veteran.
