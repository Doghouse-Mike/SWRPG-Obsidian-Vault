## Intuitive Evasion

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Maneuver)
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Racer]] (1), [[Starfighter Ace]] (2)

*Force talent.* When piloting a vehicle of silhouette 5 or smaller, the character may take the Intuitive Evasion maneuver, suffering 1 strain and committing a number of [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] dice no greater than \[their\] Force rating or \[their\] ranks of Intuitive Evasion. \[They\] then upgrade the difficulty of combat checks targeting \[their\] vehicle by an equal amount. The character suffers 1 strain at the beginning of each of \[their\] turns in which these dice remain committed.

[^1]: [[Category - Way of the Force|***Star Wars*** **Force and Destiny**]] Roleplaying Game: *Core Rulebook* (Page 145)
