## Now You See Me

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Shadow]]

*Force talent.* Once per session the character may take the Now You See Me action, making a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Deception (Cun)|Deception]]** [[Deception (Cun)|**check**]]. If successful, a number of NPCs equal to \[their\] Cunning within medium range forget any interactions they had with the character during the last thirty minutes.

At the GM's discretion, multiple [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] or [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] results may give the NPCs in question unpleasant side effects, such as confusion, nightmares, or a persistent and maddening suspicion that they've forgotten something important.

[^1]: [[Category - Way of the Force|***Star Wars*** **Force and Destiny**]] Roleplaying Game: *Core Rulebook* (Page 148-149)
