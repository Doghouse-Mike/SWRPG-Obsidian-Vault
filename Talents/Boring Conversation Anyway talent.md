## Boring Conversation Anyway

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Scoundrel (reSpec)]]

Once per round, spend three [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result from a social check to immediately make an attack against the target of that social check as an incidental.
