## Shortcut

Source: [^0-1][^1-2][^3][^4]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Courier]] (2), [[Hotshot]] (2), [[Navigator]] (2), [[Operator]] (2), [[Racer]] (3), [[Scout]] (2), [[Thief]] (1)
- **Items:** [[Overcharged Ion Engines]] (Modification Option)

During a chase, the character adds a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die per rank of Shortcut to \[their\] checks made to catch or escape an opponent.

#### Improved

Source: [^5][^0-1][^1-2]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Navigator]], [[Operator]], [[Racer]]

When engaging in a chase or race, the character may suffer two strain to add [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] results equal to ranks in Shortcut to the check.

```
While Savage Spirits: A Sourcebook for Seekers shows the Improved talent as "Activation: Passive", the Errata (Page 3) corrects this to "Activation: Active (Incidental)".
```
