## Martial Grace

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Martial Artist]], [[Steel Hand Adept]]

Once per round, the character may suffer 2 strain to add additional damage equal to ranks in [[Coordination (Ag)|Coordination]] to one hit of a successful [[Brawl (Br)|Brawl]] combat check.

[^1]: [[Category - Warrior|Knights of Fate]]: *A Sourcebook for Warriors* (Page 31)
[^2]: [[Category - Bounty Hunter|No Disintegrations]]: *A Sourcebook for Bounty Hunters* (Page 35)
