## Overkill

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Heavy (reSpec)]]

Once per attack when the character causes a foe to exceed their wound threshold, the character may suffer 2 strain to assign a hit dealing damage equal to the incapacitating hit's damage minus the original target's soak to another foe within short range of the first. If this is a melee attack, the second target must be engaged rather than short range.
