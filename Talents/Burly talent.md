## Burly

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Heavy]] (3)

The character reduces any wielded or carried weapon's [[Item Qualities#Cumbersome (Passive)|Cumbersome]] quality and Encumbrance rating by a number equal to ranks in Burly to a minimum of 1.
