## Reinforce Item

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Maneuver)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Armorer]]

*Force talent.* The character may take the Reinforce Item maneuver, committing two [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] dice. \[They\] then grant one weapon or piece of armor \[they\] are engaged with the [[Item Qualities#Cortosis (Passive)|Cortosis]] quality while two [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] dice remain committed. The character suffers 3 strain at the beginning of each of \[their\] turns in which \[they\] keep these dice committed.

[^1]: [[Category - Guardian|Keeping the Peace]]: *A Sourcebook for Guardians* (Page 31)
