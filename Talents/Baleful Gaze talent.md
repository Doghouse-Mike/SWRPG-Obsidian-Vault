## Baleful Gaze

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Warden]]

*Force talent.* When targeted by a combat check made by an enemy within medium range, the character may spend a Destiny Point to upgrade the difficulty of the check a number of times equal to \[their\] ranks of [[Coercion (Will)|Coercion]]. A character who has purchased this talent automatically gains 1 Conflict at the beginning of each session.

[^1]: [[Category - Guardian|Keeping the Peace]]: *A Sourcebook for Guardians* (Page 30)
