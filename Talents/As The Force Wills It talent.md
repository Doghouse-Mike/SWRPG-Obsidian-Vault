## As the Force Wills It

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Force Adherent]]

The character may make an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Easy difficulty]] [[Lore (Int)|Knowledge (Lore) check]]** (instead of [[Discipline (Will)|Discipline]] or [[Cool (Pr)|Cool]]) to heal strain at the end of an encounter. A [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result may be spent to heal all strain.

[^1]: [[Category - Early Rebellion|Dawn of Rebellion]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 111)
