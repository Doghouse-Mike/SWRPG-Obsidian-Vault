## Always Prepared

Source: [^0-1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Gadgeteer (reSpec)]], [[Mechanic (reSpec)]], [[Scout (reSpec)]]

Once per session, as an incidental, produce a previously undocumented piece of personal equipment or weapon, with a rarity no greater than 4, as if a Destiny Point was spent. If producing a weapon, it also has the [[Item Qualities#Limited Ammo (Passive)|Limited Ammo]] 1 quality.
