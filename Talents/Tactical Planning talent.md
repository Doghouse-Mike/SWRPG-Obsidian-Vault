## Tactical Planning

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Clone Commander]]

Once per game session when performing an activity that requires a skill check, the character may choose to substitute a [[Warfare (Int)|Knowledge (Warfare)]] check for the skill check that would normally apply. When they do this, they must explain how their tactical foresight is helping them overcome the challenge.

[^1]: [[Category - Clone Wars End|Collapse of the Republic]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 44)
