## Vaapad Control

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Juyo Berserker]]

*Force talent.* When making a [[Lightsaber (Br)|Lightsaber]] combat check, the character may suffer 1 strain to downgrade the difficulty of the check a number of times equal to the number of light side Destiny Points in the Destiny pool.

[^1]: [[Category - Warrior|Knights of Fate]]: *A Sourcebook for Warriors* (Page 31)
