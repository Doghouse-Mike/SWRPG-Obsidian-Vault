## Special Modifications

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Modder (reSpec)]]

Once per session, the character may make an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics check]]** to temporarily convert an attachment currently installed on equipment or a vehicle into another valid attachment with equal or lesser hard points and rarity. They may make this permanent by spending 6 hours and spending credits equal to the new attachment's cost minus the cost of the original attachment.
