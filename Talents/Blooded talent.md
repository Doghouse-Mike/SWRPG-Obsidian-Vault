## Blooded

Source: [^1][^0-2][^3][^4]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Alchemist]] (2), [[Commando]] (1), [[Survivalist]] (1)
- **Items:** [[Cybernetic Respirator]]

The character adds a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] dice per rank of Blooded to their checks to resist or recover from the effects of poisons, venoms, and other toxins. Reduce the duration of any related ongoing effects or damage from toxins, venoms, or poisons by one round per rank of Blooded, to a minimum of one.

### Improved

Source: [^0-2]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Alchemist]]

The character may take the Improved Blooded action, making a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Resilience (Br)|Resilience check]]**. If they succeed, they immediately recover from the effects of all poisons and the side effects or after effects of all drugs. Reduce the difficulty of the check once per rank of Blooded, to a minimum of **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Simple]]**.
