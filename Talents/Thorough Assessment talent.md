## Thorough Assessment

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Analyst]], [[Strategist]]

Once per session, the character may make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Category - Knowledge Skills|Knowledge check]]** (the GM and player should determine which [[Category - Knowledge Skills|Knowledge skill]] is most applicable in the given situation) to perform the Thorough Assessment action. If \[they\] succeed, the character may add a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to one check that another character who could reasonably benefit from this information makes before the end of the encounter. For every success beyond the first, \[they\] may add a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to one additional check this way. \[They\] cannot allocate more than one [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to any single check this way. At the end of the encounter, any unused [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] dice are lost.

### Improved

Source: [^3]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Analyst (reSpec)]]

When the character would distribute a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost die]] from Thorough Assessment, they may choose to distribute a [[Narrative Dice#Success|success result]] instead. This still counts against the total number of [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost dice]] for distribution from Thorough Assessment.
