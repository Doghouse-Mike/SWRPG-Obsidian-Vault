## Odd-Job

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Fringer (reSpec)]]

Once per session, after successfully negotiating to purchase goods or services, the character may offer to perform a task or favor in lieu of payment. If this task is not performed before the end of the session, they take Obligation equal to the original difficulty of the check to purchase, or based on credit cost, or other narrative factors, per Game Master discretion.
