## Nobody's Fool

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Species:** [[Hutt]]
- **Trees:** [[Advisor]] (2), [[Advocate]] (2), [[Ambassador]] (1), [[Agitator]] (2), [[Arbiter]] (2), [[Interrogator]] (2), [[Master]] (1), [[Niman Disciple]] (1), [[Republic Representative]] (2), [[Politico]] (1), [[Senator]] (1), [[Skip Tracer]] (1), [[Teacher]] (1), [[Trader]] (2)

The difficulty of any [[Charm (Pr)|Charm]], [[Coercion (Will)|Coercion]], and [[Deception (Cun)|Deception]] checks attempted against the character is upgraded once for each rank of Nobody's Fool.

### Improved

Source: [^4]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Master]], [[Senator]]

When targeted by a [[Charm (Pr)|Charm]], [[Coercion (Will)|Coercion]], or [[Deception (Cun)|Deception]] check, the character may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result or three [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results from the check to inflict strain on the opponent equal to the character's ranks in Nobody's Fool.
