## Pulling Strings

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Politico (reSpec)]]

Once per session, the character may make an **opposed social check** to compel the target to perform a minor favor this session. Add a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result to one check associated with this favor. The favor must be within the power of the target, and it must not conflict with the target's core principles or be obviously suicidal.

[^1]: [[Category - ReSpecialized|The reSpecialized Project]]'s Politico Folio
