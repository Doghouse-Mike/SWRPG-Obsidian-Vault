## Far Strike

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Steel Hand Adept]]

*Force talent.* The character may take the Far Strike action, making a [[Brawl (Br)|Brawl]] combat check as a ranged attack at one target at short range or further, adding [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] dice no greater than the character's Force rating to the check. The character may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]] to increase the range of the attack by one range band per [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]] spent, to a maximum of long. If the target is at medium range or further, the character must both sufficiently increase the range of the attack and succeed on the check to hit the target.

[^1]: [[Category - Warrior|Knights of Fate]]: *A Sourcebook for Warriors* (Page 30)
