## Balance

Source: [^1][^2][^0-3][^1-4]

- **Activation:** Active (Maneuver)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Ataru Striker]], [[Force-Sensitive Emergent]], [[Force Sensitive Exile]], [[Juyo Berserker]], [[Knight]], [[Nightsister]], [[Padawan Survivor]], [[Sage]]

*Force talent.* When the character recovers from strain at the end of each encounter, they may roll [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force dice]] no greater than their Force rating. They recover additional strain equal to the [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|points]] ([[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Light points]])[^0-3][^1-4] generated.
