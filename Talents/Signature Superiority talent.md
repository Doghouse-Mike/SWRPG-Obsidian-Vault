## Signature Superiority

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Modder (reSpec)]]

Once per encounter while aboard the signature Vehicle, make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics check]]**. On [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]], the character and a number of allies equal to the character's Intellect add a [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] result to all checks involving signature Vehicle until the end of the character's next turn.
