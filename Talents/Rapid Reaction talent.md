## Rapid Reaction

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Clone Veteran]] (2), [[Commodore]] (2), [[Demolitionist]] (1), [[Driver]] (1), [[Gunslinger]] (2), [[Scavenger]] (2), [[Scoundrel]] (2), [[Seer]] (2), [[Starfighter Ace]] (2), [[Thief]] (1), [[Vanguard]] (2)
- **Items:** [[Cybernetic Reflexes]]

The character may suffer a number of strain to add an equal number of [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] results to any [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]] or [[Cool (Pr)|Cool]] check to determine Initiative order. The number may not exceed \[their\] ranks in Rapid Reaction.
