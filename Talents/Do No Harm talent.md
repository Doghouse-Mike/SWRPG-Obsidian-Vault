## Do No Harm

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Doctor (reSpec)]]

Once per session, make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Cool (Pr)|Cool check]]**. If successful, they may not be targeted by combat checks until the end of the encounter or scene or until making a combat check. A Game Master may end this effect due to the character doing anything that would obviously injure, impede, or confound a foe.

[^1]: [[Category - ReSpecialized|The reSpecialized Project]]'s Doctor Folio
