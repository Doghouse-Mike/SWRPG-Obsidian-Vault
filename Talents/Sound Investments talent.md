## Sound Investments

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Entrepreneur]] (5), [[Quartermaster]] (2)

At the beginning of each game session, the character gains credits equal to \[their\] ranks in Sound Investments times 100. How this money is earned is up to the player and GM, and could be actual investments, business dealings, or even a small legal or illegal side business. The GM can also decide that the current events of the adventure may make these funds unavailable.

### Improved

Source: [^3]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Entrepreneur (reSpec)]]

At the beginning of each session, may invest all Sound Investment credits and make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Formidable difficulty]] [[Core Worlds (Int)|Knowledge (Core Worlds)]]** or **[[Underworld (Int)|Knowledge (Underworld) check]]**, decreased by ranks of Sound Investments, to gain a return on the investment. On [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]], gain 100 additional credits plus 10% of the initial investments for each [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] result past the first. On failure, lose half of the initial investment. A [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result may be spent to double the number of successes on a successful check, or recover 75% of initial investment on a failed check.
