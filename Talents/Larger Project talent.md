Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Modder]] (2), [[Rigger]] (2)

The character can choose a [[Signature Vehicle talent|Signature Vehicle]] with a silhouette value 1 higher than normal per rank of Larger Project.
