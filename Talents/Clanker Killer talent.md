## Clanker Killer

Source: [^0-1][^2]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[ARC Trooper]] (2), [[Clone Commander]] (1), [[Clone Officer]] (1), [[Clone Pilot]] (1), [[Clone Trooper]] (2), [[Clone Veteran]] (1)

Before rolling a combat check that targets a droid (including a droid vehicle or a vehicle piloted by a droid), the character may remove [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] dice up to their ranks in Clanker Killer from the pool, and add an equal number of [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] or [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results (in any combination) to the results.

### Improved

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Clone Veteran]]

When making a Brawl or Melee combat check targeting a droid, the character treats their attack's critical rating as being 1 lower per rank of Clanker Killer (to a minimum of 1).
