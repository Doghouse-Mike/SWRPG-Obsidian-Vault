## Natural Marksman

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Gunslinger]], [[Marshal]], [[Mercenary Soldier]], [[Tactician]], [[Sharpshooter]]
- **Items:** [['Accu-Strike' Integrated Targeting Computer|"Accu-Strike" Integrated Targeting Computer]] (Modification Option)

Once per game session, the character may reroll any one [[Ranged - Light (Ag)|Ranged (Light)]] or [[Ranged - Heavy (Ag)|Ranged (Heavy)]] check.
