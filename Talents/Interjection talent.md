## Interjection

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Advocate]]

After another character makes a social check, the character may suffer 3 strain and make an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance check]]**. The character may add a [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] result or [[Narrative Dice#Failure|failure]] result to the results of the chosen check equal to the [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] results on the [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]] check and an [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result or [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] result equal to [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results on the [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]] check.

[^1]: [[Category - Diplomat|Desperate Allies]]: *A Sourcebook for Diplomats* (Page 34)
