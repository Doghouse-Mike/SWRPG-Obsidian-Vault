## Resist Questioning

Source: [^1][^0-2]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Interrogator]], [[Senator]]

When targeted by an opposed social skill check, the character may suffer 2 strain to perform the Resist Questioning out-of-turn incidental, adding a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice to the check. If the check fails, they may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result to indicate they have provided false or misleading information that the target believes to be truthful.

### Improved

Source: [^0-2]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Interrogator]]

When one ally within short range is targeted by an opposed social skill check, the character may perform Resist Questioning to affect this check instead. If the check fails, \[they\] may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] to indicate the ally has provided false or misleading information that the target believes to be truthful.
