## Martyrdom

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Propagandist (reSpec)]]

Once per encounter, after being successfully hit by an attack, the character may suffer 3 strain to add a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair result]] to the check. The [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair result]] added this way do not include the [[Narrative Dice#Failure|failure result]] that are normally part of them, only the negative effects of the symbol.
