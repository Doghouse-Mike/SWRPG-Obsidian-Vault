## Renegade Form

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Force-Sensitive Outcast]]

*Force talent.* When the character purchases this talent, they choose one characteristic. They may use this characteristic in place of Brawn when making [[Lightsaber (Br)|Lightsaber]] checks.

[^1]: [[Category - Clone Wars Begin|Rise of the Separatists]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 42)
