## Combat Programming

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Droid Specialist]]

Once per encounter when working on a droid, the character may make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Computers (Int)|Computers check]]**. If successful, for the remainder of the encounter the droid gains two different [[Category - Combat Skills|combat skills]] at rank 1, or 1 additional rank in two different existing combat skills (to a maximum of 5), as chosen by the character. If the character is a droid, it may perform this action on itself.

[^1]: [[Category - Engineer|Fully Operational]]: *A Sourcebook for Engineers* (Page 30)
