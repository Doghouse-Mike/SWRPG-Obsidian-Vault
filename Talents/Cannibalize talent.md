## Cannibalize

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Outlaw Tech (reSpec)]]

Once per encounter, the character may scrap an existing piece of gear to add a [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] result to a check to create, improve or repair a similar piece of gear, mechanical object, starship, or vehicle.
