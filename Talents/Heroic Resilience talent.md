## Heroic Resilience

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Heavy]]

Immediately after being hit by a successful combat check but before damage is calculated, the character may spend one Destiny Point to increase \[their\] soak by a number equal to \[their\] ranks in [[Resilience (Br)|Resilience]].
