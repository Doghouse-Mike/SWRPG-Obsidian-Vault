## RDC Specialised Training

Source: [^0-1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[RDC Officer]], [[RDC Navigator]], [[RDC Peacekeeper]]

When the character first acquired this talent, \[they\] may choose one Career skill. When making checks with that skill, \[they\] may add a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die equal to \[their\] ranks in that skill.
