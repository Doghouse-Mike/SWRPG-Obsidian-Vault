## Master Starhopper

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Commodore]], [[Fringer]], [[Navigator]]

Once per round, the character may suffer two strain to decrease the difficulty of \[their\] next [[Astrogation (Int)|Astrogation]] check by one, to a minimum of **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Easy difficulty]]**.
