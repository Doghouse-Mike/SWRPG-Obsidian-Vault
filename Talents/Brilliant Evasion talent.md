## Brilliant Evasion

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Pilot]], [[Squadron Leader]]

The character may make the Brilliant Evasion action when piloting a vehicle or starship. The character selects one opponent and makes an **opposed** **[[Piloting - Planetary (Ag)|Piloting (Planetary]]** or **[[Piloting - Space (Ag)|Space) check]]**. If \[they\] succeed, the opponent's vehicle or starship cannot make any attacks against the character's vehicle for a number of rounds equal to the character's Agility. Brilliant Evasion can only be performed once per encounter.
