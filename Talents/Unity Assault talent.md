## Unity Assault

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Maneuver)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Peacekeeper]]

*Force talent.* Whenever the character misses an opponent with a combat check and generates a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result or three [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results, the character may spend this to perform a Force power action that targets one or more allies as a maneuver (\[they\] must still be able to perform maneuvers, and may not perform more than two maneuvers in a turn).

[^1]: [[Category - Way of the Force|***Star Wars*** **Force and Destiny**]] Roleplaying Game: *Core Rulebook* (Page 153)
