## Cruel Commander

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Separatist Commander]]

When upgrading the ability of a Mass Combat check in a mass combat in which the character is commander of the acting force (or when upgrading the difficulty, if the character is the commander of the enemy force), the character may use ranks in [[Coercion (Will)|Coercion]] instead of ranks in [[Leadership (Pr)|Leadership]].

[^1]: [[Category - Clone Wars End|Collapse of the Republic]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 38)
