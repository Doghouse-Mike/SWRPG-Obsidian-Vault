## Dynamic Personality

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Charmer (reSpec)]]

Once per encounter after making a successful social check, suffer strain up to the character's Presence to increase or decrease an affected character's defense by that number until the end of the next round. This cannot increase a character's defenses above 4.
