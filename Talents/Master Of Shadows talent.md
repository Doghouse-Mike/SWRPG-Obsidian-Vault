## Master of Shadows

Source: [^1][^2][^3][^4]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Assassin]], [[Infiltrator]], [[Padawan Survivor]], [[Shadow]], [[Thief]]

Once per round, the character may voluntarily suffer 2 strain to decrease the difficulty of the next [[Stealth (Ag)|Stealth]] or [[Skulduggery (Cun)|Skulduggery]] check by one, to a minimum of **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Easy difficulty]]**.
