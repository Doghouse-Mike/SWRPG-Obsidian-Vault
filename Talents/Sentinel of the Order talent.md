## Sentinel of the Order

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Temple Guardian]]

*Force talent.* As a maneuver, may suffer 3 strain to give the currently equipped weapon the following item qualities until the end of the next round: [[Item Qualities#Defensive (Passive)|Defensive]] 1, [[Item Qualities#Deflection (Passive)|Deflection]] 1. Weapons that already have these qualities increase their existing ratings by 1.

[^1]: [[Category - Clone Wars Heroes & Villains|Heroes on Both Sides]]: *The Unofficial Collection of Clone Wars Heroes & Villains* (Page 273)
