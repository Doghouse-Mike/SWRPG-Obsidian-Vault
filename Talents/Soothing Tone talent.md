## Soothing Tone

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Beast Rider]], [[Hermit]]

Once per encounter when riding a beast, the character may perform a Soothing Tone action, attempting an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Xenology (Int)|Knowledge (Xenology) check]]**. If successful, the beast the character is riding recovers strain equal to [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] results on the check (or heals that number of wounds if it does not have a strain threshold).

[^2]: [[Category - Ace|Stay on Target]]: *A Sourcebook for Aces* (Page 33)
