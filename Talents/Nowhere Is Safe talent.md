## Nowhere Is Safe

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Survivalist (reSpec)]]

Spend two [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result from a non-vehicle or starship attack to reduce the target's defense by the character's ranks in [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] until the end of the character's next turn. This may reduce defense from innate defense, talent, armor, and/or cover.

### Improved

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Survivalist (reSpec)]]

After the character makes a successful attack against a defenseless target, suffer 3 strain to add a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result to the check. A character is considered defenseless if they have no melee defense if the attack is melee, no ranged defense if the attack is ranged, and aren't benefiting from defense gained from cover or the Guarded Stance maneuver.
