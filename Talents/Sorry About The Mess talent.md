## Sorry About the Mess

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Force-Sensitive Outcast]], [[Gunslinger]]

The character decreases the critical rating of any non-starship weapon they wield by 1 (to a minimum of 1) against targets that have not yet acted this encounter.

[^1]: [[Category - Clone Wars Begin|Rise of the Separatists]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 43)
[^2]: [[Category - Smuggler|Fly Casual]]: *A Sourcebook for Smugglers* (Page 33)
