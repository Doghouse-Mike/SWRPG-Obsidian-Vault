## Respond In Kind

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Ambassador (reSpec)]]

The character may spend three [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat results]] or a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair result]] from an incoming social check targeting the character or an ally to add a [[Narrative Dice#Success|success result]] and an [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage result]] to the next [[Charm (Pr)|Charm]], [[Negotiation (Pr)|Negotiation]], or [[Leadership (Pr)|Leadership]] check the character or an ally makes targeting that adversary this encounter.
