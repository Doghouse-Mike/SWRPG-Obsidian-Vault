## Sincerest Flattery

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Padawan]]

Once per encounter when the character makes a check using a skill that another character has previously used during the encounter, they add two [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] dice.

[^1]: [[Category - Clone Wars Begin|Rise of the Separatists]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 42)
