## Mind Over Matter

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Executioner]], [[Magus]], [[Martial Artist]]

The character may spend one Destiny Point to recover strain equal to their Willpower rating.
