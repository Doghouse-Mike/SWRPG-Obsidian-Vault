## One With Nature

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Hermit]], [[Trailblazer]]

When in the wilderness, the character may make a **Simple (—) [[Survival (Cun)|Survival check]]** (instead of [[Discipline (Will)|Discipline]] or [[Cool (Pr)|Cool]]) to recover strain at the end of an encounter.
