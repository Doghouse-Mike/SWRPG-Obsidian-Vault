## Knowledge in Action

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Archaeologist (reSpec)]]

When making a skill check involving technology, tools, or mechanisms, the character may spend a Destiny Point to substitute a [[Category - Knowledge Skills|Knowledge skill]], per Game Master discretion, instead of the required skill.
