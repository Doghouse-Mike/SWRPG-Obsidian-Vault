## Codebreaker

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Analyst]] (1), [[Scholar]] (1), [[Shadow]] (2), [[Sleeper Agent]] (2), [[Slicer]] (2)

The character removes a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die per rank of Codebreaker from \[their\] attempts to break codes or decrypt communications. In addition, the character decreases the difficulty of \[their\] [[Computers (Int)|Computers]] or Intellect checks made to break codes or decrypt communications by one (this does not increase with additional ranks of Codebreaker).

### Improved

Source: [^4]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Analyst (reSpec)]]

Once per session, the character may choose a faction or group and make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Computers (Int)|Computers check]]** to gain insight on their plans or communications. On success, they may add [[Narrative Dice#Failure|failure results]] equal to ranks of Codebreaker to once check made by that group this session. The character must be in the same star system as the chosen group, or the character must have direct access to the group's transmissions or communications infrastructure.
