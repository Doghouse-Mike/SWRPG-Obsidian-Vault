## Droid Companion

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Droid Tech (reSpec)]]

Choose a willing PC or NPC droid as a Droid Companion. Choose two skills. The Droid Companion adds an automatic [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result to checks using those skills. These skills may be changed with a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Computers (Int)|Computers check]]** and six hours of reprogramming. If the droid is lost or destroyed, the character may apply Droid Companion to a new droid. The process of modifying and acclimating to the new droid is at the GM's discretion. No two characters may have the same Droid Companion.
