## Desperate Repairs

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Droid Specialist]]

Once per session, the character may make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics check]]** targeting one engaged droid. If the character succeeds, the droid becomes staggered and immobilized until the end of its next turn. At the end of its next turn, the droid heals all strain and one Critical Injury with a severity rating no greater than **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard]]**. If the character is a droid, it may perform this action on itself.

[^1]: [[Category - Engineer|Fully Operational]]: *A Sourcebook for Engineers* (Page 30)
