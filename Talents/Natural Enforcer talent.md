## Natural Enforcer

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Agitator]], [[Enforcer]], [[Gadgeteer]]

Once per game session, the character may reroll any one [[Coercion (Will)|Coercion]] or [[Streetwise (Cun)|Streetwise]] check.
