## Natural Charmer

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Advisor]], [[Ambassador]], [[Charmer]], [[Scoundrel]], [[Sleeper Agent]]

Once per game session, the character may reroll any one [[Charm (Pr)|Charm]] or [[Deception (Cun)|Deception]] check.
