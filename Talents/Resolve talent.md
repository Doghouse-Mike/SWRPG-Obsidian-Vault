## Resolve

Source: [^1][^2][^3][^4][^5]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Archaeologist]] (1), [[Charmer]] (1), [[Doctor]] (2), [[Figurehead]] (2), [[Force Adherent]] (2), [[Magus]] (2), [[Modder]] (2), [[Saboteur]] (2), [[Scholar]] (2), [[Senator]] (2), [[Slicer]] (1)

When the character suffers strain, they suffer 1 less strain per rank of Resolve, to a minimum of one. This does not apply to voluntary strain loss.
