## Shadowfeed Secret

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Slicer (reSpec)]]

Once per session, the character may choose target location, faction, or person and make a **[[Underworld (Int)|Knowledge (Underworld)]] check** with a difficulty based on the obscurity and vulnerability of the target, determined by the GM. On success, add a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result to any one [[Computers (Int)|Computers]] check to slice that target this session.
