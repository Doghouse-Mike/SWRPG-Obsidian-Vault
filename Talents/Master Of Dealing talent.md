## Master Of Dealing

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Scoundrel (reSpec)]]

Once per round, suffer 2 strain to reduce the difficulty of the next check to find, buy, or sell stolen, contraband or illegal goods once.
