## Kingmaker

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Advocate (reSpec)]]

Once per round, an ally may spend five [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage results]] or a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph result]] from a social check to immediately take another action. Each ally may only benefit from this once per encounter.
