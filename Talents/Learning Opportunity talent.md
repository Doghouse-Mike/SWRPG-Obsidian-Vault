## Learning Opportunity

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Padawan]]

Once per round, the character may spend three [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results from a check they fail to upgrade the ability of their next check once.

[^1]: [[Category - Clone Wars Begin|Rise of the Separatists]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 40)
