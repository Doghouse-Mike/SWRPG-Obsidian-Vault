## Eye for Detail

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Cyber Tech]] (1), [[Droid Tech]] (2), [[Shipwright]] (2)

After rolling the dice pool for a [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics]] or [[Computers (Int)|Computers]] check but before interpreting the results, the character may voluntarily suffer a number of strain no greater than ranks in Eye for Detail to convert that many [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] results into [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results. (The character must still have at least one [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] in the results at the end to succeed on the check.)
