## Intimidating

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Aggressor]] (2), [[Agitator]] (4), [[Death Watch Warrior]] (2), [[Enforcer]] (3), [[Gadgeteer]] (2), [[Interrogator]] (2), [[Juyo Berserker]] (2), [[Nightsister]] (2), [[Separatist Commander]] (1), [[Warden]] (1)
- **Items:** [[Armor Spikes]] (Modifications Option)

When attempting a [[Coercion (Will)|Coercion]] check, the character may suffer a number of strain to downgrade the difficulty of the check a number of times equal to the strain suffered. This number cannot exceed \[their\] ranks in Intimidating. When the character is the target of a [[Coercion (Will)|Coercion]] check, the character may suffer a number of strain to upgrade the difficulty of the check a number of times equal to the strain suffered. This number cannot exceed \[their\] ranks in Intimidating.
