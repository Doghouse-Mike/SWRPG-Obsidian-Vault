## Creative Design

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Shipwright]] (3)

As part of resolving a successful crafting check, the character may also apply a result equivalent to spending a number of [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results equal to \[their\] ranks in Creative Design. The GM may then apply a result equivalent to spending that same number of [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results.

[^1]: [[Category - Engineer|Fully Operational]]: *A Sourcebook for Engineers* (Page 30)
