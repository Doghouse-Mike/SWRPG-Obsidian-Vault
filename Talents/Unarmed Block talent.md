## Unarmed Block

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Martial Artist (reSpec)]]

The character may perform the Block incidental while unarmed. When the character performs the Block incidental while unarmed, reduce the strain they suffer from the Block incidental by one, to a minimum of 1.

### Improved

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Martial Artist (reSpec)]]

Whenever an engaged enemy makes a combat check, after the attack is resolved, the character may spend three [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result to disarm the attacker, and may equip the used weapon. If not equipping the weapon, it falls within short range at a location of the player's choice.
