## Construction Specialist

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Sapper]] (2)

The character removes a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die per rank of Construction Specialist from checks made to construct bases, defense works, positions, fortifications, tunnels, bunkers, and similar combat engineering projects.

[^1]: [[Category - Engineer|Fully Operational]]: *A Sourcebook for Engineers* (Page 30)
