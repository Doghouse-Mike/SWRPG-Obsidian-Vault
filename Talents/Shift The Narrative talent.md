## Shift The Narrative

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Propagandist (reSpec)]]

Once per session, the character chooses an organization and make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Charm (Pr)|Charm]]** or **[[Deception (Cun)|Deception check]]**. On success, each member of that organization adds a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback die]] or [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost die]] to each check they make this session.

When they do this, the player must explain how the PC disseminated the propaganda such that it has affected their targets. The chosen organization must be narrow and cohesive enough to be affected by targeted messaging—for example, the Ghorman Resistance, Saw's Partisans, or the 501st Legion. The Shift The Narrative action cannot affect all members of a single group as broad as the entire Empire or one as fractious as the Hutts. The PC must have time and means to spread misinformation, and cannot use this action during a combat encounter.

### Improved

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Propagandist (reSpec)]]

When the character takes the Shift The Narrative action, they may spend three [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage results]] or a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph result]] to increase or decrease the difficulty of the first check each member of the chosen organization makes each encounter during this session, excluding checks made to determine initiative.

#### Supreme

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Propagandist (reSpec)]]

The group affected by Shift The Narrative increases or decreases their wound threshold by 1, plus an additional 1 for each additional three [[Narrative Dice#Success|success results]], to a minimum of 1, until the end of the session.
