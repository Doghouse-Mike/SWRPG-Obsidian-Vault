## Creative Killer

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Recruit]], [[Sleeper Agent]]

The character reduces the critical rating of all improvised weapons \[they are\] wielding by 2 (to a minimum of 1).
