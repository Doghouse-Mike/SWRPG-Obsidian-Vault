## Planet Mapper

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Navigator]] (2), [[Operator]] (1)
- **Items:** [[Nishalorite Stone]] (Lightsaber Mod)

The character removes a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice per rank of Planet Mapper from their [[Streetwise (Cun)|Streetwise]] or [[Survival (Cun)|Survival]] checks used to navigate on a world. In addition, such checks take 50% less time (this does not decrease with additional ranks of Planet Mapper).
