## Knows the Ropes

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Pirate]]

Whenever the character is in binders or other personal restraints, \[they\] add a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to checks made to free \[themselves\] equal to \[their\] ranks in [[Underworld (Int)|Knowledge (Underworld)]]. A [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result on the check can be spent to free all other bound characters within short range.

[^1]: [[Category - Early Rebellion|Dawn of Rebellion]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 114)
