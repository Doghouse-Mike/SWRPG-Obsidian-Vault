## Fine Tuning

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Artisan]] (2), [[Driver]] (2), [[Mechanic]] (2), [[Shipwright]] (1)

Whenever the character repairs system strain on a starship or vehicle, \[they\] repair one additional system strain per rank of Fine Tuning.

```
The following is a homebrew expansion of this talent from a community-produced homebrew sourcebook!
```

### Improved

Source: [^4][^5]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Driver (reSpec)]], [[Mechanic (reSpec)]]

Once per encounter while engaged with or repairing a starship or vehicle, make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics check]]** to increase the system strain threshold of an the vessel by a number equal to the character's ranks in Fine Tuning for the rest of the encounter. Spend three [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result to have that vessel recover system strain equal to ranks in Fine Tuning.
