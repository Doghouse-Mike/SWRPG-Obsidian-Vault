## Unrelenting Skeptic

Source: [^1][^0-2]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Investigator]], [[Marshal]]

When targeted by a [[Deception (Cun)|Deception]] check, the character automatically adds [[Narrative Dice#Failure|failure]] results to the check equal to \[their\] ranks in [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]].

#### Improved

Source: [^0-2]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Marshal]]

When targeted by a [[Deception (Cun)|Deception]] check that fails, the character may spend one Destiny Point to retroactively add a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] to the pool results.
