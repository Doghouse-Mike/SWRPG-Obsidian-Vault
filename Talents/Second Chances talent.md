## Second Chances

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Gambler]] (3), [[Hotshot]] (2)

Once per encounter, after the character has rolled a skill check (but before resolving the result), \[they\] choose a number of positive dice from \[their\] dice pool up to \[their\] ranks in Second Chances and re-rolls them. The character must accept the second result. This talent cannot be used to re-roll any [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] dice.
