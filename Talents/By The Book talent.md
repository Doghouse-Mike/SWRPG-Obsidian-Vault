## By the Book

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Imperial Academy Cadet]] (3)

Once per encounter, before making a combat check, the character may suffer two strain to add an automatic [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result to the results equal to \[their\] ranks in By the Book.

[^1]: [[Category - Early Rebellion|Dawn of Rebellion]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 111)
