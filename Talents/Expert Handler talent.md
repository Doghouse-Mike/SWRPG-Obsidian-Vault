## Expert Handler

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Beast Rider]] (2)

The character removes a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die per rank of Expert Handler of \[their\] [[Survival (Cun)|Survival]] checks made to ride beasts.

[^1]: [[Category - Ace|Stay on Target]]: *A Sourcebook for Aces* (Page 32)
