## Researcher

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Alchemist]] (2), [[Analyst]] (2), [[Archaeologist]] (2), [[Knight]] (2), [[Master]] (1), [[Sage]] (2), [[Scholar]] (2), [[Scientist]] (2), [[Strategist]] (2), [[Teacher]] (1)

The character removes a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice per rank of Researcher from \[their\] [[Category - Knowledge Skills|Knowledge]] checks. Researching takes 50% less time (this does not increase with additional ranks of Researcher).

### Improved

Source: [^4][^5][^6]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Analyst]], [[Master]], [[Strategist]]

When the character makes a successful [[Category - Knowledge Skills|Knowledge]] check to gain information, they and their allies gain automatic [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results per rank of Researcher to checks they make to act on that knowledge until the end of their next turn.
