## Find The Story

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Propagandist (reSpec)]]

Once per session, the character may make an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Perception (Cun)|Perception check]]** to find a local compelling narrative. On success, the character adds a [[Narrative Dice#Success|success result]] to all checks they make to utilize that story this session, whether that is exploiting it for propagandistic purposes, or using it to sing the praises of the character's unit.
