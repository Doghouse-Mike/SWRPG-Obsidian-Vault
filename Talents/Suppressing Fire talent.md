## Suppressing Fire

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Clone Trooper]] (2), [[Vanguard]] (2), [[Warleader]] (2)

The character and each ally within short range may spend an [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result on their failed combat checks to inflict 1 strain on their respective targets per rank of Suppressing Fire. Each character can only activate this effect once per round.

### Improved

Source: [^4]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Heavy (reSpec)]]

Foes who suffer strain from the effects of the character's Suppressing Fire and foes within short range of the target character upgrade the difficulty of their next check once.
