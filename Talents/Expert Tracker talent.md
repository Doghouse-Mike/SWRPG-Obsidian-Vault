## Expert Tracker

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Species:** [[Rodian]], [[Sakiyan]]
- **Trees:** [[Beast Rider]] (2), [[Big-Game Hunter]] (2), [[Hunter]] (2), [[Navigator]] (1), [[Nightsister]] (1), [[Seer]] (1), [[Sharpshooter]] (2), [[Skip Tracer]] (1), [[Survivalist]] (2), [[Trailblazer]] (2)

The character removes a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice per rank of Expert Tracker from \[their\] checks to find or follow tracks. [[Survival (Cun)|Survival]] checks made to track targets take 50% less time than normal (this does not decrease with additional ranks of Expert Tracker).

### Improved

Source: [^4]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Survivalist (reSpec)]]

Once per session, make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Formidable difficulty]] [[Survival (Cun)|Survival]]** or **[[Perception (Cun)|Perception check]]** to find a minor item lost by a tracked target. This might identify the target or indicate destination, bearing, or mode of travel. Decrease the Difficulty once per rank of Expert Tracker. Spend a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result to find a major item instead, which might prevent the target from escaping or avoiding capture.
