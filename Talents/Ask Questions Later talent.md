## Ask Questions Later

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Gunslinger (reSpec)]]

Once per encounter as an out-of-turn incidental, before the character or an ally makes a social check, add an [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result, [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] result, or [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result to that check, determined by the strongest type of adversary defeated during the current encounter: [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result for a minion, [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] result for a rival, [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result for a nemesis.
