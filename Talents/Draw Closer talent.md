## Draw Closer

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Niman Disciple]]

*Force talent.* The character may take a Draw Closer action, making a [[Lightsaber (Br)|Lightsaber]] (Willpower) melee combat check against one silhouette 1 (or smaller) target within medium range and adding a number of [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] dice no greater than Force rating to the check. The character may spend [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|points]] before resolving the success or failure of the check to move the target one range band closer to the character (including from short to engaged). \[They\] may also spend [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|points]] to add [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] results to the combat check. If the character cannot move \[their\] target to engage \[them\], the combat check automatically misses.

[^1]: [[Category - Way of the Force|***Star Wars*** **Force and Destiny**]] Roleplaying Game: *Core Rulebook* (Page 142)
