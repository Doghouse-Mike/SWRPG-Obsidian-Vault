## Engineered Redundancies

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Cyber Tech]]

The character benefits from [[Emergency Repair Patch|emergency repair patches]] as if \[they\] were a droid. The character still benefits from a total of 5 stim packs and/or emergency repair patches per day. Additionally, the character can be healed with the [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics]] skill in the same manner as a droid.

[^1]: [[Category - Technician|Special Modifications]]: *A Sourcebook for Technicians* (Page 34)
