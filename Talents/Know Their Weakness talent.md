## Know Their Weakness

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Interrogator]] (2)

Once per session, the character may choose one target within short range and perform the Know Their Weakness action to make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Perception (Cun)|Perception check]]**. If successful, the character downgrades the difficulty of social skill checks \[they\] make against that target once per rank of Know Their Weakness.

### Improved

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Interrogator]]

The character may extend the effects of Know Their Weakness to a number of enemy characters within short range equal to \[their\] rating of Presence.
