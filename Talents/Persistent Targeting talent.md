## Persistent Targeting

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Retired Clone Trooper]]

After making one or more successful combat checks against a target, the character adds a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to combat checks against that same target for the remainder of the encounter.

### Improved

Source: [^2]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Mercenary Soldier (reSpec)]]

After the character makes one or more successful combat checks against a target allies within short range of the character add a boost die to combat checks against that target for the remainder of the encounter.
