## Mustafar Special

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Blockade Runner]]

Once per round while piloting a starship or vehicle, make an **opposed [[Piloting - Planetary (Ag)|Pilo]][[Piloting - Space (Ag)|ting]] vs. [[Piloting - Planetary (Ag)|Pilo]][[Piloting - Space (Ag)|ting]] check** against a starship or vehicle within close range. On success, that starship or vehicle suffers system strain equal to the character's Cunning plus [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] results. Spend two [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results to inflict the same amount of system strain on an additional craft within close range.
