## Skilled Orator

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Politico (reSpec)]]

Once per round, spend a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result from a social check to affect twice the number of targets, or have each target recover or suffer twice the number of strain from the check.
