## On The Lookout

Source: [^0-1][^2]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Scout (reSpec)]], [[Bodyguard (reSpec)]]

When making a check to determine initiative, spend a Destiny Point to add a [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] result to both their check and to a number of allies' equal to ranks in Cunning.
