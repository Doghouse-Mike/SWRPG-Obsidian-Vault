## Inspiring Leadership

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Ship Captain]]

The character may take the Inspiring Leadership action by making an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Leadership (Pr)|Leadership]]** [[Leadership (Pr)|**check**]]. If successful, a number of allies not exceeding \[their\] rating in Presence within short range add an automatic [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] result to their next skill check.

[^1]: [[Category - Early Rebellion|Dawn of Rebellion]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 113)
