## Captive Audience

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Performer (reSpec)]]

Once per session, make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Charm (Pr)|Charm]]** or **[[Deception (Cun)|Deception check]]** to captivate a group of people with a performance. On success, add a [[Narrative Dice#Failure|failure]] result to checks made by the audience for the duration of the character's performance. The character may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result to add two [[Narrative Dice#Failure|failure]] results instead.
