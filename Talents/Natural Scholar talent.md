## Natural Scholar

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Imperial Academy Cadet]], [[Scholar]], [[Scientist]]

Once per game session, the character may reroll any one [[Category - Knowledge Skills|Knowledge]] skill check.
