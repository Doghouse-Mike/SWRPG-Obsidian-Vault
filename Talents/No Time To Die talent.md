## No Time To Die

Source: [^1-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Maneuver)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Mercenary Soldier (reSpec)]]

Once per session, as a maneuver, spend a Destiny Point to recover strain and/or wounds in any combination up to ranks in [[Cool (Pr)|Cool]] or [[Discipline (Will)|Discipline]].
