## The Land Provides

Source: [^0-1][^2]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Scout (reSpec)]], [[Survivalist (reSpec)]]

Once per session, make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Survival (Cun)|Survival check]]** to fashion a low-tech solution to a current problem using nearby forageable materials. It functions until the end of the encounter, but the GM may impose negative qualities like Inferior or Limited Ammo.
