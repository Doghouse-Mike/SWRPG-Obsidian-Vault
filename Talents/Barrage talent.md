## Barrage

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Bodyguard]] (3), [[Heavy]] (3)
- **Items:** [[Gyrostabilizer]] (Modification Option)

Add one damage per rank of Barrage to one hit of successful [[Ranged - Heavy (Ag)|Ranged (Heavy)]] or [[Gunnery (Ag)|Gunnery]] attacks with non-starship/vehicle weapons at long or extreme range.
