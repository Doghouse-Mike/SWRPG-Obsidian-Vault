## Questionable Ethics

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Sorcerer]] (3)

Once per round, before making a [[Medicine (Int)|Medicine]] or [[Coercion (Will)|Coercion]] check, the character may suffer a number of strain and conflict up to their ranks in Questionable Ethics to add an equal number of [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] or [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results to the check.

[^0-1]: [[Category - Old Republic|The Old Republic]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 30)
