## Brainwash

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[First Order Loyalist]]

Take the Brainwash action; Make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Leadership (Pr)|Leadership check]]**. One ally within short range suffers two strain and upgrades the ability of their next check.

### Improved

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[First Order Loyalist]]

Suffer 1 strain to take the Brainwash action as a maneuver.
