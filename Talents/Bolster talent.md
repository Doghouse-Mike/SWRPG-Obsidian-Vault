## Bolster

Source: [^0-1][^2][^1-3]

- **Activation:** Active (Maneuver)
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Scout (reSpec)]] (2), [[Heavy (reSpec)]] (2), [[Mercenary Soldier (reSpec)]] (2)

The character may perform the Bolster maneuver to remove a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die per rank of Bolster imposed by a single environmental circumstance, excluding cover, from all checks they make this encounter. This may be used multiple times to remove [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setbacks]] imposed by additional environmental circumstances.

### Improved

Source: [^0-1][^1-3]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Scout (reSpec)]], [[Mercenary Soldier (reSpec)]]

The benefits of the Bolster maneuver apply to all allies within short range as long as they remain within short range of the character.
