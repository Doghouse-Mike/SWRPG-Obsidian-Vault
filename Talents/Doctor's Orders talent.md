## Doctor's Orders

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Doctor (reSpec)]]

When an ally is recovering under the character's medical supervision, they recover an additional wound whenever they passively heal wounds. Any character making a check to heal a supervised character's critical injury adds a [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] result to the check.

[^1]: [[Category - ReSpecialized|The reSpecialized Project]]'s Doctor Folio
