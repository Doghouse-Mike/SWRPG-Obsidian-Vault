## Good Cop

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Interrogator]] (1), [[Marshal]] (2), [[Skip Tracer]] (2)

The character may spend two [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results from a [[Charm (Pr)|Charm]] or [[Negotiation (Pr)|Negotiation]] check to upgrade the ability of a single ally's subsequent social interaction skill check against the same target in the same encounter. Upgrade the ability a number of times equal to the character's ranks in Good Cop. A single check may only benefit from one use of Good Cop.
