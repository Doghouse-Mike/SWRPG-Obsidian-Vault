## Logic Bomb

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Slicer (reSpec)]]

Once per session, make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Computers (Int)|Computers check]]** and choose a connected system or device the character has access to, and a triggering event. On success, when the event occurs, the character may immediately enact a single command of their choice to that system or have it shut down, chosen at the time of triggering.
