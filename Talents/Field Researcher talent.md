## Field Researcher

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Archaeologist (reSpec)]]

When the character makes a successful [[Category - Knowledge Skills|Knowledge]] check to gain information, they reduce the difficulty of the next check they make to act on that knowledge by their ranks of [[Researcher talent|Researcher]], to a minimum of **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Easy difficulty]]**.
