## Sense Emotions

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Advisor]], [[Arbiter]], [[Force Sensitive Exile]], [[Force-Sensitive Outcast]], [[Knight]], [[Niman Disciple]]

*Force talent.* The character adds a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to all [[Charm (Pr)|Charm]], [[Coercion (Will)|Coercion]], and [[Deception (Cun)|Deception]] checks unless the target is immune to Force powers.
