## Lose Them

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Courier]]

When being followed of chased, the character may take the Lose Them action and make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Stealth (Ag)|Stealth check]]**. If successful, \[they\] add two [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice to checks to follow \[them\] for the remainder of the encounter and may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] from \[their\] check to lose \[their\] pursuers completely.

[^1]: [[Category - Spy|Cyphers and Masks]]: *A Sourcebook for Spies* (Page 31)
