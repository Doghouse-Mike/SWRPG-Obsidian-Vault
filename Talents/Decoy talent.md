## Decoy

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Thief (reSpec)]]

Once per session, spend a Destiny Point to produce a facsimile of an encumbrance 1 or smaller item in the scene. Any character attempting to determine that it is counterfeit must make an **opposed [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] vs. [[Skulduggery (Cun)|Skulduggery check]]**.
