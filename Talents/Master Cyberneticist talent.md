## Master Cyberneticist

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Cyber Tech (reSpec)]]

Once per round, suffer 2 strain to decrease the difficulty of the next check made to heal a cyborg or create, install, repair, or activate a cybernetic once. This does not apply to checks that incidentally include the use of cybernetics.
