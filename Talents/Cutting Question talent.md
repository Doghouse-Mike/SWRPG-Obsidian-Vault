## Cutting Question

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Propagandist]]

Once per encounter, the character may make one [[Coercion (Will)|Coercion]] skill check using \[their\] [[Deception (Cun)|Deception]] skill. The character's [[Deception (Cun)|Deception]] skill is combined with \[their\] Willpower characteristic to form the dice pool. When the character takes the Cutting Question incidental, the player must explain how, in this particular instance, \[their\] [[Deception (Cun)|Deception]] skill is helping to erode \[their\] foe's resolve.

[^1]: [[Category - Diplomat|Desperate Allies]]: *A Sourcebook for Diplomats* (Page 33)
