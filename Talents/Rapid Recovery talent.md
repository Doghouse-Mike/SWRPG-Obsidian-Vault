## Rapid Recovery

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Entrepreneur]] (2), [[Fringer]] (3), [[Healer]] (2), [[Hunter]] (3), [[Pilot]] (1), [[Prophet]] (3), [[Saboteur]] (2), [[Scout]] (2), [[Skip Tracer]] (2)
- **Items:** [[Adrenal Implant]], [[Biofeedback System]]

When the character recovers strain after an encounter has concluded, \[they\] recover one additional strain per rank in Rapid Recovery.
