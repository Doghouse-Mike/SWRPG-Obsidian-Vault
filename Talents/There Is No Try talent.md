## There is No Try

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Master]]

*Force talent.* Once per session before any ally rolls the dice for a check, the character may spend a Destiny Point to allow the ally to automatically succeed at the check with one success and no other results.

[^1]: [[Category - Clone Wars End|Collapse of the Republic]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 44)
