## Accomplished Accomplice

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Droid Tech (reSpec)]]

Once per encounter per Droid Companion, when an ally within short range of a Droid Companion would fail a check, the character may spend a Destiny Point to roll [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] dice equal to that Droid Companion's ranks in that skill to the check.
