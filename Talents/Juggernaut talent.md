## Juggernaut

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Marauder (reSpec)]]

Once per round when the character suffers a critical injury, spend a Destiny Point to reduce the Critical Injury result by 10 per rank of [[Resilience (Br)|Resilience]], to a minimum of 1.
