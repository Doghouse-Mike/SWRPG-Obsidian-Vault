## Hired Help

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Entrepreneur (reSpec)]]

Once per Encounter, make an **opposed [[Negotiation (Pr)|Negotiation]] vs [[Discipline (Will)|Discipline]] check** targeting a non-hostile minion group or rival within medium range. On success, they work towards the character's goals until the end of the encounter. The character must pay a minion group whose services are bought this way 100 credits per minion in the group, or 500 credits for a Rival. The task they are directed to perform must not violate common sense, their core values, or be obviously suicidal.

### Improved

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Entrepreneur (reSpec)]]

Once per encounter, spend a Destiny Point to have an engaged Hired Help NPC suffer a hit in the character's stead, or have the Hired Help suffer the legal or public consequences for something the character did or orchestrated during the scene. Either option removes the Hired Help NPC(s) from the scene.

### Supreme

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Entrepreneur (reSpec)]]

The Hired Help action may now target enemy Minions and Rivals. The credit price to target them is doubled. The Game Master has ultimate say whether these minions or Rivals can be swayed by money.
