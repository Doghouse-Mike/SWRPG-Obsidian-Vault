## Kill with Kindness

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Species:** [[Anx]]
- **Trees:** [[Advisor]] (1), [[Ambassador]] (2), [[Charmer]] (2), [[Performer]] (1), [[Politico]] (2), [[Republic Representative]] (2), [[Sage]] (1)

The character removes a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die per rank of Kill with Kindness from \[their\] [[Charm (Pr)|Charm]] and [[Leadership (Pr)|Leadership]] checks.

The following is a homebrew expansion of this talent from a community-produced homebrew sourcebook!

### Improved

Source: [^4]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Charmer (reSpec)]]

Once per encounter, make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Charm (Pr)|Charm]]** or **[[Leadership (Pr)|Leadership check]]**. On success, a number of characters equal to the character's ranks of Kill With Kindness add an [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result or a [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] result to checks they make this encounter, chosen separately for each character. Spend a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result to double the number of [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] or [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results added this way.
