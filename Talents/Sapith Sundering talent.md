## Sapith Sundering

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Steel Hand Adept]]

*Force talent.* When making a [[Brawl (Br)|Brawl]] combat check, the character may include [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] dice up to the character's Force rating. The Brawl attack gains the [[Item Qualities#Sunder (Active)|Sunder]] quality, and the character may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]] generated on the check as an [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result to activate the [[Item Qualities#Sunder (Active)|Sunder]] quality.

[^1]: [[Category - Warrior|Knights of Fate]]: *A Sourcebook for Warriors* (Page 31)
