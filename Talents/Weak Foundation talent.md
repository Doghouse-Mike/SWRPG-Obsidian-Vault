## Weak Foundation

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Sapper]]

Once per game session, the character may identify one bunker, weapon emplacement, or other fixed combat structure (subject to the GM's approval) and then take a Weak Foundation action; making a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Warfare (Int)|Knowledge (Warfare) check]]**. If the character succeeds, until the end of the encounter the character and all allies add two automatic [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] result to all combat checks made targeting the structure (active character's choice).

[^1]: [[Category - Engineer|Fully Operational]]: *A Sourcebook for Engineers* (Page 31)
