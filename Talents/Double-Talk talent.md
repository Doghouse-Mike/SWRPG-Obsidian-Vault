## Double-Talk

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Pirate]]

The character may spend two [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result from a successful [[Charm (Pr)|Charm]] or [[Deception (Cun)|Deception]] check to disorient a number of opponents within short range equal to \[their\] rating in Presence for the remainder of the current round and the next two rounds.

[^1]: [[Category - Early Rebellion|Dawn of Rebellion]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 112)
