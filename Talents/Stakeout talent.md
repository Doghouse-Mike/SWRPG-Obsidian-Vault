## Stakeout

Source: [^0-1][^2]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Skip Tracer (reSpec)]], [[Thief (reSpec)]]

Once per session, make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Cool (Pr)|Cool]]** or **[[Skulduggery (Cun)|Skulduggery check]]** while surveilling a person or place. For the remainder of the session, add two automatic [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results to checks they make that are associated with that location or person.

### Improved

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Gadgeteer (reSpec)|Skip Tracer (reSpec)]]

When using the Stakeout action, spend [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results to extend this benefit to 1 ally per [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result spent, and spend a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result to add a [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] result to the checks of one character affected by Stakeout.
