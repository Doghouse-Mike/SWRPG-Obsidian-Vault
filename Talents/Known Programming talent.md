## Known Programming

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Clone Veteran]]

Once per session, the character may choose a single model of droid that they have encountered before and make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance check]]**. If the check is successful, minion and rival droids of that type upgrade the difficulty of their checks targeting the character, plus one ally per [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] result after the first, once for the rest of the encounter.

### Improved

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Clone Veteran]]

Allies affected by Known Programming also upgrade the ability of their checks targeting the chosen droid model once.
