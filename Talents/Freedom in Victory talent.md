## Freedom in Victory

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Maneuver)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Lord]]

*Force talent.* The character may perform the Freedom in Victory maneuver and commit a [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] die. When the character would become immobilized or staggered, they may instead suffer 2 strain to not be affected.

[^0-1]: [[Category - Old Republic|The Old Republic]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 28)
