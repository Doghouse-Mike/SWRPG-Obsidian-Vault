## Power From Pain

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Colossus]]

*Force talent.* Once per session, the character may spend a Destiny Point to gain +1 Force rating for each Critical Injury the character is suffering, until the end of the encounter. A character who has purchased this talent automatically gains 1 Conflict at the beginning of each game session.

[^1]: [[Category - Warrior|Knights of Fate]]: *A Sourcebook for Warriors* (Page 31)
