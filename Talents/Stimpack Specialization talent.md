## Stimpack Specialization

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Alchemist]] (2), [[Instructor]] (2), [[Medic]] (3), [[Protector]] (2), [[Retired Clone Trooper]] (1)

Whenever the character uses a [[Stimpack|stimpack]], the target heals an additional wound per rank of Stimpack Specialization. The sixth [[Stimpack|stimpack]] and beyond each day still have no effect.

[^2]: [[Category - Way of the Force|***Star Wars*** **Force and Destiny**]] Roleplaying Game: *Core Rulebook* (Page 152)
[^3]: [[Category - Rebel Ranks|***Star Wars*** **Age of Rebellion**]] Roleplaying Game: *Core Rulebook* (Page 157)
