## A Call For Aid

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Ambassador (reSpec)]]

Once per session, the character may make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Charm (Pr)|Charm check]]**. On success, the character reveals how a previously unknown call for aid has a meaningful impact on the scene. The nature of the revelation and its impact on the scene are ultimately subject to the GM's discretion, but they should give a significant advantage to the PCs. If the check fails and generates a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair result]], however, the GM may decide that the character's ineffective plea either puts them in further danger, or the intended ally is apprehended when answering the call.

[^1]: [[Category - ReSpecialized|The reSpecialized Project]]'s Ambassador Folio
