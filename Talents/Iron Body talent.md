## Iron Body

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Martial Artist]] (2), [[Steel Hand Adept]] (2)

The character removes a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die per rank of Iron Body from their [[Coordination (Ag)|Coordination]] and [[Resilience (Br)|Resilience]] checks. The critical rating of the character's unarmed attacks is reduced by one per rank of Iron Body, to a minimum of 1.

[^2]: [[Category - Bounty Hunter|No Disintegrations]]: *A Sourcebook for Bounty Hunters* (Page 34)
