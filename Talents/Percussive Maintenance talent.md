## Percussive Maintenance

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Maneuver)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Mechanic (reSpec)]]

Once per encounter as a maneuver, before making a check to repair or damage an engaged machine, starship, vehicle, or droid, suffer 2 wounds to add [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] dice equal to the character's ranks of [[Melee (Br)|Melee]] or [[Brawl (Br)|Brawl]] (whichever is higher) to the check.
