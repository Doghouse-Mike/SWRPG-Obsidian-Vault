## Defensive Driving

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Species:** [[Dug]]
- **Trees:** [[Driver]] (1), [[Fringer]] (1), [[Imperial Academy Cadet]] (1), [[Pilot]] (1), [[Squadron Leader]] (2)
- **Items:** [[Enhanced Repulsorlift]]

Any vehicle the character pilots has its defensive rating on all zones increased by one per rank of Defensive Driving.
