## Hand Off

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Thief (reSpec)]]

Once per round as an out of turn incidental, suffer 2 strain to covertly pick up or hide an engaged item that is encumbrance 1 or smaller, or exchange up to two items that are encumbrance 1 or smaller with an ally within in short range. If the character is being closed watched, an **opposed [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] vs. [[Skulduggery (Cun)|Skulduggery check]]** can be made to notice the exchange, adding two [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice to the pool.
