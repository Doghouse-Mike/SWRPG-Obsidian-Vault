## Knowledge is Strength

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[General]]

*Force talent.* Once per session, when making a check, the character may increase their Force rating by their ranks in [[Warfare (Int)|Knowledge (Warfare)]] for the duration of the check. \[A\] character who has purchased this talent gains 1 Conflict at the beginning of each game session.

[^1]: [[Category - Clone Wars End|Collapse of the Republic]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 41)
