## Quick Strike

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[ARC Trooper]] (2), [[Assassin]] (1), [[Ataru Striker]] (2), [[Big-Game Hunter]] (1), [[Executioner]] (2), [[Force-Sensitive Outcast]] (1), [[Gunslinger]] (2), [[Juyo Berserker]] (2), [[Scoundrel]] (2), [[Scout]] (2), [[Squadron Leader]] (2)
- **Items:** [[Threat Monitor]] (Modification Option)

The character adds a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die per rank of Quick Strike to \[their\] combat checks made against any target that has not yet acted in the encounter.
