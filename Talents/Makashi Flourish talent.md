## Makashi Flourish

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Makashi Duelist]]

*Force talent.* Once per encounter, the character may take the Makashi Flourish action, making an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Lightsaber (Br)|Lightsaber (Presence) check]]**. If it is successful, one engaged opponent suffers strain equal to [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] results (ignoring soak), and the character recovers an equal amount of strain. The character may also spend three [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result generated on the check to stagger the opponent until the end of the target's next turn.

[^1]: [[Category - Way of the Force|***Star Wars*** **Force and Destiny**]] Roleplaying Game: *Core Rulebook* (Page 146)
