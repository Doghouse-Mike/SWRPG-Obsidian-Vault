## Coven

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Maneuver)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Nightsister]]

*Force talent.* The character may perform the Coven maneuver. On their next Force power check, they may reroll one [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force dice]] for each ally within short range who also possesses the Coven talent.

[^1]: [[Category - Clone Wars End|Collapse of the Republic]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 38)
