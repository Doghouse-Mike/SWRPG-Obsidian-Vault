## Set The Tone

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Advocate (reSpec)]]

Once per encounter, the character may take the Set The Tone action; make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Negotiation (Pr)|Negotiation check]]**, and choose two social skills. On success, during this encounter other characters decrease the difficulty of all checks made using the first skill and increase the difficulty of all checks using the second skill. This cannot decrease the difficulty of any check below **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Easy difficulty]]**.
