## Skilled Slicer

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Slicer]]

When making a [[Computers (Int)|Computers]] check, the character may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result to gain some measure of control over the target systems. Any further [[Computers (Int)|Computers]] checks within this system can be made as maneuvers.
