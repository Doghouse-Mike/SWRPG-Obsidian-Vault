## Master Leader

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Squadron Leader]]

Once per round, the character may suffer two strain to decrease the difficulty of \[their\] next [[Leadership (Pr)|Leadership]] check by one, to a minimum of **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Easy difficulty]]**.

[^1]: [[Category - Rebel Ranks|***Star Wars*** **Age of Rebellion**]] Roleplaying Game: *Core Rulebook* (Page 151)
