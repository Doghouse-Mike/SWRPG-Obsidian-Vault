## Watch This

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Seasoned Adventurer]] (2)

Once per encounter when attempting a check with a difficulty of **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Daunting]]** or higher, add [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] results equal to ranks in Watch This to the check.

[^0-1]: [[Category - Sequels|Fires of Resistance]]: *An Unofficial Era Sourcebook* (Page 12)
