## Escape Clause

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Ambassador (reSpec)]]

Once per encounter when making a social check to avoid social or legal consequences, the character may suffer a number of strain up to ranks of [[Core Worlds (Int)|Knowledge (Core Worlds)]] to add that many [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost dice]] to the check. The character must establish a relevant legal loophole or cultural mores that strengthens their case.
