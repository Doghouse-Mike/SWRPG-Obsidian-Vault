## Inspiring Kill

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Maneuver)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[General]]

*Force talent.* The character may spend one Destiny point to perform an Inspiring Kill maneuver immediately after conducting a successful attack in which the target is incapacitated or suffers a Critical Injury. The character rolls [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force dice]] no greater than their Force rating, and may spend [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|points]] to heal 2 strain from allies within medium range (and may do so multiple times). \[A\] character who has purchased this talent gains 1 Conflict at the beginning of each game session.

[^1]: [[Category - Clone Wars End|Collapse of the Republic]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 40)
