## Local Businesses

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Cartel Dealer]]

Once per session, the character may make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Underworld (Int)|Knowledge (Underworld) check]]**. If they succeed, they are aware of the nearest black market that is friendly to them or an organization they are part of. They know how to get there, as well as any necessary passwords or customs to enter. Additional [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]], [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] or [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] results can reveal other useful information at the GM’s discretion.
