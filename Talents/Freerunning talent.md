## Freerunning

Source: [^1][^0-2]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Courier]], [[Racer]]

Once per round, before performing a Move maneuver, the character may suffer 1 strain. If \[they\] do so, \[they\] may use \[their\] Move maneuver to move to any location within short range (even straight up) as long as there is some sort of object to move across or a path to move along.

### Improved

Source: [^0-2]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Racer]]

Once per round, before performing a Move maneuver, the character may suffer 4 strain. If \[they\] do so, \[they\] may use \[their\] Move maneuver to move to any location within medium range (even straight up) as long as there is some sort of object to move across or a path to move along.
