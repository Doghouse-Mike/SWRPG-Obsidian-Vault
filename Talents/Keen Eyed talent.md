## Keen Eyed

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Hunter]] (1), [[Pathfinder]] (2), [[Seer]] (1), [[Investigator]] (2)

The character removes a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die per rank of Keen Eyed from \[their\] [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] and [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]] checks. Checks made to search a specific area take 50% less time than normal. This does not decrease with additional ranks of Keen Eyed.

```
The following is a homebrew expansion of this talent from a community-produced homebrew sourcebook!
```

### Improved

Source: [^2]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Scout (reSpec)]]

Once per session, make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Formidable difficulty]] [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] or [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance check]]** to add a [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] result to next checks they and allies make to act on the observation. Decrease difficulty of the check once per rank of Keen Eyed.
