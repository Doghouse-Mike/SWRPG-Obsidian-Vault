## Empty Soul

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Ascetic]]

*Force talent.* If the character is carrying items that total 2 encumbrance or less (after factoring in reductions such as for armor being worn), whenever \[they\] make a Force power check, \[they\] add a [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|light point]] and a [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|dark point]] to the results.

If a character normally carries items that total more than 2 encumbrance but discards or otherwise loses those items temporarily, the GM can rule that the character still does not gain the benefits of Empty Soul.

[^1]: [[Category - Consular|Disciples of Harmony]]: *A Sourcebook for Consulars* (Page 28)
