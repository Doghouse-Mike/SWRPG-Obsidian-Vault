## Makashi Finish

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Makashi Duelist]]

*Force talent.* The character may take the Makashi Finish action, making a [[Lightsaber (Br)|Lightsaber]] (Presence) combat check against an engaged target, adding [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] dice no greater than Force rating to the check. The character may spend [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|points]] to add +10 to any Critical Injury roll resulting from the check.

[^1]: [[Category - Way of the Force|***Star Wars*** **Force and Destiny**]] Roleplaying Game: *Core Rulebook* (Page 146)
