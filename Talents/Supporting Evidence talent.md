## Supporting Evidence

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Advocate]] (2), [[Analyst]] (1)

When the character assists an ally with a [[Charm (Pr)|Charm]], [[Deception (Cun)|Deception]], [[Leadership (Pr)|Leadership]], or [[Negotiation (Pr)|Negotiation]] check, that ally adds an automatic [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result to the results of the check per rank of Supporting Evidence.

[^1]: [[Category - Diplomat|Desperate Allies]]: *A Sourcebook for Diplomats* (Page 34)
