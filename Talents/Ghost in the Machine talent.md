## Ghost in the Machine

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Slicer (reSpec)]]

Suffer strain up to ranks in [[Stealth (Ag)|Stealth]] to add an equal number of [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice or remove an equal number of [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] dice from checks to trace, locate, or identify the character while they are slicing, including physical discovery.
