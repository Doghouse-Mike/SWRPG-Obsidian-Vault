## Scathing Tirade

Source: [^0-1][^2][^3][^1-4][^5]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Agitator]], [[Politico]], [[Prophet]], [[Separatist Commander]], [[Warden]]

The character may take the Scathing Tirade action by making an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Coercion (Will)|Coercion check]]**. For each [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] result, one enemy within short range suffers 1 strain. The character can also spend [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results; for every [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] spent, one target already affected suffers 1 additional strain.

### Improved

Source: [^0-1][^1-4][^6]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Agitator]], [[Politico]], [[Separatist Commander]]

Each enemy affected by Scathing Tirade suffers a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice on all skill checks for a number of rounds equal to the character's ranks in Coercion; this does not stack with itself.

### Supreme

Source: [^0-1][^1-4]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Agitator]], [[Politico]], [[Separatist Commander]]

The character may voluntarily suffer 1 strain to perform Scathing Tirade as a maneuver instead of an action.
