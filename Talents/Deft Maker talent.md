## Deft Maker

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Droid Tech]] (2)

The character removes a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die per rank of Deft Maker from \[their\] checks to repair, modify, construct, and program droids. In addition, materials to craft droids cost the character 50% less (this does not decrease with additional ranks of Deft Maker).

[^1]: [[Category - Technician|Special Modifications]]: *A Sourcebook for Technicians* (Page 34)
