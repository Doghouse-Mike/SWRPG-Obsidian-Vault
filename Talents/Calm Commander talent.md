## Calm Commander

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Figurehead]]

When upgrading the ability of a Mass Combat check in a mass combat in which the character is the commander of the acting force (or when upgrading the difficulty pool, if the character is the commander of the enemy force), the character may use ranks in [[Cool (Pr)|Cool]] instead of [[Leadership (Pr)|Leadership]].

[^1]: [[Category - Commander|Lead by Example]]: *A Sourcebook for Commanders* (Page 34)
