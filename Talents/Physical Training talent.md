## Physical Training

Source: [^1][^2][^3][^4]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[ARC Trooper]] (1), [[Ascetic]] (2), [[Clone Trooper]] (1), [[Commando]] (2), [[Instructor]] (2)

The character adds a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] dice per rank of Physical Training to their [[Athletics (Br)|Athletics]] and [[Resilience (Br)|Resilience]] checks.
