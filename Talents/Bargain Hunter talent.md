## Bargain Hunter

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Entrepreneur (reSpec)]] (1), [[Quartermaster (reSpec)]] (1), [[Trader (reSpec)]] (2)

When the character makes a check to purchase or sell goods or services, they add [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] dice or remove [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice equal to their ranks of Bargain Hunter.
