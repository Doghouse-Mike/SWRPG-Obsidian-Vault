## Raise The Stakes

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Gambler (reSpec)]]

Once per check, the character may suffer 2 strain to increase and upgrade the difficulty of their next check once. If successful, add [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results equal to the final difficulty of the check to the results.

### Improved

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Gambler (reSpec)]]

Double the number of [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] and [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] results on checks affected by Raise The Stakes, regardless of Success.
