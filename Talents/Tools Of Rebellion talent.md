## Tools Of Rebellion

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Agitator (reSpec)]]

Once per session, the character may take the Tools Of Rebellion action; make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Underworld (Int)|Knowledge (Underworld)]]** or **[[Warfare (Int)|Knowledge (Warfare) check]]** to reveal that weapons and equipment suitable to outfit allies equal to [[Narrative Dice#Success|success results]] is stashed nearby. Spend three [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage results]] or a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph result]] to have all equipment be already distributed among those allies. Each piece of equipment found should be unrestricted and have a rarity no greater than 4 and an encumbrance no greater than 2. The character may increase the rarity and encumbrance allowed by 1 or allow restricted equipment by reducing the character's Duty by 2, or do both and reduce it by 3.

[^1]: [[Category - ReSpecialized|The reSpecialized Project]]'s Agitator Folio
