## Jack Of All Trades

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Fringer (reSpec)]] (3)

When making a skill check using a skill with fewer ranks than their ranks of Jack Of All Trades, suffer a number of strain up to the difference to upgrade the ability of the check that many times.
