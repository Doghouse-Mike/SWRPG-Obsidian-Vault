## Stalker

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Assassin]] (3), [[Big-Game Hunter]] (2), [[Scout]] (2), [[Survivalist]] (2), [[Thief]] (1), [[Trailblazer]] (1)
- **Items:** [[Reflec Shadowskin]]

The character adds a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] dice per rank of Stalker to \[their\] [[Coordination (Ag)|Coordination]] and [[Stealth (Ag)|Stealth]] checks.
