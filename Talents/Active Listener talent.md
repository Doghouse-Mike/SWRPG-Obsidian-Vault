## Active Listener

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Analyst (reSpec)]]

When targeted by an opposed social check, the character may suffer 2 strain to oppose the check with [[Perception (Cun)|Perception (Cunning)]] instead of [[Cool (Pr)|Cool]] or [[Discipline (Will)|Discipline]].

### Improved

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Analyst (reSpec)]]

When an ally within short range is targeted by an opposed social check, the character may suffer strain up to the character's ranks in [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] to add an equal number of [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback dice]] to the check.
