## Improvised Detonation

Source: [^1][^0-2]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Demolitionist]], [[Sapper]]

Once per session, the character may make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics check]]** to perform the Improvised Detonation action and build an explosive device out of available materials. The device can be detonated via any logical means (timed delay, pressure activated, or even a fuse) and when it explodes deals damage equal to the character's ranks in Intellect plus ranks in [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics]] plus [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] results on the initial check, and possesses the [[Item Qualities#Blast (Active)|Blast]] quality at an equal value. The character can spend a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result to increase the damage by an additional 2. A [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result causes the device to detonate immediately in the owner's face. If the check would have been otherwise successful, the premature detonation does damage based on the successful check (and has an equivalent [[Item Qualities#Blast (Active)|Blast]] quality). If the check would have failed, the premature detonation only does damage equal to ranks in [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics]] plus ranks in Intellect.

### Improved

Source: [^0-2]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Demolitionist]]

Reduce the difficulty of Improvised Detonation's check to **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average]]** and increase the damage dealt by the explosive to ranks in Intellect plus twice the character's ranks in [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics]] plus [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] results on the initial check (this includes the damage dealt on a premature detonation triggered by a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result).

#### Supreme

Source: [^3]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Demolitionist (reSpec)]]

When taking the Improvised Detonation action, the character may add [[Item Qualities#Burn (Active)|Burn]] +1, [[Item Qualities#Concussive (Active)|Concussive]] +1, [[Item Qualities#Ensnare (Active)|Ensnare]] +1, [[Item Qualities#Ion (Passive)|Ion]], [[Item Qualities#Knockdown (Active)|Knockdown]], [[Item Qualities#Pierce (Passive)|Pierce]] +2, [[Item Qualities#Stun (Active)|Stun]] +2, and/or [[Item Qualities#Stun damage (Passive)|Stun Damage]] qualities to the explosive, upgrading the difficulty of the check once for each quality chosen. Any number of qualities may be chosen, but each ranked option may only be chosen three times.
