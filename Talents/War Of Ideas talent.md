## War Of Ideas

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Propagandist (reSpec)]]

When a foe makes a social check to persuade a third party, the character may suffer strain up to their ranks of [[Warfare (Int)|Knowledge (Warfare)]] to add that many [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback dice]] to the check. If the check fails, upgrade the ability of all social checks the character or their allies make that target or involve the party or the foe once, for the remainder of the session. Multiple uses of this talent are not cumulative, this talent only ever upgrades those checks once.
