## Disorient

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Force-Sensitive Outcast]] (2), [[Gadgeteer]] (1), [[Scout]] (2), [[Trailblazer]] (2)

After hitting with a combat check, the character may spend two [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results to disorient their foe. Disoriented targets add a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice to all skill checks. The target is disoriented for a number of rounds equal to the character's ranks in Disorient.
