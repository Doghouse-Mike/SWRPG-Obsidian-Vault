## Valuable Facts

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Analyst]], [[Investigator]], [[Padawan]], [[Sage]]

Once per encounter, the character may take a Valuable Facts action, making an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Category - Knowledge Skills|Knowledge check]]** (the GM and player should determine which [[Category - Knowledge Skills|Knowledge skill]] is most applicable in the given situation). If successful, the character may add a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result to any one check made by an allied character subsequently during the encounter.

The subsequent check should relate in some way to the facts the character learned, or the player should come up with an explanation of why the information their character learned is instrumental to the success of the ally's check.
