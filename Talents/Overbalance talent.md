## Overbalance

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Martial Artist]], [[Warden]]

Whenever an enemy engaged with the character makes a combat check, after the attack is resolved, the character may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result or three [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results to stagger the attacker until the end of the attacker's next turn.

[^1]: [[Category - Bounty Hunter|No Disintegrations]]: *A Sourcebook for Bounty Hunters* (Page 35)
[^2]: [[Category - Guardian|Keeping the Peace]]: *A Sourcebook for Guardians* (Page 31)
