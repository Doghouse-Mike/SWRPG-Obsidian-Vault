## Personal Body Guard

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Bodyguard (reSpec)]]

Once per encounter before an ally within short range is targeted by a combat check, engage that ally and perform the Body Guard maneuver as an out of turn incidental.

### Improved

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Bodyguard (reSpec)]]

Once per session when performing Body Guard, spend a Destiny Point. All attacks targeting one guarded ally target the character instead until the end of the character's next turn, and the effects of Body Guard affect the character instead.
