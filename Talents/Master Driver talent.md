## Master Driver

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Driver]]

Once per round when piloting a vehicle using the [[Piloting - Planetary (Ag)|Piloting (Planetary)]] skill, the character may voluntarily suffer two strain to perform any action as a maneuver instead.
