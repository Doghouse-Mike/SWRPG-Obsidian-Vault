## Merciful Shot

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Marshal (reSpec)]]

Before making a non-[[Gunnery (Ag)|Gunnery]] ranged attack, suffer 2 strain to inflict damage as strain instead of wounds. If using a weapon with the [[Item Qualities#Stun damage (Passive)|Stun Damage]] quality or in [[Item Qualities#Stun Setting (Active)|Stun Setting]], add two [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] dice to the attack. Some weapons are unable to be used non-lethally and cannot benefit from Merciful Shot, per GM discretion.

### Improved

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Entrepreneur (reSpec)|Marshal (reSpec)]]

While making an attack benefiting from Merciful Shot, spend four [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result to stagger or immobilize the target until the end of their next turn.
