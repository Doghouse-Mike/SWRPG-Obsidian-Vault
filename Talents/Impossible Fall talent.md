## Impossible Fall

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Sentry]]

*Force talent.* Once per session, when the character is falling, \[they\] may perform the Impossible Fall incidental. \[They\] make a Force power check and may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]] to land somewhere safe, such as on a pile of empty boxes in a street or a muddy spot in the middle of a lava field. The character may then spend additional [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|points]] to reduce the effects of the fall by one range band, and may do so multiple times.

[^1]: [[Category - Sentinel|Endless Vigil]]: *A Sourcebook for Sentinels* (Page 31)
