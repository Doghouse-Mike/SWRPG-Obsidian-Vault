## Survival of the Fittest

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Hermit]]

*Force talent.* Once per session, when making a single check the character may treat \[their\] Force rating as being equal to \[their\] ranks in [[Survival (Cun)|Survival]].
