## Imperial Valor

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Imperial Loyalist]]

Once per encounter, take the Imperial Valor action and make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Discipline (Will)|Discipline check]]**. If successful, this character plus one ally per [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] result within short range increases their strain threshold by 1 for the rest of the encounter.

### Improved

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Imperial Loyalist]]

May spend two [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results to increase strain threshold of one ally affected by Imperial Valor by 1 for the remainder of the encounter.
