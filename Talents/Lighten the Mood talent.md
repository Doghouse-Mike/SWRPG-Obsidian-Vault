## Lighten the Mood

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Performer (reSpec)]]

When recovering a the end of an encounter, the character may make an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Charm (Pr)|Charm check]]** instead of rolling to recover strain. On success, allies up to Presence add a [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] result to their recover rolls. Spend two [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results to have an affected ally add an additional [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] result to their check for each two [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results spent, and spend a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result to recover the same amount of strain as one affected ally.
