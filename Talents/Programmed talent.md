## Programmed

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[First Order Defector]], [[First Order Loyalist]]

When making a check, the character may suffer strain up to twice ranks in [[Discipline (Will)|Discipline]] to add a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to the next check for each 2 strain suffered this way.

### Improved

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[First Order Loyalist]]

When taking the Programmed incidental, may flip a destiny point to instead upgrade the ability of the next check for each 2 strain suffered this way.
