## Terrify

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Aggressor]]

*Force talent.* The character may take a Terrify action, making a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Coercion (Will)|Coercion check]]** and rolling [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] dice no greater than Force rating as part of the check. If successful, one target per [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] result within medium range of the character is disoriented until the end of the next round. The character may spend two [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results to increase the duration of disorientation for all affected targets by one round, and may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]] to immobilize an affected target until the end of the next round. A character who has purchased this talent automatically gains 1 Conflict at the beginning of a game session.

### Improved

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Aggressor]]

*Force talent.* The difficulty of the Terrify action decreases to **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]]**. In addition, the character may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result generated on the check to stagger an affected target until the end of the next round.
