## Disciplined Soul

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Force Adherent]]

The character may spend one Destiny Point after making a [[Discipline (Will)|Discipline]] check to spend a number of [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results not exceeding the character's ranks in [[Lore (Int)|Knowledge (Lore)]] as [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results.

[^1]: [[Category - Early Rebellion|Dawn of Rebellion]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 112)
