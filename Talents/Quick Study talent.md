## Quick Study

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Scholar (reSpec)]] (2)

When making a skill check with a skill they possess no ranks in, suffer a number of strain no greater thank ranks in Quick Study to add an equal number of [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] dice to the check.
