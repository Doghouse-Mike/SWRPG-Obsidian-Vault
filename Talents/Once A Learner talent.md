## Once a Learner

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Teacher]]

*Force talent.* The character may perform the Once a Learner action. The character suffers 4 strain, increases the Force rating of one ally within short range by an amount equal to the character's current Force rating until the end of the character's next turn. The character may not make any Force Power checks until after \[their\] next turn.

[^1]: [[Category - Consular|Disciples of Harmony]]: *A Sourcebook for Consulars* (Page 29)
