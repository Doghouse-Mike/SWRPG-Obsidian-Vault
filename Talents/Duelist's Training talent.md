## Duelist's Training

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Makashi Duelist]]

The character adds a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] dice to \[their\] [[Melee (Br)|Melee]] and [[Lightsaber (Br)|Lightsaber]] checks when engaged with only a single opponent. A single minion group counts as multiple opponents.

[^1]: [[Category - Way of the Force|***Star Wars*** **Force and Destiny**]] Roleplaying Game: *Core Rulebook* (Page 142)
