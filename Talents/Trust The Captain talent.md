## Trust the Captain

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Ship Captain]] (2)

The character may take the Trust the Captain action by making a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Leadership (Pr)|Leadership check]]**. If successful, for the remainder of the encounter all allies within short range increase their ranks in [[Discipline (Will)|Discipline]] by an amount equal to the character's ranks in Trust the Captain.

[^1]: [[Category - Early Rebellion|Dawn of Rebellion]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 116)
