## Encoded Communiqué

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Analyst]]

Upgrade the difficulty of checks to decrypt the character's coded messages without the proper cipher a number of times equal to the character's ranks in [[Computers (Int)|Computers]].

[^1]: [[Category - Diplomat|Desperate Allies]]: *A Sourcebook for Diplomats* (Page 33)
