## Coordination Dodge

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Martial Artist]], [[Performer]]

When targeted by a combat check, the character may spend one Destiny Point to add a number of [[Narrative Dice#Failure|failure]] results equal to \[their\] ranks in [[Coordination (Ag)|Coordination]] to the check.

[^2]: [[Category - Colonist|Far Horizons]]: *A Sourcebook for Colonists* (Page 32)
