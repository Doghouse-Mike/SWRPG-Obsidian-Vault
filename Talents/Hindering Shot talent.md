## Hindering Shot

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Operator]]

The character may voluntarily increase the difficulty of a [[Gunnery (Ag)|Gunnery]] combat check once to inflict a hindering shot on a vehicle. If \[they\] succeed and deal damage to the target vehicle's hull trauma threshold, the vehicle suffers system strain equal to its current speed whenever it moves until the end of the encounter.

[^1]: [[Category - Bounty Hunter|No Disintegrations]]: *A Sourcebook for Bounty Hunters* (Page 34)
