## Master Slicer

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Slicer]]

Once per round, the character may perform a Master Slicer incidental to suffer two strain and decrease the difficulty of the next [[Computers (Int)|Computers]] or other slicing-related check by one, to a minimum of **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Easy difficulty]]**.
