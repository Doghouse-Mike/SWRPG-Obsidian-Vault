## Rule By Fear

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Blademaster]] (2), [[Lord]] (2)

The character removes a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die per rank of Rule by Fear from their [[Leadership (Pr)|Leadership]] and [[Coercion (Will)|Coercion]] checks.
