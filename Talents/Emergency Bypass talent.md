## Emergency Bypass

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out-of-turn)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Mechanic (reSpec)]]

Once per encounter, when an engaged starship or vehicle suffers a critical hit, spend a Destiny Point and have the engaged starship or vehicle suffer system strain equal to the severity of that critical hit to choose a different critical hit of the same severity.
