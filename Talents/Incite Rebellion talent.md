## Incite Rebellion

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Agitator]]

Once per game session, the character can take the Incite Rebellion action to make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Coercion (Will)|Coercion check]]**. If successful, a number of beings up to \[their\] ranks in [[Coercion (Will)|Coercion]] become unhappy and try to take action against an organization or authority with power over them until the end of the encounter. This could be due to something the character did or said, or just because the beings were already unhappy with their position.

[^1]: [[Category - Rebel Ranks|***Star Wars*** **Age of Rebellion**]] Roleplaying Game: *Core Rulebook* (Page 149)
