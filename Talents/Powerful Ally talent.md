## Powerful Ally

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Master]]

*Force talent.* The character may spend a Destiny Point to use the basic version of a Force power they have not purchased or to apply the effects of a single unpurchased control upgrade to a power they have purchased.

[^1]: [[Category - Clone Wars End|Collapse of the Republic]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 42)
