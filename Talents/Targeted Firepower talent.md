## Targeted Firepower

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Imperial Academy Cadet]]

Once per session the character may take the targeted Firepower action by identifying one detected enemy character, vehicle, starship, or structure and make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Warfare (Int)|Knowledge (Warfare) check]]**. If successful, for the remainder of the encounter all allies within short range of the character add an automatic [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result to their combat attacks against that target equal to [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] results that the character gained in the check.

[^1]: [[Category - Early Rebellion|Dawn of Rebellion]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 116)
