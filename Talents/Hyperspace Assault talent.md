## Hyperspace Assault

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Republic Navy Officer]]

The character may use [[Astrogation (Int)|Astrogation]] or [[Warfare (Int)|Knowledge (Warfare)]] to determine their Initiative during an encounter in which they are piloting or commanding a starship. If they do so, they may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result from the check to choose the starting locations of all allied ships in the encounter.

[^1]: [[Category - Clone Wars Begin|Rise of the Separatists]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 39)
