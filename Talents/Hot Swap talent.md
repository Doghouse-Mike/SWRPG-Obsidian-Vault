## Hot Swap

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Gadgeteer (reSpec)]] (2)

After changing weapons, add a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die per rank of Hot Swap to the next attack made with weapons not yet used in this encounter. "Changing weapons" is inclusive of adding a weapon for two weapon fighting, utilizing thrown weapons, armor-mounted weapons, improvised weapons, and unarmed strikes.

[^1]: [[Category - ReSpecialized|The reSpecialized Project]]'s Gadgeteer Folio
