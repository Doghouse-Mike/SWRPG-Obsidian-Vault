## Conditioned

Source: [^1][^2][^3][^4][^5]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[ARC Trooper]] (1), [[Ataru Striker|Ataru Strike]] (2), [[Death Watch Warrior]] (2), [[Hermit]] (2), [[Imperial Academy Cadet]] (2), [[Instructor]] (2), [[Sentry]] (1), [[Shien Expert]] (1), [[Shii-Cho Knight]] (1), [[Vanguard]] (2)

The character removes a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice per rank of Conditioned from their [[Athletics (Br)|Athletics]] and [[Coordination (Ag)|Coordination]] checks. They reduce the damage and strain suffered from falling by 1 per rank of Conditioned.

```
While the Force and Destiny Roleplaying Game: Core Rulebook refers to "per rank of Coordination", the Errata (Page 1) corrects this to "per rank of Conditioned".
```

```
The following is a homebrew expansion of this talent from a community-produced homebrew sourcebook!
```

### Improved

Source: [^6]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Archaeologist (reSpec)]]

Once per session while falling, spend a Destiny point to reduce range bands fallen by ranks of Conditioned, to a minimum of short. This affects the effective damage and strain suffered from the fall, not the fall distance itself.
