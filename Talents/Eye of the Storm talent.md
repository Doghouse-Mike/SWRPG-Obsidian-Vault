## Eye of the Storm

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Maneuver)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Martial Artist (reSpec)]]

Suffer 1 strain and perform this maneuver to have the next [[Brawl (Br)|Brawl]] attack they make this turn gains a [[Item Qualities#Blast (Active)|Blast]] quality with a rating equal to their Brawn rating plus ranks of [[Discipline (Will)|Discipline]]. When [[Item Qualities#Blast (Active)|Blast]] is activated on this attack, it only affects foes.
