## Falling Avalance

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Armorer]], [[Shien Expert]]

*Force talent.* Once per round the character may suffer 2 strain to add additional damage equal to Brawn to one hit of a successful Lightsaber combat check.

[^1]: [[Category - Way of the Force|***Star Wars*** **Force and Destiny**]] Roleplaying Game: *Core Rulebook* (Page 142)
