## Natural Driver

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Driver]]

Once per game session, the character may reroll any one [[Piloting - Planetary (Ag)|Piloting (Planetary)]] or [[Gunnery (Ag)|Gunnery]] check.
