## More Machine than Man

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Cyber Tech]] (4)

Each rank of More Machine than Man increases the character's cybernetic implant cap by 1.

```
The following is a homebrew expansion of this talent from a community-produced homebrew sourcebook!
```

### Improved

Source: [^2]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Cyber Tech (reSpec)]]

Before making a check, suffer strain up to half of the the number of character's installed cybernetics, rounded up, to add that many [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] dice to check. The player must specify how one or more of their cybernetics are aiding them for this check.
