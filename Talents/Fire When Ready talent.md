## Fire When Ready

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Republic Navy Officer]]

The character may take the Fire When Ready action, making a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Leadership (Pr)|Leadership check]]**. If the check succeeds, the character chooses a number of crew aboard their ship equal to their Presence. Those crew members downgrade the difficulty of their next [[Gunnery (Ag)|Gunnery]] check once, plus one time for every two additional [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] results on the check.

[^1]: [[Category - Clone Wars Begin|Rise of the Separatists]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 39)
