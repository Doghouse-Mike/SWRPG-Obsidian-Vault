## Jetpack Expertise

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Death Watch Warrior]]

The character may spend an [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result from checks they make to remove 1 system strain from a jetpack they are equipped with. (They may choose this effect multiple times when spending [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] \[results\].)

[^1]: [[Category - Clone Wars End|Collapse of the Republic]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 41)
