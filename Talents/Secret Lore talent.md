## Secret Lore

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Magus]] (2)

The character removes [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die per rank of Secret Lore from their [[Lore (Int)|Knowledge (Lore)]] checks. The character reduces the difficulty of all [[Lore (Int)|Knowledge (Lore)]] checks they make by 1 (this does not increase with additional ranks of Secret Lore).

[^1]: [[Category - Mystic|Unlimited Power]]: *A Sourcebook for Mystics* (Page 33)
