## Heightened Awareness

Source: [^0-1][^1-2][^2-3]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Species:** [[Kalleran]]
- **Trees:** [[Big-Game Hunter]], [[Protector]], [[Scout]]

Allies within short range of the character add a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to their [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] and [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]] checks. Allies engaged with \[them\] add two [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] dice instead.

```
While the Force and Destiny Roleplaying Game: Core Rulebook shows this as "Ranked: Yes", the Errata (Page 1) corrects this to "Ranked: No".
```
