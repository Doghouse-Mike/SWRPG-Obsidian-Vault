## Exit Strategy

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Thief (reSpec)]]

Once per session, make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance check]]** to notice a discrete path of egress. On success, add a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to all checks the character or allies with medium range make to leave the encounter. This includes attacks against characters who intentionally impede their escape.
