## Hunter

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Hunter]] (2), [[Survivalist]] (2)

The character adds a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die per rank of Hunter to all skill checks when interacting with wild beasts and animals, including combat checks. The character adds +10 per rank of Hunter to all of \[their\] Critical Injury rolls against animals.

### Improved

Source: [^0-3]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Big Game Hunter (reSpec)]]

After the character inflicts a critical injury on or incapacitates a foe with an attack or another effect, the character may perform a maneuver as an incidental. They may not perform more than 2 maneuvers on their turn, but may make a maneuver out-of-turn.

#### Supreme

Source: [^0-3]

- **Activation:** Active (Maneuver)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Big Game Hunter (reSpec)]]

Before making a combat check against a non-sentient creature as a maneuver, suffer strain up to the target's silhouette to reduce that attack's critical rating by the same amount, to a minimum of 1.
