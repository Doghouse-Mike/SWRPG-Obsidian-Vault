## Mission Critical

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Clone Pilot]]

Once per session after rolling a [[Piloting - Planetary (Ag)|Piloting (Planetary)]] or [[Piloting - Space (Ag)|Piloting (Space)]] check but before resolving it, the character may spend a Destiny Point to add [[Narrative Dice#Success|successes]] or [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantages]] to the results equal to their ranks in that skill. (The character may divide the results between successes and advantages in any combination.)

[^1]: [[Category - Clone Wars Begin|Rise of the Separatists]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 40)
