## Time to Go

Source: [^0-1][^1-2]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Demolitionist]], [[Saboteur]]

The character may spend 1 Destiny point to perform a Move maneuver as an incidental to attempt to move into cover or out of the blast range of a weapon or explosion. This may be performed out of turn. This incidental occurs after the character determines he would be hit by the blast, but before damage is applied.

### Improved

Source: [^0-1][^1-2]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Demolitionist]], [[Saboteur]]

When activating Time to Go, the character may allow one ally \[they\] are engaged with to also perform an out of turn Move maneuver as an incidental to attempt to move into cover or out of the blast range of a weapon or explosion. This incidental occurs after the ally determines \[they\] would be hit by the blast, but before damage is applied.
