## Master of Arms

Source: [^0-1][^1-2]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Gadgeteer (reSpec)]], [[Modder (reSpec)]]

Once per encounter, make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics check]]**. On success, a [[Item Qualities|weapon quality]] (excluding [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]], [[Item Qualities#Concussive (Active)|Concussive]], or [[Item Qualities#Linked (Active)|Linked]]) of a weapon within engaged range has its rating increased by 2 until the end of the encounter. The GM may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result to have an affected weapon malfunction at the end of the encounter; it is considered broken until the character spends six hours making an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics check]]** to repair it.

### Improved

Source: [^0-1][^1-2]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Gadgeteer (reSpec)]], [[Modder (reSpec)]]

Once per encounter, when using the Master of Arms action, spend two [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result to immediately take an additional action.

### Supreme

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Gadgeteer (reSpec)]]

When making the Master of Arms action, choose any number of additional carried weapons to increase an existing quality's rating of each of those weapons by 2, and may choose a different quality for each affected weapon. Upgrade the difficulty once for each chosen weapon beyond the first. Each of the chosen qualities cost one less [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result to activate, to a minimum of 1. Qualities that do not have a rating, such as [[Item Qualities#Auto-fire (Active)|Auto-Fire]], are not applicable for the reduction.
