## It's A Steal!

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Trader (reSpec)]]

When selling damaged or misrepresented goods, the character may make an **opposed [[Deception (Cun)|Deception]] vs. [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance check]]**, and treat their value as if they were undamaged and genuine. Add a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die for each step of damage the item has, and upgrade the difficulty of the check if the item is not genuine.
