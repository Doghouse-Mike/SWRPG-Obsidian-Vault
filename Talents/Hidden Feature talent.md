## Hidden Feature

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Modder (reSpec)]]

Once per session as an incidental, while the character is aboard a vessel they created or are intimately familiar with, they may activate a previously unknown minor feature of the vessel as if a Destiny Point had been spent, per GM discretion. This feature lasts until the end of the encounter. At any future point, any character may reactivate this feature by spending a Destiny Point.
