## Overstocked Ammo

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Rigger]] (1)

The character increases the value of the [[Item Qualities#Limited Ammo (Passive)|Limited Ammo]] quality of any weapons mounted on \[their\] [[Signature Vehicle talent|Signature Vehicle]] by 1 per rank of Overstocked Ammo.

[^1]: [[Category - Ace|Stay on Target]]: *A Sourcebook for Aces* (Page 33)
