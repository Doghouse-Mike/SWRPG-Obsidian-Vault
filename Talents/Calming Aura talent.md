## Calming Aura

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Arbiter]], [[Healer]]

*Force talent.* When an opponent targets the character with a Force power, after the opponent generates [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|points]], reduce the total [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|points]] generated by one, to a minimum of 0.

### Improved

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Maneuver)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Healer]]

*Force talent.* The character may spend a maneuver and suffer 2 strain to extend the effects of Calming Aura to a number of allies within short range equal to \[their\] Willpower. This lasts until the beginning of \[their\] next turn.
