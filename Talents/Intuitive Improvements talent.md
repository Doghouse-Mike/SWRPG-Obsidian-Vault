## Intuitive Improvements

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Artisan]]

*Force talent.* When making a check to repair or craft a non-starship or vehicle item, the character may add [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] dice no greater than Force rating to the check. The character may spend two [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|points]] to permanently increase the number of hard points the item has by 1, to a maximum of 2 additional hardpoints. An item may only be improved in this way once.

[^1]: [[Category - Way of the Force|***Star Wars*** **Force and Destiny**]] Roleplaying Game: *Core Rulebook* (Page 145)
