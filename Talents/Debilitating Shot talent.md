## Debilitating Shot

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Gunner]], [[Operator]], [[Shipwright]]

Upon making a successful attack with a starship or vehicle weapon, may spend two [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results to reduce the maximum speed of the target by 1 (to a minimum of 1) until the end of the next round. If the starship or vehicle was traveling its maximum speed, it has its current speed reduced to the new maximum.

[^2]: [[Category - Rebel Ranks|***Star Wars*** **Age of Rebellion**]] Roleplaying Game: *Core Rulebook* (Page 145)
