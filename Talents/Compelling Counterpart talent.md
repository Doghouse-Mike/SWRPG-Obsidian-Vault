## Compelling Counterpart

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Droid Tech (reSpec)]]

Choose one social skill per Droid Companion. While engaged with a Droid Companion, add an [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result to all checks using the chosen skill for that companion.
