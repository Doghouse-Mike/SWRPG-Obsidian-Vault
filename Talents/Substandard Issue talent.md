## Substandard Issue

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Quartermaster (reSpec)]]

Once per encounter, the character may make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]]** or **[[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance check]]** to determine that a piece of standard-issue enemy equipment is ill-suited, poorly maintained, or otherwise substandard. On success, a number of enemies equal to [[Narrative Dice#Success|success results]] add two [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat results]] to checks that utilize the specified equipment. The character may spend four [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage results]] or a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph result]] to have this affect all present enemies in the current scene.
