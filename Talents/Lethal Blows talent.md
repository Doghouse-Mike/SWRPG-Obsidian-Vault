## Lethal Blows

Source: [^1][^2][^3][^4][^5]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Species:** [[Anx]]
- **Trees:** [[Assassin]] (3), [[Clone Veteran]] (2), [[Executioner]] (3), [[Gunslinger]] (2), [[Juyo Berserker]] (2), [[Marauder]] (3), [[Mercenary Soldier]] (1), [[Sharpshooter]] (2)
- **Items:** [[Hunting Barrel]] (Modification Option)

The character adds +10 per rank of Lethal Blows to any Critical Injury rolls inflicted on opponents.
