## Happy To Help

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Charmer (reSpec)]]

When providing or accepting assistance, add a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to the check in addition to the normal effects of assistance.

### Improved

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Charmer (reSpec)]]

Once per encounter, when assisting or being assisted, may choose to have Happy To Help add a [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] result to the check instead of a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die.
