## Rain of Death

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Active (Maneuver)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Heavy]]

The character may perform the Rain of Death maneuver. If \[they\] do so, when \[they\] make an attack in the same turn \[they\] do not increase the difficulty of the attack due to the [[Item Qualities#Auto-fire (Active)|Auto-fire]] quality.
