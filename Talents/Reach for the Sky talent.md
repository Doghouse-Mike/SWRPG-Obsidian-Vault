## Reach for the Sky

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Marshal (reSpec)]]

Once per encounter, the character may make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Coercion (Will)|Coercion]]** or **[[Leadership (Pr)|Leadership check]]**. On success, a number of foes up to ranks in Willpower suffer 1 strain whenever they move until the end of the encounter. Spend a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result to force an affected character to perform a move maneuver in a direction of the character's choice.

[^1]: [[Category - ReSpecialized|The reSpecialized Project]]'s Marshal Folio
