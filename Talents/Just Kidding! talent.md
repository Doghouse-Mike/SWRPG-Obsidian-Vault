## Just Kidding!

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Charmer]]

Once per round as an incidental, when the character or any of \[their\] allies in short range generates a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] on a social skill check, the character may spend one Destiny Point to convert a single [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] generated on the check to a [[Narrative Dice#Failure|failure]] result. When \[they\] do this, \[they\] must explain what \[they\] say or does to smooth things over after the verbal misstep.

[^1]: [[Category - Smuggler|Fly Casual]]: *A Sourcebook for Smugglers* (Page 33)
