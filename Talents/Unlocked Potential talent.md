## Unlocked Potential

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Modder (reSpec)]]

Once per session, the character or an ally within short range may spend three [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result from a check using a modded vehicle or piece of equipment to temporarily install a uninstalled Mod of an installed attachment of that item or vehicle until the end of the session (including a previously failed mod option). Additional [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] results may be spent from this or a subsequent check using the weapon or vehicle this session, paying normal modding costs, to unlock it permanently.
