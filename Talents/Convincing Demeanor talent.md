## Convincing Demeanor

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Species:** [[Bothan]]
- **Trees:** [[Agitator]] (2), [[Force Sensitive Exile]] (1), [[Gambler]] (2), [[Performer]] (1), [[Scoundrel]] (3), [[Sleeper Agent]] (2), [[Trader]] (1)

The character removes a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die per rank of Convincing Demeanor from \[their\] [[Deception (Cun)|Deception]] and [[Skulduggery (Cun)|Skulduggery]] checks.
