## Defensive Circle

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Soresu Defender]]

*Force talent.* While wielding a lightsaber, the character may take the Defensive Circle action, making a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Lightsaber (Br)|Lightsaber (Intellect) check]]**. If successful, the character plus one ally per [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] result within short range gains ranged and melee defense X until the beginning of the character's next turn. X equals 1, plus 1 for every two [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results.

[^1]: [[Category - Way of the Force|***Star Wars*** **Force and Destiny**]] Roleplaying Game: *Core Rulebook* (Page 141)
