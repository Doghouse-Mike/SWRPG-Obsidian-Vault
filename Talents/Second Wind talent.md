## Second Wind

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Courier]] (2), [[Enforcer]] (1), [[Mercenary Soldier]] (2), [[Peacekeeper]] (3), [[Performer]] (1), [[Recruit]] (3), [[Saboteur]] (2), [[Shii-Cho Knight]] (2)

Once per encounter, the character may use a Second Wind incidental to recover an amount of strain equal to \[their\] ranks in Second Wind.
