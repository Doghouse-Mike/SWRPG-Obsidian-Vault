## Historian

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Archaeologist (reSpec)]] (2)

When recalling information about or interacting with ancient or abandoned places or things, the character may choose to add a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] or remove a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die from the check per rank of Historian

### Improved

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out-of-Turn)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Archaeologist (reSpec)]]

Once per session when interacting with ancient or abandoned places or things, the character may spend a Destiny Point to cancel a single [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result or [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results equal to ranks of Historian from a check the character or an ally makes.
