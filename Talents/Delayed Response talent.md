## Delayed Response

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Slicer (reSpec)]]

Once per session after being detected or discovered, spend a Destiny Point to delay alarm, pursuit or reprisal until the end of the character's next turn. How they are delayed is per GM discretion.
