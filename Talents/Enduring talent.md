## Enduring

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Species:** [[Droid]] (1), [[Hutt]] (1)
- **Trees:** [[Archaeologist]] (1), [[Bodyguard]] (1), [[Clone Trooper]] (1), [[Colossus]] (1), [[Demolitionist]] (1), [[Gunner]] (2), [[Hermit]] (1), [[Marauder]] (2), [[Mechanic]] (1), [[Pathfinder]] (1), [[Recruit]] (1), [[Survivalist]] (1), [[Retired Clone Trooper]] (1)

The character gains +1 soak value per rank of Enduring.
