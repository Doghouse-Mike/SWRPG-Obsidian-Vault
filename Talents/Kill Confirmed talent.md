## Kill Confirmed

Source: [^1-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Mercenary Soldier (reSpec)]]

Once per session, upgrade the difficulty of the next ranged attack the character makes once to have one hit of that attack ignore the target's soak. The Game Master may spend two [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result to have the weapon run out of ammo or otherwise malfunction.
