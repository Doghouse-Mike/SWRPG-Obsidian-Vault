## Reckless Charge

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Blademaster]] (1)

After using a maneuver to engage an adversary, the character may suffer 2 strain to use this talent. They then add two [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] and two [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results to the results of the next [[Brawl (Br)|Brawl]], [[Melee (Br)|Melee]], or [[Lightsaber (Br)|Lightsaber]] check they make this turn.
