## Diagnostician

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Doctor (reSpec)]]

Once per session, make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Medicine (Int)|Medicine check]]**. On success, the character (or player) details an observation they've made of a character within short range that reveals a medical condition they are suffering from. The Game Master may choose to impose negative effects on the diagnosed character.

[^1]: [[Category - ReSpecialized|The reSpecialized Project]]'s Doctor Folio
