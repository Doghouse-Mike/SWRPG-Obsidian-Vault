## Grapple

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Active (Maneuver)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Martial Artist]], [[Warden]]

Once per round, the character may perform the Grapple maneuver. Until the beginning of \[their\] next turn, enemies must spend two maneuvers instead of one maneuver to move from engaged range to short range of \[them\].

[^1]: [[Category - Bounty Hunter|No Disintegrations]]: *A Sourcebook for Bounty Hunters* (Page 34)
[^2]: [[Category - Guardian|Keeping the Peace]]: *A Sourcebook for Guardians* (Page 30)
