## Directive

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Maneuver)
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Droid Tech (reSpec)]] (2)

Once per round as a maneuver, suffer strain no more than ranks in Directive. Upgrade the ability of the check made by an allied droid within short range a number of times equal to strain suffered.

### Improved

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Cyber Tech (reSpec)|Droid Tech (reSpec)]]

Once per encounter when performing the Directive maneuver, may affect an additional allied droid per rank of Directive, and the range of Directive increases to medium.

#### Supreme

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Cyber Tech (reSpec)|Droid Tech (reSpec)]]

Once per session, choose a skill and make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Leadership (Pr)|Leadership check]]**. On success, a number of allied droids within medium range up to ranks of Directive may treat their ranks of that skill as equal the character's ranks until end of character's next turn. Spend a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result to extend this effect 1 round.
