## Meditative Trance

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Ascetic]]

*Force talent.* When suffocating, the character suffers 1 strain each round instead of 3. When exposed to vacuum, the character suffers 1 wound each round instead of 3.

[^1]: [[Category - Consular|Disciples of Harmony]]: *A Sourcebook for Consulars* (Page 29)
