## Fight What You Hate

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[First Order Defector]]

Once per encounter, the character may suffer 3 strain and perform the Fight What You Fate Incidental; choose an adversary within long range. Whenever the character or the adversary makes a combat check against the other, decrease the critical rating of the weapon(s) used by 1.

### Improved

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[First Order Loyalist|First Order Defector]]

Whenever The characters affected by Fight What You Hate make a combat check against the other, the check generates two automatic [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] results, and each two [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results generated is automatically converted into a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result.
