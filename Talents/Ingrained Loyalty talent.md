## Ingrained Loyalty

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Maneuver)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Clone Commander]]

When the character makes a [[Leadership (Pr)|Leadership]] check targeting one or more clones and no other characters, they may change the result of one [[Narrative Dice#Challenge (Red)|challenge]] dice to any nonblank face.

[^1]: [[Category - Clone Wars End|Collapse of the Republic]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 40)
