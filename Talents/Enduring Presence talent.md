## Enduring Presence

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Clone Commander]]

Once per session, the character may upgrade the ability of a Mass Combat check (if they are the commander of the acting force) or the difficulty of a Mass Combat check (if they are commander of the enemy force) even if the character is not present or is prevented from communicating with their troops.

[^1]: [[Category - Clone Wars End|Collapse of the Republic]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 39)
