## Witchcraft

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Nightsister]]

The character gains a Force rating of 1. If the character already has a Force rating of 1 or higher, there is no effect.

[^1]: [[Category - Clone Wars End|Collapse of the Republic]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 44)
