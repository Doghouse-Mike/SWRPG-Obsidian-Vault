## Make Your Own Luck

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Gambler (reSpec)]]

Once per session, make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Cool (Pr)|Cool check]]**. On success, allies equal to ranks of [[Cool (Pr)|Cool]] may reroll 1 positive die on the next check they make, plus one additional die for every two [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] results past the first. Spend two [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result to allow one of those allies to reroll positive or negative instead.
