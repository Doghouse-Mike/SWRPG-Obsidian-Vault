## Prophetic Aim

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Warleader]]

*Force talent.* While the character is benefiting from an aim maneuver, a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result from \[their\] [[Ranged - Heavy (Ag)|Ranged (Heavy)]] and [[Ranged - Light (Ag)|Ranged (Light)]] checks cannot be spent to cause \[their\] attacks to hit any of \[their\] allies who are engaged with the target.

[^1]: [[Category - Guardian|Keeping the Peace]]: *A Sourcebook for Guardians* (Page 31)
