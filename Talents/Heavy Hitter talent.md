## Heavy Hitter

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Heavy]]

Once per session, the character may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result on a successful [[Ranged - Heavy (Ag)|Ranged (Heavy)]] or [[Gunnery (Ag)|Gunnery]] check to add the [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] 1 quality to the attack, or increase an existing [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] rating by 1.

[^2]: [[Category - Hired Gun|Dangerous Covenants]]: *A Sourcebook for Hired Guns* (Page 32)
