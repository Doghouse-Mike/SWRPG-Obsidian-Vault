## Cease Hostilities!

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Temple Guardian]]

*Force talent.* Once per encounter, as an action, may make an opposed [[Coercion (Will)|Coercion]] vs. [[Discipline (Will)|Discipline]] check to stagger an enemy Rival or Minion target within short range until the end of their next turn.

### Improved

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Temple Guardian]]

*Force talent.* May use the Cease Hostilities talent twice per encounter, and may spend a Destiny Point to target an enemy Nemesis instead.
