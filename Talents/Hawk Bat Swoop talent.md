## Hawk Bat Swoop

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Ataru Striker]]

*Force talent.* The character may take a Hawk Bat Swoop action, performing a [[Lightsaber (Br)|Lightsaber]] (Agility) melee combat check against one target within short range and adding [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] dice no greater than Force rating to the pool. The character may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]] before resolving the success or failure of the check to engage the target immediately as an incidental, and may spend [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|points]] to add [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results to the check. If the character cannot move to engage the target, the attack automatically misses.

[^1]: [[Category - Way of the Force|***Star Wars*** **Force and Destiny**]] Roleplaying Game: *Core Rulebook* (Page 144)
