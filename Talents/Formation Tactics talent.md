## Formation Tactics

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Clone Commander]], [[Imperial Academy Cadet]]

The character may take the Formation Tactics action by making a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Leadership (Pr)|Leadership check]]**. If successful, they may choose a number of allies within short range equal to [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] results generated. The difficulty of attacks against these allies is upgraded once until the end of the character's next turn. The effects of this action do not stack.

### Improved

Source: [^3][^4]

- **Activation:** Active (Maneuver)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Clone Commander]], [[Imperial Academy Cadet]]

The difficulty of taking the Formation Tactics action is reduced to **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average]]** instead of **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard]]**. The character may also spend a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result or six [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results to have the effect last until end of encounter.
