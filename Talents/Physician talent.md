## Physician

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Healer]] (3), [[Protector]] (1)

When this character makes a [[Medicine (Int)|Medicine]] check to help a character heal wounds, the target heals one additional strain per rank of Physician.

[^1]: [[Category - Way of the Force|***Star Wars*** **Force and Destiny**]] Roleplaying Game: *Core Rulebook* (Page 149)
