## Bound Together

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Maneuver)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Master]]

*Force talent.* The character may perform the Bound Together maneuver. Until the start of the character's next turn, when the character or an ally within short range suffers strain involuntarily, they reduce the amount suffered by the current number of light side Destiny Points in the destiny pool.

[^1]: [[Category - Clone Wars End|Collapse of the Republic]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 37)
