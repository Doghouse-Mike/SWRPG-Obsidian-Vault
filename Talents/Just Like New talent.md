## Just Like New

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Outlaw Tech (reSpec)]]

Spend a Destiny Point to treat a non-destroyed piece of equipment within medium range as if it isn't damaged until the end of the character's next turn.
