## Circle of Shelter

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Knight]], [[Protector]]

*Force talent.* When an ally engaged with the character suffers a hit from a combat check, the character may use a [[Parry talent|Parry]] or [[Reflect talent|Reflect]] incidental to reduce the damage the hit deals (even though the character is not the target of the combat check).

[^2]: [[Category - Way of the Force|***Star Wars*** **Force and Destiny**]] Roleplaying Game: *Core Rulebook* (Page 140)
