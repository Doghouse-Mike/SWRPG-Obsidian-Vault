## Hunter's Quarry

Source: [^0-1][^1-2]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Big-Game Hunter]], [[Executioner]]

The character may take the Hunter's Quarry action against an opponent within long range, making a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Survival (Cun)|Survival check]]**. If the check succeeds, upgrade the ability of all attacks made against the target by one until the end of the character's next turn.

### Improved

Source: [^0-1][^1-2]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Big-Game Hunter]], [[Executioner]]

The character can choose to suffer 2 strain to perform the Hunter's Quarry action as a maneuver instead.
