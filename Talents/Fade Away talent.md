## Fade Away

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Thief (reSpec)]]

Once per encounter, when targeted by a combat check, spend a Destiny Point to add [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice equal to the character's ranks in [[Stealth (Ag)|Stealth]]. If that attack fails, the character may not be targeted by attacks until the beginning of their next turn.
