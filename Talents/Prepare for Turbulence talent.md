## Prepare for Turbulence

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Operator (reSpec)]]

Once per encounter, make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Astrogation (Int)|Astrogation check]]**. On success, the character notices or navigates towards a moderate (two [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice) navigation hazard at close range from their vehicle. Spend three [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result for a severe (three [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice) navigational hazard. The nature, size, and duration of the hazard is determined by the GM.
