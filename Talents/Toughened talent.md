The character increases \[their\] wound threshold by two per rank of Toughened.

### Improved

Source: [^4]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Colossus]]

Once per session, the character may heal a number of wounds equal to their ranks in Toughened.
