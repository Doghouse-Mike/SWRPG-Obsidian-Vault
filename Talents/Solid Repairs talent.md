## Solid Repairs

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Artisan]] (3), [[Commodore]] (3), [[Mechanic]] (4), [[Outlaw Tech]] (1), [[Shipwright]] (2), [[Starfighter Ace]] (2)

Whenever the character repairs hull trauma on a starship or vehicle, \[they\] repair one additional hull trauma per rank of Solid Repairs.

```
The following is a homebrew expansion of this talent from a community-produced homebrew sourcebook!
```

### Improved

Source: [^4]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Mechanic (reSpec)]]

Once per encounter, the character may make a [[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics check]] to increase the armor of an engaged starship or vehicle by 1 until the end of the encounter. Spend three [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result to have that vessel recover hull trauma equal to the character's ranks of Solid Repairs.
