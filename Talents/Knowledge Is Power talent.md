## Knowledge is Power

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Advisor]], [[Magus]]

*Force talent.* Once per session, when making a single check, the character may treat \[their\] Force rating as being equal to \[their\] ranks in [[Lore (Int)|Knowledge (Lore)]].

```
While the Force and Destiny Roleplaying Game: Core Rulebook description for this talent begins with, "Force power", it is confirmed to be a "Force talent" by Table 4-1 as well as the two Mystic trees on which it appears.
```

[^1]: [[Category - Way of the Force|***Star Wars*** **Force and Destiny**]] Roleplaying Game: *Core Rulebook* (Page 146)
