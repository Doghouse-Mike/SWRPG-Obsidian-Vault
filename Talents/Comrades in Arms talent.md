## Comrades in Arms

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Retired Clone Trooper]]

Once per encounter, the character may take the Comrades in Arms action to make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Discipline (Will)|Discipline check]]**. If successful, the character plus one ally per [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] result within medium range gains +1 defense for the remainder of the encounter. Should an ally move outside of medium range, that ally loses this benefit.

### Improved

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Retired Clone Trooper]]

Whenever the character takes the Comrades in Arms action, \[they\] may spend three [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result to also gain +1 soak or to give one affected ally +1 soak.
