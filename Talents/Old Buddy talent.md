## Old Buddy

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Seasoned Adventurer]]

Once per session, the character may make the Old Buddy action; Make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Streetwise (Cun)|Streetwise check]]** to establish a prior relationship with an NPC. The nature of this relationship is up to GM discretion. [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|Advantage]] or [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] results may be spent to insert that this NPC will go above and beyond for the character. [[Narrative Dice#Threat|Threat]] and [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] results my be spent to say that this relationship has soured significantly, even to the point of enemies.

[^0-1]: [[Category - Sequels|Fires of Resistance]]: *An Unofficial Era Sourcebook* (Page 11-12)
