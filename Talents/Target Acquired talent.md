## Target Acquired

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Gadgeteer (reSpec)]]

Make an **opposed [[Coercion (Will)|Coercion check]]** **vs. [[Discipline (Will)|Discipline check]]** against an adversary within medium range. On success, immobilize that adversary until the end of the next round. Spend two [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results to effect an additional foe within medium range of the character.

### Improved

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Gadgeteer (reSpec)]]

When taking the Target Acquired Action, add a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die per status effect affecting the target. Spend a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result to Stagger an affected foe. Disorient, burn, involuntary prone, immobilized, staggered, and suffering from [[Crippling Blow talent|Crippling Blow]] are all status effects, but GMs may include other non-named effects.
