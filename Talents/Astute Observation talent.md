## Astute Observation

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Scholar (reSpec)]] (2)

Once per encounter, add [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] dice equal to ranks in Astute Observation to an ally's check by communicating an observation the character has made, and establish how it assists the ally.

### Improved

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Scholar (reSpec)]]

Once per session, if an ally would fail a check, spend 1 Destiny Point to make a [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] check using the original difficulty. On success, the ally's check succeeds with the same results as the [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] check. When doing so, the character must justify how their observation of the situation actively turned a failure into a successful outcome.
