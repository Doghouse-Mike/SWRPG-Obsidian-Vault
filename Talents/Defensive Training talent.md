## Defensive Training

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Makashi Duelist]] (1), [[Niman Disciple]] (2), [[Shii-Cho Knight]] (1)

When the character wields a Lightsaber weapon, Melee weapon, or Brawl weapon, that weapon gains ranks in the [[Item Qualities#Defensive (Passive)|Defensive]] item quality equal to the character's ranks in Defensive Training (this replaces any ranks in [[Item Qualities#Defensive (Passive)|Defensive]] the weapon already has).

[^1]: [[Category - Way of the Force|***Star Wars*** **Force and Destiny**]] Roleplaying Game: *Core Rulebook* (Page 141)
