## It's A Bargain!

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Trader (reSpec)]]

When buying goods, may have the item become damaged 1 step to reduce the price by 20%. Whether an item can be used while damaged is at Game Master discretion.
