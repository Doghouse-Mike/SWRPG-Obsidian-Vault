## Dead to Rights

Source: [^0-1][^1-2]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Hotshot]], [[Pilot]]

The character may spend one Destiny Point to add additional damage equal to half \[their\] Agility rating (rounded up) to one hit of a successful attack made by ship or vehicle-mounted weaponry.

### Improved

Source: [^0-1][^1-2]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Hotshot]], [[Pilot]]

The character may spend one Destiny point to add additional damage equal to \[their\] Agility rating to one hit of a successful attack made by ship or vehicle-mounted weaponry. This replaces the normal ability for Dead to Rights.
