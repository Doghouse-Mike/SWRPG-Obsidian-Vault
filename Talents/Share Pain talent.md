## Share Pain

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Pathfinder]]

*Force talent.* When an animal bonded to the character by the [[Animal Empathy talent|Animal Empathy]] talent suffers wounds, the character may take the Share Pain incidental. \[They\] reduce the wounds the animal suffered by half (rounded up), then suffers a number of wounds equal to the number reduced.

[^1]: [[Category - Way of the Force|***Star Wars*** **Force and Destiny**]] Roleplaying Game: *Core Rulebook* (Page 151)
