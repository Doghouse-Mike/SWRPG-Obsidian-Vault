## Ravage

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Force-Sensitive Outcast]]

*Force talent.* Once per encounter, the character may suffer 3 strain to add [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] dice up to their current Force rating to a combat check targeting an engaged opponent. The character may spend [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|points]] to add [[Narrative Dice#Success|successes]] or [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantages]] to the results of the check. A character who has purchased this talent gains 1 Conflict at the beginning of each game session.

[^1]: [[Category - Clone Wars Begin|Rise of the Separatists]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 41)
