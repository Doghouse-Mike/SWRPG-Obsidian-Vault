## Discredit

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Advocate]]

Once per encounter, the character may take the Discredit action by make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Deception (Cun)|Deception check]]**. If \[they\] succeed, one chosen character upgrades the difficulty of social checks once, and one additional time for every two [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results on the [[Deception (Cun)|Deception]] check, until the end of the encounter.

[^1]: [[Category - Diplomat|Desperate Allies]]: *A Sourcebook for Diplomats* (Page 33)
