## Hard-Boiled

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Skip Tracer]] (3)

When making a check to recover strain at the end of an encounter, the character may spend an [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result to recover 1 wound. [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|Advantage]] results spent this way cannot exceed \[their\] ranks in Hard-Boiled.

[^1]: [[Category - Bounty Hunter|No Disintegrations]]: *A Sourcebook for Bounty Hunters* (Page 34)
