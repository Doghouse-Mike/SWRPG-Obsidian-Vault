## Spare Clip

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Gadgeteer]], [[Gunner]], [[Heavy]], [[Recruit]], [[Retired Clone Trooper]], [[Trader]]

The character does not run out of ammo on a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result. Items with [[Item Qualities#Limited Ammo (Passive)|Limited Ammo]] quality run out of ammo as normal.
