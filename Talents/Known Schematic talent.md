## Known Schematic

Source: [^1][^2][^3][^4]

- **Activation:** Active (Maneuver)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Commodore]], [[Imperial Academy Cadet]], [[Outlaw Tech]], [[Republic Navy Officer]], [[Sapper]], [[Shipwright]]

Once per game session, the character may spend a maneuver to make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Education (Int)|Knowledge (Education) check]]**. Success means they are familiar with a building or capital ship's design. They now know the location of critical components or facilities within the ship or vehicle, as well as their own location. They can also plan unconventional routes around obstacles. Additional [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]], [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] or [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] results can reveal other useful information at the GM's discretion.
