## Defy Fate

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Dark Side Cultist]]

Once per session, when the character makes a check that results in a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result, the character may spend one destiny point to resist the effects of that [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]]. The [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] is still rolled, and is affected by any other effect that regards results, but when it would be spent, the [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result is simply cancelled.

### Improved

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[First Order Loyalist|Dark Side Cultist]]

A number of allies equal to ranks in [[Discipline (Will)|Discipline]] may also use Defy Fate. Each ally may only use this talent once per session.
