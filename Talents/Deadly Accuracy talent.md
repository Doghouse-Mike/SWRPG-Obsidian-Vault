## Deadly Accuracy

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Assassin]] (1), [[Clone Trooper]] (1), [[Gadgeteer]] (1), [[Gunslinger]] (1), [[Mercenary Soldier]] (1), [[Sharpshooter]] (2)

Each time the character gains a rank of Deadly Accuracy, they must choose one [[Category - Combat Skills|combat skill]]. The character may add their ranks in that combat skill as additional damage to one hit of successful attacks made with that skill using a personal-scale weapon. They cannot choose the same combat skill twice.
