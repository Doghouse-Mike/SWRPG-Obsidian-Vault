## Dirty Tricks

Source: [^0-1][^2]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Skip Tracer (reSpec)]], [[Thief (reSpec)]]

Once per session, make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Skulduggery (Cun)|Skulduggery check]]** to add [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice equal to [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] results to the next check made by a number of enemies equal to the character's ranks in Cunning.
