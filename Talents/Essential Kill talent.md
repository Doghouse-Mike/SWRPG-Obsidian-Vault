## Essential Kill

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Executioner]]

*Force talent.* When making a combat check that does not involve either using the [[Gunnery (Ag)|Gunnery]] skill or operating a non-starship weapon, the character may add [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] dice no greater than \[their\] Force rating to the check. The character may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]] (◐) to add an [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result, or three [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|points]] (◐◐◐) to add a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result (character's choice), to the result. [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|Advantage]] results gained in this way cannot be used to recover strain.

[^1]: [[Category - Seeker|Savage Spirits]]: *A Sourcebook for Seekers* (Page 30)
