## Transmogrify

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Alchemist]], [[Magus]]

*Force talent.* When making a crafting check, the character may add [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] dice no greater than Force rating to the check. The character may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]] to add a [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] to the result, and may spend two [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|points]] to add an [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] to the result.

[^1]: [[Category - Mystic|Unlimited Power]]: *A Sourcebook for Mystics* (Page 33)
