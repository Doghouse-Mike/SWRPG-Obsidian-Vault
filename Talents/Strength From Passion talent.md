## Strength From Passion

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Acolyte]], [[Lord]]

Once per session when making a check, the character may spend a Destiny Point to add a number of [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] and [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results equal to the number of dark side Destiny Points in the Destiny pool to the results of the check. A character who has purchased this talent automatically gains 1 Conflict at the beginning of a game session.

[^0-1]: [[Category - Old Republic|The Old Republic]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 31)
