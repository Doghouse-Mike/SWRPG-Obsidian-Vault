## Unyielding

Source: [^0-1][^2]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Marauder (reSpec)]], [[Martial Artist (reSpec)]]

After being hit by a ranged attack, suffer 2 strain to perform a Move maneuver as an out of turn incidental toward the attacker.
