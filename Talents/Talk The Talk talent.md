## Talk the Talk

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Enforcer]], [[Investigator]]

When making a [[Category - Knowledge Skills|Knowledge skill]] check, the character may spend one Destiny Point to substitute [[Underworld (Int)|Knowledge (Underworld)]] or [[Streetwise (Cun)|Streetwise]] for the required skill. The GM may suitably embellish any information gathered due to a success on the check to reflect its criminal and underworld origins.
