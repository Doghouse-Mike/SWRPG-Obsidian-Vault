## Heal Thyself

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Doctor (reSpec)]]

When performing a check to heal themselves, the character may substitute [[Resilience (Br)|Resilience]] instead of the required skill to avoid increasing the difficulty of the check due to self-healing. This includes [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics]] checks to heal cyborgs or droids, but does not apply to [[Resilience (Br)|Resilience]] checks to passively heal critical injuries. Talents that affect [[Medicine (Int)|Medicine]] checks still apply to this roll.

[^1]: [[Category - ReSpecialized|The reSpecialized Project]]'s Doctor Folio
