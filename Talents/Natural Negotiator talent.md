## Natural Negotiator

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Gambler]], [[Quartermaster]], [[Republic Representative]], [[Sage]], [[Trader]]

Once per game session, the character may reroll any one [[Cool (Pr)|Cool]] or [[Negotiation (Pr)|Negotiation]] check.
