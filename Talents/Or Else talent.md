## ...Or Else

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Enforcer (reSpec)]]

Once per session, after the character or an ally within short range makes a [[Coercion (Will)|Coercion]] check that fails or results in a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]], spend a Destiny Point to add a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] to the result. The [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result adds a [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] result as normal.
