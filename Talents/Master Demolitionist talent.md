## Master Demolitionist

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Sapper]]

When resolving an attack from a personal (non starship/vehicle) explosive or ordnance weapon, the character may spend an [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] or [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result to have the weapon's [[Item Qualities#Blast (Active)|Blast]] quality affect all characters within short range (rather than engaged). If the weapon normally affects all characters within short range, then the range of effect is increased to medium instead.

[^1]: [[Category - Engineer|Fully Operational]]: *A Sourcebook for Engineers* (Page 31)
