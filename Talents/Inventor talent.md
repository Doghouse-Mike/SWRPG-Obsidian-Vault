## Inventor

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Armorer]] (2), [[Artisan]] (1), [[Outlaw Tech]] (2), [[Scientist]] (2)

When constructing new items or modifying existing attachments, the character may choose to add [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] dice or remove [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice from the check per rank of Inventor.
