Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Artisan]], [[Mechanic]], [[Modder]]

Once per game session, the character may reroll any one [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics]] check.
