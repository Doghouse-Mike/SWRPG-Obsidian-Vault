## Paths Uncharted

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Fringer (reSpec)]]

Once per session, make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Daunting difficulty]] [[Astrogation (Int)|Astrogation check]]** to find an uncharted hyperspace route. On success, the route is either immediately ready for the character jump as an incidental, untraceable and unrepeatable by anyone but the character, or the shortest possible in both distance and time. Spend three [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result to choose an additional option..
