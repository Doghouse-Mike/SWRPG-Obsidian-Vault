## Reconstruct the Scene

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Investigator]], [[Skip Tracer]]

Once per session, the character may perform the Reconstruct the Scene action. The character makes a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Perception (Cun)|Perception check]]** when present at a single crime scene (or similar location). If the character succeeds, \[they\] identify all prominent physical characteristics of one person who was at the crime scene in the last 24 hours per success.
