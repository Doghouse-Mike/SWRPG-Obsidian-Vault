## Precise Aim

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Active (Maneuver)
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Assassin]] (2), [[Executioner]] (2)
- **Items:** [[Motion Tracker]], [[Sonic Scope]] (Modification Option)

Once per round on the character’s turn, the character may perform a Precise Aim maneuver before attempting a combat check and suffer a number of strain. The number of strain cannot exceed their ranks in Precise Aim. They then decreases the target’s defense (ranged and melee) by one per strain suffered for that combat check.

### Improved

Source: [^4]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Assassin (reSpec)]]

When performing the Precise Aim maneuver, all non-Gunnery attacks made by the character this round have their [[Item Qualities#Pierce (Passive)|Pierce]] rating increased by ranks in Precise Aim.
