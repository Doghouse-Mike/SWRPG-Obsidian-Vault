## Situational Awareness

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Squadron Leader]]
- **Items:** [[Tactical Command, Control, and Communications Suite]] (Modification Option)

When in a starship or vehicle with functional coms, allies within short range add a [[Narrative Dice#Boost|boost]] die to their [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] and [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]] checks. Allies within close range add two [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] dice instead. This talent operates on planetary scale, not personal scale.

[^1]: [[Category - Rebel Ranks|***Star Wars*** **Age of Rebellion**]] Roleplaying Game: *Core Rulebook* (Page 156)
