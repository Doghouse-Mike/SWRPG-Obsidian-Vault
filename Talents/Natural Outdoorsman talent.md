## Natural Outdoorsman

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Beast Rider]], [[Commando]], [[Hermit]], [[Pathfinder]], [[Survivalist]], [[Tactician]]

Once per game session, the character may reroll any one [[Resilience (Br)|Resilience]] or [[Survival (Cun)|Survival]] check.
