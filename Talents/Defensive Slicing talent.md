## Defensive Slicing

Source: [^1][^0-2][^1-3]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Artisan]] (2), [[Slicer]] (3)

When attempting to defend a computer system against intrusion (or when an opponent attempts to slice a computer owned or programmed by the character), the character adds a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die per rank of Defensive Slicing to \[their\] opponent's checks.

### Improved

Source: [^0-2][^1-3]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Slicer]]

When attempting to defend a computer system against intrusion, the character upgrades the difficulty of an opponent's dice pool a number of times equal to \[their\] rank of Defensive Slicing. This replaces the usual benefits from Defensive Slicing.
