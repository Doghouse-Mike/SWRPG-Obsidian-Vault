## Fearsome Rep

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Pirate]] (2)

The character adds an automatic [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result to the results of \[their\] [[Coercion (Will)|Coercion]] checks equal to \[their\] ranks in Fearsome Rep.

[^1]: [[Category - Early Rebellion|Dawn of Rebellion]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 112)
