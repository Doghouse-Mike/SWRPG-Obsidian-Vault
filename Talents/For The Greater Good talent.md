## For The Greater Good

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Ambassador (reSpec)]]

Once per session after being successfully attacked, the character may make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Discipline (Will)|Discipline check]]** as an out-of-turn incidental to ignore the damage and the effects of this attack until the end of the encounter. After which, the character suffers the damage, and the effects of any exceeded thresholds, status effects, or critical injuries, in that order.

[^1]: [[Category - ReSpecialized|The reSpecialized Project]]'s Ambassador Folio
