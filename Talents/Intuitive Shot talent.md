## Intuitive Shot

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Hunter]]

*Force talent.* When making a [[Ranged - Heavy (Ag)|Ranged (Heavy)]] or [[Ranged - Light (Ag)|Ranged (Light)]] combat check, the character may add [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] dice no greater than Force rating to the check. The character may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]] to add a [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] result or [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result (character's choice) to the results.

[^1]: [[Category - Way of the Force|***Star Wars*** **Force and Destiny**]] Roleplaying Game: *Core Rulebook* (Page 145)
