## Lead from the Front

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Separatist Commander]] (1)

After succeeding on a [[Leadership (Pr)|Leadership]] check or other check to inspire, lead, or rally an audience, the character may spend 1 Destiny Point to upgrade the ability of the next combat check made by each ally within short range once.

[^1]: [[Category - Clone Wars End|Collapse of the Republic]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 41)
