## Energy Siphon

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Cyber Tech (reSpec)]]

Once per encounter, make an **opposed [[Athletics (Br)|Athletics]] vs. [[Athletics (Br)|Athletics check]]** against an engaged foe to drain the energy from an equipped, powered item and recover strain equal to total encumbrance of drained item(s). Spend two [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result to drain an additional equipped item. Items drained cease functioning until either recharged or reloaded.
