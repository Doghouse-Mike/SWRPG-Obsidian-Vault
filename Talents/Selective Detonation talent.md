## Selective Detonation

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Demolitionist]] (3), [[Saboteur]] (2)

When using weapons with the [[Item Qualities#Blast (Active)|Blast]] quality or explosive devices, the character may spend an [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result to exclude one target that would normally be affected by the explosion. Multiple targets may be excluded in this manner, but the total number may not exceed \[their\] ranks in Selective Detonation. The character may also spend a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result to exclude multiple targets, as long as the total number of targets excluded does not exceed \[their\] ranks in Selective Detonation.
