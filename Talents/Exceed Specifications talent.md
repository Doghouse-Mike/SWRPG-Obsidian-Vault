## Exceed Specifications

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Scavenger]]

Before making a check using an item, the character may take the Exceed Specifications incidental, adding a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to the check. The GM may spend two [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results on the check to damage the item one step, or a despair to damage it beyond usability. [[Narrative Dice#Threat|Threat]] or [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] results may have additional consequences depending on the nature of the item, at the GM's discretion.

#### Improved

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Scavenger]]

When the character takes the Exceed Specifications incidental, upgrade the ability of the check once instead of adding a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die.

### Supreme

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Scavenger]]

If the character's check benefitting from the Exceed Specifications incidental generates a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result, they may damage the item one step to roll one additional [[Narrative Dice#Proficiency (Yellow)|proficiency]] die and add it to the check results. If the check generates a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result, roll a [[Narrative Dice#Challenge (Red)|challenge]] die and add it to the results.
