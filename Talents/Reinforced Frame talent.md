## Reinforced Frame

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Rigger]]

The character add the Massive 1 rule to \[their\] [[Signature Vehicle talent|Signature Vehicle]] (when making an attack targeting the starship or vehicle, the Critical rating of any weapon used counts as 1 higher).
