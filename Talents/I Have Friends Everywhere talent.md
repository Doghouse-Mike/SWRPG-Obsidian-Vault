## I Have Friends Everywhere

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Agitator (reSpec)]]

Once per session, the character may perform a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Streetwise (Cun)|Streetwise check]]**. On success, the character chooses an NPC in the scene, or reveals a previously unknown NPC; that NPC is sympathetic to the character's cause and performs a favor for the character. This favor may be rebellious in nature and may result in legal scrutiny and consequence, but must not be obviously suicidal. The Game Master determines if an NPC cannot be considered sympathetic.

[^1]: [[Category - ReSpecialized|The reSpecialized Project]]'s Agitator Folio
