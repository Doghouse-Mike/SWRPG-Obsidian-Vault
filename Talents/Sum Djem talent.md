## Sum Djem

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Makashi Duelist]], [[Master]], [[Niman Disciple]], [[Shii-Cho Knight]]

*Force talent.* When the character makes a successful [[Lightsaber (Br)|Lightsaber]] combat check, they may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result or two [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results to disarm their opponent (with GM's approval). The disarmed weapon lands anywhere within short range of the engagement (character's choice).

[^1]: [[Category - Clone Wars End|Collapse of the Republic]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 44)
[^2]: [[Category - Way of the Force|***Star Wars*** **Force and Destiny**]] Roleplaying Game: *Core Rulebook* (Page 152-153)
