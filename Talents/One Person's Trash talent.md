## One Person's Trash

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Scavenger]]

The character may make a [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] check in order to locate a particular item amid a battlefield, shipwreck, junkyard, or other location with potential salvage. The difficulty of the check is equal to half the item's rarity, rounded down. There is no cost to acquire an item this way, but the item is in need of moderate repairs when found.

[^1]: [[Category - Clone Wars Begin|Rise of the Separatists]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 40)
