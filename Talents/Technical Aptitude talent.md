## Technical Aptitude

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Analyst]] (1), [[Artisan]] (1), [[Slicer]] (2)
- **Items:** [[Integrated Slicer Gear]] (Modification Option)

For each rank, the character reduces the amount of time needed to complete computer-related tasks by 25%.
