## Block

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Martial Artist (reSpec)]]

When the character suffers a hit from a melee combat check, after damage is calculated (but before soak is applied) the character may take a Block incidental. They suffer 3 strain and reduce the damage dealt by that hit by 4. This talent may only be used once per hit and when the character is wielding a Lightsaber, Melee, or other appropriate item per GM discretion.
