## Saw It Coming

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Bodyguard (reSpec)]]

Once per encounter, make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Perception (Cun)|Perception check]]** to add two [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results to all attacks targeting the character or allies within short range until their next turn. Spend a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result to add an additional [[Narrative Dice#Failure|failure]] result to those attacks.
