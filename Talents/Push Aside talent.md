## Push Aside

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Force Adherent]]

After making a successful [[Melee (Br)|Melee]], [[Brawl (Br)|Brawl]], or [[Lightsaber (Br)|Lightsaber]] combat check against an engaged foe, the character may force the opponent to move to short range as an out-of-turn incidental in a direction of the opponent's choosing. The character may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result to cause the opponent to fall prone after moving.

[^1]: [[Category - Early Rebellion|Dawn of Rebellion]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 115)
