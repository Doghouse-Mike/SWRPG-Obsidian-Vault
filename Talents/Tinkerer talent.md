## Tinkerer

Source: [^1][^2][^3][^4]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Armorer]] (1), [[Gadgeteer]] (1), [[Modder]] (3), [[Outlaw Tech]] (2), [[Scavenger]] (1), [[Scientist]] (2)

The character makes one piece of equipment more modifiable. They choose one piece of equipment and increases its number of hard points by one. They can only do this once per piece of equipment, but can modify a number of pieces of equipment equal to their ranks in Tinkerer. If they lose a modified piece of equipment, they may apply Tinkerer to a new one.

### Improved

Source: [^5]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Modder (reSpec)]]

Once per session as an incidental, spend a Destiny Point. For each item benefiting from the character's Tinkerer, add [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results equal to the character's ranks in Tinkerer to the next check using it.
