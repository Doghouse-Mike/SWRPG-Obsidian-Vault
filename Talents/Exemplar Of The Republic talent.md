## Exemplar of the Republic

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[General]]

*Force talent.* When making a Mass Combat check for which the character is the acting commander or takes a prominent role on the battlefield, the character may add [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force dice]] no greater than their Force rating to the check. The character may spend [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|points]] to add [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] or [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantages]] (character's choice) to the result.

[^1]: [[Category - Clone Wars End|Collapse of the Republic]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 39)
