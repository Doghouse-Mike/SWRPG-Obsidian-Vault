## Saber Swarm

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Maneuver)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Ataru Striker]]

*Force talent.* The character may take the Saber Swarm maneuver, suffering 1 strain. \[Their\] next [[Lightsaber (Br)|Lightsaber]] (Agility) combat check this turn gains the [[Item Qualities#Linked (Active)|Linked]] item quality, with ranks equal to Force rating.

[^1]: [[Category - Way of the Force|***Star Wars*** **Force and Destiny**]] Roleplaying Game: *Core Rulebook* (Page 151)
