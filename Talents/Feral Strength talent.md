## Feral Strength

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Commando]] (2), [[Marauder]] (3)

The character adds 1 damage per rank of Feral Strength to one hit of \[their\] successful Brawl and Melee attacks.
