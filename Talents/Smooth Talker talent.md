## Smooth Talker

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Advisor]] (2), [[Charmer]] (2), [[Gambler]] (1), [[Padawan Survivor]] (2), [[Performer]] (2), [[Quartermaster]] (2), [[Republic Representative]] (2), [[Sage]] (2), [[Trader]] (2)

When a character first acquires this talent, \[they\] choose one skill: [[Charm (Pr)|Charm]], [[Coercion (Will)|Coercion]], [[Negotiation (Pr)|Negotiation]], or [[Deception (Cun)|Deception]]. When making checks with that skill, \[they\] may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result to gain additional [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] results equal to \[their\] ranks in Smooth Talker.
