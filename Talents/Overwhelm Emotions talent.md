## Overwhelm Emotions

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Force Sensitive Exile]]

When the character performs a [[Charm (Pr)|Charm]], [[Coercion (Will)|Coercion]], or [[Deception (Cun)|Deception]] check, \[they\] may include [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] dice equal to \[their\] Force rating. Each [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Light point]] adds a [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] result to [[Charm (Pr)|Charm]] checks. Each [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|Dark point]] adds a [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] result to [[Coercion (Will)|Coercion]] or [[Deception (Cun)|Deception]] checks. However, every two [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Light points]] add a [[Narrative Dice#Failure|failure]] result to [[Coercion (Will)|Coercion]] or [[Deception (Cun)|Deception]] checks, and every two [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|Dark points]] add a [[Narrative Dice#Failure|failure]] result to [[Charm (Pr)|Charm]] checks. This does not apply to targets immune to Force powers.

[^1]: [[Category - On the Fringe|***Star Wars*** **Edge of the Empire**]] Roleplaying Game: *Core Rulebook* (Page 141)
