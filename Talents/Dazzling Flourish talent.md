## Dazzling Flourish

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Performer (reSpec)]]

The character may spend three [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result from a melee attack check to upgrade the ability of the next attack of each ally targeting the same enemy this encounter.
