## Voice of Reason

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Doctor (reSpec)]], [[Scholar (reSpec)]]

Once per encounter when making a [[Leadership (Pr)|Leadership]] or [[Negotiation (Pr)|Negotiation]] check, the character may substitute a [[Category - Knowledge Skills|Knowledge]] skill instead. The character's [[Category - Knowledge Skills|Knowledge]] skill is combined with their Presence characteristic to form the dice pool. When the character does this, the player must explain how their knowledge is persuading others or driving the conversation.

[^2]: [[Category - ReSpecialized|The reSpecialized Project]]'s Scholar Folio
