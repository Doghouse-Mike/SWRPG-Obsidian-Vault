## Reversal Of Fortunes

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Gambler (reSpec)]]

Once per session, after the character makes a check resulting in a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result, spend 1 Destiny Point to cancel a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result and add a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result to the next check made by an adversary in the scene. How the character turned their misfortune around is per GM discretion.
