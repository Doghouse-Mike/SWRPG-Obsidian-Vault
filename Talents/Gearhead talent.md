## Gearhead

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Driver]] (2), [[Droid Specialist]] (2), [[Rigger]] (2), [[Mechanic]] (2), [[Modder]] (2), [[Armorer]] (2)

The character removes a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die per rank of Gearhead from \[their\] [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics]] checks. In addition, the credit cost to add mods to attachments decreases by 50% (this does not increase with additional ranks of Gearhead).
