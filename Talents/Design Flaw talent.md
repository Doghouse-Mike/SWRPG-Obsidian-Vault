## Design Flaw

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Droid Specialist]] (2)

When making a combat check with a personal scale weapon against a droid, the character adds [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results equal to \[their\] ranks of Design Flaw.

[^1]: [[Category - Engineer|Fully Operational]]: *A Sourcebook for Engineers* (Page 30)
