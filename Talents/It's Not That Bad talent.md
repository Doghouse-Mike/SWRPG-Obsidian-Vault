## It's Not That Bad

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Medic]]

Once per game session, when an ally is about to suffer a critical injury, the character can take an It's Not That Bad incidental to make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Medicine (Int)|Medicine check]]**. If successful, the ally does not gain the Critical Injury and does not suffer any of its normal effects (although the attack that caused the Critical Injury still deals damage as normal).

[^1]: [[Category - Rebel Ranks|***Star Wars*** **Age of Rebellion**]] Roleplaying Game: *Core Rulebook* (Page 150)
