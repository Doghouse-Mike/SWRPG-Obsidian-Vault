## Preemptive Intervention

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[General]]

*Force talent.* Immediately after an opponent moves to engage an ally within short range of the character, the character may spend one Destiny Point to engage that opponent as an out-of-turn incidental.

[^1]: [[Category - Clone Wars End|Collapse of the Republic]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 42)
