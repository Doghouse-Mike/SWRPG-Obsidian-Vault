## Embellishment

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Propagandist (reSpec)]] (2)

The character adds a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost die]] per rank of Embellishment to checks made to convince others of their allies' deeds or reputation. Additionally, when any character's Duty would increase by 1 or more, it increases that amount plus 1 per rank of Embellishment instead.

### Improved

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Propagandist (reSpec)]]

Once per encounter, the character may make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Charm (Pr)|Charm]] or [[Deception (Cun)|Deception check]]**. On success, add [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage results]] equal to character's rank of Embellishment to each social check made by an ally, plus one additional ally per additional two [[Narrative Dice#Success|success results]].

Additionally, once per session, if no character's Duty has triggered, the character may make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Charm (Pr)|Charm]] or [[Deception (Cun)|Deception check]]**. If the character succeeds, one other chosen PC's Duty counts as having triggered this session. When the Propagandist does this, the player must explain what event they engineered to highlight their comrade's contributions to the cause. Note that this only triggers the mechanical effects of Duty, and the GM decides what narrative impact this has.

#### Supreme

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Propagandist (reSpec)]]

Once per session, the character may spend Destiny point to add [[Narrative Dice#Success|success results]] equal to their ranks of Embellishment to an ally's check before it is rolled. If that ally's Duty triggered this session, they mad add a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph result]] to the check instead. This could be the result of character's involvement, or simply the character witnessing or recording their ally's heroic deeds.
