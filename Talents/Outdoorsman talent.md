## Outdoorsman

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Species:** [[Dathomirian]]
- **Trees:** [[Beast Rider]] (2), [[Big-Game Hunter]] (2) [[Clone Veteran]] (1), [[Pathfinder]] (2), [[Recruit]] (1), [[Survivalist]] (2), [[Tactician]] (2), [[Trailblazer]] (2)

The character removes a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die per rank of Outdoorsman from \[their\] checks to move through terrain or to manage terrain or environmental effects. Decrease overland travel times by 50% (this does not decrease with additional ranks of Outdoorsman).
