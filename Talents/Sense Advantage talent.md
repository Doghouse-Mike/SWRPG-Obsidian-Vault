## Sense Advantage

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Advisor]], [[Aggressor]], [[Nightsister]], [[Seer]], [[Warden]]

*Force talent.* Once per game session, the character may add two [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice to the skill check of one NPC within extreme range.
