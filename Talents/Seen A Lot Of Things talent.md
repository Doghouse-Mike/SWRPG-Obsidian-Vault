## Seen a Lot of Things

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Ship Captain]]

Whenever the character fails a [[Category - Knowledge Skills|Knowledge]] check, \[they\] may spend three [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results to roll the check again during \[their\] next turn.
