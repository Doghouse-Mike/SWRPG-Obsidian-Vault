## Blather

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Senator]]

The character may take the Blather action, making a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Charm (Pr)|Charm]]** or **[[Deception (Cun)|Deception check]]**. If the check is successful, one adversary per [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] result within medium range is disoriented for a number of rounds equal to the character's Presence rating. The character may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result from the check to stagger one affected target for one round.

[^1]: [[Category - Clone Wars End|Collapse of the Republic]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 37)
