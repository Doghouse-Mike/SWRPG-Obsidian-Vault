## Powerful Blast

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Demolitionist]] (3), [[Retired Clone Trooper]] (2), [[Saboteur]] (2), [[Sapper]] (2)

The character increases the [[Item Qualities#Blast (Active)|Blast]] quality damage dealt by explosives, explosive weapons, and grenades \[they\] use by 1 per rank of Powerful Blast.
