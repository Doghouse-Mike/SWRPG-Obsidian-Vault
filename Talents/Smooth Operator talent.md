## Smooth Operator

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Operator (reSpec)]]

After making a successful Gunnery attack, spend 1 Destiny Point and suffer a number of strain no greater than the number of [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] results to convert that many [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] results to [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results.
