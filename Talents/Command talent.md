## Command

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Clone Officer]] (2), [[Commodore]] (2), [[Figurehead]] (2), [[General]] (1), [[Mercenary Soldier]] (2), [[Peacekeeper]] (2), [[Republic Navy Officer]] (2), [[Separatist Commander]] (1), [[Squadron Leader]] (2), [[Strategist]] (2)

The character gains a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] dice per rank of Command when making [[Leadership (Pr)|Leadership]] checks (or other checks to inspire, lead, or rally an audience). Inspired targets also add a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die per rank to any subsequent [[Discipline (Will)|Discipline]] checks they make over the next twenty-four hours (this does not increase with additional ranks of Command).
