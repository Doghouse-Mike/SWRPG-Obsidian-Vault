## Unarmed Parry

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Martial Artist]], [[Steel Hand Adept]]

The character may perform the [[Parry talent|Parry]] incidental while unarmed. When the character performs the [[Parry talent|Parry]] incidental while unarmed, reduce the strain they suffer from the [[Parry talent|Parry]] incidental by one, to a minimum of 1.
