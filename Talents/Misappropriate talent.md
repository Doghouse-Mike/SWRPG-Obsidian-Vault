## Misappopriate

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Quartermaster (reSpec)]]

Once per session, the character may take a Misappropriate action, making a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Computers (Int)|Computers]]** or **[[Skulduggery (Cun)|Skulduggery check]]**. If successful the character (or player) reveals how their knowledge of and access to supply lines or the means of enemy readiness has a meaningful impact on the scene. The nature of the revelation and its impact are ultimately subject to the GM's discretion, but they should significantly hamper enemy readiness.

For example, an enemy platoon may have had their E-11 rifles stolen, and must make do with carbines or sidearms, or perhaps their shipment of sublight fuel never arrived, and thus their starships' top speed is reduced by 1. The player must justify how they or an intermediary had access to enemy supplies or logistics, and may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph result]] to establish that the character or their allies have access to the missing resource instead, and my leverage it in the current scene.
