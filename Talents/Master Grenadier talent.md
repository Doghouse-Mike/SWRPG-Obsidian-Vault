## Master Grenadier

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Demolitionist]], [[Saboteur]], [[Sapper]]

The character decreases the [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] cost to active the [[Item Qualities#Blast (Active)|Blast]] quality on any weapon \[they\] use by 1 to a minimum of 1. This includes the cost to activate the [[Item Qualities#Blast (Active)|Blast]] quality if the weapon misses.

[^2]: [[Category - Hired Gun|Dangerous Covenants]]: *A Sourcebook for Hired Guns* (Page 32)
