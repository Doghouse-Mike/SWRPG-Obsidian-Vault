## Utinni!

Source: [^1][^2]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Outlaw Tech]] (2), [[Scavenger]] (2)

The character removes [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice equal to the character's ranks in Utinni! from checks made to find or scavenge items or gear. These checks could include [[Negotiation (Pr)|Negotiation]], [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]], or other checks, depending on circumstances and the GM's discretion. Such skill checks take half their normal time (this does not decrease with multiple ranks of Utinni!).
