## Kill Or Be Killed

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental, Out of Turn)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Marauder (reSpec)]]

Once per session when exceeding wound threshold, spend a Destiny Point to make a melee attack. If the target dies, wounds become equal to ranks of [[Resilience (Br)|Resilience]] below threshold instead.
