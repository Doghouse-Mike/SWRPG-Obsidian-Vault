## Snap Out Of It

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Action)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[First Order Defector]]

Once per session, Make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Daunting difficulty]] [[Discipline (Will)|Discipline check]]** to recover strain equal to half of the the character's current strain, rounded down.
