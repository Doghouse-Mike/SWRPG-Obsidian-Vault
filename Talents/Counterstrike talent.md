## Counterstrike

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Shien Expert]]

*Force talent.* Whenever an attack misses the character, the character may spend two [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result to upgrade the next [[Lightsaber (Br)|Lightsaber]] (Cunning) combat check \[they\] make against the attacker during \[their\] next turn once.

[^1]: [[Category - Way of the Force|***Star Wars*** **Force and Destiny**]] Roleplaying Game: *Core Rulebook* (Page 141)
