## Sidearm Diplomacy

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[Marshal (reSpec)]] (2)

Once per encounter, while carrying a visible weapon, the character may upgrade the difficulty of a social check they make once to add [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] results equal to ranks in Sidearm Diplomacy to the results.

[^1]: [[Category - ReSpecialized|The reSpecialized Project]]'s Marshal Folio
