## Assassinate

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Assassin (reSpec)]]

When inflicting a critical injury on a minion group, if undetected by them, spend additional [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results to kill or incapacitate an additional member per [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result spent.

### Improved

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Action (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Assassin (reSpec)]]

Before rolling a combat check while undetected by the target, suffer a number of strain up to the number of [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] dice in the dice pool to remove that many [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] dice and add that many [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results to the pool. Additionally, when inflicting a critical injury on a rival while undetected by the target, spend three additional [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result to incapacitate or kill the rival. Assassinate may affect nemesis foes at Game Master discretion.
