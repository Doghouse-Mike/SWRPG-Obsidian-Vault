## Where It Hurts

Source: [^0-1]

- **Activation:** Active (Maneuver)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Heavy (reSpec)]]

Once per encounter as a maneuver, the character may suffer strain up to their ranks of [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] and choose a foe within long range. The character's attacks targeting the foe increase their [[Item Qualities#Pierce (Passive)|Pierce]] rating equal to the number of strain suffered this way until the end of the encounter.
