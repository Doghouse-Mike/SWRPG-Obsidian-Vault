## Extended Reach

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Active (Incidental)
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Force Adherent]]

While armed with a two-handed melee weapon, the character may suffer one strain to make a [[Melee (Br)|Melee]] combat check with that weapon targeting an enemy at up to short range (rather than engaged range). The difficulty of the check remains **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average]]**.

[^1]: [[Category - Early Rebellion|Dawn of Rebellion]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 112)
