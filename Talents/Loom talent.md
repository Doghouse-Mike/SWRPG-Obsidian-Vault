## Loom

Source: [^1]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Enforcer]]

When an ally engaged with the character makes a successful [[Charm (Pr)|Charm]], [[Deception (Cun)|Deception]], or [[Negotiation (Pr)|Negotiation]] check, the character adds an [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result per rank in [[Coercion (Will)|Coercion]] to the ally's check.

[^1]: [[Category - Hired Gun|Dangerous Covenants]]: *A Sourcebook for Hired Guns* (Page 32)
