## All-Terrain Driver

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Driver]], [[Operator]]

When piloting a vehicle using the [[Piloting - Planetary (Ag)|Piloting (Planetary)]] skill, the character does not suffer the penalties for driving through difficult terrain.
