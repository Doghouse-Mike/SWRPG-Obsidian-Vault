## Forager

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** No
- **Trees:** [[Beast Rider]], [[Big-Game Hunter]], [[Force Sensitive Exile]], [[Hermit]], [[Medic]], [[Pathfinder]], [[Scavenger]], [[Scout]], [[Seer]], [[Survivalist]]

The character removes up to two [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice from \[their\] skill checks to find food, water, or shelter. [[Survival (Cun)|Survival]] checks to forage take half the time.
