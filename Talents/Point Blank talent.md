## Point Blank

Source: [^1][^2][^3][^4]

- **Activation:** Passive
- **Ranked:** Yes
- **Trees:** [[ARC Trooper]] (2), [[Commando]] (1), [[Death Watch Warrior]] (2), [[Gadgeteer]] (1), [[Marshal]] (2), [[Mercenary Soldier]] (2), [[Vanguard]] (1)
- **Items:** [[Forearm Grip]] (Modification Option)

The character adds one damage per rank of Point Blank to one hit of successful [[Ranged - Heavy (Ag)|Ranged (Heavy)]] or [[Ranged - Light (Ag)|Ranged (Light)]] attacks made while at short range or engaged.
