## NOW THAT'S PODRACING

#### BEYOND RACING

Since it's inception in **Star Wars Episode I: The Phantom Menace**, podracing has become an iconic part of the franchise and held a place into some of the most memorable scenes of the movies. \[...\]

### HIGH OCTANE

#### IN-RACE GAMEPLAY

After setting the mood for a race and preparing all the required materials to fit into a campaign, the racetrack event itself still remains to be run. This section will detail the most common rules covering races and racetracks: race Sections, the starting grid, the Pole, Initiative order, etc.

> Read the full rules on pages 9 to 11 of **Now That's Podracing:** *A Podracing Expansion*.

### THAT'S SO WIZARD ANI!

This chapter rewords and adds many new attributes to podracers and combines them into a more engaging flight model. \[...\]

> Read the full rules on pages 14 to 21 of **Now That's Podracing:** *A Podracing Expansion*.
