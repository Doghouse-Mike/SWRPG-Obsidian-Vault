While darts filled with tranquilizer compounds are common tools used to deal with dangerous animals, the diverse battlefields of the galactic war have recently given more purpose to these tools as weapons. By exchanging the compound or replacing the payload of a dart entirely, they make for quiet and versatile weaponry.
