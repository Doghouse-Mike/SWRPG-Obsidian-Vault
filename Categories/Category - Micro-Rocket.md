### USING MICRO-ROCKETS

Source: [^1]

Generally, micro-rockets are fired using a specialized launching system, such as the micro-rocket armour mounting. In a pinch, a micro-rocket can be jury-rigged to launch without any sort of firing system, though not without risk of losing fingers (or worse). If a character attempts to fire a micro-rocket without any sort of launcher or mounting, \[they\] use the [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics]] skill for the combat check instead of [[Ranged - Heavy (Ag)|Ranged (Heavy)]], and the difficulty of the check is upgraded once.

Micro-rockets can only be modified with custom attachments specifically designed for them.

[^1]: [[Category - Bounty Hunter|No Disintegrations]]: *A Sourcebook for Bounty Hunters* (Page 46)
