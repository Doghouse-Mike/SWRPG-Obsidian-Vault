In the *Star Wars* Roleplaying Game, transportation revolves around two big categories, space-faring and planetside craft. The former is referred to generally as **Vehicles** and the latter as **Starships**. These are among the most expensive assets available to characters, and it is assumed that groups start with a shared resource from among these, allowing them access to a greater area of operation than they would have if bound to their own locomotion.

## [[Category - Vehicles|VEHICLES]]

Vehicles are controlled through use of the **[[Piloting - Planetary (Ag)|Piloting (Planetary)]]** skill and range from the ancient wheeled cars to starship-like high atmospheric airspeeders. Each of these type of vehicle has its purpose and history to account for in the narrative.

## [[Category - Starships|STARSHIPS]]

Starships are the quintessential medium of transportation, used with the **[[Piloting - Space (Ag)|Piloting (Space)]]** skill. Starships come in all shapes and sizes, from the hyperdrive-lacking ship's boats and snubfighters, to the great battlecruisers deployed across the galaxy to fight grand battles.

## [[Category - Riding|RIDING BEASTS]]

The riding beast is an oddity among forms of transportation, as it is widely present across the Outer Rim to replace more expensive technology where the creatures are native or imported for other purposes. The logistics follow a completely different chain for this option, and they are controlled by the **[[Survival (Cun)|Survival]]** skill.
