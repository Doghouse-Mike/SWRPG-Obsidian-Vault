#### SIDEBAR: USING ICONIC NPCS

Source: [^2]

This book contains information on many iconic *Star Wars* characters, from Obi-Wan Kenobi to Darth Vader. Their profiles offer representation of some, but not all of the important roles and abilities famously associated with them. GMs are encouraged to modify profiles as needed for specific time frames and character situations, and for the needs of ongoing campaigns. \[...\]
