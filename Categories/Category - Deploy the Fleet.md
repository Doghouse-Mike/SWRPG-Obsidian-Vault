## DEPLOY THE FLEET

#### WHAT'S IN THIS BOOK?

**Deploy the Fleet** is an unofficial sourcebook for the **Star Wars Roleplaying Game** that covers space encounters, with a particular focus on capital ship-scale encounters from small freighters up to Star Destroyers.

### SHIPS OF THE GALAXY

#### ITEM QUALITIES

##### TRACKING (PASSIVE)

Source: [^0-1]

Weapons with the Tracking quality are heavy, fixed in a mount, or otherwise have a limited ability to turn to follow fast-moving targets.

When attacking a starship or vehicle with a weapon with the Tracking quality, compare the speed of the target with the Tracking rating. For every point the target's speed is above the weapon's Tracking, increase the difficulty of the attack by 1.

###### SIDEBAR: APPLYING TRACKING

Source: [^0-1]

The Tracking quality is applied to new weapons in this book, but can also be applied to weapons for other sourcebooks. If you would like to apply tracking to existing weapons, recommended values are:

- **Tracking 3:** Light turbolasers, heavy ion cannons.
- **Tracking 2:** Medium turbolasers, battleship ion cannons.
- **Tracking 1:** Heavy turbolasers.
- **Tracking 0:** Proton bombs and other bombs.
