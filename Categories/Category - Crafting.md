Given materials and time, anyone can cobble together some pieces of equipment, and those with expertise can produce some exceptional items at a fraction of the cost of manufactured options. These range from on-the-go dug-out fortifications, to crafting weapons and droids, to the mystical alchemy of force sensitives. If you've got the expertise, there's little limit to what you may create.

### [[Alchemical Crafting]]

Draughts, potions, elixirs and more... with the right knowledge and connection to the force, these can be powerful boons...

### [[Armor Crafting|Armour Crafting]]

New set of robes for protection, all the way to fully powered battle armour. With the right resources you could become a tank...

### [[Droid Crafting]]

Create technological life to suit your purposes, fully automated servants only take time and resources to produce...

### [[Fortifications Building]]

Digging in a position to prepare against an assault is critical when you lack the manpower or force advantage...

### [[Gadget & Cybernetic Crafting]]

When you need a tool for a job, or an enhancement, which simply isn't available on the market...

### [[Lightsaber Crafting]]

For the most attuned of force users, this process is a grand milestone in their path towards enlightenment...

### [[Vehicle & Starship Crafting]]

Difficult to do from scraps, with the right equipment and dry dock... and a whole team... one can put together the largest pieces of equipment in the galaxy...
