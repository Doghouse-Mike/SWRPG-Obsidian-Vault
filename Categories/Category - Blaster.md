Blaster technology is the cornerstone of all ranged weapons in the *Star Wars* galaxy. It is ubiquitous and typically packs the best bang for a character's credits. There is a wide variety of models, categorised by size and intent of use. Pistols vary in their weight and ability to be concealed, usually informing their power, with the heaviest certainly conveying hostility. Long-arms give a lot more space for customizability, allowing a character to pick up whatever best suits their interests.

> The categories used here are arbitrary, based on the text of an item and their entries on Wookieepedia. While some attachments make reference to similar terms (restriction to pistols or rifles, etc.); these are still up to the GM in your game and there are no formal indications beyond the name of the weapon as to what precise category they may be in.
