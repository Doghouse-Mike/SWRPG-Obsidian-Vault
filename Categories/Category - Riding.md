Many cultures throughout the galaxy have domesticated creatures to ride and serve as pack beasts. Some of these have been dispersed all over, like the venerable Bantha. Force sensitive characters may even form bonds with one of these beasts, making them an impressive duo.

> These creatures are more than their base characteristics and derived attributes. Most have ranks in skills, though most share the same skills as prey or predators. Some are more suited for riders, being domesticated; others are capable of being fighters in their own right.
