###### SIDEBAR: [[Octuptarra Magna Tri-droid#DROID VEHICLES|DROID VEHICLES]]

Source: [^8]

Some of the vehicles deployed by the CIS are simply gigantic droid models. For the purposes of skill checks, the droids in these section are best represented as rivals. They should be given a 1 in all characteristics and have the following skills: [[Computers (Int)|Computers]] 2, [[Gunnery (Ag)|Gunnery]] 3, [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] 2, [[Piloting - Planetary (Ag)|Piloting (Planetary)]] 3, and [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]] 2. To represent improvements of the droid programming as the Clone Wars progresses, GMs should consider increasing the Agility or Cunning of the droid, or the ranks in key skills.

A droid vehicle is programmed to execute the role of multiple crew. To represent this, they should be able to take two actions per turn. The difficulty of each check is upgraded once.

##### SIDEBAR: [[BX-series Droid Commando#VOICE EMULATORS|VOICE EMULATORS]]

Source: [^0-11]

Commando droids sent on infiltration and other spec ops missions made use of vocal emulators to appear as clones and other individuals over comms. When doing so, the droid can imitate a voice with few lines of heard speech and a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Computers (Int)|Computers check]]**. Due to the large amount of clone speech patterns available for analysis in their database, the difficulty for imitating this voice is reduced twice to **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Easy difficulty]]** instead.
