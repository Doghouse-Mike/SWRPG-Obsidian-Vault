## SUNS OF FORTUNE

#### SO WHAT'S IN THIS BOOK, ANYWAY?

**Suns of Fortune** is a setting book designed to take readers on a whirlwind tour of the Corellian Sector, providing material for players and Game Masters alike. \[...\]

### THE CORELLIAN SYSTEM

The following \[section\] deals with \[the Corellian System's\] history, detailing each world and the events which led to the system's current political and economic ascendency. \[...\]

### THE CORELLIAN SECTOR
