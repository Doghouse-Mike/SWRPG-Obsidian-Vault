A character's gear is part of building their appearance and overall narrative. It ensures they have the tools they need to complete the job and fit the role of their archetype and career. There is an endless number of tools, made by a plethora of manufacturers, from obscure to market dominating. The categories below broadly split out gear into clearer distinctions (although there are arguments for overlap).

> The tables below include a plethora of items, some generic, some with specified models and clear mechanics. Ultimately, players and GMs should remember that tools operate as **right tools for the job** and are narrative implements rather than mechanical items.
