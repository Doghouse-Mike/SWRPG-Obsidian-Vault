The role of a Commander is to organize, manage, and lead the fighters and other people who serve in a military force so they accomplish truly great things.

> More information is available in **[[Category - Rebel Ranks|*Star Wars* Age of Rebellion]]** Roleplaying Game: *Core Rulebook* (page 70-72).
