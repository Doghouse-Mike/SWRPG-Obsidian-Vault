## COLLAPSE OF THE REPUBLIC

### WHAT'S IN THIS BOOK?

**Collapse of the Republic** is a sourcebook for *Star Wars* Roleplaying covering the period of the latter Clone Wars and the rise of the Galactic Empire.

#### SIDEBAR: USING ICONIC NPCS

Source: [^1]

This book contains information on many iconic *Star Wars* characters, from Skywalker to Cad Bane. Their profiles offer some representation of some, but not all, of their important roles and abilities the have during this era. \[...\]

#### SIDEBAR: THERE IS ANOTHER

Source: [^0-2]

The careers of [[Category - Clone Soldier|Clone Soldier]] and [[Category - Jedi|Jedi]] also appear in **[[Category - Clone Wars Begin|Rise of the Separatists]]**, the sourcebook for the early days of the Clone Wars. \[...\]

#### SIDEBAR: HEROIC-LEVEL PLAY

Source: [^0-2]

The characters seen in *Star Wars*: Episode III *Revenge of the Sith* and in the *Clone Wars* film and series are highly skilled and capable of amazing feats. \[...\]

Heroic-level play is intended to depict PCs with capabilities closer to those heroes of the *Star Wars* films and series. To reflect this, PCs gain an extra 150 XP. These experience points cannot be spent on characteristics, as they are intended to reflect the character's training and experiences rather than inherent abilities. Players also may not purchase any skills above rank 3 while building their Heroic-level PC.

Further, characters constructed for this gameplay approach gain access to 9,000 credits' wroth of starting gear (this can include a vehicle). \[...\]

###### SIDEBAR: LIGHTSABER HILTS

Source: [^3-9]

\[...\] Each lightsaber hilt requires a crystal (otherwise it has it deals no damage, has no critical rating, and lacks many of its standard item qualities). However, a PC wanting to construct their own lightsaber should start with a hilt. The GM can have them make a check to find the hilt, with a difficulty based on its rarity, and pay its price. This represents the PC finding and paying for the raw materials. Then it is simply a matter of spending a few hours putting the materials together to build the hilt—no checks are necessary.

##### SIDEBAR: USING JETPACKS

Source: [^12]

A character using a jetpack operates as a vehicle, which means they require some special considerations during an encounter. \[...\]

If a character using a jetpack does not perform any maneuvers to move, that does not mean they are staying still. \[...\]

It's also important to remember that a character using a jetpack must perform a maneuver to accelerate or decelerate in order to take off or land safely.
