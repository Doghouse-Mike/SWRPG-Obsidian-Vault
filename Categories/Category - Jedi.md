Before the Clone Wars, the Jedi acted as an independent troubleshooting body and also as a monastic order meditating on the nature of the Force and philosophizing on members' role in the galaxy. Now, they serve as generals and commanders constantly involved in war.

> More information is available in **[[Category - Clone Wars End|Collapse of the Republic]]:** *An Era Sourcebook* (page 24-25) or **[[Category - Clone Wars Begin|Rise of the Separatists]]:** *An Era Sourcebook* (page 24-25).

### JEDI TRAINING

Like the careers in **[[Category - Way of the Force|Force and Destiny]]**, the Jedi career has only six career skills and begins with a free rank in three of these. Although the rules for character creation in **[[Category - On the Fringe|Edge of the Empire]]** and **[[Category - Rebel Ranks|Age of Rebellion]]** state that a career includes eight career skills and that character gains ranks in four of these at character creation, \[...\] simply follow the more specific instructions for gaining career skills and choosing starting ranks in the Jedi career description, which supersede the guidance in \[those\] core rulebooks.
