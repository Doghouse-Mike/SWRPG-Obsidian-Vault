Content with this category tag is found in one of the following homebrew supplements. On this page you can find links to the index pages (category tag) for homebrew on this wiki, as well as links to the homebrew download page.

> The use of homebrew content is always up to the Game Master's discretion. There are many assumptions and design choices made when translating a piece of *Star Wars* content into the Narrative Dice system which one may agree or disagree with. Content can work in one campaign and not in the next. As with the official content, what rules work for your game will depend on your group and the story you want to tell.
