A character can control a remote directly through a linked datapad or advanced slicer gear. Commanding a remote generally requires a maneuver, though more complex commands might require an action and a [[Computers (Int)|Computers]] check. At the GM's discretion, especially convoluted orders might require far longer to program.[^1]
