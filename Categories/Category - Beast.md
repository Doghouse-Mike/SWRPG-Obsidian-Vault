The *Star Wars* Roleplaying Game usually presents beasts as adversaries to encounter in the wilds, but those in the **Ace** and **Seeker** careers may have a more nuanced look upon them. The former may seek to use beasts as a mount, riding them into engagements with the Empire. The latter is able to form a connection with beasts through the Force and bond with them.

> There is much more to a beast than can be presented in the tables below. Each has their own special abilities drawn from the environment they developed in. Their skills are often quite similar, though they vary somewhat depending on their ecological niche!
