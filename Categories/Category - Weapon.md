In the *Star Wars* Roleplaying Game, a character is usually in need of a weapon. This category includes any piece of gear or equipment that can be used as a weapon, by design or as a particularly unique improvised tool.

> Weapons are primarily distinguished by whether they are **melee** or **ranged**.

## [[Category - Ranged Weapon|RANGED]]

Ranged weapons are typically used with the [[Gunnery (Ag)|Gunnery]], [[Ranged - Light (Ag)|Ranged (Light)]] or [[Ranged - Heavy (Ag)|Ranged (Heavy)]] skill to make attacks. They include energy weapons, blasters, slugthrowers, explosives, ordnance, and other exotic items.
