Attachments can improve a piece of armor or a weapon, or drastically change its function. Attachments are largely plug-and-play in the narrative system, though as aftermarket (and often custom) modifications to an item, they can come with narrative drawbacks... especially when modified further.

> These tables only show the many options available but do not make clear what the limitations, requirements, and effect of the mods are (albeit some can be implied from their name). For more information or when looking for something specific, the sub category pages and attachment pages themselves will be more useful.
