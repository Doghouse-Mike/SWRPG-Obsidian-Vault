While starships are distinguished by size-based classes, vehicles are distinguished primarily by their form of propulsion for getting across planets. These define what hazards they might encounter and what environments they are best suited to operate in. Propulsion also determines the price, capacity for certain amenities, and overall use-cases. Broadly, vehicles are either tied to the ground and below or able to fly to some degree.

> The profiles presented in these tables are a very limited look at a vehicle. Key characteristics such as **Maximum Altitude** and **Depth**, **crew numbers**, **capacity** (both passengers and encumbrance), **weapons**, and **customisability** are all important to consider! If you want to compare between these, there are further tables once the type of vehicle is selected.
