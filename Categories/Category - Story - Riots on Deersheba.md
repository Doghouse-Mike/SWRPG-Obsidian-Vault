## RIOTS ON DEERSHEBA

**Riots on Deersheba** is a full-length campaign for play with an experienced group of Player Characters (PCs) from **Edge of the Empire** (for PCs with around 300xp beyond character creation) wishing to involved themselves in the early days of the rebellion on a relatively untravelled planet of the Mid Rim. \[...\]
