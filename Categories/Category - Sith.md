This career is designed for characters deeply immersed and dedicated to the dark side of the force.

> More information is available in **[[Category - Old Republic|The Old Republic]]:** *An Era Sourcebook* (pages 11-13).

**Career Skills:** [[Coercion (Will)|Coercion]], [[Discipline (Will)|Discipline]], [[Lore (Int)|Knowledge (Lore)]], [[Lightsaber (Br)|Lightsaber]], [[Resilience (Br)|Resilience]] and [[Stealth (Ag)|Stealth]]

**Sith begin the game with a Force rating of 1.**

**Specializations**

- [[Acolyte]]
- [[Blademaster]]
- [[Lord]]
- [[Sorcerer]]

### SITH SCHOOLING

Like the careers in **[[Category - Way of the Force|Force and Destiny]]**, the Sith career has only six career skills and begins with a free rank in three of these. Although the rules for character creation in **[[Category - On the Fringe|Edge of the Empire]]** and **[[Category - Rebel Ranks|Age of Rebellion]]** state that a career includes eight career skills and that character gains ranks in four of these at character creation, \[...\] simply follow the more specific instructions for gaining career skills and choosing starting ranks in the Jedi career description, which supersede the guidance in \[those\] core rulebooks.
