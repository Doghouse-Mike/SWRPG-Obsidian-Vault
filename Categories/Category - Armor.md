While characters are assumed to have their personal clothing items as a matter of course, and the GM should encourage characters to consider the narrative implications of their mundane outfit, the outfits presented in the tables below are broadly categorised as **Armor**. They are the key source of additional **Defense**and **Soak** for a character, yet not all do so, fitting more like type of clothing or mundane outfit with *explicit mechanical effects*.

> The categories presented here are drawn from the narrative of these armor sets and not a hard rule presented in the rulebook. Most outfits have more effects than their shared characteristics and attributes, which could be guessed at from their name.
