This sturdy, infamous factory-custom podracer relies on an experimental engine design using a special injection system made to generate a more advanced and effective thrust than regular models. It trades boost and efficient cooling for more raw performances in speed and maneuverability, also at the cost of finicky commands and inputs and a relatively unstable fuel mixture. The sheer size of these ramjet engines and their angular, menacing design make it instantly recognizable, all without sacrificing air intakes.

> More information about the vehicle is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Plug-F_Mammoth_Split-X_engine).
