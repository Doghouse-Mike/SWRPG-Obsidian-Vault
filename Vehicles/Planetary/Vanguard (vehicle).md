The *Vanguard*, nicknamed the V-Wheel, is a ground vehicle designed by Valkeri Enterprises for use exclusively by the Jedi Order. It’s the landbased equivalent of their Jedi *Vector* starfighters, with a smooth and sleek aesthetic. It uses six treads on its underside to move over rough terrain. Jedi outposts have at least one *Vanguard* in their standard kit, engineered to operate in the environments of the outposts' locations. To increase it’s adaptability, the *Vanguard* is able operate as a repulsorlift speeder as well as a wheeled transport. The *Vanguard* is capable of storing two Veil speeders within it. As with the *Vector*, the weapons systems of the *Vanguard* require a Jedi lightsaber to act as a key.
