### MAGNETIC GRAPNEL LAUNCHER

Similar to the electromagnetic harpoon launcher found on Incom's [[T-47 Airspeeder|T-47 airspeeder]], the Swift 3's magnetic grapnel launcher (MGL) is a non-lethal tool used to capture and ensnare targets.

Using the MGL to control or tow a piloted vehicle requires a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Piloting - Planetary (Ag)|Piloting (Planetary) check]]**. The exact outcome of the skill check is left to the Game Master's discretion.
