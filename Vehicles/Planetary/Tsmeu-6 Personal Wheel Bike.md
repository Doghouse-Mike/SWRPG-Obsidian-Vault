### ADDITIONAL RULES

**All-Terrain Mode:** The Tsmeu-6's legs can take over the vehicle's locomotion to allow it to cross over rough terrain. As long as it's moving at speed 2 or lower, the Tsmeu-6 removes three [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice applied to [[Piloting - Planetary (Ag)|Piloting (Planetary)]] checks by navigational hazards.
