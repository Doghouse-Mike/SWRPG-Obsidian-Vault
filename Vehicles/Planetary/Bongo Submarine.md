### PLENTY IN THE SEA

Source: [^0-1]

The profile presented represents a "typical" bongo submarine. Because each bongo is handcrafted, GMs should feel free to modify this profile for individual examples. The most common variations occurs in the passenger capacity, encumbrance capacity, and silhouette of the vehicle. Due to their unique nature, it is very unlikely for a bongo to have customization hard points.
