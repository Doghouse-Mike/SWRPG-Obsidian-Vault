Produced by the relatively unknown Utapau-based vehicle and starship manufacturer Buuper Torsckil Abbey Devices, the scoop skimmer is a civilian speeder which has gone beyond filling the sinkholes of Utapau and found itself used across many core planets of the galaxy. A relatively standard civilian model, the repulsor was more reliable due to the tighter regulations and standards found on Utapau. The reliability is sought after by a market which doesn’t expect to get into fights with their speeders, hence how quickly the vehicle spread across the galaxy.

On Utapau, a special model of the scoop skimmer is often seen in the local authority’s livery and mounting emergency lights. While not particularly impressive as a pursuit craft, it saved the local governing body from spending money in the importing of specialised police vehicles. The special model is actually an after-market modification made to the civilian model, and with the right tools and specialty parts from the factory, anyone with a scoop skimmer could make the conversion of their own craft.
