Known more commonly as the Wookiee catamaran, this airspeed is another example of their exceptional hand-craftsmanship. The hulls built from the strong wood of the wroshyr trees, the craft was originally designed for sports and fishing. As such, this vehicle was not particularly apt for combat—though many were pressed into service during the Battle of Kashyyyk. Thanks to their civilian nature, they were quite adaptive and easy to retrofit.

By the end of the clone wars, the catamarans had been retrofitted with missile launchers, bomb chutes, and repeating blasters. Early on, passengers would use the small mounts designed for hunting with their personal bowcasters to fire on the move.
