The primary artillery piece used by the Grand Army of the Republic, the AV-7 by Taim & Bak was a ubiquitous sight in all forward operating bases and ground campaigns, supporting the advance. It was specifically an anti-vehicle cannon, though its plasma ammunition was also particularly effective against the mechanical opponent it often faced, droids. It is crewed by only a single clone trooper, reducing the manpower needed to establish a full artillery battery.

The weapon would deploy to the battlefield using a repulsorlift, as many hover tanks could, but would need to deploy its walker feet to stabilise for firing the main cannon. These walker legs could be used to move as well, to retain firing capacity. The cannon was similar to those mounted in the alcoves of a Venator-class star destroyer, with the damage output of a heavy turbolaser.
