This podracer immediately became (in)famous due to its unusual design using four engines instead of the usual two. Experimental and whimsical, and difficult to pilot without being multiped, it comes without rival for sheer, raw speed, and also boasts an impressive boost package but at the expense of a very poor cooling and a very stiff piloting. Its turbofan engines take time to spool up and have poor speed control, making it somewhat hard for the podracer to reach its impressive ceiling.

> More information about the vehicle is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BT310_Quadra).
