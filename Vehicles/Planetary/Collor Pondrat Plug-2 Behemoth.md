This model of podracer definitely stays in the line of large, intimidating factory-custom engines made by its manufacturer. Unlike its rather experimental predecessor - the Plug-F Mammoth - however, this one favors a more balanced approach with a very good overall performance, solid controls on top of a powerful boost. The large radiators mounted in a radial shape at the back of the engines offer a stellar ability at sustaining heat in spite of the decidedly abysmal cooling package coming with it, which stays pretty much in line with Collor Pondrat’s philosophy on designs able to shrug off heat so well that they don’t need much cooling in the first place. The engines also tend to be on the more fragile side for their size and their gigantic air intakes feed into a blocky combustion chamber, giving onto four exhausts on each engine and offering great propulsive power in spite of its mass. This amount of factory-custom tuning however comes heavily at the cost of modding possibilities.
