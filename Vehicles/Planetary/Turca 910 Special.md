The 910 Special, also known as the Turca Special, features modest performances and parts, and the factory Cooling on the 910 base model was so poor that when podracing pilot Toy Dampner installed a completely new one, it became standard on the design. Indeed, customizability being the main feature of the craft, it helped the model be improved over time like a good vintage, and alleviate the mediocre default attributes attributes of the frame. Sporting square, shield plates on top of both turbofan engines, the podracer gets a modicum of added protection against collisions, be it with the track environment, or with other podracers.

> More information about the vehicle is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/910_Special).
