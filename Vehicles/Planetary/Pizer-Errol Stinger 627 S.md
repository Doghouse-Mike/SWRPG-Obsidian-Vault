The Stinger 627 S radically invests into safety, an advanced repair system and pilot controls, as well as a state of the art cooling device. This is definitely not an oversight considering the frailty of the design, which counts among the most brittle jet engines in podracing. Extremely maneuverable, the podracer is however not known for its speed performances. The base platform also presents fundamental imbalances between cooling, strain resilience and the minimal heat generated by its weak boost, which tends to encourage racers to modify their craft pretty extensively
