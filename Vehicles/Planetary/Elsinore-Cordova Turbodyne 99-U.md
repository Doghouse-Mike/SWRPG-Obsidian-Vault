Known for its highly unusual, insectoid profile, the Turbodyne 99-U’s main strength lies in its maneuverability and its ability to sustain high engine strain. With its lighter, shorter engines, the design is also focused on mastery over speed variations rather than pure optimal speed, boasting good acceleration on top of advanced airbrakes, and the pod behind offers unparalleled control and avionics. Otherwise unremarkable, it still allows for an unusually high service ceiling with powerful repulsors, probably tied to its need for a higher clearance than the average podracer.

> More information about the vehicle is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TurboDyne_99-U).
