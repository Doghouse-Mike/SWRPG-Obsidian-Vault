A very sturdy podracer produced on Vulpter for the famous vulptereen racer Dud Bolt, able to endure more than a beating and hold all manners of strenuous situations very well, the 327 comes equipped with a surprisingly good track grip, solid braking, good speed, but no real maneuverability. With not a lot more to show for, the podracer has nevertheless been called an incredible machine by famous podracing annoncer Fodedesinbeed Annodue
