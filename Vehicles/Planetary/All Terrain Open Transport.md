The All Terrain Open Transport is the Republic’s troop carrier of choice for traversing difficult terrains where aerial transport is inadvisable. The AT-OT is reminiscent of the AT-TE, with an additional pair of legs, two missing frontal guns and no mass driver cannon. Instead of a cannon, the hull is an open structure in which the clones can be carried across hazardous terrain. When seated, passengers are protected from ground attack due to the height of the armour and walker’s clearance. However, the open top was a massive weak spot which could easily be exploited by aircraft.

The AT-OT was used extensively on swampy planets with thick forest cover, reducing its weakness against air attack and serving the purpose of troop transport where other vehicles would otherwise struggle. Knowing that the AT-OT was not supposed to see combat was not the relief clones would like it to be when they were required to board this vehicle, and many preferred to walk alongside it rather than put themselves in the crosshairs on these lumbering walkers.

### ADDITIONAL RULES

**Top Attack Vulnerability:** Any flying vehicle attacking the AT-OT can use the Gain the Advantage action to make an attack run from above. This reduces the Critical Rating of the attacker’s weapons against the AT-OT by one and reduces its Armour by 2. In addition, any damage dealt to the AT-OT this way is also dealt to the passengers of the walker.
