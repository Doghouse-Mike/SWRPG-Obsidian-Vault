Sporting retro-styled asymmetrical engine housings, this podracer has been outclassed after its discontinuation and displays very lax performance compared to other racing machines. With its short fanjets, The KRT is mediocre in every way, but if the machine has one potentially redeeming factor, it is its Boost threshold granted by its enormous chambers dedicated to post combustion, which more than doubles the design's design's intended limits. This means that the 410C can potentially boast a maximum velocity approaching 820kph, but this is not advisable, as performing at that speed can quickly overheat the engines. Speeds over 1000 kph have been recorded utilizing the KRT-410C's overdrive "design flaw", but this was achieved only after intense and very expensive modifications.

> More information about the vehicle is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/KRT_410C).
