The XT Beetle was the solution Baktoid Armour Workshop to the [[Multi Troop Transport|MTT]]’s inability to function in a range of environments. Instead of using the reliable yet temperamental repulsorlifts which were already being stretched in lifting such a heavy vehicle, they used 10 pairs of mechanical legs. The engineers also developed the hull into a larger size and took the opportunity to improve on the MTT design. This resulted in a craft much larger, one that could serve as a command centre after the deployment of its carried forces.

Republic forces only rarely see an XT Beetle in combat, and often only when the CIS is on their back legs and have been pushed back to their command post. They aren’t particularly capable of combat, but are heavily armoured so that they can be part of an initial landing in a new battle theater.

> More information about the vehicle is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/XT_Beetle_Transport).
