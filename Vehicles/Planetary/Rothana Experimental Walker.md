The Rothana Experimental Walker (abbreviated REW) is the latest and greatest large personnel walker developed by Rothana Heavy Engineering. The REW is as yet unnamed and only a handful of prototypes have been produced, including one for the Republic Fair on Valo.

The REW is a six- legged, low slung walker designed to hold both passengers and cargo though rough terrain and hazardous environments. A number of significant mining companies have already placed orders for the REW, for when it enters full production. In addition to being incredibly sturdy, the REW is designed to be very user friendly, with a sophisticated ED-N droid brain managing the majority of the walker’s operations.

> More information about the vehicle is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rothana_experimental_walker).
