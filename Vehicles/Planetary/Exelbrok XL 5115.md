This is a simple model of podracer, which is nonetheless capable of reaching solid speeds and boasts a decently layered performance, where nothing is truly lacking. Its wide, double-layered turbofan powerful engines are capable of handling big speed variations and sustain its boost quite effectively, although their durability leaves something to be desired, which its designers seem to have had in mind when accomodating for its repair system.

> More information about the vehicle is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/XL_5115_engine).
