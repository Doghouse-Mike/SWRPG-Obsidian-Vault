A craft distinguished for its hefty accelerator and low to average top speed, the Wellos & Allos 825 also proves easy to maneuver and displays a sizeable combustion chamber. Thanks to the design of the radiator fins attached to the engines, the podracer is able to cool down pretty well at the expense of a wider silhouete, and proves unusually durable for its average size. The overall concept focuses best on track performances, but leaves all the support systems to be somewhat desired, notably its cheap repair module.

> More information about the vehicle is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wellos_and_Allos_825).
