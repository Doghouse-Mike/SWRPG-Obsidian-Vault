The Mining Guild often rolls out a large landspeeder known as an ore crawler to planets intended for full exploitation of resources. They are capable of clearing landscapes of all biological life to allow for direct access to surface deposits of ore and raw materials. They were once restricted to uninhabited planets, cleared for use by Republic license, but the Empire has relaxed such regulations. They are slow and bulky, but particularly well armored for a civilian vehicle.

> More information about the vehicle is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ore_crawler).
