The culmination of the insectoid and odd designs of the Techno Union, the octuptarra magna tri-droid is an upscaled version of the already large octuptarra droid. Designed for all terrain combat, this droid is a true artillery walker and is also sufficiently armoured to fight close to the front lines. As per its name, it carries a trio of weapons upon a rotating disk at the peak of three legs. These weapons will swap out during an advancing assault, allowing the droid to make multiple attacks with its laser cannons in quick succession. Additionally, the ordnance pods run alongside these cannons, and they can also fire in quick succession while they reload in a different facing position.

The octuptarra was deployed in similar battlefield formations to the [[OG-9 Homing Spider Droid|OG-9]], but was better suited in the role of frontline artillery. Eventually the tri-droid would come to occupy all major battle of the Clone Wars, particularly in urban fights where its legs can be put to good use in climbing city facades.

### DROID VEHICLES

Source: [^2]

Some of the vehicles deployed by the CIS are simply gigantic droid models. For the purposes of skill checks, the droids in these section are best represented as rivals. They should be given a 1 in all characteristics and have the following skills: [[Computers (Int)|Computers]] 2, [[Gunnery (Ag)|Gunnery]] 3, [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] 2, [[Piloting - Planetary (Ag)|Piloting (Planetary)]] 3, and [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]] 2. To represent improvements of the droid programming as the Clone Wars progresses, GMs should consider increasing the Agility or Cunning of the droid, or the ranks in key skills.

A droid vehicle is programmed to execute the role of multiple crew. To represent this, they should be able to take two actions per turn. The difficulty of each check is upgraded once.
