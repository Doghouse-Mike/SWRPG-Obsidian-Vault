The Titan 2150 is an incredibly fast podracer, its two massive high-pass turbofan engines bringing the speed and boost package needed to achieve those breakneck speeds. It is found severely lacking in maneuverability and takes time spooling up however. It became apparent that the injection system was finicky when famous pilot Ratts Tyerell jammed the accelerator of one of the engines of his own model, the Scatalpen, Scatalpen, leading to a lethal crash in the Laguna Caves of the Boonta Eve Classic.
