### ADDITIONAL RULES

**Stealth Systems:** The personal stealth flyer benefits from state-of-the-art stealth systems that render it almost invisible to passive and active scanners. This ship will not register on passive scanners that are short range or farther away from it (only passive scanners at close range can pick it up). In addition, increase the difficulty of any checks made to detect the vessel by two.
