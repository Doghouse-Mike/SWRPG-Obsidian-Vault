The civilian version of the heavily armoured 20-T railcrawler, the 16-M was widely used throughout frontier worlds for efficient transport where security was less of an issue or the value of cargo was low. Despite less armour plating and the lighter frame, it also held a less powerful engine, meaning it barely had a faster top speed than its younger sibling. The Mining Guild has oft used these transports in their operations to reduce costs in hauling the copious amounts of raw materials.

The railcrawler is capable of hauling 16-M intermodal cargo pods like the 20-T, but doesn’t have the engine power to pull more than a handful of the armoured pods. However, it can pull any number of the cargo pods built for this model. These each cost 5,000 credits with the same rarity as the drive car, are each Silhouette 4, have Armour 2, and a Hull Trauma Threshold of 18. They come in a variety of setups, with  up to  500 encumbrance,  50 passengers (in pull-down seats), or some combination thereof. Custom luxury cabins can be ordered for twice the price, which allow up to 6 passengers to travel in upmost luxury.
