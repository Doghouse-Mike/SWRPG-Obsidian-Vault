Manufactured exclusively for the Jedi Order by Valkeri Enterprises, the *Veil* is a high performance speeder bike. Roughly four metres long the Veil is, like most speeder bikes and swoops, little more than a powerful repulsor strapped to an incredibly light frame and saddle. The pilot controls its movements via two sets of handlebar mounted controls and two sets of footrest mounted controls.

While the Veil is an extremely fast speeder, it is this speed that is its main vulnerability. One wrong twitch of the controls and the *Veil* is sent careening off-course. To keep the vessel as light as possible, all armour plating and weaponry have been removed from the *Veil*.
