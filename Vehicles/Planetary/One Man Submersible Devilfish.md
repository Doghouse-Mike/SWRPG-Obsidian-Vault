The OMS Devilfish was produced for the Grand Army of the Republic SCUBA troopers for use in theaters located on water worlds. Conceptually similar to a STAP or speeder bike, the Devilfish doesn’t have a compartment for its pilot. Instead, the user holds onto the control bars and is tugged along by the submarine. While the Devilfish is rated for a depth of 350 metres, it is simple enough to function much deeper, as long as the user is also capable of surviving at those depths.

The on-board weapons could make quick work of most CIS droid models deployed underwater, and also fight off larger submarine craft. However, it did not provide much cover for the user, who could easily be shot off the submarine. This meant that the Devilfish often survived engagements and was regularly salvaged by the opposition to use for themselves. It wasn’t un- common to find a Devilfish painted in the CIS livery.
