The Vesper Dux Ultra-3 wasn’t initially designed for podracing, but for battlefields on Ghishi, the native homeworld of Kraid Nemmeso. Unable to field the machine and compete, its designer and owner decided instead to adjust it for podracing competition. It remains a thickly armoured, slumbering beast of a podracer with oversized engines able to push surprisingly fast and profit from its power to reach top notch speeds. Everything tied to maneuverability and handling however, is an absolute afterthought, and the Vesper Dux is prone to fast overheat due to the amount of effort its engines have to produce in order to move it, and even moreso at these speeds.

> More information about the vehicle is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vesper_Dux_Ultra-3).
