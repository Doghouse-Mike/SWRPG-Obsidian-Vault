One of the iconic weapons used at the outset of the Clone Wars during the Battle of Geonosis, the LR1K is a particular specimen of Geonosian engineering. Manufactured by Gordarl Weaponsmiths, the sonic cannon was a vehicle without its own method of propulsion. It did have an integrated repulsor engine but required a beast of burden such as the Orray to pull it along.

Primarily used as a crowd control weapon, with the same technology as used in the [[Geonosian Sonic Blaster|Geonosian sonic blaster]], it could easily kill a group of clones or jedi with a single blast. The cannon required two crew members to operate, though a targeting computer system did most of the heavy lifting in its use.

> More information about the vehicle is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/LR1K_sonic_cannon).
