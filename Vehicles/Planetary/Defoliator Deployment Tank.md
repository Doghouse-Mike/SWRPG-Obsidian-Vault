The DDT was a platform for a new experimental weapon which used the [[AAT-1 Hover Tank|AAT]] hull as its basis. The new weapon, the defoliator capsule launcher, replaced the original turret. In addition, stabilisers were extended out to the rear of the tank’s base to account for the heavier main weapon. Only two prototypes of this tank were ever reported to Republic Intelligence, and both were destroyed in action. However, it is entirely possible that the DDT would enter limited production in the later years of the war.

Unlike the AAT, the DDT was more artillery than tank. The launcher needed to be loaded manually by droids outside the tank. While it could be close to the front lines, it needed to be properly protected at all times. The defoliator was a fearsome piece of technology to face as a clone or other biological being. Using a high intensity radiation blast which appeared as a raging wildfire or incendiary wall, the defoliator would obliterate all organic matter within a sizeable area. As with many of the CIS’ devastating weapons, the blast is not too difficult to outrun as trained fighter.

**Defoliator Capsule:** The defoliator only deals damage to organic matter, leaving the earth barren while droids and vehicles are unharmed.
