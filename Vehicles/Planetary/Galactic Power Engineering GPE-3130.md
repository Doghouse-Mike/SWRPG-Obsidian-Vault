The GPE-3130 is a model of podracer designed for straight-line speed performance, but favoring a good weight to thrust ratio instead of using oversized engines for raw power. Its high speed under boost combined with a good baseline maneuverability makes it a terror on raceways with broad, gentle curves, but otherwise difficult to control in tight spaces or sharp corners. Its twin ThrustMaster 9000 aerospike rocket engines, augmented by a pair of smaller ramjet engines are also known to require seemingly endless maintenance and the fuel needed to power them is highly volatile and prone to exploding. The vehicle is awkward-looking and its repulsorpod is uncomfortable at best, with an unintuitive control layout, which makes the craft universally panned by critics.

> More information about the vehicle is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/GPE-3130_Podracer).

### SPECIAL RULES

**Neverending maintenance:** The podracer takes 1 Hull Trauma after every race.

**Volatile fuel:** If the vehicle overheats, roll a Force dice every Round as long as the podracer is overheating. One black pip will generate a small explosion resulting in one more additional Hull Trauma, 2 black pips will cause a major explosion and deal +2 HT (or 20 personal damage) to the podracer and to any other vehicle in the vicinity at Close Range.

**Auxiliary Engines Profile:** Gain a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die to negotiate tight obstacles.
