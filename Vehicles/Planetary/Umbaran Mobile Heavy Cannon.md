The heaviest vehicle produced by Ghost Armaments, the MHC was a heavy walker used in the role of mobile artillery platform. It used the same unique heavy weapon found on the Umbaran manufactured starfighter and repulsor tank. Its heavy armour and shielding were used to prevent the walker from being destroyed as it got closer to the front lines. This was necessary, as its intuitive movement controls made it easy to walk right into enemy formations for particularly fanatic pilots. At these ranges, it could use its ventral laser cannons to eliminate infantry looking for weakspots (of which there were virtually none). Only ordnance from starships and other heavy vehicles could really penetrate its defenses.

The MHC was a vehicle that either dominated the field or was quickly dispatched by opposing ordnance. This made its deployment require good intel of the situation to use adequately. The umbaran designers have certainly gained a reputation for producing niche and powerful situational craft.

> More information about the vehicle is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Umbaran_mobile_heavy_cannon).
