Originally produced for daimlo pilot Zanales, this model of podracer sports strong air scoops not dissimilar to the Radon-Ulzer models at the front of the engines, which increases maneuverability and cornering dramatically. The materials used in its making are sturdy and the frame robust, but this comes at the cost of repair efficiency and acceleration. Fortunately, the cooling system coming with it ensures that heat is only an afterthought, for otherwise relatively standard low-pass turbofan engines.

> More information about the vehicle is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Banshee-11).
