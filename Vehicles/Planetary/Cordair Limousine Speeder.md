The Cordair Limousine Speeder is a luxury, personal airspeeder. Perma-polished quadranium steel is moulded into beautiful flowing curves and decorated with silver highlights. Polished intake radiators sit on the speeder’s two front vanes in a neo- Industrial style. The Cordair’s two-seater passenger compartment is appointed in plush nerf leather and lamas wood-grain. Discretely hidden buttons allow for a privacy screen between the passengers and the speeder’s single driver. Currently available in “Cobalt Burn”, “Rose Scarlet” and “Polished Onyx”, the Cordair is a favourite among Imperial elites, with the trend, like all trends, starting on Coruscant. Prominent Senators such as Mon Mothma, Orn Free Taa and Gila Thule all travel the capital in style in their Cordairs.

Despite the Cordair’s sleek and elegant design, the speeder is surprisingly robust under the hood. The quadranium steel hull offers a higher degree of protection than in most luxury vehicles, one of the things that attracts VIP riders to the Cordair. Additionally, the speeder’s intake radiators aren’t just for show, and if necessary, the Cordair can achieve an impressive top speed.
