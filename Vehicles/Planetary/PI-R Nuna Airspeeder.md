Something of a luxury airspeeder, the PI-R *Nuna* can be seen in the skies of wealthy worlds across the Core and Inner Rim. The PI-R *Nuna* comes from the Naboo design house of Verger Primaris, designers best known for their *Command* line of staryachts. The PI-R *Nuna* follows in that lineage, a sleek, flat, wide-winged vessel, resembling the Nuna bird, of which it is named.

Equipped with powerful Arcex IX ion engines, and a lightning quick Mahara nav system the *Nuna* is capable of astonishingly straight- ahead speed and aerial agility from low altitude to the limits of the atmosphere. The *Nuna* is also capable of defending itself when necessary with a pair of light, wing mounted light laser cannons.
