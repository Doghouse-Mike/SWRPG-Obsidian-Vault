The Shelba 730, once adorned with proper livery, is often considered as one of the most flashy and elegant podracers in tournaments. What its medium rocket engines lack in the speed department, they make up for with a potent thrust and an elaborate, efficient control system. Average elsewhere overall, it also comes with unusually short steelton cables tying the pod to the engines. The long fins affixed at the front of the flat intake of each engine can be used as air flaps, much like the ones attached at the back around the exhausts. A series of lateral radiators also help the podracers shedding excess heat and cool the engines but at the expense of aerodynamics.
