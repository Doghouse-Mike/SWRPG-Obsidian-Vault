This massive podracer boasts powerful twin ramjet reactors linked into one, bringing it up there with some of the best models in terms of raw power and straight line performance. But like for a lot of super-heavy machines it takes time for the engines to reach their full potential, engines that are well respected for their resilience before seeing their integrity threatened. It is however poorly maneuverable, and the support systems are average to passable.
