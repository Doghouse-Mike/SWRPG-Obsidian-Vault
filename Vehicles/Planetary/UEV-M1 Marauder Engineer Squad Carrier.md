The Marauder engineer squad carrier by SoroSuub is a medium armored repulsorlift vehicle designed to carry a squad of twelve combat engineers and their attendant gear into the field with armor support.

> More information about the vehicle is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/UEV-M1_Marauder_engineer_squad_carrier).
