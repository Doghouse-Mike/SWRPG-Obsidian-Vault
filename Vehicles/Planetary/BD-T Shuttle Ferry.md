A common sight on worlds all across the galaxy, shuttle ferries are used as intercity civilian transports. They come in a variety of models from hundreds of different manufacturers, but the BD-T from Claymatsu Heavy Industries is the most popular.

These two-engine repulsorcraft have a spacious interior including seating for at least thirty-one passengers arranged in sets of four along with a rear gallery. They also include overhead space for luggage storage. The shuttle is driven by a single pilot who is seated in an elevated glass cockpit in the front of the ship. Entry and exit from the vehicle is facilitated by use of a staircase droid, which is deployed in ferry stations. The BD-T is usually equipped with a screen above the door displaying information about the shuttle's destination, and overhead lights, as well as wide windows which offer a clear view of the surrounding locales.

While not particularly handsome, the BD-T Shuttle Ferry is sturdy and comfortable and many of them have been in service for multiple decades without any issue. Often they are painted in custom colours, or with eyecatching designs to help them stand out or blend in, depending on the environment. The BD-T Shuttle Ferries on Ferrix are painted with a muted red and gold, traditional Ferrix colours.
