### ADDITIONAL RULES

**Rapid Walker Deployment:** The LAAT/c can deploy a walker to the field while still moving. As an action, the pilot may make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Piloting - Planetary (Ag)|Piloting (Planetary) check]]** upgraded a number of times equal to the LAAT/c's current speed. If the pilot succeeds, the walker deploys safely. If the pilot fails, the walker deploys but suffers an immediate Critical Hit as if it had suffered a collision.
