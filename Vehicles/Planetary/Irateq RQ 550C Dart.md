The RQ 550C Dart is designed around a philosophy of precision and pure performances over any other consideration. With absolutely no room for weight, most of its plating and systems are made from incredibly lightweight materials, at the cost of sturdiness, leaving in fact, no room for mistakes either. All margins have been cut and pushed to their absolute limits in order to get a podracer with unmatched maneuverability, controls and support systems. However, the frame, on top of proving extremely fragile and brittle, cannot support the strongest engines, which are also very prone to overheating, somewhat balanced by an impressive cooling system.
