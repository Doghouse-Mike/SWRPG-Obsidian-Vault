Colloquially known as Freeco bikes—clone trooper shorthand for "freezing cold"—CK-6s are small, fast, one-position swoops optimized for use in sub-zero and artic conditions.

> More information about the vehicle is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/CK-6_swoop_bike).
