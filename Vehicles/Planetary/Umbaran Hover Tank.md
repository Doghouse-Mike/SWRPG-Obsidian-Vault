This weapon platform produced by the Umbaran Ghost Armaments was built around two large electromagnetic plasma cannons, the signature weapon used as heavy ordnance by the planet’s military. Similar in strength to a concussion missile and using the same technological concept as a plasma disruptor, the electromagnetic plasma cannon melts through armour and has tremendous explosive power. It was highly effective against the vehicles used by the Republic during the invasion of Umbara, but this particular platform was the most vulnerable of vehicles with this weapon.

The hover tank was used as an assault vehicle, but its main weapons allowed it to act as close quarters artillery. Its armored cockpit protected the pilot from most smallarms, but its massive weapons made it vulnerable to flanking maneuvers as they were not well armoured and presented a visual blind spot. Their large size was useful for storing the tank in a compact manner, serving as landing struts and allowing to dock vertically.

> More information about the vehicle is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Umbaran_hover_tank).
