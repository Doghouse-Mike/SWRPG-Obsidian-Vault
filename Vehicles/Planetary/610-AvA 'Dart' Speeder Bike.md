The 610-AvA Dart is a civilian version of Aratech's [[614-AvA Speeder Bike|614-AvA]] military speeder bike, a predecessor of the famous 74-Y used by the Imperial Army.

> More information about the vehicle is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/610-AvA_%22Dart%22_speeder_bike).

## 610-AVA "DART" SPEEDER BIKE

Source: [^1]

Collapsing or expanding the Dart speeder biker requires a maneuver. When collapsed, the vehicle is silhouette 1 and cannot be used.
