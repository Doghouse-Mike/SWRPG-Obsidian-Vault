The Kulless Squall is an unique podracer of custom design created by the former podracer mechanic Shrivel Braittrand, using materials coming entirely from a refurbished Bin Gassi Quadrijet, and radically redesigning the engines by removing the quad barrel for a single main exhaust centered around a jet cone. While it took away the sheer speed performances of the late Bin-Gassi, it still retains a solid capacity in that field, and gains what its progenitor lacked: some turning and maneuverability. While unable to keep most of the heavy plating built around the engines of the Quadrijet, the Kulless Squall still proves surprisingly tough for a podracer that shed most of its shell.

> More information about the vehicle is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kulless_Squall).
