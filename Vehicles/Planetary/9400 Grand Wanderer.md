### SPECIAL RULE

**Smooth Ride:** The 9400 Grand Wanderer, for all its lack of style, is a relatively comfortable accommodation, easily customizable to individual needs. A character who lives in one gains a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die on any checks to recover strain while in the 9400.
