A more combat focused variant of the Imperial Troop Transport, the Trexler Marauder boasts improved durability and handling at the expense of cargo/passenger capacity.

A somewhat recent addition to the Imperial arsenal, these vehicles went into production shortly after the battle of Hoth. After suffering crippling losses at the battles of Endor and Scariff the Imperial hierarchy shifted its focus to re-building the navy. As a result very few Marauders ever made it off the assembly line, making them something of a rarity.

> More information about the vehicle is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trexler_906_Armored_Marauder).
