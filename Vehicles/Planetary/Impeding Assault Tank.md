The IAT was an exotic anti-infantry walker designed by the Umbaran Ghost Armaments corporation. It was controlled by a single crew member and was covered in over a dozen blaster cannons. The pilot could use its weapon systems in a targeted way, especially its fire-linked and articulated forward-facing blaster cannons, or use its dorsal turrets as an area of denial weapon to suppress masses of infantry. Its name comes from the role it took in battlefields, where it would tunnel into the middle of enemy formations and emerge with a blanket of blaster fire.

As with many of Ghost Armaments’ products, the IAT was not widely available for sale, used exclusively by the Umbaran forces to deter invasions of their planet. It has cutting edge technology, using the same reflex control interface found in all their vehicles. This carried an inherent rayshielding that protected the tank from the small arms fire it was likely to face. As with many vehicles, it was ultimately susceptible to rocket launchers, which were capable of countering its shields.

### ADDITIONAL RULES

Source: [^2]

**Fire-linked Automated Guns:** The pilot can use the Blanket Barrage action (usually reserved to Silhouette 5 vehicles) except it affects all adversaries of Silhouette 1 or larger.

**Reflex Control Interface:** The shield containment of the cockpit projects a field of control systems that a pilot can use at the wave of a hand of movement of their body. The IAT requires the use of the [[Coordination (Ag)|Coordination]] skill to drive instead of the [[Piloting - Planetary (Ag)|Piloting (Planetary)]] skill. If the character has no skill ranks in [[Coordination (Ag)|Coordination]], the IAT becomes Handling -1 (or -2 while underground).

**Subterranean Operations:** The IAT is capable of digging through most natural earth. While traversing underground, the IAT reduces its speed to 1 and its handling to +0.
