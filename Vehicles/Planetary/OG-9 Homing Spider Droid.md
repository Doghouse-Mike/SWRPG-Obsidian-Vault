A model of self-propelled artillery manufactured by the Baktoid Armour Workshop and widely used by the Commerce Guild, the OG-9 was an entirely independent unit on the battlefield. Their role was to enforce Guild laws and perform asset protection on the wilderness ridden planets where many of their exploits are located. Unfortunately for this model, they were often employed in the role of regular armed walkers, something the manufacturer had not accounted for.

While the spider droid is capable of traversing all sorts of terrains, this was with the intent of positioning the artillery in prime positions which would be advantageous for providing cover fire. The legs aren’t well armoured, and the main mass of the droid contains an over-clocked reactor core which is prone to violent explosions when the casing is ruptured. The OG-9 was never intended to march directly onto the front lines of a battlefield.

> More information about the vehicle is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/OG-9_homing_spider_droid).

### DROID VEHICLES

Source: [^2]

Some of the vehicles deployed by the CIS are simply gigantic droid models. For the purposes of skill checks, the droids in these section are best represented as rivals. They should be given a 1 in all characteristics and have the following skills: [[Computers (Int)|Computers]] 2, [[Gunnery (Ag)|Gunnery]] 3, [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] 2, [[Piloting - Planetary (Ag)|Piloting (Planetary)]] 3, and [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]] 2. To represent improvements of the droid programming as the Clone Wars progresses, GMs should consider increasing the Agility or Cunning of the droid, or the ranks in key skills.

A droid vehicle is programmed to execute the role of multiple crew. To represent this, they should be able to take two actions per turn. The difficulty of each check is upgraded once.
