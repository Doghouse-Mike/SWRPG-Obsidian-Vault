The Block6 Tri-Ram is an old podracer design that still holds fast in the recent days. Its bizarre air intake configuration adding two auxiliary, smaller entries angled outward was specifically added to help maintain a good feed no mater how hard the podracer is cornering, but otherwise offers very run of the mill jet engines. It displays average to good performances, brakes well, cools down relatively fast, but doesn’t come equipped with a serious traction system nor a great maneuverability. Still, many consider it to be a good “vanilla” base to work on and customize.

> More information about the vehicle is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BRE_Block6_Tri-Ram).
