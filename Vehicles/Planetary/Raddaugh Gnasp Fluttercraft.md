Another example of wookiee engineering, this ornithopter is a particularly exotic form of airspeeder. Named after the predatory insects found on the Wookiee colony moon of Alaris Prime, this scouting craft was often used by rescue and reconnaissance operations through the jungles of Kashyyyk.

As such, developed originally for civil security, the fluttercraft was eventually pressed into service during the Clone Wars for quick transportation of couriers and recon units. It had to rely on its nimble and rapid frame to avoid being hit by enemy fire, but it was fitted with a rear-mounted laser cannon so that it could deter opponents from pursuit while escaping.

> More information about the vehicle is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Raddaugh_Gnasp_fluttercraft).
