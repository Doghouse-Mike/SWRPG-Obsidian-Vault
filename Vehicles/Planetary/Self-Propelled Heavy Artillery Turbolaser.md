A particularly massive walker produced in the same vein as the others made by Rothana Heavy Engineering, the SPHA-T was a mobile weapons platform with a built-in full personnel deck for operating and directing the main weapon system. This system was a modular one, capable of being swapped out for other specialised weapons for use on the battlefield. When used by the Republic, the special turbolaser mounting was used. This weapon was capable of outputting multiple pulses worth the fire of a battery of turbolasers within a short period of time. Unlike most artillery units, however, its construction prevented it from firing indirectly, needing a direct line of fire to its target.

However, its huge size was a major impediment to its role as ‘mobile’ artillery. Its 12 legs weren’t capable for much more than positioning, and certainly not capable for shifting the weapon to engage fast moving targets.
