A custom podracer fully designed by professional pilot Gasgano, the Ord Pedrovia boasts a nice speed boost package helped by a gigantic combustion chamber and four side boosters affixed to it, although the engines turn very hot after boosting. Its wide exhaust cones also help funneling the airflow output while keeping the exhausts thin. The whole machine has been so finely tuned to its limits that only the best pilots can take advantage of its full potential. Sporting a camera built in the back of the pod directly connected to a holoscreen on the control panel, it is possible for the pilot to keep an eye on a fully comprehensive three dimensional footage of any competitor tailing the vehicle during a race.

> More information about the vehicle is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ord_Pedrovia).
