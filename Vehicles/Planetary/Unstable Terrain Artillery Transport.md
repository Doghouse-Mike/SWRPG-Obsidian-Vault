Known as the “Trident” to the clones which served alongside it, the UT-AT was quite a unique design from Kuat Drive Yards. This is likely due to the Mekuun Corporation’s involvement in its development. Despite the moniker of All Terrain, walkers were not truly best suited to all environments. Following a number of AT-TE losses from collapsing terrain, the Grand Army of the Republic commissioned a heavy weapons platform that could be used in the gaps left by walkers. The result was a craft with unique repulsor emitters never before seen.

Mounting sixteen Mekuun Corporation repulsors in pairs on hydraulic pads, the UT-AT would be able to hover as a landspeeder despite its mass. The innova- tion was in its drive computer which automatically adjusted the repulsors to compensate for disturbances in the terrain beneath it and keep it smoothly trudging along. To ensure the systems respond properly, the vehicle was not much faster than most of its walker siblings. As an additional development, the UT-AT was given more powerful weaponry than the [[AT-TE]] and other contemporary walkers, giving it greater anti-armour ability.
