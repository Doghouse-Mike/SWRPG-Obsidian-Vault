With a solid maneuverability and an emphasis on integrated systems, and sizeable fins acting as radiators for an outstanding cooling system making up for medium sized jet engines prone to overheat, this podracer is designed for performance and agility before toughness: it is in fact fragile, its fins pretty much doubling engine size, but very also nimble and offers a solid speed. Able to accelerate very well at will, the J930 Dash-8 is perfectly suited to face any unexpected scenario, at the cost of durability.
