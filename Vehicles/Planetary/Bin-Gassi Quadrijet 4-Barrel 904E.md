This tough and sturdy podracer was built for speed and very powerful boosts with the help of its sizeable combustion chamber. Two variable sets on its quad barrel exhausts help it perform both at low or higher speeds. Able to handle high temperatures and taking advantage of an impressive boost module, the Quadrijet 904E can generate great speed out of its engines on demand, but the lack of cooling will be felt after a couple of uses. Agility was definitely an afterthought during its conception though, and it is prone to a lot of skid. The Quad Barrel is also known to be difficult to control for racers unfamiliar with the commands and the pilot interface, but will prove surprisingly responsive otherwise.
