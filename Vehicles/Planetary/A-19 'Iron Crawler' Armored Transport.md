Following the loss of several mining facilities and digger crawlers in the Outer Rim Territories, the need to provide adequate defenses for its valuable assets on uncivilized, frontier worlds of the galaxy led by the Corellia Mining Corporation to manufacture a new armored transport with enough firepower to deter marauders and large, aggressive predators.

> More information about the vehicle is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/A-19_%22Iron_Crawler%22_armored_transport).
