The Serabyss Howler is a cumbersome podracer that in the fashion of the Plug-G Behemoth, hits surprising degrees of agility unheard of for vehicles of that size. Its straight turbofan engines boast a solid speed but prove unable to accelerate well due to their size, which indicates that more consideration was brought to other aspects of the craft. Unparalleled controls and cooling however complement its boost package, allowing for repeated uses of the latter even considering its otherwise average performances.
