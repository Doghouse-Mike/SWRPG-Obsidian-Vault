**Vehicle Type/Model:** Walker[^0-1] *or* Heavy Assault Walker[^1-2]/AT-TE. \
**Manufacturer:** Rothana Heavy Engineering[^0-1]/Kuat Drive Yards[^0-1][^1-2]. \
**Sensor Range:** Short. \
**Crew:** One pilot, one co-pilot/spotter, one vehicle commander, up to five gunners. \
**Vehicle Complement:** 2 [[All Terrain Reconnaissance Transport|AT-RT Recon Transport walkers]].[^0-1] \
**Encumbrance Capacity:** 40. \
**Passenger Capacity:** Up to 20 troops with full combat gear.\
**Price/Rarity:** 100,000 credits (R)/4. \
**Customization Hard Points:** 2.

**Weapons:**[^0-1]

- Dorsal turret-mounted mass driver cannon (Fire Arc All; Damage 7; Critical 3; Range \[Short\]; [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] 2, [[Item Qualities#Slow-Firing (Passive)|Slow-firing]] 1).
- Forward-mounted quad medium laser cannons (Fire Arc Forward; Damage 6; Critical 3; Range \[Close\]; [[Item Qualities#Linked (Active)|Linked]] 3).
- Aft-mounted twin medium laser cannons (Fire Arc Rear; Damage 6; Critical 3; Range \[Close\]; [[Item Qualities#Linked (Active)|Linked]] 1).

*or* **weapons:**[^1-2]

- Turret-mounted mass-driver cannon (Fire Arc All; Damage 7; Critical 3; Range \[Short\]; [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] 2, [[Item Qualities#Slow-Firing (Passive)|Slow-firing]] 1).
- Two front-mounted twin light blaster cannons (Fire Arc Forward; Damage 4; Critical 3; Range \[Close\]; [[Item Qualities#Linked (Active)|Linked]] 1).
- Two aft-mounted light blaster cannons (Fire Arc Rear; Damage 4; Critical 3; Range \[Close\]).
