The 8T8's design is unusual in that the cockpit is joined to the fanjet engines forward of their intakes via fixed, fully faired brackets. With the Twin Block2 Special, the need for traditional podracer devices is eliminated, making the craft bear more of a resemblance to a traditional airspeeder. However, this configuration also puts the pilot at greater risk of injury or death from head-on collisions and prevents a good view and control by having most of the vehicle located behind instead of forward. Because of this threat, the pod puts heavy emphasis on turning, acceleration and braking power over speed, allowing great control. The cockpit is aerodynamic and fully enclosed, including a factory-equipped ejection system rare in factory customs and luxury lines. Despite this, the model is among the slowest podracers on the market due to its weight, size, and overbearing pilot assistance.

> More information about the vehicle is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/FG_8T8-Twin_Block2_Special).
