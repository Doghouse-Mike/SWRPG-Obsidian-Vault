Developed by Rothana Heavy Engineering to serve the needs of the Old Republic's civilian exploratory corps, the AT-EST is a six-legged, low-slung walker designed to haul both passengers and cargo through rough terrain and hazardous environments.

> More information about the vehicle is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/All_Terrain_Exploration_and_Survey_Walker).
