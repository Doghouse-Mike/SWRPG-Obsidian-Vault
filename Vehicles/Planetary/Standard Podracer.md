Podracer cockpits, also known as gondolas, are what pass for a crew compartment on a Podracer.

> More information about the vehicle is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Podracer).

## PODRACER COCKPIT

Podracer cockpits are simply a base upon which to build a custom Podracer. Cockpits come only with the listed systems and do not include engines, control cables, or any custom modifications.[^0-2]
