This podracer became instantly famous when the human racer Anakin Skywalker won the Boonta Eve Classic circa 32 BBY. Heavily customized and modified by its pilot, it earned the title of fastest podracer in the galaxy. However, this is the standard version, which doesn’t shine by its speed performance, but rather its highly adaptable profile, solid controls and top notch maneuverability. Not among the toughest podracers podracers on the market, its engines can still pack a punch for their size when compared to the competition on the smaller end.
