Based upon the popular [[Undicur-class Jumpspeeder|*Undicur*-class jumpspeeder]], the Flasherfish was designed specifically to fill the personal transportation niche on water planets where the local meteorological conditions precluded standard flyers and swoops.

> More information about the vehicle is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Flasherfish_personal_watercraft).
