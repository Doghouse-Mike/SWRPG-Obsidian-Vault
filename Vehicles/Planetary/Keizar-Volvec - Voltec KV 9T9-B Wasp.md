Produced by the typically unimaginative engineers at Keizar-Volvec, the 9T9-B does not defy expectations in its blandness. The first Podracer produced by the company, the Wasp is competent and well-designed, if not aggressively average. Using a pair of KVT Series 400 fanjets, the control system for the pod is sophisticated and durable, allowing the Podracer to take full advantage of their ample thrust power albeit bad brakes. Despite its staidness, the 9T9-B is highly stable due to its efficiently designed exhaust cones, and reliable overall, a rare quality in a Podracer. Easily maintained and modified, its hull is modular in design which makes it a prime candidate for racers who enjoy tinkering.
