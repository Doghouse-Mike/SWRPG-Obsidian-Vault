This tank entered its first round of production during the Second Battle of Geonosis and their factory became the primary target of a Repblic offensive following the first wave of the invasion. Despite their first deployment in battle being thwarted by a cunning trap laid by the legendary Anakin Skywalker, the plans may have survived and the tanks later deployed during the Clone Wars final years.

Similar in silhouette and shape to the [[Multi Troop Transport|MTT]] by the same manufacturer, Baktoid Amour Workshop, this heavily armoured combat speeder was also protected by ray shields. This made it virtually untouchable, even by the most powerful artillery units. In addition to this, it was heavily armed with a suite of weapon batteries to deploy the most appropriate response in every foreseeable situation.
