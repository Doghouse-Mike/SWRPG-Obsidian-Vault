This short podracer with unusual vertical and flat air intakes was built specifically with marine species in mind: indeed, the cockpit is enclosed and hermetically sealed so that it can host water, or any liquid necessary for certain species to pilot the vehicle without dying. This specific design can also come in handy for subaquatic species that can breathe in normal atmospheres, but still perform better in their natural environment. As a result, the podracer is relatively fragile and the enclosed pod especially vulnerable, which is why it comes equipped with a powerful repair suite.

> More information about the vehicle is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Enser_Flay_254).

### SPECIAL RULES

**Sealed Friendly Environment:** species of a subaquatic origin or requiring specific atmospheres can reduce personal Strain spent in any way by 1 when piloting this podracer.

**Fragile Enclosure:** whenever the podracer suffers from a Collision, the GM can spend an additional [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result or three [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results to [[Item Qualities#Sunder (Active)|sunder]] the glass canopy by one increment (instead of adding +10 to the Critical Hit). If it gets destroyed, the vehicle loses the “Sealed Friendly Environment” quality.
