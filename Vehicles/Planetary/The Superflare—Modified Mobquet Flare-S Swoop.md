### ADDITIONAL RULES

**Oversized Weapons:** When the pilot rolls two [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results on a **[[Gunnery (Ag)|Gunnery check]]**, the bike's speed immediately drops by one (to a minimum of one) and the pilot must make and immediately **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Piloting - Planetary (Ag)|Piloting (Planetary) check]]** to keep the swoop from careening out of control in a random direction. If the **[[Gunnery (Ag)|Gunnery check]]** results in a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result the swoop immediately loses all power. The pilot must makes spend his next action making a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics check]]** to restart the swoop before it crashes at the end of the following round (or longer at the GM's discretion).
