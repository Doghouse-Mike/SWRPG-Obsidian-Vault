### ADDITIONAL RULES

**Salvo:** As a maneuver, if the vehicle's current speed is 0, the pilot may fire its weapons in salvo mode. Until the vehicle moves, increase the value of the [[Item Qualities#Linked (Active)|Linked]] quality of its twin forward-mounted light blaster cannon to 3[^0-1] *or* 4[^1-2].
