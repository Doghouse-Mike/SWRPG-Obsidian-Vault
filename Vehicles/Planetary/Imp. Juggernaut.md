The Imperial Combat Assault Transport, sometimes referred to as a Juggernaut (Not to be confused with the HAVw A6 Juggernaut) is a heavy cargo vehicle. Riding on massive wheels, it is a compromise between AT walkers and repulsor craft.

At the height of the empire the unarmed vehicle would have been escorted by repulsor tanks and TIE fighters. With most of their combat vehicles destroyed in the war these transports must rely now on their armor and speed for protection.

> More information about the vehicle is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/HCVw_A9.2_juggernaut_transport).
