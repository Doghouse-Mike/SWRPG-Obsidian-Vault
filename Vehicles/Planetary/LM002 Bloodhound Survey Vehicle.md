### SOROSUUB PVA.Q LONG-RANGE TERRAIN SCANNER

The Bloodhound is equipped with SoroSuub's PVA.q LRTS, a powerful scanning suite designed to render three-dimensional maps of surrounding terrain.

Using the LRTS requires the operate to make an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Computers (Int)|Computers check]]**. Maps produced with the LRTS add an automatic [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] result to any [[Survival (Cun)|Survival]] checks made to navigate using the map plus an additional [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] result for every two [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results generated by the [[Computers (Int)|Computers]] check.
