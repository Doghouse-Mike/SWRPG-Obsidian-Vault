Produced by Baktoid Armor Workshop, the Multi Troop Transport is a heavy, broad-shouldered, snub-nosed vehicle designed to transport battle droids into combat.

> More information about the vehicle is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Multi-Troop_Transport).

### ADDITIONAL RULES

**Droid Deployment:** As a maneuver, a crew member can begin deployment of the MTT's complement of battle droids, which will be deployed and active at the end of the following round.

**Ram Prow:** An MTT traveling at top speed may ram a stationary target of silhouette 2 or greater by using a maneuver to engage it. If the target is mobile, this instead requires an action and a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Piloting - Planetary (Ag)|Piloting (Planetary) check]]**. \[Add\] +20 to any Critical Hit rolls the MTT inflicts on its targets.
