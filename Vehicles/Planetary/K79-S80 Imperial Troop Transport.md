The Imperial Troop Transport, sometimes called the Recon Troop Transport, is a large, heavy-duty landspeeder used to move stormtroopers, prisoners, and supplies between planetside locations.

> More information about the vehicle is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/K79-S80_Imperial_Troop_Transport).
