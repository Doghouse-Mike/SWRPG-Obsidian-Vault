The primary artillery piece used across the Clone Wars by the CIS, the J-1 came with a pre-installed droid brain capable of following basic orders from a command unit nearby. However, it also had an operator seat from which the cannon could be fired and moved. Unlike its counterpart, the [[AV-7 Anti-Vehicle Artillery Cannon|AV-7]], this cannon was particularly more powerful but required a crew to reload the canister after each shot.

A group of J-1 proton cannons could alone defend an emplacement from aerial assault, capable of ripping through a dropship with a single round, or even penetrating an *Acclamator*-class assault ship’s shields. It was however, slow and lumbering, making it particularly easy to avoid. In addition, the cannons were susceptible to ground attacks as the powerful shell was too dangerous to fire at short range.
