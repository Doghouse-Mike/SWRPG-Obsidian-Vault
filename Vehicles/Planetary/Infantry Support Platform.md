Known colloquially as the Republic’s swamp speeder, the ISP was designed as a heavier version of the [[BARC Speeder|BARC speeder]]. The design lines are reminiscent of the bike’s unique shapes, certainly showing Aratech’s influence in the production of this weapon platform. While it was shipped standard with light blasters equipped to its frontal mounts, these weapon systems were often swapped out for more appropriate ordnance for its support role in the particular battle.

While the BARC speeder bike is good for conducting recon, the additional armour and weapons on board the ISP allows for patrol duties to be conducted in greater safety for the clones. Beyond patrol duties, the ISP was particularly effective against vehicles which it was able to outmaneuver by skimming along watery and uneven surfaces at high speeds.
