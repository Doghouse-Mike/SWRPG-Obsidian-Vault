Sporting the longest low-pass turbofan engines on the market, The Longtail is characterized by its solid commands and integrated support systems, of which the circular, gyrostabilized cockpit rotating at 230 rpm is the main feature. This ensures that the unaerodynamic pod remains stable during flight, and is also known to shield the pilot in the case of catastrophic events leading the pod to collide with the ground, rolling it to safety instead. The pod blocking all view ahead means that it comes equipped with a screen linked to an external camera for the pilot.

### SPECIAL RULES

**Gyrostabilized Pod:** Helps the pilot fight against straining maneuvers, reducing the amount of personal Strain taken at any time by 1, down to a minimum of 1. On top of it, the racer can reduce any Critical Injury by 50 at the time of a crash, and any Critical Injury resulting in a 0 or lower rating is ignored.

**Massive Profile:** In Section checks with a Silhouette modifier, count as one Silhouette higher.
