This podracer is powered by two cluster arrays, consisting of no less than sixteen engines (eight each). Probably one of the toughest podracers ever built, and besides a solid cooling system helped by the open air channel in the middle of each cluster, it performs very well in high speed variations with the help of its multiple engines, all shaped differently and acting as a ‘gearbox’ of sorts, able to adjust to any speed variation. However this craft has very little else to show for and its awkward profile hinders its speed potential.

> More information about the vehicle is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Plug-8G_927_Cluster_Array_Podracer).
