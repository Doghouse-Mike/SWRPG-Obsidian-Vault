The AA-9 was designed as a massive bulk freighter, though it was quickly retrofitted into a low cost passen- ger liner. In the lead up to the Clone Wars, with the Separatist crisis on the horizon, these vessels were bought up by philanthropists to use as refugee trans- ports. Manufactured by a relatively obscure shipyard, the AA-9 popularised Botajef into galactic prominence with its iconic design.

Despite a subpar hyperdrive, especially for a vessel that would be so widely used as a starliner, the AA-9 had a powerful trio of CEC Dyne 2577 radial sunlight engines. These massive thrusters gave the vessel a particularly high speed for its size. Its shape made it easy to land and dock in the starports across the galaxy. Commandos might be familiar with the vessel, as the GAR used the AA-9 as supplementary troop transports.
