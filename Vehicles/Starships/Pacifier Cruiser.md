A versatile and reliable light cruiser, Dynar Engineering's *Pacifier*-class patrol cruiser has been a popular choice for security forces for many decades. A single Pacifier often makes up the bulk of a smaller system’s security forces. Long and thin, the *Pacifier* has a backwards rake that gives the ship as licked back look, a look that still keeps the cruiser looking handsome even after close to forty years in service. A large pair of Zordaine ion engines sit at the aft of Pacifier, ensuring the cruiser maintains a solid cruising speed.

The cruisers usuallymount a pair of Taim & Bak twin turbolasers in dorsal and ventral midship turrets as their main weapon. Along with these turbolasers are four light turbolasers, two to a side, mounted dorsally in banks on the midship hull section. This makes the *Pacifier* well suited to running down pirates as well as acting as a heavy escorts in patrol duties.
