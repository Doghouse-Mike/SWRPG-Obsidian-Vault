The GS-100 salvage ship, more commonly known as a Trandoshan scavanger ship, is a Gallofree Yards vessel, designed to navigate debris fields in the wake of space battles, picking up salvageable parts to sell for scrap. A durable vessel, capable of dealing with dangerous space flotsam, it requires a small crew to operate, which allows the owner to split the profit from salvage with associates.

> More information about the starship is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/GS-100_salvage_ship).
