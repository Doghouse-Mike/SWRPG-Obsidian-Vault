One of the most popular freighters produced in the decades prior to the Clone Wars, the *Barloz*-class is a basic, no-frills cargo transport that still sees use in many parts of the galaxy.

> More information about the starship is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Barloz-class_freighter).
