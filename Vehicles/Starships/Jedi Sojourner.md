During the final years of the Old Republic, the Jedi Order commissioned Kuat Systems Engineering (designers of the Delta-7 *Aethersprite* interceptor, fielded in large numbers by the Jedi) to provide them with a ship designed to carry Jedi Knights and their Padawans on long journeys of meditation and training.

> More information about the starship is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sojourner_Transport).
