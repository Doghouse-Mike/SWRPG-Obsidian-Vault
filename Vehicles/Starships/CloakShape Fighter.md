### CUSTOMIZATIONS

**Curich Engineering Stabilizer Conversion Kit:** Produced by Curich Engineering, a subsidiary of Santhe/Sienar Technologies, this bolt-on wing and stabilizer kit was designed specifically for the CloakShape fighter to address a number of its handling shortcomings. \[...\] The effects of the Stabilizer Conversion Kit are already included in the CloakShape's stat block. If the Player Characters manage to find a CloakShape without the Conversion Kit, its handling is -1 and it has 4 Customization Hard Points.
