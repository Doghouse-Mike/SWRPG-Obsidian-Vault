The *Crusader* is a fast picket boat whose bold, angular design bristles with point-defence weaponry. Though the sharp lines of the craft evoke battleships of the Mandalorian crusades of ages past, the protective nature of the design better fits with the New Mandalorian philosophy of pacifism and defence. With oversized engines and highly sophisticated software, the *Crusader* is designed to rush to the defence of ships threatened by starfighters; this makes it an ideal patrol boat, defending freighters from encroaching pirates, but just as capable as a support player in a major fleet battle.
