As the New Republic expands its reach, more and more systems fall under the jurisdiction of the New Republic Correctional Corps. Short staffed and spread thin, NRCC Transports are designed to securely carry hundreds of prisoners with only minimal security. A single organic operator can pilot the ship, monitor prisoners, and coordinate automated defenses all from one console.

In the first months of the New Republic these vessels were accompanied by starfighter escorts. Rapid demilitarization and the demand for Outer Rim provincial patrols has made that something of a rarity.
