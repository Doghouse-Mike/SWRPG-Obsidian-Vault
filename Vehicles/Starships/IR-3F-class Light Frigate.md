The predecessor to Republic Sienar Systems' *IPV-1*-class corvette, the *IR-3F*-class light frigate is an old and outdated patrol craft first introduced during the later decades of the Old Republic.

> More information about the starship is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/IR-3F-class_light_frigate).
