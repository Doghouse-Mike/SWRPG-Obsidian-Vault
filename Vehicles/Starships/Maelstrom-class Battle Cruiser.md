The *Maelstrom*-class battle cruiser belongs to the family of dagger-shaped proto–Star Destroyers, such as the *Venator* and *Acclamator* classes, used by the Republic Navy at the outset of the Clone Wars.

> More information about the starship is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Maelstrom-class_battle_cruiser).
