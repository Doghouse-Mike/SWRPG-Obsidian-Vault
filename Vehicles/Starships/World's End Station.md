Over two centuries old, the station was first established as a frontier asteroid mining operation by independent prospectors. As the number of mines grew and tibanna gas harvesters arrive in-system, the earliest and most well-established mining station gradually developed from a nearly-automated installation crewed by a few hardy shift workers, to a small outpost offering repairs and R&R, to a full-blown station with thousands of permanent inhabitants and families.
