The Techno Union’s own bulk transport vessel, the *Hardcell*-class was an ageing craft by the time of the Clone Wars. Originally filling the role of bulk transport for Techno Union droids and with a secondary capacity as a refuelling station, it was one of the first vessels to see battle in the Clone Wars. Unfortunately, it was relatively ineffective due to its glaring weak points and lacking military ability. The weapons were only intended for deterrence of piracy, not fighting in a war.

Despite featuring a military grade hyperdrive, the rest of the ship systems were ancient. It used rocket boosters as its main engines, giving it awful maneuverability and even worse atmospheric performance. These boosters enabled the capital class vessel to make planetary landings unassisted, and gave a significant speed boost to the ship (once it was able to get back off the ground).
