### ADDITIONAL RULES

**Optimized for Atmosphere:** While in space, the handling of the TIE/sk switches to +0 and speed to 4.

**Multi-Role Fighter:** The TIE/sk can also be fitted to deploy proton bombs in a ground assault role from a ventral-mounted ejector. These explode with the following profile: (Fire Arc Down; Damage 8; Critical 2; Range \[Close\]; [[Item Qualities#Blast (Active)|Blast]] 6, [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] 6)[^0-1] *or* (Fire Arc All; Damage 8; Critical 2; Range \[Close\]; [[Item Qualities#Blast (Active)|Blast]] 6, [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] 6)[^1-2].
