Built by Hoersch-Kessel Drive at the beginning of the Clone Wars as a competitor to Incom/SubPro's popular [[Republic Z-95 Headhunter|Z-95 Headhunter]], the R-41 Starchaser is an old, solidly built, and reliable multi-role starfighter.

> More information about the starship is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/R-41_Starchaser).
