This unique starfighter was designed by the Geonosian Huppla Pasa Tisc Shipwrights Collective. Another example in the use of a solar sail, it was capable of greater speed and shield. The downside to this was making the starfighter give off a greater signal on sensor arrays.

These were largely made to commission, making them particularly rare; especially beyond the Clone Wars era. While the crimson energy sail provides this vessel with exceptional dogfighting potential in space, it must be stowed when entering the atmosphere and when preparing to go to hyperspace.

> More information about the starship is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ginivex-class_fanblade_starfighter).
