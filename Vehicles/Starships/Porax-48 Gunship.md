With the success of the Incom Corp. X-Wing series, many manufacturers have been rushing to produce their own advanced snubfighters. The Porax-48, successor to the Porax-38, is the first new design by Baktoid Armor Workshop since its involvement in the Clone Wars. Highly versatile, the P-48 can match a TIE fighter’s speed while packing enough punch to take on larger assault ships. Top of the line reactors power this ship’s heavy weapons and advanced shields. Popular among mercenaries and private militias, they are prohibitively expensive and do not see wide spread use.
