One of many experimental TIE designs tested and produced by Sienar Fleet Systems, the TIE Advanced v1 was designed to be a sturdy starfighter capable of working independently of fleet support.

> More information about the starship is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TIE_Advanced_v1).

### ADDITIONAL RULES

**XX-23 S-Thread Tracer:** This missile contains a device allowing the target's movements to be tracked regardless of where it travels, including through hyperspace. After a character makes a successful combat check with an XX23 tracer, they may make an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Astrogation (Int)|Astrogation check]]** to determine the target's location anywhere in the galaxy until the tracer is removed.
