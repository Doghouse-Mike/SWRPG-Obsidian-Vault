Any pilot who has spent his life near space docks is no stranger to the cargo tug. Simple, and made for pure utility, these working vessels populate many cargo transfer stations, space docks, and loading points. Pilots spend a full shift laboring in the cramped conditions of a simple one-man tug. Not built for comfort, these small crafts have a single purpose in mind, moving space born objects from one location to another. By using standard tractor beam technology, they can lock on to relatively large objects and deliver them to their destination. Though not fast, their engines are designed to deliver consistent performance despite moving a considerable load.
