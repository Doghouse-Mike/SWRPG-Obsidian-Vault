A variant of the old, reliable [[Z-95-AF4 Headhunter|Z-95 Headhunter]], the Heavy-95 is the result of a recent service life extension program for Headhunters serving in the Rebel Alliance.

> More information about the starship is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Z-95_AF4-H).
