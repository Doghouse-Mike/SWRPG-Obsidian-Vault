The Beacon is a custom ship of Gorbo's own design. As paranoid as he is, it is rife with hidden compartments and hiding places.

In addition, several of the compartments are large enough for a character to squeeze into, albeit uncomfortably. There are about 4 such compartments, many smaller compartments, as well as a pilot and copilot's seat.
