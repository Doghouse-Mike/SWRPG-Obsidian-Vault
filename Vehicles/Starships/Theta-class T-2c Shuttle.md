A special order by the Supreme Chancellor himself, the *Theta*-class was custom designed for the Republic and the plans never officially released for public sale. Built to the specifications of Palpatine, this shuttle was fit for purpose to carry the leader of the Republic to distant worlds.

Maintaining the signature appearance of a Cygnus Spaceworks shuttle, the central wing was squatter and overslung the cockpit. Its compact frame reduced the number of passengers capable of travelling within, especially with the inclusion of the medical capsule. Designed to keep the chancellor alive and protected in an emergency evacuation, it was particularly well equipped for a shuttle.

> More information about the starship is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Theta-class_T-2c_shuttle).
