Perhaps the largest dedicated starfighter carrier in the Imperial arsenal, the massive *Secutor*-class battlecarrier is an old design from the earliest days of the Imperial Navy.

> More information about the starship is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Secutor-class_Star_Destroyer).
