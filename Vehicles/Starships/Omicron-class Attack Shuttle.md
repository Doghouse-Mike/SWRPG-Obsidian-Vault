Used by some special forces groups during the Clone Wars, the Omicron was not an immediately recognisable GAR shuttle. Designed by Cygnus Spaceworks and not KDY, it appeared as just another shuttle in its market offering. As with many of their other shuttles, it features the same articulated flanking wings that are emblematic of this shipyard. However, it was one of the more dagger-like designs, with a profile that was more reminiscent of Sienar or KDY.

Armed relatively lightly, with a pair of frontal guns and a rear-facing turret, it was largely designated an attack shuttle due to its armoured frame, shields, and speed. Capable of flying into hostile airspace for rapid deployment, while maintaining a relatively unknown profile
.
