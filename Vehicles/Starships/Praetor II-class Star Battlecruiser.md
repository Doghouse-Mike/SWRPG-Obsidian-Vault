One of the largest classes of ships commissioned in the Imperial Navy, the *Praetor II*-class battlecruiser is second only to the rare *Executor*-class Super Star Destroyer in size, mass, and destructive potential.

> More information about the starship is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Praetor_II-class_ship).
