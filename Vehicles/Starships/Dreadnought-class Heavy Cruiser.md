Although considered old and outdated at the outbreak of the Clone Wars, Rendili StarDrive's *Dreadnought*-class heavy cruiser is still regarded as one of the more capable vessels of its class and size in use today.

> More information about the starship is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dreadnought-class_heavy_cruiser).

**Hull Type/Class:** Heavy Cruiser/*Dreadnought*. \
**Manufacturer:** Rendili Star Drive. \
**Hyperdrive:** Primary: Class 4, Backup: Class 18. \
**Navicomputer:** Yes. \
**Sensor Range:** Medium. \
**Ship's Complement:** 9,000 to 16,000 officers, pilots, and enlisted crew, depending on configuration. \
**Starfighter Complement:** 12 starfighters. \
**Encumbrance Capacity:** 7,500. \
**Passenger Capacity:** 3,000 troops. \
**Consumables:** One year. \
**Price/Rarity:** 7,200,000 credits/6. \
**Customization Hard Points:** 4.

**Weapons:**

- Five forward and five aft twin medium (Dreadnought)[^1-2] turbolaser batteries (Fire Arc Forward, Port, and Starboard *or* Aft; Damage 10; Critical 3; Range \[Long\]; [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] 3, [[Item Qualities#Linked (Active)|Linked]] 1, [[Item Qualities#Slow-Firing (Passive)|Slow-Firing]] 1).
- Ten port and ten starboard quad light (Dreadnought)[^1-2] turbolasers (Fire Arc Port *or* Starboard; Damage 9; Critical 3; Range \[Medium\]; [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] 2, [[Item Qualities#Linked (Active)|Linked]] 3, [[Item Qualities#Slow-Firing (Passive)|Slow-Firing]] 1[^0-1] *or* 2[^1-2]).
- Five port and five starboard heavy laser cannons (Fire Arc Port *or* Starboard; Damage 6; Critical 3; Range \[Short\]).

### ADDITIONAL RULES

**Massive 1:** When making an attack targeting this starship, the critical rating of any weapons used counts as 1 higher.
