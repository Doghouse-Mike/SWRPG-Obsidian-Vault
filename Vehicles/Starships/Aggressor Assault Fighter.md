### ADDITIONAL RULES

**Safety Limiters:** As a maneuver, the pilot of an Aggressor can shut off the automatic safety limiters and inertial dampers. This changes the craft's handling to +3, but whenever the pilot performs a Pilot Only maneuver, each character aboard suffers 3 strain. Droids and, at the GM's discretion, characters with four or more cybernetic enhancements suffer only 1 strain each time instead.
