Hastily designed and assembled by a Pau’an subsidiary of the Techno Union, the Mankvim-814 was a stubby starfighter with an oversized ion drive. The engineers at the Techno Union had to make a number of additions to the original hull so that the interceptor could effectively dogfight, installing repulsors and additional deployable winglets for atmospheric operations. As it would be deployed from carrier groups and operate as planetary defence craft, atmospheric performance could not be overlooked.

The resulting starfighter was an affordable interceptor that could be mass produced by the Techno Union’s self-replicating factories. Due to the number of these produced, they could be found cheap following the Clone Wars, and many were acquired to protect the Corporate Sector Authority systems on the Outer Rim. In some cases, it would serve well as an intra-system craft used by individuals to travel around at greater speeds than speeders or public shuttles.
