### SPECIAL RULES

**X10-D Droid Brain:** The droid brain can perform [[Piloting - Space (Ag)|Piloting checks]] for the *Hound's Tooth* with a [[Piloting - Space (Ag)|Piloting (Space)]] skill of 3 and an Agility of 0. It may be commanded via comlink with an authorized voiceprint.
