The *Rogue*-class starfighter is an example of the effect that CIS cost cutting can have on an artisinal starfighter model from a well established manufacturer. Taking the [[Porax-38 Starfighter|Porax-38]] as a base model, the engineers at the Baktoid Armour Workshop sought to create a copy which could be produced more widely and assigned to elite units such as the MagnaGuards. In the process, each change they made to reduce costs was matched by another change which made it just as expensive once again.

All the bells and whistles of the Porax-38 were left aside, including its enhanced sensors and commonly fitted Class 1 hyperdrive. This freed up the budget to upgrade the weaponry of the Porax-38 to heavy laser cannons. The enhanced weaponry left the craft only marginally cheaper than the original model. With the stripped systems, the *Rogue*-class was more modifiable than the original craft. This is the biggest feat achieved in the redesign, and permitted the ship to be made into the fearsome *Xanadu Blood* by infamous bounty hunter Cad Bane.

> More information about the starship is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rogue-class_Porax-38_starfighter).
