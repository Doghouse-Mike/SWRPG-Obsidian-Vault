Originally conceived by the legendary Walex Blissex as a Star Destroyer escort in the heavy battle lines of the Clone Wars, the Gladiator was repurposed by the Galactic Empire as an independent, long-range patrol craft.

> More information about the starship is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gladiator-class_Star_Destroyer).
