Built during the height of the Clone Wars, the *Victory*-class was designed as an all-purpose heavy line-of-battle ship meant to support planetary landings and take part in fleet actions.

> More information about the starship is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Victory-class_Star_Destroyer).
