The YC-123B transport hauler was a model of transport manufactured by the Corellian Engineering Corporation. A troop transport, the YC-123B transport hauler could rapidly deploy up to 120 passengers (Or a couple dozen cavalry units) onto the battlefield via a large carrying compartment under the cockpit. It was armed with two CEC FP-35 forward heavy laser cannons, one on each side of the ship, as well as a CEC AG-3M triple laser cannon turret aft of the cockpit.

> More information about the starship is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/YC-123B_transport_hauler).
