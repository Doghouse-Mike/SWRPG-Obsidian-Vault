### SPECIAL RULES

**Integrated R2 Droid Gunner:** The *Punishing One*'s integrated R2 droid can fire the quad laser cannon with a [[Gunnery (Ag)|Gunnery]] skill of 4 and Agility of 0. During structured gameplay, the droid gunner takes a single action directly following the pilot's turn.
