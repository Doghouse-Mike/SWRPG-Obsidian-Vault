The *Purgill*-class was named after the massive spacefaring whale-like creatures that were capable of natural travel through hyperspace. The class actually contained a variety of vessel profiles, but the MPO-1400 was specifically designed by the engineer known as Shug Drabor, using their particular thirteen pod engine formation.

This cruiser was quite large, capable of holding a few hundred passengers in relative luxury while visiting the vistas of the galaxy, especially the edges of the Unknown Regions. In service since the romantic and peaceful era of the High Republic, the longevity of this vessel is a testament to its quality of build and fitness for purpose.
