Designed by Loronar, more traditionally known for their civilian exploration vessels, construction of the *Strike-class* Cruisers was licensed to the experience military shipwrights of Rendili Star Drive. The *Strike-class* was marketed towards the Empire as a flexible, multi-purpose ship. It is designed around a series of modular components, allowing the military garrisons, starfighter hangars or intelligence centres to be rapidly swapped in or out to serve any mission profile. On top of this modularity, the ship is surprisingly capable; pairing the speed and maneuverability of a light cruiser with the weaponry and shield of a heavy cruiser.

This flexibility was not achieved without cost. The civilian experience of its designers led them to build the ship around a single set of spinal conduits, not the redundant system common in most military ships. Most modules are connected to the spine via only a few attachment points. A single solid hit can leave a module without power or life support; several hits can detach one altogether. This makes large-scale fleet engagements something of a liability for the *Strike-class*.
