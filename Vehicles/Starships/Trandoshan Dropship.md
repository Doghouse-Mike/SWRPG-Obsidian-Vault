The dagger-like and sharp shapes which form the profile of this bulky transport immediately convey the image of a predatory vessel. Produced by the Trandoshan Wasskah Shipyards, this odd transport certainly had a distinctive appearance—especially when landed. While on a planetary surface, the upper section of the vessel would raise, turning the cockpit into a sort of observation tower.

Designed for the deployment of Trandoshan paramilitary forces, the ship was well armoured and capable of deploying troops on the move. However, it was not made to engage in firefights, often attempting to weather the attacking forces long enough to escape.
