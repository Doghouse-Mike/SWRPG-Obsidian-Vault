Another unique system defence fighter, the Zenuas 33 is a product of the Umbaran manufacturer Ghost Armaments. Fitting an array of advanced technology seen nowhere else in the galaxy, this Umbaran fighter is superior to most widely produced starfighters, though lacks any space for further modifications. The lack of a hyperdrive and its odd avionics interface would also hamper its entry into the wider galactic market—if the Umbarans ever sought to commercialise the craft.

Appearing like a pelvic bone of a great beast found on Umbara’s ever-night planet, the Zenuas 33 airframe was capable of tight turns and high speeds thanks to its light construction. It relied on powerful rayshielding to protect the craft against destruction and the combination of the flexible frame with the special avionics interface allowed for an extremely maneuverable fighter while maintaining high speeds.

> More information about the starship is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zenuas_33_Umbaran_starfighter).
