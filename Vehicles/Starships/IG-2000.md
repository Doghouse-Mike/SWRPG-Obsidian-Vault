### ADDITIONAL RULES

***IG-2000* Droid Brains:** *IG-2000* can perform vehicle actions and maneuvers and provide assistance on checks. It has characteristics of 0 and the following skills: [[Astrogation (Int)|Astrogation]] 4, [[Computers (Int)|Computers]] 4, [[Gunnery (Ag)|Gunnery]] 4, and [[Piloting - Space (Ag)|Piloting (Space)]] 4. In structured gameplay, *IG-2000* receives a full turn (including an action and maneuver) immediately following its pilot each round.

**Safety Limiters:** As a maneuver, the pilot of *IG-2000* can shut off the automatic safety limiters and inertial dampers. This changes the craft's handling to +3, but whenever the pilot performs a Pilot Only maneuver, each character aboard suffers 3 strain. Droids and, at the GM's discretion, characters with four or more cybernetic enhancements suffer only 1 strain each time instead.
