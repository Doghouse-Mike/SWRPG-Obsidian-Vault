Certainly, the most famous YT-1300 in existence, the *Millennium Falcon* is the ship that made the Kessel Run in less than twelve parsecs, the ship that spirited the Death Star plans to the Rebels' Massassi base, and the ship crewed by Heroes of Yavin.[^1]

> More information about the starship is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Millennium_Falcon).

### ADDITIONAL RULES

**Calrissian Chronicles:** A character may add two [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost dice]] to [[Charm (Pr)|Charm]] and [[Deception (Cun)|Deception checks]] they make to leverage the *Falcon*'s reputation as the transport of famous adventurer Lando Calrissian, but their targets add \[an\] automatic [[Narrative Dice#Success|success result]] to checks they make to subsequently identify or track down the character.

## HAN SOLO'S *MILLENNIUM FALCON*

Source: [^0-2]

![Silhouette 4 Speed 5 Handling +1 Defense (Fore/Port/Starboard/Aft) 2/-/-/2 Armor 5 Hull Trauma Threshold 30 System Strain Threshold 15|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/1/10/Profile_Han_Solo%27s_Millennium_Falcon.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Silhouette 4 Speed 5 Handling +1 Defense (Fore/Port/Starboard/Aft) 2/-/-/2 Armor 5 Hull Trauma Threshold 30 System Strain Threshold 15

**Hull Type/Class:** Freighter/Modified YT-1300f. \
**Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation. \
**Hyperdrive:** Primary: Class 0.5, Backup: Class 10. \
**Navicomputer:** Yes. \
**Sensor Range:** Long. \
**Ship's Complement:** One pilot, one co-pilot/engineer, two gunners. \
**Encumbrance Capacity:** 165. \
**Passenger Capacity:** 6. \
**Consumables:** Two months. \
**Price/Rarity:** 425,000 credits (R)/10. \
**Customization Hard Points:** 6 (1 available).

**Weapons:**

- One dorsal and one ventral turret-mounted quad laser cannon (Fire Arc All; Damage 5; Critical 3; Range \[Close\]; [[Item Qualities#Accurate (Passive)|Accurate]] 1, [[Item Qualities#Linked (Active)|Linked]] 3).
- Forward-mounted concussion missile launchers (Fire Arc Forward; Damage 6; Critical 3; Range \[Short\]; [[Item Qualities#Blast (Active)|Blast]] 4, [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] 4, [[Item Qualities#Guided (Active)|Guided]] 8, [[Item Qualities#Limited Ammo (Passive)|Limited Ammo]] 3, [[Item Qualities#Linked (Active)|Linked]] 1, [[Item Qualities#Slow-Firing (Passive)|Slow-Firing]] 1).
- Ventral-mounted Ax-108 "Ground Buzzer" blaster cannon—this weapon can be fired by the ship's computer with [[Gunnery (Ag)|Gunnery]] 3 and Agility 0, and its entire profile uses personal scale, not planetary scale (Fire Arc All; Damage 12; Critical 3; Range \[Medium\]; [[Item Qualities#Auto-fire (Active)|Auto-fire]], [[Item Qualities#Pierce (Passive)|Pierce]] 2).

### ADDITIONAL RULES

**Hunk of Junk:** Once per session, a character may remove \[a\] [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair result]] from a [[Piloting - Space (Ag)|Piloting]] or [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics]] check they make using the *Millennium Falcon*. The GM may then add \[a\] [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair result]] to a subsequent roll any character makes using the *Millennium Falcon* that session.
