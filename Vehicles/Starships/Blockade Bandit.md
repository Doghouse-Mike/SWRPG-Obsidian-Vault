## *BLOCKADE BANDIT*

> [!quote] Image text
> Silhouette 5 Speed 2 Handling -2 Defense (Fore/Port/Starboard/Aft) 2/1/1/2 Armor 5 Hull Trauma Threshold 50 System Strain Threshold 15

**Hull Type/Class:** Corvette/CR90. \
**Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation. \
**Hyperdrive:** Primary: Class 2, Backup: None. \
**Navicomputer:** Yes. \
**Sensor Range:** Long. \
**Ship's Complement:** Forty. \
**Encumbrance Capacity:** 2,000 (currently 750). \
**Passenger Capacity:** 100 (currently 50). \
**Consumables:** One month (current stores). \
**Price/Rarity:** 700,000 credits/5. \
**Customization Hard Points:** 4 (2 unusable/junked).

**Weapons:** Add one [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die to each attack check due to poor condition.

- Dorsal and Ventral Turret-Mounted Twin Medium Turbolaser Batteries (Fire Arc All; Damage 11; Critical 3; Range \[Long\]; [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] 3, [[Item Qualities#Linked (Active)|Linked]] 1, [[Item Qualities#Slow-Firing (Passive)|Slow-Firing]] 1, only one on each side is functional).
- Two Port and two Starboard Turret-Mounted Light Turbolasers (Fire Arc Port and Starboard; Damage 10; Critical 3; Range \[Medium\]; [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] 2, [[Item Qualities#Slow-Firing (Passive)|Slow-Firing]] 1).

**Junker Starship:** Any [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result on a **[[Computers (Int)|Computers]]**, **[[Gunnery (Ag)|Gunnery]]**, or [[Mechanics (Int)|**Mechanics** **check**]] result shuts down the associated system or weapon for one round and adds a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die to all subsequent checks until the system is repaired with a successful **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Computers (Int)|Computers]]** or **[[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics check]]**. A [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result on a **[[Piloting - Space (Ag)|Piloting (Space) check]]** reduces the handling, adding a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die until the control system is repaired with a successful **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics check]]**.
