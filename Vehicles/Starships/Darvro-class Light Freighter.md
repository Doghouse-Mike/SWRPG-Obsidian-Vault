Along with the *Tallanx* fighter, the *Darvro* is a ship built on stealth and speed commonly used by the Ord Gimmel Defense Fleet.

> More information about the starship is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darvro-class_light_freighter).

### CUSTOMIZATIONS

**Gimmellian Stealth Device:** Reduces the range band of enemy ships' passive sensors by 1 (to a minimum of close range) for detecting the *Darvro*. In order to function, all power from shields and weapons must be channeled into the cloaking device. Rerouting power takes one action.

**Electronic Countermeasures Suite:** Blocks sensors within range when active, counting the *Tallanx*'s silhouette as 3. Immediately notifies all ships in a star system, or within 100 kilometers if planetside, to the *Darvro*'s existence.
