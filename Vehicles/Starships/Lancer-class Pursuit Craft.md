### SPECIAL RULES

**Mk IV FCS:** As a maneuver, the pilot may activate or deactivate the Mk. IV FCS. While the system is active, the ship's forward-mounted twin light laser cannons' [[Item Qualities#Linked (Active)|Linked]] item quality increases to 4, and it gains the [[Item Qualities#Tractor (Passive)|Tractor]] 2 item quality, but only this weapon system can be fired.
