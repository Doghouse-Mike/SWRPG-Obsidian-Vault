Manufactured by Kaniff Yards, and used primarily by smaller Outer Rim trading and transport companies, the Class A Modular Transport a well-rounded workhorse. It’s not unusual to see Class As As that have been in service for well over a century. A massive, lumbering vessel, it carries 20,000 tons of bulk cargo. in its cavernous cargo hold. A massive, lumbering ship, it carries 20,000 tons of bulk cargo. in its cavernous cargo hold. The ship itself is as utilitarian as a ship can be. A small module containing the bridge, crew quarters and primary systems sits off to the side of the vessel, connected by a secured access tunnel. The remainder of the ship is given over to cargo space. Abaft the command the hulking, multi-decked cargo hold comes equipped with countless sliding bulkheads, retractable decks, sophisticated anti-boarding systems, and a state-of-the-art cargo handling system.

While the Class A doesn’t not come with any armaments as standard, the ship’s hull is reinforced and heavily armoured. While the Class A is primarily configured for cargo transport. Many owners have converted part of the cargo hold into passenger quarters and operate the ship as a passenger liner. Though travel conditions are likely to be far from luxurious.
