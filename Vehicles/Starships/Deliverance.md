The Galactic Standard medical symbols painted on the clean white hull of the *Deliverance* seem somewhat incongruous with the sweeping wings and blunt, turbolaser-studded prow of the ship. An old but proven design, the *Deliverance* has served in corporate fleets for decades. Since its acquisition by Athakam Medtech, it has been refitted with advanced on-board medical facilities so it can deliver emergency medical care in the most hostile of situations. Despite this, however, it mostly serves to protect the corporation's valuable assets- whether that be from piracy or defaulting clients...
