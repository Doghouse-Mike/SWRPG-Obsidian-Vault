The [[TIE - hu 'Hunter' Multi-role Starfighter|Hunter]] is a recently developed TIE unit designed to compete with the speed, mobility, and ordnance of the Alliance X-wing, featuring S-foils that close when the ship is flying at top speeds as well as a hyperdrive.

> More information about the starship is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tam_Blackstar%27s_TIE_Hunter).
