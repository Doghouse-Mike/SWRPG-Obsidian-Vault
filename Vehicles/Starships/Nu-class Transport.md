### MODULAR CARGO POD

Source: [^0-1]

Some *Nu*-class ships are fitted with a factory-built modular cargo pod slung beneath the hull. \[...\] Shuttles so equipped have an encumbrance capacity of 400, but see their speed reduced to 2 and their handling reduced to –1. If the cargo pod is used to carry troops, the shuttle's troop capacity is increased to 75.
