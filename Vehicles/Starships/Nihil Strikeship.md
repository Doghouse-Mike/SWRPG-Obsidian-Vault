As shrouded in mystery as the rest of their shadowy operation, the Nihil‘s Strikeships are the core of their front-line attack force. Brutal, ugly and viciously deadly, Nihil Strikeships pour from massive transports like a deadly stream of predators

The vast majority of these vessels are “Uglies,” starfighters cobbled together from a number of different ships. The jagged wings of a KSE Waverunner crudely grafted to the fuselage of a M-1000 Jumpmaster, with as many cannons, missiles and harpoons jammed on to it as possible. Nihil Strikeships are not built to last, but built to inflict asmuch pain and damdge as possible.

Some Strikeships have relatively standardised designs, but most are unique and have wildly varied performance traits. These Strikeships generally tend to be a poorer match for amodern conventional military-built fighter, as they typically end up with more of the weaknesses than the strengths of their component parts. However, bristling with weapons and in the hands of a dangerous and violent Nihil pilot, these Strikeships are some of the deadliest starfighters in the stars today, especially in numbers.
