One of the Galactic Republic’s newest light cruisers, the *Longbeam* is already making waves across the galaxy. Designed at the Republic Shipyards on Hosnian Prime, the Longbeam is the brainchild of shipwright, Joors Tankar. The ship fills a need in the Republic, that of a cruiser armed enough to protect itself while on missions, but not too armed as to constitute a purely military vessel. The ship’s profile resembles that of a Glee Anselm hammerfish, with a blunt forward skull, tapering behind into a long tailfin.

The *Longbeam* can be handily crewed by only three, and a single pilot can control many of the ship’s functions alone, in an emergency. The rest of the ship’s interior space is divided between well appointed state rooms, medical facilities, storage bays and a modular unit. This modular unit can be removed and swapped for mission specific loadouts. Extended medical units, laboratory units, or even a very small hangar unit, capable of carrying a pair of Z-28 Skywings, ensure that the *Longbeam* cruiser will be a valuable asset for years to come.
