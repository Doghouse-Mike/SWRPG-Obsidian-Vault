### ADDITIONAL RULES

**Living Coral Hull:** The starship is made out of living material and is almost its own sentient organism. As such, it can heal itself overtime. It can heal 2 hull trauma every day but can only be healed using the [[Xenology (Int)|Knowledge (Xenology)]] skill as a Yuuzhan Vong shaper. Crew members can use two [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result generated on any ship-related check to heal 1 hull trauma and a Critical Hit respectively.
