**Hull Type/Class:** Bulk Cruiser/*Neutron Star*–class. \
**Manufacturer:** Rendili StarDrive. \
**Hyperdrive:** Primary: Class 3, Backup: Class 20. \
**Navicomputer:** Yes. \
**Sensor Range:** Long. \
**Ship's Complement:** 2,200 crew. \
**Starfighter Complement:** None (36 with the carrier conversion)[^0-1]. \
**Vehicle Complement:** None (2 shuttles with the carrier conversion)[^0-1]. \

**Consumables:** One year. \
**Price/Rarity:** 2,800,000 credits/4 (2,871,000 credits with the Carrier Conversion)[^0-1]. \
**Customization Hard Points:** 6 (2 after the carrier conversion)[^0-1].

**Weapons:**

- Ten port, ten starboard, and four forward heavy bulk cruiser turbolasers (Fire Arc Port *or* Starboard *or* Forward; Damage 11; Critical 3; Range \[Long\]; [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] 3, [[Item Qualities#Slow-Firing (Passive)|Slow-Firing]] 2).
- Ten port, ten starboard, and five dorsal heavy laser cannons (Fire Arc Port *or* Starboard *or* All; Damage 6; Critical 3; Range \[Short\]).
- Two forward-mounted heavy tractor beam emitters (Fire Arc Forward; Damage –; Critical –; Range \[Short\]; [[Item Qualities|Tractor]] 6).
