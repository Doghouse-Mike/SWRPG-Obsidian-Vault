The Nantex is a unique starfighter produced specifically for the defence of Geonosis. It was used by the Geonosian hives, with a pilot caste specifically bred to pilot these—operating as a swarm. In some ways, these light starfighters would serve as a model for the design of TIE fighters and their use doctrine.

This slim starfighter and interceptor was exceptionally nimble and speed, often appearing out of the nooks and crannies of the Geonosian landscape to destroy the largely defenseless LAAT/v dropships. While easy to render inoperable, there was an un-ending number of these to replenish the ranks in the sky.
