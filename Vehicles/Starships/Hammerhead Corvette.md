### ADDITIONAL OR SPECIAL RULES

**Designed to Ram:** When a Hammerhead makes a head-on impact, it is always treated as a minor collision for this vessel and so subtracts 30 from the roll to resolve its Critical Hit. The enemy vessel always treats this as a major collision and adds 30 to its roll to resolve its own Critical Hit.
