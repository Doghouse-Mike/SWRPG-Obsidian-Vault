The *Diamond*-class was colloquially known as the Commerce Guild cruiser due to its wide use along guild tradelines. Produced by the very guild, it was designed as a hyperspace patrol cruiser and given an experi- mental hyperdrive capable of traveling 30% faster than even the top military drives. This allowed it to respond faster than pirates may expect. In addition, it had a great sublight speed for pursuit of pirate vessels. Standard armament was lacking, but its massive size was more than enough to intimidate vessels into fleeing or submitting.

The *Diamond*-class was also capable of landing on planetary surfaces unaided and unloading a mass of guild droids to rapidly establish order and compliance. The vessel soon came to represent the backbone of the Commerce Guild fleet, showing up in realspace in mere hours after the thought of contravening Guild law would appear in an offender’s mind.
