### ADDITIONAL RULES

**Stygium Cloaking Device:** Activating the cloaking device on a TIE phantom requires the pilot to spend an action. Once activated, the cloaking device makes the phantom almost impossible to detect, requiring anyone attempting to spot it to make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Daunting difficulty]] [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]]** or **[[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance check]]**. Once detected, any attacks made against the Phantom while the cloaking device remains active count its silhouette as 0. Using the cloaking device does come with some severe side effects for the phantom. While the cloaking device is active, the TIE phantom has its speed reduced by 3. Additionally, the phantom pilot cannot use active scanners, comms, or weapons while the cloak is active. The pilot can turn off the cloaking device by spending a maneuver.
