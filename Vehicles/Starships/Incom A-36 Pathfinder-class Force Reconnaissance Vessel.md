### ANQ 3.6 TRACKING COMPUTER

The Anq 3.6 is a computer/fire control system that allows the Pathfinder's sensor operator to coordinate fire among all ship weapons. The sensor operator must designate one target and make an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Computers (Int)|Computers check]]**. If \[they\] succeeds, all combat checks made by ship weapons against \[their\] designated target upgrade the ability of their check once. If \[they\] have three [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result on \[their\] check, \[they\] may designate an additional target.
