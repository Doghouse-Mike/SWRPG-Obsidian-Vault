The *EF-97 Intelligence Cruisers* operated by the Imperial Security Bureau are an insidious threat to freedom of thought across the galaxy. Fitted with high-resolution sensor arrays and powerful computer systems, they lurk in the darkness of inhabited systems, sifting through the communications of entire planets for signs of seditious or disloyal activity. An EF-97 does not announce its presence as a Star Destroyer would; in many cases by the time rebels or political dissidents in-system realise the eyes of the Empire are upon them, and the ISB's trap is ready to be sprung.

To prevent intelligence leaks, the EF-97 has a small sentient crew augmented by a large contingent of droids who are routinely memory-wiped. A small number of Imperial Security Bureau analysts oversee a bank of tactical droids who tirelessly sift through the data. The minimal staffing of the EF-97 is both a blessing and a curse, however- if a Rebel cell can catch a lurking EF-97 it is relatively easy to board and sieze control of the vessel. As a result, the Rebel Alliance now possesses several, turning them from tools of oppression into vital early-warning systems.
