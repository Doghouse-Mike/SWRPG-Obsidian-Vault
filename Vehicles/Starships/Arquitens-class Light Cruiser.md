Known colloquially as the Imperial light cruiser, the Imperial Navy's *Arquitens* class is its premier multipurpose cruiser.

> More information about the starship is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Arquitens-class_light_cruiser).

**Hull Type/Class:** Light Cruiser/*Arquitens*-class. \
**Manufacturer:** Kuat Drive Yards. \
**Hyperdrive:** Primary: Class 2, Backup: Class 12. \
**Navicomputer:** Yes. \
**Sensor Range:** Medium. \
**Ship's Complement:** 750[^0-1][^2-3] *or* 100[^1-4] officers, pilots, and enlisted crew. \
**Starfighter Complement:** Three TIE-series starfighters, two TIE-series bombers, or one [[Sentinel-class Landing Craft|*Sentinel*-class landing craft]][^0-1][^2-3] *or* none[^1-4].\
**Vehicle Complement:** Assorted landspeeders, small shuttles, and ship's boats.\
**Encumbrance Capacity:** 1,200. \
**Passenger Capacity:** 100. \
**Consumables:** Three months. \
**Price/Rarity:** 5,000,000[^0-1][^2-3] *or* 4,000,000[^1-4] credits (R)/5. \
**Customization Hard Points:** 2.

**Weapons:**[^0-1]

- Two dorsal and two ventral turret-mounted light turbolaser (Fire Arc All; Damage 9; Critical 3; Range \[Medium\]; [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] 2, [[Item Qualities#Linked (Active)|Linked]] 1, [[Item Qualities#Slow-Firing (Passive)|Slow-Firing]] 1).
- Four port and four starboard quad laser cannons (Fire Arc Port or Starboard; Damage 5; Critical 3; Range \[Close\]; [[Item Qualities#Accurate (Passive)|Accurate]] 1, [[Item Qualities#Linked (Active)|Linked]] 3).
- Four forward-mounted concussion missile launchers (Fire Arc Forward; Damage 6; Critical 3; Range \[Short\]; [[Item Qualities#Blast (Active)|Blast]] 4, [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] 4, [[Item Qualities#Guided (Active)|Guided]] 3, [[Item Qualities#Slow-Firing (Passive)|Slow-Firing]] 1).
- Forward-mounted medium tractor beam emitter (Fire Arc Forward; Damage –; Critical –; Range \[Short\]; [[Item Qualities#Tractor (Passive)|Tractor]] 4).

*or* (during the Clone Wars)[^1-4]

- Two dorsal and two ventral retractable turret-mounted light turbolasers (Fire Arc All Dorsal or All Ventral; Damage 9; Critical 3; Range \[Medium\]; [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] 2, [[Item Qualities#Linked (Active)|Linked]] 1, [[Item Qualities#Slow-Firing (Passive)|Slow-Firing]] 1).
- Two port and two starboard retractable quad heavy laser cannons (Fire Arc Port or Starboard; Damage 6; Critical 3; Range \[Short\]; [[Item Qualities#Linked (Active)|Linked]] 3).
- Four forward-mounted concussion missile launchers (Fire Arc Forward; Damage 6; Critical 3; Range \[Short\]; [[Item Qualities#Blast (Active)|Blast]] 4, [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] 4, [[Item Qualities#Guided (Active)|Guided]] 3, [[Item Qualities#Limited Ammo (Passive)|Limited Ammo]] 3, [[Item Qualities#Linked (Active)|Linked]] 3, [[Item Qualities#Slow-Firing (Passive)|Slow-Firing]] 1).
