Produced in vast numbers by the Incom Corporation, the Z-28 *Skywing* is one of the most common starfighters in service in local security forces and defence groups. The Republic is under direct control of a number of *Skywings*, which it uses for VIP escort duty and support tasks while further squadrons of *Skywings* are owned by the fleets of many member worlds of the Republic Defence Coalition.

The small fighter sports an arrowhead-shaped profile. With rear mounted twin sublight engines, that feature Incom’s proprietary fuisal thrust design, the small fighter boasts a not inconsiderable top speed. The *Skywing* also features two underslung, forward-facing RX Defender laser cannons. A small shielding unit to the rear of the starfighter gives the vessel extra protection, compensating for its slightly below average armouring. While finally, a short-range hyperdrive affords the Z-28 a level of agility and distance that many similar security fighters lack.

While the Z-28 *Skywing* is unlikely to win any individual awardsfor its speed, armament, shields or hyperdrive, taken as a whole the Z-28 is a very capable starfighter, that in the right pilot’s hands can be pushed to excellent.
