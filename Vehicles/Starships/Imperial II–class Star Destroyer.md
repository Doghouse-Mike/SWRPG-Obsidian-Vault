More casually known as the "ImpStar Deuce" by the Rebels who fly against it, the *Imperial II*–class Star Destroyer is the next-generation successor to the *Imperial I*–class.

> More information about the starship is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_II-class_Star_Destroyer).
