For most of the Empire’s history, starfighters were almost exclusively launched from carriers or installations with dedicated TIE hangars. Now on its back foot, the Imperial Navy has been forced to adapt, utilizing vehicles that may land and launch from even the most rugged of environments.

Visually identical to the iconic TIE/ln starfighter, the Outland TIE features folding solar collection panels and a landing gear. These enable it to land without a standard TIE docking armature and grants easy access for pilots. These added mechanism add mass to the fighter, making it slightly more sluggish than a standard TIE.
