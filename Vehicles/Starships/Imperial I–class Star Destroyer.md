Considered the "face of the Imperial Navy," and one of the most powerful ships in current service, KDY's *Imperial*–class Star Destroyer is part of a long line of frighteningly powerful battleships fielded by the Imperial Navy.

> More information about the starship is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_I-class_Star_Destroyer).
