Officially designated the *Nau'ur*-class, "illumination" in Mando'a, these yachts are commonly referred to simply as Kalevalans as the custom-order nature of their production precludes the commonalities of a typical ship class.

> More information about the starship is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nau%27ur-class_yacht).
