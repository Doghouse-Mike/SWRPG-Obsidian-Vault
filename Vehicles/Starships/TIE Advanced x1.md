On Darth Vader's TIE Advanced x1, the following unique attachment takes up the vehicle's hard point:

**Life-Support Suit Integration:** When piloted by Darth Vader, his TIE Advanced X1 increases its handling by 1 and system strain threshold by 2, and all mounted weapons gain Accurate 1.
