Rothana Heavy Engineering’s Mobile Tactical Pod is a troop transport and dropship used by security forces across the galaxy. Famed for their engineering masterpiece that was the Republic’s Low Altitude Assault Transport (LAAT) during the Clone Wars, Rothana wanted to adapt the principles of that ship but on a much smaller scale, the Mobile Tac-Pod was the result.

The small dropship is capable of ferrying up to 10 passengers to planetary engagements in its cramped, padded interior. A single pilot in an elevated cockpkit controls the ship as well as its weapons systems. The passengers can enter and exit the Pod through a rear entry ramp, which is accessible when the Pod’s wings are retracted into landing mode. The Tac-Pods are armed with a single laser cannon placed in a ball mount on the chin, as well as a fixed cannon located on each of the wing root -just outside of the engine cowling. This ensures that the Tac-Pod can perform well as an air support vehicle and take care of itself if landing in a hot zone.

Rothana hoped that the lower price tag attached to the Mobile Tac-Pod as well as its defensive capabilities would make the the dropship an attractive prospect for Imperial use. However the Empire found the cramped interior and the small carrying capacity too much of a downside, and instead opted to develop the *Sentinel*-class landing craft with Sienar Fleet Systems. Looking to recoup some of their development losses, Rothana began selling the Mobile Tac-Pod in bulk to planetary and corporate security forces. Now, the Mobile TacPod can be seen in use by the Corporate Sector Authority, the Eriadu police force and Preox Morlana to name just a few.
