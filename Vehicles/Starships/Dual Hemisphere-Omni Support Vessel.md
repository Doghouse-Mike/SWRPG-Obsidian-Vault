A smaller version of the *Lucrehulk*-class by the same manufacturer, the DH-O support vessel was intended as a slow commercial freight prior to the Clone Wars. In comparison to the larger vessel, this one was dedicated to freight and not expected to be used in any combat scenario. As such, it was reliant on shielding and armour to survive piracy. With marginally less cargo capacity than a *Lucrehulk*, the slower hyperdrive was not of great concern.

During the Clone Wars, the DH-O was not widely seen until later in the conflict. It was pressed into service beyond the role of a supply transport, and into a support role during blockades and planetary defence or invasion. In some cases, it was used as a secondary command and control vessel, due to its greater hardiness than the *Lucrehulk*.
