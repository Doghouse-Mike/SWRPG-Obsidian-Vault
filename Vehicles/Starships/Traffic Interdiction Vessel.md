Appearing as an old freighter to sensors, and visually, this vessel was one of many models used for Traffic Interdiction Operations (TIOPS) for the SO BDE. It was heavily armed, capable of bringing down a large amount of ordnance onto a target, often coercing a surrender ahead of time simply by revealing their weapon systems.

The goal of this freighter was to get within range of a vessel suspected of being a Separatist craft. These could be smugglers working for the CIS, or even a CIS’ own special operations mission attempting to go unnoticed. While the shuttle did not operate any ion weapons or assault boarding tubes, these were rarely necessary. There were however instances of these vessels being confused for pirates.
