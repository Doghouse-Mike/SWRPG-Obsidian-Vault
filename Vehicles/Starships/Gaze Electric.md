A truly one of a kind vessel, the *Gaze Electric* is the flagship of Marchion Ro, Eye of the Nihil and commander of its legions. The ship was built almost 150 years ago as a mobile home and temple for the Path of the Open Hand cult. An extremist missionary order originally from the frontier world of Dalna, the Path believed that the Force should be free and never be used by anyone, including the Jedi Order. At some point, the Path’s mobile temple fell into the hands of the Ro family and they took the strange ship as their own flag ship.

More haunted fortress than starship, the *Gaze Electric* has a distinctive profile with a narrow midship section flanked by a bulbous and jagged module forward and a flat arrowhead section aft. Running all along the top of the ship are sensor aerials, spires and towers, giving the upper decks the look of crumbling castle parapets. Once the Ros took over the Gaze Electric they began retrofitting new weapons systems to the vessel, turning it into a true fortress for them.

The *Gaze Electric*’s massive interior was designed to carry thousands of passengers and crew, yet its corridors, hallways and rooms sit empty and abandoned. Unlike the other vessels utilised by the Nihil, the *Gaze Electric* has been rigged to run with a minimal crew of droids and hired personnel rather than by Nihil pirates themselves. Its captain’s paranoia forbidding him from trusting any of his Nihil underlings with his prized vessel. Because of this reliance on automation and only a skeleton crew, the *Gaze Electric* is powerful but not as creative or responsive in combat as a ship of a similar size that is crewed by organics.
