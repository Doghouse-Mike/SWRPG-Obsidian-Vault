### ADDITIONAL RULES

**Cluster Bombs:** The standard MC30c is equipped with cluster bombs, which are dispersed by launchers to destroy or deter enemy fighters. When the ship fires its cluster bombs, they are deployed around the frigate and remain in that area. Whenever a ship enters close range with an MC30c frigate that has dispersed its cluster bombs, it is exposed to the devices. The pilot of such a ship must succeed on a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Piloting - Space (Ag)|Piloting]] [[Piloting - Planetary (Ag)|check]]** or suffer a hit for 6 damage. If the pilot generates two [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results on the check, the GM can have another friendly ship nearby suffer 6 damage as well.

If the MC30c moves away from the area where it deployed the cluster bombs, those bombs remain as a hazard at that location, and a new field of bombs must be deployed if the MC30c wishes to maintain that defense.[^0-1]
