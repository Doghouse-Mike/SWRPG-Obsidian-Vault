A popular mid-range ship, the Fondor Haulcraft has developed a reputation as an extreme solid ship and is commonly seen in the hands of traders and travellers looking for the reliability and precision associated with the Fondor name.

Perpetually ranked third behind Corellia and Kuat in starship construction, Fondor has staked its reputation not on mass-production but instead on quality. Many of the best designed frigates and capital ships are built at the shipyards there, from the Super Star Destroyer Executor to luxury Hutt yachts. The Haulcraft was Fondor’s attempt to enter the smaller, freighter market, a market dominated by Corellia’s YT series of starships. Unable to compete with the YT series’ low price or high modularity, the Fondor Haulcraft instead emphasised reliability and build quality, two aspects that are often complained about in YT ships.

The Fondor Haulcraft consists of a 35 metre long, wedge-shaped central fuselage and a pair of retractable wings that span the length of the ship. The ship can be accessed by a personal loading ramp and a larger cargo loading ramp that leads directly into the ship’s small cargo bay. The ship’s interior takes cues from Fondor’s work on Hutt luxury ships and includes polished brass surfaces as well as plush interior padding and seating. Some owners however have been known to strip away these interior accents and use the Fondor Haulcraft with its handsomely functional default metallic look. The ship’s off-centre cockpit houses seating for a single pilot who has access to all of the ship’s functions from their consoles.

Under the hood is where the Fondor Haulcraft really shines, the ship borrows components and circuitry from Fondor’s Imperial contracts, ensuring that the ship computes and reacts faster than many others of a similar price. The Haulcraft’s communication system is also more powerful than expected, with a long range and a degree of clarity and signal strength unusual in a civilian ship. The ship’s engines, while not as fast as many on the market, are tested for extreme stressand durability and many manoeuvres that would fry other freighter’s engines can be performed by the Haulcraft with ease.

The Fondor does have a few drawbacks however. It’s relatively small cargo space holds the ship back from ever being a true transport ship, relegating the ship to the category of courier shuttle or light freighter at best. The ship’s single pilot, while an asset in many situations, is a drawback in combat as the pilot must operate the Haulcraft’s light laser cannon while also flying. Fondor Shipyards have recently begun offering a proprietary droid mod to counter this. The mod can be installed aboard the Haulcraft to assist with operations and help manage duties in intense situations.

Despite Fondor Shipyard’s usual distaste for customisation of their products, the Haulcraft is surprisingly modifiable, with the ship having dozens of systems that can be swapped out and replaced by an enterprising engineer. This boosts the usefulness of the small ship and the Haulcraft is beginning to be seen in the employ of pirates and politicians, couriers and colonists from the Core to the Outer Rim.

### THE CANNOK FLARE

Source: [^2]

Luthen Rael currently flies a heavily modified Fondor Haulcraft officially known as the *Cannok Flare*. An advanced custom droid mod has been added to handle a majority of the ship’s operations, including navigation, precision targeting and more. The *Cannok Flare* also features sensor jamming stealth technology, designed to make the ship invisible to most scanners. For defence, the *Cannok Flare* also features countermeasures and weapons not seen on the standard Haulcraft model; a hidden, top-mounted dual laser cannon and a disguised variable munitions flechette launcher. The *Cannok Flare*’s most unique weapon however is its sustained composite beam weapon, a miniaturised version of the scrap cutters seen employed on worlds such as Raxus Prime and Bracca. These beams are able to slice through light, shielded ships, tearing them to pieces.

Despite its plain exterior appearance, the *Cannok Flare* is undoubtedly far more dangerous and advanced than any other Fondor Haulcraft in the space lanes.
