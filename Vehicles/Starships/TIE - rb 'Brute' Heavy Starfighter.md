The TIE/rb heavy starfighter is an up-gunner variant of the standard TIE/ln starfighter. Because of its bulky frame and heavy cannons, it earned the nickname "the TIE brute" from its pilots.

> More information about the starship is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TIE/rb_heavy_starfighter).

During structured gameplay, the droid brain takes a turn directly following the pilot's turn.
