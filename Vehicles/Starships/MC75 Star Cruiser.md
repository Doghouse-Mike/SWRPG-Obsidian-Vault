> More information about the starship is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MC75_Star_Cruiser).

**Hull Type/Class:** Star Cruiser/MC75. \
**Manufacturer:** Mon Calamari Shipyards. \
**Hyperdrive:** Primary: Class 1, Backup: Class 9. \
**Navicomputer:** Yes. \
**Sensor Range:** Long. \
**Ship's Complement:** 3,225 officers, pilots, and crew. \
**Starfighter Complement:** 36 starfighters. \
**Vehicle Complement:** [[CR90 Corvette|CR90 corvette]] or comparable in launch bay; numerous shuttles, landing craft, and utility vehicles. \
**Encumbrance Capacity:** 65,000. \
**Passenger Capacity:** 1,000 troops and support staff. \
**Consumables:** Two years. \
**Price/Rarity:** 88,000,000 credits/8. \
**Customization Hard Points:** 4.

**Weapons:**

- Six port and six starboard medium turbolasers (Fire Arc Port *or* Starboard; Damage 10; Critical 3; Range \[Long\]; [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] 3, [[Item Qualities#Slow-Firing (Passive)|Slow-Firing]] 1).
- Two port and two starboard battleship ion cannons (Fire Arc Forward, Aft, and Port *or* Forward, Aft, and Starboard; Damage 9; Critical 4; Range \[Medium\]; [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] 3, [[Item Qualities#Ion (Passive)|Ion]], [[Item Qualities#Slow-Firing (Passive)|Slow-Firing]] 2).
- Five forward, five port, five starboard, and five aft point defense twin heavy laser cannons (Fire Arc Forward, Port, and Starboard *or* Forward, Port and Aft *or* Forward, Starboard, and Aft *or* Aft, Port, and Starboard; Damage 6; Critical 3; Range \[Short\]; [[Item Qualities#Linked (Active)|Linked]] 1).
- Three forward, three port, three starboard, and three aft proton torpedo launchers (Fire Arc Forward *or* Port *or* Starboard *or* Aft; Damage 8; Critical 2; Range \[Short\]; [[Item Qualities#Blast (Active)|Blast]] 6, [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] 6, [[Item Qualities#Guided (Active)|Guided]] 2, [[Item Qualities#Slow-Firing (Passive)|Slow-Firing]] 1).
- Six hull-mounted heavy tractor beams (Fire Arc All; Damage –; Critical –; Range \[Short\]; [[Item Qualities|Tractor]] 6).

### ADDITIONAL RULES

**Massive 2:** When making an attack targeting this starship, the critical rating of any weapons used counts as 2 higher.

**Inspiring Presence:** When this starship is engaged with the enemy, all allies gain two [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost dice]] on any [[Cool (Pr)|Cool]] or [[Discipline (Will)|Discipline]] checks to resist fear caused by combat.
