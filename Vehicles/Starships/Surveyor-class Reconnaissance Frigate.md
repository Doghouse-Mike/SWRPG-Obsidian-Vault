### ADDITIONAL RULES

**Sensor Suite:** The powerful sensors and signals intelligence systems in the *Surveyor* class upgrade the ability of any [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] or [[Computers (Int)|Computers]] checks made while using the sensors.

**Run Silent:** *Surveyor*-class ships are equipped with an array of stealth systems that makes them incredibly difficult to detect, even when running active scans. Add three [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice to any skill check made to detect a *Surveyor*-class ship.
