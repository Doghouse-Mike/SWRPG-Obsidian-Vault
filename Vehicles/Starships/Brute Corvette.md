Recently spotted leading Nihil attacks, these fearsome corvettes are assumed to be some kind of command or flagship vessel for the marauders. The *Brute* corvette was produced in limited numbers by Creako Collective, an obscure Outer Rim manufacturer. The secretive and fiercely independent shipwrights have a long history of producing strange vessels and the *Brute* is no exception to this lineage.

The ship has clearly been designed from the keel up as a pure, single- purpose warfare machine. Composed as one, large solid wedge shape, the *Brute* is powered by a pair of oversized ionisation reactor engines giving it significant speed.

Bristling with turbo lasers, quad laser turrets, and concussion missile launchers, these ships are dedicated starfighter killers. They are heavily armed and shielded to protect them from massed fighter attacks, and are equipped with powerful targeting computers that allow the weapons to track fast and agile starfighters. This means the *Brute* Corvette is a consummate anti-starfighter and anti-capital ship vessel. Outer Rim planets have come to fear the sight of a Nihil *Brute* jumping into system ahead of a swarm of deadly Nihil Strikeships.
