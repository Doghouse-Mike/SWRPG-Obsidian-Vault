Nubia Star Drives makes some of the most distinctive vessels in the galaxy.

> More information about the starship is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/A-type_Stiletto).

The missiles are incredibly accurate and generally can kill another starfighter in a single shot. However, reloads must be purchased on Nubia and are expensive: each missile costs 1,000 credits and is (R).
