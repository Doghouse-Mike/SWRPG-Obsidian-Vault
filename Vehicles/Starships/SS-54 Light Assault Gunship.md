Designed to compete with the likes of the GAT-12 Skipray and the *Firespray-31-*class patrol craft, the SS-54 is a small, relatively fast vessel built for short-range patrols and ground attack.

> More information about the starship is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SS-54_assault_ship).
