### ADDITIONAL RULES

**Interior Ordnance:** Unlike most ship-mounted weapons, a *Barrage*'s torpedo tubes can be manually reloaded from internal reserves. As an action, anyone aboard may make an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Athletics (Br)|Athletics check]]** to reload one proton torpedo launcher.
