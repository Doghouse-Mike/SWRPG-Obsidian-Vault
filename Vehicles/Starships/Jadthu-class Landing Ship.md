### ARAKYD INDUSTRIES CALTROP-5 CHAFF GUN

\[*Jadthu*-class ships\] were equipped with defensive laser turrets and physical countermeasure systems, such as the Arakyd Industries Caltrop-5 Chaff Gun\[.\]

The Caltrop-5 by Arakyd Industries is ostensibly a physical countermeasures system that uses clouds of allow splinters to spoof sensors.

As a physical countermeasures system, the Caltrop-5 upgrades the difficulty of all [[Gunnery (Ag)|Gunnery]] checks and checks triggered by the [[Narrative Dice#Guided (Active)|Guided]] quality made using missiles, rockets, or torpedoes targeting the starship once.

In addition, it upgrades the difficulty of [[Piloting - Space (Ag)|Piloting]] checks for other ships within close range once. A despair result on a [[Piloting - Space (Ag)|Piloting]] check may be spent to inflict a Critical Hit on that pilot's ship.
