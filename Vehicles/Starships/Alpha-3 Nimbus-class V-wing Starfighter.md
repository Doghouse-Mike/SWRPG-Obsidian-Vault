### SYLUIRE-45 HYPERSPACE DOCKING RING

Source: [^1-2]

Designed by TransGalMeg in association with KSE, the Syliure-45 is a medium range hyperspace sled designed to provide hyperspace capabilities to both the [[Eta-2 Actis-class Light Interceptor|Eta-2 Actis]] and the Alpha-3 Nimbus interceptors. The successor to the Syliure-31 used by the previous generation Delta-series fighters, the Syliure-45 is a relatively simple affair consisting of six power ion engines and a KSE Mk.II Jumpmaster Class 1 hyperdrive.
