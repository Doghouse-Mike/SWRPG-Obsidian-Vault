Designed with a niche purpose in mind, the LH75 Low Orbit Specialty Live-Haul Frigate or L.O.S. Live-Haul manufactured by Rothana Heavy Engineering was the default choice for the transport of massive live cargo by the Republic's Department of Agriculture and Nonsentient Creatures at the time the Clone Wars began.

> More information about the starship is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/LH75_Low_Orbit_Specialty_Live-Haul_Frigate).
