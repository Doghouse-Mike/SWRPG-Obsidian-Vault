Kuat Drive Yards' Nebulon-B frigate was one of the first capital ships deployed to Imperial fleets, coming into service alongside the first *Imperial*-class Star Destroyers.

> More information about the starship is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/EF76_Nebulon-B_escort_frigate).
