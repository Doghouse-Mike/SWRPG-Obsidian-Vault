- Five forward- and five aft-mounted light quad turbolasers (Fire Arc Forward, Port and Starboard *or* Aft; Damage 9; Critical 3; Range \[Medium\]; [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] 2, [[Item Qualities#Linked (Active)|Linked]] 3, [[Item Qualities#Slow-Firing (Passive)|Slow-Firing]] 1).
- Five forward-, five port-, and five starboard-mounted heavy laser cannons (Fire Arc Forward *or* Port *or* Starboard; Damage 6; Critical 3; Range \[Short\]).
