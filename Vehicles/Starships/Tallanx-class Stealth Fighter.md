Sleek and angular, with a matter black hull that camouflages it against the darkness of space, the *Tallanx*-class stealth fighter is one of the few starfighter designs to see action on Ord Gimmel during the Galactic Civil War.

> More information about the starship is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tallanx-class_stealth_fighter).

### CUSTOMIZATIONS

Source: [^2]

**Gimmellian Stealth Device:** Reduces the range band of enemy ships' passive sensors by 1 (to a minimum of close range) for detecting the *Tallanx*. In order to function, all power from shields and weapons must be channeled into the cloaking device. Rerouting power takes one action.

**Electronic Countermeasures Suite:** Blocks sensors within range when active, counting the *Tallanx*'s silhouette as 2. Immediately notifies all ships in a star system, or within 100 kilometers if planetside, to the *Tallanx*'s existence.
