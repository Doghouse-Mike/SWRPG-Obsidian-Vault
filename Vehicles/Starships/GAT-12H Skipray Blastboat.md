Another pre-Clone Wars design, Sienar Fleet Systems' Skipray Blastboat is a heavily armed and armored patrol craft and gunboat descended from the ancient Turbostorm-class gunships used by the Old Republic.

> More information about the starship is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/GAT-12_Skipray).
