### *TEMPLE*-CLASS MODULAR CARGO HOLDS

One of the *Temple*-class heavy freighter's more attractive features is a series of modular cargo holds designed for it by Kuat Drive Yards. Each hold is capable of transporting up to 90,000 tons of cargo, depending on configuration. Though they have no locomotive systems of their own, most of the *Temple*-class cargo holds have self-contained life support systems. At the freighter captain's option, individual cargo bays can be detached in open space \[...\].

Each type of modular cargo hold is detailed below. This list is far from exhaustive. GMs with specific ideas \[...\] should feel free to modify them however they wish.

#### BULK CARGO HOLD

The standard cargo hold utilized by the *Temple*-class heavy freighter is known as the bulk cargo hold.

#### DETENTION BLOCK/PRISON CONTAINMENT

Detention block/prison containment holds are designed to confine prisoners and other unwilling passengers. \[...\] Prisoners in detention block are kept under one of three circumstances: solitary confinement, shared confinement, or general confinement.

Each detention block hold provides cramped accommodations for up to fifty additional security personnel, though this number varies depending on how many prisoners are kept aboard.

#### ENVIRONMENTAL CONDITIONED HOLD

From scorching temperatures to sub-freezing climates, environmentally-conditioned cargo holds can maintain any form of internal climate.

#### HAZARDOUS MATERIALS CONTAINMENT

Similar in design to an environmentally conditioned hold, hazardous material containment holds are slightly different in that their cargo is volatile in some way. \[...\] Shielding comes standard with such holds, preventing unwanted emissions from harming nearby ships or orbital environments.

#### PASSENGER COMPARTMENTS

Unlike detention/prisoner containment holds, *Temple*-compatible passenger compartments are practically luxurious.
