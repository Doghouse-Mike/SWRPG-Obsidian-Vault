- Forward mounted twin proton torpedo launcher (Fire Arc Forward; Range \[Short\]; Damage 8; Critical 2; [[Item Qualities#Blast (Active)|Blast]] 6, [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] 6, [[Item Qualities#Guided (Active)|Guided]] 2, [[Item Qualities#Linked (Active)|Linked]] 1, [[Item Qualities#Limited Ammo (Passive)|Limited Ammo]] 8, [[Item Qualities#Slow-Firing (Passive)|Slow-Firing]] 1).
