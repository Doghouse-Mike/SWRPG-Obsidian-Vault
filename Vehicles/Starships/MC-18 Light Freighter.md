**Consumables:** Two months. \
**Price/Rarity:** 140,000 credits/6. \
**Customization Hard Points:** 2.

**Weapons:**

- Dorsal turret-mounted medium laser cannon (Fire Arc All; Damage 6; Critical 3; Range \[Close\]).

### ADDITIONAL RULES

**Custom Construction:** When acquiring an MC-18 light freighter, choose one attachment that requires a single hard point and add it to the ship at no additional cost. This attachment cannot be removed and does not count against the ship's total hard points.
