A product of the same Sienar Fleet Systems/Cygnus Spaceworks collaboration that produced the *Lambda*-class T-4a shuttle, the *Sentinel* class is the Imperial Navy's primary assault ship and landing craft.

> More information about the starship is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sentinel-class_landing_craft).

### ADDITIONAL RULES

Source: [^0-1]

**Modular Cargo Module:** The *Sentinel*-class landing craft can be fitted with a large cargo module, allowing it to transport larger payloads, including ground vehicles. While this module is attached, make the following changes to the vehicle profile: Handling: −2, Encumbrance Capacity: 1,500
