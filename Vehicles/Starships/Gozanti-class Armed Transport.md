The CEC/Gallofree Yards *Gozanti*-class armed transport is a cruiser-sized vessel used by numerous private and governmental organizations as a bulk cargo carrier and convoy escort.

> More information about the starship is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Gozanti-class_cruiser).
