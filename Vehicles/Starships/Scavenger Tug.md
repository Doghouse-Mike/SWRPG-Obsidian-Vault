Heavy salvage jobs require specialized tools for the trade. When derelict bulk freighters cannot be repaired or towed into dock for a complete overhaul, or when capital ships meet catastrophic ends, they must be dismantled and disposed of. Usually based from another ship or space dock, a salvage crew is often dispatched in a Scavenger Tug, equipped with fine manipulator arms, and plasma cutting torches. Once the wreckage has been separated into smaller pieces, the tug uses its tractor beam projector to haul them to a collection ship or cargo container.
