This highly modified pursuit craft was intentionally designed to provide planetary security for worlds that couldn't afford to maintain their own starfighter squadrons or to purchase extravagant capital ships with which to defend themselves.

> More information about the starship is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_Caster).
