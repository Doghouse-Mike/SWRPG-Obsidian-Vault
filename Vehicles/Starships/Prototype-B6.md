Prototype-B6, also known as the Blade Wing, is a one-off starfighter that served as the prototype for the Rebellion's powerful B-wing heavy attack starfighter.

> More information about the starship is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Prototype_B6).
