### ENGINEERING ACCESS

One of the Explorer's more intelligent design features is the network of sub-deck access passages. \[..\] Anyone working on the internal systems of the E-9 Explorer-class vessels, such as the power system, hyperdrive, computers, sensors, etcetera, gains a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to any [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics]] or [[Computers (Int)|Computers]] skill checks thanks to the ease of reaching systems that are typically hidden behind bulkheads and sealed beneath deck plates..
