Designed for scouts and lone pilots, Sublight Products Corporation's WTK-85A is one of the finest examples of a long-range reconnaissance ship on the market today. Considered by many Core World designers as a second- class, backwater manufacturer, Sublight Products Corporation (SPC) was founded just under a century ago by a group of Trandoshan shipwrights. Despite the snobbish attitude of designers at Kuat, CEC or Fondor, SPC nevertheless have developed a strong range of high-quality and reasonably-priced starships. SPC got their start working jointly with Incom Corporation on their incredibly successful Z-95 Headhunter range. The two companies further expanded on this partnership with the successful ARC-170 starfighter, which debuted during the early days of the Clone Wars. Despite this successful partnership, SPC never got its fair share of the credit for their work and were overshadowed by the far larger Incom Corporation and after developing the ARC-170, the two companies split.

One of the first ships SPC produced as a solo company was the WTK-85A Interstellar Transport. SPC took many of the aspects of the ARC-170 and extrapolated them into a freighter to produce an excellent long-range reconnaissance transport. Every aspect of the WTK has been designed from the ground up for solo travellers and scouts. All of the ship’s systems can be handled from the pilot’s consoles, including weapons. A built-in Mahara V2 navicomputer allows the WTK to move through hyperspace faster and more safely, without the need for a co-pilot or atsromech. The ship is also packed with redundancies, to ensure that a lone pilot can handle any and all emergencies. The WTK also comes with comfortable, well-appointed living quarters, perfect for extended travel. For defence, the ship comes with a rotating laser turret as well as a pair of chin-mounted laser cannons, when combined with the ship’s impressive speed, these help make the WTK a very effective hit and fade freighter.
