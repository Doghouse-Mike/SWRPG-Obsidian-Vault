Manufactured by the Corellian Engineering Corporation, featuring their iconic triple engine block, this carrier was used by the GAR as a dropship where the *Acclamator* was considered too large for purpose. The larger cousin to the CR-20, this carrier was the dropship of choice for the SO BDE.

Used in small operational theaters, the dropship could carry a full platoon of regular infantry or a troop of commandos with all necessary gear and support vehicles to a combat zone. Later in the Clone Wars, some started to relate the CR25 as a stubby version of the new *Gozanti*-class freighters that had entered production..

> More information about the starship is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/CR25_troop_carrier).
