The *Vindicator*-class heavy cruisers were designed by Sienar Fleet Systems to take the place of the elderly *Dreadnought*-class heavy cruisers as the standard, multi-role fleet cruiser for the Imperial Navy.

> More information about the starship is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vindicator-class_heavy_cruiser).
