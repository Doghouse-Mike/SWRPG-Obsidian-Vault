**Maneuvering Mode:** As a maneuver, the pilot may switch the StarViper into or out of maneuvering mode. When in maneuvering mode, the StarViper increases its speed and handling by 1. However, its twin arm-mounted heavy laser cannons change their Fire Arc to "Forward."
