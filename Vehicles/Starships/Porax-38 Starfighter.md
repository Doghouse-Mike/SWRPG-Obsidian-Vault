The Porax-38 was purpose designed for the Utapau Skyforce by a longtime Pau’an military starship designer. As a patrol fighter, it was given an exceptional sensor suite. This allowed it to detect incoming craft and still have time to call back to command and organise a response. The starfighter was a stand-out design and overall of high build quality. What sets the Porax-38 aside from most system defence craft is that it had a hyperdrive and navicomputer, Class 1 no less on most models. This allowed the Skyforce to project their defence network to the greater Tarabba sector which Utapau was located in. With the CIS arriving on Utapau, the P-38 was one of the many fighters co-opted by the corporate interests and scavenged into models more suited the model of mass warfare.

> More information about the starship is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rogue-class_Porax-38_starfighter).
