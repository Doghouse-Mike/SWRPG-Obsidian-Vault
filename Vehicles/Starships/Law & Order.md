Ageing [[Arquitens-class Light Cruiser|Arquitens-class light cruisers]], the duo of *Law* and *Order* once served the Republic in the Clone Wars, helping break Separatist blockades of beleaguered worlds. Now, they serve the Galactic Empire as oppressors themselves. Helmed by officers in dead-end career postings, they enforce petty Imperial directives with vigour of the bored and resentful.
