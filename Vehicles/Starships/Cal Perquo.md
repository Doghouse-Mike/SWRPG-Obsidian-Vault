A truly ancient design, the *Cal*-class (otherwise known as the *Kumauri*-class) dates back to the earliest days of the Republic. Before effective planetary shields, its asteroid accelerators were key weapons for planetary bombardment. The sturdy design of the *Cal*-class has enabled them to last long beyond their expiry dates. The *Cal Perquo* has been a pirate vessel for generations, menacing under-developed planets and isolated mining stations with its vast mass driver cannon.
