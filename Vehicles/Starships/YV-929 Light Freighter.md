A rarity among the Corellian Engineering Corporation's YV-series, which primarily consists of cargo vessels, the YV-929 was designed from the keel up as an armed transport.

> More information about the starship is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/YV-929_light_freighter).
