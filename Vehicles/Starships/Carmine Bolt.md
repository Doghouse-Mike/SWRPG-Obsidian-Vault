The frigate *Carmine Bolt* is one of the few true warships owned by the Crimson Dawn. A *[[Starbolt Assault Carrier]]* designed for independent planetary assault, it is the perfect platform for a full-spectrum shakedown; allowing the Crimson Dawn to overwhelm isolated locations with starfighters and troops, then fill the capacious hold with their ill-gotten gains.
