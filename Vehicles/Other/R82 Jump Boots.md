Aratech's R82 jump boots are commonly used for sports and leisure activities, but many intrepid explorers and scouts have also found them useful for exploring less accessible areas on newly located worlds.

## R82 JUMP BOOTS

Source: [^0-1][^2]

![Encumbrance 2 Price 2,000 Rarity 6|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/9/93/Profile_R82_Jump_Boots.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 2 Price 2,000 Rarity 6

Jump boots allow the user to function as a silhouette 1, speed 2, handling –1, system strain threshold 3 vehicle that can only operate in atmosphere, has a maximum altitude of thirty-five meters, and requires [[Piloting - Planetary (Ag)|Piloting (Planetary)]] to operate.[^0-1][^3]
