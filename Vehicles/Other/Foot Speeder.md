These curious contraptions combine the swiftness of a speeder car with an easy of storage and simple construction that makes keeping and maintaining them much less expensive than doing so for an actual vehicle.

## FOOT SPEEDER

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 4 Price 2,000 Rarity 4|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/c/ce/Profile_Foot_Speeder.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 4 Price 2,000 Rarity 4

A foot speeder allows a person to function as a Silhouette 1, Speed 1, Handling –1, System Strain Threshold 4 vehicle that can only operate in atmosphere, and requires [[Piloting - Planetary (Ag)|Piloting (Planetary)]] to operate. It has a flight ceiling of five meters.
