Usually deployed to worlds without much of an Imperial presence, the Light Infantry Speeder is intended to be a versatile all-round vehicle that can perform well in a variety of situations. The four-seater landspeeder is manned by one pilot, and can hold three other passengers , one of which can take control of the forward-mounted WD-90 repeating blaster cannon. This gunner usually rides in the prow, scanning for targets. The mounting attachment gives the gunner a wide, lateral field of fire. The blaster cannon can also decompress or elevate to defend against aerial and ground threats and can even be removed completely and placed in the prow, when the speeder is not on patrol duty.

The LIF is an ugly, boxy, wedge shaped design, the much smaller and cheaper brother of the Occupier Assault Tank. Imperial designers have speculated that the LIF would be a good candidate for adaptation into a cold-weather specific vehicle.With the removal of one of the speeder’s seats, a heating system could be added and the vehicle would be much more capable in frigid environments, an area where the Empire is currently lacking in vehicles.
