## ROCKET BOOTS

Source: [^1][^2]

![Encumbrance 2 Price 2,500 Rarity 8|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/1/13/Profile_Rocket_Boots.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 2 Price 2,500 Rarity 8

Rocket boots enable a person to function as a silhouette 1, speed 1, handling –3, system strain threshold 4[^0-3] *or* 3[^1-4] vehicle that can only operate in atmosphere and requires [[Piloting - Planetary (Ag)|Piloting (Planetary)]] to operate. At the end of each round of use, rocket boots suffer 1 system strain as their fuel depletes.[^0-3][^1-4]
