Designed to add hyperspace capabilities to smaller ships without hyperdrives like starfighters and some patrol boats, TransGalMeg Industries Syliure-25 is one example of a number of similar sleds and hyperspace rings in use throughout the galaxy.

> More information about the starship is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Syliure-25_hyperdrive_module).

### SYLIURE-25 HYPERDRIVE SLED

Source: [^1]

Equipped with a class 2 hyperdrive and either an R1 or R2 astromech droid, the Syliure-25 is good for five hyperspace jumps. It attaches to the starfighter (any starship with silhouette 3) and reduces its handling by 2 and speed by 1, but can be jettisoned and reattached in space. A Syliure-25 costs 10,000 credits.
