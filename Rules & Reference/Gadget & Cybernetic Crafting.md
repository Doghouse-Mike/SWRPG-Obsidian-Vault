### INSTALLING CYBERNETICS

While a full medical facility can usually install cybernetics without a check, a PC might need to make a check when working in less than ideal circumstances.

Installing a cybernetic requires at least basic med supplies (such as an [[Emergency Medpac|emergency medpac]]). The character must dedicate six hours and make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Medicine (Int)|Medicine]]** check. Increase the difficulty twice if the character is operating on \[themselves\]. If the character succeeds, the cybernetic is now attached and functional. If the character fails, the cybernetic does not take and suffers minor damage from the failed integration. The GM may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] result to inflict 3 strain or 1 wound on the patient, and may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result to have the patient suffer a Critical Injury from the botched surgery.
