## CLONE REGIMENTS

\[...\] The following regiment profiles list unique benefits for members of these units. These are best used with the profile for a Rival-level clone NPC, but GMs are encouraged to apply them to any characters that are members of each respective regiment. Membership of a clone regiment and its subsequent benefit can also serve as a mid-level Duty reward for player characters.

### REGIMENT PROFILES

#### 13TH "IRON" BATTALION

Source: [^0-1]

Working under the command of Jedi Master Jaro Tapal, the 13th "Iron" Battalion operates as a quick-deployment response unit, defending Republic worlds against surprise Separatist strikes.

- **Commanding Officer(s):** Jedi General Jaro Tapal, Jedi Commander Cal Kestis.
- **Armor Markings:** Yellow and white.
- **Regiment Ability:** Members of the 13th gain one rank in [[Rapid Reaction talent|Rapid Reaction]] talent.

#### 21ST NOVA CORPS

Source: [^0-1]

Commonly referred to as the Galactic Marines, the 21st Nova Corps are always ready to fight at any time, in any environment.

- **Commanding Officer(s):** Jedi General Ki-Adi-Mundi, Clone Commander Bacara.
- **Armor Markings:** Purple and white.
- **Regiment Ability:** Members of the 21st remove a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die on [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] and [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]] checks imposed due to environmental hazards.

#### 41ST ELITE CORPS

Source: [^0-1]

Serving under the distinguished Jedi Master Luminara Unduli, the 41st has gained a reputation for being stubborn in their pursuit of victory.

- **Commanding Officer(s):** Jedi General Luminara Unduli, Clone Commander Gree.
- **Armor Markings:** Green and white / mottled green (forest camouflage).
- **Regiment Ability:** Members of the 41st add automatic [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] and [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results to [[Stealth (Ag)|Stealth]] and [[Survival (Cun)|Survival]] checks made in forest and jungle environments.

#### 66TH ENFORCER CORPS

Source: [^1-2]

A black-ops unit shrouded in secrecy. Its very existence is denied by Republic High Command, but rumors say it exists to deal with rogue Jedi.

- **Commanding Officer(s):** Clone Commander Stryker.
- **Armor Markings:** Black and white.
- **Regiment Ability:** Members of the 66th can negate 1 rank of the [[Reflect talent|Reflect]] talent when used against an attack they have made.

#### 78TH RECOVERY AND RESCUE UNIT

Source: [^1-2]

Under the command of the Togruta Jedi healer Talahara Osey, the 78th head into the deadliest battlefronts to recover lost or wounded troopers.

- **Commanding Officer(s):** Jedi General Talahara, Clone Commander Mercury.
- **Armor Markings:** Teal and white.
- **Regiment Ability:** Members of the 78th gain one rank in the [[Stimpack Specialization talent|Stimpack Specialization]] talent.

#### 87TH SENTINEL CORPS

Source: [^1-2]

The 87th Sentinel Corps specialize in fighting in the narrow streets of towns and cities, using the urban jungle to their advantage.

- **Commanding Officer(s):** Jedi General Tsui Choi, Clone Commander Ryyk.
- **Armor Markings:** Maroon and white.
- **Regiment Ability:** Members of the 87th gain an additional +1 ranged defense when taking cover in urban environments.

#### 91ST RECON CORPS

Source: [^1-2]

With a focus on fast deployment at a moment's notice, members of the 91st are trained to operate a vast array of Republic ground vehicles.

- **Commanding Officer(s):** Jedi General Mace Windu, Jedi General Adi Gallia, Clone Commander Neyo.
- **Armor Markings:** Dark red and white.
- **Regiment Ability:** Members of the 91st gain one rank in the [[Piloting - Planetary (Ag)|Piloting (Planetary)]] skill. Minion groups add this to their group skills instead.

#### 95TH RIFLE REGIMENT

Source: [^2-3]

One of the few clone units not under the direct command of a Jedi, this group of sharpshooters is tasked with targeting enemy officers from afar.

- **Commanding Officer(s):** Clone Major Sharp, Clone Captain Falco.
- **Armor Markings:** Dark green and white.
- **Regiment Ability:** Members of the 95th gain one rank in the [[True Aim talent|True Aim]] talent.

#### 104TH "WOLFPACK" BATTALION

Source: [^2-3]

The "Wolfpack" are known for their utilization of shock-and-awe tactics to take the fight to the droid armies, often with devastating effect.

- **Commanding Officer(s):** Jedi General Plo Koon, Clone Commander Wolffe.
- **Armor Markings:** Gray-blue and white.
- **Regiment Ability:** Members of the 104th gain one rank in the [[Clanker Killer talent|Clanker Killer]] talent.

#### 131ST "HORN" COMPANY

Source: [^2-3]

"Horn" Company distinguished itself as a crack company for ship-to-ship boarding actions.

- **Commanding Officer(s):** Jedi General Eeth Koth, Clone Captain Lock.
- **Armor Markings:** Green, yellow and white.
- **Regiment Ability:** When taking cover, members of the 131st add two automatic [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results to enemy attacks against them.

#### 177TH ELITE BATTALION

Source: [^2-3]

The 177th is so renowned amongst the Grand Army that the presence of Kiffar Jedi Kledas Sul and his men is said to be worth three regiments.

- **Commanding Officer(s):** Jedi General Kledas Sul, Clone Commander Doyen.
- **Armor Markings:** Blue, bronze and white.
- **Regiment Ability:** Members of the 177th increase the strength rating of their force by one for the purpose of Mass Combat checks.

#### 181ST SHOCK BATTALION

Source: [^3-4]

Led by Jedi Council member Coleman Kcaj, the 181st specialize in lightning attacks against unprepared targets.

- **Commanding Officer(s):** Jedi General Coleman Kcaj, Clone Commander Dumas.
- **Armor Markings:** Dark brown and white.
- **Regiment Ability:** Members of the 181st gain one rank in the [[Quick Strike talent|Quick Strike]] talent.

#### 187TH AIRBORNE LEGION

Source: [^3-4]

Specializing in assault drops, the 187th are often called upon to carry out airborne ambushes of enemy ground forces.

- **Commanding Officer(s):** Jedi General Mace Windu, Clone Commander Rawls.
- **Armor Markings:** Purple and white.
- **Regiment Ability:** Members of the 187th gain the [[Let's Ride talent|Let's Ride]] talent.

#### 212TH ATTACK BATTALION

Source: [^3-4]

Under the command of Obi-Wan Kenobi, the 212th has fought on some of the war's most deadly battlefronts.

- **Commanding Officer(s):** Jedi General Obi-Wan Kenobi, Clone Commander Cody.
- **Armor Markings:** Orange and white.
- **Regiment Ability:** Members of the 212th do not suffer strain when targeted by the [[Field Commander talent|Field Commander]] talent.

#### 284TH ASSAULT BATTALION

Source: [^3-4]

Led by the dour Klatooinian Jedi General Ragnar Tuvok, the 284th spurn defensive deployments in favor of aggressively attacking the enemy at close range.

- **Commanding Officer(s):** Jedi General Ragnar Tuvok, Clone Commander Rust.
- **Armor Markings:** Brown, pale green and white.
- **Regiment Ability:** Members of the 284th gain one rank in the [[Point Blank talent|Point Blank]] talent.

#### 327TH STAR CORPS

Source: [^4-5]

Under the command of Jedi General Aayla Secura, the 327th follow the principle of taking their time to make sure every shot counts.

- **Commanding Officer(s):** Jedi General Aayla Secura, Clone Commander Bly.
- **Armor Markings:** Yellow and white.
- **Regiment Ability:** Members of the 327th add the [[Item Qualities#Vicious (Passive)|Vicious]] 2 item quality to attacks made with blasters.

#### 332ND COMPANY

Source: [^4-5]

Formed from detachments of the 501st, the 332nd serves under Ahsoka Tano's command.

- **Commanding Officer(s):** Ahsoka Tano, Clone Commander Rex.
- **Armor Markings:** Orange, blue and white.
- **Regiment Ability:** Members of the 332nd gain an automatic [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result on checks when in medium range of an allied Jedi.

#### 349TH MECHANIZED REGIMENT

Source: [^4-5]

The Pantoran Jedi General Dag Rola oversees this regiment of hard-nosed walker pilots and stubborn artillerists.

- **Commanding Officer(s):** Jedi General Dag Rola, Clone Commander Trig.
- **Armor Markings:** Pale green and white.
- **Regiment Ability:** Members of the 349th upgrade [[Gunnery (Ag)|Gunnery]] checks once when piloting ground vehicles.

#### 442ND SIEGE BATTALION

Source: [^4-5]

When Republic High Command need something blown up, they call the 442nd and their fiery Iakaru Jedi General, Umber Tokk.

- **Commanding Officer(s):** Jedi General Umber Tokk, Clone Commander Ash.
- **Armor Markings:** Green and white.
- **Regiment Ability:** Members of the 442nd upgrade Gunnery checks once when targeting vehicles or buildings.

#### 492ND "HEAVY HITTERS" DIVISION

Source: [^5-6]

Known as the "Heavy Hitters", the 492nd are a division of some of the toughest clones in the Grand Army. True to their name, they are often the first into battle - and the last out of it.

- **Commanding Officer(s):** Jedi General Mekla Hath, Clone Commander Stak.
- **Armor Markings:** Red, black and white.
- **Regiment Ability:** Members of the 492nd gain one rank in the [[Toughened talent|Toughened]] talent.

#### 501ST LEGION

Source: [^5-6]

One of the most celebrated units in the Grand Army, the 501st are the clone troopers under the personal command of Republic hero Anakin Skywalker.

- **Commanding Officer(s):** Jedi General Anakin Skywalker, Clone Captain Rex.
- **Armor Markings:** Blue and white.
- **Regiment Ability:** Members of the 501st gain two [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results when targeted by the [[Coordinated Assault talent|Coordinated Assault]] talent instead of one [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result.

#### 556TH DESERT REGIMENT

Source: [^5-6]

The first unit to break the enemy lines at Geonosis, the 556th now serve as the Republic's first resort for desert warfare.

- **Commanding Officer(s):** Jedi General Uri Mado, Clone Commander Jet.
- **Armor Markings:** Brown and yellow.
- **Regiment Ability:** Members of the 556th add an automatic [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] and [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result to [[Resilience (Br)|Resilience]] and [[Survival (Cun)|Survival]] checks made in arid or desert environments.

#### 615TH ASSAULT LEGION

Source: [^5-6]

With two Jedi Generals in joint command, the 615th often favor a hard-hitting offensive as the best strategy in battle.

- **Commanding Officer(s):** Jedi Generals Tiplee and Tiplar, Clone Commander Doom.
- **Armor Markings:** Green, yellow and white.
- **Regiment Ability:** Members of the 615th gain one rank in the [[Suppressing Fire talent|Suppressing Fire]] talent.

#### 784TH ADVANCE RECON BATTALION

Source: [^6-7]

The Nikto Jedi Drok Rhom commands this elite recon unit as the eyes and ears of many major Republic operations.

- **Commanding Officer(s):** Jedi General Drok Rhom, Clone Commander Rakghoul.
- **Armor Markings:** Red and white.
- **Regiment Ability:** Members of the 784th gain one rank in the [[Stalker talent|Stalker]] talent.

#### 789TH "HUNTER" LEGION

Source: [^6-7]

Enemy scouts can be the death of any Republic operation; Givin Jedi General Saff Jelcho and his "Hunter" battalion are tasked with rooting out and eliminating Separatist recon forces.

- **Commanding Officer(s):** Jedi General Saff Jelcho, Clone Commander Styx.
- **Armor Markings:** Pale brown and white.
- **Regiment Ability:** Members of the 789th gain one rank in the [[Expert Tracker talent|Expert Tracker]] talent.

#### CORUSCANT GUARD

Source: [^6-7]

Charged with the duty of keeping the peace in the Republic capital, some argue the Coruscant Guard are more akin to police than soldiers.

- **Commanding Officer(s):** Clone Commander Fox, Clone Commander Thorn.
- **Armor Markings:** Red and white.
- **Regiment Ability:** Members of the Coruscant Guard gain one rank in the [[Coercion (Will)|Coercion]] skill. Minion groups add this to their group skills instead.
