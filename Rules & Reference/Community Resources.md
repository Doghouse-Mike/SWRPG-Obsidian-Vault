There are a myriad of online (and offline) communities for the Star Wars RPG by FFG including websites, Discords, and forums to visit and discuss the game. Below is a collection of these gathering places where players and GMs alike discuss the game, share content and creations, and organize resources.

## Websites

[SWRPGCommunity.com](https://swrpgcommunity.com) - A resource hub and community base for Star Wars RPG by FFG. The site features a forum, an archive of the old FFG forum, hundreds of player and GM resources, and guides for new players. Additionally, there is a plethora of [homebrew content](https://www.swrpgcommunity.com/gm-resources/homebrew) and [adventures](https://www.swrpgcommunity.com/gm-resources/adventures) to peruse through.

[MySWRPG.com](https://myswrpg.com/) - An alternative community blog and forum built around incredible homebrew content.

[Legends of the Galaxy](https://legendsofthegalaxy.com/) - An adventure league style online and game store based campaign hosting a variety of useful resources and information about the Star Wars RPG game.

[Star Wars RPG Homebrew Distillery](https://starwarsrpghomebrewdistillery.weebly.com) - A compendium of homebrew for Edge of the Empire, Age of Rebellion, and Force & Destiny.

[Star Wars Adversaries](http://swa.stoogoff.com) - A comprehensive list of NPC statblocks from all official FFG publications. Includes options to directly track wounds, strain and minion group size.\

## Discord

Discord is an online platform reminiscent of IRCs that allows users to chat in real time about popular subjects

[Star Wars RPG Discord](https://discord.gg/TmBHaN5) - The Star Wars RPG Discord Server is the largest discord community catering to Fantasy Flight Game's SW RPG series. Through the server, they organize games on diverse platforms, while providing a space to chat about anything from GM tips to rules questions. If you are an avid player looking for a new campaign amidst the Outer Rim or a grizzled GM looking to lead ragtag Rebellion heroes - this is the best place to instantly organize via chat.

[RPG Sessions](https://discord.com/invite/pfG65Tax57) - A discord built up and around RPGSessions.com, a web and discord-based dice roller and game manager for Star Wars RPG.\

## Forums

[Forum.SWRPGCommunity.com](https://forum.swrpgcommunity.com) - A replacement forum for the retired FFG forums. The forums feature many of the popular posts from the FFG forums, along with an active userbase of old veterans for the game. If you're looking for a place to talk about the game at a slower pace, this is it!

[FFG Forum Archive](https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/) - Constructed by EntropicDreamer, this is a complete archive of the original FFG forums.\

## Social Media Groups

[Star Wars RPG Community Facebook Group](https://www.facebook.com/groups/StarWarsFFG) - A facebook group catering to all things Star Wars RPG by FFG.
