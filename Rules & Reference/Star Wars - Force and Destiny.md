**Star Wars: Force and Destiny** was the third set of roleplaying rules designed by Fantasy Flight Games.

![SWFD showcase|220](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/9/90/SWFD_showcase.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

Play revolves around those sensitive and attuned to the ways of the Force, allowing play as Jedi or Sith-like characters. Character archetypes are: [[Category - Consular|Consulars]], [[Category - Guardian|Guardians]], [[Category - Mystic|Mystics]], [[Category - Seeker|Seekers]], [[Category - Sentinel|Sentinels]] and [[Category - Warrior|Warriors]]. Racial Templates included are: [[Cerean|Cereans]], [[Human|Humans]], [[Kel Dor]], [[Mirialan|Mirialans]], [[Nautolan|Nautolans]], [[Togruta|Togrutans]], [[Twi'lek|Twi'Lek]] and [[Zabrak|Zabraks]].

They released an array of [[Category - Force Power|force powers]] for use by these careers.
