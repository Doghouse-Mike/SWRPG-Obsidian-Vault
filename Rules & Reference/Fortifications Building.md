Building fortifications follows three simple steps: **Step 1: Select Template**, in which the PCs decide on the nature and scale of the fortification, **Step 2: Acquire Tools and Materials**, in which the PCs collect the tools and supplies they need, and **Step 3: Construct Fortification**, in which the PCs actually build the fortification.

## STEP 1: SELECT TEMPLATE

Source: [^0-1]

Before the PCs can construct a fortification, they must decide on its form. \[...\]

In addition to selecting the type of fortification, during this step the PCs should make the necessary decisions on the construction, including placement, size, shape, and any other considerations the GM feels relevant. At the GM's discretion, these decisions can impact the materials and quantities needed in **Step 2: Acquire Tools and Materials**.

## STEP 2: ACQUIRE TOOLS AND MATERIALS

Source: [^0-1]

Before the PCs can begin preparing a fortification they need the right tools and construction materials. The necessary supplies are included in the fortification's description, although the GM can allow PCs to make do with alternate materials if appropriate.

### TRENCHES

Source: [^1-2]

Trenches are a time-tested defensive measure used throughout the galaxy.

**Tools Needed:** [[Entrenching Tool|Entrenching tool]]. If the ground is any material other than soil, the GM might require the PCs to have heavier or more specialized tools, increase the difficulty of the check, or require additional time to complete the trench.\
**Occupancy:** Six characters.\
**Benefits:** A character in a trench can completely stay out of line of sight of characters at medium range or farther away, although if \[they\] do so, \[they\] cannot see these characters either. A trench grants an occupant cover with ranged defense 3.

### BUNKERS

Source: [^2-3]

Bunkers are relatively simple defensive buildings, which in some cases are largely or entirely underground.

**Tools Needed:** [[Tool Kit|Toolkit]], sheet metal. At the GM's discretion, characters can construct a bunker from raw materials, such as ferrocrete or any natural materials at hand, although this could increase the construction time significantly or diminish the benefits it provides.\
**Occupancy:** Four characters.\
**Benefits:** A bunker is treated as an immobile vehicle with an armor of 2, a hull trauma threshold of 8, a silhouette of 3, and a speed of 0 that cannot be increased in any way. Attacks that inflict system strain either inflict hull trauma instead or have no effect, at the GM's discretion based on the source of the attack. Characters inside can attack targets in the forward arc of the bunker with personal ranged weapons.

At the GM's discretion, the called shot use of the Aim maneuver and damage inflicted by the [[Item Qualities#Blast (Active)|Blast]] quality ignore the bunker and strike the occupants directly.

### BLINDS

Source: [^2-3]

Blinds are structures used to camouflage a position.

**Tools Needed:** [[Tool Kit|Toolkit]] or knife.\
**Occupancy:** Two characters.\
**Benefits:** A blind provides concealment equivalent to dense underbrush (+3) to each character within.

## STEP 3: CONSTRUCT FORTIFICATION

Source: [^0-1]

After acquiring the materials to make the fortification, the character must make the associated check, listed under "Check" on the table above. If the character succeeds, the fortification is fully functional and provides the benefits listed previously. If the character fails on the check, the group either does not complete the construction, or the fortification is so shoddily made that it provides negligible protection. The table below provides suggestions for ways the GM and players can spend dice symbols from the skill check. First, the crafter spends [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] and [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] results, then the GM spends [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] and [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] results.

The amount of time **Step 3: Construction** takes is determined by the estimate of working hours listed in the template under "Time." Every [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] result the character scores on the check beyond the first reduces this time by 2 hours (to a minimum of 1 hour). At the GM's discretion, other factors, such as assistance, can also affect the time required.

### SPENDING [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|ADVANTAGE]], [[Narrative Dice#Threat|THREAT]], [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|TRIUMPH]], AND [[Narrative Dice#Despair|DESPAIR]] RESULTS ON FORTIFICATIONS

Source: [^1-2]

| **Dice Symbols** | **Effect** |
| --- | --- |
| [[Narrative Dice#Advantage\|Advantage]] or [[Narrative Dice#Triumph\|Triumph]] | **Expanded Capacity:** Increase the fortification's occupancy by 1. |
| Two [[Narrative Dice#Advantage\|advantages]] or a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph\|triumph]] | **Additional Cover:** Increase the ranged defense that the fortification provides to occupants by 1 (this can only be selected once). |
| Three [[Narrative Dice#Advantage\|advantages]] or a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph\|triumph]] | **Additional Feature:** Add a useful feature to the fortification, such as a weapon turret or camouflage. |
| [[Narrative Dice#Triumph\|Triumph]] | **Reinforced Armor:** Increase the fortification's armor by 1, if applicable (this can only be selected once). |
| [[Narrative Dice#Threat\|Threat]] or [[Narrative Dice#Despair\|Despair]] | **Exhausting Effort:** Each character participating in the construction suffers 3 strain. |
| Two [[Narrative Dice#Threat\|threats]] or a [[Narrative Dice#Despair\|despair]] | **Inferior Protection:** Reduce the ranged defense that the fortification provides to occupants by 1 (this can only be selected once). |
| Three [[Narrative Dice#Threat\|threats]] or a [[Narrative Dice#Despair\|despair]] | **Vulnerability:** The fortification includes a critical flaw that a cunning enemy could use to easily neutralize it. |
| [[Narrative Dice#Despair\|Despair]] | **Limited Lifespan:** The fortification can protect against only a limited number of attacks before it is rendered useless, as determined by the GM. |
