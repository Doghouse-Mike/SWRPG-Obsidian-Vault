## SITH ALCHEMY

An INT-based custom skill for the Edge of the Empire roleplaying game, adapted heavily from the rules set forth in The Dark Side Sourcebook (2001, d20 system).

Requirements: Dark Side Force User, can only be trained at GM's discretion, never trained with XP

The ancient Sith used the dark side's alchemical arts to alter the molecular composition of living beings, creating mutants steeped in the dark side and bound to their will. They could also reshape inanimate matter, making it sharper or stronger, using this ability to forge incredible weapons and armor.

### USING ALCHEMY ON LIVING BEINGS

Source: [^1]

Utilizing this skill generates 10 conflict if used on a living being. Each check takes just a minute or two, and requires 10,000 credits of raw materials that are of rarity 9. Additional checks on living beings who have already been affected by alchemy only cost 500 credits. When performing additional checks on the same living being, upgrade the difficulty of each check beyond the first by the number of successful alchemy checks ever made against that subject. Any alchemist attempting to perform alchemy on themselves adds to the check. In addition, the process of alchemical alteration is incredibly painful and sometimes even fatal. All subjects undergoing alchemical changes must make a [[Resilience (Br)|Resilience]] check of the same difficulty rating as the Alchemy check. They take wounds per and strain per, ignoring soak. Wounds/strain given this way cannot be treated by normal medical procedures, the subject must be given time to adjust to the changes in their body. Thus, they lower the threshold of the subject rather than inflicting wounds/strain directly. They recover their threshold at a rate of one per day after the procedure. Finally, this process is taxing on the user as well as the subject. The user suffers 2 wound threshold damage (in the same way as a failed [[Resilience (Br)|resilience]] check would for a subject) for each check, recovering at the same rate a subject would.

The following are some examples of checks that can be taken on living beings:

#### SITH ALCHEMY ALTERATIONS

Source: [^2]

##### **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] Alchemy Checks:**

Add claws or fangs, granting the subject a natural weapon with Damage +2, Crit 4, [[Item Qualities#Pierce (Passive)|Pierce]] 1, [[Item Qualities#Vicious (Passive)|Vicious]] 1 – also add a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die to all social checks the subject makes, due to the horrifying nature of their new weapon.

##### **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] Alchemy Checks:**

Grant the ability to see in the dark, as well as the Light Sensitive ability of the Defel, forcing the subject to protect their eyes outside of dark environments.

Force the subject to add two [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice to all [[Discipline (Will)|Discipline]] checks when resisting a [[Leadership (Pr)|Leadership]] check made by the alchemist.

The subject grows an unnatural hard layer on top of their skin, granting +1 ranged/melee defense. As with claws/fangs, this looks repulsive and they take the appropriate [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die to social checks when their skin can be seen.

Increase the subject's silhouette by 1

##### **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Daunting difficulty]] Alchemy Checks:**

Permanently alter a characteristic of the subject, with the following possibilities:

- Add a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to all Brawn checks, [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] to all Agility, Intellect, and Presence checks.
- Add a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to all Brawn checks, [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] to all Willpower, Intellect, and Presence checks.
- Add a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to all Agility checks, [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] to all Brawn, Cunning, and Willpower checks.
- Add a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to all Agility checks, [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] to all Cunning, Intellect, and Presence checks.
- Add a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to all Cunning checks, [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] to all Agility, Intellect, and Willpower checks.
- Add a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to all Cunning checks, [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] to all Agility, Willpower, and Presence checks.
- Add a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to all Intellect checks, [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] to all Brawn or Cunning checks.
- Add a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to all Willpower checks, [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] to all Brawn or Cunning checks.
- Add a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to all Presence checks, [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] to all Intellect or Willpower checks.

Failing an alchemy check still requires the subject to roll [[Resilience (Br)|Resilience]]. It also wastes 10% of the alchemical ingredients per [[Narrative Dice#Failure|failure]] result without achieving the desired effect. In addition, all alchemy rolls that generate [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] or [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] results and can have the following negative effects:

#### SITH ALCHEMY [[Narrative Dice#Threat|THREAT]]/[[Narrative Dice#Despair|DESPAIR]]

Source: [^0-3]

- One [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] **Wracked with Pain:** The subject takes one additional wound/strain threshold damage, GM's choice.
- Two [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threats]] **This Will Take Some Getting Used To:** The subject takes to all relevant checks relating in any way to their changes for a number of days equal to the difficulty of the Alchemy check.
- Three [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threats]] **Horrible Disfigurement:** The subject grows some disgusting growths that betray their changes in an obvious manner. They receive to all social checks, unless they take extreme effort to find a way to hide their disfigurement. The GM is encouraged to make this difficult.
- One [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] **Something Went Wrong:** The subject must roll [[Resilience (Br)|Resilience]] twice, or take a critical injury with +30 to the roll, GM's choice.

### USING ALCHEMY ON INANIMATE OBJECTS

Source: [^0-3]

Using Alchemy on an inanimate object generates just one conflict. While less horrific than practicing on the living, using alchemy to enhance objects is a much more time consuming and expensive, and can only be done when crafting a new item. Use the rules from [[Weapon Crafting|crafting melee weapons]] and from for [[Armor Crafting|crafting armor]]. During Step 3: Construction, have the alchemist do an alchemy skill check. The following are the types of checks that can be made to enhance armor/weapons, along with difficulty and cost:

#### SITH ALCHEMY WEAPON/ARMOR UPGRADES

**Dark Side Stealth:** Applied to armor, this adds two [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice to all [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] or [[Sense]] checks by light side force users to detect that the user or the armor are touched by the force. Requires a [[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] check and 10,000 credits worth of materials.

**Dark Side Energy:** Applied to armor, this adds two [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] to all force power checks made by light side force users to affect the armor's wearer. Requires an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] Alchemy check** and 10,000 credits worth of materials.

**Damage Increase/Reduction:** Applied to armor, this adds 1/2 defense. Applied to melee weapons, this add 1/2 damage. Requires an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average or Daunting difficulty]] Alchemy check** and 10,000 or 25,000 credits worth of materials for the weaker or stronger effect, respectively.

Failing an alchemy check wastes 10% of the alchemical ingredients per [[Narrative Dice#Failure|failure]] result without achieving the desired effect. [[Narrative Dice#Threat|Threat]]/[[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] results may damage or completely destroy the original item as well.
