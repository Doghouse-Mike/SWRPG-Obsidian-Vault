#### BLASTER CANNONS

Source: [^0-4][^3-5][^7-7]

Known colloquially as "flash cannons," blaster cannons are heavy, crew-served version of the common personal blaster.

##### AUTO-BLASTER

Auto-blasters are rapid-fire variants of common blaster cannons.

#### ION WEAPONS

Source: [^1-8][^4-9][^7-7]

Ion weapons are anti-ship and anti-vehicle weapons designed to disable rather than destroy their targets.

#### LASER WEAPONS

Source: [^1-8][^4-9][^7-7]

These weapons use focused light beams combined with charged particles to produce long-range, accurate, and incredibly damaging blasts of energy.

##### LASER CANNON

The standard anti-ship weapons mounted to starships, laser cannons are found on nearly every class of ship from the smallest starfighter to the mightiest capital ship.

##### QUAD LASER CANNON

Source: [^8-10]

Quad lasers are dedicated anti-starfighter weapons consisting of four small-caliber laser cannons linked to a single firing mechanism and slaved to a sophisticated fire-control system.

##### TURBOLASER

Source: [^8-10]

Massive, heavy-caliber weapons with incredible energy requirements, turbolasers are the largest weapons mounted to starships.

#### TRACTOR BEAMS

Source: [^1-8][^4-9][^8-10]

Tractor beams are modified shield generators that project a beam of energy that can ensnare starships.

#### ORDNANCE

Source: [^1-8][^4-9][^8-10]

Ordnance provides vehicles and starships with a powerful one-use weapon that can punch through armor or even lock on to opponents and track them.

##### LIMITED AMMO AND STARFIGHTERS

Source: [^2-11][^5-12]

Sometimes starfighters or other small ships have weapons such as proton torpedoes or concussion missile launchers that have the [[Item Qualities#Limited Ammo (Passive)|Limited Ammo]] quality. Although normally [[Item Qualities#Limited Ammo (Passive)|Limited Ammo]] weapons can be reloaded with a maneuver once exhausted, starfighters generally carry one payload of torpedoes or missiles, and must be reloaded back at a base or carrier starship. They cannot be reloaded in mid-flight, and especially not in mid-combat!

##### CONCUSSION MISSILES AND GRENADES

Source: [^2-11][^5-12]

The more common and less powerful of the two types of shipboard ordnance, concussion missiles are found on all manner of spacecraft, from tiny starfighters to capital ships.

Additional concussion missiles cost 500 credits each and are restricted.

##### PROTON TORPEDOES

Source: [^2-11][^5-12]

Proton torpedoes are highly advanced anti-ship projectiles typically carried by starfighters and patrol boats.

Additional proton torpedoes cost 750 credits each and are restricted.
