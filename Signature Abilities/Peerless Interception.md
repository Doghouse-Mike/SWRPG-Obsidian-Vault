Peerless Interception ability tree

Jedi are famed across the galaxy for their preternatural feats of martial prowess.

> More information found on page 46 of **[[Category - Clone Wars End|Collapse of the Republic]]:** *An Era Sourcebook*.

### BASE ABILITY

Once per session as an out-of-turn incidental, the character may spend 2 Destiny Points to activate Peerless Interception. Until the end of the character's second turn after activating Peerless Interception, when they use [[Parry talent|Parry]] or [[Reflect talent|Reflect]] to reduce the damage of a hit, they reduce the damage by an additional amount equal to their current Force rating.

### UPGRADES

**Activate Quality Upgrade:** When the character uses [[Parry talent#Improved|Improved Parry]] or [[Reflect talent#Improved|Improved Reflect]] to inflict a hit on an attacker while Peerless Interception is active, they may choose to activate one [[Item Qualities|item quality]] of the attacker's weapon (if using [[Reflect talent|Reflect]]) or their own melee weapon (if using [[Parry talent|Parry]]). Apply the effects of the [[Item Qualities|item quality]] as if the character had spent the minimum necessary [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results to activate it as part of a combat check.

**Critical Counter Upgrade:** While Peerless Interception is active, when the character spends a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result to inflict a hit on their attacker using [[Parry talent#Improved|Improved Parry]] or [[Reflect talent#Improved|Improved Reflect]], they may choose to also inflict a Critical Injury.

**Destiny Upgrade:** Peerless Interception costs 1 fewer Destiny point to activate.

**Effortless Counter Upgrade:** Once per round when the character uses [[Parry talent|Parry]] or [[Reflect talent|Reflect]] while Peerless Interception is active, they may use [[Parry talent#Improved|Improved Parry]] or [[Reflect talent#Improved|Improved Reflect]] to inflict a hit on their attacker without spending a [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] or [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result.

**Increase Duration Upgrade:** Peerless Interception lasts for one additional round.

**Increase Frequency Upgrade:** The character may activate Peerless Interception one additional time per game session.

**Reduce Strain Upgrade:** While Peerless Interception is active, the character only suffers strain to use [[Parry talent|Parry]] or [[Reflect talent|Reflect]] the first time they use each talent during a round. Subsequent uses of [[Parry talent|Parry]] or [[Reflect talent|Reflect]] during the same round do not require the character to suffer additional strain.
