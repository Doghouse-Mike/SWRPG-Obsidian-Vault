Insightful Revelation ability tree

Many fringers may scoff at the highly educated individual, mocking \[them\] as an ivory tower academic with no experience in the real world.

> More information found on page 35 of **[[Category - Colonist|Far Horizons]]:** *A Sourcebook for Colonists*.

### BASE ABILITY

Once per game session, the character may spend two Destiny Points to perform the Insightful Revelation action. If \[they\] do so, \[they\] make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Education (Int)|Knowledge (Education) check]]**. If \[they\] succeed, \[they\] learn some valuable information that \[they\] did not previously possess pertaining to \[their\] current situation. What \[they\] learn is up to the GM, but it must be valuable to the player in overcoming \[their\] immediate encounter or situation, and the information cannot be obtainable by any other immediately available means.

When making the skill check to activate Insightful Revelation, the GM can add a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die or [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die for any situational effects that might cause the check to be more easy or difficult.

### UPGRADES

**Reduce Setback:** When making the skill check to activate Insightful Revelation, the character removes a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die.

**Add Boost:** When making the skill check to activate Insightful Revelation, the character adds a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die.

**Destiny:** To activate Insightful Revelation, the character only needs to spend one Destiny Point instead of the normal two.

**Reduce Difficulty:** The skill check to activate Insightful Revelation is **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]]** instead of **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard]]**.

**Increase Effect:** The character may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result generated on a successful Insightful Revelation check to gain one additional piece of information. The information must be as useful as the original information.

**Duration:** The character may perform the Insightful Revelation action one additional time per game session.

**Additional Skills:** When making the Insightful Revelation action, the character may replace [[Education (Int)|Knowledge (Education)]] with any other [[Category - Knowledge Skills|Knowledge skill]].
