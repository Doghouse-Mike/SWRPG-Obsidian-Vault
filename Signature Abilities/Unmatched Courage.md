Unmatched Courage ability tree

The Soldiers of the Rebellion face a terrifying foe: a vast Empire obsessed with their annihilation.

> More information found on page 39 of **[[Category - Soldier|Forged in Battle]]:** *A Sourcebook for Soldiers*.

### BASE ABILITY

Once per game session, as an out of turn incidental, the character may spend 2 Destiny Points to ignore the effects of all Critical Injuries for 2 rounds (\[they\] still suffer these Critical Injuries, even though \[they\] ignore their effects). When this effect ends at the end of the second round, \[they\] immediately suffer the effects of all Critical Injuries \[they\] are suffering as normal.

### UPGRADES

**Duration:** Unmatched Courage lasts for 2 additional rounds per Duration upgrade purchased.

**Increase Effect:** While Unmatched Courage is active, whenever the character would suffer strain, \[they\] may suffer that many wounds instead.

**Finish the Job:** While Unmatched Courage is active, the character adds +2 damage for each Critical Injury \[they\] are suffering to the first hit of each successful combat check \[they\] make.

**See it Through:** While Unmatched Courage is active, the character does not become incapacitated when \[their\] current wounds exceed \[their\] wound threshold. \[They\] still suffer a Critical Injury each time \[their\] current wounds increase to a value greater than \[their\] wound threshold.

**Survivor:** While Unmatched Courage is active, add a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die per Survivor upgrade purchased to checks to remove the character's Critical Injuries.

**Too Tough to Die:** When Unmatched Courage ends, the character may immediately make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Resilience (Br)|Resilience check]]**. If \[they\] succeed, \[they\] remove 1 Critical Injury \[they\] are suffering, plus 1 additional Critical Injury per [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result \[they\] spend on the check.

While Forged in Battle A Sourcebook for Soldiers reads "at the end of the third round", the Errata (Page 4) corrects this to "at the end of the second round".
