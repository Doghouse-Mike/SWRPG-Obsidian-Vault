Unmatched Destiny ability tree

The most powerful Mystics have an unmatched mastery of the Force.

> More information found on page 38 of **[[Category - Mystic|Unlimited Power]]:** *A Sourcebook for Mystics*.

### BASE ABILITY

Once per game session, after making a Force power check, the character may spend 2 Destiny Points to reroll up to 2 [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] dice. If the character gains Conflict as a result of the check, the Conflict they gain is doubled.

### UPGRADES

**Destiny Upgrade:** Unmatched Destiny requires one fewer Destiny Points to activate.

**Frequency Upgrade:** Unmatched Destiny can be used one additional time per session.

**Increased Number Upgrade:** Reroll up to one additional [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] die per Increase Number upgrade.

**Shared Destiny Upgrade:** Unmatched Destiny can be used after another character at short range makes a Force power check to allow them to reroll a number of their [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] dice up to the number that the character would be allowed to reroll.

**Unleashed Power:** For each two [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|light point]] results generated on the Force power check, add one automatic [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|light point]] to the results. For each two [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|dark point]] results generated on the Force power check, add one automatic [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|dark point]] to the results.
