Unmatched Tradecraft ability tree

The essence of tradecraft is that of perfect deception.

> More information found on page 35 of **[[Category - Spy|Cyphers and Masks]]:** *A Sourcebook for Spies*.

### BASE ABILITY

Once per session as an incidental, after the player rolls the dice for a [[Deception (Cun)|Deception]] check but before resolving the results of the check, \[they\] may spend two Destiny Points to remove one of the rolled [[Narrative Dice#Difficulty (Purple)|difficulty]] die from the pool. The results from that [[Narrative Dice#Difficulty (Purple)|difficulty]] die are ignored, and the check is then resolved as normal.

### UPGRADES

**Change Skill Upgrade:** Unmatched Tradecraft may be used with the [[Stealth (Ag)|Stealth]] skill instead of [[Deception (Cun)|Deception]].

**Change Skill Upgrade:** Unmatched Tradecraft may be used with the [[Skulduggery (Cun)|Skulduggery]] skill instead of [[Deception (Cun)|Deception]].

**Destiny Upgrade:** To activate Unmatched Tradecraft, the character needs to spend only one Destiny Point instead of the normal two.

**Frequency Upgrade:** Unmatched Tradecraft can be used one additional time per game session.

**Increase Effect Upgrade:** Unmatched Tradecraft may also be used on [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice.

**Increase Effect Upgrade:** Unmatched Tradecraft may also be used on [[Narrative Dice#Challenge (Red)|challenge]] dice.

**Increase Number Upgrade:** The number of dice removed through Unmatched Tradecraft increases by one per Increase Number upgrade.
