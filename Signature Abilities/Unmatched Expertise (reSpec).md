### BASIC POWER

Once per game session as a maneuver, the character may spend two Destiny Points to reduce the difficulty of all career skill checks they make by one, to a minimum of **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Easy difficulty]]**, until the end of the next round.

### UPGRADES

#### ACTIVATION

Unmatched Expertise becomes an incidental that may be triggered out of turn, instead of a maneuver.

#### DESTINY

To activate Unmatched Expertise, the character only needs to spend on Destiny Point instead of the normal two.

#### DURATION

The character increases the number of rounds that Unmatched Expertise is active by one round for each purchased Duration upgrade.

#### REDUCE DIFFICULTY

Unmatched Expertise reduces the difficulty of subsequent career skill checks by two instead of one.

#### REDUCE SETBACK

The character removes two [[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|setback dice]] from career skill checks while Unmatched Expertise is active.

#### REMOVE DIFFICULTY

Unmatched Expertise reduces the difficulty of subsequent career skill checks to a minimum of Simple instead of Easy.

#### SUPERIOR REDUCTION

Once per session, once the character has activated Unmatched Expertise, \[they may also use it to reduce the difficulty of one non-career skill check \[they\] make. This follows the same rules as using Unmatched Expertise to reduce the difficulty of career skills.
