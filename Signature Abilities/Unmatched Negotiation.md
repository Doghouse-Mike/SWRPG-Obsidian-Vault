Unmatched Negotiation ability tree

The best negotiators do not just see any conflict as a potential negotiated settlement waiting to be resolved; they also have the foresight to anticipate any potential pitfalls, mistakes, or points of contention before the negotiations even begin.

> More information found on page 35 of **[[Category - Consular|Disciples of Harmony]]:** *A Sourcebook for Consulars*.

### BASE ABILITY

Once per game session as an incidental, the character may spend 2 Destiny Points. For the rest of the round, whenever that character makes a [[Charm (Pr)|Charm]], [[Coercion (Will)|Coercion]], [[Deception (Cun)|Deception]], or [[Negotiation (Pr)|Negotiation]] check, the character downgrades the difficulty of the check the number of times needed to remove all [[Narrative Dice#Challenge (Red)|challenge]] dice from the pool, before rolling the dice.

### UPGRADES

**Add Triumph:** Whenever the character fails a [[Charm (Pr)|Charm]], [[Coercion (Will)|Coercion]], [[Deception (Cun)|Deception]], or [[Negotiation (Pr)|Negotiation]] check while Unmatched Negotiation is active, the character adds an automatic [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result to the results of the check (this does not add an additional [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] result to the check, however).

**Destiny:** To activate Unmatched Negotiation, the character only needs to spend 1 Destiny Point instead of the normal 2.

**Duration:** Unmatched Negotiation lasts for 1 additional round per Duration upgrade.

**Increase Effect:** While Unmatched Negotiation is active, allied characters engaged with the character who make [[Negotiation (Pr)|Negotiation]], [[Charm (Pr)|Charm]], [[Deception (Cun)|Deception]], or [[Coercion (Will)|Coercion]] checks may downgrade the difficulty of those checks once.

**Increase Range:** Increases the maximum range at which allies can be affected to medium range.

**Remove Setback:** When making a skill check affected by Unmatched Negotiation, the character removes a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die.
