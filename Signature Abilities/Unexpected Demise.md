Unexpected Demise ability tree

It's a dangerous and hostile galaxy, and only the most deadly survive to live another day.

> More information found on page 34 of **[[Category - Seeker|Savage Spirits]]:** *A Sourcebook for Seekers*.

### BASE ABILITY

Once per session as an action, the character may spend two Destiny Points and make **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Perception (Cun)|Perception check]]**. If successful, for the next two rounds the character may spend one maneuver to add one automatic [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result to \[their\] next combat check made in that turn. In addition, for the next two rounds, when the character inflicts a Critical Injury on a rival NPC, the target is immediately incapacitated in the same way as a minion NPC would be.

### UPGRADES

**Change Skill Upgrade:** To activate Unexpected Demise, the character can use [[Discipline (Will)|Discipline]] (instead of [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] as normal) to ready the mind and calmly perform the perfect strike.

**Destiny Upgrade:** To activate Unexpected Demise, the character only needs to spend 1 Destiny Point instead of the normal 2.

**Duration Upgrade:** Unexpected Demise lasts for 1 additional round per Duration Upgrade.

**Reduce Difficulty Upgrade:** The skill check difficulty to activate Unexpected Demise is **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average]]** instead of **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard]]**. The Game Master may still add a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] or [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die as the situation warrants.

**Reduce Setback Upgrade:** When making a combat check while Unexpected Demise is active, the character removes a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die.
