Calling In A Favor ability tree

Even at the rim of the galaxy, no person is truly an island, doubly so for Colonists. They have made their way to their new life somehow, making contacts and allies, and hopefully earning a few favors along the way. In moments of great need and greater opportunity, a shrewd (or desperate) Colonist will leverage debts owed to accomplish their goals.

### BASIC POWER

Once per session, may spend 2 Destiny Points and make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Charm (Pr)|Charm check]]**. If successful, the character establishes a contact who owes them a favor, and will provide otherwise unobtainable information to the character. This information must be immediately relevant to the character's current challenge, but sharing it must not put the contact in immediate danger. For the remainder of the session, the character may suffer 2 strain to add a [[Narrative Dice#Success|success result]] to a check directly related to the information provided by the favor.

### UPGRADES

#### CHANGE OF SKILL: NEGOTIATION

When activating Calling In A Favor, the character may make a [[Negotiation (Pr)|Negotiation]] check instead of a [[Charm (Pr)|Charm]] check.

#### CHANGE OF SKILL: KNOWLEDGE (UNDERWORLD) OR STREETWISE

When activating Calling In A Favor, the character may make a [[Underworld (Int)|Knowledge (Underworld)]] or [[Streetwise (Cun)|Streetwise]] check instead of a [[Charm (Pr)|Charm]] check.

#### DESTINY

To activate Calling In A Favor, the character needs to spend only 1 Destiny Point instead of 2.

#### FAVOR FOR GOODS

The favor the character calls in may now come in the form of legal goods instead of information. The credit cost of the goods may not exceed 200 times the character's Presence, and the rarity may not be higher than a number equal to the character's Presence. The delivery or appearance of the equipment is up to GM discretion, but should directly aid the character in their current challenge. For the remainder of the session, the character may suffer 2 strain to add a [[Narrative Dice#Success|success result]] to a check to which the provided goods are relevant.

#### FAVOR FOR SERVICES

The favor the character calls in may now come in the form of legal services instead of information. The nature and scope of the service is up to the Game Master, but it should not be obviously suicidal or lead to immediate pursuit by authorities. The service should be able to be performed during this session, and should directly aid the character in their current challenge. For the remainder of the session, the character may suffer 2 strain to add a [[Narrative Dice#Success|success result]] to a check to which the provided services are relevant.

#### GREATER FAVOR

After activating Calling In A Favor, the character may instead suffer 2 strain to add a [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] and an [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage result]] to a check related to the favor.

#### REDUCE DIFFICULTY

The character reduces the difficulty of the skill check to activate Calling In A Favor to **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]]**.

#### SUPREME FAVOR

The character may spend three [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage results]] or a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph result]] from the check to activate Calling In A Favor to remove the restriction from the favor provided. If the favor is information, that information may put the contact in immediate peril, and be even more valuable or sought-after. If the favor is goods, the credit cost limit is doubled, and the rarity value limit is increased by 3, and the good may be restricted. If the favor is a service, the service may be illegal and/or incredibly dangerous.
