### SKILL ISSUE - CYBERNETICS

*Special Modifications* has a sidebar on page 28 suggests that, at the GM's and player's discretion, the Cybernetics custom skill could be introduced into the game.

The Cyber Tech has been designed to use the base game skills but should a player and GM want to implement the Cybernetics skill, the Cyber Tech should gain it is an additional career skill.

The Cybernetics skill is used for building, modifying, and installing cybernetic enhancements and replacements, as well as other appropriate checks at the GM's discretion. Talents that apply to [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics]] or [[Medicine (Int)|Medicine]] checks might apply to Cybernetics at the GM's discretion.

If introduced, we recommend that the following talents from this tree be adjusted and errated as so:

**[[Engineered Redundancies talent|Engineered Redundancies]]:** Gain the ability to use emergency repair patches to heal. Additionally, can be healed with Cybernetics checks.

**[[Eye For Detail talent|Eye for Detail]]:** After making a [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics]], [[Computers (Int)|Computers]], or Cybernetics check, may suffer strain up to ranks in Eye for Detail to convert that many [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] results to [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results.
