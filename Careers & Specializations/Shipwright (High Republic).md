Most shipwrights are at home behind a variety of vessels, from all corners of the galaxy.

Shipwrights and ship designers come from a variety of backgrounds. Some come from a long line of shipwrights, such as Ania Sinear, Director of the midsize Sienar Fleet Systems. Others work their way up inside massive corporations, such as Roan Cassius, current Head of Development for Corellian Engineering Corporations. Others still are eccentric trailblazers, independent designers working on their own ship designs such as Dee Ossin and Jaa Randar the architects of the *Mynevik*-class patrol boat.

> More information is available in **[[Category - High Republic|For Light and Life]]:** *An Era Sourcebook* (page 33).
