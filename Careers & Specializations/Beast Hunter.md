Though some true pacifists may take great exception to the notion. Beast Hunters tend to believe they are performing a service to the people of the galaxy. They generally seek out truly dangerous and deadly creatures to fight and tame or kill. thus making more areas in the galaxy safe for sentient habitation.

Beast Hunters tend to knowhow to get where their prey is, how to stay alive once they get there., and all about whatever it is they are there to do battle with.

> More information is available in **[[Category - High Republic|For Light and Life]]:** *An Era Sourcebook* (page 35).
