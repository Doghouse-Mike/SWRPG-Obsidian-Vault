Jedi Archivists are dedicated to the pursuit and safeguarding of knowledge throughout the galaxy. Archivists follow the force, allowing it to guide them to their area of speciality, whether that is at Jedi Temple libraries across the galaxy, or travelling with Jedi Explorers to visit new and long forgotten worlds.

The peace of the High Republic era has allowed a number of Jedi to step awayfrom the day to day work of the Order and focus on the pursuit of knowledge for the good of the whole galaxy.
