The RDC can be mobilised to respond to a variety of large crises all across the galaxy and often at only a moment’s notice. Whether piloting large cruisers, leading starfighter wing,s or delivering aid and equipment into a hot zone, RDC Navigators are vital to the success of operations.

Like all RDC personnel, RDC Navigators come from a variety of backgrounds. Racing pilots and military patrol crews might be assigned missions such as flying support for transports delivering aid into a hostile area. Incom’s sleek Z-28 *Skywings* are the most common RDC starfighters. Many RDC Navigators with capital ship experience also operate aboard the Republic’s *Emissary* and *Pacifier*-class cruisers, working to direct the large ships to the heart of a crisis, as quickly as possible.
