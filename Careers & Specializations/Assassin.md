Assassin's talent tree

Whereas most Bounty Hunters focus primarily on the capture of quarry, those who specialize as Assassins are generally tasked with the straightforward proposition of terminating their targets.

> More information is available in **[[Category - On the Fringe|*Star Wars* Edge of the Empire]]** Roleplaying Game: *Core Rulebook* (page 57).
