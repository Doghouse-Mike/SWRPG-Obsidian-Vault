#### GENESYS CONVERSION

The Star Wars Roleplaying game was released in 2013 in form of the *Edge of the Empire Core Rulebook*. Many years later, elements of the Star Wars RPG were revised and improved for the generic setting based *Genesys Core Rulebook*.

One element of criticism in the Star Wars RPG was the vehicle. Genesys did improve these rules and most conversions these days use that as a basis. The one we recommend is the *Order 66 Genesys Conversion Rules* from the *Order 66 Podcast*.

As such, some talents that are a part of the reSpecialized project need to be specially adapted for use with the Genesys system.

Any talents that require an errata when using rule sets that implement the Genesys rules are marked with a bar and circle logo.

```
Full Stop: When piloting a ship or vehicle and performing the Decelerate maneuver to reduce speed to 0, the vehicle does not suffer any system strain for the sudden reduction of speed. The vehicle must decelerate to 0.
```

```
Tricky Target: When being targeted by an attack from a starship or Vehicle 1 Silhouette or more larger than the piloted vehicle, increase the difficulty of the attack once.
```
