Republic Diplomats must be able to quickly adapt to a variety of situations and cultures at a moment’s notice.

Republic Diplomats come to their profession in a variety of ways. Altruistic Diplomats believe it is their duty and responsibility to serve the Republic and provide a shining example. More selfish Diplomats seek the fame, power and wealth often associated with their position. Power- hungry Diplomats take advantage of the system and wind up helping others only to help themselves. The halls of power are calling. How the Diplomats answers can make all the difference.

> More information is available in **[[Category - High Republic|For Light and Life]]:** *An Era Sourcebook* (page 37).
