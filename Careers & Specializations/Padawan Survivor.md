Padawan Survivor's talent tree

Order 66 effectively erased the Jedi from existence, but a small handful of Padawans across the stars managed to escape.

> More information is available in **[[Category - Early Rebellion|Dawn of Rebellion]]:** *An Era Sourcebook* (page 102).

When characters purchase this specialization, they automatically receive a Force rating of 1. If such characters already have a Force rating of 1 or higher, it does not improve when they purchase the Padawan Survivor specialization.
