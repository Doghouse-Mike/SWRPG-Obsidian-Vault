A Jedi’s learning is never fully complete, even venerable Jedi Masters can still learn more about their connection to the force. For some Jedi, this can involve taking a temporary step away from the dictates of the Jedi Council.

Jedi Wayseekers operate independently from the Jedi Council, following their own instincts and connections to the Force. This independent sabbatical can take many forms, Wayseekers have been known to undergo solitary meditation on mountaintops or assist revolutionaries against planetary tyrants. In one memorable case, a Jedi Wayseeker became a minor singing sensation on Alderaan. Becoming a Wayseeker is not a choice to be made lightly. All Jedi have been deeply connected with the Jedi Council since they were younglings and the isolation from the Order and Council can be very difficult. However certain Jedi feel that stepping away from the council’s purview and exploring the Force independently is the only way to truly understand their place in the galaxy
