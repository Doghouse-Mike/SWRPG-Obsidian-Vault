Force-Sensitive Emergent's talent tree

Within the ranks of Rebel organizations, Force-sensitives are safe from the Empire and have access to one of the few remaining societies in the galaxy that still openly acknowledges and encourages an understanding of the light side of the Force.

> More information is available in **[[Category - Rebel Ranks|*Star Wars* Age of Rebellion]]** Roleplaying Game: *Core Rulebook* (page 292).

**Gain Force Rating 1**
