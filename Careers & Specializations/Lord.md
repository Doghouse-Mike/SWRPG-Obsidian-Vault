A Sith Lord is a prestigious title acquired after a Sith has committed important deeds for the Empire. The rank itself commands respect, but also indicates the experiences the Force user has made and the powers and potential acquired along the way. Sith Lords are also privy to more secrets of their Order, including scriptures containing many secrets and powerful abilities the dark side of the Force provides.

> More information is available in **[[Category - Old Republic|The Old Republic]]:** *An Era Sourcebook* (page 13).
