Force-Sensitive Outcast's talent tree

The Jedi and the Sith are not the sole masters of the Force.

> More information is available in **[[Category - Clone Wars Begin|Rise of the Separatists]]:** *An Era Sourcebook* (page 28).

**A character who purchases this specialization gains a Force rating of 1.** If they already had a Force rating of 1 or higher, it does not increase.
