Every RDC operation depends upon effective leadership from its officers. Officers who need to be able to respond calmly and swiftly at the first sign of trouble.

RDC Officers are drawn from volunteers from hundreds of different planetary defence forces across the galaxy. From Outland Regions Security Force Captains, to members of Naboo’s Royal Fighter Corp every RDC officer brings unique perspectives, viewpoints and experiences to a crisis.

> More information is available in **[[Category - High Republic|For Light and Life]]:** *An Era Sourcebook* (page 21).
