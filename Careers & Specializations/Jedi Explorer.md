Jedi Explorers fall under the overview of the Jedi Exploration Corp and have a long history of uncovering ancient civilisations and finding new ones amongst the wilderness of the stars.

Originally founded to survey and chart the myriad stars and planets in the galaxy, the Exploration Corps is made up of Jedi Explorers who use their skills in navigation, surveying, and survival to achieve this goal. With bases on nearly every Ordnance/Regional Depot throughout the galaxy, Jedi Explorers make use of a small fleet of second-hand survey vessels.
