Standard Peacekeeper groups make up the largest portion of the RDC’s personnel. These groups handle the direct on-the-ground work of the RDC.

RDC Peacekeepers could often be charged with delivering supplies and aid to disaster areas, or worlds suffering under large pirate or bandit raids. This can often put Peacekeepers directly in the line of fire from a number of different groups. Luckily RDC volunteer Peacekeepers come from a variety of backgrounds, from retired Judicials to CorSec officers, from Twi’lek brush scouts to Wookiee doctors, This means that RDC Peacekeepers are well-trained and experienced long before they hit the crisis zone, ensuring the very best response that the Republic’s volunteer worlds can muster.
