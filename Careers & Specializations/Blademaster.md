Sith Blademasters are warriors of the order. While they specialize in the lightsaber, their training encompasses most common weaponry as well, making them formidable combatants on most battlefields. On the front lines, they wear heavy armor and lay waste to Republic troops as they command the Empire’s military and lead it into battle. The scars they acquire on their way are signs of their dedication, and the pain they experience only serves to fuel their hate and strength. Many Blademasters are found later in life having limbs or other parts of their body replaced with cybernetics as a result of the damages they incurred.
