Retired Clone Trooper's talent tree

Literally bred for battle, these warriors were the bulk of the Republic's fighting force in the Clone Wars.

> More information is available in **[[Category - Early Rebellion|Dawn of Rebellion]]:** *An Era Sourcebook* (page 106).

**Retired Clone Trooper Bonus Career Skills:** [[Discipline (Will)|Discipline]], [[Warfare (Int)|Knowledge (Warfare)]], [[Ranged - Heavy (Ag)|Ranged (Heavy)]], [[Ranged - Light (Ag)|Ranged (Light)]], [[Resilience (Br)|Resilience]], [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]]

Player Characters who take this universal specialization must be human males, given that they were once clone troopers, but gain a wider set of career skills from their Kaminoan training.
