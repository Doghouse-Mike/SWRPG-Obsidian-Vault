For a trader, every new planet unlocked by the High Republic’s expansionary efforts is a new opportunity for profit and every newly connected culture is a potential customer base.

Most traders know how to build connections from Coruscant to the frontier edges of the Outer Rim.

Traders are exceptionally adept at commerce, naturally; they know how to find out what is needed, and can usually find some way of bypassing the kind of restrictions that often stymie larger trading conglomerates. At the same time, traders can get the most out of a transaction, as well as talk themselves in or out of almost anything.

> More information is available in **[[Category - High Republic|For Light and Life]]:** *An Era Sourcebook* (page 31).
