A popular alcoholic drink from Corellia, distilled anywhere from three to twenty times and aged for several years in wooden casks.

Whyren's Reserve, an expensive label aged for up to two decades, is considered the finest Corellian whiskey available and often traded on the black market.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Corellian_whiskey) (and this [article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Whyren%27s_Reserve) for Whyren's Reserve).
