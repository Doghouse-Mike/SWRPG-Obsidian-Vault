Produced by LaKiave Amalgamated Foodstuffs, Crunchies are a breakfast cereal popular among humans.

Blue and brown in colour, these sugary pebbleshaped, spherical treats are usually served in bowls of blue milk. The mascot for Crunchies is CRUNCH-0, the cereal loving droid. Though during the Clone Wars, CRUNCH-0 was briefly retired in favour of the Clone Commander Crunch.
