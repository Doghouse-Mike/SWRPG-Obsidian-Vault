The CZ-88 model heavy loader produced by Czerka is a flexible piece of equipment used for everything from moving freight to collecting salvage to replacing damaged hull plating on starships.

## CZ-88 HEAVY LOADER ARM

Source: [^0-1]

![Encumbrance 30 Price 1,500 Rarity 3|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/4/46/Profile_CZ-88_Heavy_Loader_Arm.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 30 Price 1,500 Rarity 3

A heavy loader arm includes a monotask droid brain that can perform checks to heft objects; it has an [[Athletics (Br)|Athletics]] skill of 1 and a Brawn of 5 (if unassisted, it rolls one [[Narrative Dice#Proficiency|proficiency]] and four [[Narrative Dice#Ability|ability]] dice for [[Athletics (Br)|Athletics]] checks).

Sometimes, salvagers install loader arms directly onto starships or other vehicles for easy retrieval of scrap metal and other components, using the arm as a low-cost alternative to a tractor beam projector. A CZ-88 heavy loader arm can be installed onto a vehicle of silhouette 3 or larger as an attachment, and uses one hard point. The occupants of the craft can use it to perform [[Athletics (Br)|Athletics]] checks to lift objects into or out of the vessel (and other checks, at the GM's discretion) without disembarking.
