## CONCEALED ESCAPE KIT

Source: [^1][^2]

![Encumbrance – Price 450 Rarity 7|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/d/d9/Profile_Concealed_Escape_Kit.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance – Price 450 Rarity 7

A concealed escape kit upgrades the ability of all [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics]] and [[Skulduggery (Cun)|Skulduggery]] checks made to escape confinement once. The laser cutter works for a single round before its energy cell burns out, usually just enough time to destroy a mechanical lock or cut a metal bar. The monoknife has the following profile: ([[Melee (Br)|Melee]]; Damage +1; Critical 3; Range \[Engaged\]; [[Item Qualities#Pierce (Passive)|Pierce]] 1). Characters upgrade the difficulty of checks to detect a concealed escape kit twice.
