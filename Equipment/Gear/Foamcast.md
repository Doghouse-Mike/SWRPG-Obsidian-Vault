Foamcast is the commonly used term for a type of quick-setting aerosol foam found in construction and building maintenance.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Foamcast).

## FOAMCAST

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 1 Price 25 Rarity 2|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/3/3a/Profile_Foamcast.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 1 Price 25 Rarity 2

Foamcast adds a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to checks to repair structures and vehicle hulls. The GM may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] on such checks to indicate the foamcast dispenser can is depleted. A can of foamcast can be made into an improvised explosive with a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics check]]**. When used in this way, a can of foamcast has the following profile: [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics]]; Damage 4; Critical 5; Range (Short); [[Item Qualities#Blast (Active)|Blast]] 4, [[Item Qualities#Ensnare (Active)|Ensnare]] 4, [[Item Qualities#Inferior (Passive)|Inferior]], [[Item Qualities#Prepare (Passive)|Prepare]] 2. A [[Narrative Dice#Failure|failure]] result on the mechanics check means the can ruptures and covers the user in foamcast.
