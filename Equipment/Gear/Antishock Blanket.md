## ANTISHOCK BLANKET

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 2 Price 250 Rarity 2|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/1/10/Profile_Antishock_Blanket.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 2 Price 250 Rarity 2

When a character uses an antishock blanket to help heal a Critical Injury, the character may reduce the difficulty of the [[Medicine (Int)|Medicine]] check made to heal the injury by one, to a minimum of [[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|**Easy**]].[^2]
