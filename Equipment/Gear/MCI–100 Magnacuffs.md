Produced by Locris Syndicated Securities, MCI-100 magnacuffs are a type of heavy-duty binders designed to restrain unusually powerful or dangerous individuals.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MagnaCuffs).

## MCI–100 MAGNACUFFS

Source: [^0-1][^2]

A character may only escape magnacuffs with a successful **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Formidable difficulty]] [[Coordination (Ag)|Coordination check]]** (the bindings on the cuffs are too strong to break with brute force).[^0-1][^3]
