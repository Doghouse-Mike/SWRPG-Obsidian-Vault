Useful for those needing to make a speedy getaway, a remote activation controller (also known as a beckon call) is a small, handheld device that can be worn on the wrist or stowed in a pocket.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Remote_DVI_Activator).

## REMOTE DVI ACTIVATOR (LONG RANGE)

Source: [^0-1][^1-2]

![Encumbrance – Price 1,500 Rarity 7|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/f/f7/Profile_Remote_DVI_Activator_%28Long_Range%29.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance – Price 1,500 Rarity 7

Inexpensive models simply activate the vehicle or ship's engines from no more than five kilometers away and run simple preflight checks, allowing the pilot to take off right away. More advanced beckon calls can summon a vehicle or starship, though the autopilot is only capable of performing simple maneuvers, which can be complicated by weather conditions and other obstacles. Some models can be used to remotely pilot a vehicle or starship via slave circuit, though this requires a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Daunting difficulty]] [[Piloting - Space (Ag)|Piloting]] [[Piloting - Planetary (Ag)|check]]**.[^0-1][^3]

If purchasing the short range variant, the price is 300 credits and the rarity is 4.[^0-1][^1-2]
