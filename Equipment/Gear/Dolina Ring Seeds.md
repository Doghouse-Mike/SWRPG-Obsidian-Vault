## DOLINA RING SEEDS

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance - Price 18,000 Rarity 10|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/f/fd/Profile_Dolina_Ring_Seeds.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance - Price 18,000 Rarity 10

A Force-sensitive character who possesses a dolina ring seed may perform one free Move maneuver during each of \[their\] turns. This Move maneuver may be in addition to any other maneuvers performed this turn and can allow the character to perform three maneuvers in a turn. (The user also ignores the ring seed's weight.)[^0-2]
