Primarily intended for hunters and survivalists, an environmental cocoon provides a secure space in an otherwise hostile locale.

## CZERKA BC-7 ENVIRONMENTAL COCOON

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 3 Price 2,000 Rarity 4|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/e/e2/Profile_Czerka_BC-7_Environmental_Cocoon.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 3 Price 2,000 Rarity 4

The cocoon reduces the difficulty of [[Resilience (Br)|Resilience]] checks made to resist effects of extreme cold or heat by three. It also removes up to three [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice added to checks due to extreme heat or cold, for as long as the user remains within it.[^0-2]
