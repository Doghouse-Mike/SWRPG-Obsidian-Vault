## DIOXIS GAS

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 0 Price (R) 100 Rarity 6|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/f/f5/Profile_Dioxis_Gas.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 0 Price (R) 100 Rarity 6

A single dose has a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]]**, while two or more doses combined have a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Daunting difficulty]]**. The poison inflicts 5 wounds if the target fails a check at the relevant difficulty (this ignores soak). In addition, each [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] result generated inflicts 2 strain on the target (this ignores soak), who becomes overwhelmed by the nausea and muscle spasms caused by the choking gas. The gas lingers in the air for up to 3 rounds unless dissipated by high winds.
