Good surveillance detectors identify active devices that transmit on any electromagnetic frequency, as well as recognizing the power supplies required to keep such bugs functional.

A surveillance detector adds two [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] dice to any [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] or [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]] checks to attempt to identify the presence of a bug within short range of the user.
