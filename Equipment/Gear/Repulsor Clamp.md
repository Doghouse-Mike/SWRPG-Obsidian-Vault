This pneumatic clamp, attached to a repulsor engine, allows Technicians to hold heavy objects in the air as they work on repairs.

## REPULSOR CLAMP

Source: [^0-1]

![Encumbrance 3 Price 250 Rarity 1|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/a/a9/Profile_Repulsor_Clamp.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 3 Price 250 Rarity 1

A repulsor clamp can hold a single object of 10 encumbrance or less aloft. Further, having a repulsor clamp on hand removes a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die from checks to repair hull trauma and Critical Hits on starships and vehicles.
