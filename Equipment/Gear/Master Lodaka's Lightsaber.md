## MASTER LODAKA'S LIGHTSABER

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance - Price (R) 20,000 Rarity 10|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/c/c2/Profile_Master_Lodaka%27s_Lightsaber.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance - Price (R) 20,000 Rarity 10

Master Lodaka's Lightsaber is a weapon with the following profile: ([[Lightsaber (Br)|Lightsaber]]; Damage 10; Critical 1; Range \[Engaged\]; [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] 1, [[Item Qualities#Sunder (Active)|Sunder]], [[Item Qualities#Vicious (Passive)|Vicious]] 2).[^0-2]

Master Lodaka's Lightsaber has an encumbrance of 1 and zero hard points. Attacks made with this lightsaber ignore the [[Item Qualities#Cortosis (Passive)|Cortosis]] quality. It also cannot be shut down due to contact with refined cortosis.[^0-2]
