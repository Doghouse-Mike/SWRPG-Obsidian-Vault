## ADAMITE SIGNET RING

Source: [^1]

Possessing a signet ring signifies to anyone on Weik that the character is one of the Paladins of the Adamite Tower. Possessing a signet ring under false pretenses earns the lasting enmity of all Paladins and their allies.[^2]
