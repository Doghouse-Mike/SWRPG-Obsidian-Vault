Cybernetic eyes were designed to restore sight to beings who have been blind since birth or have lost their sight due to disease or accident.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cybernetic_eyes_Mod_III).

**Models Include:** Athakam MedTech SharpEyes, Athakam MedTech Cybernetic Eyes Mod III.
