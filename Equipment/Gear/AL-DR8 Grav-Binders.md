Designed to restrain the particularly slippery individuals, grav-binders use microrepulsor technology to artificially apply the effects of extreme gravity to a captive.

## AL-DR8 GRAV-BINDERS

Source: [^1]

> [!quote] Image text

Breaking free from grav-binders requires a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Formidable difficulty]] [[Athletics (Br)|Athletics check]]** (due to the restrictive effects of the artificially raised gravity on the whole body, a character cannot slip free of grav-binders using flexibility alone).[^0-2]
