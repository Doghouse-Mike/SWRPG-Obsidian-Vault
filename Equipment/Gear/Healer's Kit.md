Those who live on the fringes of society, whether by choice or by circumstance, tend to be more hardy and skillful than the typical galactic citizen.

## HEALER'S KIT

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 2 Price – Rarity –|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/9/94/Profile_Healer%27s_Kit.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 2 Price – Rarity –

Rather than being purchased, a character must fashion \[their\] own contained and equip the empty pack with native medicinal items through a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Survival (Cun)|Survival check]]**. A healer's kit allows the character to use the [[Medicine (Int)|Medicine]] skill to heal \[themself\] or other individuals without penalty. The GM may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result on a Medicine check when a character is using a healer's kit to indicate that some or all of the medicinal items have been exhausted in the attempt. In such a case, the healer's kit cannot be used again until the character spends some time restocking and makes an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Survival (Cun)|Survival check]]**.
