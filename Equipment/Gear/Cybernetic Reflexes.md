A series of specialized implants designed to stimulate and help regulate the sympathetic nervous system of the user, cybernetic reflexes can greatly increase an individual's reaction time to outside stimuli and threats.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cybernetic_reflexes).

**Models Include:** BioTech Industries Rapid 12 Cyber-Reflex System.
