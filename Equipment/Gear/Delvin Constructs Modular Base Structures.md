Delvin Constructs has made a name for itself designing and building cheap and affordable prefabricated buildings. They allow for a modular configuration of different structures to be easily transported by most freighters and deposited to suit the needs of the user. Environmental adaptation kits are also available for when the prefabs are used in hostile environments.

Setting up a single module requires an Easy (1 difficulty) Athletics check and roughly 2 hours.

## Com C-52 Modular Command Center

This module can be with up to 20 people, featuring a large room for a command and control space with holotables and workstations. There are also additional smaller rooms for comms stations and offices.

| Price | Rarity |
| --- | --- |
| 1750 | 2 |

Add 1 boost dice to occupants' Computers, Leadership and Vigilance checks.

## BKR B-70 Modular Reinforced Bunker

This module is made from reinforced armorplast-faced ferrocrete. It can be configured as a sealed bunker, open-topped fortification or weapon emplacement. It can hold about 6 average-sized humanoids or one large piece of equipment.

| Price | Rarity |
| --- | --- |
| 4900 | 3 |

Add 2 ranged defense to any individual or material inside.

## Fac L-120 Modular Motor Pool

This module is little more than a large, low-ceilinged open space with a large door taking up one long wall.

| Price | Rarity |
| --- | --- |
| 1200 | 2 |

This area can hold a number of land vehicles with silhouettes totaling no more than 6.

## HNG F-104 Modular Hangar

This module is virtually the same as the L-120, but it is equipped with some more specialised tools for maintaining the near-priceless military vehicles.

| Price | Rarity |
| --- | --- |
| 2200 | 2 |

This area can hold a number of airspeeders or starfighters with silhouettes totaling no more than 12.

## HNG F-304 Modular Hangar/Motor Pool

This module is virtually the same as the L-120 or F-304, but it is equipped with some more specialised tools for maintaining bigger vehicles.

| Price | Rarity |
| --- | --- |
| 4200 | 2 |

This area can hold a number of airspeeders or starships with silhouettes totaling no more than 12, no single vehicle/starship larger than silhouette 4.

Includes a full set of Mechanics tools and an Oil Bath fo droids.

When used to repair starships or vehicles, repair +1 hull trauma and +2 system strain.

Multiple modules of this unit may be combined. Three of these units can be combined to increase the silhouette limit by 10 per unit (biggest vehicle can still only be silhouette 4).

## MilBar F-221 Modular Barracks

This module is built to house an entire platoon of troopers as well as the armoury of a size required for their equipment. There are also common areas and some smaller rooms for officer private suites. It is sealed against chemical and biological agents.

| 2000 | 3 |
