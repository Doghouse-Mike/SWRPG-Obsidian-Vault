A tool of saboteurs and infiltrators, the explosives belt is a small and convenient concealed demolitions kit.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Explosives_belt).

## EXPLOSIVES BELT

Source: [^1][^2]

![Encumbrance 1 Price (R) 450 Rarity 7|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/7/7d/Profile_Explosives_Belt.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 1 Price (R) 450 Rarity 7

Setting the explosive charge hidden in the belt requires an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics check]]**. The charge detonates with the following profile: (Damage 15; Critical 3; Range \[Engaged\]; [[Item Qualities#Blast (Active)|Blast]] 5, [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] 1, [[Item Qualities#Limited Ammo (Passive)|Limited Ammo]] 1). Characters upgrade the difficulty of checks to detect the belt's true nature twice.
