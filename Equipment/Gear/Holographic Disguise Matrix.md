## HOLOGRAPHIC DISGUISE MATRIX

Source: [^0-1]

![Encumbrance 1 Price (R) 10,000 Rarity 9|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/d/de/Profile_Holographic_Disguise_Matrix.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 1 Price (R) 10,000 Rarity 9

When using a holographic disguise matrix, the character can effectively impersonate the visual appearance of whomever the device has previously recorded. This device adds two [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] dice to the user's [[Deception (Cun)|Deception]] checks to impersonate the target and adds two [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice to onlookers' [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] checks to detect the imposter.[^2]
