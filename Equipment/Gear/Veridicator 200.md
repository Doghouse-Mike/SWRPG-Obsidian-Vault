Designed and distributed by Saldalith Manufacturing, the Veridicator is billed as a personal lie-detection device.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Veridicator_200).

## VERIDICATOR 200

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 1 Price 4,000 Rarity 8|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/b/b3/Profile_Veridicator_200.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 1 Price 4,000 Rarity 8

\[...\] Only one target can be scanned and monitored at a time, and they must be relatively nearby. Both the user and the target must remain relatively still, though small movements are unlikely to affect the device's readings.[^0-2]

A character wearing a Veridicator 200 adds two [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] dice to checks to discern whether another character is lying or omitting significant information and adds two [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] dice to checks to ascertain another character's mental state.[^0-2]
