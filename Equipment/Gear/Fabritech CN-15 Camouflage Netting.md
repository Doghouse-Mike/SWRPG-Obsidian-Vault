## FABRITECH CN-15 CAMOUFLAGE NETTING

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 4 Price 100 Rarity 2|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/7/76/Profile_Camouflage_Netting.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 4 Price 100 Rarity 2

\
(one roll)

CN-15 Camouflage Netting adds two automatic [[Narrative Dice#Failure|failure]] results to all checks made to detect items concealed by it. One roll of netting can conceal one object up to silhouette 2, two rolls can conceal one object up to silhouette 3, and three rolls can conceal one object up to silhouette 4. Anything silhouette 5 and larger is too large to conceal by camo netting alone.[^2]
