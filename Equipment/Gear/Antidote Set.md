## ANTIDOTE SET

Source: [^0-1][^2]

![Encumbrance 1 Price 250 Rarity 5|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/b/b3/Profile_Antidote_Set.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 1 Price 250 Rarity 5

A character using an antidote set reduces the difficulty of any check to resist a poison by two (to a minimum of **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Easy difficulty]]**). If they do not know which poison was used, they must make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Underworld (Int)|Knowledge (Underworld) check]]** to make an educated guess at the poison based on the region, the poison's qualities, and other evidence at their disposal to benefit from this item.[^0-1][^3]
