One of the most common tools in a technician's toolkit, an Anx is primarily used for prying open and separating plates and panels. About the size of a human’s forearm, anxes are wedge shaped, a thick, reinforced grip narrowing a thin, flat blade. This blade is then inserted between tight plates and with pressure, pops off the plates much easier than other tools.

In a pinch, some spacers have even used an anx as an improvised weapon, though it is unlikely to be particularly effective.
