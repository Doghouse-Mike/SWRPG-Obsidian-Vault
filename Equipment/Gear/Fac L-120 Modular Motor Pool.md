A combination garage, workshop, fabrication facility, and shelter, the L-120 modular motor pool is designed to hold ground vehicles and their support equipment.

## FAC L-120 MODULAR MOTOR POOL

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance - Price 1,200 Rarity 2|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/3/3a/Profile_Fac_L-120_Modular_Motor_Pool.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance - Price 1,200 Rarity 2

Little more than a large, low-ceilinged open space with a large door taking up one long wall, the L-120 model modular motor pool can hold a number of land vehicles with silhouettes totaling no more than 6 and all the tools, lifts, and heavy equipment required to keep them in working order.[^2]
