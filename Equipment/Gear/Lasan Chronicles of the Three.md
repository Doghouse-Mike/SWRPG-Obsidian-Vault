## LASAN CHRONICLES OF THE THREE

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 0 Price 3,000 Rarity 7|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/d/df/Profile_Lasan_Chronicles_of_the_Three.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 0 Price 3,000 Rarity 7

When a character studies the Chronicles, they may use [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]], [[Lore (Int)|Knowledge (Lore)]], or [[Discipline (Will)|Discipline]] to decipher it. They must then spend an hour studying the Chronicles, succeeding at a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] check** of the appropriate skill at the end of it. Depending on their chosen skill, they receive a unique bonus that can be used once during the remainder of the session. [[Lore (Int)|Knowledge (Lore)]] grants the Perspicacity of the Child, whose desire to learn outstrips all else. The character may add a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to any one Knowledge skill check they make. [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] gives them the Insight of the Fool: in knowing nothing, they see everything. Once when making a combat skill check, they may re-roll one [[Narrative Dice#Ability (Green)|ability]] die of their choice. Using [[Discipline (Will)|Discipline]] leaves them with the Purpose of the Warrior, whose devotion and tenacity overcomes all obstacles. The may remove a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die on a single skill check that they make.[^0-2]
