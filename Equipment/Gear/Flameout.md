Flameout is an extremely strong alcoholic beverage that, when mixed correctly, is known for producing sensations similar to a scalding of the tongue and afreezing of the throat. The drink is very popular in spaceport cantinas from Coruscant to Bakura. Jedi Master Bantis Keeg is reportedly a champion flameout mixer aboard Starlight Beacon.
