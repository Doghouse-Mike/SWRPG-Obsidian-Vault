Carried by doctors and healers on backwater worlds, physician's kits are bigger and more comprehensive than their medpac cousins.

**Models Include:** Numerous variants.

## PHYSICIAN'S KIT

Source: [^0-1]

![Encumbrance 2 Price 400 Rarity 2|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/b/bf/Profile_Medpac.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 2 Price 400 Rarity 2

A physician's kit allows a user to perform relatively complex medical procedures in the field. Like emergency medpacs, physician's kits allow a character to use the [[Medicine (Int)|Medicine]] skill without penalty, and in addition grant a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to all [[Medicine (Int)|Medicine]] skill checks. Also, thanks to their stock of stimulants and other unguents, these kits add an automatic [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result to successful [[Medicine (Int)|Medicine]] checks made while using the kit.[^2]
