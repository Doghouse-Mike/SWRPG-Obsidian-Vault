A popular alcoholic drink, Revnog currently comes in four varieties; cloudy dusk, azure dawn, scarlet sun and vert glow. The drink’s strength is covered by its sweetly spiced taste, leaving many drinkers surprisingly more inebriated than they thought. Revnog can be spotted at cantinas and parties from Coruscant to Morlana One
