Frangawl cultists are masters of dark side techniques, including ones that deal with capturing and corrupting the Living Force in a sentient being.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Frangawl_Force_powder).

## FRANGAWL FORCE POWDER

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 0 Price (R) 2,000 Rarity 10|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/1/17/Profile_Frangawl_Force_Powder.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 0 Price (R) 2,000 Rarity 10

 \
(one dose)

A Force-sensitive character can consume a dose of Frangawl Force powder as a maneuver. Upon doing so, the PC gains 10 Conflict and adds two automatic [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|dark points]] to the PC's next Force power check during that encounter.[^2]
