Small coins or medals made of aurodium mined from Varl, Ardos disks are valuable not merely for their rare metal content, but for what they represent.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ardos_disk).

## ARDOS DISK

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 1 Price (R) 10,000 Rarity 5|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/7/79/Profile_Ardos_Disk.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 1 Price (R) 10,000 Rarity 5

Upon displaying an Ardos disk, a character adds two [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] dice to any [[Charm (Pr)|Charm]] or [[Negotiation (Pr)|Negotiation]] skill checks made with Hutts or their henchmen or servants. In general, most Hutts will honor the implicit significance of the Ardos disk and refrain from overtly harming or hindering the bearer. If the disk bears the sigil of a rival clan, however, the GM can upgrade the difficulty of any [[Charm (Pr)|Charm]] or [[Negotiation (Pr)|Negotiation]] skill checks with the Hutt in question once instead, though the Hutt will still be loath to openly harm the bearer.

[^1]: [[Category - Hutt Space|Lords of Nal Hutta]]: *A Sourcebook for Hutt Space* (Page 111)
