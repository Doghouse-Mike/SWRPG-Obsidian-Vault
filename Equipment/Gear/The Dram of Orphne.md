## THE DRAM OF ORPHNE

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance - Price 9,000 Rarity 8|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/b/bf/Profile_The_Dram_of_Orphne.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance - Price 9,000 Rarity 8

A Force-sensitive character who possesses the Dram of Orphne heals 1 additional strain at the end of an encounter. A Force-sensitive character who posses the Dram of Orphne also heals 1 additional wound when benefiting from natural rest. Finally, when making a [[Medicine (Int)|Medicine]] check to heal another character, any character with the Dram of Orphne may add a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to the check.[^0-2]
