Extracted from the glandular toxin sacs of Sriluurian dark wolves, raquor venom is a powerful paralytic enzyme inhibitor.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Raquor_venom).

## RAQUOR VENOM

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 0 Price (R) 150 Rarity 7|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/0/03/Profile_Data_Dead_Drop.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 0 Price (R) 150 Rarity 7

Any sized dose has a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Daunting difficulty]]**. The poison Staggers the target for 2 rounds if the target inflicts 2 strain on the target (this ignores soak), and the GM may spend a despair result to Immobilize the target for 2 rounds. While raquor venom is usually introduced via injection, it can be aerosolized for use in [[Poison Gas Grenade|poison gas grenades]], though this reduces the effectiveness to a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]]**.
