Synthetic neurotoxin is a general poison synthetically produced, often illegally, on hundreds of worlds.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Neurotoxin).

## SYNTHETIC NEUROTOXIN

Source: [^0-1][^2][^3][^4]

![Encumbrance – Price (R) 50 Rarity 6|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/e/e2/Profile_Synthetic_Neurotoxin.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance – Price (R) 50 Rarity 6

This poison may be introduced into the target's body via aerosol deployment, food ingestion, or injection with an applicator or dart. Resisting a single dose requires an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Resilience (Br)|Resilience check]]**, while two or more doses combined into a single application require a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Resilience (Br)|Resilience check]]**. The poison inflicts 5 wounds if the target fails the check. Each [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] result generated may be spent to inflict 1 strain on the target as the effort of fighting the poison overwhelms them. Finally, a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result may be spent to make the target test against the poison again during the next round, as the poison remains in their system.[^0-1][^5][^6][^7]
