## SPORTING MACROBINOCULARS

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 1 Price 250 Rarity 2|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/a/aa/Profile_Sporting_Macrobinoculars.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 1 Price 250 Rarity 2

Sporting macrobinoculars magnify objects up to five kilometers away, and can record objects viewed. They also remove a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die imposed due to long range or poor light.[^2]
