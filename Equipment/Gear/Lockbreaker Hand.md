A lockbreaker hand is a cybernetic hand packed with tiny tools that allow an agent to bypass most common mechanical and electrical locks.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lockbreaker_hand).

A lockbreaker hand provides one rank in the [[Bypass Security talent]] and adds a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to [[Skulduggery (Cun)|Skulduggery]] and [[Computers (Int)|Computers]] checks made to defeat locks.
