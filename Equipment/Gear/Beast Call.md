Beast calls are some of the more simple and effective tools used by hunters and trappers.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Beast_call).

## BEAST CALL

Source: [^0-1][^2][^3]

![Encumbrance 1 Price 100 Rarity 3|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/d/d3/Profile_Beast_Call.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 1 Price 100 Rarity 3

Using a beast call requires no skill check, but the beast being targeted must make an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Discipline (Will)|Discipline check]]** to resist the call. Recording a new call to the mechanism's memory requires an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Computers (Int)|Computers check]]** modified by any other environmental conditions (heavy background noise, atmosphere interference, etc.)[^0-1][^4][^5]
