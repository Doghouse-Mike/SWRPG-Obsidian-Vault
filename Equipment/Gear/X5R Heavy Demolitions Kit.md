## X5R HEAVY DEMOLITIONS KIT

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 4 Price 1,000 Rarity 6|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/a/ad/Profile_X5R_Heavy_Demolitions_Kit.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 4 Price 1,000 Rarity 6

The user of an X5R kit gains two [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] dice on [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics]] checks to prepare, set, or disarm explosives. In addition, once per session, a character using an X5R can remove one [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result or up to three [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results (after canceling dice symbols but before resolving the effect) from a [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics]] check dealing with explosives.
