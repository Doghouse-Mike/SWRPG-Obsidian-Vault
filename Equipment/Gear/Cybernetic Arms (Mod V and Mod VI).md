Built by companies such as BioTech to fit nearly every sentient species in the galaxy, cybernetic arms are designed to increase an individual's strength or agility, depending on the model.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mod_V_cyberarm) [article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mod_VI_cyberarm).

**Models Include:** BioTech Industries Cyber Arm Mod V and Mod VI.

## CYBERNETIC ARMS (MOD V AND MOD VI)

Source: [^0-1][^2][^3][^4]

![Encumbrance – Price 10,000 Rarity 6|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/b/bf/Profile_Cybernetic_Arms_%28Mod_V_and_Mod_VI%29.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance – Price 10,000 Rarity 6

Mod V cyberarms provide +1 [[Brawn]], while Mod VI cyberarms provide +1 [[Agility]]. If a character replaces both arms with cybernetic enhancements, both arms must be the same model, as they are designed to work in tandem. Modifiers from both arms do not stack.[^0-1][^5][^6][^7]
