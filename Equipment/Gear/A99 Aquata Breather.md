The aquata breather is a portable device consisting of two small tanks connected to a mouthpiece.

## A99 AQUATA BREATHER

Source: [^1][^2]

![Encumbrance 0 Price 100 Rarity 7|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/7/7e/Profile_A99_Aquata_Breather.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text

\[...\] it supplies up to two hours of breathable air, and is particularly suited to subaquatic environments.[^0-3][^1-4]
