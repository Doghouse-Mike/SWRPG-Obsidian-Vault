This high-output stun weapon by Merr-Sonn takes the form of a flat lens small enough to conceal in the palm of a hand.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Model_31_palm_stunner).

## MODEL 31 PALM STUNNER

Source: [^0-1][^2]

![Encumbrance 1 Price (R) 500 Rarity 5|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/b/bf/Profile_Model_31_Palm_Stunner.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 1 Price (R) 500 Rarity 5

A character can use a palm stunner on an unaware target with an **opposed [[Skulduggery (Cun)|Skulduggery]]** **check vs. [[Resilience (Br)|Resilience]]**[^0-1] ***or* [[Stealth (Ag)|Stealth]]**[^1-3]. A successful check automatically incapacitates a minion or deals 12 damage to the strain threshold of a rival or nemesis. In addition, each [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] result on the opposed [[Stealth (Ag)|Stealth]] check increases the damage dealt by one.

```
While Endless Vigil: A Sourcebook for Sentinels shows this as "opposed Stealth versus Resilience check", the Errata (Page 2) corrects this to "opposed Skulduggery versus Stealth check".
```
