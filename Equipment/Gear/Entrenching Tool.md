The modest and versatile entrenching tool is one of the most critically important tools in a Warrior's kit.

**Models Include:** Various models.

## ENTRENCHING TOOL

Source: [^0-1][^2][^3]

![Encumbrance 1 Price 20 Rarity 1|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/9/9c/Profile_Bacta_%28Liter%29.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 1 Price 20 Rarity 1

Besides allowing the user to dig holes and trenches, an entrenching tool counts as a small improvised weapon that deals +2 damage, has a critical rating of 4, and does not break due to [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] or [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] results.[^0-1][^4]
