The **EnhanceScan Long-Range Terrain Scanner** was a device that uses a low-power ground-following radar to measure and produce a topographical map of a planetary surface in very little time. It is often mounted to speeders to accelerate the mapping process.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/EnhanceScan_Long-Range_Terrain_Scanner).

Successfully using the LRTS requires an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficult]] [[Computers (Int)|Computers check]]** on the part of the operator. Maps produced with the LRTS add a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to any [[Survival (Cun)|Survival]] check made to navigate using the map plus an automatic [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] result for every two [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results gained on the [[Computers (Int)|Computers]] check.[^2]
