Comlinks are the most common communication devices in the galaxy.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Comlink).

## COMLINK

### (HANDHELD)

Source: [^0-1][^2-2][^4-3]

![Encumbrance 0 Price 25 Rarity 0|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/e/e5/Profile_Comlink_%28Handheld%29.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 0 Price 25 Rarity 0

Most are simple two-way devices that can transmit and receive to a single mated unit at relatively long ranges over land and even into low orbit, but there are models that can transmit to multiple users at once or network whole groups together on the same frequency.[^1-4][^3-5][^5-6]

### (LONG RANGE)

Source: [^0-1][^2-2][^4-3]

![Encumbrance 2 Price 200 Rarity 1|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/5/5c/Profile_Thermal_Cloak.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 2 Price 200 Rarity 1

Larger, longer-range models are caried in a satchel or backpack and can reach across entire planets.[^1-4][^3-5][^5-6]
