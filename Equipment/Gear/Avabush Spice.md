Avabush spice is a drug that induces relaxation and lethargy, and some use it as an illicit sleep aid.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Avabush_spice).

**Restrictions:** Avabush spice is banned or controlled on most worlds with a strong Imperial presence.

A character who has consumed avabush spice feels lethargic, and can only upgrade a single ability die to a proficiency die on any given check because of this mental torpor. Further, whenever this character intentionally tries to lie or conceal the truth, the GM upgrades any one difficulty die in the dice pool into a challenge die. Avabush spice's effects last for one scene or combat encounter.[^2]
