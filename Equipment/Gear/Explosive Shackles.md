A particularly intimidating and malicious method of security slaves and prisoners, explosive shackles have small but lethal charges built into them to deter escape attempts.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Explosive_shackles).

**Models Include:** Numerous variants.

## EXPLOSIVE SHACKLES

Source: [^0-1][^2]

![Encumbrance 2 Price (R) 500 Rarity 6|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/4/4d/Profile_Explosive_Shackles.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 2 Price (R) 500 Rarity 6

Removing explosive shackles without the proper key requires a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Daunting difficulty]] [[Skulduggery (Cun)|Skulduggery check]]**. If a character fails this check, the shackles detonate, inflicting 10 wounds on the wearer and each character in engaged range (this ignores soak).[^0-1][^3]
