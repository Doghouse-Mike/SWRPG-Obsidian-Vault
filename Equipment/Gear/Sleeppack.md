Physically similar to a stimpack, a sleeppack is an auto-injection tube filled with a variety of natural and synthetic sedatives.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sleeppack).

## SLEEPPACK (1 DOSE)

Source: [^0-1]

![Encumbrance 0 Price 250 Rarity 3|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/e/ed/Profile_Sleeppack.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 0 Price 250 Rarity 3

Applying a sleeppack to an aware and unwilling target requires the wielder to make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Brawl (Br)|Brawl]]** or **[[Melee (Br)|Melee combat check]]** against the target. Instead of inflicting damage, if the attacker succeeds, the target must immediately resist the effects of the medications that flood the system as a poison (with a Resilience check). Any sized dose has a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]]**, and failure inflicts 10 strain.[^2]
