When taken by characters who are not Force users, a single dose of karrak spice removes three [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice added to checks from pain or illness for twelve hours, but it imposes an automatic [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] result on all checks for twenty four hours as it slows down thought and reflexes. Force users who take a dose of karrak spice receive both effects for twice the duration and also count their Force rating as one lower while it is active.[^2]
