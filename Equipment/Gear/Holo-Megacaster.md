One of the standard tools for a public speaker, a holo-megacaster is a variation on a standard holocomm system.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Holo-Megacaster).

## HOLO-MEGACASTER

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 1 Price 600 Rarity 3|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/8/87/Profile_Holo-Megacaster.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 1 Price 600 Rarity 3

A character using a holo-megacaster adds a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to Presence-based checks to address a crowd, and allows the character's image and voice to be clearly seen and heard at up to extreme range, depending on the settings used.[^0-2]
