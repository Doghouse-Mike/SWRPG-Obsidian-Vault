The *Phoenix II* was manufactured by the Drever Corporation for breaching through all sorts of locked doors. Initially delivered to planetary customs throughout the galaxy, it was quickly bought up by commando units and then the Republic’s SO BDE. As a breaching tool, it was not particularly subtle but was reliable in getting the job done. Some commando units realised that the plasma blast would likely be effective at eliminating droids and improvised the tool as a weapon against them. This eventually led the Drever Corporation to roll out the [[DN Bolt Caster]] to fill this newfound niche.

## *PHOENIX II* PLASMA DISRUPTOR

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 3 Price (R) 900 Rarity 5|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/e/e2/Profile_Phoenix_II_Plasma_Disruptor.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 3 Price (R) 900 Rarity 5

A *Phoenix II* can be used to bypass locked doors by using an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics check]]**, increasing the difficulty as necessary depending on the strength of the door (such as **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Daunting difficulty]]** for locked down blast doors). Breaching this way leaves clear evidence of the disruptor’s use and completely fries the circuitry and locking mechanisms of the door.

The plasma disruptor can be used as a weapon using the following profile: ([[Ranged - Light (Ag)|Ranged (Light)]]; Damage 12; Critical 4; Range \[Short\]; [[Item Qualities#Burn (Active)|Burn]] 1, [[Item Qualities#Disorient (Active)|Disorient]] 2, [[Item Qualities#Inaccurate (Passive)|Inaccurate]] 2, [[Item Qualities#Limited Ammo (Passive)|Limited Ammo]] 3). When used against non-droids, the disruptor loses the [[Item Qualities#Burn (Active)|Burn]] quality and has its damage halved.
