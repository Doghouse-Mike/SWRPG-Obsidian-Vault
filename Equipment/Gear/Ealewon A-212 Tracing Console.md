Tracing consoles are most commonly found with espionage agencies and other sensitive installations, but they could be used by any group concerned about the security of its electronic systems.

## EALEWON A-212 TRACING CONSOLE

Source: [^0-1]

![Encumbrance 12 Price 3,000 Rarity 6|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/7/79/Profile_Ealewon_A-212_Tracing_Console.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 12 Price 3,000 Rarity 6

A tracing console can be installed to monitor a single system, such as a building's data network or a ship's computer. [[Computers (Int)|Computers]] checks to slice a system protected this way suffer a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die due to the difficulty of avoiding notice. A defending slicer using the console gains two [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] dice to [[Computers (Int)|Computers]] checks to identify an intruder, such as by the Trace User action.
