Shadowcloaks are peculiar devices designed to protect a user's anonymity during meetings in person.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shadowcloak_(disguise%29).

**Models Include:** Tenloss SX-1 shadowcloak, Creshaldyne Industries "Static Shadow" generator.
