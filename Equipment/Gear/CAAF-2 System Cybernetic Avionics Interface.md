Implanted in a user's palm and fingers (or incorporated into prosthetic hands) and linked to their brainstem, the TaggeCo CAAF-2 system cybernetic avionics interface allows a pilot to connect with their airspeeder or starship's avionics and flight control systems at a digital level.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/CAAF-2_system_cybernetic_avionics_interface).

## CAAF-2 SYSTEM CYBERNETIC AVIONICS INTERFACE

Source: [^1][^2]

![Encumbrance – Price 8,000 Rarity 6|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/6/60/Profile_CAAF-2_System_Cybernetic_Avionics_Interface.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance – Price 8,000 Rarity 6

A character with a cybernetic avionics interface and at least one rank in [[Piloting - Planetary (Ag)|Piloting (Planetary)]] or [[Piloting - Space (Ag)|Piloting (Space)]] gains +1 rank in the appropriate Piloting skill when flying a starship or airspeeder. The avionics in landspeeders, swoops, and other repulsorcraft are generally too rudimentary to interface with, but a player may do so at the GM's discretion if the vehicle is equipped with a particularly advanced flight control computer.
