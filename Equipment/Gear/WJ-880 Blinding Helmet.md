In the days of the former Republic and the Jedi Order, the WJ-990 blinding helmet was a common training aid for a Jedi Padawan, helping the aspirant to learn to sense the Force flowing through all things by cutting out sensory distractions.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/WJ-880_blinding_helmet).

## WJ-880 BLINDING HELMET

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 3 Price (R) 10 Rarity 8|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/7/70/Profile_WJ-880_Blinding_Helmet.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 3 Price (R) 10 Rarity 8

A character wearing a WJ-880 binding helmet adds three [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice to Combat checks (and any other checks that rely on sight). At the GM's discretion, training while wearing such a helmet might help a character to develop \[their\] perception of the Force that shapes and guides the universe around \[them\].[^1-2]
