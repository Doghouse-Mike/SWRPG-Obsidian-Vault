Ion weapons, electrical phenomena, and overloads are a danger to anyone who relies too heavily on cybernetics.

## SURGE OVERRIDE SWITCH

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance – Price 1,000 Rarity 6|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/4/43/Profile_Surge_Override_Switch.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance – Price 1,000 Rarity 6

Once per encounter as an action, a character with a surge override switch may make an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Discipline (Will)|Discipline check]]**. If \[they\] succeed, \[they\] may reactivate any number of cybernetic implants that have been overloaded by weapons with the [[Item Qualities#Ion (Passive)|Ion]] quality, the [[Overcharge talent|Overcharge]] talent, or similar effects. For each implant \[they\] reactivate this way, \[they\] suffer 2 strain. A surge override switch cannot be disabled by [[Item Qualities#Ion (Passive)|Ion]] weapons or overloaded by other means.[^2]
