Using technology originally designed to minimize the constant background noise of a crowded metropolis, Veslox Corporation ingeniously realized the effect could be localized, and its sound dampener quickly became a best-seller in the criminal underworld.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sound_dampening_belt).

**Models include:** Veslox 11b, TaggeCo. Sound Dampener, Brennkeyes Syndicate Privacy Protector.
