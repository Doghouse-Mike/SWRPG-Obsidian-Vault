If the wearer of the Talisman of Iron Fists is Force-sensitive, \[they\] gain the [[Item Qualities#Cortosis (Passive)|Cortosis]] quality, which means that any armor \[they\] wear gains the [[Item Qualities#Cortosis (Passive)|Cortosis]] quality and \[their\] Brawl attacks gain the [[Item Qualities#Cortosis (Passive)|Cortosis]] quality. Furthermore, \[their\] Brawl attacks gain [[Item Qualities#Pierce (Passive)|Pierce]] X, where X is \[their\] current Force rating.[^2]
