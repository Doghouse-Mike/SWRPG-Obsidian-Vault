When activated, these paired belts are tethered by a power coupling. The wearers are drawn together by the coupling, adding a level of complexity to most physical tasks. They have been used in both formal gladiatorial tournaments as well as small town bar brawls.

Prevents the wearers from separating by more than 2 meters. The coupling can be manipulated like a rope, but contact with the electrified tether deals 1 strain per turn. [[Athletics (Br)|Athletic]] and [[Coordination (Ag)|Coordination]] checks suffer a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die while the belt is worn and powered.
