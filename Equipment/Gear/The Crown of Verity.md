## THE CROWN OF VERITY

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance - Price 6,000 Rarity 9|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/a/a2/Profile_The_Crown_of_Verity.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance - Price 6,000 Rarity 9

A Force-sensitive character who dons the Crown of Verity automatically fails all [[Deception (Cun)|Deception]] checks while wearing it. At the same time, \[they\] gain three automatic [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] results on any checks to detect lies or determine whether someone is telling the truth.[^0-2]
