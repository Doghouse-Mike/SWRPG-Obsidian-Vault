A small implant affixed to the suprarenal glands, an adrenal implant stimulates the production of epinephrine in the body when the individual's nervous system is subjected to strain.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Adrenal_implant).

**Models Include:** Athakam MedTech A.9a Adrenal Stimulator, BioTech Industries .
