Thermal cloaks are versatile pieces of equipment that can be worn or used as a blanket and provide protection from extreme heat and cold.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Thermal_cloak/Legends).

**Models Include:** Numerous variants.
