Intended to save pilots in the event of an emergency, anti-grav chutes are small backpacks with miniature repulsorlifts incorporated into them.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Savior_anti-grav_chute).

## ANTI–GRAV CHUTE

Source: [^0-1][^2]

![Encumbrance 1 Price 450 Rarity 5|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/2/2c/Profile_Anti%E2%80%93Grav_Chute.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 1 Price 450 Rarity 5

Characters equipped with an anti-grav chute take no damage or strain from falls. The speed of the fall decreases significantly once the anti-grav chute activates, which means reaching the ground can take multiple rounds.[^0-1][^3]
