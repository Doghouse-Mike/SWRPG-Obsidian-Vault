Designed by Piccatech Ltd., the Pannier system is designed to fit as much cargo on a beast as it can carry in an efficient and secure manner.

### PANNIER MODULAR SYSTEM EXTRA CARGO CONTAINER

![Encumbrance – Price 350 Rarity 4|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/1/18/Profile_Pannier_Modular_System_Extra_Cargo_Container.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance – Price 350 Rarity 4

The Pannier Modular Cargo Handling System comes with two cargo containers and can only be worn by riding or working animals of silhouette 2 or larger. It increases the creature's encumbrance threshold by 6, plus an additional 3 points of encumbrance threshold for each additional cargo container added to the system (to a maximum increase of 12).[^2]
