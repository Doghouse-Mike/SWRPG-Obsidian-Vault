While bacta is still far and away the most popular and effective way of treating injuries throughout the galaxy, it is also one of the more expensive ways. For spacers in a pinch, looking for a cheaper way to heal, there is Med nog.

Med nog consists of a very small amount of bacta diluted by water and stim-cot, an anti infection medication.When ingested, Med nog can quickly prevent any internal infection from a wound. Unlike pure bacta however, Med nog cannot regrow tissue or help knit broken bones back together. Med nog also tastes very bad, with many people only able to stand a sip or two.
