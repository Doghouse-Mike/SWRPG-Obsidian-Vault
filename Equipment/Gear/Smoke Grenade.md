Once triggered, smoke grenades quickly expel a dense cloud of harmless particles to obscure vision. They can provide cover in open spaces or mask an escape. Thermal and sonic imaging devices are not hindered by the haze.

## SMOKE GRENADE

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 1 Price 100 Rarity 2|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/0/08/Profile_Smoke_Grenade.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 1 Price 100 Rarity 2

\
(Single use item)

As an action, make an [[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Easy difficulty]] [[Ranged - Light (Ag)|Ranged (Light)]] ranged attack. On a success, the grenade lands where intended and envelopes everyone engaged with it in a dense cloud. Ranged and melee attacks made from inside the smoke suffer a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die, ranged attacks at targets obscured by smoke suffer two [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice. As a physical visual barrier, these setback are not considered "darkness" but can be negated by thermal or sonic imaging.

**Example:** Adversaries are at Medium range from the PCs. A PC throws a bomb to short range, between the two parties. All ranged attacks suffer two [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice because it obscures both parties. Melee characters from both groups engage each other in the smoke, their attacks against each other suffer a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die.
