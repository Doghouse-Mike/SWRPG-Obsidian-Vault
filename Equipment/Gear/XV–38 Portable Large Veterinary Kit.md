Designed for use by veterinarians, beast breeders, or anyone who deals with large and dangerous animals for a living, the PLAV contains enough drugs, medical supplies, specialty tools, and other gear to stabilize wounded creatures in the field until they can be moved.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/XV-38_Portable_Large_Animal_Veterinary_Kit).

## XV–38 PORTABLE LARGE VETERINARY KIT

Source: [^0-1][^2]

![Encumbrance 3 Price 600 Rarity 3|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/7/77/Profile_XV%E2%80%9338_Portable_Large_Veterinary_Kit.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 3 Price 600 Rarity 3

The PLAV allows a user to examine, analyze, and perform medical procedures on all manner of creatures. As the medpac does for humanoid species, the PLAV allows a character to medically treat alien creatures without penalty. Additionally, it grants a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to all [[Medicine (Int)|Medicine]], [[Survival (Cun)|Survival]], and [[Xenology (Int)|Knowledge (Xenology)]] skill checks made to analyze or treat these creatures.[^0-1][^3]
