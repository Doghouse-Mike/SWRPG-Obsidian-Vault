## RETINAL IMPLANT

Source: [^0-1][^2]

![Encumbrance – Price (R) 1,250 Rarity 6|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/d/dc/Profile_Retinal_Implant.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance – Price (R) 1,250 Rarity 6

Programming a retinal implant with a retina pattern requires a few minutes of studying the pattern and an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Perception (Cun)|Perception check]]**. Retinal implants fool most retina scanners with an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Easy difficulty]] [[Cool (Pr)|Cool]]** or [[Deception (Cun)|**Deception check**]]. More advanced retina scanners require an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]]** or **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] check** for the wearer to defeat them. If the retinal scanner operator has reason to be suspicious, using the retinal implant may also require an **opposed [[Deception (Cun)|Deception]] vs. the scanner operator's [[Computers (Int)|Computer]] skill**.[^0-1][^3]
