## SCRAMBLE KEY

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 1 Price (R) 750 Rarity 4|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/3/3e/Profile_Scramble_Key.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 1 Price (R) 750 Rarity 4

A scramble key bypasses most simple electronic locks within five minutes of application. At the Game Master's discretion, more complicated locks may require additional time to crack or might trigger alarms at the brute force incursion. The user may attempt to speed up this process with a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Daunting difficulty]] [[Skulduggery (Cun)|Skulduggery check]]**, relying on pure intuition to select the correct code from those the scramble key generates. Success reduces the time needed to crack a common lock to a single minute, but failure immediately triggers any relevant alarms. Force-sensitive characters may make a Force power check as part of this check, adding either a [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] or [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result for each [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]] generated.[^0-2]
