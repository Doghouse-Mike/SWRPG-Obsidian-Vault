Mine detectors are specialized handheld scanners designed to locate buried or concealed anti-personnel and anti-vehicle mines.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mine_detector).

## MINE DETECTOR

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 1 Price 350 Rarity 4|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/f/fa/Profile_Mine_Detector.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 1 Price 350 Rarity 4

Using a mine detector to locate concealed mines requires a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Computers (Int)|Computers]]** or **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance check]]** with appropriate modifications for environmental conditions such as weather, flora, roughly terrain, or the composition of the ground. On a successful check, all mines and other explosive devices within medium range are revealed to the user.
