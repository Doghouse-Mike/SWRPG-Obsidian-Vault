Dataspikes, also known as computer spikes, are a common tool for bypassing computer security.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Computer_spike).

## DATASPIKE

Source: [^1][^0-2]

![Encumbrance 1 Price 100 Rarity 3|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/4/4d/Profile_Dataspike.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 1 Price 100 Rarity 3

Once per round, a character with a dataspike may expend it to attempt to disable a security program as a maneuver (rather than as an action). \[...\] Reduce the difficulty of subsequent [[Computers (Int)|Computers]] check to disable the program by 2, to a minimum of **Simple (—)**. After one use, the dataspike burns out and becomes useless.[^3][^0-2]
