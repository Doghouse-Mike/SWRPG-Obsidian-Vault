Also called "Bith hiding cubes," these transparisteel cubes are about three-quarters of a meter on each side and function as small safes designed to be invisible to the naked eye.

**Models Include:** Veretex Securities hiding cube SCT-1000, SecuriCorp "Sleepsafe" shift coffer.

## SHIFT COFFER

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 5 Price 2,500 Rarity 7|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/e/e3/Profile_Shift_Coffer.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 5 Price 2,500 Rarity 7

Shift coffers can contain up to a total 5 encumbrance in items, though items more than about half a meter in length do not fit inside (at least, not without being bent or folded). When the coffer is in its wall niche, attempts to discover it require a successful **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Formidable difficulty]] [[Perception (Cun)|Perception check]]**. The locks on shift coffers are usually good but not extremely complex or sophisticated, requiring a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Skulduggery (Cun)|Skulduggery]]** check to pick.
