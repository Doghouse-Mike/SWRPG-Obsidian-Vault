This thick paint comes in small tubes holding five applications and is intended to camouflage exposed skin, but can be used on everything from weapons to personal gear.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/CN-21_camouflage_paint).

## FABRITECH CN-21 CAMOUFLAGE PAINT

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 0 Price 40 Rarity 2|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/a/ab/Profile_Camouflage_Paint.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 0 Price 40 Rarity 2

CN-21 Camouflage Paint adds an automatic [[Narrative Dice#Failure|failure]] result to all checks made to detect items concealed by it. Note that camo paint does not have any of the advanced circuitry that provides concealment from electronic scanning and surveillance. Camouflage paint only works for item or individuals with a silhouette of 1 or smaller.[^2]
