A weapon detector is a specialized sensor built into goggles that is able to detect the presence of explosives, the power cells used in blasters, and the chemical propellants used in slugthrowers, as well as many types of melee weapons, such as knives, swords, and stun batons.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Armascan_Weapon_Detection_Goggles#:~:text=Produced_by_CryonCorp,_Armascan_Weapon,They_cost_750_credits.).
