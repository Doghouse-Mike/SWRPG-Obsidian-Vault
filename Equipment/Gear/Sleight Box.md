A favorite of smugglers and spies everywhere, the sleight box is a crate or box modified for hiding cargo.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sleight_box).

## SLEIGHT BOX

Source: [^0-1][^2]

![Encumbrance 2–20 Price (R) 400 Rarity 6|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/6/62/Profile_Sleight_Box.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 2–20 Price (R) 400 Rarity 6

Sleight boxes can vary from encumbrance 2 to encumbrance 20, and each can conceal a number of items with a total encumbrance value up to half of that amount. To find the something hidden within a sleight box, a character must make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Daunting difficulty]] [[Perception (Cun)|Perception check]]**. At the GM's discretion, an unaware character might use [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]] instead.[^0-1][^3]
