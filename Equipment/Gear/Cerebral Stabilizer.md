## CEREBRAL STABILIZER

Source: [^0-1][^2]

![Encumbrance 3 Price 2,200 Rarity 7|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/8/8d/Profile_Cerebral_Stabilizer.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 3 Price 2,200 Rarity 7

A cerebral stabilizer must be applied to a patient within one round of suffering a Critical Injury. This device may be applied to a character suffering from a "Bleeding Out" or "The End is Nigh" Critical Injury, or to a character who has died during the current round. Applying the device requires an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Medicine (Int)|Medicine check]]**. This stabilizes the character, negating the effects of the Critical Injury as long as the device remains attached. If the character died, they are brought back to life, and their Critical Injury changes to the "Gruesome Injury" Critical Injury result (gaining all that injury's effects instead).[^0-1][^3]
