A small electric capsule that can be surgically installed into a being, generally in their tooth. If an individual is taken prisoner they can activate the capsule which administers a lethal shock.

## ELECTRO-IMMOLATION CAPSULE

Source: [^1]

A conscious character can activate the capsule as a maneuver, immediately killing themself. A PC may spend a Destiny Point to cause the capsule to misfire, preventing a potential prisoner from dying.
