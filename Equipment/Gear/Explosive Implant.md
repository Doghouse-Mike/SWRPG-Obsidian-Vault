## EXPLOSIVE IMPLANT

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 0 Price (R) 2,000 Rarity 8|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/0/09/Profile_Explosive_Implant.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 0 Price (R) 2,000 Rarity 8

A corresponding handheld device similar to a droid caller can locate the implanted target with a **Simple (—) [[Computers (Int)|Computers]] check**. With the press of a button, the implant can explode, instantly killing its victim. A (once upgraded) **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Daunting difficulty]] [[Medicine (Int)|Medicine]]** or **[[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics check]]** is required remove the implant. However, a [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] result alerts the transmitter of the tampering, and a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result triggers the device.
