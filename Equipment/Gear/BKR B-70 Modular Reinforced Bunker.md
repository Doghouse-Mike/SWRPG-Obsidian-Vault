## BKR B-70 MODULAR REINFORCED BUNKER

Source: [^1]

> [!quote] Image text

The B-70 can hold six average-sized humanoids or one large piece of equipment like a turbolaser emplacement or shield generator. It provides 2 ranged defense to any individual or materiel inside.[^0-2]
