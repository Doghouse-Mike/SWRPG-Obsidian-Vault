Produced by every major galactic arms manufacturer and many general tool and instrument makers, weapon maintenance kits are essential for keeping portable weapons operating under field conditions.

## WEAPON MAINTENANCE KIT

Source: [^0-1][^2]

![Encumbrance 2 Price 450 Rarity 3|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/3/35/Profile_Weapon_Maintenance_Kit.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 2 Price 450 Rarity 3

When purchasing one of these kits, the player must specify the kit type (blaster, slugthrower, etc.) Each kit provides two [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] dice to any [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics]] checks made to repair, maintain, or modify a weapon, but only for that specific class of weapon.[^0-1][^3]
