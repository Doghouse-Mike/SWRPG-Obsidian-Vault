## PIONEER SQUAD TOOL KIT

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 25 Price 4,000 Rarity 5|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/2/2a/Profile_Pioneer_Squad_Tool_Kit.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

The tools and equipment included in a pioneer squad tool kit adds two [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] dice on checks made to clear land, build structures, set up obstacles, set up or disable explosives, or engage in other construction or engineering tasks in the field.
