## NANNARIUM ROOT

Source: [^0-1]

![Encumbrance 0 Price 200 Rarity 6|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/1/1d/Profile_Nannarium_Root.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 0 Price 200 Rarity 6

\
(dose)

A character under the effects of distilled nannarium root extract may upgrade the ability of any Intelligence or [[Cunning]]-related check once, though doing so requires single-minded concentration and causes them to suffer 2 strain. The effects of a single dose of nannarium root lasts for about an hour.[^2]
