Affide crystals are commonly used as a last-resort "suicide pill" in the event of capture or torture.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Affide_crystal).

## AFFIDE CRYSTAL

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 0 Price (R) 500 Rarity 8|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/3/3a/Profile_Affide_Crystal.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 0 Price (R) 500 Rarity 8

A single affide crystal is a poison that requires an upgraded **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Daunting difficulty]]** **[[Resilience (Br)|Resilience check]]** to overcome when ingested. The poison inflicts 10 wounds and a Critical Injury on a failed check. Each [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] result generated adds +40 to the roll for the Critical Injury result. GMs may spend three [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result to kill a target instantly, leaving no opportunity for resuscitation.[^2]
