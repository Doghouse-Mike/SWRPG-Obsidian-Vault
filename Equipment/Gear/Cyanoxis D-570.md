Cyanoxis D-570 was originally developed by Toxico as an all-purpose rodenticide.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cyanoxis_D-570).

## CYANOXIS D-570

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 1 Price 50 Rarity 4|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/c/cf/Profile_Cyanoxis_D-570.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 1 Price 50 Rarity 4

When used as an assassination tool, D-570 is typically administered through food and drink.

One dose of Cyanoxis D-570 requires a character to make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Resilience (Br)|Resilience check]]** to overcome. On a failed check, the poison inflicts 2 wounds and 2 strain immediately, plus 1 wound and 1 strain per day for ten days or until the target succumbs to death. Each [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] result generated inflicts 1 additional strain per day as internal blood loss takes its toll on the target's constitution. A [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result can be spent to increase the number of days the poison stays in effect by one.
