A vehicle control system designed to be integrated directly into the nervous system, this ingenious (if notoriously difficult to use) device allows the user to control a vehicle with a thought, moving it as if it were part of their own body.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Neuromachine_interface).

**Models Include:** Neuro-Saav Technologies C-39 Link.
