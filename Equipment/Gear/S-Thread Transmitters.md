S-Threads, otherwise known as Simu-tunnels, are an interstellar phenomena of microscopic threads of information beamed across hyperspace, and are so imperceptibly small that they are untraceable. This technology has been used across the galaxy going back eons, as it is the technology that allowed ships to communicate navigational buoys while in the stream of hyperspace. In this form, the S-Thread Transmitter constantly sends a thread of data to its partner giving it fairly precise galactic coordinates of its location. Barring interference, these Transmitters can potentially guide its user from one side of the galaxy to the other, within a square kilometer.

(Pair)

Once per session, a character in possession of one of the S-Thread Transmitters may make an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Astrogation (Int)|Astrogation check]]** to attempt to identify the galactic whereabouts of the paired Transmitter. If successful, the character can trace and navigate to the coordinates of the system where the other Transmitter resides. Two [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results can be used to determine the coordinates of the planet within that system. A [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result can be used to find the exact coordinates on said planet.[^0-2]
