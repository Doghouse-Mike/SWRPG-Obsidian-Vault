Modular barracks come in many configurations for easy tailoring to a unit's specific needs. The F-221 model is a medium-sized building made to hold a platoon-sized unit and that unit's equipment. The barracks include a large common area; smaller rooms for offices, lounges, and refreshers; climate control; and an environmental sealing system proof against chemical and biological attacks.[^0-1][^3]
