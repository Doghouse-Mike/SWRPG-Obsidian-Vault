## SENNARI TOXIN

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance - Price (R) 250 Rarity 6|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/c/ce/Profile_Sennari_Toxin.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance - Price (R) 250 Rarity 6

Any number of doses have the same effect, and must be injected into the bloodstream (such as through a successful hit from a bladed or piercing weapon). A character affected must make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Simple difficulty]] [[Resilience (Br)|Resilience check]]**. The number of [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] results determines how many rounds (or minutes outside of structured time) the target has before they begin suffocating. After this time, they suffer the effects of suffocation.

Given that this toxin is well known, antidotes capable of counteracting it are found in nearly every medpac produced. With one of these on hand, a character can have the effects of this poison ended with an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Medicine (Int)|Medicine check]]**.
