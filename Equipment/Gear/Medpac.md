Often described as "a medical frigate you can fit in your pocket," a standard medpac is a larger and better-equipped version of the common emergency medpac.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Medpac).

**Models Include:** Athakam MedTech First Responder, BioTech FastFlesh Medpac.

## MEDPAC

Source: [^0-1][^2][^3]

![Encumbrance 2 Price 400 Rarity 2|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/b/bf/Profile_Medpac.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 2 Price 400 Rarity 2

Standard medpacs allow a user to perform relatively complex medical procedures in the field. Like emergency medpacs, they allow a character to use the [[Medicine (Int)|Medicine]] skill without penalty. They also grant a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to all [[Medicine (Int)|Medicine]] checks. Thanks to their stock of stim applicators, medpacs grant a group the equivalent of one [[Stimpack|stimpack]] per scene, at the GM's discretion.[^0-1][^4][^5]
