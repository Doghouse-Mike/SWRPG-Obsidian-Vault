Bond gauntlets are a Verpine invention designed to strengthen and reinforce an individual's forearms, while providing stabilization to hand and arm movements.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Verpine_bond_gauntlets).

## VERPINE BOND GAUNTLETS

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 1 Price 250 Rarity 6|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/d/de/Profile_Verpine_Comlink.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text

Due to this additional stability, Verpine bond gauntlets grant their users a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to checks made to handle volatile materials or perform delicate work. Such circumstances should be adjudicated by the GM on a case-by-case basis, and may include [[Computers (Int)|Computers]] and [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics]] checks to make delicate repairs and [[Skulduggery (Cun)|Skulduggery]] checks to pick locks or disable traps.[^1-2]
