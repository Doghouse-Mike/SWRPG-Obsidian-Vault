Designed to encode messages sent over comlink or via subspace, encryption modules attach to comlinks or hypertransceivers and allow for secure communications on the battlefield or across long distances.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Whistler_encryption_module).

Attempts to decode messages sent through an encryption module without a receiver with the decryption key are **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Formidable difficulty]]** and require the use of sophisticated equipment, such as a ship's mainframe or a Class One droid. Attempts to decode an encrypted message without a computer are impossible.[^0-2]
