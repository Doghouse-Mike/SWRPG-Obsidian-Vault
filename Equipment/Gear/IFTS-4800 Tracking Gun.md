The IFTS-4800 tracking gun was a handheld radar device utilized by the First Order. It was equipped with an electromonoscope and calibration dials to improve tracking. It can be calibrated to scan for specific ship types for convenient alerts without requiring a full review of the scanning data.

## IFTS-4800 TRACKING GUN

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 2 Price 650 Rarity 6|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/6/6e/Profile_IFTS-4800_Tracking_Gun.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 2 Price 650 Rarity 6

The IFTS functions like medium range vehicle scanners. Calibrating it to alert for a pre-determined list of known ship types requires an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Computers (Int)|Computers check]]**. The tracking gun may be used to scan the area as a maneuver, and will automatically alert if it detects a ship from its current watchlist. Reviewing unknown results may require more time or a check at the GM's discretion, and should take into account current traffic, urgency, and signal interference.
