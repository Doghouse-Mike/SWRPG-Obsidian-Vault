This spray is a proprietary concoction and dispenser system using the universally available bacta. Priced almost eight times higher than simply purchasing the bacta directly, it is certainly worth the price when you don’t have the time to rest your wounds. Rather than provide a full healing process, it was used to temporarily dull the pain and hold the criticality until a medic could reach the patient or proper measures could be taken.

## BACTA SPRAY

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 1 Price 75 Rarity 3|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/f/f9/Profile_Bacta_Spray.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 1 Price 75 Rarity 3

A character may spend an action, or just under a minute outside of structured play, to apply the spray to a wound. This consumes the full spray and increases the patient’s wound threshold by 3 until the end of the next encounter. This did not have a cumulative effect, with further doses applied while the other was still active having no benefit.[^2]
