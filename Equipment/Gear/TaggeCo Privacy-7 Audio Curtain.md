## TAGGECO PRIVACY-7 AUDIO CURTAIN

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 4 Price 500 Rarity 6|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/8/88/Profile_TaggeCo_Privacy-7_Audio_Curtain.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 4 Price 500 Rarity 6

Characters attempting to listen in on a conversation which is being obscured by one or more audio curtains must make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Perception (Cun)|Perception check]]** to understand anything being said. The GM can spend a [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] or [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result to have the character misunderstand key words or phrases.[^0-2]
