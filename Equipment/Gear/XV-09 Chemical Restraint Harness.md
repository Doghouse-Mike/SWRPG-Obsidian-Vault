## XV–09 CHEMICAL RESTRAINT HARNESS

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 2 Price 1,200 Rarity 5|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/5/55/Profile_XV-09_Chemical_Restraint_Harness.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 2 Price 1,200 Rarity 5

Using a Pacifier on a wild animal is a temporary fix at best, and should only be done in emergencies. Mounting the harness on a creature requires a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Survival (Cun)|Survival check]]** to ensure the harness is fitted properly and the auto-hypo is situated correctly. The difficulty of this check can be reduced by 1 with a successful **[[Xenology (Int)|Knowledge (Xenology) check]]**. If successful, the XV-09 grants two [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] dice to any [[Survival (Cun)|Survival]] checks made to control the beast wearing the harness. In addition, creatures that are successfully pacified gain a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to any [[Discipline (Will)|Discipline]] checks. Game Masters can spend any [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] result generated by the initial Survival check to reflect one or more negative side effects caused by the drugs. The exact nature of these side effects is left to the Game Master's discretion. In addition, the Game Master can spend three [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result to reflect a catastrophic reaction to the drugs, such as a heart attack or brain aneurysm, which results in the animal's death. While safe for use on animals, the powerful mixture of chemicals in the harness can be dangerous to the minds of sentient beings. Attaching the harness to a sentient being can result in permanent brain damage and even death.
