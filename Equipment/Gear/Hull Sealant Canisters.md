As indicated by their name, hull sealant canisters contain hull sealant.

## HULL SEALANT CANISTERS

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 1 Price 100 Rarity 2|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/c/cc/Profile_Hull_Sealant_Canisters.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 1 Price 100 Rarity 2

The sealant is under pressure and can be applied to small gaps or cracks in a ship's hull to prevent air loss due to vacuum. Doing so requires a successful **[[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics check]]**, the difficulty of which depends on the size of the hole. Holes larger than three centimeters in diameter also require additional material, such as a piece of metal or ceramic plating that can be secured in place with hull sealant.

### HULL SEALANT DIFFICULTIES

1 [[Narrative Dice#Difficulty (Purple)|difficulty die]]: Tiny but noticeable punctures in the hull.

2 [[Narrative Dice#Difficulty (Purple)|difficulty dice]]: Breaches in the hull of up to three centimeters in diameter.

3 [[Narrative Dice#Difficulty (Purple)|difficulty dice]]: Breaches in the hull of up to six centimeters in diameter.

4 [[Narrative Dice#Difficulty (Purple)|difficulty dice]]: Breaches in the hull up to ten centimeters in diameter.

5 [[Narrative Dice#Difficulty (Purple)|difficulty dice]]: Hull ruptures up to .5 meters.
