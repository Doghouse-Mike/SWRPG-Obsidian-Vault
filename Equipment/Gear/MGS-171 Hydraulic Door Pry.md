While cutting lasers and plasma welders are frequent choices for cutting through a ship's blast doors, they are not always the most pragmatic choice. Most blast doors are designed with energy resistance in mind, and if a door maintains a magnetic seal, cutting through is nearly impossible. On such occasions, brute force is a simple and practical solution. Hydraulic door levers are portable devices of heavy equipment designed with two clamp-like diamagnetic wedges. Once the thin wedges are fitted into any crevice in the door, the diamagnetic wedges begin to disrupt the magnetic seal while the hydraulic pressures pry sections of the door apart.
