Technicians, Engineers, and Tinkerers have long known how to modify the gas conversion XCiter in a blaster to yield a more highly energetic—and correspondingly more destructive—payload.

## ENHANCED XCITER

Source: [^1][^2]

![Encumbrance - Price (R) 1,500 Rarity 6|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/d/d7/Profile_Enhanced_XCiter.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance - Price (R) 1,500 Rarity 6

This attachment can be applied to any blaster.

**Models Include:** Outlaw Tech Custom XCiter.\
**Base Modifiers:** Decrease critical rating by 1 (to a minimum of 1). The GM may spend two [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] results from any combat check using the weapon to cause the blaster to explode, destroying it and inflicting an automatic Critical Injury on the wielder.\
**Modification Options:** 3 Item Quality ([[Item Qualities#Pierce (Passive)|Pierce]] +1) Mod.
