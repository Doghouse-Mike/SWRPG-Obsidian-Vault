Vid-vox scramblers are small personal signal-jamming generators prized by intelligence agents, corporate officers, military brass, crime bosses, and anyone else interested in keeping their private conversations private.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vid-vox_scrambler).

## VID–VOX SCRAMBLER

Source: [^0-1][^2]

![Encumbrance 2 Price 2,400 Rarity 8|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/f/f1/Profile_Vid%E2%80%93Vox_Scrambler.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 2 Price 2,400 Rarity 8

Vid-vox scramblers affect all electronic audio, video, and holographic recording devices within a four-meter radius. A **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Daunting difficulty]] [[Perception (Cun)|Perception check]]** can be made to attempt to overhear a pertinent part of the conversation from within the jamming bubble. Using a recording device to listen in requires a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Daunting difficulty]] [[Computers (Int)|Computers check]]**; even if the check is successful, any information recorded is incomplete and spotty at best. Recording devices used outside the area of effect cannot detect anything at all.[^0-1][^3]
