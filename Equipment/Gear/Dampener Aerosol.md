An expensive consumable produced by Rafkind Industrials, the dampener aerosol was sold in a can akin to the old smoke grenades. These containers were compartmentalised in such a way that activating the mistifier would only consume a third of the capacity. When activated, it would produce a thick blue mist which would conceal the user and was specifically designed to interact with blaster energy—absorbing bolts and rendering them considerably less powerful.

## DAMPENER AEROSOL

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 1 Price 600 Rarity 6|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/f/fd/Profile_Dampener_Aerosol.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 1 Price 600 Rarity 6

As a maneuver, a character may activate the aerosol to make a cloud which provides heavy concealment to all those within Engaged range. In addition to the normal effects of concealment, blaster weapons have their damage reduced by 3 when shooting into or through the cloud. The cloud disperses after two round, or quicker depending on the airflow.[^2]
