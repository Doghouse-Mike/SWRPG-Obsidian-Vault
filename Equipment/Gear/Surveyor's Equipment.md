The first step in building anything on a planet is a good land survey, so most military engineers and long-range scouts are trained in equipment to aid in this task.

## SURVEYOR'S EQUIPMENT

Source: [^0-1][^2]

> [!quote] Image text

Surveyor's equipment adds two [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] dice to a character's [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] checks when determining locations on a planet's surface, measuring distances, plotting settlements or bases, or creating detailed planetary maps.[^0-1][^3]
