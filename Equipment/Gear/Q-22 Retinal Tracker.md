Consisting of microsensors implanted directly behind the user's retina and a tiny tracking computer installed at the base of the skull, a retinal tracker aids the user in targeting imminent threats.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Q-22_retinal_tracker).

**Models Include:** Neuro-Saav Technologies Q-22.
