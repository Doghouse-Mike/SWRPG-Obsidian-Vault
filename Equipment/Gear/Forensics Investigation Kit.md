Essentially specialized tool kits for crime-scene investigations, forensic investigation kits are produced by a number of security companies for use by law enforcement agencies.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Forensics_investigation_kit).

## FORENSICS INVESTIGATION KIT

Source: [^0-1][^2]

![Encumbrance 4 Price 450 Rarity 4|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/6/6d/Profile_Forensics_Investigation_Kit.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 4 Price 450 Rarity 4

When used during the investigation of a crime, a forensic investigation kit adds an automatic [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] result to all [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] checks made by the user to spot and gather evidence. It may also count as the "right tools for the job" when making checks to analyze evidence gathered. At the Game Master's discretion, this item can also be used for related purposes such as archaeological digs.[^0-1][^3]
