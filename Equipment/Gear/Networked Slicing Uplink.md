Some slicers make use of a rare and highly illegal set of droid modifications to mask their slicing signatures by routing them through a droid's systems, which fill the signature with junk data from the droid's base-level processes.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Networked_slicing_uplink).

## NETWORKED SLICING UPLINK

Source: [^0-1][^2]

![Encumbrance – Price (R) 1,250 Rarity 7|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/b/bf/Profile_Networked_Slicing_Uplink.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance – Price (R) 1,250 Rarity 7

A networked slicing uplink is a cybernetic implant that can only be installed in droids. In addition to its other effects, it counts as a set of [[Slicer Gear|slicer gear]].[^0-1][^1-3]

When the droid is slicing or assisting with slicing tasks, add a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die to other characters' [[Computers (Int)|Computers]] checks to identify the slicer based on these activities.[^0-1][^1-3]
