Synthesized from a high concentrated admixture of methylpenatox, glucose, and inotropic chemicals, gunjack markedly increases the strength of its user at the cost of reduced motor coordination, diminished mental acuity, and severe long-term health risks.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gunjack).

A dose of gunjack increases the user's [[Brawn]] characteristic by 1 (to a maximum of 6) until the end of the scene or combat encounter. Additionally, each dose a character consumes upgrades the difficulty of \[their\] [[Agility]]-, [[Intellect]]-, and [[Cunning]]-related checks once until the end of the game session.
