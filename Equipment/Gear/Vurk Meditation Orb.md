The orbs are perfect obsidian spheres, roughly 15 centimeters across.

## VURK MEDITATION ORB

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 2 Price 1,000 Rarity 6|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/2/28/Profile_Vurk_Meditation_Orb.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 2 Price 1,000 Rarity 6

Studying the opaque sphere allows a Mystic to focus inwards, leaving the world behind. In this state, they see only the connections between beings, hear only the whispers of the Force. This meditation lasts for at least an hour, and for the 24 hours following this meditation, they may suffer 2 strain to add a [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]] to the results of the next check to use the [[Alter]] or [[Battle Meditation]] Force power.[^2]
