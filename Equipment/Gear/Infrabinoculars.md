Infrabinoculars are specialized tools designed to enhance awareness in urban evironments.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Infrabinoculars).

## INFRABINOCULARS

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 1 Price 350 Rarity 5|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/3/3f/Profile_Infrabinoculars.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 1 Price 350 Rarity 5

A character using infrabinoculars can see through up to two meters of wall, possibly allowing surveillance of a whole building at once. Walls made of dense materials or constructed to shield their interiors may impose one or more [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice on [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] checks.[^0-2]

### (WITH LONG-RANGE SENSORS)

![Encumbrance 1 Price 500 Rarity 6|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/a/a1/Profile_Verpine_Headband.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 1 Price 500 Rarity 6

Some models of infrabinoculars can alternate between this infrared sensor package and a long-range view equivalent to that of [[Electrobinoculars|electrobinoculars]].[^0-2]
