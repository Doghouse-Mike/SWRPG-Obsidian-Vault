## AQUASUIT

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 3 Price 250 Rarity 2|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/d/de/Profile_Aquasuit.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 3 Price 250 Rarity 2

An aquasuit provides six hours of atmosphere to an air breather. During this time, the wearer also has access to fresh water, nutrition supplements, and other biological necessities. While wearing an aquasuit, a character does not suffer movement penalties for traveling through water.
