The success and survival of an operation often hinges on preventing the enemy from listening in on friendly comm traffic.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Comm_unit_scrambler).

**Models Include:** Chedak Mk. V Com Scrambler.

## COMM SCRAMBLER

Source: [^0-1][^2][^3]

![Encumbrance – Price 1,000 Rarity 4|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/a/a8/Profile_Comm_Scrambler.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance – Price 1,000 Rarity 4

Using a comm scrambler adds two [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice to any checks made to decode an encrypted transmission.[^0-1][^4][^5]

```
While the Age of Rebellion Roleplaying Game: Core Rulebook shows this as "Encum 4 | Rarity 3", the Errata (Page 1) corrects this to "Encum 0 | Rarity 5".
```
