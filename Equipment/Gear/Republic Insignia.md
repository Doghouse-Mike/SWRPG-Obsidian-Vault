Clone troopers, Navy officers, and even Jedi who have served with distinction in the various theaters of the Clone Wars may be nominated for awards to recognize their valor and service.

## REPUBLIC INSIGNIA

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 0 Price Special/(R) 100 Rarity 3/6|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/8/8a/Profile_Republic_Insignia.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 0 Price Special/(R) 100 Rarity 3/6

A character with one or more Republic insignia adds a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to checks made to impress or persuade other characters based on their past service. At the GM's discretion, a character with an exceptional number of insignia might receive an additional [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die on such checks.

Genuine Republic insignia cannot be purchased on the open market, as each represents honor granted for a specific owner's accomplishments. However, black-market replicas can be found. If a black-market replica is examined, a successful **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Perception (Cun)|Perception check]]** reveals the fake. The first rarity value listed is that of a counterfeit insignia, and the second indicates the general rarity of encountering insignia. The listed cost is for a stolen or counterfeit insignia.
