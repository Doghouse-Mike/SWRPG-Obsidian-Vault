DefenStar Ltd. produces a number of planetary shield generators designed to protect everything from small, platoon-sized camps to huge forward garrisons that house thousands of troops, personnel, and weapons.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nyalsan_II_Planetary_Shield).

## DEFENSTAR LTD. NYALSAN II PLANETARY SHIELD GENERATOR

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance - Price (R) 460,000 Rarity 4|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/5/5c/Profile_DefenStar_Ltd._Nyalsan_II_Planetary_Shield_Generator.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance - Price (R) 460,000 Rarity 4

The Nyalsan II provides a ranged defense of 2 to each quadrant (north, south, east, and west) of a base. Ranged defense points can be reassigned to different quadrants by the shield generator's crew based on need, in a manner similar to the Angle Deflector Shields maneuver.[^2]
