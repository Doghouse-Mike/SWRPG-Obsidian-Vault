Tech recycling stations are used to speed up the time-consuming task of sifting through damaged or discarded technology to find items of value.

## OR-SERIES TECH RECYCLING STATION

Source: [^0-1]

![Encumbrance 10 Price 1,500 Rarity 5|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/3/31/Profile_OR-Series_Tech_Recycling_Station.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 10 Price 1,500 Rarity 5

A character using a tech recycling stations adds two [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] dice to [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] and [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics]] checks to scavenge parts or raw materials, and can complete such checks in half the time (or one quarter of the time with the [[Utinni! talent|Utinni!]] talent). Additionally, a character with a tech recycling station on hand may recover supplied worth half of the Material Price from any failed crafting check, to a maximum of the full value.
