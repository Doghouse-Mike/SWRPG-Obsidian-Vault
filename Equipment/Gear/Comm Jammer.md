## COMM JAMMER

Source: [^0-1][^2][^3]

![Encumbrance 4 Price 400 Rarity 3|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/8/8e/Profile_Comm_Jammer.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 4 Price 400 Rarity 3

Cutting through the noise created by a comm jammer requires a [[Computers (Int)|Computers]] check, with a difficulty proportional to the distance from the jammer and power of the device. The closer and more powerful the jammer, the harder the difficulty.[^0-1][^4][^1-5] The "Hail" forces an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] check**, which becomes more difficult at close ranges (the exact difficulty is up to the GM, but should increase to [[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard]] or [[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Daunting]] if at medium range or closer to the device).[^0-1][^1-5]
