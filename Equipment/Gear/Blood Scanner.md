Utilized for medical diagnostics, blood scanners are sensitive devices capable of scanning blood samples from nearly any species.

## BLOOD SCANNER

Source: [^0-1][^2]

![Encumbrance 1 Price 400 Rarity 5|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/e/e0/Profile_Blood_Scanner.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 1 Price 400 Rarity 5

As an action, a character with a blood scanner may make an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Easy difficulty]] [[Medicine (Int)|Medicine check]]** to identify foreign elements (including poisons) in a blood sample. In addition to allowing the character to treat any present toxins with the proper antidote, the extensive information about the patient's health adds two automatic [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results to the next [[Medicine (Int)|Medicine]] check the character makes to treat the patient.[^0-1][^3]
