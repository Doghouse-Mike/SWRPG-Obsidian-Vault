Fibra-line is synthetic rope manufactured in small, thin spools. The rope is fairly durable, consisting of three large fibrous "cables" that are tightlywoven together. These filaments are usually between six and eight centimetres in diameter. While fibra-line may seem flimsy, it is actually capable of supporting up to 750 kilograms of weight.
