Popular among pilots and vehicle crews, crash survival kits are a necessary precaution for those who regularly travel through inhospitable areas.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Crash_Survival_Kit).

**Models Include:** Pretormin Environmental Crash Survival Kit.

## CRASH SURVIVAL KIT

Source: [^0-1][^2][^3]

![Encumbrance 5 Price 300 Rarity 2|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/9/91/Profile_Crash_Survival_Kit.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 5 Price 300 Rarity 2

Most survival kits include a [[Thermal Cloak|thermal coat]], a [[Tool Kit|multitool]] or [[Explorer's Knife|survival knife]], a [[Saladar Systems PED-21 Distress Beacon|distress beacon]], two emergency [[Comlink|comlinks]], a spool of wire, ration bars, a basic [[Medpac|medpac]] (equivalent of the [[Emergency Medpac|GLiS Emergency Medpac]])[^1-4], two [[Breath Mask and Respirator|respirators]], a water jug with filter, a [[Glow Rod|glow rod]], fifty meters of [[Degan Explorations Syntherope|high-tensile microfiber line]], ten [[Field Ration Pack|ration packs]], and an emergency [[Luma Flare|flare gun]]. Military pilots, smugglers, and bounty hunters often include a [[Holdout Blaster|small-frame blaster pistol]] as well.[^0-1][^5][^1-4]
