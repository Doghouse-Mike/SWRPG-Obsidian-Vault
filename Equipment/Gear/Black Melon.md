Native to the Jundland Wastes of Tatooine, black melons have a brittle shell and contain a potable “milk”. Most humanoids find the milk repulsive, but it can provide life saving hydration during droughts. In Tusken culture it was extremely insulting to refuse an offer to share the beverage.
