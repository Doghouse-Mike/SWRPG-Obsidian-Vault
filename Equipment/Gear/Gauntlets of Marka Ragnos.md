Crafted from black scales of an unknown creature, these gloves were worn by one of the most powerful Sith of his time. The rage and fury Marka Ragnos imbued into these gauntlets as he slew countless Jedi with his lightsaber can still be felt emanating from them. Their dark presence tempts and corrupts Force users that wear them, slowly drawing them towards the dark side.

A Force-sensitive character wearing the Gauntlets of Marka Ragnos adds a [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] and [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result to any [[Lightsaber (Br)|Lightsaber]] check they make. When presented with the opportunity to lethally incapacitate a foe, they must make a successful **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]]** **[[Discipline (Will)|Discipline check]]** to resist doing so. At the start of a session, a character in possession of the gauntlets gains 1 Conflict.
