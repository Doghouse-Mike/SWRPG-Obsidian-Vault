Power fences are specialized shield emitters designed to provide perimeter protection for civilian and military installations.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/PSF_Mk_III_power_fence).

## PSF MK III POWER FENCE

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 10 Price 10,000 Rarity 7|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/8/85/Profile_PSF_Mk_III_Power_Fence.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 10 Price 10,000 Rarity 7

\[...\] It comes complete with a high-output PF.L-99 Barricade power fence generator and twenty amplification pylons, each four meters tall.[^0-2]

Power fences can be destroyed by either attacking the energy walls with ion weapons or by attacking the amplification pylons with regular energy or slugthrowing weapons. When attacked, power fences have the following profile: Defense 2, Armor 1, Wound Threshold (pylons) 15, Strain Threshold (energy wall) 20.[^0-2]
