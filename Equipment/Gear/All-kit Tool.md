One of the handiest tools a spacer can have on their belt, an all-kit is a type of all-purpose combination tool, that contains a torque wrench, arc probe and fusion cutter.

While the versions of these tools included in the all-kit are not as powerful as the standalone versions, for portability, for portability, the all-kit can’t be beat. Popular manufacturers of all-kit tools include; Noxtorv, Ishami Metals and Wallen Industrial.
