Yaladai spice has effects that last for one encounter. When a character under the effects of the spice suffers strain, \[they\] suffer three less strain, to a minimum of one. This does not apply to voluntary strain loss. Additionally, a Force user under the effects of yaladai spice can add a [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] die to any Force power checks \[they\] make. When \[they\] do so, however, the spice doubles any strain and Conflict suffered for using a [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|dark point]] to generate [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]].[^2]
