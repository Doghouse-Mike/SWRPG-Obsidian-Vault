An unleavened bread commonly seen throughout the Outer Rim, the thickness and denseness of gorgo loaf makes it ideal for workers needing energy during a long day.

While the basic recipe for gorgo loaf is simple, many planets and people add their own twist to the bread, based on the availability of certain ingredients or the significance of certain foodstuffs.
