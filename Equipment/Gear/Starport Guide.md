With new routes and planets seemingly opening daily for travel and trading, the galaxy can seem a wild and confusing place for travellers. That’s where Starport Guides come in. These guides contain maps and diagrams, detailed port information and useful tips for spacers and traders travelling throughout the galaxy.

These guides also often include a summary of local or Imperial spacefaring regulations in the area. Though they are ostensibly guides for legitimate traders and spacers, many smugglers and black marketeers find these guides invaluable when moving beneath the radar.
