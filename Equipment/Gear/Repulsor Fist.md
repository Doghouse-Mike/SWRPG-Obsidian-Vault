Also called a repulse–hand or a enforcer grip, this durasteel weapon cybernetic is typically neither covered in synthskin nor intendeded to appear natural.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Repulsor_fist).

**Models Include:** Illicit Electroniocs Juggernaut Mag Fist, Control Zone Ltd. Repulse-hand.

## REPULSOR FIST

Source: [^0-1][^2]

![Encumbrance – Price 4,750 Rarity 7|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/9/98/Profile_Repulsor_Fist.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance – Price 4,750 Rarity 7

A repulsor fist provides one rank of the [[Defensive Stance talent]] and counts as a Brawl weapon with a damage of 8, a critical rating of 3, and the [[Item Qualities#Concussive (Active)|Concussive]] 1 and [[Item Qualities#Slow-Firing (Passive)|Slow–Firing]] 2 qualities. While a character may still make Brawl attacks when this weapon is "recharging" due to the [[Item Qualities#Slow-Firing (Passive)|Slow–Firing]] quality, they do so without any benefits from the weapon—they simply make an unarmed Brawl attack.[^0-1][^3]
