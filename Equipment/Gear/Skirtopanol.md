Originally produced for Corellian Security (CorSec) intelligence agents by Chiewab Amalgamated Pharmaceuticals, Skirtopanol is a powerful truth serum used to coerce confessions out of suspects.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Skirtopanol).

## SKIRTOPANOL

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 0 Price (R) 500 Rarity 8|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/3/3a/Profile_Affide_Crystal.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 0 Price (R) 500 Rarity 8

Administered through injection\[.\]

One dose of Skirtopanol requires a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Resilience (Br)|Resilience check]]** to overcome. On a failed check, the drug inflicts 5 strain, and the difficulty of all checks the target makes to resist [[Charm (Pr)|Charm]] or [[Coercion (Will)|Coercion]] for the remainder of the encounter is upgraded once. In addition, each [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] result generated on the original check inflicts 2 strain on the target. Finally, the GM can spend a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result to make the target check against the drug again during the next round, as it remains in \[their\] system.
