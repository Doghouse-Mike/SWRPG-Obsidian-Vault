Laser torches and plasma cutters are standard tools across the galaxy, varying in price and effectiveness. Most are heavy and cumbersome, though smaller, lower yield units are found in machine shops across the galaxy. Salvagers most often use power-torches for cutting through doorways, walls, and decks. While bulkier than the standard F-187 Fusion Cutter, the 9-Alpha torch has an increased laser output and longer-lasting power cell.
