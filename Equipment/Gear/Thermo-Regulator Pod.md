This backpack sized pod is ideal for transporting organic materials that require a climate controlled environment. It can maintain an internal temperature between 50 and -10 Galactic Standard Degrees for up to two weeks on a single charge. Despite regular requests from disgruntled customers, they currently do not feature safety locks.
