An improvement on MicroData's versatile Pocket Secretary and the more common Versafunction88 datapad, the P-2 Pocket Attaché provides the user with a handheld multipurpose administrative tool and organizer combined with state-of-the-art security and encryption rivaling that of Imperial intelligence agencies.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/P-2_Pocket_Attach%C3%A9).

**Models Include:** MicroData P-2 Pocket Attaché, Versafunction108 Traveller datapad.
