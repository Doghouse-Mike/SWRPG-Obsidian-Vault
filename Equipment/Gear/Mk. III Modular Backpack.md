### MK. III MODULAR BACKPACK EXTRA POUCH

![Encumbrance – Price 15 Rarity 1|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/e/e8/Profile_Mk._III_Modular_Backpack_Extra_Pouch.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

The Mk. III modular backpack increases a wearer's encumbrance threshold by 2, plus an additional point of encumbrance threshold for each accessory pouch added to the backpack (to a maximum increase of 6).[^2]

```
The description of the item specifies that 6 pouches can be added, so the cumulative increase in encumbrance threshold would be 8, the maximum increase is referring to just "from pouches".
```
