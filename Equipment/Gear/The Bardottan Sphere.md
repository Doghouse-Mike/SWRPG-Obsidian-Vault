One of the most powerful objects ever encounter by the legendary Jedi Master Mace Windu was the Bardottan Sphere, a Force artifact designed to capture the Force from living beings and store it as though in some battery.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bardottan_Sphere).

## THE BARDOTTAN SPHERE

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance - Price 50,000 Rarity 10|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/d/d1/Profile_The_Bardottan_Sphere.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance - Price 50,000 Rarity 10

While the Sphere is in a character's possession, the character's Force rating increases by one. If the character sacrifices a being to store its life force in the Sphere, then the character's Force rating increases by the Force rating of the sacrificed being, as long as that creature's Force rating was 1 or higher. However, a character who uses the Sphere during a session gains 5 Conflict at the at the end of the session, in addition to any Conflict accrued due to any sacrifices the character makes to power the Sphere.[^0-2]
