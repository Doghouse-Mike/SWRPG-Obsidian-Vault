A character who chews a stimstick removes a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die from their [[Discipline (Will)|Discipline]], [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]], [[Resilience (Br)|Resilience]], or [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]] checks to stay awake or notice something due to fatigue or lack of sleep until the end of the encounter. At the end of the encounter, the character suffers 1 strain.[^0-1][^3]
