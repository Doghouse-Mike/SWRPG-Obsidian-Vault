## SURVIVAL EQUIPMENT INC. FIELD KITCHEN

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 5 Price 500 Rarity 4|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/c/c4/Profile_Survival_Equipment_Inc._Field_Kitchen.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 5 Price 500 Rarity 4

Setting up a field kitchen takes about thirty standard minutes. Thanks to the efficient design and the information included in the datapad, using a field kitchen grants two [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] dice to any [[Survival (Cun)|Survival]] or [[Education (Int)|Education]] skill checks made to store, identify, cook, or sterilize comestibles (and can, at the GM's discretion, allow the processing of foods that normally could not be made edible).[^2]
