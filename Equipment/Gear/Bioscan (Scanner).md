A bioscan is a device that scans for life-forms as well as checking their physical makeup for anomalies and identifiers and verifying their identity.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bioscan).

Using a bioscan to scan an area for life signs grants a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to any **opposed [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] checks vs [[Stealth (Ag)|Stealth]]**. Using the device to verify a person's identity requires an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Computers (Int)|Computers check]]** and a biometric identifier (such as DNA or a record of physiological characteristics) for comparison. The Game Master may modify this difficulty based upon any unusual anatomical characteristics of the target's species.[^0-1][^3]
