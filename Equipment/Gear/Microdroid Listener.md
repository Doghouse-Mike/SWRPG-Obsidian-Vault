## MICRODROID LISTENER

Source: [^0-1][^2]

![Encumbrance – Price (R) 1,500 Rarity 6|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/4/4f/Profile_Microdroid_Listener.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance – Price (R) 1,500 Rarity 6

Planting this device without being noticed requires an **opposed [[Skulduggery (Cun)|Skulduggery]] check vs. [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]]**. Once the bug is in place, a character must make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Daunting difficulty]] [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance check]]** to locate it even when they are specifically searching for one. The microdroid has no self-defense or evasive capabilities, although some manufacturers fit their models with a self-destruct in case of tampering.[^0-1][^3]
