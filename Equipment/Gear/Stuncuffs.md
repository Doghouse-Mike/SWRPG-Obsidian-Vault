## STUNCUFFS

Source: [^1][^2]

![Encumbrance — Price 100 Rarity 5|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/c/c0/Profile_Stuncuffs.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance — Price 100 Rarity 5

Breaking free from stuncuffs requires a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Daunting difficulty]] [[Athletics (Br)|Athletics]]** or **[[Coordination (Ag)|Coordination check]]**. The GM may spend two [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result on the check to have the character suffer 4 strain (this ignores soak).[^3] Similarly, a character holding the associated remote may trigger the stuncuffs to inflict 4 strain.[^4]
