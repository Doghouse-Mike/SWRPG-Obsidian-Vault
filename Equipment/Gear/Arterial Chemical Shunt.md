Common to the Guavian Death gang, an arterial chemical shunt worn on the leg acts as a second heart, injecting a mixture of chemicals that boost speed and aggressiveness. This also means any other drugs, chemicals, or poisons proliferate faster and with greater intensity.

## ARTERIAL CHEMICAL SHUNT

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance - Price 9,000 Rarity 8|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/a/a4/Profile_Arterial_Chemical_Shunt.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance - Price 9,000 Rarity 8

Characters with an arterial chemical shunt may suffer 1 strain as an incidental to add a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to any Agility check. They also add two [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice to any check to recover strain.

When this character is affected by drugs or poisons, the effect is doubled, but always ends after 1 round. If the substance has side effects, they function and last normally.
