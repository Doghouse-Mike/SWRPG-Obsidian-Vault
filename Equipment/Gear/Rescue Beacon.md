An essential item for anyone exploring the wildlands of Outer Rim worlds. Portable rescue beacons are worn on the wrist or on the belt and broadcast a location signal to another device, ensuring that the location of the rescue beacon and its wearer is always known. Cheaper units may broadcast a signal every 30 minutes and narrow the rescue beacon’s location to within a 3 km radius. More expensive models provide an up to the minute and specific location. Rescue beacons can also be hardwired into certain droid models.
