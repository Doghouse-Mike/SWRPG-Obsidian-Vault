## SYNTHETIC NEUROPARALYTIC

Source: [^0-1][^2][^3][^4]

![Encumbrance – Price (R) 75 Rarity 6|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/5/51/Profile_Synthetic_Neuroparalytic.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance – Price (R) 75 Rarity 6

This poison may only be introduced into a target via injection, as with an applicator or dart. Any dose size requires a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Resilience (Br)|Resilience check]]** to resist. The poison immobilizes the target for three rounds if the target fails the check. In addition, each [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] result may be spent to inflict 1 strain on the target. A result of [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] may be spent to make the target test against the poison again during the next round, as the poison remains in their system.[^0-1][^5][^6][^7]
