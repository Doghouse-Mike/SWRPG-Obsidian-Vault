Used to hide everything from campsites to supply caches to parked starships, camouflage netting is a necessary piece of equipment for the Warrior operating in the field.

**Models Include:** [[Fabritech CN-15 Camouflage Netting]], SoroSuub BLR-14 Concealment System.

## CAMOUFLAGE NETTING

Source: [^1]

(one roll)

Camouflage netting adds an automatic [[Narrative Dice#Failure|failure]] result to all checks made to detect items concealed by it. One roll of camo netting can conceal a single object up to silhouette 2, two rolls can conceal an object up to silhouette 3, and three rolls can conceal an object up to silhouette 4. Objects silhouette 5 and higher are too large to be effectively concealed.
