Usually taking the form of a prosthetic finger or other artificial appendage, a cyberjack allows its use to directly interface with a computer system via a universal serial socket.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Implanted_cyberjack).

**Models Include:** Neuro-Saav Technologies Cyber-Interface Unit, Illicit Electronics Cyberjack Package.

## IMPLANTED CYBERJACK

Source: [^0-1][^2]

![Encumbrance – Price 3,750 Rarity 6|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/c/cf/Profile_Implanted_Cyberjack.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance – Price 3,750 Rarity 6

Once per check, a character with an implanted cyberjack may suffer 2 strain to decrease the difficulty of a [[Computers (Int)|Computers]] check to disable a security device or slice into a computer system by one.[^0-1][^3]
