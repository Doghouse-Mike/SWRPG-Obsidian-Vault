Data disks are small, coin-sized coding devices. They are metallic stepped cone shapes that appear to be three disks stacked on top of one another. Commonly seen on the caps and belts of high ranking Imperial officers and contain coded identification information.

Data disks are not limited to Imperial use however, and these small devices can be seen galaxy-wide, employed by trillions of beings in countless different scenarios.
