A popular game of chance, throughout the galaxy, sabacc is difficult to master and even more difficult to cheat at.

**Models Include:** Numerous variants.

## MARKED SABACC DECK

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 1 Price (R) 500 Rarity 6|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/1/19/Profile_Marked_Sabacc_Deck.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 1 Price (R) 500 Rarity 6

Players using a marked sabacc deck gain a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to [[Deception (Cun)|Deception]] checks when gambling. Noticing that a deck is marked requires a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]]** or **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Daunting difficulty]] [[Perception (Cun)|Perception check]]**, depending on the quality of the deck (a higher quality marked sabacc deck costs 1,000 credits).
