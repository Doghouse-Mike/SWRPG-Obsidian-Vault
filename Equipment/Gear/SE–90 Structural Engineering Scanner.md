## SE–90 STRUCTURAL ENGINEERING SCANNER

Source: [^1][^2]

![Encumbrance 1 Price 675 Rarity 6|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/f/f0/Profile_SE%E2%80%9390_Structural_Engineering_Scanner.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text

Using an SE–90 requires an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Computers (Int)|Computers check]]** modified according to construction materials and environmental interference per the GM's discretion. A character who succeeds can see through walls or bulkheads up to short range until the end of the encounter or the scanner is moved.
