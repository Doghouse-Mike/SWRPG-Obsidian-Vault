A high in the sky idea by many sharpshooters who use blasters, these pads have been released by a number of ‘market edge’ blaster companies. Largely a laughing stock, these small corporate entities swear by these reflective pads and assure buyers that they perfectly and fully reflect a blaster bolt with its full power. The issue is that the degree of skill needed to use them simply makes them appear as useless. With enough reflex and cunning, however, a skilled sniper can put these to good use for firing around cover.

## REFLECTIVE PADS

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 0 Price 3 Rarity 7|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/4/40/Profile_Reflective_Pads.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 0 Price 3 Rarity 7

As an incidental on their turn, the character can make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]]** **[[Perception (Cun)|Perception check]]** and identify a location within Short range to throw the sticky pad to which will allow them to gain a visual on a target as far as Medium range around cover. As a maneuver, on a successful check, they may make an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]]** **[[Ranged - Light (Ag)|Ranged (Light) check]]** to throw the pads to the specified location. With a pad successfully placed, the character can make an attack against their desired target behind cover but increases and upgrades the difficulty of the attack once. Each of the checks using these pads gain a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die for each [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] result generated on a prior check, and increase the difficulty of subsequent checks on three [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results or [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result.
