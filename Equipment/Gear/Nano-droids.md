## NANO-DROIDS

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance — Price (R) 50,000 Rarity 9|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/7/7c/Profile_Nano-droids.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance — Price (R) 50,000 Rarity 9

Add three [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice to a character's [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] checks to notice nano-droids, unless the searcher knows to look for them and has an appropriate scanner.[^0-2]

Nano-droids can be reprogrammed to explode. When activated, they deal 20 damage with the [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] 1 item quality to everything within short range, including the object in which they are contained.[^0-2]
