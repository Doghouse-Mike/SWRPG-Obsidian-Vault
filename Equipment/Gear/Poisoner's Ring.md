## POISONER'S RING

Source: [^0-1][^2]

![Encumbrance – Price (R) 500 Rarity 7|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/0/0b/Profile_Poisoner%27s_Ring.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance – Price (R) 500 Rarity 7

A poisoner's ring can hold a single dose of any [[Category - Drugs & Poisons|poison]]. As an action, a character wearing the ring may make an **opposed [[Skulduggery (Cun)|Skulduggery]] check vs. [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]]** against any onlookers to deposit the contents into a target's meal or beverage without being noticed.[^0-1][^3]
