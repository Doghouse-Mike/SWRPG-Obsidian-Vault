Binders are simple restraints used to keep prisoners subdued and manageable.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Stuncuffs).

**Models Include:** LSS Bind-1.

Breaking free from a set of binders requires either brute force or feats of agility and flexibility. The difficulty varies by the make and model of binder, but the most common restraints require a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Daunting difficulty]] [[Athletics (Br)|Athletics]]** or **[[Coordination (Ag)|Coordination check]]** to escape.[^0-1][^5][^6][^7]
