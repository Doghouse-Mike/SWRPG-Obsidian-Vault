Popular with weapon crews, squad support gunners, and anyone who makes a living behind the sights of a heavy portable or vehicle-mounted weapon, Farbitech's AG/1 Mk III targeting goggles are the best–selling targeting aid on the galactic market.

## AG/1 MK III TARGETING GOGGLES *OR* AG/L

Source: [^0-1][^2]

![Encumbrance 1 Price 1,000 Rarity 5|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/b/b4/Profile_AG1_Mk_III_Targeting_Goggles.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text

Wearing AG/1[^0-1] or AG/l[^1-3] Mk III targeting goggles allows the character to count the silhouette of their target as one greater when making [[Gunnery (Ag)|Gunnery]] checks against a starship or vehicle.[^0-1][^1-3]
