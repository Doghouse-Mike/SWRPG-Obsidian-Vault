Lando Calrissian has a fondness for capes, and he keeps dozens in a large cape armoire aboard the Millennium Falcon.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cape).

## LANDO CALRISSIAN'S CAPE ARMOIRE

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 10 Price 10,000 Rarity 10|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/3/38/Profile_Lando_Calrissian%27s_Cape_Armoire.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 10 Price 10,000 Rarity 10

If a single cape is found, it weighs a base of 1 encumbrance, has a rarity of 6, and its price starts at 900 credits. Game Masters should determine if these statistics are appropriate for the specific cape in question.

**Dressed for Success:** Once per session, the owner of Lando's armoire may make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Perception (Cun)|Perception check]]** to select a flattering cape. If successful, they can upgrade one Charm check they make during the next encounter while wearing the selected cape. The wearer may spend three advantage results or a triumph result from the Perception checks to upgrade all Charm checks they make during the encounter once, instead.

See the table below for a list of some of the capes PCs might find on their travels. A GM may choose to roll a d100 to randomly determine what cape is discovered.
