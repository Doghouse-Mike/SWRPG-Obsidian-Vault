One of the benefits of Bounty Hunter’s Guild membership is the free use of tracking fobs. It is rare for unaffiliated bounties offer such assistive devices. A fob can be attuned to an individual’s biometric signal by inputing their chain code, providing short range tracking data. While this certainly make it easier to hunt down targets they are limited by their 100 km effective range.

## TRACKING FOB

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 0 Price 350 Rarity 5|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/2/2e/Profile_Tracking_Fob.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 0 Price 350 Rarity 5

Acts like a compass, pointing toward the programmed individual if they are within range. They are generally loaned to bounty hunters for the duration of a hunt. Some individuals opt to simply purchase their own fobs for personal use, but programing them requires knowledge of the desired target’s chain code.
