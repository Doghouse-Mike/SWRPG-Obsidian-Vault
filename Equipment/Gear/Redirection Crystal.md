Redirection crystals are an invention of the Imperial Security Bureau.

> More information about the weapon available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Redirection_crystal).

### REDIRECTION CRYSTAL

Source: [^1]

Setting up a redirection crystal requires a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Skulduggery (Cun)|Skulduggery check]]**. Due to its size, hitting a redirection crystal requires a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Ranged - Light (Ag)|Ranged (Light)]]** or **[[Ranged - Heavy (Ag)|Ranged (Heavy) check]]**. Redirection crystals have the following profile: (Damage 9; Critical 4; Range \[Short\]; Limited Ammo 1). They cost 500 credits (R) and have a rarity of 9.

[^1]: [[Category - Spy|Cyphers and Masks]]: *A Sourcebook for Spies* (Page 39)
