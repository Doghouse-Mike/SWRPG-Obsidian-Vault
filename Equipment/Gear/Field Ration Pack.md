Ration packs are typically bland, flavorless affairs that contain an entire meal packed with necessary nutrients and vitamins in a small, easily portable bag or box.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ration_pack).

**Models Include:** Adventure Hiker and Hunter Trail Rations, Chiewab Nutrition ReadiMeal, [[Imperial Army Field Rations|Imperial Army Ration Packs]].
