Generally a pair of six-sided dice, Vision Cubes are not all that different from [[Chance Cubes]].

## VISION CUBES

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 0 Price 1,000 Rarity 6|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/0/09/Profile_Vision_Cubes.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 0 Price 1,000 Rarity 6

A character can use a vision cube while performing a check (including a Force power check) to divine the future. If the check results in having at least one uncancelled [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result, the character adds a [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] result and [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Light point]] to the results as well. If the check results in having at least one uncancelled [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] result, the character instead adds a [[Narrative Dice#Failure|failure]] result and [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|Dark point]] to the results. If the check has no [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] or [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results, the vision cubes do nothing—except potentially look impressive.[^2]
