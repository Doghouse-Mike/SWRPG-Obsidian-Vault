Microaxial LLC is a relatively small technology firm from the Core Worlds region that specializes in navicomputers and navigational aids.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Navigation_computer).

## MICROAXIAL STARMAPPER HANDHELD NAVICOMPUTER

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 1 Price 3,500 Rarity 8|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/0/0c/Profile_Microaxial_Starmapper_Handheld_Navicomputer.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 1 Price 3,500 Rarity 8

Using a Starmapper Handheld Navicomputer grants a character two [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] dice to all [[Astrogation (Int)|Astrogation]] checks. Game Masters can reduce this to one [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die or eliminate the bonus altogether if the character using the Starmapper has been out of HoloNet range for an extended period of time.[^2]
