Leia Organa of Alderaan faces immense pressure to be both brilliant and elegant at all times.

## LEIA ORGANA'S DIPLOMATIC WARDROBE

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 10 Price 24,000 Rarity 10|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/4/42/Profile_Leia_Organa%27s_Diplomatic_Wardrobe.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 10 Price 24,000 Rarity 10

If a single outfit is found, it weighs a base of 1 encumbrance, has a rarity of 6, and its price starts at 1,700 credits. Game Masters should determine if these statistics are appropriate for the specific outfit in questions.

**Prepared to Lead:** Once per session, the owner of Leia Organa's Diplomatic Wardrobe may make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Category - Knowledge Skills|Knowledge]]** or **[[Perception (Cun)|Perception check]]** to curate an outfit. If successful, select one social skill and add a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to all checks the wearer makes during the next encounter. The wearer may spend two [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results to increase the bonus to two [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] dice, or may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result to select one additional social skill.

See the table below for a selection of Leia's outfits PCs might find on their travels. A GM may choose to roll a d100 to randomly determine what outfit is discovered.
