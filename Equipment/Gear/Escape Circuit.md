## ESCAPE CIRCUIT

Source: [^0-1][^2]

![Encumbrance – Price (R) 1,000 Rarity 6|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/4/4b/Profile_Escape_Circuit.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance – Price (R) 1,000 Rarity 6

An escape circuit is a cybernetic implant that can only be installed in droids. A droid installed with an escape circuit can resist a [[Restraining Bolt|restraining bolt]] with an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Easy difficulty]]** **[[Discipline (Will)|Discipline check]]** instead of the normal difficulty. The droid may spend two [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results from a check made to resist a restraining bolt to overload the bolt, rendering it entirely useless.[^0-1][^3]
