Riding tack is a catchall term for the assorted bits of equipment needed to ride or control a beast.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Saddle).

## RIDING TACK

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 3 Price 100 Rarity 4|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/5/51/Profile_Riding_Tack.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 3 Price 100 Rarity 4

Tack can only be worn by riding or working animals. Using tack allows a character to ride the beast without suffering the bareback riding penalties.[^2]
