## A99 CUSTOM BIOME GENERATOR

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 45 Price 9,000 Rarity 4|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/a/a4/Profile_A99_Custom_Biome_Generator.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 45 Price 9,000 Rarity 4

Equipped with powerful chemical analyzers and synthesizers, the A99 can produce comfortable, safe environments for even the most stringent biological requirements. When installed, they are commonly integrated into existing ventilation and temperature regulation systems, allowing the A99 to replicate nearly any biome in the universe.
