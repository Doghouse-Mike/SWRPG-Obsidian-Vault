Made by purveyors of animal-handling gear such as VeTech and Piccatech, saddlebags are indispensable pieces of equipment for those who make their livings riding or working with beasts.

## SADDLEBAGS

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance – Price 25 Rarity 4|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/6/62/Profile_Saddlebags.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance – Price 25 Rarity 4

Saddlebags can only be worn by riding animals, They increase an animal's encumbrance threshold by 4.[^2]
