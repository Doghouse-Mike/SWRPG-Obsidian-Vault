Data breakers are common tools used by slicers to force their way into secure systems.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Data_breaker).

**Models Include:** Numerous variants.

**Restrictions:** The Empire and many planetary governments restrict possession of data-breaking technology. Military and law-enforcement organizations make legal use of these devices, but typically civilian use of such technology is prohibited.

## BLACKOPS DATA BREAKER *OR* OUTLAW TECH DATA BREAKER

Source: [^2-1][^0-2]

![Encumbrance 1 Price (R) 1,000 Rarity 6|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/3/3b/Profile_BlackOps_Data_Breaker.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 1 Price (R) 1,000 Rarity 6

Data breakers add two [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] dice to any [[Computers (Int)|Computers]] check made to slice computers, but they also add a boost die to any attempts to locate use of a data breaker in a computer system due to the "noise" these devices make.[^3-3]

Droid slicers can choose to have a data breaker (or equivalent systems) installed internally at the same cost. Data breakers add two [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] dice to any [[Computers (Int)|Computers]] check made to slice computers (as opposed to the usual one for having the right tools for the job).[^0-2]
