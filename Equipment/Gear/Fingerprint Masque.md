**Models Include:** Numerous variants.

## FINGERPRINT MASQUE

Source: [^0-1][^1-2][^3]

![Encumbrance – Price (R) 1,000 Rarity 7|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/6/60/Profile_Fingerprint_Masque.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance – Price (R) 1,000 Rarity 7

Fingerprint masques can fool basic fingerprint readers with nothing more than an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Easy difficulty]] [[Cool (Pr)|Cool]]** or **[[Deception (Cun)|Deception check]]**, while more advanced scanners require an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average]]** or **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] check**, at the GM's discretion. If the biometric scanner operator has reason to be suspicious of a character wearing a fingerprint masque, it may require an **opposed social skill check vs. [[Computers (Int)|Computers]]** against the operator.[^0-1][^1-2][^2-4] A character must make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Daunting difficulty]] [[Perception (Cun)|Perception check]]** to actually notice the physical presence of a fingerprint masque on an individual's fingers.[^1-2][^2-4]
