Only the size of a stylus, this miniaturised technology contained a small battery capable of discharging a localised EMP burst that would affect only the closest electronic systems. Originally designed for use by technicians working in tight environments for circuitry repairs, the disruptor has gained popularity among the spec ops groups and nefarious criminal elements of the galaxy to assist in slicing attempts.
