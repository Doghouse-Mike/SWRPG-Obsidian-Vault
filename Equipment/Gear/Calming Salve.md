## CALMING SALVE

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 0 Price 25 Rarity 3|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/d/db/Profile_Calming_Salve.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 0 Price 25 Rarity 3

Once per session as an action, a Force-sensitive character may use the Calming Salve and make a Force power check. They may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]] generated from [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Light point]] results to recover one strain per [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]] spent this way.
