The number of different musical traditions across the galaxy is truly staggering.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Musical_instruments).

**Anklet-chimes:** These small, clear-toned chimes are secured to the performer's ankels with a length of string or chain, tinkling softly as the performer moves.

**Bandfill:** Bandfills are complicated wail instruments featuring several horn bells.

**Chin-harp:** Developed by the Weequay, the chin-harp creates music by striking notes with a small chord hammer.

**Common Instruments:** Most cultures invent some form of horns, drums, and reed instruments as part of their development.

**Floonorp:** Scratch-built musical instruments made out of spare Podracer parts, floonorps are more likely to be assembled than purchased.

**Kloo horn:** Double-reeded wind instruments, kloo horns are commonly used by jatz musicians and create that music's distinctive higher notes.
