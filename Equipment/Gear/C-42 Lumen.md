More powerful than a civilian glow rod, the C-42 Lumen was developed by Glo-corp, one of the galaxy’s preeminent glow rod manufacturers, exclusively for security markets. Its bright beam is especially useful at cutting through smoke, mist and fog and can be attached to a number of blaster weapons.
