Larger, sturdier, and more powerful than standard handheld comlinks, these military-grade communication devices are built to withstand the rigors of battlefield use.

**Models Include:** [[ShockHold Hardened Comlink|TaggeCo Shockhold Hardened Comlink]], Naboo Technologies Secure-A3 Comlink.
