Typically built into a helmet or a pair of goggles, combat scanners provide users with indispensable real-time battlefield data.

**Models Include:** Fabritech AF-q.1a Squad Support Scanner, Chedak ADC Mk II Battlefield Data Suite.

## COMBAT SCANNER

Source: [^1][^2]

![Encumbrance 1 Price 2,000 Rarity 6|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/3/3b/Profile_Combat_Scanner.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 1 Price 2,000 Rarity 6

Combat scanners remove two [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice added to the user's checks due to darkness. Enemies add two [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice to any checks they make to intercept or decrypt data broadcast from the unit.[^0-3]

The Mark 6 offers some of the features of a general purpose scanner, scanner goggles for night-vision capability, and a com scrambler. This means it allows the user to see normally in dark conditions, can be set to track motion, heat, and metal, and adds two [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice to any checks made to attempt to decode and understand transmissions from the combat scanner to other combat scanners.[^4]
