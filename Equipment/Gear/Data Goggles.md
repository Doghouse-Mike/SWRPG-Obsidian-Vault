These goggles overlay the wearer's field of vision with additional information.

## DATA GOGGLES

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 1 Price 250 Rarity 1|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/a/a5/Profile_Data_Goggles.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 1 Price 250 Rarity 1

At the beginning of an encounter, a character may select one [[Category - Knowledge Skills|Knowledge skill]]. For the remainder of the encounter, the character adds an automatic [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] result to all checks \[they\] make using that skill while wearing the data goggles. The GM may rule that a character needs to obtain specialized data files to upload into the data goggles before using them.[^2]
