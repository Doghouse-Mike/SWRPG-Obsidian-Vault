## ASHES OF MALACHOR

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 0 Price (R) 750 Rarity 8|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/0/0d/Profile_Ashes_of_Malachor.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text

A Force-sensitive character may spend a maneuver to inhale the Ashes of Malachor. They gain 5 Conflict but add a [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] result and [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] result to all attacks they make until the end of the encounter.
