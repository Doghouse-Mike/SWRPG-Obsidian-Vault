The extra-vehicular activity (EVA) powersuit is a fully sealed powered spacesuit designed to facilitate external starship repairs in vacuum.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/EVA_powersuit).

## EVA POWERSUIT

Source: [^0-1][^2]

![Encumbrance 12 Price 1,500 Rarity 7|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/4/42/Profile_EVA_Powersuit.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 12 Price 1,500 Rarity 7

A character in an EVA powersuit ignores penalties for moving in zero gravity. Using the suit's inbuilt thrusters, they can effectively "fly" in zero-g. The EVA powersuit has a built-in arc welder, small cutting laser, and repair tools, and it counts as having a [[Tool Kit|tool kit]] for making repairs.[^0-1][^3]
