Another sensor suite produced by CryonCorp, the GPSP is a powerful ground-penetrating radar suite marketed to companies involved in gas and minerals exploration and chartered search and rescue.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ground-penetrating_sensor_pack).
