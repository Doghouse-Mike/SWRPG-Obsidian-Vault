Unusual for cybernetic implants, a biofeedback regulator achieves nothing for the recipient when installed on its own.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Biofeedback_regulator).

## BIOFEEDBACK REGULATOR

Source: [^1][^2]

![Encumbrance – Price 3,000 Rarity 8|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/c/c0/Profile_Biofeedback_Regulator.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance – Price 3,000 Rarity 8

A biofeedback regulator increases the character's cybernetic implant cap by two. It does not itself count toward the character's cybernetic implant cap. Biofeedback regulators cannot be installed on droids, and a character can only have a single biofeedback regulator installed.
