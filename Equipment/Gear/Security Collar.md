More commonly known as slave collars, the security collars used by Thalassian slavers are technologically sophisticated pieces of equipment despite their simple function.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Security_collar).

**Models Include:** Numerous variants.

## SECURITY COLLAR

Source: [^1][^2]

![Encumbrance 1 Price (R) 300 Rarity 5|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/a/a9/Profile_Security_Collar.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 1 Price (R) 300 Rarity 5

Safely removing a Thalassian security collar without use of the proper key requires a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Daunting difficulty]] [[Skulduggery (Cun)|Skulduggery check]]**. If a character fails this check, the wearer suffers 2 strain and 1 wound (this ignores soak) and becomes disoriented for five rounds.
