Clawdites adjust their shape by moving fluid through their body, and reorganizing the structures on their skin's surface.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Automated_saline_pump).

## SALINE PUMP

Source: [^0-1]

![Encumbrance 1 Price 2,500 Rarity 7|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/5/55/Profile_Saline_Pump.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 1 Price 2,500 Rarity 7

A [[Clawdite]] wearing a saline pump can increase or decrease his mass and size by as much as a tenth, enabling \[them\] to shape-change into a broader range of forms. Add [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to the Clawdite's checks to pass \[themself\] off as another person using \[their\] species ability, and add a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die to other characters' checks to the Clawdite's physiological deception. Non-Clawdites gain no benefit to disguise from this item (and would likely find it exceedingly uncomfortable to use).[^2]
