## ELECTRO-SNARE

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 1 Price 50 Rarity 5|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/a/a1/Profile_Electro-Snare.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 1 Price 50 Rarity 5

To use an electro-snare, an individual must make a **[[Survival (Cun)|Survival check]]** to set and conceal the snare with a difficulty set by the Game Master based on surrounding environmental factors. Once successfully hidden, any animal or sentient approaching the snare must make an **[[Perception (Cun)|Opposed Perception check]]** against the [[Survival (Cun)|Survival]] skill of the character who originally set the snare. If the snare remains undetected, it fires a powerful stun blast at the target once it enters engaged range, automatically hitting and dealing 10 stun damage (the damage is applied to the target's strain threshold but does not ignore soak), plus 2 additional damage per [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] result scored on the initial [[Survival (Cun)|Survival]] check.[^2]
