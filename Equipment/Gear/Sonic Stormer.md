Designed primarily with scouts and hunters in mind, sonic stomrers have also been adapted for use in settlements and camps in remote areas. When activated, these hand-sized sonic devices generate waves of high frequency sound-inaudible to humans and many aliens- that wards off most creatures and beasts in the vicinity, before they become a threat. While these are very useful, some creatures-especially tenacious predators- can become enraged by certain sounds generated by the sonic stormer (This can occur immediately or after prolonged exposure, depending on the creature.)
