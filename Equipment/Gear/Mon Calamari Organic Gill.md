Despite their amphibious nature, the Mon Calamari have long made use of a symbiotic blob to breathe underwater for extended periods of time.

## MON CALAMARI ORGANIC GILL

Source: [^0-1][^2]

![Encumbrance 1 Price 200 Rarity 5|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/3/3d/Profile_Mon_Calamari_Organic_Gill.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 1 Price 200 Rarity 5

When placed over an individual's nose and mouth (or equivalent), the symbiote filters oxygen from the ocean water. Organic gills are capable of lasting for several weeks in the water before they begin to die.[^0-1][^1-3]

Wearing an organic gill prevents speech, but this drawback can be alleviated by the introduction of small, waterproof microphones and earphones into the blob. Most species can wear the organic gill easily, as long as they breathe an atmosphere similar to that of the Mon Calamari. Sullustans, however, have demonstrated an allergic reaction to the gills, which prevents their species from employing them.[^0-1][^1-3]
