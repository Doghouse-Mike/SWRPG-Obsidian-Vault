Restraining bolts are to droids what binders are to sentient creatures.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Restraining_bolt).

**Models Include:** Industrial Automaton Master Control Restraining Bolt, Numerous Variants.

## RESTRAINING BOLT

Source: [^0-1][^2][^3][^4]

![Encumbrance – Price 35 Rarity 0|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/0/0e/Profile_Restraining_Bolt.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance – Price 35 Rarity 0

Most droids cannot withstand a restraining bolt. However, Player Character droids are made of sterner stuff than their NPC comrades and can, with the right amount of effort, overcome a restraining bolt. PC droids may make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Daunting difficulty]] [[Discipline (Will)|Discipline check]]** to shake off the effects of a restraining bolt and act normally after one is applied. If the check fails, they may make another attempt at a later time, at the GM's discretion.[^0-1][^5][^6][^7]
