An essential part of any explosive ordnance disposal (EOD) trooper's gear, this kit is small (roughly thirteen centimeters long closed) and constructed of nonreactive, nonconductive materials.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Demolitions_kit).

## DEMOLITIONS TOOL KIT

Source: [^1]

A demolitions tool kit counts as a [[Tool Kit|tool kit]] for checks involving preparing, setting, or defusing explosives. When using it for these checks, the wielder may spend four [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results to remove a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result.[^2]
