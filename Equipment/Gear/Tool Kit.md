Tool kits offer their users a wide variety of tools necessary for mechanical jobs.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tool_kit).

**Models Include:** Regalis Engineering QuickFix 20-Piece Portable Tool Kit, Regalis Engineering 60-Piece Ship's Engineering Tool Kit, Numerous Variants.
