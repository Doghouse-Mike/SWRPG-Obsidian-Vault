Althoug blaster weapons generally have large clips, it is a good idea to carry some reloads if those clips run out.

**Models Include:** Numerous variants.

## EXTRA RELOAD

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

![Encumbrance 1 Price 25 Rarity 1|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/f/f2/Profile_Breath_Mask_and_Respirator.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 1 Price 25 Rarity 1

Extra reloads allow a character to overcome an "out of ammo" [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result with a ranged weapon. By spending a maneuver, a character can switch out a power pack or reload a weapon back into the action.[^4][^5][^6]
