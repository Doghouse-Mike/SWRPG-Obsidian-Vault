Neuro-Saav's cyborg/droid interface is a supplementary cybernetic that must be used in conjunction with a [[Cybernetic Brain Implant|cybernetic brain implant]].

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cyborg/Droid_Interface).

## NEURO-SAAV CYBORG/DROID INTERFACE

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance – Price 600 Rarity 4|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/e/ec/Profile_Neuro-Saav_CyborgDroid_Interface.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance – Price 600 Rarity 4

Not only does this device let a cyborg access hidden information stored in a droid's memory banks, but it also allows it, when attempting to access information that a hostile droid does not wish to reveal, to upgrade the ability of all its [[Computers (Int)|Computers]] checks once.[^2]
