## ELYSIUM MODEL 2 ATMOSPHERIC PROCESSOR

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 10,000 Price 4,500,000 Rarity 7|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/9/9e/Profile_Elysium_Model_2_Atmospheric_Processor.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

\

Powered by a planet's natural subsurface heat and energy, the Model 2 resembles a massive ziggurat: a stepped, flat-topped pyramid full of systems designed to produce breathable atmospheres, clean water, and even weather patterns from the raw materials at hand. Roughly the size of a star cruiser, the Elysium Model 2 requires a crew of at least one hundred engineers and technicians, not to mention numerous droids to work in areas unfit for their organic colleagues.[^2]
