## TEAR OF THE ELDERS

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance - Price 7,500 Rarity 9|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/5/56/Profile_Tears_of_the_Elders.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance - Price 7,500 Rarity 9

Once per game session, when a Force-sensitive character holds the Tear of the Elders, the character may make a Force power check. As part of this check, the character may spend two [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|points]] to detect one threat the PC is likely to encounter during the current game session. The GM has the final say as to what this threat is and how the character perceives it. The Force user could see the threat clearly, or in a metaphorical sense. A battalion of stormtroopers, for instance, might appear as a solid white wall, impossible to penetrate, while a dark side Force user might manifest as a figure formed out of storm clouds, lightning flickering malevolently within.
