CryonCorp is a relative newcomer to the galactic technology market, having been founded in the last decade of the Clone Wars.

## CRYONCORP MK. I CAT'S EYE MULTIPURPOSE SENSOR SUITE

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance - Price (R) 2,750 Rarity 5|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/9/92/Profile_CryonCorp_Mk._I_Cat%27s_Eye_Multipurpose_Sensor_Suite.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance - Price (R) 2,750 Rarity 5

The Cat's Eye Multipurpose Sensor Suite uses the planetary range scale and has a range of long. It adds two [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] dice to any [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]], [[Computers (Int)|Computers]], or [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]] checks its operators make to detect incoming orbital air traffic within its range, and it adds two [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice to [[Stealth (Ag)|Stealth]] checks made by land units approaching the device and operator.[^2]
