Whether for relatively tame reasons such as traveling incognito, or more nefarious reasons such as government infiltration, the demand for fake credentials has remained strong.

**Models Include:** Numerous variants.

## FALSIFIED CREDENTIALS

Source: [^0-1][^1-2][^3]

![Encumbrance – Price (R) 800 Rarity 7|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/a/a8/Profile_Falsified_Credentials.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance – Price (R) 800 Rarity 7

False credentials can fool most cursory examinations, and characters using scanners require a successful **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Computers (Int)|Computers check]]** to recognize the credentials as fakes. Attempting to detect fake credentials without appropriate equipment requires a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Daunting difficulty]] [[Perception (Cun)|Perception check]]**.[^0-1][^1-2][^4]
