## "QUICKFLASH" BURNING GEL

Source: [^0-1][^2]

![Encumbrance 1 Price 150 Rarity 5|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/d/d5/Profile_Quickflash_Burning_Gel.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 1 Price 150 Rarity 5

Exposure to burning gel deals damage to materials and sentient beings as if it were a weapon. It deals 10 damage and has the [[Item Qualities#Burn (Active)|Burn]] 5 and [[Item Qualities#Pierce (Passive)|Pierce]] 5 weapon qualities. Using burning gel requires no skill check. However, placing it effectively might, at the GM's discretion, require a [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics]] check.[^0-1][^3]
