## COM-SCAN

Source: [^0-1]

![Encumbrance 10 Price (R) 5,000 Rarity 6|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/e/e8/Profile_Com-Scan.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 10 Price (R) 5,000 Rarity 6

Using a com-scan, a character can triangulate the location and movement of a known comlink or a transceiver with an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Computers (Int)|Computers check]]**. The device functions out to close range on planetary scale. Prevailing atmospheric conditions or excessive background signals can reduce its efficacy.
