Republic Seal of Service refers to a number of similar awards granted to those who have proven themselves to be exemplars of the Republic's ideals and paragons of service and duty.

## REPUBLIC SEAL OF SERVICE

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 0 Price Special Rarity 9|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/5/56/Profile_Republic_Seal_of_Service.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 0 Price Special Rarity 9

Characters who have been awarded a Republic Seal of Service add a [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] result to all checks involving formal Republic proceedings, proof of identity, or verification of past actions. Presenting a Seal of Service may also upgrade the ability of checks the character makes to impress or persuade those loyal to the republic, at the GM's discretion.

Republic Seals of Service cannot be purchased on the open market. Instead, the seals are awarded by a Senatorial committee for exemplary service to the Republic. The rarity value indicates the relative infrequency of encountering these seals.
