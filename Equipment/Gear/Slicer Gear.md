Slicers, talented computer jockeys who navigate the labyrinthine guts of computer code and mainframe systems as easily as an ace pilot navigates through an asteroid belt, use specially constructed gear to gain access to secure computer systems.

### THE BASICS OF SLICING EQUIPMENT

Source: [^0-1]

\[...\] Some form of slicing tool is needed to attempt slicing at all. This can be a temporary tool like a [[Dataspike|dataspike]], but is more commonly a permanent set of slicer gear. At the GM's discretion, a slicer might be able to break into a relatively unsecured system with little more than a datapad and some ingenuity, although attempting the job with limited resources could impose one or more [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice on the check.
