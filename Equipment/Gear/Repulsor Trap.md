These traps for sentient beings subvert repulsorlift technology to create localized patches of extremely high gravity.

## REPULSOR TRAP

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 7 Price 5,000 Rarity 9|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/5/5f/Profile_Repulsor_Trap.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 7 Price 5,000 Rarity 9

Repulsor traps are unobtrusive, adding two [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice to all [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] checks made to notice these devices. Any single target who comes within engaged range of an active repulsor trap is ensnared for six rounds.
