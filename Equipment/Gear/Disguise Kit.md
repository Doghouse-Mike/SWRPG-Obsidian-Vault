Disguise kits are simple collections of makeup, wigs, hairpieces, prosthetic features, and chromatic iris alternators; some even contain programmable skinweave for altering fingerprints and DNA cloaks that spoof advanced genetic scanners.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Disguise_kit).

**Models Include:** LV Labs Flex-Mask Disguise System, Mehrak Corporation AlterEgo Street Makeup Kit, Mehrak Corporation QuickChange Theatrical Makeup, Numerous Variants.
