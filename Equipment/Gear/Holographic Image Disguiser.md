Also known as holoshrouds, holographic disguisers (HIDs) use a small, self-contained holoprojector to create a holographic disguise for a single person or object.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Holographic_image_disguiser).

## HOLOGRAPHIC IMAGE DISGUISER

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 2 Price 1,100 Rarity 6|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/5/58/Profile_HoloNet_Homing_Beacon.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 2 Price 1,100 Rarity 6

A holographic disguise needs to be the same size as the target or larger. Detecting an HID with the naked eye requires a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Daunting difficulty]] [[Perception (Cun)|Perception check]]**. Droids and anyone viewing the target through a scanner, electrobinoculars, or other technological optical device require only an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Perception (Cun)|Perception check]]**.
