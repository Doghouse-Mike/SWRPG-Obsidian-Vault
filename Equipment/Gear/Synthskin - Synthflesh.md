Synthskin and synthflesh are two versions of a synthetic, skin-like covering used in medicine and the production of cybernetic limbs.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Synthskin).

**Models Include:** BioTech FastFlesh Synthskin Slap Patch, Numerous Variants.
