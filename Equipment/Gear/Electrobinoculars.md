Electrobinoculars are, at their most basic, a pair of macrobinoculars with one or more integrated optical enhancement systems, such as long-range digital zoom, passive light amplification, thermal imaging, ultrasound imaging, and the like.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Electrobinoculars).

**Models Include:** Neuro-Saav Model TD2.3 Electrobinoculars, Fabritech "Longsight" Electrobinoculars, TaggeCo Model 3 Imperial Army Electrobinoculars.

Electrobinoculars allow their user to see normally in low light, extremely bright conditions, and inclement conditions. They also provide magnification of targets up to ten kilometers away. When using electrobinoculars, characters may remove a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die from any [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] checks made to identify an object in low light or at long distances.[^0-1][^5][^6][^7]
