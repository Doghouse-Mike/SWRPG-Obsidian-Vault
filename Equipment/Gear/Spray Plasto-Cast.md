Quickly immobilizing a limb injury after it happens can prevent the extremity from becoming more seriously damaged through accidental misuse.

## SPRAY PLASTO-CAST

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 1 Price 35 Rarity 1|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/4/4c/Profile_Spray_Plasto-Cast.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 1 Price 35 Rarity 1

A character can make an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Easy difficulty]] [[Medicine (Int)|Medicine check]]** to apply a spray plasto-cast to a limb that has been crippled or disabled due to a Critical Injury. If successful, the Critical Injury heals automatically after one week of narrative time.[^2]
