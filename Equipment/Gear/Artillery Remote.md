## ARTILLERY REMOTE

Source: [^1][^2]

![Encumbrance 1 Price 400 Rarity 7|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/2/2e/Profile_Artillery_Remote.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 1 Price 400 Rarity 7

A character can link a single Gunnery weapon to an Atgar artillery remote with a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Computers (Int)|Computers check]]**. If the weapon is an Atgar model, this check is **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]]** instead. If the character succeeds, the weapon gains the [[Item Qualities#Inaccurate (Passive)|Inaccurate]] 2 quality, and the holder of the remote can fire the linked weapon via the remote from up to medium range.
