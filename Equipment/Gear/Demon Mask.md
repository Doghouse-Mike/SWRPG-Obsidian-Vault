## DEMON MASK

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 1 Price (R) 4,000 Rarity 10|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/5/55/Profile_Meditation_Focus.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 1 Price (R) 4,000 Rarity 10

A Force-sensitive character wearing a demon mask increases \[their\] wound threshold by 2 while \[they\] are a dark side Force user. Furthermore, character making [[Discipline (Will)|Discipline]] checks to resist fear caused by a character wearing a demon mask add [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice equal to that character's Force rating.[^2]
