Scanner goggles are a lighter, wearable version of [[Electrobinoculars|electrobinoculars]] equipped with a more limited set of optical enhancements.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Scanner_goggles).

**Models Include:** Fabritech X-2000 Scanner Goggles, Torjeka B3, TaggeCo Luminator Goggles.
