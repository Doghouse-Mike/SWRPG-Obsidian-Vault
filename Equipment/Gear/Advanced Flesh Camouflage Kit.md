More comprehensive than regular disguise kits, advanced flesh camouflage kits allow users to drastically change their appearance all the way down to a genetic level.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Advanced_Flesh_Camouflage_Kit).

**Restrictions:** Many elements within the kit are perfectly legal on their own, but most planetary and Imperial authorities take a dim view of anyone actually carrying a set of items that allows the user to mask \[themself\] down to a genetic level.

## ADVANCED FLESH CAMOUFLAGE KIT *OR* OUTLAW TECH FLESH CAMOUFLAGE KIT

The disguises created by a flesh camo kit are so good that seeing through them requires a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Formidable difficulty]] [[Perception (Cun)|Perception check]]**.[^0-1][^4]

This disguise is good enough that any checks made to ascertain the user's true identity have their difficulty set at **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Formidable]]**.[^1-3]
