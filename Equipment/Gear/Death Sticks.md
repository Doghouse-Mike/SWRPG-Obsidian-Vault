Death sticks are an unpleasant creation derived from the balo mushroom of the planet of Balosar and distributed in a liquid form in distinctive small red and yellow rods.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Death_stick).

**Restrictions:** Death sticks are controlled substances on most worlds, and at the very least those under the age of majority cannot purchase them. Death sticks are completely illegal on many other worlds, especially those with high health standards.
