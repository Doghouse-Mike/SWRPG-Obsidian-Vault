A relatively mild but addictive and potentially dangerous spice, ryll is mined primarily on Ryloth, the dry, rocky homeworld of the Twi'leks.

A dose of ryll allows a character to ignore the effects of a single Easy or Average Critical Injury Result until the end of the scene or combat encounter. Two doses allow the character to ignore the effects of a single Hard Critical Injury result until the end of the scene or combat encounter instead. Once its effects end, the character suffers the effects of the Critical Injury as normal, and also suffers two [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice on all checks \[they\] make until the end of the game session.
