A dose of impact lasts for one encounter and allows the user to upgrade the ability of any Agility-based checks until the end of the encounter. After the effects of impact wear off, the character suffers a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die to any Agility-, Cunning-, or Intellect-based checks for the remainder of the game session.
