## TRACKER'S GOGGLES

Source: [^1][^2]

![Encumbrance – Price 600 Rarity 5|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/c/c6/Profile_Tracker%27s_Goggles.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance – Price 600 Rarity 5

Tracker's goggles grant a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to the wearer's [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] checks to locate a target by sight. Additionally, they remove two [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice imposed due to concealment, darkness, fog, or mist from the wearer's [[Ranged - Light (Ag)|Ranged (Light)]] and [[Ranged - Heavy (Ag)|Ranged (Heavy)]] checks.
