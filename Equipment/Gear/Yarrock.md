For untold ages, the spice called yarrock has been produced on Iridonia, created by their shamans to grant their warriors a greater clarity of mind and violent purpose.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yarrock).

**Restrictions:** Yarrock is quite rare, and thus few planets explicitly regulate its use, as the only shipments go through black market traders.
