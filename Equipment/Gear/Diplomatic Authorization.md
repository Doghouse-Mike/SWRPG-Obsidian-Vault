A character with diplomatic authorization adds two automatic [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results to social skill checks involving members of a planetary of system government, and they can often get access to individuals and services within that government that would otherwise be unavailable. Unless lawfully granted by an authorized body, however, this item can only be acquired illegally.
