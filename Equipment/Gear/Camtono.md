Camtonos are security containers used to safeguard valuable materials. They can be programmed to open only when the rocker switches on the lid are activated in the proper combination. The user manual states that standard Camtonos can “Store up to 4 quarts of Blue Bantha Ice Cream”. The manual does not go on to specify why anyone would wish to safeguard this frozen treat.
