To assist those who have lost legs to accident or disease, many biotechnology companies produce replacement cyberlegs.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cyberlegs_Mod_II) [article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cyberlegs_Mod_III).

**Models Include:** BioTech Industries Cyberleg Mod II and Mod III.

## CYBERNETIC LEGS (MOD II AND MOD III)

Source: [^0-1][^1-2][^3][^4]

![Encumbrance – Price 10,000 Rarity 6|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/b/bf/Profile_Cybernetic_Arms_%28Mod_V_and_Mod_VI%29.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance – Price 10,000 Rarity 6

Mod II cyberlegs provide +1 [[Brawn]], and Mod III cyberlegs provide +1 [[Agility]]. Both legs must be replaced to gain these benefits. Both legs must be the same model, as they are designed to work in tandem. Modifiers from both legs do not stack.[^0-1][^1-2][^5][^6]
