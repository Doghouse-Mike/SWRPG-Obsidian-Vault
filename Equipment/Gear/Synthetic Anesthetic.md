This is a general anesthetic produced on many worlds for medical procedures and designed to render the user unconscious.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Anesthetic).

## SYNTHETIC ANESTHETIC

Source: [^0-1][^2][^3][^4]

![Encumbrance – Price 35 Rarity 4|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/d/d9/Profile_Synthetic_Anesthetic.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance – Price 35 Rarity 4

This poison may be introduced into a target's body via aerosol deployment, food ingestion, or injection with an applicator or dart. Resisting a single dose requires an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Resilience (Br)|Resilience check]]**, while two or more doses combined into a single application requires a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Resilience (Br)|Resilience check]]**. The poison inflicts 5 strain if the target fails the check. One to two [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results my be spent to force the target to give up their free maneuver during their next turn (the target may still take two maneuvers, however). Three [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results or more may be spent to stagger the target during their next turn. Finally, a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result may be spent to make the target test against the poison again during the next round, as the poison remains in the target's system.[^0-1][^5][^6][^7]
