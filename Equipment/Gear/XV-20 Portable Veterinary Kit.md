The XV-20 is produced by Xenovet, a biotech firm based on Commenor known for its designer pets and respected line of veterinary equipment.

## XV-20 PORTABLE VETERINARY KIT

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 2 Price 500 Rarity 3|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/2/2e/Profile_General_Purpose_Scanner.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 2 Price 500 Rarity 3

The XV-20 operates in a manner similar to a portable healing kit in that it allows an individual to treat a wounded or ill creature without penalty. Additionally, the kit grants a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to all [[Medicine (Int)|Medicine]], [[Survival (Cun)|Survival]], and [[Xenology (Int)|Knowledge (Xenology)]] skill checks made to diagnose or treat such animals.[^2]
