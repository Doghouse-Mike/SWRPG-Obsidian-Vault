A Jedi utility belt contains a three-day supply of food capsules, an [[Emergency Medpac|emergency medpac]], a [[Lightsaber Maintenance Kit|lightsaber maintenance kit]][^0-3] *or* [[Lightsaber Maintenance Kit|jedi multi-tool]][^1-4], a slim [[Glow Rod|glow rod]], a [[Comlink|comlink]], and an [[A99 Aquata Breather|aquata breather]]. A number of empty pouches are concealed along the belt's interior side, allowing the wearer to hide small items. In addition, a standard lightsaber can be securely hung from the belt for easy access. A Jedi utility belt also increases the wearer's encumbrance threshold by 1.[^0-3][^1-4]
