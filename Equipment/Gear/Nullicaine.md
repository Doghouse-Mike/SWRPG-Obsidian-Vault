Nullicaine is a staple of field treatment useful for helping soldiers to fight on despite injuries.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nullicaine).

## NULLICAINE

Source: [^0-1][^2]

![Encumbrance – Price 25 Rarity 2|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/c/cf/Profile_Nullicaine.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance – Price 25 Rarity 2

A character may apply nullicaine to themself or an engaged character by making an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Easy difficulty]] [[Medicine (Int)|Medicine check]]** and choosing one **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Easy]]**, **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average]]**, or **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]]** Critical Injury the target is currently suffering. If the check succeeds, the target immediately suffers 3 strain and ignores the chosen Critical Injury until the end of the encounter. Nullicaine only affects living creatures.[^0-1][^3]
