The Hutts of the Jahibakti clan, and its vassal clans, are known also as Grakks (named after Grakkus the Hutt). They are immediately recogni- sable by their use of cybernetic legs to overcome the weakness of their slug-like bodies. These legs are often custom designs to the preference of the Hutt having them installed, ranging in number and appearance.

## GRAKKS CYBERNETIC SUITE

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance - Price 12,500 Rarity 7|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/2/2d/Profile_Grakks_Cybernetic_Suite.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance - Price 12,500 Rarity 7

Regardless of the design, this cybernetic provides allows a Hutt character to ignore the Ponderous ability. It also provides a +1 to their Agility.

At the GM's discretion, a Hutt character may start with this cybernetic by spending 20 of their starting experience.
