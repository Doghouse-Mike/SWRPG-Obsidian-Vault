Countless credits have been won and lost on the roll of simple chance cubes.

**Models Include:** Numerous variants.

## LOADED CHANCE CUBES

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 1 Price (R) 100 Rarity 5|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/8/89/Profile_Loaded_Chance_Cubes.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 1 Price (R) 100 Rarity 5

Characters using loaded chance cubes may add up to two [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] dice to [[Deception (Cun)|Deception]] checks when gambling. Noticing that chance cubes are weighted requires a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Formidable difficulty]] [[Perception (Cun)|Perception check]]** on the first roll of the dice, but the difficulty decreases by one for each subsequent roll.
