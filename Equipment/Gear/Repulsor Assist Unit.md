## REPULSOR ASSIST UNIT

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 1 Price 620 Rarity 3|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/e/e7/Profile_Repulsor_Assist_Unit.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 1 Price 620 Rarity 3

A character using a repulsor assist unit reduces the difficulty to climb by 2 (to a minimum of 0). A repulsor assist unit can also be attached to a cargo to reduce the encumbrance value of the cargo by 7.[^2]
