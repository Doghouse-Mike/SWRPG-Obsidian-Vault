The Collar-Amp is a deceptively simple and useful device that is essentially an unobtrusive and very small amplifier.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Collar-Amp).

## HERZFALL COMMUNICATIONS COLLAR-AMP

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 0 Price 50 Rarity 1|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/e/ee/Profile_Herzfall_Communications_Collar-Amp.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 0 Price 50 Rarity 1

The Collar-Amp can boost the wearer's voice so that \[they\] can be heard up to long or extreme range (this depends on terrain, environmental conditions, and even weather). For an extra 100 credits, the user can purchase a model that hooks into a sound system or entertainment system which improves sound quality.[^2]
