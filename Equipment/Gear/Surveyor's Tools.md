## SURVEYOR'S TOOLS

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 6 Price 500 Rarity 6|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/3/3d/Profile_Surveyor%27s_Tools.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 6 Price 500 Rarity 6

A set of basic surveyor's tools adds a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to a character's [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] checks when plotting specific locations on a planet's surface, measuring distances, or creating detailed maps. Larger kits may add two [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] dice at the Game Master's discretion.[^2]
