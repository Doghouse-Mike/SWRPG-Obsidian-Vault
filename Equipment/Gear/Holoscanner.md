Used in spaceports and high-security locations throughout the galaxy, holoscanners can detect the presence of dangerous or illegal items without the need to physically search individuals or cargo containers.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Holoscanner).

**Models Include:** BAS44 customs-grade holoscanner, Fabritech HB-2 holoscanner, TaggeCo. SecureScan 1500, Neuro-Saav Technologies 7320/H scanning array.

Holoscanners reduce the difficulty of [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] or [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]] checks to discover concealed items on a person or in a container or vehicle by two. A holoscanner cannot typically scan an item or group of items with encumbrance higher than its own..[^2]
