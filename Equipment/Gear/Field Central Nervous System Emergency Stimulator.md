Only the size of a heavy blaster pistol, this bulky hand-held device is a miniaturised version of common medical equipment found in medbays across the galaxy. It delivers a specially calibrated electric shock to the organic body, stimulating the central nervous system and awakening a downed individual. Even entirely untrained individuals can usually revive a squadmate with this device, as long they weren’t outright killed. It was developed long before the Clone Wars, and was often given to spec ops groups that lacked dedicated medics or were expected to face continuous combat with little time to address wounds.

## FIELD CENTRAL NERVOUS SYSTEM EMERGENCY STIMULATOR

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 2 Price 4,500 Rarity 8|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/f/fe/Profile_Field_Central_Nervous_System_Emergency_Stimulator.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 2 Price 4,500 Rarity 8

With an action, a character can attempt to revive an incapacitated individual on the field by making a [[Medicine (Int)|Medicine]] check with the CNS stimulator. The difficulty is set by the number of Critical Injuries the target is suffering from. Success brings a character to one under their wound or strain threshold (depending on what incapacitated them), healing one wound and strain for each three additional [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] and [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result respectively. Failure while using this equipment instead deals another Critical Injury to the target, +10 per [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] generated.
