Common in every Jedi survival kit and pilot emergency pack of this era, sunflares are used to signal in the absence of communications. When the flares detonate, they spread a luminous compound over a wide blast area, completely illuminating the area (effectively turning dark areas to the equivalent of full daylight). A sunflare is practical for illuminating target points for blaster artillery, as well as for attracting the attention of rescue ships (sunflares are often used by downed pilots). They come in a variety of colours, so that their meanings can be understood quickly, with red being the universal sign for rescue needed.
