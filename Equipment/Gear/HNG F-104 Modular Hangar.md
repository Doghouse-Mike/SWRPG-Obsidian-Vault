Similar in construction to the L-120 modular motor pool, the F-104 modular hangar provides ample space for starfighters or airspeeders and all of the equipment needed to maintain and repair these near-priceless military assets.

## HNG F-104 MODULAR HANGAR

> [!quote] Image text
