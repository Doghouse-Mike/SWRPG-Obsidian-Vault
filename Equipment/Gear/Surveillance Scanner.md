Surveillance scanners are handheld devices used to detect active and passive surveillance systems.

## SURVEILLANCE SCANNER

Source: [^1][^2]

![Encumbrance 2 Price 750 Rarity 5|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/f/f7/Profile_Surveillance_Scanner.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 2 Price 750 Rarity 5

Surveillance scanners have a range of close on the planetary scale or long on the personal scale. Using a surveillance scanner upgrades the ability of any checks made to locate bugs, sweep rooms for hidden cameras, pick up data streams, or engage in any other counter-surveillance activities once.
