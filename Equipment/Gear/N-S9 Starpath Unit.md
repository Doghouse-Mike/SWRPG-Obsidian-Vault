An extremely valuable and extremely restricted piece of equipment, Starpath units are installed in the bridges of Imperial capital ships at construction. The Starpath uses proprietary Imperial signals and frequencies to coordinate and map the relation of a starship to every Imperial asset, whether it is an installation or a vessel, for nine radial parsecs. These units are invaluable in fleet deployment and assignment, allowing an Imperial captain to see every asset available to them within a specified range.

Of course, those with more nefarious needs, would also find a Starpath to be an incredibly useful piece of equipment, allowing them to see every Imperial ship of installation and allowing them to steer well clear of them.

Starpath Units can only be found at Imperial shipyards or at high-security factory sites, so getting a hold of one on the black market is incredibly rare. Occasionally fakes make their way to sale, but they are quickly discovered by those in the know. Real, new Starpath Units come with sealed and intact vector crystals and an authentic Imperial seal.
