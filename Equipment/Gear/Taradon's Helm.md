Somewhere in the icy wastes of Ilum, the wreckage of a starfighter serves as the tomb of the Jedi Taradon, a Corellian human possessed of extraordinary prowess as a pilot.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Taradon%27s_Helm).

## TARADON'S HELM

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance - Price 11,000 Rarity 10|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/2/2f/Profile_Taradon%27s_Helm.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance - Price 11,000 Rarity 10

A Force-sensitive character who possesses Taradon's Helm may reroll one [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] die when making checks that involve piloting or using a starship or vehicle. If there is some question as to whether the check involves piloting or using a starship or vehicle, the GM is the final arbiter.[^0-2]
