An ocular is a circular lens that lets the user, on a specific setting, see through walls to pick up heat signatures from the outside. It looks similar in appearance to a slightly larger jewellers loupe. Oculars are often used by spacers checking their hulls for my nocks or other unwanted hangers on. When using an ocular, a character may see through one wall, they can see the vague shape of a heat signature but cannot determine specific species or specific people.
