## PORTABLE POWER GENERATOR

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 10 Price 500 Rarity 2|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/9/9b/Profile_Portable_Power_Generator.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 10 Price 500 Rarity 2

A portable generators can recharge a depleted piece of equipment as a maneuver, or a droid or larger piece of machinery in an hour.[^0-2]

Additionally, a portable power generator can allow an engaged character to ignore an "out of ammo" [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result in the same manner as an [[Extra Reload|extra reload]]; the generator is usually not depleted by such use, except at the GM's discretion.[^0-2]
