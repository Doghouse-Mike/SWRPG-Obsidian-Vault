Hunting goggles are a type of scanner goggles specifically designed to assist professional guides an hunters in tracking their quarry.

## HUNTING GOGGLES

Source: [^0-1][^2]

![Encumbrance – Price 600 Rarity 5|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/3/3b/Profile_Hunting_Goggles.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance – Price 600 Rarity 5

Hunting goggles grant a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to any [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] checks made to locate prey, and thanks to their image magnification and enhancement, they remove up to two [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice imposed due to concealment, darkness, fog, or mist from all [[Ranged - Light (Ag)|Ranged (Light)]] and [[Ranged - Heavy (Ag)|Ranged (Heavy)]] checks.[^0-1][^3]
