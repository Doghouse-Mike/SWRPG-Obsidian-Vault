A unique delicacy originating from the Steness Node, , this meat and pastry concoction has been spreading across the Outer Rim of late. Despite repeated questioning of the people of the Steness Node, galactic gastrologists have been unable to determine if Lizard Pie is for lizards, or contains lizards. Either way, the Trandoshan consulate on Coruscant have lodged a formal complaint with the Republic’s Gastro Safety Agency.
