More than a simple cybernetic replacement, this cybernetic arm includes a basic scanner for medium-range detection.

> More information about the gear available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cyberscanner_limb).

**Models Include:** Neuro-Saav Technologies CyberScanner Limb, NeuroFabritech ScanMaster Arm.

## CYBERSCANNER LIMB

Source: [^0-1][^2][^3][^4]

![Encumbrance – Price 4,000 Rarity 7|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/5/51/Profile_Cybernetic_Weapon_Implant.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text

The Mod IV Scanner Limb incorporates a portable scanner (such as the [[General Purpose Scanner|general purpose scanner]])[^0-1] and, unlike most cybernetics, is concealed by a synthflesh covering to disguise that there is anything out of the ordinary about the limb.[^0-1][^5][^6][^7]
