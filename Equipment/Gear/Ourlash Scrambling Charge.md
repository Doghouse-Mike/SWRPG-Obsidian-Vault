## OURLASH SCRAMBLING CHARGE

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 0 Price 250 Rarity 3|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/b/b3/Profile_Ourlash_Scrambling_Charge.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 0 Price 250 Rarity 3

This small, handheld device device is able to block a communications system from receiving and transmitting data. It can be set to activate after a certain time from its placement has passed, making it harder to be detected. Unlike a standard EMP, a Scrambling Charge only affects communication systems, allowing for even more subterfuge, as a fault with a communication system may not be initially seen as anything nefarious.[^2]
