Chandrila has a rich history, full of heritage and judicious customs. Even their food and drinks come from a long tradition, and the Chandrilan Squig is no exception.

A cocktail of mango blossom and whiskwine, the drink also features a squig- edible Chandrilan eels. The addition of squigs cause the cocktail to fizz and foam, change colour and a tasty chew to the bubbly drink. The drink is certainly an acquired taste.
