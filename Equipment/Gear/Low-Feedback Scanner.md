## LOW FEEDBACK SCANNER

Source: [^1]

![Encumbrance 4 Price 700 Rarity 5|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/d/d2/Profile_Low-Feedback_Scanner.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance 4 Price 700 Rarity 5

This sensor can detect radiation sources of silhouette 1 or larger within short range, life forms within medium range, power sources within long range, and comms signals within extreme range. Extend the range of detection by 1 range band for every silhouette size by which the target's size exceeds silhouette 1.[^2]
