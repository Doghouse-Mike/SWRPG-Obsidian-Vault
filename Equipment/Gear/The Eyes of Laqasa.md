## THE EYES OF LAQASA

Source: [^1]

When wearing the Eyes of Laqasa, Force-sensitive characters remove all [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice imposed due to darkness, concealment, camouflage, or similar effects, and can see normally as in normal daylight.[^0-2]
