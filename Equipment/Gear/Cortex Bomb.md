Cortex bombs are last-resort measures to keep an agent from breaking under interrogation.

## CORTEX BOMB

Source: [^1][^2]

![Encumbrance – Price (R) 1,200 Rarity 6|377](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/f/f8/Profile_Cortex_Bomb.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Encumbrance – Price (R) 1,200 Rarity 6

Cortex bombs detonate when the implanted individual speaks, hears, or sees a predetermined code word or signal, and they kill only the wearer. They deal very little discernible damage to the target, though, and no damage to the surrounding area.
