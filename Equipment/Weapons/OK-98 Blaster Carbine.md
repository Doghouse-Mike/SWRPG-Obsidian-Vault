## OK-98 BLASTER CARBINE

Source: [^0-1][^2]

![Skill Ranged (Heavy) Range Medium Encumbrance 4 Price 1,100 Rarity 5 Damage 9 Critical Rating 3 Hard Points 3 Special Stun setting|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/5/55/Profile_OK-98_Blaster_Carbine.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Skill Ranged (Heavy) Range Medium Encumbrance 4 Price 1,100 Rarity 5 Damage 9 Critical Rating 3 Hard Points 3 Special Stun setting

A character using an OK-98 can ignore the first "out of ammo" [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result during a combat, just as if they had an extra reload, without expending a maneuver. Modifying an OK-98 (requiring an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard difficulties|Easy difficulty]] [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics check]]**) increases its damage by two, replacing the option to ignore the first "out of ammo" result. The GM can spend a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result to have a modified OK-98 catastrophically overheat and explode, inflicting one hit from the weapon (dealing base damage) on the user and destroying the weapon after the attack has been resolved.[^0-1][^3]
