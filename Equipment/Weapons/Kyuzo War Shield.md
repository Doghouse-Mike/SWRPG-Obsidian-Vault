Originally utilitarian in function, the Kyuzo war shield was designed by the inhabitants of the Kwaidan Peaks, as a headpiece to protect travelers from torrential rains during monsoon season in southern Phatrong.

## KYUZO WAR SHIELD

A Kyuzo war shield may be thrown as a ranged weapon. If used in this manner, change the skill to [[Ranged - Light (Ag)|Ranged (Light)]] and the range of the weapon to short. The weapon also gains the [[Item Qualities#Limited Ammo (Passive)|Limited Ammo]] 1 quality (but if retrieved, it can be used again).

Additionally, the wielder of a Kyuzo war shield may spend three [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results from any combat check to throw the weapon to cause the shield to ricochet back to \[their\] grasp.
