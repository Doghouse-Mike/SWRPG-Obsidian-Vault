Through a delicate chemical process, a particularly creative demolitions expert has perfected a method of turning detonite into a liquid form that can be dispensed from an aerosol can. While the process reduces the volatility of the explosive, the liquid form allows for fast and discreet deployment. A small trigger mechanism is required to set off the explosion. These mechanisms can simply be placed on the surface where the liquid has been applied and can usually be activated by timer or remote detonation.
