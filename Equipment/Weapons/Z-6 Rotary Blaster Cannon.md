The Z-6 rotary blaster cannon is an exceptionally potent heavy squad support weapon built for the GAR by BlasTech.

> More information about the weapon available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Z-6_rotary_blaster_cannon).

## Z-6 ROTARY BLASTER CANNON

Source: [^0-1][^2]

> Skill Ranged (Heavy) Range Long Encumbrance 6 Price (R) 3,000 Rarity 7 Damage 12 Critical Rating 4 Hard Points 3 Special Auto-fire (Only), Cumbersome 3, Prepare 1

This weapon can only be used with its [[Item Qualities#Auto-fire (Active)|Auto-fire]] item quality active. The GM may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result or two [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results from any combat check made with this weapon to have it overheat. When the weapon overheats, the wielder suffers 2 strain, and the weapon becomes damaged one step, from undamaged to minor, minor to moderate, or moderate to major.[^0-1][^3]
