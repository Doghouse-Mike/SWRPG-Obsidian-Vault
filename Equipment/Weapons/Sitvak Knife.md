This small, thin blade is designed to slip away somewhere hidden, usually inside of a boot. The 45 degree angled blade features a loop at one end, where a wielder can slip their finger and a razor sharp point and tip at the other. Sitvak knifes are usually crafted from a single piece of durasteel and feature cutout sections along the blade, to lighten the weapon further.

Sitvak are favourites of criminals and mercenaries, and many a barfight has ended in blood when one party whipped out a hidden blade.
