## HEARTWOOD BLASTER

When wielding a heartwood blaster, a character may suffer strain up to their Force rating as a maneuver. If they do, they add an equal number of automatic [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results to the next combat check they make that turn.[^2]
