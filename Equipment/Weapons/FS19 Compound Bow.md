## FIELD SPORTS FS19 COMPOUND BOW (BROADHEAD ARROW)

Source: [^1]

![Skill Ranged (Heavy) Range Medium Encumbrance 4 Price 500 Rarity 7 Damage 5 Critical Rating 4 Hard Points 2 Special Cumbersome 3, Limited Ammo 1, Pierce 1, Vicious 2|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/9/9f/Profile_Field_Sports_FS19_Compound_Bow_%28Broadhead_Arrow%29.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Skill Ranged (Heavy) Range Medium Encumbrance 4 Price 500 Rarity 7 Damage 5 Critical Rating 4 Hard Points 2 Special Cumbersome 3, Limited Ammo 1, Pierce 1, Vicious 2

The basic ammunition used with the FS19 is Field Sports' Mk. IV "Broadhead" arrow, which consists of a meter-long plasteel shaft with a sharp, bladed point at one end and synthetic, spin-stabilizing fletching on the other. These arrows are cheap to replace; the GM who wishes to set a price could charge 1 credit per arrow. In addition, Field Sports produces a line of other arrows for use with the FS19 that increase the weapon's versatility. \[...\] \[Below are\] the profiles for non-standard arrows for the bow.[^2]
