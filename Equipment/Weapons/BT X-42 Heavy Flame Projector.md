Game Masters can spend three [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results to cause the weapon to overheat, leaving it inoperable for three turns, or a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result to have the weapon explode. An exploding X-42 deals full damage to the user and Blast damage to anything engaged with them.[^2]
