Falling somewhere between brass knuckles and a sword, vamblades are dagger-length blades extending from durasteel vambraces, which mount the blade and provide a degree of protection to the wielder's forearms.

> More information about the weapon available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/S-1_Vamblade).

## S-1 VAMBLADE

Source: [^1][^2]

Vamblades are designed to be worn as a pair and open up a whole range of combat options when wielded by a properly trained fighter. When used as a pair, these wristblades gain the [[Item Qualities#Sunder (Active)|Sunder]] and [[Item Qualities#Accurate (Passive)|Accurate]] 1 item qualities. Vamblades cannot deal damage to a target's strain threshold.
