An evolution of a breaching tool developed by Drever Corp, the DN bolt caster used plasma disruptor technology to great effect against droid opponents. It was also capable of damaging organic targets, with less effectiveness. The weapon ended up being particularly expensive, but the Grand Army of the Republic quickly scooped up many of these weapons once the Clone Wars began. Due to their limited production, they were handed to special units for self defence, such as vehicle clone pilots who might see combat when their ride was disabled.

When used against a droid or target with cybernetic enhancements, this weapon deals 4 additional damage. The DN bolt caster can be charged by using a maneuver before firing. For each maneuver, reduce the Critical Rating by 1 (to a minimum of 1) against droids, and the user can spend two [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results to have the ionised energy jump to another target within Short range of the first target, as if by the [[Item Qualities#Auto-fire (Active)|Auto-fire]] quality. Each maneuver used allows for additional jumps to another target within Short range of the last, spending two [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results each time.[^2]
