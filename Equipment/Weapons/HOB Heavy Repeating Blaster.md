The HOB, or high-output repeating blaster, is the SoroSuub Corporation's attempt to loosen BlasTech's and Merr-Sonn's strangleholds on the heavy repeater market.

> More information about the weapon available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/HOB_heavy_repeating_blaster).
