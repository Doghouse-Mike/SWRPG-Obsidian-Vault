Developed by Concordian Crescent Technologies in an open tender for a lightweight improvement of the [[DC-15 Blaster Rifle|DC-15 blaster rifle]], the WESTAR-M5 focused on making the weapon considerably lighter and more ergonomic. The weight reduction was achieved by increasing the height of the weapon and cutting its length to one of a carbine. It features an extendable stock for greater accuracy at long range. What resulted in the end was a weapon very similar to the eventual [[DC-15A Blaster Carbine|DC-15a]] produced by BlasTech in the same bidding process. While the Mandalorian company lost the army-wide bid, the WESTAR-M5 was still used by particular commando groups.
