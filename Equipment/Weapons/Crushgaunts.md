Crushgaunts, also called shuk'orok in Mando'a, are strength-enhancing gloves designed centuries ago by the Mandalorians.

## CRUSHGAUNTS

Source: [^1]

![Skill Brawl Range Engaged Encumbrance 1 Price (R) 5,000 Rarity 8 Damage +4 Critical Rating 2 Hard Points 0 Special Cortosis, Sunder, Vicious 2|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/6/6a/Profile_Crushgaunts.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Skill Brawl Range Engaged Encumbrance 1 Price (R) 5,000 Rarity 8 Damage +4 Critical Rating 2 Hard Points 0 Special Cortosis, Sunder, Vicious 2

A character wearing crushgaunts upgrades the ability of Brawn checks they make to grip, crush, or hold on to items one time. A Game Master may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result or three [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results generated on any checks made to crush or destroy something with crushgaunts to deal 2 wounds to the wearer.[^2]
