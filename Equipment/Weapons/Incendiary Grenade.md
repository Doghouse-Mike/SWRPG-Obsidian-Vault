Highly restricted and banned on most civilized worlds, incendiary grenades are anti-personnel grenades that cause damage with fire and heat rather than with an explosive charge.

**Models Include:** Wynz-Tek Mk IV Firestorm, [[D-24 'Inferno' Grenade|Merr-Sonn D-24 Inferno Grenade]].
