A suppression fire and crowd control weapon used by the Imperial Army, the Imperial heavy repeater fires ionized bursts of metallic bolts at an incredibly rapid rate.

As a maneuver, a character wielding an Imperial heavy repeater may prime the weapon to expend all of its ammunition in a single, devastating burst. The next time the weapon is fired this encounter, it counts as having the [[Item Qualities#Blast (Active)|Blast]] 7 and [[Item Qualities#Concussive (Active)|Concussive]] 1 qualities, but does not count as having the [[Item Qualities#Auto-fire (Active)|Auto-fire]] quality. After being discharged this way, the weapon runs out of ammunition.[^2]
