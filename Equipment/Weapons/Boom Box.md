These explosive devices are commonly used in ship deconstruction, seen in decommissioning yards on worlds such as Bracca or Kortis III. They consist of a sphere of Accellerite held in a Magnopium ferrocongregate case. At room temperature, these chemicals are inert, but once heated, they react and cause a massive explosion. Boom boxes are highly regulated and kept under strict lock and key in decommissioning yards, however they are a popular item for theft by criminal gangs and outlaw parties.
