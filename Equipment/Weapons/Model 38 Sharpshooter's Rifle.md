One of the best-selling firearms in Czerka's Legacy Arms slugthrower product line, each Model 38 is laser measured to accomodate a shooter's natural stance and includes a custom-fitted stock, trigger, and optic for optimal shooting comfort.

> More information about the weapon available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Model_38_sharpshooter%27s_rifle).

### CZERKA DETONATOR ROUND

Source: [^1-3]

While the standard penetrator ammunition offered with the Model 38 works well in most situations, sometimes a shooter needs a little extra punch to fell \[their\] prey. To this end, Czerka offers the Detonator Round for use with the Model 38. \[...\] When used with the Model 38, these rounds have the following effect on the weapon's stats: Remove the [[Item Qualities#Pierce (Passive)|Pierce]] quality and add the [[Item Qualities#Blast (Active)|Blast]] (5) quality and [[Item Qualities#Knockdown (Active)|Knockdown]] quality. Detonator Rounds cost 450 credits for a box of rounds, and have a rarity of 7. If the weapon ever runs out of ammo, a new box of rounds must be purchased to reload it.
