Not as clumsy or random as a baradium detonator, micro detonite charges produce contained high energy explosions. They are easily affixed to most surfaces and can be triggered by a proximity sensor, impact detector, remote activation, or a timer.

When the charge is triggered Engaged targets roll a [[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Daunting difficulty]] [[Resilience (Br)|Resilience check]]. Each [[Narrative Dice#Failure|failure]] result inflicts + wound, and three [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results activates a critical injury.

Entering engaged range triggers the charge's proximity sensor which emits a soft tone. An [[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Perception (Cun)|Perception check]] can alert a character to the noise, allowing them time to take one maneuver at the cost of 4 strain.

**Special:** A charge gains the quality [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] 1 if it affixed directly to a target. A [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result on a ranged attack (throwing it) can affix the charge to the target, granting the bonus ([[Item Qualities#Blast (Active)|Blast]] damage does not benefit from the [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] quality).
