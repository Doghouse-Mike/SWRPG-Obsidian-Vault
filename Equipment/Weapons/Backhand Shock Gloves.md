Produced by Corellian Personal Defense and marketed toward bodyguards, security forces, and law enforcement, Backhand series shock gloves pack the power of a stun baton into a glove.

While a character may still make [[Brawl (Br)|Brawl]] checks when this weapon is recharging, they do so without any benefit from the weapon—they simply make an unarmed melee attack.
