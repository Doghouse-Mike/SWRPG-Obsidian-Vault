## GARROTE

Source: [^1][^2]

![Skill Brawl Range Engaged Encumbrance 1 Price 50 Rarity 2 Damage - Critical Rating - Hard Points 0 Special Stun setting|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/a/a8/Profile_Garrote.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Skill Brawl Range Engaged Encumbrance 1 Price 50 Rarity 2 Damage - Critical Rating - Hard Points 0 Special Stun setting

A character who makes a successful Brawl attack when using a garrote against a target can cause the target to become suffocated. Targets may attempt to break free of the garrote with an **opposed [[Brawl (Br)|Brawl check]]**, adding a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die for each round they have been caught.
