**Bastion of Safety:** Kenobi's lightsaber draws the eye of every combatant on the field if ignited. Once per encounter before igniting, the wielder may make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Leadership (Pr)|Leadership]]** or **[[Negotiation (Pr)|Negotiation check]]** as an incidental to draw fire. If successful, until the wielder's next turn, whenever an enemy character in the current encounter makes a ranged combat check that could target the wielder, they must target the wielder. The GM may spend two [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result to exclude an enemy from this effect.

### DISARMING NATURE

\[...\] Whenever Kenobi makes a successful combat check with his lightsaber that results in a Critical Injury and the Critical Injury result is over 75, he may treat the result as if it were 105.
