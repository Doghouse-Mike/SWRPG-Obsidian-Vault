A fuel-air bomb's [[Item Qualities#Concussive (Active)|Concussive]] value increases by one for every additional charge. A [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result on a check to set up this bomb means it immediately detonates, dealing its total damage to everything in its current blast radius.[^0-1][^3]

Additional Damage +10.[^0-1][^1-2]
