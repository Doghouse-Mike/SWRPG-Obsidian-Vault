## BARADIUM CHARGE

Source: [^0-1][^1-2][^2-3]

![Skill Mechanics Range Long Encumbrance 2 Price (R) 750 Rarity 5 Damage 3 Critical Rating - Hard Points - Special None|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/4/40/Profile_Baradium_Charge.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Skill Mechanics Range Long Encumbrance 2 Price (R) 750 Rarity 5 Damage 3 Critical Rating - Hard Points - Special None

Baradium weapons deal damage on a planetary scale, but their blast radius is measured in personal scale.[^0-1][^2-3]

If the demolitionist generates a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result when setting up the device, it detonates immediately, dealing the device's damage (base plus additional damage) to everything in the current blast radius. Anyone and anything engaged with the device when it detonates should add +50 to their subsequent Critical Injury or Critical Hit roll, if any.[^0-1][^1-2][^2-3]

Additional Damage +1 (planetary scale).[^0-1][^1-2][^2-3]
