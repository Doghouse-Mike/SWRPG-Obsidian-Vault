Highly modified lightsabers, lightwhips consist of a short lightsaber haft built from alloys or composites laced either cortosis, phrik alloy, or a comparable energy-resistant alloy.

> More information about the weapon available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightwhip).

When the wielder makes a successful [[Lightsaber (Br)|Lightsaber]] combat check, they may spend two [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result to prevent their opponent from using the [[Parry talent|Parry]] incidental to reduce damage. A user may not add lightsaber crystal attachments to a lightwhip and cannot use a lightwhip to parry or reflect attacks.[^0-1][^3]

This weapon can be used to make melee attacks against targets up to short range (the difficulty remains **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]]**).[^0-1]
