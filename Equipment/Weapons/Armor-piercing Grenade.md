Armor-piercing grenades provide a lightweight option for infantry troops to combat armored vehicles and emplacements or to breach armored fortifications.

> More information about the weapon available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Armor-piercing_grenade).

**Models Include:** Merr-Sonn Model 9 Armor Piercing Grenade, Merr-Sonn "Door Buster" Anti-Materiel Grenade.
