What is essentially a downscaled version of an anti-starfighter emplacement, the reciprocating quad blaster cannon is the heaviest personal weapon in Merr-Sonn Munitions' entire arsenal.

> More information about the weapon available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Reciprocating_quad_blaster_cannon).
