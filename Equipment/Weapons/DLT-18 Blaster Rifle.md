The DLT-18 were manufactured by BlasTech industries, but never saw as wide a distribution as many of their other rifles due to their weight and length, which made them difficult to carry comfortably. Due to these drawbacks, they were commonly seen in use by combat droids, or more physically imposing sentient species, such as Houks or Trandoshans
