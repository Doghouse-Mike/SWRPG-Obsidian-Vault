## CPSL-12 PROJECTILE LAUNCHER

The CSPL-12 profile assumes it is loaded with [[Fragmentation Grenade|frag grenades]], but other grenades \[...\] may be loaded instead. When this launcher uses other grenades, they replace the CSPL-12's damage, critical rating, and item qualities with their own.

The GM may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result from a combat check with this weapon to cause it to jam, detonating and inflicting a hit from the loaded grenade on the wielder and every other character at engaged range. Further, the weapon becomes damaged two steps; from undamaged to Moderate, from Minor to Major, or Moderate to Major. The GM may spend additional [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] results to trigger one of the loaded grenade's qualities.
