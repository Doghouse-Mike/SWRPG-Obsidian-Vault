One of the more powerful rifles in the Merr-Sonn Munitions inventory, the MR-90 proton rifle is a particle beam weapon originally designed for hunting the biggest, most dangerous prey in the galaxy.

> More information about the weapon available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MR-90_proton_rifle).

As a maneuver, a character using an MR-90 can set the weapon to its charge setting, which allows the shooter to expend all of the rifle's energy in a single, devastating blast. Once charged, the MR-90 counts as having the [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] 1 and [[Item Qualities#Concussive (Active)|Concussive]] 2 item qualities. After the charged beam is fired, the weapon runs out of ammunition.[^0-1][^3]
