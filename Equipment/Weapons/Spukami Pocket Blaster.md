Manufactured on Talus and Tralus by Fed-Dub Protective Services, the Spukami is sold throughout the Corellian system as a home-defense weapon.

> More information about the weapon available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Spukami_pocket_blaster).

Add one [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback die]] to a character's [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] checks when attempting to find a Spukami on a person's body.[^0-1][^3]
