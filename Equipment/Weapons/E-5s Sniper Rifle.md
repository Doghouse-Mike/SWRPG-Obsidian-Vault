Despite its name suggesting a relationship to the E-5 blaster rifle, this light sniper rifle has no features in common with the original weapon. It has an extremely long and slim barrel, providing it with excellent long range accuracy. Paired with a high magnification scope, it makes for a great marksman’s weapon. All these features also mean that it makes for a poor weapon in close quarters. The barrel is awkward to use from the hip, and the scope makes it unusable firing down sights. Another limitation is that it’s only capable of only a few shots before needing a reload. It is truly a situational weapon used by dedicated sniper units which provide fire then disengage.

> More information about the weapon available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/E-5s_sniper_rifle).
