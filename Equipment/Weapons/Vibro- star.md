A lethal throwing weapon, vibro-stars operate under the same principle as vibroswords. A small but powerful device within the weapon sending out high-frequency sonic pulses that cause anything attached to the device to be sent into an extremely quick vibration. This vibration turns even a glancing blow into a gaping would, Because of this, vibro-stars are incredibly sharp and able to cut effortlessly through many materials, including bone and metal.
