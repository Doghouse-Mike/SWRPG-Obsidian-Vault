While producing the WESTAR-34, they began production on a more experimental entry into their line of pistols. The WESTAR-33 was rather stocky and crude in design, distinguishing it from the early luxurious weapons of Concordian Crescent Technologies. Despite its outward appearance, this weapon was given no less attention by the Mandalorian engineers. Using a special tibanna blend and spin chamber, this pistol would produce a rare purple beam shot. The process of creating this beam when pulling the trigger was so instant that the engineers built-in a fairly light mechanism. This allowed for users to fire at much higher rates than a regular pistol and potentially land multiple killer shots on their target at once.
