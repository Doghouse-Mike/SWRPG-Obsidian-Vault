The success of the close range and multi-target bolt caster had the Republic seeking similar weapons in a cheaper package. BlasTech Industries was quick to respond to this new demand, given how lucrative their partnership with the GAR had been thus far. The DP-23 was yet another weapon using the DC-15’s frame as a design basis. A stubby and simple redesign, the DP-23 concentrated the punch of a DC-15 into a tightly spread shot, increasing armour piercing capabilities while sacrificing range. At longer ranges, it could be used to sweep through groups of droids.
