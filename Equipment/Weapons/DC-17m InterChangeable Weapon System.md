Specifically designed for use by the SO BDE, this BlasTech Industries proprietary design was marked as a great success by all who used it. Built with similar components to the carbine produced for the regular infantry, the base model is effectively a compact assault rifle. What makes this weapon special is that it serves as a platform for multiple systems. As a standard, the ICWS was shipped with an extended barrel sniper mode and a sort of underslung armour penetrating grenade launcher as an anti-tank mode.

The profile in this section is that of the base weapon system. Other weapon modes are detailed as custom attachments. These attachments are mutually exclusive but can be swapped out with a maneuver.[^0-2]

### CUSTOM ATTACHMENT

Source: [^0-2]

**Sniper Mode:** This mode involves an extended barrel which encases the blaster bolts in energy, similarly to the quarrels of a bowcaster. In addition, aiming is assisted by a simple electromagnetic scope system which deploys from the barrel. \
**Base Modifiers:** Increases range to Extreme and damage +1. Also increases [[Item Qualities#Pierce (Passive)|Pierce]] quality by 1 and grants [[Item Qualities#Accurate (Passive)|Accurate]] 1. However, add two [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice when using this weapon at Short or Engaged range and the weapon gains the [[Item Qualities#Slow-Firing (Passive)|Slow-Firing]] 1 quality. \
**Modification Options:** 1 Reduce the difficulty of attacks at Long or Extreme range by one Mod, 1 Innate Talent ([[Precise Aim talent|Precise Aim]]) Mod, 1 May see living creatures through solid objects up to Short range Mod. \
**Hard points Requirement:** 0 (mutually exclusive with other custom attachments). \
**Price/Rarity:** 1,000 credits (R)/9. \
**Encumbrance:** 1.

### CUSTOM ATTACHMENT

Source: [^0-2]

**Anti-Armour Mode:** This mode replaces the blaster system and reroutes the energy to a launcher tube with a semi rotating barrel. The rounds are similar to AP grenades, but are smaller and custom made for this weapon system. A pack of four rounds costs 50 credits and has an encumbrance of 1. \
**Base Modifiers:** The weapon profile becomes: ([[Gunnery (Ag)|Gunnery]]; Damage 10; Critical 2; Range \[Short\]; [[Item Qualities#Blast (Active)|Blast]] 3, [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] 1, [[Item Qualities#Limited Ammo (Passive)|Limited Ammo]] 4, [[Item Qualities#Prepare (Passive)|Prepare]] 1). \
**Modification Options:** None. \
**Hard points Requirement:** 0 (mutually exclusive with other custom attachments). \
**Price/Rarity:** 1,000 credits (R)/9. \
**Encumbrance:** 1.

### CUSTOM ATTACHMENT

Source: [^0-2]

**PEP Laser Mode:** Only released a year after the outset of the war, the Pulsed Energy Projectile mode consisted in replacing the tibanna gas output system with one filled with deuterium fluoride. This created a stunning laser beam which was less-than-lethal. Clones reported this appearing as a flashbang when fired. \
**Base Modifiers:** The weapon profile becomes ([[Ranged - Heavy (Ag)|Ranged (Heavy)]]; Damage 12; Critical –; Range \[Short\]; [[Item Qualities#Concussive (Active)|Concussive]] 1, [[Item Qualities#Disorient (Active)|Disorient]] 1, [[Item Qualities#Knockdown (Active)|Knockdown]], [[Item Qualities#Slow-Firing (Passive)|Slow-Firing]] 1, [[Item Qualities#Stun damage (Passive)|Stun Damage]]). \
**Modification Options:** 1 Weapon Quality ([[Item Qualities#Concussive (Active)|Concussive]] +1) Mods, 2 Weapon Quality ([[Item Qualities#Disorient (Active)|Disorient]] +1) Mods. \
**Hard points Requirement:** 0 (mutually exclusive with other custom attachments). \
**Price/Rarity:** 500 credits (R)/10. \
**Encumbrance:** 1.

### CUSTOM ATTACHMENT

Source: [^3]

**Rapid-Entry Breaching Grenade:** Designed by Merr-Sonn in the third year of the Clone Wars, this mode appeared as a somewhat miniature and alternative version to the anti-armour mode. It differed from the other modes in that it could attach to the base model without changing the weapon mode. This allowed for the user to quickly follow up with a normal burst of the blaster, or even a sniper shot. \
**Base Modifiers:** Enables the weapon to fire using the following profile at the user’s choice: ([[Ranged - Heavy (Ag)|Ranged (Heavy)]]; Damage 5; Critical 3; Range \[Short\]; [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] 2, [[Item Qualities#Limited Ammo (Passive)|Limited Ammo]] 1, [[Item Qualities#Inaccurate (Passive)|Inaccurate]] 2). \
**Modification Options:** 1 Weapon Quality ([[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] +1) Mod, 2 Weapon Quality ([[Item Qualities#Limited Ammo (Passive)|Limited Ammo]] +1) Mods. \
**Hard points Requirement:** 1. \
**Price/Rarity:** 250 credits (R)/10. \
**Encumbrance:** 0.
