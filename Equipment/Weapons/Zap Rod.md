Melee weapons usually seen in the hands of local security personnel, zap rods are designed to give an electric shock when one end is touched to an individual. They usually come equipped with a small slider, to change the level of electric shock, everything from a mild buzz to a bolt strong enough to cause semipermanent damage to many species.

Cheap and effective, zap rods are often employed in riot control scenarios, though the weapons have been known to react poorly, and even fail completely, in adverse weather. Imperial personnel prefer to use the more expensive, but more reliable electrobaton in their riot control operations.
