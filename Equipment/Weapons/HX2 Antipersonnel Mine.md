The HX2 is a particularly old model of anti-infantry mine produced by Conner Ship Systems. The HX2 followed on from an Old Republic design, the HX mine, but with little innovation. Due to its availability during the High Republic period, there was no reason for the company to innovate in its production. The fairly small load of detonite within this mine made it cheap by the time of the Clone Wars, giving it a blast only somewhat larger than most fragmentation grenades. At least it was fairly small, able to be carried by commando and other specialty units fairly easily.
