To all appearances, needle gloves look like typical hand coverings; however, with a specific hand gesture, the wearer can extend an ultrathin needle that is otherwise nearly undetectable.

## NEEDLE GLOVES

Needle gloves may be loaded with one dose of [[Synthetic Neurotoxin|neurotoxin]], [[Synthetic Anesthetic|anesthetic]], [[Synthetic Neuroparalytic|neuroparalytic]], or any other poison the GM deems appropriate. Whenever the wielder successfully attacks a target with the weapon (even if they deal no damage), the target also suffers the effects of being exposed to the poison. Add three [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice to a character's [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] checks to determine that a set of needle gloves contains a lethal weapon.
