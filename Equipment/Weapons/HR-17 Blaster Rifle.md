Originally developed for use on colony worlds, the HR-17 has become one of the most common blaster rifles for planetary security forces. Although it lacks the range of many other comparable blaster rifles, the HR-17’s relatively light weight, reliability, and ease of maintenance make it an effective weapon in the hands of a capable shooter.
