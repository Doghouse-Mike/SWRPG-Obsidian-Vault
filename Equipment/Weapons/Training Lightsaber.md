Ostensibly non-lethal variants of the standard lightsaber, training lightsabers are low-powered lightsabers used to train Jedi youngling ins lightsaber combat.

**Models Include:** None; each training lightsaber is individually crafted.

> More information about the weapon available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber).

This lightsaber is a [[Basic Lightsaber Hilt|basic training lightsaber hilt]] containing an unmodded [[Training Lightsaber Emitter|training lightsaber crystal]]. The crystal occupies two of the weapon's hard points.[^2-1][^3]

Lightsabers cannot be Sundered.[^4][^5]
