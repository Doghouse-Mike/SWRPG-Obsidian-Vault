The DE-10's telescopic optical sight reduces the difficulty of combat checks made at long and extreme range by one. A DE-10 in need of minor repairs does not add a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die to checks. Add a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to checks made to repair a damaged DE-10.[^2]
