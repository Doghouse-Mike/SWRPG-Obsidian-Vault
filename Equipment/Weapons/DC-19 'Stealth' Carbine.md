In need of a true stealth operations weapon, BlasTech Industries was contracted to provide the Republic with a weapon that would create virtually no noise or visual trace when fired. They used the DC-15a carbine as a starting point, adding on a proprietary sound suppressor unit. Then, in a technique which would be replicated by the Xerrol Corporation in their [[Nightstinger Blaster Rifle|Nightstinger]] line of hunting rifles, BlasTech engineers developed a special mixture of tibanna gas. The mixture resulted in blaster bolts which emitted no light to the naked eye, making them virtually invisible. The carbine was given a optional stealth mode which would dispense an additive to the gas before it was used to fire. This made reloads for the weapon expensive, as they were only available directly from BlasTech; and the resulting shot emitted much more heat into the weapon.

The built-in suppressor increases the difficulty of [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] and [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]] checks to locate the user by sound twice. Using the DC-19 in Stealth mode requires a maneuver to activate. This makes locating the shooter by tracing the blaster bolt or spotting a muzzle blast effectively impossible. While in this mode, it gains the [[Item Qualities#Slow-Firing (Passive)|Slow-Firing]] 1 quality and runs out of ammunition when a combat check generates two [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results.[^0-2]

The special gas spin used in this blaster means that a special [[Extra Reload]] priced at 500 credits is needed to reload it.[^0-2]
