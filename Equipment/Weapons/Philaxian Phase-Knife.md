## PHILAXIAN PHASE-KNIFE

Source: [^0-1][^2]

![Skill Lightsaber Range Engaged Encumbrance 1 Price 1,500 Rarity 8 Damage +1 Critical Rating 3 Hard Points 1 Special Pierce 4, Vicious 1|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/0/02/Profile_Philaxian_Phase-Knife.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Skill Lightsaber Range Engaged Encumbrance 1 Price 1,500 Rarity 8 Damage +1 Critical Rating 3 Hard Points 1 Special Pierce 4, Vicious 1

When used with the [[Saber Throw talent]], a character may spend two [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result (instead of a [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]]) to have the phase-knife return to their hand. A user may not add lightsaber crystal attachments to a phase-knife. Anyone searching a character carrying a deactivated phase-knife adds a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die to the [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] check to find the weapon.[^0-1][^3]
