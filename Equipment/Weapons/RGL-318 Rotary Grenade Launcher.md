## RGL-318 ROTARY GRENADE LAUNCHER

Source: [^1]

> [!quote] Image text
> Skill Gunnery Range Medium Encumbrance 5 Price (R) 1,000 Rarity 4 Damage 8 Critical Rating 4 Hard Points 3 Special Blast 6, Cumbersome 3, Limited Ammo 5

The weapon's listed price does not include ammunition. Grenades for the RGL-318 cost the same as equivalent hand grenades, but many dealers offer a 50-percent discount on cases of six. Any type of grenade can be chambered by the RGL-318, but the grenades must be specifically made to be fired from the grenade launcher. Launcher grenades cannot be thrown. The listed profile represents an RGL-318 loaded with standard [[Fragmentation Grenade|frag grenades]].[^2]
