The Panic Pistol is Oriolanis Defense Systems' compact civilian self-defense slugthrower pistol.

> More information about the weapon available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Panic_Pistol).

While at engaged range, a character wielding a Panic Pistol does not add the +1 difficulty modifier for ranged attacks in melee. However, the GM may spend two [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat results]] or a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair result]] to damage the weapon one step.[^0-1][^3]
