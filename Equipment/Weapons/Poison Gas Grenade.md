## POISON GAS GRENADE

Upon impact, the grenade uses several emitters to spray aerosolized [[Synthetic Neurotoxin|neurotoxin]], [[Synthetic Anesthetic|anesthetic]], [[Synthetic Neuroparalytic|neuroparalytic]], or any other poison at the GM's discretion.[^0-1]

Poison gas grenades can be loaded with up to 1 dose of [[Synthetic Neurotoxin|neurotoxin]], [[Synthetic Anesthetic|anesthetic]], or [[Synthetic Neuroparalytic|neuroparalytic]] or any other poison at the GM's deems suitable. Whenever the wielder successfully attacks a target with the weapon (whether or not \[they\] deal damage), the target also suffers the effects of being exposed to the poison.[^3]

Unless a poison gas grenade is used outdoors in a windy environment, the poison gas lingers for two rounds, affecting anyone caught in the cloud. If the grenade is detonated in a closed, poorly ventilated space, such as a small room or narrow starship corridor, the gas lingers for five rounds instead. Poison gas grenades do not harm droids and other creatures that do not breathe air. Characters equipped with vacuum-sealed suits, breath masks, respirators, or rebreathers are also unaffected by poison gas, at the GM's discretion.[^0-1][^4]
