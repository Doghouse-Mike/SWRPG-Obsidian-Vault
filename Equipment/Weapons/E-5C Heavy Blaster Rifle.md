The Baktoid Armour Workshop continued to iterate on the [[E-5 Blaster Rifle|E-5 blaster rifle]] they shamelessly reverse engineered. In a rare display of creativity from the very same corporate entity which stole BlasTech designs, they increased the rate of fire by increasing the spin capacity of the main barrel. Of course, given that this weapon was due for the hands of dispensible B1 model battledroids, it was not reinforced and the safety features were left stripped. This makes this ‘heavy’ rifle particularly light for the firepower available, while dangerous.

As with the base model, the E-5C is of poor build quality. When the weapon would suffer damage, it suffers an additional step. In addition, the removed safety features are particularly dangerous with the E-5C. The GM may use two [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result to cause the weapon to overheat. This damages the weapon and causes an organic using the weapon to suffer 3 wounds.[^0-2]
