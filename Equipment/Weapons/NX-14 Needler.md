A needler removes all [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice added by a personal deflector shield. Anyone searching an individual carrying a concealed needler adds one [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die to their [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] check to find the weapon.[^0-1][^3]
