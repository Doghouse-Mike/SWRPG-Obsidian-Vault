Missile tubes are shoulder-fired, portable launchers capable of firing a variety of small but deadly warheads.

> More information about the weapon available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Missile_launcher).

**Models Include:** Golan Arms HH-15, Merr-Sonn PLX-2M.

Additional ammo for a missile tube costs 500 credits per rack of six.[^0-1][^5] Other missiles may be substituted for the standard type, replacing the damage, critical rating, range, and special qualities with that of the alternate missile. Alternate missiles may be purchased for their listed price.[^0-1]
