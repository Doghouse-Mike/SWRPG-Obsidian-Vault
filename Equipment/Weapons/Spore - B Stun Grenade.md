## SPORE/B STUN GRENADE

Source: [^1]

> [!quote] Image text
> Skill Ranged (Light) Range Short Encumbrance 1 Price 400 Rarity 5 Damage 6 Critical Rating 3 Hard Points 0 Special Blast 6, Concussive 2, Limited Ammo 1, Stun Damage

The first time a character is hit with an attack from this weapon (even if \[they\] suffer no damage), \[they\] must roll a single [[Narrative Dice#Challenge (Red)|challenge]] die. As long as the character doesn't roll a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result on this, \[they\] are affected normally by the Spore/B stun grenade. If the roll does result in a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]], the character immediately suffers 6 strain and begins to suffocate. A **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Medicine (Int)|Medicine check]]** is required to stop the subject from suffocating.[^2]
