BlasTech's HH-50 is a heavy, double-barreled, large frame blaster pistol designed mainly for hunting large and dangerous animals.

During combat, Game Masters may make an HH-50 run out of ammunition by spending two [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results. In addition, the Game Master can spend three [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result to force an HH-50 to overheat, damaging it by one step as if stuck by a weapon with the [[Item Qualities#Sunder (Active)|Sunder]] quality.
