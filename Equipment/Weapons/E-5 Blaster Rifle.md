The E-5 blaster rifle is a copy of a BlasTech design produced by Baktoid Armor Workshop for the Separatist armed forces.

> More information about the weapon available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/E-5_blaster_rifle).

If an E-5 suffers damage, it suffers one additional step of damage. Game Masters may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result or three [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results generated on a combat check with an E-5 to cause the weapon to overheat. This damages the weapon and deals 2 wounds to the wielder if they are organic.
