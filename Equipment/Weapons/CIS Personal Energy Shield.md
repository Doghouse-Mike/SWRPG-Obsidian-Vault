Seen primarily in the hand of the Confederacy’s BX- series commando droids, this energy shield has been stylised with the CIS insignia as inspiration. Able of providing near-full cover from incoming fire, the shield allows a user to operate like a droideka. The energy field allows for physical interaction to pass through it, used by commando droids to keep their arms defended while shooting their E-5 carbines slipped through the field. Unfortunately, this benefit causes the shield to be weak against a grenade assault or the rarer non-energy weapons of the galaxy.
