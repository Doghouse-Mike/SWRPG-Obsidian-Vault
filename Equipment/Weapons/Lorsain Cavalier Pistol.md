Beautifully hand-crafted by artisan weaponsmiths from the Lorsain House on the planet Caber Callis, these elegant pistols are a sight to behold. The Lorsain Cavalier pistols were originally designed centuries ago as duelling pistols, however when that practice was outlawed, the Lorsain House re-positioned the Cavalier, as a “premier hunting pistol.” While the weapon’s beautiful build ensures the weapon is forever precise, its low capacity and weak stopping power cement it as a highly-sought out collector’s item and less of a day-to-day blaster.
