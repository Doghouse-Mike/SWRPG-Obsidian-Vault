Heavy repeating blasters are among the heaviest portable weapons deployed on the galaxy's battlefields.

> More information about the weapon available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Heavy_repeating_blaster).

**Models Include:** BlasTech EWHB-12, BlasTech E-Web, Merr-Sonn Mk. II.

Thanks to their size and weight, heavy repeating blaster are nearly impossible to use effectively without being mounted to a [[Tripod Mount|tripod]] \[...\]. The weapon's cost includes the cost of a [[Tripod Mount|tripod]] and power pack. Setting up a heavy repeater requires the gun crew to spend three actions.[^4][^5]
