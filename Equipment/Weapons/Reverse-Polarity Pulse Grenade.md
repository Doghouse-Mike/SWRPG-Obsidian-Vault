Named after the special form of anti-machinery pulse produced by the grenade, a reverse-polarity pulse wave was capable of entirely shutting down droids and other electronics by causing their systems to backfire and trigger emergency cut-off to the power capacitor. This grenade featured a distinct profile, appearing as a disk rather than the common sphere or ovoid structure. Due to their specialised nature, they were used by the Republic’s ARC troopers, who had the skill to use them without shorting out their own armour’s systems.

If a droid is dealt damage by the RPP grenade, the user may spend three [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result to force them to make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]]** **[[Discipline (Will)|Discipline]]** or [[Resilience (Br)|**Resilience check**]]. Failure on this check causes the droid to shut down until the end of the encounter. If a check against a target within Short range or closer generates two [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result, the GM may cause the pulse to short out an electronic system of the user until the end of the encounter.[^0-2]
