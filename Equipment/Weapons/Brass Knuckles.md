Known colloquially as "knuckle-dusters" in many locations, brass knuckles are a set of thick metal rings welded to a horizontal bar held in the fist.

> More information about the weapon available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Brass_knuckles).

**Models Include:** Various models.
