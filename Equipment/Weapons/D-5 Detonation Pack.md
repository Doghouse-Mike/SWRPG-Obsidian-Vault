The D-5 detonation pack is a self-contained explosive package produced by Merr-Sonn Munitions Inc. for use by industrial interests. It uses a pair of stabilised baradium half-charges, containing them in boxy structures on either side of a central signal receiver and detonator matrix. Due to its intended use as a demolitions charge, once active it produces a bright and flashing red light from the signal receiver unit. Despite this, the D-5 has been picked up widely for use by armed forces across the galaxy as a quick and easy anti-vehicle weapon. Due to its high yield, it has been made illegal for civilian ownership on most worlds.
