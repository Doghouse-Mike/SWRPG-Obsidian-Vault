Iterating on BlasTech Industries’ DC-15, the LE model was intended to turn the already excellent—albeit cumbersome—rifle into the equivalent of a light repeater. With the LE signifying ‘light expansion’, this particular model had its plating reinforced. The resulting rifle was a thicker and almost ‘upscaled’ version of the DC-15. The additional build strength allowed for the powercell and spincycle to be overclocked for additional stopping power and fire rate.

> More information about the weapon available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DC-15LE).

When using the DC-15LE, the user can use a maneuver to flick on the explosive shot setting. While in this mode the Critical Rating is increased to 4 and the weapon gains the [[Item Qualities#Blast (Active)|Blast]] 6 quality. While using this setting, the GM may spend three [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results to damage the rifle by one step as the volatile bolts overheat the barrel.[^2]
