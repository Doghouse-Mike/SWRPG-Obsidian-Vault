An ancient design of the Stokhli people from the Inner Rim world of Manress, the spray stick is a meter-long rod shouldered like a rifle that fires a concentrated spraynet mist.

> More information about the weapon available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Spray_stick).
