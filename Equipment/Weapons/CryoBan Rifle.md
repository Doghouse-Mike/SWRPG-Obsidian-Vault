When used to attack a target wearing armor that removes one or more [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice due to extreme cold, the weapon's [[Item Qualities#Pierce (Passive)|Pierce]] quality counts as 0. Characters who make a melee attack against a target struck by a CryoBan rifle in the current round add two [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] dice to the check.
