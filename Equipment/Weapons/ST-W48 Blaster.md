The ST-W48 is an aggressive blaster employed by the deadly Sith troopers, designed primarily for close-quarters combat. It packs the rapid-fire power of a heavy blaster rifle into a smaller frame, while including a strengthened mounting to mitigate the increased recoil. When an explosive punch is needed, the ST-W48 also incorporates under-barrel bowcaster technology without the weight or strength needed for an actual Wookiee bowcaster.

> More information about the weapon available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ST-W48_blaster_rifle).
