> Skill Ranged (Light) Range Medium Encumbrance 2 Price Not for Sale Rarity 10 Damage 7 Critical Rating 3 Hard Points - Special Stun setting, Overcharged

**Overcharged:** Once per round as a maneuver, the wielder may set Han Solo's blaster to fire an overcharged shot. Upgrade the difficulty of the next attack with this weapon once to add the [[Item Qualities#Pierce (Passive)|Pierce]] 7 item quality to that attack. If the attack generates two [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat results]] or a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair result]], the GM may cause the blaster to run out of ammo.

### BORING CONVERSATION ANYWAY

Once per encounter when Han Solo fails a Deception check, he may spend two [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage results]] to immediately draw his blaster and make a combat check targeting the target of the failed check as an incidental.
