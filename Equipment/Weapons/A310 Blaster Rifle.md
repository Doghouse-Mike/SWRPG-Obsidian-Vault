A high impact sniper rifle, the A310 is produced by BlasTech and bears a striking resemblance to many other rifles in the company’s “A” series. The A310 features a longer, perforated, air-cooling barrel shroud coated in porcecrete which allows the weapon to perform much better at long ranges. The hefty, two-handed blaster also features an enhanced scope for targeting through darkness and adverse weather conditions. The A310 is highly effective at piercing through armour, thanks to its increased power pack. However, unlike the Imperial E-22, the A310 is a much more budget rifle, constructed from generally cheaper and more inferior parts, making it more prone to breakdowns and parts replacement.

During the Clone Wars, the A310 was widely used by local planetary defence forces and after the war, a great many of them slipped on to the black market and became readily available.
