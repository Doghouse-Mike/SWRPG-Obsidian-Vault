Ceremonial weapons from the remote Outer Rim world of Utapau, the Utapaun Monk Cudgel is a half metre long, spiked weapon of pure bronzium.

Half of the weapon consists of a handle, which has a ribbed portion at the top and series of inset opalescent squares lower down. Each square represents an era of Utapaun history. The rest of the weapon is the solid barrel of the cudgel. Two rows of spikes line this portion of the weapon, and culminate in a pyramidal head.

While replicas of these weapons are common throughout the galaxy, original cudgels from the eighth archaic settlement are rare and expensive treasures indeed.
