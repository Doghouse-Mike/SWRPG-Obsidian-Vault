Holdout blasters are lightweight, small-framed pistols designed for ease of handling and concealment.

> More information about the weapon available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hold-out_blaster).

**Models Include:** BlasTech HSB-200, Imperial Munitions Model 22T4 ISB Special, [[12 Defender|Gee-Tech No.12 "Defender" Disposable MicroBlaster]], Imperial Army Scout Trooper Blaster Pistol, Merr-Sonn Q2 Holdout.
