In an explosive device using proton grenades, the second grenade adds +20 damage, the third adds +30, the fourth adds +40, and the fifth adds +50, for a total of 150 damage for five grenades. After the fifth grenade, additional grenades do not add additional damage, but they do increase the blast radius by one range band per additional grenade.[^0-1][^3]
