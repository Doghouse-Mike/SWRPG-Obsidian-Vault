Officially outlawed by the Republic, the Ricot Buzzclaw is a brutal and evil weapon. Developed on an unknown Inner Rim planet, the Buzzclaw is heavy blaster pistol. Almost insectoid in nature, the Buzzclaw is all jagged edges and twisting curves. Unlike the vast majority of modern blasters, the Buzzclaw is a slughtrower. A cartridge is fired at high velocity from the weapon, this cartridge splits apart while being fired and explodes into a mass of razor sharp blades. This scattershot approach makes the weapon incredibly inaccurate at long range, but brutally effective at short range. The Ricot Buzzclaw is often seen in the hands of only the most bloodthirsty of pirates and outlaws.
