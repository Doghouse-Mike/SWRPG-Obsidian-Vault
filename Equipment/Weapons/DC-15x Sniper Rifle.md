When clones speak of using the DC-15x sniper rifle they don’t usually distinguish between what amounts to a DC-15 with an improved scope and sighting system, and the actual DC-15x manufactured for dedicated sniper units. BlasTech’s specially designed DC-15x heavily strips down the excess armour and cooling coils of the base rifle. This allows it to be more single-shot accurate, with a loss of sustained fire abilities. The telescopic sight installed on this rifle keeps snipers in a familiar environment as it is largely identical to that installed by non-dedicated sharpshooter units.

The DC-15x features a built-in scope that reduces the difficulty of combat checks at Long and Extreme range by one. A character can ignore the [[Item Qualities#Slow-Firing (Passive)|Slow-Firing]] quality of the DC-15x by adding a cumulative [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback die]] to the combat check for each time the weapon is fired without going unused for a round. During this time, it also loses the [[Item Qualities#Accurate (Passive)|Accurate]] quality.[^2]
