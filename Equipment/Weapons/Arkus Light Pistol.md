Produced by Czerka Arms, the inexpensive Arkus pistol is often seen in the hands of law enforcement personnel or cost-conscious mercenaries and bounty hunters.

The small black pistol has a longer stun range than most other light blasters, which gives the weapon an advantage for those who wish to cause non-lethal damage. The Arkus also has a mounting rail for a scope, allowing the weapon to function as a simplistic targeting pistol. It’s common to see customised logos, symbols stamped into the grip of the Arukus.
