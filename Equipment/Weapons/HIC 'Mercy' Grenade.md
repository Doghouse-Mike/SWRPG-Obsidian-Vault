The "Mercy" grenade is a unique gas-based stun grenade which is typically used by large corporations and by covert agencies via shell corporations.

> More information about the weapon available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/%22Mercy%22_grenade).

## HIC "MERCY" GRENADE

Source: [^1][^0-2]

![Skill Ranged (Light) Range Short Encumbrance 1 Price 175 Rarity 8 Damage 5 Critical Rating - Hard Points 0 Special Blast 5, Limited Ammo 1, Stun Damage|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/a/a7/Profile_HIC_Mercy_Grenade.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Skill Ranged (Light) Range Short Encumbrance 1 Price 175 Rarity 8 Damage 5 Critical Rating - Hard Points 0 Special Blast 5, Limited Ammo 1, Stun Damage

A character hit by a Mercy grenade must make a [[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|**Hard difficulty**]] **[[Resilience (Br)|Resilience check]]** to avoid having an incomplete or inaccurate memory of events that transpire during that encounter. Additionally, they become disoriented for one round per [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] result on the check.

### TRAPS AND TRIPWIRES

Source: [^0-2]

As with frag grenades, a character can set \[...\] "Mercy" grenades \[...\] to detonate on impact, set them with a timer to detonate up to three rounds after being activated, or set up the explosive with connected a pressure detonator.
