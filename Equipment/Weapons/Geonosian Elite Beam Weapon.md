This exotic and powerful weapon was specifically designed for use by the elites of the Geonosian warrior caste. It would bury into the arm of a Geonosian and drain some of its body fluids to produce the intense beam which could cut through biologicals with ease. While otherwise unusable by other species, they could be found already loaded with the Geonosian fluids, allowing a few shots before the weapon becomes useless.

The profile assumes use by a non-Geonosian. When used by a Geonosian, the elite beam weapon loses the [[Item Qualities#Unwieldy (Passive)|Unwieldy]] and [[Item Qualities#Inaccurate (Passive)|Inaccurate]] qualities. In addition, if the weapon would run out of ammunition (due to the [[Item Qualities#Limited Ammo (Passive)|Limited Ammo]] quality), the weapon can be refilled by with their own bodily fluids. The Geonosian suffers 2 wounds and 2 strain as a maneuver to reload the weapon.
