A blaster being "lucky" is less about its manufacture and more about its behavior.

## LUCKY BLASTER

Source: [^1]

![Skill Ranged (Light) Range Medium Encumbrance 1 Price 400 Rarity 10 Damage 5 Critical Rating 4 Hard Points 2 Special Stun Setting|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/c/c8/Profile_Lucky_Blaster.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Skill Ranged (Light) Range Medium Encumbrance 1 Price 400 Rarity 10 Damage 5 Critical Rating 4 Hard Points 2 Special Stun Setting

When a characters uses a single maneuver with a Lucky Blaster to Aim, they gain two [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die on their next combat check. This can only occur once per round, and the PC may not use their second maneuver to aim again.
