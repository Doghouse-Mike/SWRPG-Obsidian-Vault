Tenloss Syndicates' rifle-sized L70 acid projector, which is banned in virtually all systems, fires a stream of xenoboric acid capable of penetrating virtually any material.

## L70 ACID PROJECTOR

Source: [^0-1][^2]

Acid projectors operate similarly to flame projectors, spraying targets in a narrow cone. When triggering the weapon's [[Item Qualities#Burn (Active)|Burn]] quality, the user may choose to apply it to any one target hit by the attack; this quality may be triggered multiple times, affecting a different target each time. The GM can spend two [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result to apply the weapon's [[Item Qualities#Burn (Active)|Burn]] quality to an ally hit by the original attack. Since the burn is chemical in nature, the Burn damage cannot be stopped by rolling around on the ground or jumping into water.[^0-1][^3]
