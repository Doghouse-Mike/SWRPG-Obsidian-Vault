The LS-150 was the heavy cousin to the [[ACP Repeater Gun]] produced by Arakyd Industries. Bringing the name “Trandoshan Chopper” to an entirely new level, the sheer volume of fire coming out of this repeater would easily overcome the usual trouble it had with armoured targets. Given how often it would run out of ammunition and general lack of utility in close quarter combat, the standard model included a basic bayonet.
