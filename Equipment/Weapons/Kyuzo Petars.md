Designed by the nomadic warrior Kyuzo species, Kyuzo Petars are brass knuckles with wicked curved blades extending from either side as if the wearer were holding a knife in a standard and backhand grip simultaneously.

> More information about the weapon available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Petar).
