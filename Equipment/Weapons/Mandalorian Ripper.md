An energy-propelled slugthrower of Mandalorian make, the ripper is a powerful weapon that fires unguided shrapnel at high speeds. The projectiles are capable of piercing personal shielding and cause deep, grievous wounds to the target. Despite being slugthrowers, these weapons are classified as disruptor weaponry and are illegal to produce or possess on any Republic world.
