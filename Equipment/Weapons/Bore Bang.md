The bore bang, as it is referred to by most users, is essentially a high potency sonic grenade on a drill end. Largely useless anywhere but the wilderness, where soft ground is plentiful, this niche ordnance had its use-case where it would excel. As along as you knew where to throw the grenade, it would bury deep into the soil before detonating. This rendered the area momentarily porous and buried anyone standing nearby.
