Created by BlasTech Industries for use by the Grand Army of the Republic, this light blaster pistol was exclusively used by clone commandos. Designed as an efficient backup weapon system which could recharge itself on the field, it was used for conserving ammunition or for particularly close quarters combat.

> More information about the weapon available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unidentified_blaster_pistol_model).
