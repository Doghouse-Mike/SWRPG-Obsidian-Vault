SoroSuub's Wipe-3 data-purge grenade erases electronically stored data from all devices within its blast radius with a brief, but intense, electromagnetic pulse.

> More information about the weapon available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wipe-3_data-purge_grenade).

## WIPE-3 DATA-PURGE GRENADE

Source: [^1][^0-2]

![Skill Ranged (Light) Range Short Encumbrance 1 Price 100 Rarity 7 Damage - Critical Rating - Hard Points 0 Special Limited Ammo 1|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/b/b5/Profile_Wipe-3_Data-Purge_Grenade.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Skill Ranged (Light) Range Short Encumbrance 1 Price 100 Rarity 7 Damage - Critical Rating - Hard Points 0 Special Limited Ammo 1

Any computer or similar device hit by a data-purge grenade loses all programming, memory, and any other kind of electronically stored data but remains otherwise undamaged. A droid hit by a data-purge grenade must succeed on a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Resilience (Br)|Resilience check]]** or suffer 1 strain, plus 1 additional strain per [[Narrative Dice#Failure|failure]] result. At the GM's discretion, three [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result on the check can cause the droid to lose portions of its memory.

### TRAPS AND TRIPWIRES

Source: [^0-2]

As with frag grenades, a character can set \[...\] data-purge grenades \[...\] to detonate on impact, set them with a timer to detonate up to three rounds after being activated, or set up the explosive with connected a pressure detonator.
