Considered by many to be the smallest ranged weapon in the galaxy, the Prax Arms Stealth-2VX palm shooter can, as then name suggests, easily fit in the palm of a user's hand.

> More information about the weapon available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Stealth-2VX_palm_shooter).

Each dart can be loaded with one dose of [[Synthetic Neurotoxin|neurotoxin]], [[Synthetic Anesthetic|anesthetic]], [[Synthetic Neuroparalytic|neuroparalytic]], or any other poison the GM deems appropriate. Whenever the wielder successfully attack a target with the weapon (whether or not they deal damage), the target also suffers the effects of being exposed to the poison.[^0-1][^1-3]

Add two [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice to [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] checks to find a Stealth-2VX Palm Shooter on a wearer's body.[^1-3]
