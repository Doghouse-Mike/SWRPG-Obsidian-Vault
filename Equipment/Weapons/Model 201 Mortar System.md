The Model 201 mortar is a portable light artillery system used by specialized clone artillery troopers.

> More information about the weapon available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Model_201_mortar_system).

## MODEL 201 MORTAR SYSTEM

The weapon consists of three parts—the launcher, a heavy bipod, and the ammunition magazine—and is operated by a specially trained mortar team. A mortar team consists of three clones: one to carry the weapon, one to carry the bipod, and one to carry the ammo.[^0-2]

The Model 201 can only be used at medium range or further due to the arc of its shells.[^0-2]
