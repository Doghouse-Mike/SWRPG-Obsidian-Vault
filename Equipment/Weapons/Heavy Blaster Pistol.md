Nearly as powerful as most blaster rifles, heavy blaster pistols are large, bulky sidearms that are nearly impossible to conceal.

> More information about the weapon available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Heavy_blaster_pistol).

**Models Include:** BlasTech DT-57 "Annihilator", BlasTech DL-44, SoroSuub SSK-7, Merr-Sonn Model 434.
