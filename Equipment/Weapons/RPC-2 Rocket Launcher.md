The RPC-2 rocket launcher was a lightweight and downscaled version of the [[RPS-6 Rocket Launcher|RPS-6 rocket launcher]] by Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.. Designed with space for a single rocket, this launcher was carried on the AT-RT used by the ARF troopers of the GAR. The weapon was a simple anti-armour solution and was used extensively in ambushes, taking the opportunity of weak sides and rears to disable CIS armour columns. In so far as it was a lightweight anti-vehicle weapon produced cheaply, the RPC-2 is the GAR’s equivalent to the [[E-60R Missile Launcher|E-60R]]. However, it doesn’t suffer as many setbacks in its package and has a quality build for what it seeks to achieve.
