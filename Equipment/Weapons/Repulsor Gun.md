Little more than toys for bored technicians, repulsor guns are makeshift devices crafted from a small repulsor engine, a charge capacitor, and a trigger assembly.

> More information about the weapon available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Repulsor_gun).
