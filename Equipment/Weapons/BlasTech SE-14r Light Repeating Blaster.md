The BlasTech SE-14r, also known as the Imperial repeating pistol, combines the portability and ease of a blaster pistol with a high rate of fire.

> More information about the weapon available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/SE-14r_light_repeating_blaster).

Due to its high rate of fire, the SE-14r is more likely to run out of ammo during an extended firefight or series of engagements. The GM may spend three [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results to cause a character's SE-14r to run out of ammo.[^2]
