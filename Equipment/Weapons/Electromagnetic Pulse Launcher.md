The EMP launcher is a highly specialised form of grenade launcher which was produced for use by the GAR to fight the droid armies of the CIS. Merr-Sonn engineers sought to create a weapon which would specifically destroy droids as effectively as a single shot could. Using similar technology to what is found in droid disablers and ion blasters, the EMP launcher would fire a highly concentrated EMP contained in a stable field which gave it a proton torpedo-like appearance. Once it impacts with a surface, the pulse is released and any electronics at the point of impact receives an overload much greater than from a regular EMP grenade or personal ion weapon. As with other [[Item Qualities#Ion (Passive)|Ion]] weapons, the EMP launcher can only deal damage to droids and those with considerable cybernetic enhancements.
