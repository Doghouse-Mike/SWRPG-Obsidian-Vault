A miniaturized lightsaber, the shoto was initially designed as an offhand weapon for duelists training in two-handed Jar'Kai lightsaber combat.

**Models Include:** None; each shoto is individually crafted.

> More information about the weapon available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shoto_lightsaber).

### DURING THE CLONE WARS: SHOTO

Source: [^1-3]

![Skill Lightsaber Range Engaged Encumbrance 1 Price 8,000 Rarity 10 Damage 5 Critical Rating 2 Hard Points 3 Special Accurate 1, Breach 1, Sunder|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/3/37/Profile_Shoto.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Skill Lightsaber Range Engaged Encumbrance 1 Price 8,000 Rarity 10 Damage 5 Critical Rating 2 Hard Points 3 Special Accurate 1, Breach 1, Sunder

This lightsaber is a [[Shoto Hilt|basic shoto hilt]] containing an unmodded [[Ilum Crystal|Ilum lightsaber crysta]]l. This crystal occupies two of the weapon's hard points.[^0-1][^1-3][^2-2]

When installing a lightsaber crystal into a shoto, reduce the damage of that lightsaber crystal by one.[^0-1][^1-3][^2-2]

Lightsabers cannot be Sundered.[^4][^5]
