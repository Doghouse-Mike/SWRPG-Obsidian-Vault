Sonic pistols are small single-handed weapons similar to a blaster pistol. Instead of firing blaster bolts however, they produce high-intensity sonic blasts that are powerful enough to shatter solid objects and cause injuries to organic beings. While initially designed as civilian defense weapons, their usefulness against Lightsaber users has caused a spike in purchases as the war escalates across the galaxy.
