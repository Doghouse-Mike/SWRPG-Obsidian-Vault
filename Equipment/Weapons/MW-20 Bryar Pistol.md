A highly accurate sidearm produced by Blastech Industries, the MW-20 Bryar is slightly less common than its older brother, the K-16 Bryar. The large, single-handed pistol features a long and bulky frame built around a slim grip.

Unlike the K-16, the MW-20 features a larger middle section to account for the pistol’s unique rotating barrel ability. To prevent the fast-firing weapon from overheating, a rotating middle section could install a new, cold barrel into the front end of the weapon, allowing it to continue its sustained fire. This feature increases the weight and bulk of the weapon, sacrificing some of the portability and lightness that makes the K-16 Bryar so popular. Nevertheless, for someone looking for accuracy and increased stopping power, the MW-20 is hard to beat.
