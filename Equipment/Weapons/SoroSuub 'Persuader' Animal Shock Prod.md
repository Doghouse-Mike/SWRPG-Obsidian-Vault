## SOROSUUB "PERSUADER" ANIMAL SHOCK PROD

> [!quote] Image text
> Skill Melee Range Engaged Encumbrance 4 Price 1,000 Rarity 4 Damage +2 Critical Rating 4 Hard Points 2 Special Concussive 1, Slow Firing 1, Stun Setting

Persuader Animal Shock Prods require two hands to wield. This weapon has the [[Narrative Dice#Slow-Firing (Passive)|Slow-Firing]] 1 rule, representing its electrical charge having to recharge. On rounds during which it is recharging, if the character wants to attack with it, \[they\] can only use it as an improvised weapon.

```
While Stay on Target: A Sourcebook for Aces does not specify the Slow-Firing rating, the Errata (Page 3) corrects this to "Slow-Firing 1".
```
