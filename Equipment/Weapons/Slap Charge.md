These explosive devices come from hundreds of different brands and are some of the cheapest and most basic explosives available on the market today. First developed by Grimmalite Tech as “Kinetic Decommissioning Blast Charges”, the design of these explosives was immediately copied and distributed across the galaxy. Slap charges work simply, as soon as the small disc is removed from its container and affixed to a surface with a small amount of pressure, it is activated and will explode in 30 standard seconds time. There is no complicated fuse to set, or power level to monitor or remote trigger to activate, it is just slapped and then it explodes.

Because of the cheapness and ease of use of slap charges, they are ubiquitous amongst criminal gangs, budget security forces and resistance groups.
