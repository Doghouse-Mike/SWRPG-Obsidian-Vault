A luxurious weapon produced by Concordian Crescent Technologies, this blaster was produced with dallorian alloy, making it capable of withstanding the high temperatures of continual firing. This weapon had a short production run and was quickly discontinued when the Mandalorian government mandated stun settings in all weapons oriented for civilian sale.
