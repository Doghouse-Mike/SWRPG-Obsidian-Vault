Also called lightstaves or Sith lightsabers, double-bladed lightsabers date from the mythical Sith Lord Exar Kun of the Qel-Droma Epics.

> More information about the weapon available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Double-bladed_lightsaber).

## DOUBLE-BLADED LIGHTSABER

Source: [^0-1][^1-2][^3]

![Skill Lightsaber Range Engaged Encumbrance 2 Price (R) 18,600 Rarity 10 Damage 6 Critical Rating 2 Hard Points 4 Special Breach 1, Linked 1, Sunder, Unwieldy 3|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/2/27/Profile_Double-Bladed_Lightsaber.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Skill Lightsaber Range Engaged Encumbrance 2 Price (R) 18,600 Rarity 10 Damage 6 Critical Rating 2 Hard Points 4 Special Breach 1, Linked 1, Sunder, Unwieldy 3

Although this weapon contains two lightsaber crystals (one for each blade), for the purposes of gameplay it is treated as having a single crystal. This lightsaber is a [[Double-Bladed Lightsaber Hilt|double-bladed lightsaber hilt]] containing an unmodded [[Ilum Crystal|Ilum lightsaber crystal]]. This crystal occupies two of the weapon's hard points.[^0-1][^1-2][^2-4]

When purchasing attachments for this weapon (including crystals), each attachment costs double the listed price. This represents the fact that the double-bladed lightsaber generally requires two of each attachment (one for each end). However, when installing and modding these attachments, follow all normal rules.[^0-1][^1-2][^2-4]

Lightsabers cannot be sundered.[^5]
