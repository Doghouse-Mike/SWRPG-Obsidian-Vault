Whenever a Force-sensitive wielder of a Sith shield suffers a hit in combat, \[they\] may commit a [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] die to channel \[their\] fury into the shield as an out of turn incidental. Over multiple attacks, \[they\] may commit multiple [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] dice this way, up to a total number of [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] dice no greater than \[their\] Force rating.[^0-2]

As an action, the wielder may uncommit all [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] dice committed to the Sith shield to unleash the stored power in a torrent of hate. When \[they\] do so, \[they\] gain Conflict equal to the number of [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] dice \[they\] had committed to the shield and then makes a Force power check with that many [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] dice. \[They\] may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]] to inflict 1 wound and 1 strain (these ignore soak) on each other character within short range of \[them\]; \[they\] may activate this multiple times, inflicting 1 additional wound and 1 additional strain each time.[^0-2]
