These elegant, light and effective weapons were crafted in honor of some of the greatest Echani duelists centuries ago. With the ability to stand up to a Lightsaber, and a honed edge that easily pierces flesh and armor, they are truly weapons of class. Unfortunately, impurities in the cortosis weave has seen few of these weapons survive the test of time.
