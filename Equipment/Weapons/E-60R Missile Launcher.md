Another cheap product courtesy of the Baktoid Armor Workshop, the E-60R missile launcher was designed by the Techno Union as an inexpensive and lightweight missile launcher for use by B1-series battle droids. Further cost cutting meant that models were produced without a built-in sighting system. The resulting missile launcher is an accessible and cheap anti-armour weapon, though the price reduction can certainly be felt by untrained users.

> More information about the weapon available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/E-60R).

Rockets or missiles fired using the E-60R missile launcher cannot benefit from the [[Item Qualities#Guided (Active)|Guided]] quality, and combat checks at longer than Medium range gain a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice for each band. This can be remedied by acquiring a model already equipped with a surplus viewfinder scope for an additional 750 credits and using the only hard point of the weapon profile.[^0-2]

The E-60R can be used with other missiles. The profile assumes a [[High-Explosive Missile|high-explosive missile]] is loaded. Additional missiles to reload the launcher are an encumbrance of 1.[^0-2]
