Well known as the traditional weapon of the Wookiees of Kashyyyk, bowcasters, also called laser crossbows, magnetically accelerate metal quarrels encased in a plasma energy sheath.

> More information about the weapon available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bowcaster).

**Models Include:** Unique designed weapon.

The bowcaster presented here does not have an automatic re-cocking mechanism. It requires the user to spend a maneuver cocking the weapon before each shot.[^3]

### BOWCASTER QUARRELS

Source: [^4]

The bowcaster can benefit from an array of specialty quarrels that can replace the standard microexplosive quarrels. A case of six specialty quarrels costs 150 credits and has a rarity of 7.

As a maneuver, a character may load a specialized quarrel, which replaces the bowcaster's [[Item Qualities#Knockdown (Active)|Knockdown]] quality with the effect described below for its next attack.

- **Armor-Piercing Quarrel:** Grants the bowcaster the [[Item Qualities#Pierce (Passive)|Pierce]] 3 item quality instead of [[Item Qualities#Knockdown (Active)|Knockdown]].
- **High Explosive Quarrel:** Grants the bowcaster the [[Item Qualities#Blast (Active)|Blast]] 6 quality instead of [[Item Qualities#Knockdown (Active)|Knockdown]].
- **Sonic Detonator:** Grants the bowcaster the [[Item Qualities#Blast (Active)|Blast]] 4 and Disorient 2 weapon qualities instead of [[Item Qualities#Knockdown (Active)|Knockdown]].
- **Flash Quarrel:** Grants the bowcaster the [[Item Qualities#Concussive (Active)|Concussive]] 1 weapon quality instead of [[Item Qualities#Knockdown (Active)|Knockdown]].
- **Smoke Quarrel:** Removes the [[Item Qualities#Knockdown (Active)|Knockdown]] item quality. The next attack, if successful, deals no damage. Instead, all characters within short range are covered in smoke, adding a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die to incoming attacks, but they suffer two [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice on any ranged attacks and [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] and [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]] checks they make for their next two turns, or until they spend a maneuver to step away from the smoke.
