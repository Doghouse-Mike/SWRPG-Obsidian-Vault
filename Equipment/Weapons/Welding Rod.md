A welding rod adds an automatic [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] result to [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics]] checks to repair droids, vehicles, and starships.

The GM may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result on any check made with a welding rod to inflict a Critical Injury on the user.
