A simple but sturdy weapon most often found among the primitive or poor, a quarterstaff is nonetheless effective and draws little attention compared to more advanced weapons. They might be made of simple wood, salvaged metal, or something of significance to the owner, but should not be underestimated in the hands of a skilled wielder however humble the appearance.
