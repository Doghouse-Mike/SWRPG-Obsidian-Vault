A sleek and simple design, the BlasTech Industries L-X4 Mortar Launcher is compact enough to be transported and operated by a single soldier. The owner’s manual insist that only BlasTech brand baradium core thermal shells may be used in the launcher. However many foolhardy operators claim that Thermal Imploders and Jogan fruit seem to fly just as far.
