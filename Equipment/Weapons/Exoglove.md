Common around spaceports on backwater worlds, exogloves mechanically enhance strength, ostensibly for shifting cargo and performing other menial labor.

## EXOGLOVE

Source: [^1]

![Skill Brawl Range Engaged Encumbrance 2 Price 2,000 Rarity 4 Damage +2 Critical Rating 4 Hard Points 1 Special Knockdown|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/d/d0/Profile_Exoglove.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Skill Brawl Range Engaged Encumbrance 2 Price 2,000 Rarity 4 Damage +2 Critical Rating 4 Hard Points 1 Special Knockdown

Wearing one or more exogloves increases a character's [[Brawn]] by 1. As an action, a character may make an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Ranged - Light (Ag)|Ranged (Light) check]]** to secure the exoglove's grappling hook to an object within medium range. If successful, as a maneuver on their next turn, they may reel in the cord, pulling themselves to the object (or, if the object is unsecured and lighter than they are, pulling it to them).
