## ACTIVV1 RIOT SHIELD

Source: [^0-1][^2][^3]

Riot shields can only be used with one-handed weapons, but some incorporate a slot or mag lock to support a Ranged (Heavy) weapon with the shield itself (users fire the weapon one-handed while wielding the riot shield in the other hand). A riot shield with this feature costs an additional 100 credits and has no hard points. Firing a Ranged (Heavy) rifle locked to the shield this way adds a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die to combat checks with both weapons.[^0-1][^4][^5]
