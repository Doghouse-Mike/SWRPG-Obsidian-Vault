Scatterguns are powerful slugthrower weapons that fire a spray of solid pellets at a close range. The resulting injuries are often grievous enough to maim or incapacitate the target, though the weapon is not very effective at longer ranges.

Scatterguns are common on backwater worlds, though their lethal efficiency as also made them popular in the hands of smugglers, mercenaries and agents.
