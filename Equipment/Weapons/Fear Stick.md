The fear stick is only the size of a stylus or a hypodermic injector and injects a potent neurologic compound in a target's system.

## FEAR STICK

Add two [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice to [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] checks to find a fear stick on a wearer's body. On a successful hit with a fear stick, the attacker may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result to force a living target to make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Formidable difficulty]] fear check**. Fear sticks have no effect on droids or nonliving creatures.[^0-1][^3]
