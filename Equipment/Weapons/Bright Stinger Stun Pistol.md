Idran Arms’ Bright Stinger is one of the most popular stun blasters on the market today. The Bright Stinger is commonly seen in the hands of wary travellers and cautious tourists. The Bright Stinger is light, compact and easy to use. The pistol lacks the stopping power of more military-grade stun weapons, but its electronic sting is usually enough to ward off all but the most dedicated of enemies.
