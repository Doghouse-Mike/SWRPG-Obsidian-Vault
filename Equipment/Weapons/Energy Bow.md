The preferred weapon of the Nightsisters of Dathomir, the energy bow is a carbon-alloy bow with a plasma coupler string, a similar technology to the energy binders that link podracing engines.

> More information about the weapon available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Energy_bow).
