Flame projectors come in a variety of shapes and sizes, but generally include a rifle-like emitter connected to a number of canisters.

> More information about the weapon available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Flamethrower).

**Models Include:** [[BT X-42 Heavy Flame Projector|BlasTech X42 Heavy Flamethrower]], [[Czerka CZ-28 Flamestrike|CZ-28 Flamestrike Flame Cannon]], Merr-Sonn C-22 Flame Carbine, Merr-Sonn CR-24 Flame Rifle.

## FLAME PROJECTOR

Source: [^0-1][^2][^3]

![Skill Ranged (Heavy) Range Short Encumbrance 6 Price 1,000 Rarity 6 Damage 8 Critical Rating 2 Hard Points 2 Special Blast 8, Burn 3|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/4/41/Profile_Flame_Projector.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Skill Ranged (Heavy) Range Short Encumbrance 6 Price 1,000 Rarity 6 Damage 8 Critical Rating 2 Hard Points 2 Special Blast 8, Burn 3

Thanks to their broad reach, flame projectors can easily catch multiple targets in a single blast. When triggering a flame projector's [[Item Qualities#Burn (Active)|Burn]] quality, the user may choose to apply it to any single target hit by the attack; this quality may be triggered multiple times, affecting a different target each time. The downside to this weapon's volatility and indiscriminate area of effect is that allies can easily be caught in its flames. Game Masters may spend two [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result to apply a flame projector's [[Item Qualities#Burn (Active)|Burn]] quality to an ally hit by the original attack.[^0-1][^4][^5]
