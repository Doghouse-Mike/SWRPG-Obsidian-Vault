Initially designed by the VacuTech Combine—which ran out of capital and declared bankruptcy during the development of this weapon thanks to Imperial meddling—the Vac Attack is a small projectile launcher worn around the wrist like a chrono.

> More information about the weapon available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vac_Attack_Mk-127_disc_launcher).

## VAC ATTACK MK-127 CONCEALABLE DISK LAUNCHER

> [!quote] Image text

Changing the weapon's spread from single to arc requires a maneuver and grants the weapon the [[Item Qualities#Blast (Active)|Blast]] 6 item quality. If [[Item Qualities#Blast (Active)|Blast]] is activated, the weapon's supply of discs is exhausted in the shot. When worn on both wrists, Mk-127s can be fired as if they were a single weapon with a regular combat check, gaining the [[Item Qualities#Linked (Active)|Linked]] 2 quality. Noticing that an individual is wearing one or a pair of these weapons requires a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Perception (Cun)|Perception check]]**. At the Game Master's discretion the discs can be coated with various toxins. Reloads for the Mk-127 contain four discs and cost 100 credits.[^2]
