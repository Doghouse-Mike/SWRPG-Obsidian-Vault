## SEMBLAN OBSIDIAN DAGGER

Source: [^1]

![Skill Melee Range Engaged Encumbrance 1 Price 350 Rarity 4 Damage +0 Critical Rating 1 Hard Points 0 Special None|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/d/da/Profile_Semblan_Obsidian_Dagger.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Skill Melee Range Engaged Encumbrance 1 Price 350 Rarity 4 Damage +0 Critical Rating 1 Hard Points 0 Special None

When a Vurk makes a Discipline check at the end of an encounter to heal strain, they heal one additional strain as long as they are holding a Semblan dagger. At the GM's discretion, non-Vurk characters can also benefit from this if they have a thorough grounding in Vurk culture and the mentality to appreciate the meditative properties.
