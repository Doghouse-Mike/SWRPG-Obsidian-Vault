This weapon is a [[Sabercane Hilt|sabercane hilt]] equipped with an unmodded [[Ilum Crystal|Ilum crystal]]. The crystal occupies two of the weapon's hard points.

When installing a crystal into a lightsaber cane, reduce the damage of that crystal by one.
