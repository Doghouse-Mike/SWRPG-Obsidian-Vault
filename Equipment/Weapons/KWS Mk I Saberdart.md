Kaminoan Weaponsmiths, a subsidiary of Kaminoan Armorsmiths Lts., is a small weapons producer based on the water planet of Kamino.

Saberdarts may be loaded with 1 dose of [[Synthetic Neuroparalytic|synthetic neuroparalytic]], or any other poison the GM deems appropriate. Whenever the wielder successfully attacks a target with the weapon (even if \[they\] deal no damage), the target also suffers the effects of being exposed to the poison.[^2]
