## ION MICRO-ROCKET

Source: [^0-1][^2]

> Skill Ranged (Heavy) Range Short Encumbrance 1 Price 100 Rarity 6 Damage 10 Critical Rating 3 Hard Points 1 Special Ion, Limited Ammo 1, Sunder

An ion micro-rocket's [[Item Qualities#Sunder (Active)|Sunder]] quality can only be activated to damage powered equipment, such as sensors, blasters, and vibroblades. It cannot be used to damage items that operate without power, as they have no circuitry to disrupt.[^0-1][^3]
