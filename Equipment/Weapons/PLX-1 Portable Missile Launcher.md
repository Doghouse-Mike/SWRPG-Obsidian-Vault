The PLX-1 was the missile launcher of choice used by the Grand Army of the Republic during the clone wars. Manufactured by Merr-Sonn Munitions, as was the case with most clone ordnance, the PLX-1 was barely considered portable. While ultimately as heavy as the RPS-6 also used by clone troopers, its bulk was excessive for a weapon only capable of holding two missiles in the tube. Users of the weapon called it the “Plex”, and was standard issue to clone troopers with dedicated anti-armour roles. In this role, it excelled at disabling vehicles and starships alike.

The standard missiles used in the PLX-1 can be bought for 275 credits and are two encumbrance each. Increase the difficulty of the combat check using this weapon twice if the target is at ground level and of Silhouette 2 or smaller, as the missiles struggle to track and move slower closer to the ground. This also applies when activating the [[Item Qualities#Guided (Active)|Guided]] quality against these targets.[^0-2]

Whenever a vehicle is hit with a PLX-1 missile, any Critical Hit results in a “Engine Damaged” effect, unless the roll would result in a more damaging hit.[^0-2]
