Wrist blasters are custom-made self-defense weapons worn by diplomats, espionage agents, businesspeople, and other individuals who need an inconspicuous personal defense weapon.

> More information about the weapon available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wrist_blaster).

Noticing that an individual is wearing a wrist blaster requires a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Daunting difficulty]] [[Perception (Cun)|Perception check]]**. At the Game Master's discretion, three [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result can be spent on a user's [[Ranged - Light (Ag)|Ranged (Light)]] check to have the weapon burn the user and deal 3 wounds.[^2]
