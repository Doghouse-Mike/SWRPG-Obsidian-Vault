## PULSE CANNON

Source: [^0-1][^2]

![Skill Ranged (Heavy) Range Extreme Encumbrance 5 Price (R) 2,750 Rarity 6 Damage 9 Critical Rating 3 Hard Points 1 Special Cumbersome 3, Pierce 2, Slow-Firing 1|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/d/de/Profile_Pulse_Cannon.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Skill Ranged (Heavy) Range Extreme Encumbrance 5 Price (R) 2,750 Rarity 6 Damage 9 Critical Rating 3 Hard Points 1 Special Cumbersome 3, Pierce 2, Slow-Firing 1

As a maneuver, a character wielding a pulse cannon may prime the weapon to expend all of its ammunition in a single, devastating shot. The next time the weapon is fired that encounter, it counts as having the [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] 1 and [[Item Qualities#Vicious (Passive)|Vicious]] 3 qualities. After being discharged this way, the weapon runs out of ammunition.[^0-1][^3]
