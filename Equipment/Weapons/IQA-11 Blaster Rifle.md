Little more than a generic blaster rifle with an extended barrel, the IQA-11 is a no frills and simple rifle sold to working hunters. As a civilian model, it is inexpensive and easy to acquire. This has made it a favourite among bounty hunters and mercenaries interested in a reliable long range weapon. Its design is fairly bland, and save for the give-away long barrel, appears as an unintimidating weapon on a frontier world.
