The N-20 thermal detonator was likely the most mass-produced grenade in the galaxy in recent times. Found on the armour belt of every clone trooper, and later on every storm trooper. It was a much more regulated form of the infamous weapon, with a variable blast intensity and built-in security systems which prevented others from using them after being salvaged from the battle- field. Some enterprising technicians, particularly those who don’t value their life that much, swear they are able to slice through the extreme encryption of the explosives, but you’d be better off finding a black market salesman of regular thermal detonators instead.

> More information about the weapon available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/N-20_Baradium-core_thermal_detonator).

The blast range of the N-20 can be set by its user to vary between Engaged and Short range prior to being thrown. In addition, the time to detonation can be varied 6 to 18 seconds (up to 1 round in structured time). The N-20 is bought with a code key which is produced randomly at the point of sale. Using the N-20 without a demonstration of the key input requires a twice upgraded **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Daunting difficulty]] [[Computers (Int)|Computers check]]** to slice through the encryption, reducing the difficulty once if the key is known.[^0-2]

## *OR* N-20 BARADIUM-CORE THERMAL DETONATOR

Source: [^3]

![Skill Ranged [Light Range Short Encumbrance 1 Price 2000 Rarity 8 Damage 20 Critical Rating 2 Hard Points 0 Special Blast 15, Breach 1, Limited Ammo 1, Vicious 4|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/6/66/Profile_N-20_Baradium-core_Thermal_Detonator.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Skill Ranged [Light Range Short Encumbrance 1 Price 2000 Rarity 8 Damage 20 Critical Rating 2 Hard Points 0 Special Blast 15, Breach 1, Limited Ammo 1, Vicious 4

The N-20 has adjustable time settings, ranging from six to eighteen seconds and a small range of five meters within which all matter can be vaporised depending on the setting.[^1-4]

The N-20 can only be activated only with the right code tapped into it; to prevent captured detonators from being used against troopers. Also, the buttons are unlabelled, which ensured that even if someone discovered the code, they couldn't figure out which buttons to press in order to activate the detonator. Owing to this, stormtroopers need to employ pattern memorisation when using the N-20.[^1-4]
