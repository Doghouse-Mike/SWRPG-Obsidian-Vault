Ionization blasters are short-ranged weapons used to disable droids and other electronics.

> More information about the weapon available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ion_blaster).

**Models Include:** Merr-Sonn DEMP 2 Ion Carbine, scavenged Jawa scrap ion blaster.

Ionization blasters work only on droids and cyborgs as per their Stun Quality. They have no effect on organic beings beyond a brief and painful sting.[^5]
