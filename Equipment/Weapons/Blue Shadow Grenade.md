## BLUE SHADOW GRENADE

Source: [^0-1][^2]

> [!quote] Image text
> Skill Ranged (Light) Range Short Encumbrance 1 Price (R) 2,000 Rarity 9 Damage 3 Critical Rating 3 Hard Points 0 Special Blast 3, Limited Ammo 1

When a Blue Shadow grenade detonates, all targets who suffer at least 1 wound from the grenade—or are within medium range of the detonation while not wearing a rebreather, sealed armor, or other form of environmental protection—must make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Formidable difficulty]] [[Resilience (Br)|Resilience check]]**. Targets suffer 2 wounds and 2 strain per [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] result, and a Critical Injury with a +50 modified if they fail the check. A [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result means they must make the check again at the start of their next turn as the virus runs rampant through their system.[^0-1][^3]
