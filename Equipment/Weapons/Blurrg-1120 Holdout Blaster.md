Eiriss Ryloth Defense Tech manufactured many of the weapons utilised by the freedom fighters of Ryloth during the Clone Wars. Proudly carrying this maverick legacy forward, their guns are often seen as a symbol of resistance unto themselves.

The Blurrg-1120 is named after a large Ryloth reptile that while mostly docile, could chew through almost anything when angered.

Source: [^1]

> Skill Ranged (Light) Range Short Encumbrance 1 Price 750 Rarity 6 Damage 5 Critical Rating 4 Hard Points 1 Special (DUal-fire mode: Linked 1, Inaccurate 1), Stun Setting.

Being a holdout blaster it adds a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die to a character's [[Perception (Cun)|perception]] check to find the weapon on a person's body.[^0-2]

As a maneuver the user may switch to dual-fire mode, adding [[Item Qualities#Linked (Active)|Linked]] 1 and [[Item Qualities#Inaccurate (Passive)|Inaccurate]] 1. While in dual-fire mode the GM may spend three [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result to make the weapon run out of ammo.[^0-2]
