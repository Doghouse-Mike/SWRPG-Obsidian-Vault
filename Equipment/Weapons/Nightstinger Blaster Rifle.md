Officially classified as a sporting blaster, Xerrol Corporation's Nightstinger was marketed as a powerful, well-balanced rifle for big game hunting, which, while true, omits its ability to fire completely invisible blaster bolts with the flick of a switch.

> More information about the weapon available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nightstinger_blaster_rifle).

## NIGHTSTINGER BLASTER RIFLE

Switching the Nightstinger to stealth mode requires a maneuver. Stealth mode makes locating the shooter by tracing the blaster bolt or spotting a muzzle blast effectively impossible. It also grants the weapon the [[Item Qualities#Slow-Firing (Passive)|Slow-Firing]] 2 item quality for the remainder for the encounter.[^0-1][^3]
