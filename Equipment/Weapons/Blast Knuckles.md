## BLAST KNUCKLES

Source: [^1][^2]

![Skill Brawl Range Engaged Encumbrance 2 Price (R) 500 Rarity 6 Damage +4 Critical Rating 4 Hard Points 0 Special Inaccurate 1|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/5/5c/Profile_Blast_Knuckles.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Skill Brawl Range Engaged Encumbrance 2 Price (R) 500 Rarity 6 Damage +4 Critical Rating 4 Hard Points 0 Special Inaccurate 1

When a character uses blast knuckles, every successful hit inflicts 1 strain on the wielder (in addition to any strain suffered due to check results). Blast knuckles cannot deal damage to a target's strain threshold.
