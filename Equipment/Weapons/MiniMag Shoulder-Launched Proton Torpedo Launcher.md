The MiniMag PTL is named due to its specific purpose as a launcher for proton torpedoes. This makes it a devastating weapon, and extremely dangerous to use at short range. The warheads used by the MiniMag are half-yield versions of those found on starships, and lack the already-limited homing systems. It was developed by Krupx Munitions—a starship weapons manufacturer—after being commissioned by the late Republic who sought an anti-armour weapon to deal with the increasing presence of heavily armoured droid craft that filled the late Clone Wars. Despite being built for proton torpedoes, the MiniMag is still capable of firing other ordnance.

A full refill of seven proton torpedoes for the MiniMag can be bought for 2,500 credits, stored in a 5 encumbrance crate. The Blast quality reaches out to Short range, and the GM may use three [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result to have an attack with a target at Short range trigger the quality and hit the user’s allies in range. In addition, any attack made with the MiniMag at Short range is upgraded once and adds two [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice to the difficulty..[^0-2]
