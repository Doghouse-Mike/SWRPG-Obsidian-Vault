Through specialized focusing lenses and emitters, this lightsaber hilt produces a blade that is broader and flatter than a standard lightsaber's cylindrical blade, lending it a profile closer to that of a traditional metal sword.

This weapon's profile represents a [[Broadsaber Hilt|broadsaber hilt]] containing an unmodded [[Ilum Crystal|Ilum crystal]]. This crystal occupies two of the weapon's hard points.[^0-2]

When installing a lightsaber crystal into a broadsaber, increase the damage of that lightsaber crystal by 1.[^0-2]
