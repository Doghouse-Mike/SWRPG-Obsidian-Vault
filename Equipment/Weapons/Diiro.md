A diiro can be loaded with up to three doses of any poison (most locals use [[Bearsloth Venom|bearsloth venom]]). Whenever the wielder successfully attacks a target with a diiro (whether or not damage is dealt), the target also suffers the effects of being exposed to the poison it contains.

Due to its lightweight construction, when a diiro suffers one or more levels of damage due to a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result on a combat check or the effects of the [[Item Qualities#Sunder (Active)|Sunder]] quality, it receives one additional level of damage.
