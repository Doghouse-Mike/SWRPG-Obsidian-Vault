Kiavene’s are pike weapons favoured on the remote world of Shili, especially by members of the Togruta Royal Guard. Tall and lightweight, kiavene are expertly balanced and dangerously effective in trained hands. One end of the weapon is bladed, like a spear and the other end holds a tiny blaster unit, allowing the kiavene to be pointed horizontally and used against longer range targets. Due to the thin shaft of the kiavene the ammo capacity for this blaster feature is small and is often used as a last-ditch attempt in an a emergency,
