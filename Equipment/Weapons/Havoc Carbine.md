Cheaper, lighter and with a faster rate of fire than a standard blaster rifle, the Havoc is a carbine rifle commonly used by local security forces across the galaxy. Its low production cost combined with its usefulness in tight environments makes this the favoured weapon of many urban security officers. However, as a trade off, the Havoc has a shorter range, is less accurate and has far less stopping power than a standard blaster rifle.

The Czerka Arms produced blaster features a heavily shrouded long barrel and a compact two-handed grip, and features attachment points for a glowlight and a simple scope. Painted in a matte black, the weapon has an annoying tendency to easily pick up scratches and marks, leaving many older models more chromium than black.

Security forces such as Pre-Mor Enforcement use the Havoc Carbine alongside the Arkus pistol, also produced by Czerka Arms.
