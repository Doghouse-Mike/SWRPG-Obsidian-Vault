## LIGHTFOIL

Source: [^0-1][^2]

![Skill Lightsaber Range Engaged Encumbrance 1 Price (R) 9,850 Rarity 10 Damage 5 Critical Rating 2 Hard Points 1 Special Breach 1, Defensive 2, Sunder|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/6/64/Profile_Lightfoil.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Skill Lightsaber Range Engaged Encumbrance 1 Price (R) 9,850 Rarity 10 Damage 5 Critical Rating 2 Hard Points 1 Special Breach 1, Defensive 2, Sunder

This lightsaber uses a [[Lightfoil Hilt|basic lightfoil hilt]] containing an unmodded [[Ilum Crystal|Ilum lightsaber crystal]]. This crystal occupies two of the weapon's hard points.[^0-1][^1-3]

When installing a lightsaber crystal into a lightfoil, reduce the damage of that lightsaber crystal by one.[^0-1][^1-3]
