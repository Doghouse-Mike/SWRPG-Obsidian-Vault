A unique, locally produced holdout blaster manufactured on Ord Gimmel, the variable holdout blaster can switch between seven different power levels, potentially putting it on par with military holdout blasters.

> More information about the weapon available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Variable_holdout_blaster).

## VARIABLE HOLDOUT BLASTER

Source: [^0-1][^2]

![Skill Ranged (Light) Range Short Encumbrance 1 Price 400 Rarity 7 Damage 1-7 Critical Rating 4 Hard Points 0 Special Limited Ammo 12|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/f/f0/Profile_Variable_Holdout_Blaster.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Skill Ranged (Light) Range Short Encumbrance 1 Price 400 Rarity 7 Damage 1-7 Critical Rating 4 Hard Points 0 Special Limited Ammo 12

When firing the weapon, the pistol's wielder must choose a power setting between 1 and 7. This is the amount of damage a successful shot inflicts on a target, but it's also the amount of ammo that is consumed for the shot. If set to a power setting higher than the available ammo left, the weapon deals damage equal to the amount of ammo remaining.[^0-1][^1-3]

Add one [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback dice]] to a character's [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] checks when attempting to find a variable holdout blaster on a person's body.[^0-1][^1-3]
