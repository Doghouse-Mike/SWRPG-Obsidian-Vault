## VOSS WARSPEAR

Source: [^1]

> [!quote] Image text
> Skill Melee Range Engaged Encumbrance 4 Price 215 Rarity 8 Damage +2 Critical Rating 4 Hard Points 2 Special Defensive 2

The Voss warspear is a one-handed weapon, however it cannot be wielded with other weapons. It may, at the GM's discretion, be wielded while using a shield.

A spear may be thrown as a ranged weapon. If used in this manner, change the skill to [[Ranged - Light (Ag)|Ranged (Light)]] and the range of the weapon to short. The weapon also gains the [[Item Qualities#Limited Ammo (Passive)|Limited Ammo]] 1 quality (but if retrieved, it can be used again).
