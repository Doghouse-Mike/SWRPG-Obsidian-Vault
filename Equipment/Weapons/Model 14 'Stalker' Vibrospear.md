## MODEL 14 "STALKER" VIBROSPEAR

Source: [^1][^2]

![Skill Melee Range Engaged Encumbrance 4 Price 490 Rarity 6 Damage +2 Critical Rating 2 Hard Points 2 Special Defensive 1, Pierce 2, Vicious 2|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/a/a7/Profile_Model_14_Stalker_Vibrospear.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Skill Melee Range Engaged Encumbrance 4 Price 490 Rarity 6 Damage +2 Critical Rating 2 Hard Points 2 Special Defensive 1, Pierce 2, Vicious 2

The GM may spend two [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results from any combat check with this weapon to have it produce a loud noise, spooking nearby animals, alerting foes, and adding two [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice to the user's next [[Survival (Cun)|Survival]] check in that area to hunt or track prey.

The Stalker vibrospear requires two hands to wield.
