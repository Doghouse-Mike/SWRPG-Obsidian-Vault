Similar in construction to a force pike or electrostaff, a lightsaber pike consists of a long, sturdy haft typically made of composites or strong alloys.

> More information about the weapon available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_pike).

**Models Include:** Non; each of these weapons is individually constructed.

This lightsaber is a [[Lightsaber Pike Hilt|basic lightsaber pike hilt]] containing an unmodded [[Ilum Crystal|Ilum lightsaber crystal]]. This crystal occupies two of the weapon's hard points.[^3]

Lightsabers cannot be Sundered.[^4]
