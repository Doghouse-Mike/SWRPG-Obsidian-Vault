The Zygerrian Empire is well known for their slaving operations and have developed an array of non-lethal weapons that they may use to take down escaping slaves or capturing new products. The Zygerrian energy bow is one of a handful of these exotic weapons. Pulling back on the energy link ring focuses a contained bolt for release. Unique among these weapons, the Zygerrian version uses an energy frequency tuned in a way that can disrupt biological processes instead of dealing damage. This can be swapped on the fly so that it deals more physical damage instead.
