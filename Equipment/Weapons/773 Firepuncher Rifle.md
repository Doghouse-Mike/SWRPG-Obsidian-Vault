Manufactured by Merr-Sonn Munitions Inc., the “Firepuncher” was a favourite among the snipers of the Grand Army of the Republic. While not the standard issue rifle, that being a modified DC-15, it was available to those who demonstrated an elite capacity in long range combat. Largely standard for a sniper rifle, it was well known for its special shot: the wide-beam burning mode.
