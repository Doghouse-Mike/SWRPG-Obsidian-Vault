## CROSSGUARD LIGHTSABER

Source: [^0-1][^2]

![Skill Lightsaber Range Engaged Encumbrance 1 Price (R) 9,700 Rarity 10 Damage 6 Critical Rating 2 Hard Points 4 Special Breach 1, Defensive 1, Sunder|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/e/ec/Profile_Crossguard_Lightsaber.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Skill Lightsaber Range Engaged Encumbrance 1 Price (R) 9,700 Rarity 10 Damage 6 Critical Rating 2 Hard Points 4 Special Breach 1, Defensive 1, Sunder

Whenever an enemy engaged with a character armed with a crossguard lightsaber makes a [[Melee (Br)|Melee]] or [[Lightsaber (Br)|Lightsaber]] check, after the attack is resolved, the character may spend three [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result to hook their opponent's blade and disarm them. Aside from losing the weapon, any other effects of being disarmed are left to the GM's discretion.

This lightsaber is a [[Crossguard Lightsaber Hilt|crossguard lightsaber hilt]] containing an unmodded [[Ilum Crystal|Ilum lightsaber crystal]]. This crystal occupies two of the weapon's hard points.[^0-1]
