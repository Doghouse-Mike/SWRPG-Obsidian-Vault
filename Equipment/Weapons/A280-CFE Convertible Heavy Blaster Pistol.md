## A280-CFE CONVERTIBLE HEAVY BLASTER PISTOL

Source: [^1][^2]

![Skill Ranged (Light) Range Medium Encumbrance 3 Price (R) 1,700 Rarity 7 Damage 8 Critical Rating 3 Hard Points 1 Special Stun setting|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/c/c2/Profile_A280-CFE_Convertible_Heavy_Blaster_Pistol.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Skill Ranged (Light) Range Medium Encumbrance 3 Price (R) 1,700 Rarity 7 Damage 8 Critical Rating 3 Hard Points 1 Special Stun setting

A character can spend a maneuver to convert the CFE from pistol to rifle mode, or vice versa. With the rifle accessories attached, the weapon's range becomes long, and it gains the [[Item Qualities#Accurate (Passive)|Accurate]] 1 and [[Item Qualities#Pierce (Passive)|Pierce]] 1 item qualities. Characters use the [[Ranged - Heavy (Ag)|Ranged (Heavy)]] skill when firing the weapon in rifle mode.
