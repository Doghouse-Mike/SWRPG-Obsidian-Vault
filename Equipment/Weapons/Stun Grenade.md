Stun grenades are effectively non-lethal defensive grenades used to disable, rather than kill, an opponent.

> More information about the weapon available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Stun_grenade).

**Models Include:** Merr-Sonn C-10 Stun Grenade, Imperial Arms "Starfire" Flash Bang Grenade.
