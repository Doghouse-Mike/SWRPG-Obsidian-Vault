The weapon used by virtually the whole Geonosian warrior caste, this sonic blaster was designed by Gordarl Weaponsmiths, a subsidiary of Geonosian Industries. A particularly complex weapon which used experimental sonic wave theory based technology, it fired a contained sphere of condensed sonic energy. Upon coming into contact with a physical entity, or energy shield, the sphere would rupture and release the sonic blast on the spot. This could easily cause an instantaneous death by rupturing a target’s internal organs.
