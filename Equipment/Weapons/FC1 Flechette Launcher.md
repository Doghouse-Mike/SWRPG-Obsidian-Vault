Rebel partisans have used the now-banned Golan Arms FC1 flechette launcher in a number of attacks, most notoriously at Inusagi's Sakoola Blossom Festival, killing hundreds of guests and just-announced Imperial Governor Tophervin.

When using anti-vehicle canisters against targets of silhouette 1 or smaller, the weapons gains the [[Item Qualities#Inaccurate (Passive)|Inaccurate]] 3 quality.[^0-1][^2-3]

Some mercenaries tend to load the FC1 with a mix of both, so that they can respond to any situation. In this case, the character should not what \[their\] weapon is loaded with, and use the appropriate profile with each shot. \[They\] still have to track total shots as per the Limited Ammo quality.[^2-3]
