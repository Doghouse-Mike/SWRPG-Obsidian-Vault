A Huttese weapon of sinister design, the Boonta Blaster is named for the infamous, deified Hutt who allegedly commissioned the first one: Boonta Hestilic Shad'ruu.

> More information about the weapon available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Boonta_Blaster).
