## GREAT LIGHTSABER

Source: [^0-1][^2]

![Skill Lightsaber Range Engaged Encumbrance 2 Price (R) 12,000 Rarity 10 Damage 7 Critical Rating 2 Hard Points 3 Special Breach 1, Cumbersome 3, Sunder|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/3/39/Profile_Great_Lightsaber.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Skill Lightsaber Range Engaged Encumbrance 2 Price (R) 12,000 Rarity 10 Damage 7 Critical Rating 2 Hard Points 3 Special Breach 1, Cumbersome 3, Sunder

This lightsaber is a [[Great Lightsaber Hilt|great lightsaber hilt]] containing an unmodded [[Ilum Crystal|Ilum lightsaber crystal]]. This crystal occupies two of the weapon's hard points.[^0-1][^1-3]

When installing a lightsaber crystal into a great lightsaber increase the damage of that lightsaber crystal by one.[^0-1][^1-3]
