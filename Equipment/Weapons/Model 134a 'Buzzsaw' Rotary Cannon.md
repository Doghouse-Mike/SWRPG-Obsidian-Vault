## MODEL 134a "BUZZSAW" ROTARY CANNON

Source: [^0-1][^2]

![Skill Gunnery Range Long Encumbrance 8 Price (R) 4,500 Rarity 7 Damage 12 Critical Rating 3 Hard Points 4 Special Auto-fire, Cumbersome 5, Pierce 3|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/9/99/Profile_Model_134a_Buzzsaw_Rotary_Cannon.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Skill Gunnery Range Long Encumbrance 8 Price (R) 4,500 Rarity 7 Damage 12 Critical Rating 3 Hard Points 4 Special Auto-fire, Cumbersome 5, Pierce 3

The Model 134a can only be fired using the Auto-fire quality. Game Masters may spend three [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat results]] or a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair result]] to have the weapon overheat and jam. When a Buzzsaw jams, the user must perform a maneuver to clear the jam before it can be fired again.[^0-1][^3]
