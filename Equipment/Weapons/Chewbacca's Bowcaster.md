**Quarrel Bandolier:** Once per encounter, the wielder may spend a maneuver to load a specialized quarrel and either add the [[Item Qualities#Blast (Active)|Blast]] 6, [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] 1, [[Item Qualities#Concussive (Active)|Concussive]] 1, [[Item Qualities#Disorient (Active)|Disorient]] 2, [[Item Qualities#Ion (Passive)|Ion]], or [[Item Qualities#Vicious(Passive)|Vicious]] 4 quality to the next combat check made with Chewbacca's Bowcaster.

### PROTECTIVE FIRE

As an action, Chewbacca may make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Coercion (Will)|Coercion]]** or **[[Ranged - Heavy (Ag)|Ranged (Heavy) check]]**. If he succeeds, one rival or minion group within medium range is staggered until the end of their next turn. Chewie may spend two [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results to affect an additional rival or minion group within range and may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result to force affected targets to spend their next maneuver to move away from Chewbacca.
