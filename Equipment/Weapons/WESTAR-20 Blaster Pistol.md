The precursor to the WESTAR-34, which itself was a precursor to the modern WESTAR-35 used universally by the Mandalorians during the Clone Wars, this short barreled pistol was able to be heavily customised to the owner’s specifications. As one of Concordian Crescent Technologies earlier entries, it lacked the stun option that the pacifist Mandalorian government would eventually mandate.
