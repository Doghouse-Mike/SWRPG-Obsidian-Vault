### CZERKA DETONATOR ROUNDS

Source: [^0-1]

Czerka produces a number of specialty ammunitions for use with its precision rifle lines, one of the most popular of which are the Detonator rounds. \[...\] When loaded in its Model 57 "Homesteader" rifle, these rounds have the following effect on the weapon's profile: Add the [[Item Qualities#Blast (Active)|Blast]] (4) and [[Item Qualities#Knockdown (Active)|Knockdown]] item qualities. Detonator rounds cost 450 credits per box and have a rarity of 7. If the weapon ever runs out of ammo, a new box of rounds must be purchased to load it.
