The typical Jedi training suit features bulky, heavily padded chest, leg, and arm pieces designed to absorb the stunning blows of training lightsabers.

## TRAINING SUIT

Source: [^0-1][^2]

![Defense 0 Soak 2 Hard Points 0 Encumbrance 6 Price (R) 400 Rarity 9|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/c/c4/Profile_Training_Suit.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Defense 0 Soak 2 Hard Points 0 Encumbrance 6 Price (R) 400 Rarity 9

Unless the integrated weights are removed, a Jedi training suit has an encumbrance of 6. Removal of these weights, which requires an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Easy difficulty]] [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics check]]**, reduces the suit's encumbrance to 4. At the GM's discretion, training while wearing the weights might help a character develop physical fitness or adapt to high-gravity environments.[^0-1][^3]
