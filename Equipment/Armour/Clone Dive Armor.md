## CLONE DIVE ARMOR

Source: [^1]

![Defense 0 Soak 2 Hard Points 2 Encumbrance 3 Price (R) 4,500 Rarity 7|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/9/9d/Profile_Clone_Dive_Armor.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Defense 0 Soak 2 Hard Points 2 Encumbrance 3 Price (R) 4,500 Rarity 7

Clone dive armor provides twelve hours of oxygen. During this time, the wearer also has fresh water, nutrition supplements, and other biological necessities. While wearing clone dive armor, a character does not suffer movement penalties for travelling through water and removes up to one [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die from [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] checks due to underwater conditions.[^2]
