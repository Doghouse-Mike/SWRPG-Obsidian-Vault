## WING COMMANDER ARMORED FLIGHT SUIT

Source: [^0-1][^2]

Thanks to its rugged construction and integrated armor, the Wing Commander suit reduces any strain received from Critical Hits dealt to the wearer's vehicle by one (to a minimum of 1) and the flame-resistant coating reduces damage dealt to the wearer by fires and weapons with the [[Item Qualities#Burn (Active)|Burn]] quality by 1.[^0-1][^3]
