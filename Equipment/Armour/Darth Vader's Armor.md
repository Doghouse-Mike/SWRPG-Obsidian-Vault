## DARTH VADER'S ARMOR

Source: [^1]

![Defense 1 Soak 2 Hard Points - Encumbrance 5 Price Not for Sale Rarity 10|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/c/ca/Profile_Darth_Vader%27s_Armor.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Defense 1 Soak 2 Hard Points - Encumbrance 5 Price Not for Sale Rarity 10

Darth Vader's customized armor comes with an enhanced optics suite (remove two [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice from [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]], [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]], and combat checks due to environmental effects that obscure vision), an [[SmartMed 2000 Integrated Med-systems|integrated medical system]] (add a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to all Medicine checks to heal Vader; once per session, may activate this attachment as an incidental; this counts as using a stimpack), and are [[Vacuum Sealed|vacuum-sealed]] (can ignore the effects of vacuum for up to an hour).

**Peerless Armor:** Once per encounter as an out-of-turn incidental, the wearer of Darth Vader's armor may spend a Destiny Point to increase the soak provided by this armor to +6 until the end of the wearer's next turn.

### VISAGE OF DEATH

Once per encounter after Darth Vader kills an Imperial character, he may spend a Destiny Point to add a [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] and [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result to checks made by other Imperial characters in the current encounter for the remainder of the encounter.
