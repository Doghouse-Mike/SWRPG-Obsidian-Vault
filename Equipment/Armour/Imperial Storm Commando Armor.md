## IMPERIAL STORM COMMANDO ARMOR

Source: [^1][^2]

![Defense 0 Soak 2 Hard Points 4 Encumbrance 4 Price (R) 5,000 Rarity 9|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/b/b2/Profile_Imperial_Storm_Commando_Armor.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Defense 0 Soak 2 Hard Points 4 Encumbrance 4 Price (R) 5,000 Rarity 9

The armor's helmet is equipped with [[Macrobinoculars|macrobinoculars]] and [[Scanner Goggles|scanner goggles]], as well as a [[Comlink|comlink]].

While wearing storm commando armor, a character removes a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die due to darkness, smoke, or similar environmental factors that obscure vision from [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]], [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]], and combat checks. Additionally, this armor adds a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die to other characters' [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] checks to detect the wearer.
