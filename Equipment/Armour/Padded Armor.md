Made of thick, reinforced, wear-resistant textiles woven with energy dispersion mesh, this light armor is one of the most common forms of personal armor protection in the galaxy.

> More information about the armor available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Padded_armor).

**Models Include:** Creshaldyne Industries Light Combat Armor, TaggeCo. "Protector X" Law Enforcement Armor.
