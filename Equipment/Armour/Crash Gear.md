Most sets of crash gear consist of an assortment of protective wear designed to allow a user to better survive the inevitable collisions and buffeting that are part of every race or mission.

## CRASH GEAR

Source: [^1]

![Defense 0 Soak 1 Hard Points 1 Encumbrance 1 Price 550 Rarity 3|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/1/13/Profile_Crash_Gear.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Defense 0 Soak 1 Hard Points 1 Encumbrance 1 Price 550 Rarity 3

A character wearing crash gear ignores the effects of ongoing Critical Injuries on any Intellect- or Cunning-related checks, but still suffers from the injuries themselves. \[They\] also cannot be disoriented.[^2]
