Popular and lightweight, capacitive armor offers moderate protection against most forms of attack whenever worn.

**Models include:** A/KT Tuff-Powered Armor

## POWERED CAPACITIVE ARMOR

Source: [^0-1][^2]

![Defense 1 Soak 1 Hard Points 2 Encumbrance 4 Price (R) 3,000 Rarity 8|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/7/79/Profile_Powered_Capacitive_Armor.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Defense 1 Soak 1 Hard Points 2 Encumbrance 4 Price (R) 3,000 Rarity 8

As a maneuver, the wearer may power up or power down the armor. While the armor is powered and the plates are locked in place, the wearer gains +1 soak and +1 defense but loses their free maneuver during each of their turns.[^0-1][^3]
