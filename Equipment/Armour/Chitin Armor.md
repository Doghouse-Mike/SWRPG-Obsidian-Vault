Chitin armor makes a strong statement about the wearer and these suits tend to have a startling effect on people in civilized society. A character wearing a suit of chitin armor adds an automatic [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result to \[their\] [[Coercion (Will)|Coercion]] checks and an automatic [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] result to \[their\] [[Charm (Pr)|Charm]] checks. The Game Master may determine that the armor provides further benefits or drawbacks, depending the situation.
