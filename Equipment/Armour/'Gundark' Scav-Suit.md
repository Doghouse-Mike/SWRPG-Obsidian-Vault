## "GUNDARK" SCAV-SUIT

Source: [^1]

![Defense 1 Soak 1 Hard Points 2 Encumbrance 4 Price 3,000 Rarity 6|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/9/93/Profile_Gundark_Scav-Suit.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Defense 1 Soak 1 Hard Points 2 Encumbrance 4 Price 3,000 Rarity 6

The inbuilt sensor array on a "Gundark" scav-suit adds a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to the wearer's [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] checks, and an additional [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to all checks to detect or analyze technological parts and items. However, if the suit's systems become damaged due to a Critical Injury, aimed shot, or other circumstance, the wearer instead suffers a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die due to sensor feedback on all [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] checks instead until the suit is repaired..[^2]
