Kamperdine armored jackets are each tailored to a specific wearer. When worn by that wearer, they add an [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result to any successful [[Charm (Pr)|Charm]], [[Deception (Cun)|Deception]], or [[Negotiation (Pr)|Negotiation]] checks the character makes (the GM may decide that the bonus does not apply in certain situations, such as dealings over a comlink).
