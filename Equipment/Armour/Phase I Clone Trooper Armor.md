## PHASE I CLONE TROOPER ARMOR

Source: [^1]

![Defense 0 Soak 2 Hard Points 3 Encumbrance 4 Price (R) 2,000 Rarity 6|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/b/be/Profile_Phase_I_Clone_Trooper_Armor.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Defense 0 Soak 2 Hard Points 3 Encumbrance 4 Price (R) 2,000 Rarity 6

It is pressurized against vacuum—not enough to allow extended operations, but sufficient for a few minutes of life support in an emergency.[^0-2]

Phase I clone trooper armor includes a [[Comlink|comlink]]. At the GM's discretion, characters unused to moving in this armor may suffer a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die to [[Coordination (Ag)|Coordination]] checks until they acclimatize to the distribution of its weight.[^0-2]
