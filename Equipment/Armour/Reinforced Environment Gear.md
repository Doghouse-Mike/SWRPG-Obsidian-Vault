Produced by a number of companies, such a Pretormin Environmental and Gandorthral Atmospherics, reinforced environment gear is essentially improved [[Adverse Environment Gear|adverse environment gear]].

## REINFORCED ENVIRONMENT GEAR

Source: [^1][^2]

![Defense 0 Soak 1 Hard Points 2 Encumbrance 2 Price 850 Rarity 4|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/6/67/Profile_Reinforced_Environment_Gear.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Defense 0 Soak 1 Hard Points 2 Encumbrance 2 Price 850 Rarity 4

Reinforced environment gear allows a wearer to ignore a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die imposed by environmental conditions and reduces Critical Injuries suffered from falling by 20. The suit also includes a filter, granting a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to [[Resilience (Br)|Resilience]] checks to resist toxic atmospheres or airborne toxins.
