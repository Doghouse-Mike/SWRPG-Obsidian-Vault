Imperial hazardous environment protective armor, or "hazard trooper armor," is one of the most rugged armor suits in the Stormtrooper Corps.

> More information about the armor available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_hazardous_environment_protective_armor).

## IMPERIAL HAZARD TROOPER ARMOR

Source: [^0-1][^2]

![Defense 0 Soak 3 Hard Points 3 Encumbrance 4 Price (R) 18,000 Rarity 8|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/0/0a/Profile_Imperial_Hazard_Trooper_Armor.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Defense 0 Soak 3 Hard Points 3 Encumbrance 4 Price (R) 18,000 Rarity 8

Once active, the armor's internal life support and power systems allow the wearer to survive in radiation zones, acid[^0-1] and toxic environments, extreme pressures[^0-1], and total vacuum for up to twenty-four hours. The armor also incorporates a high-powered comlink able to communicate from the ground to low orbit. The helmet is equipped with a multifrequency targeting and acquisition system that removes two [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die due to darkness, smoke, or similar environmental factors that obscure vision from the wearer's [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]], [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]], and combat checks.
