The HT-77 pattern of clone armor is a specialized suit designed for operation in cold environments, such as polar regions or planets far from the light of their local sun.

> More information about the armor available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/HT-77_Cold_Assault_Armor).

## HT-77 COLD ASSAULT ARMOR

Source: [^1]

![Defense 0 Soak 2 Hard Points 2 Encumbrance 3 Price (R) 4,000 Rarity 7|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/b/b8/Profile_HT-77_Cold_Assault_Armor.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Defense 0 Soak 2 Hard Points 2 Encumbrance 3 Price (R) 4,000 Rarity 7

HT-77 cold assault armor comes with a built-in [[Comlink|comlink]], and it adds two [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] dice to the wearer's checks to avoid adverse effects from exposure to cold temperatures and weather. Additionally, the wearer removes a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die imposed by cold-weather conditions on their [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] checks.
