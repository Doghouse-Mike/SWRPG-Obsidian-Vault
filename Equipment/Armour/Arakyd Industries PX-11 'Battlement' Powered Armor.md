## ARAKYD INDUSTRIES PX-11 "BATTLEMENT" POWERED ARMOR

Source: [^1]

![Defense 1 Soak 3 Hard Points 3 Encumbrance 3 Price (R) 9,000 Rarity 9|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/b/b2/Profile_Arakyd_Industries_PX-11_Battlement_Powered_Armor.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Defense 1 Soak 3 Hard Points 3 Encumbrance 3 Price (R) 9,000 Rarity 9

The suit forms a vacuum-sealed life support system, allowing the wearer to survive in a vacuum or toxic environment for up to one hour. The suit's helmet incorporates a [[Comlink|comlink]] as well as sophisticated optical and auditory sensors, allowing the wearer to deduct two [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice from all [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]], Surveillance, [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]] and combat skill checks due to darkness, smoke, or similar environmental factors that obscure vision. PX-11 armor suit charge packs supply enough power for 12 hours of use.[^0-2]

In addition to its protective value, the armor increases the wearer's [[Brawn]] by 1 while wearing the armor. This does not increase \[their\] soak or wound threshold. The wearer also gains +1 rank of the [[Athletics (Br)|Athletics]] skill as long as \[they\] are wearing the armor. The listed encumbrance value assumes the armor is powered. If a Battlement suit loses power or ceases to function for any reason, its encumbrance becomes 12, its defense becomes 0, and the wearer loses the Brawn increase. Equipping the armor without assistance takes five minutes. Assistance cuts this time in half.[^0-2]
