A reflect body glove is a skintight suit that can be worn underneath traditional garments.

## REFLECT BODY GLOVE

Source: [^0-1][^2]

![Defense 0 Soak 3 Hard Points 0 Encumbrance 2 Price 2,500 Rarity 5|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/d/d0/Profile_Reflect_Body_Glove.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Defense 0 Soak 3 Hard Points 0 Encumbrance 2 Price 2,500 Rarity 5

Add two [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice to a character's [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]] and [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] checks to notice the user is wearing armor. After a successful combat check has been resolved against the wearer, the reflect body glove's soak is reduced by one, to a minimum of zero. The suit's soak may be restored to its original value by making an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics check]]**.[^0-1][^3]
