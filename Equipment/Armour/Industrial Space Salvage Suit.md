Though many salvagers make do with standard space suits, there are some who look for just a bit more edge in making targeted collection runs. A salvage suit adds a bit more protection as well as a bit more comfort with onboard heating and a rebreather atmosphere scrubber. Further industrial tools are included in these suits to help zero gravity workers get their jobs done.
