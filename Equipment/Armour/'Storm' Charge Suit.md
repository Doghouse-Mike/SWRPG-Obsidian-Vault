If the wearer of the charge suit is hit by a melee attack and the attack generates a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result, the attacker is automatically hit by an attack dealing 8 damage. This damage is applied to the attacker's strain threshold. If the attack generates two threat results, the attacker is disoriented for one round. Wearing a charge suit counts as being equipped with [[Shock Gloves|shock gloves]] ([[Brawl (Br)|Brawl]]; Damage +0; Critical 5; Range \[Engaged\]; [[Item Qualities#Stun (Active)|Stun]] 3).[^0-1][^3]
