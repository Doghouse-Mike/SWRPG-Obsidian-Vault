Developed by various interests and groups in secret on Nubia following the Empire's rise to prominence, Kav-Dann series power armor is named after a reptilian predator native to the world.

> More information about the armor available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kav-Dann_power_armor).

## KAV-DANN POWER ARMOR

Source: [^1]

![Defense 2 Soak 2 Hard Points 6 Encumbrance 4 Price (R) 13,500 Rarity 7|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/9/9c/Profile_Kav-Dann_Power_Armor.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Defense 2 Soak 2 Hard Points 6 Encumbrance 4 Price (R) 13,500 Rarity 7

Like many other varieties of powered armor, a Kav-Dann suit is spaceworthy and includes a full life-support suite that can be modified for most alien physiologies. A wearer can survive for four hours in a vacuum or unbreathable atmosphere, and gains a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to checks to resist the effects of radiation. The helmet incorporates a [[Comlink|long-range comlink]] and a basic visual package that allows the wearer to remove a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die caused by to darkness, smoke, or other environmental factors that might affect vision from all [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]], [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]], and Combat checks.[^2]

Kav-Dann power armor increases the wearer's [[Brawn]] by 1 so long as it remains powered, though this bonus does not increase the user's soak or wound threshold. The listed encumbrance of 4 reflects the bulk of the armor when powered. If the suit loses power for any reason, the encumbrance increases to 12, defense is reduced to 0, and the bonus to Brawn is lost. Donning a suit of Kav-Dann power armor takes ten minutes, though this time can be halved if the wearer is assisted.[^3]
