Phase I ARC trooper armor includes a [[Comlink|comlink]].[^0-3] The various systems built into Phase I ARC trooper armor remove a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die from all [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] checks made by the wearer.[^0-3][^1-4] \[And provide all the benefits of a [[Hardened Comlink|hardened comlink]].\][^1-4] In addition, the integrated [[Load-Bearing Gear|load-bearing harness]] increases the wearer's encumbrance threshold by three.[^0-3][^1-4]
