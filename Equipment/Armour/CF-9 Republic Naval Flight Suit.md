CF-9 flight suits include a built-in [[Breath Mask and Respirator|respirator]]. Characters wearing CF-9 Republic naval flight suits remove up to one [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die from [[Piloting - Space (Ag)|Piloting (Space)]] and [[Piloting - Planetary (Ag)|Piloting (Planetary)]] checks added due to the danger or physical stress of the action.[^2]
