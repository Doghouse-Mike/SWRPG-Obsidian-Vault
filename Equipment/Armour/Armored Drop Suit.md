Armored drop suits are light laminate body armor constructed around a powerful repulsorlift unit, allowing the wearer to fly between firing positions or rain down attacks from above.

> More information about the armor available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Armored_drop_suit).

## ARMORED DROP SUIT

Source: [^1][^2]

![Defense 1 Soak 1 Hard Points 2 Encumbrance 6 Price 7,500 Rarity 7|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/f/f2/Profile_Armored_Drop_Suit.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Defense 1 Soak 1 Hard Points 2 Encumbrance 6 Price 7,500 Rarity 7

As a maneuver, the wearer can engage the suit's flight systems to function as a silhouette 0, speed 2, handling +2, system strain threshold 3 vehicle that requires [[Piloting - Planetary (Ag)|Piloting (Planetary)]] to operate. Further, while the flight systems are engaged and the wearer is conscious, they do not suffer damage from falling.
