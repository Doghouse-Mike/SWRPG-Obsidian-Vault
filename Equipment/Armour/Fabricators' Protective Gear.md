Worn by welders, machinists, tinkerers, and mechanics throughout the galaxy, fabricators' protective gear is a catchall term for a varied collection of heavy padded clothing.

## FABRICATORS' PROTECTIVE GEAR

Source: [^1]

![Defense 0 Soak 1 Hard Points 0 Encumbrance 1 Price 450 Rarity 1|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/9/94/Profile_Fabricators%27_Protective_Gear.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Defense 0 Soak 1 Hard Points 0 Encumbrance 1 Price 450 Rarity 1

This armor allows the wearer to ignore the effects of fires or acids of rating 1 or 2. It has a soak value of 3 against damage from weapons with the [[Item Qualities#Blast (Active)|Blast]] quality, but only when the quality is activated and used to inflict damage on the wearer. It provides only 1 point of soak against all other damage that was not inflicted through that quality.[^0-2]
