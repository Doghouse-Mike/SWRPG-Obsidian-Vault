Released less than a year following the Battle of Geonosis, the armour was reinforced further to be capable of sustaining heavier ordnance, to the point of partly deflecting laser cannon rounds. In addition to this, the increased internal power sources was able to be rerouted into a proprietary optical camouflage system that was noted as the Night Ops mode.

## MK III KATARN-CLASS COMMANDO ARMOUR

Source: [^1-1]

![Defense 1 Soak 2 Hard Points 3 Encumbrance 6 Price (R) 9,000 Rarity 10|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/1/12/Profile_Mk_III_Katarn-class_Commando_Armour.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Defense 1 Soak 2 Hard Points 3 Encumbrance 6 Price (R) 9,000 Rarity 10

This armour provides all the same benefits as the [[Mk I Katarn-class Commando Armour (homebrew)|Mk I]] and [[Mk II Katarn-class Commando Armour|Mk II variants]].[^1-1]

Gunnery checks with energy weapons targeting a character wearing the Mk III armour have their difficulty upgraded twice. The Night Ops mode can be activated as a maneuver and functions similarly to the [[Optical Camouflage System]] attachment . While this mode is active, the armour loses its defence rating.[^2]

### THE OLD MK I ARMOUR

Source: [^1-1]

The Mark II and Mark III Katarn armour was released quite early into the Clone Wars, though they did not make many improvements which can be directly represented as substantive mechanics in the game. In this way, a Game Master may want to restrict the expanded modularity and rules of the Mk II and III version to those versions. However, in the original continuity, most of these rules applied to Mk I as well. In addition, one might choose to increase the cost of the armour profiles by more than double if the campaign is set after the Clone Wars.
