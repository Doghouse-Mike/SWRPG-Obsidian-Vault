## MK I KATARN-CLASS COMMANDO ARMOUR

Source: [^0-1]

![Defense 1 Soak 2 Hard Points 3 Encumbrance 4 Price (R) 6,500 Rarity 7|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/2/27/Profile_Mk_I_Katarn-class_Commando_Armour_%28homebrew%29.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Defense 1 Soak 2 Hard Points 3 Encumbrance 4 Price (R) 6,500 Rarity 7

\[I\]ts insulated bodysuit grants the wearer protection from heat, cold, radiation, and most known toxins. A six-hour air supply enables the wearer to function in hostile environments, including the vacuum of space.[^0-1]

The wearer of this armour gains the following benefits from the integrated systems:[^1-2]

- Remove two [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice added to the user's checks due to darkness. Enemies add two [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice to any checks they make to intercept communications from the character through squad comms.
- Add a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to all [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] and [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]] checks while within Medium range of one or more allies wearing Katarn-class commando armour; and the full benefits of an integrated comlink.

The armour includes a pair of retractable vibroknives in the bracers which can be used with the following profile: ([[Brawl (Br)|Brawl]]; Damage +1; Critical 2; Range \[Engaged\]; [[Item Qualities#Pierce (Passive)|Pierce]] 2, [[Item Qualities#Vicious (Passive)|Vicious]] 1).[^1-2]

In addition, the integrated backpack increases encumbrance threshold by 2 and may be equipped with one of the following modular units:[^1-2]

- **Bacta Pods:** Storage for up to six [[Stimpack|stimpacks]] and two [[Bacta Spray|bacta sprays]]. Counts as the right tools for the job to make [[Medicine (Int)|Medicine]] checks to heal characters without a penalty.
- **Computers & Communications Unit:** Allows for encrypted communications up to a relay in orbit. Counts as the right tools of the job in any [[Computers (Int)|Computers]] check (such as slicing).
- **Ordnance Pods:** Storage for eight rounds of the anti-armour mode of the [[DC-17m InterChangeable Weapon System|DC-17m ICWS]] and two additional [[Category - Grenade|grenades]] (of encumbrance 1 or lower).
- **Integrated Holster:** May draw or stow a weapon of encumbrance 5 or lower on their backpack as an incidental, instead of a maneuver.

### THE OLD MK I ARMOUR

Source: [^1-2]

The Mark II and Mark III Katarn armour was released quite early into the Clone Wars, though they did not make many improvements which can be directly represented as substantive mechanics in the game. In this way, a Game Master may want to restrict the expanded modularity and rules of the Mk II and III version to those versions. However, in the original continuity, most of these rules applied to Mk I as well. In addition, one might choose to increase the cost of the armour profiles by more than double if the campaign is set after the Clone Wars.
