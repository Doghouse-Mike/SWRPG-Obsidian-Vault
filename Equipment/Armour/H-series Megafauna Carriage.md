## H-SERIES MEGAFAUNA CARRIAGE

A beast must have a slihouette of at least 3 and a Brawn of at least 5 to be fitted with a howdah. Basic howdah configurations are readily available for beasts with a tetrapodal or quadrupedal stance, like [[Fambaa|fambaa]] or [[Dewback|dewback]], at the listed price. Howdahs for creatures with more appendages, like the six-legged acklay, can be had for a small additional fee, generally 500 credits. Howdahs can be armored and fitted with one or two Gunnery or vehicle-scale weapons, following the usual restrictions for silhouette \[...\].

A megafauna carriage can only be equipped to [[Category - Riding|bestial riding animals]]. In addition to its other effects, using it allows the character to ride the beast without suffering \[...\] bareback riding penalties \[...\].
