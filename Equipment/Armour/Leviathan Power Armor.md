Designed by the Mon Calamari for use in subaquatic military operations, Leviathan power armor has seen plenty of use within the Rebel Alliance.

## LEVIATHAN POWER ARMOR

Source: [^0-1]

![Defense 2 Soak 2 Hard Points 1 Encumbrance 8 Price 15,000 Rarity 8|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/a/aa/Profile_Leviathan_Power_Armor.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Defense 2 Soak 2 Hard Points 1 Encumbrance 8 Price 15,000 Rarity 8

Any character wearing a Leviathan suit that was not built for \[them\] upgrades the difficulty of Agility and Brawn-based checks once.

A Leviathan suit is fully sealed and has an integrated [[Breath Mask and Respirator|respirator]]. It automatically possesses the following attachments: a [[Heating System|heating system]], an [[Enhanced Optics Suite|enhanced optics suite]], and a [[Strength Enhancing System|strength enhancement system]]. These attachments are already installed and cannot be removed. Each suit is also equipped with one [[Mon Cal Defenses Mini-Torpedo Launcher|Mon Cal Defenses mini torpedo launcher]] and one [[DF-D1 Duo-Flechette Rifle|Salus DF-D1 duo-flechette rifle]].
