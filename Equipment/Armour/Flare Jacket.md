Originally designed by a high-end clothing manufacturer for making literally flashy entrances, the flare jacket caught the attention of organizations that saw potential military use and contracted Ayelixe/Krongbing Textiles to produce a more intense version for a personal defense coat.

> More information about the armor available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Flare_Jacket).

**Models include:** Ayelixe/Krongbing Personal Defense Coat, Brennkeyes Syndicate flare jacket.

Once per encounter, a character wearing a flare jacket can trigger the charges as a maneuver. Each character within short range (besides the wearer) must make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance check]]**. Each character who fails is staggered for one round, plus one additional round per three [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results.
