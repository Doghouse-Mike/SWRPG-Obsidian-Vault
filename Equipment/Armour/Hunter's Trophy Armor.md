## HUNTER'S TROPHY ARMOR (CUSTOM-MADE)

> Defense 0 Soak 2 Hard Points 1 Encumbrance 4 Price (R) 1,000 Rarity 6

A character wearing trophy armor suffers a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die to [[Charm (Pr)|Charm]] checks, but gains two [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to [[Coercion (Will)|Coercion]] checks due to the armor's intimidating appearance. At the GM's discretion, [[Charm (Pr)|Charm]] checks made to impress or persuade other characters of the wearer's prowess may receive two [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] dice instead of a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die.[^0-1]

Trophy armor has two Prices and Availabilities. Use the first price listed (\[R\] 2,000/9) if a character is attempting to purchase an existing suit of trophy armor outright, and the second price listed (\[R\] 1,000/6) if a character has already procured a suitable hide and wishes to have it fashioned into trophy armor by an artisan.[^2]
