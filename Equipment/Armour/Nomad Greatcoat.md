Long associated with the inhabitants of the Nomad Mountains, this distinctive greatcoat has become popular since its adoption by the military of Corellia as cold weather gear.

## NOMAD GREATCOAT

Source: [^1]

![Defense 0 Soak 1 Hard Points 0 Encumbrance 1 Price 100 Rarity 4|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/e/e6/Profile_Nomad_Greatcoat.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Defense 0 Soak 1 Hard Points 0 Encumbrance 1 Price 100 Rarity 4

The Nomad Greatcoat reduces the difficulty of Resilience checks made to resist the effects of extreme cold by one, and removes a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die added to checks due to extreme cold.
