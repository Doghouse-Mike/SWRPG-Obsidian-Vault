Specially designed for those who make their living behind the wheel, the Shockrider crash suits from Ayelixe/Krongbing Textiles Corporation are built to help pilots of speeders, speeder bikes, swoops, and other fast atmospheric vehicles survive even the worst crashes.

> More information about the armor available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shockrider_crash_suit).

## A/KT SHOCKRIDER CRASH SUIT

Source: [^1][^2]

![Defense 0 Soak 2 Hard Points 1 Encumbrance 2 Price 3,000 Rarity 3|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/c/c8/Profile_AKT_Shockrider_Crash_Suit.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Defense 0 Soak 2 Hard Points 1 Encumbrance 2 Price 3,000 Rarity 3

Thanks to its rugged construction and integrated armor, a crash suit reduces any strain received from Critical Hits dealt to the wearer's vehicle by 1 (to a minimum of 1) and the flame-resistant coating reduces the damage dealt to the wearer by fires and weapons with the [[Item Qualities#Burn (Active)|Burn]] quality by 1.[^3][^4]
