At their most basic, enviro-suits are fully sealed, full-body suits made of sturdy materials designed to keep sentients alive in inhospitable places.

> More information about the armor available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/EVA_suit).

## ENVIRO-SUIT

Source: [^0-1][^2]

![Defense 0 Soak 2 Hard Points 1 Encumbrance 2 Price 750 Rarity 2|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/3/31/Profile_Enviro-suit.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Defense 0 Soak 2 Hard Points 1 Encumbrance 2 Price 750 Rarity 2

Enviro-suits have an operation life of eight hours until they need to be recharged and refilled. Due to their many layers of protective shielding, enviro-suits tend to be bulky and reduce a wearer's sense of their surroundings. An individual wearing an enviro-suit suffers one [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die on [[Agility]]- and [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]]-based checks.[^0-1][^3]
