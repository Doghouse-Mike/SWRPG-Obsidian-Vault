## SCAVENGED CLONE ARMOR

Source: [^1]

![Defense 0 Soak 2 Hard Points 2 Encumbrance 5 Price (R) 1,000 Rarity 4|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/0/05/Profile_Scavenged_Clone_Armor.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Defense 0 Soak 2 Hard Points 2 Encumbrance 5 Price (R) 1,000 Rarity 4

Due to the piecemeal nature of its construction and the difficulty of adapting the specific fit of clone armor, all wearers of scavenged clone armor suffer a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die to [[Coordination (Ag)|Coordination]] and [[Stealth (Ag)|Stealth]] checks while wearing it. Characters wearing scavenged clone armor may suffer one or more [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice on interactions with Republic officials and representatives, at the GM's discretion.[^2]
