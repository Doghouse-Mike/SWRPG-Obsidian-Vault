### or MK I KATARN-CLASS COMMANDO ARMOR

\[I\]ts insulated bodysuit grants the wearer protection from heat, cold, radiation, and most known toxins. A six-hour air supply enables the wearer to function in hostile environments, including the vacuum of space. Integrated scanners and communications suites enable the wearer to track hostiles while keeping in touch with allies.[^0-1][^1-2]

The armor's backpack is modular, enabling it to incorporate modifications suited to specific battlefield needs such as survival gear, additional oxygen supplies, and field electronics.[^0-1][^1-2]

A full suit of Katarn-class armor provides a wearer with all the benefits of a [[Combat Scanner|combat scanner]] and a [[Breath Mask and Respirator|breath mask]], and increases the wearer's encumbrance threshold by 3. In addition, the suit's systems remove a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die from all [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] and [[Survival (Cun)|Survival]] checks made by the wearer.[^3]
