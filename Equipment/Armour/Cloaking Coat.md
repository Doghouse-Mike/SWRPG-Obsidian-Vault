## CLOAKING COAT

Source: [^0-1][^2]

![Defense 0 Soak 1 Hard Points 1 Encumbrance 4 Price (R) 550 Rarity 8|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/7/7d/Profile_Cloaking_Coat.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Defense 0 Soak 1 Hard Points 1 Encumbrance 4 Price (R) 550 Rarity 8

A cloaking coat upgrades the difficulty of checks using sensors or electronic forms of detection to find an individual wearing it twice. Droids with an Intellect lower than the wearer's ranks in [[Computers (Int)|Computers]] are affected similarly when trying to detect the wearer.[^0-1][^3]
