## CLONE RECONNAISSANCE ARMOR

Source: [^1]

![Defense 0 Soak 2 Hard Points 2 Encumbrance 3 Price (R) 4,000 Rarity 6|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/8/8d/Profile_Clone_Reconnaissance_Armor.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Defense 0 Soak 2 Hard Points 2 Encumbrance 3 Price (R) 4,000 Rarity 6

Characters wearing clone reconnaissance armor add a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to [[Stealth (Ag)|Stealth]] checks they make. These suits incorporate visors with integrated thermal and low-light sensors, allowing wearers to remove a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die from visual [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] checks they make.[^2]
