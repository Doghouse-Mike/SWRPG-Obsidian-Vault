Baffleweave fabric scrambles sensors, and is popular among criminals who want to carry weapons undetected and leave unusable footage of security feeds. Garments made of baffleweave are often designed in loud or deceptive patterns to fool the naked eye as well. It can cause as many problems as it solves if not removed when not intended to be in use.
