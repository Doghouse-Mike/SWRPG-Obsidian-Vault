Fiber ultramesh armor is the standard armor of Verpine colony guards. It is a more advanced version of the Verpine fiber mesh armor, with a tighter weave and sturdier construction.

> More information about the armor available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Verpine_fiber_ultramesh).

## VERPINE FIBER ULTRAMESH ARMOR

Source: [^1][^2]

As a maneuver, the wearer may power up or power down the armor. While the armor is powered up, the wearer gains +2 defense. However, any character may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result or two [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results from a combat check against the wearer to cause it to run out of power, at which point the armor becomes powered down and cannot be powered up until the end of the encounter.
