Ayelixe/Kronbing's Climbsuit is a popular set of lightweight armor designed for climbing and rappelling.

> More information about the armor available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Climbsuit).

## CLIMBSUIT

Source: [^0-1][^2]

![Defense 0 Soak 1 Hard Points 2 Encumbrance 2 Price 1,450 Rarity 4|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/7/79/Profile_Climbsuit.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Defense 0 Soak 1 Hard Points 2 Encumbrance 2 Price 1,450 Rarity 4

Wearing a Climbsuit upgrades the ability of the wearer's checks to climb or rappel down a surface once. The Climbsuit also reduces damage and strain suffered from falling by 5 and reduces any Critical Injury results from falling by 10.[^0-1][^3]
