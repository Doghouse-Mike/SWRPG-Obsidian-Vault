## BOBA FETT'S ARMOR

Source: [^1]

> [!quote] Image text
> Defense 2 Soak 3 Hard Points 0- Encumbrance 6 Price Not for Sale Rarity 10

Boba Fett's customized Mandalorian armor comes equipped with an [[Enhanced Optics Suite|enhanced optics suite]] (remove two [[Narrative Dice#Setback|setback]] dice from [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]], [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]], and combat checks due to environmental effects that obscure vision), several weapon systems, an integrated jetpack with rocket, and range finder (adds an automatic [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result on all integrated weapon attacks).

**Integrated Armor Weapons:** Flamethrower (Ranged \[Light\]; Damage 5; Critical 2; Range \[Short\]; [[Item Qualities#Blast (Active)|Blast]] 2, [[Item Qualities#Burn (Active)|Burn]] 2, [[Item Qualities#Pierce (Passive)|Pierce]] 2, [[Item Qualities#Vicious (Passive)|Vicious]] 3), jetpack missile tube (Gunnery; Damage 20; Critical 2; Range \[Extreme\]; [[Item Qualities#Blast (Active)|Blast]] 10, [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] 1, [[Item Qualities#Cumbersome (Passive)|Cumbersome]] 3, [[Item Qualities#Guided (Active)|Guided]] 3, [[Item Qualities#Limited Ammo (Passive)|Limited Ammo]] 1, [[Item Qualities#Prepare (Passive)|Prepare]] 1), whipcord (Ranged \[Light\]; Damage 1; Critical –; Range \[Short\]; [[Item Qualities#Ensnare (Active)|Ensnare]] 3, [[Item Qualities#Ensnare (Active)|Knockdown]], [[Item Qualities#Limited Ammo (Passive)|Limited Ammo]] 1).

**Premeditated Contingency:** Once per encounter as a maneuver, a character wearing Boba Fett's armor may make an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance check]]** to draw any weapon or item of a rarity equal to or less than the total number of net [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] and [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results at the GM's discretion.

### HEARTLESS SILENCE

Once per session, as an out-of-turn incidental \[Boba Fett\] may spend a Destiny Point to cause a social check targeting him to fail. The character may not attempt another social skill check targeting Boba Fett for the remainder of the encounter.
