Sith Troopers wear an improved version of the armor other stormtroopers wear, with higher quality gammaplast plating kitted out with a number of attachments. Gammaplast armor found elsewhere is almost always made to order. Its sturdy and light construction is highly configurable.

> More information about the armor available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gammaplast).
