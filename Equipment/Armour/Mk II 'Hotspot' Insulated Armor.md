## MK II "HOTSPOT" INSULATED ARMOR

Source: [^1]

![Defense 0 Soak 2 Hard Points 2 Encumbrance 6 Price (R) 5,500 Rarity 7|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/5/50/Profile_Mk_II_Hotspot_Insulated_Armor.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Defense 0 Soak 2 Hard Points 2 Encumbrance 6 Price (R) 5,500 Rarity 7

Mk II "Hotspot" insulated armor comes with a built-in [[Comlink|comlink]]. It adds two [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] dice to the wearer's checks to avoid adverse effects from exposure to extreme heat or toxic gases and reduces the damage the wearer takes from fire and hazardous environments by 1. Additionally, when targeting the wearer, attackers must spend one additional [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result to activate the [[Item Qualities#Burn (Active)|Burn]] item quality and treat the quality's rating as one lower.
