In rare circumstances, Jedi were called in to treat and societies for which status and associated symbols played a significant factor in negotiations.

## FORMAL COUNCIL ARMOR

Source: [^1]

![Defense 0 Soak 2 Hard Points 2 Encumbrance 5 Price (R) 8,000 Rarity 10|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/2/2e/Profile_Formal_Council_Armor.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Defense 0 Soak 2 Hard Points 2 Encumbrance 5 Price (R) 8,000 Rarity 10

A character wearing formal Council armor adds two automatic [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results to [[Negotiation (Pr)|Negotiation]] checks when interacting with individuals who hold Jedi in high regard. At the GM's discretion, the character may add two automatic [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results to [[Coercion (Will)|Coercion]] checks when interacting with individuals who hate or fear the Jedi (such as representatives of the Galactic Empire).
