Suits of laminate armor consist of a number of formfitted plastoid plates designed to protect important areas, worn over a snug, reinforced armorweave body glove.

> More information about the armor available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Plastoid).

**Models Include:** Imperial Department of Military Research Stormtrooper Armor.
