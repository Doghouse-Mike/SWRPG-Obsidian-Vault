Rebels, Force users, and scoundrels may find themselves operating in some of the harshest, most inhospitable environments the galaxy has to offer.

> More information about the armor available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Adverse_environment_gear).

**Models Include:** Pretormin Environmental HS-90 Hazmat Suit, ScorSear 200 Survival Kit, Pretormin Environmental Froz 350.
