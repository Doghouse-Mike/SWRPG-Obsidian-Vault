## SMUGGLER'S TRENCHCOAT

> Defense 1 Soak 1 Hard Points 0 Encumbrance 3 Price 1,650 Rarity 7

Checks made to find anything hidden within a smuggler's trenchcoat while it is being worn are opposed by the [[Skulduggery (Cun)|Skulduggery]] of the wearer. The coat can conceal items with a total encumbrance value of up to 2.
