## DESTRI-SERIES LAMINATE BEAST ARMOR

Source: [^1]

![Defense 0 Soak 4 Hard Points 1 Encumbrance 7 Price 8,500 Rarity 7|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/7/7e/Profile_Destri-series_Laminate_Beast_Armor.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Defense 0 Soak 4 Hard Points 1 Encumbrance 7 Price 8,500 Rarity 7

Destri armor is relatively heavy, and although it's designed to be as easy as possible on the beast wearing it, the armor still necessarily restricts movement and tends to make a creature slower. Beasts wearing Destri-series beast armor have their Agility reduced by 1, to a minimum of 1.

Laminate beast armor can only be equipped to [[Category - Riding|bestial riding animals]]. In addition to its other effects, using it allows the character to ride the beast without suffering \[...\] bareback riding penalties \[...\].
