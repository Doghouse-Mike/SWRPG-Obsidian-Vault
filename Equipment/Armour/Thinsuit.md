Used by professional scouts, science-lab workers, and special forces the galaxy over, the Thinsuit by Karflo Corporation is among the most popular environmental protection gear around.

> More information about the armor available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Thinsuit).

## THINSUIT

Source: [^0-1][^2]

![Defense 0 Soak 1 Hard Points 0 Encumbrance - Price 1,200 Rarity 6|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/f/f0/Profile_Thinsuit.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Defense 0 Soak 1 Hard Points 0 Encumbrance - Price 1,200 Rarity 6

\[...\] It comes with a facemask and filtration system that purifies standard atmosphere for up to six hours and provides one hour of internal power. The stock suit comes with six removable filters and power packs, easily stored in its many pockets.[^0-1][^1-3]

A character equipped with a Thinsuit removes two [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice imposed by the environment from their checks.[^0-1][^1-3]
