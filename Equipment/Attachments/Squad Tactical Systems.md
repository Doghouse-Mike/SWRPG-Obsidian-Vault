Sensor and comm systems are common installations on armor, but some groups take the practice a step further, equipping all their members with uniform packages designed to work in tandem.

## SQUAD TACTICAL SYSTEMS

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance — Price 2,000 Rarity 5|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/1/11/Profile_Squad_Tactical_Systems.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance — Price 2,000 Rarity 5

This attachment may be installed in the helmet of any armor.

**Models Include:** Fabritech Mk.II "Crosslink" Sensors.\
**Base Modifiers:** Wearer adds a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to all [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] and [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]] checks while within medium range of one or more allies also using armor with this attachment. Includes integrated comlink.\
**Modification Options:** 1 Skill ([[Perception (Cun)|Perception]]) Mod, 1 Skill ([[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]]) Mod.

[^1]: [[Category - Clone Wars Begin|Rise of the Separatists]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 65)
