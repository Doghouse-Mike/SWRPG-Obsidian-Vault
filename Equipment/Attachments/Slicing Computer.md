## SLICING COMPUTER

Source: [^1]

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 5,000 Rarity 6

This attachment can be applied to any starship.

**Models Include:** Various custom models.\
**Base Modifiers:** Counts as [[Slicer Gear|slicer gear]] that be used by one passenger. Add an automatic [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result to [[Computers (Int)|Computers]] checks made to slice enemy ships' systems.\
**Modification Options:** 2 Innate Talent ([[Codebreaker talent|Codebreaker]]) Mods, 1 Innate Talent ([[Defensive Slicing talent|Defensive Slicing]]) Mod.
