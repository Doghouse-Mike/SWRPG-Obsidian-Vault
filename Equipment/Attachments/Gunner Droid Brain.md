Much more than a simple targeting computer, a specialized droid brain can be installed on a ship to operate its weapons independently or assist a sentient gunner.

> More information about the attachment available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/CD-14c_Gunner_droid_brain).

## GUNNER DROID BRAIN

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 0 Encumbrance - Price 7,000 Rarity 6|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/7/74/Profile_Gunner_Droid_Brain.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 0 Encumbrance - Price 7,000 Rarity 6

This attachment can be applied to any vehicle or starship.

**Models Include:** GoCorp CD-14c.\
**Base Modifiers:** Droid brain can perform [[Gunnery (Ag)|Gunnery]] checks for vehicle with a [[Gunnery (Ag)|Gunnery]] skill of 2 and an [[Agility]] of 0 (if unassisted, it rolls two [[Narrative Dice#Ability (Green)|ability]] dice for [[Gunnery (Ag)|Gunnery]] checks). Although a droid brain can be connected to more than one weapon system, it is still limited to one action per turn.\
**Modification Options:** 2 Increase the droid brain's [[Gunnery (Ag)|Gunnery]] skill by 1 Mods.

[^1]: [[Category - Technician|Special Modifications]]: *A Sourcebook for Technicians* (Page 66)
