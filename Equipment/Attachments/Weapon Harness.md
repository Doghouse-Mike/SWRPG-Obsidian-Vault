Designed to allow a single sentient to field even the heaviest portable weapon with no support, weapon harnesses are common among heavy weapon squads.

> More information about the attachment available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/WW480_weapon_harness).

## WEAPON HARNESS

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

![Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price 500 Rarity 2|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/8/8f/Profile_Weapon_Harness.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price 500 Rarity 2

This attachment can only be used with Ranged (Heavy) and Gunnery weapons.

**Models Include:** Arakyd WW480 "Goliath" Weapons Harness, Galactic Arms HR12 Power Harness, SoroSuub "Little Giant" Heavy Weapon Power Assist.\
**Base Modifiers:** Decrease the weapon's [[Item Qualities#Cumbersome (Passive)|Cumbersome]] rating by 2.\
**Modification Options:** 1 Innate Talent ([[Brace talent|Brace]]) Mod.
