This attachment can be applied to any starship or vehicle.

**Models Include:** Various models.\
**Base Modifiers:** Increase the difficulty of checks to detect this vessel using passive sensors by two, and the difficulty to detect this vessel using active sensors by one, while it is travelling at speed 3 or lower.\
**Modification Options:** None.
