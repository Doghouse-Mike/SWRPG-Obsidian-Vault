An overcharge valve is a simple (if dangerous) modification that allows the wielder of a blaster to discharge the entire ammunition reserve in a single, devastating shot.

> More information about the attachment available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Overcharge_valve).

## OVERCHARGE VALVE

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance – Price (R) 1,500 Rarity 6|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/8/8b/Profile_Overcharge_Valve.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance – Price (R) 1,500 Rarity 6

This attachment can be applied to any blaster or energy weapon that could logically benefit from it.

**Models Include:** Czerka "One-Shot" Overcharge Valve.\
**Base Modifiers:** As an incidental, the wielder may have the weapon gain the [[Item Qualities#Prepare (Passive)|Prepare]] 1 quality and increases its damage by 4 for the next attack \[they\] make with it. After firing, the weapon runs out of ammunition.\
**Modification Options:** None.
