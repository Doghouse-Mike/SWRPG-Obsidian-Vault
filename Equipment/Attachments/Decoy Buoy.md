A favorite of scoundrels and pirates, decoy buoys are often devices cobbled together from salvaged parts, including transponders and comm systems.

> More information about the attachment available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Buoy).

## DECOY BUOY

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 0 Encumbrance - Price (R) 5,000 Rarity 5|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/e/ee/Profile_Decoy_Buoy.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 0 Encumbrance - Price (R) 5,000 Rarity 5

This attachment can be applied to any starship.

**Models Include:** Numerous custom variants.\
**Base Modifiers:** A decoy buoy can be jettisoned as an action. The buoy appears to sensors to be a ship, and it can be programmed with a specific transponder signal with a [[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|**Hard difficulty**]] **[[Computers (Int)|Computers check]]**. In addition, the buoy can be set to transmit a distress signal or other data. A character operating a ship's sensors can make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Computers (Int)|Computers check]]** to identify that the buoy is not an actual ship. After the buoy is jettisoned, it no longer counts as being attached to the ship until reattached.\
**Modification Options:** 2 Add [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die to [[Computers (Int)|Computers]] checks to identify the buoy Mods.
