## ENHANCED HYPERSPACE SHIELDS

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 4,000 Rarity 5|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/e/e1/Profile_Enhanced_Hyperspace_Shields.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 4,000 Rarity 5

This attachment can be applied to any starship with a hyperdrive.

**Models Include:** Kuat Drive Yards PR-2 Enhanced Particle Shielding, numerous custom variants.\
**Base Modifiers:** Downgrade the difficulty of [[Astrogation (Int)|Astrogation]] checks to navigate this ship through hyperspace once.\
**Modification Options:** 2 Remove a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die from [[Astrogation (Int)|Astrogation]] checks to navigate this ship through hyperspace Mods.
