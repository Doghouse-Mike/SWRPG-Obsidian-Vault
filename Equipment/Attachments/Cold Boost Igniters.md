One of the main drawbacks of the Boost system on any podracer is that it requires enough running power for combustion to occur at the highest temperature and pressure possible. The lack of oxygen after the initial combustion can also play a role and air from bypass requires speed to take effect. No matter the engineering solution chosen (lower pressure fan air bypass, alternate exotic energy sources, additional boosters linked to an independent manifold and a different feed, etc), the system allows riggers to decrease the minimum speed threshold significantly without drowning the boosters.
