Over the course of their many wars with the Jedi, ancient Mandalorians found flame projectors to be highly effective against lightsaber wielding foes. Though direct conflicts with Jedi are now few and far between, the weapon has remained an essential part of most Mandalorians’ arsenals. The projector itself is generally mounted on a gauntlet, with a remote line connecting to a concealed fuel reservoir.
