In the midst of hectic races, piloting and what usually lies ahead takes the main focus of many pilots and it is often hard to keep an eye on anything that happens behind the podracer. Depending on the pod configuration as well as the specific anatomy of the pilot, taking a few seconds off piloting to cast a glance behind can often lead to catastrophic results. On the other hand, not being notified of opponents and threats coming from the rear can also cost someone their victory, which is why holocameras mounted at the back of podracers can prove incredibly handy to keep tabs on opponents.
