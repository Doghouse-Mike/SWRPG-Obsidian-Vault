Developed to overcome personal cloaking devices and other sensor-baffling defenses, a sonic scope uses ultrasonic sound waves to detect a target and generate a visual representation.

> More information about the attachment available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sonic_scope).

## SONIC SCOPE

Source: [^1][^2]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 1,500 Rarity 8|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/d/de/Profile_Sonic_Scope.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 1,500 Rarity 8

This attachment can be used with any Ranged (Heavy) weapon.

**Models Include:** CryonCorp PL-1 "Second Sight" Scope, SoroSuub Model Z Integrated Sonar System.\
**Base Modifiers:** The character may observe and target enemies who are completely hidden by solid objects that the weapon can penetrate. At the Game Master's discretion, the target might receive additional defense or even soak to reflect shooting through a solid object.\
**Modification Options:** 1 Skill ([[Cool (Pr)|Cool]]) Mod, 1 Skill ([[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]]) Mod, 2 Innate Talent ([[Precise Aim talent|Precise Aim]]) Mods.
