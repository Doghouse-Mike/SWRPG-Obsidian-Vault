## SMARTMED 2000 INTEGRATED MED-SYSTEMS

Source: [^1][^2]

![Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance 1 Price 2,500 Rarity 7|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/0/0b/Profile_Kiirium_Coating.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance 1 Price 2,500 Rarity 7

This attachment can be added to any armor that covers the majority of the wearer's body, subject to the GM's discretion.

**Models Include:** N/A.\
**Base Modifiers:** Once per session, the user may activate the attachment as an incidental, counting as one use of a [[Stimpack|stimpack]]. Additionally, all [[Medicine (Int)|Medicine]] checks made to heal the wearer gain a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die.\
**Modification Options:** 2 All successful Medicine checks made to heal wounds the wearer is suffering heal 1 additional wound Mods.
