## VIBRO-BAYONET

Source: [^1][^2]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance 1 Price 300 Rarity 3|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/8/81/Profile_Vibro-bayonet.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text

This attachment can only be applied to any Ranged (Heavy) weapon that could logically mount a bayonet.

**Models Include:** Czerka Arms "Last Resort" Vibro-Bayonet, Merr-Sonn Model 3 Combat Attachment.\
**Base Modifiers:** Enables the weapon to be used in melee combat with the following profile at the user's choice: ([[Melee (Br)|Melee]]; Damage +1; Critical 2; Range \[Engaged\]; [[Item Qualities#Inaccurate (Passive)|Inaccurate]] 1, [[Item Qualities#Pierce (Passive)|Pierce]] 2, [[Item Qualities#Vicious (Passive)|Vicious]] 1).\
**Modification Options:** 2 Increase the Melee weapon's damage by 1 Mods.
