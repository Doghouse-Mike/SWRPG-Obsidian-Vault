Similar to the spread barrel modification for blaster rifles, beam splitters function by modifying the Galven pattern and carefully mounting three or more emitters into a honeycomb configuration inside a stock blaster barrel.

> More information about the attachment available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Beam_splitter).

## BEAM SPLITTER

Source: [^1][^2]

![Hard Points Required 3 Encumbrance - Price (R) 1,250 Rarity 5|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/9/92/Profile_Beam_Splitter.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 3 Encumbrance - Price (R) 1,250 Rarity 5

This attachment can be applied to any blaster or suitable energy weapon that does not have the Blast quality.

**Models Include:**ArMek RE-312 Beam Splitter, Safidine "Sweeper" Emitter Modification Kit, Concordian Crescent Technologies ZE-1111 Spread Barrel.\
**Base Modifiers:** Grants the [[Item Qualities#Blast (Active)|Blast]] item quality with a value equal to half the weapon's damage (rounded up). Reduces the weapon's range by 1 to a minimum of engaged.\
**Modification Options:** 2 Item Quality ([[Item Qualities#Disorient (Active)|Disorient]] +1) Mods.
