Small, dull, and brittle, dragite gems are found in the caves and caverns of the D'olop mountain range on M'haeli, an agriworld in the Expansion Region.

> More information about the crystal available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dragite_gem).

## DRAGITE GEM

Source: [^1][^2]

![Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price (R) 14,000 Rarity 7|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/9/9a/Profile_Dragite_Gem.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price (R) 14,000 Rarity 7

**Base Modifiers:** Installing this crystal changes a lightsaber's damage to 7, critical rating to 3, and the lightsaber gains the [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] 1, [[Item Qualities#Disorient (Active)|Disorient]] 1, and [[Item Qualities#Sunder (Passive)|Sunder]] item qualities. If the crystal is ever removed, the lightsaber loses these qualities and reverts to its previous base damage and critical rating.\
**Modification Options:** 1 Damage +1 Mod, 2 Item Quality ([[Item Qualities#Concussive (Active)|Concussive]] +1) Mods, 2 Item Quality ([[Item Qualities#Disorient (Active)|Disorient]] +1) Mods.
