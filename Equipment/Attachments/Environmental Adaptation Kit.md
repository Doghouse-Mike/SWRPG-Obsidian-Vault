The varied and often hazardous environments found throughout the galaxy have a tendency to hinder and harm even the sturdiest, most well-built pieces of equipment.

## ENVIRONMENTAL ADAPTATION KIT

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance — Price 100 Rarity 2|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/9/9c/Profile_Environmental_Adaptation_Kit.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance — Price 100 Rarity 2

Environmental adaptation kits can be attached to any ranged weapon.

**Models Include:** Various models and manufacturers.\
**Base Modifiers:** Prevents a weapon from breaking down as a result of a specific type of environment.\
**Modification Options:** None.
