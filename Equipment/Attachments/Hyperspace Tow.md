For those rare occasions that a salvager cannot repair a vessel, but wishes to remove it from a salvage site, she can opt to attach a hyperspace tow rig. The tow is little more than an external hyperspace docking rigging that attaches to the exterior of the towed ship, and patches into the astrogation computer of the salvagers ship with a simplified slave circuit. It allows the salvager to make short trips.
