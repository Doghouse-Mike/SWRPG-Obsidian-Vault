**Base Modifiers:** Installing a sorian crystal changes a lightsaber's damage to 6, its critical rating to 3, and the lightsaber gains the [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] 1 and [[Item Qualities#Sunder (Passive)|Sunder]] weapon qualities. Whenever the lightsaber is used with the [[Parry talent]], the wielder adds a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to their next combat check against their attacker before the end of the next round. If the crystal is ever removed, the lightsaber loses these qualities and reverts to its previous base damage and critical rating. \
**Modification Options:** 3 Damage +1 Mods, 1 Innate Talent ([[Parry talent|Parry]]) Mod, 1 Item Quality ([[Item Qualities#Disorient (Active)|Disorient]] +1) Mod.
