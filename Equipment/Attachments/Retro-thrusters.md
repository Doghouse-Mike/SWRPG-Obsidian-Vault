Feeding directly from the main thrust output, auxiliary thrusters are positioned at the front of the engines. Whenever the pilot brakes, a system of valves generate a powerful thrust forward to assist the main airbrakes, requiring compromises on structural integrity and impacting Cooling by involving the engines in braking procedures.
