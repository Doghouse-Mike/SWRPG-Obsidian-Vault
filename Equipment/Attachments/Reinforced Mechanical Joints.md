It is possible to strengthen the mechanical resilience of podracer systems by using stronger joints, or just add redundancy to the most critical parts in order to ease their burden and absorb stress significantly. This type of modification is one of the most commonly applied to podracers, always with the aim to gain that little edge that can mean the difference between the survival of an engine and it going up in flames, and it is usually pretty affordable with cheap materials and a sizeable volume of elbow’s (and motor) grease.
