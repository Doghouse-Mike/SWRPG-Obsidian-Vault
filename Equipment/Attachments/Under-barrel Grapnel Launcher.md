Often seen among mercenary and special operations forces, under-barrel grapnel launchers provide a quick way to scale sheer surfaces in the midst of a combat situation or during a covert infiltration.

## UNDER-BARREL GRAPNEL LAUNCHER

Source: [^0-1][^1-2]

![Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance 2 Price 500 Rarity 3|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/d/d0/Profile_Under-barrel_Grapnel_Launcher.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance 2 Price 500 Rarity 3

This attachment can be used with any [[Ranged - Light (Ag)|Ranged (Light)]] or [[Ranged - Heavy (Ag)|Ranged (Heavy)]] weapon that could logically have a grapnel launcher mounted on it.

**Models Include:** Merr-Sonn KR-2 Auxiliary Grapnel, TaggeCo "Skysailer" Grapnel Launcher.\
**Base Modifiers:** As an action, a character may make an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Ranged - Light (Ag)|Ranged (Light) check]]** to secure the grappling hook to an object within medium range. On success, as an action, they may reel in the cord, pulling themselves to the object (or, if the object is unsecured and lighter than they are, pulling it to them). A character may use the grappling hook to pull another character aloft with them; if they do, they must make an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Athletics (Br)|Athletics check]]** to avoid losing their grip on their partner or the gun.[^0-1][^1-2] Increases weapon's encumbrance by 2.[^1-2]\
**Modification Options:** None.
