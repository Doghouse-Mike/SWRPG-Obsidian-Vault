## SHIELD DISCHARGE PACK

Source: [^1][^2]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance 1 Price 800 Rarity 5|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/5/53/Profile_Shield_Discharge_Pack.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text

This attachment only be applied to a shield or buckler.

**Models Include:** Koromondian "Deterrent" Shield Pack.\
**Base Modifiers:** When the wielder suffers a hit from a [[Melee (Br)|Melee]], [[Brawl (Br)|Brawl]], or [[Lightsaber (Br)|Lightsaber]] combat check, after the attack is resolved, the character may spend two [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result to cause the attacker to suffer 3 strain (this ignores soak).\
**Modification Options:** 1 Innate Talent ([[Defensive Stance talent|Defensive Stance]]) Mod, 2 Item Quality ([[Item Qualities#Disorient (Active)|Disorient]] +1) Mods.
