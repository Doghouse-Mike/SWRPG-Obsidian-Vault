Podracing attracts a wide range of pilots, from those who delight in testing their skill against fellow speed enthusiasts to those who are only interested in winning, no matter what it takes. For the latter, one method is not to boost their own vehicle’s performance, but instead to hamper their opponents’ podracers. Using concealed compartments (known more informally as “dirty tricks kits”), pilots can release small nails, reactive chemicals, or other debris designed to foul the engines of a trailing vehicle, slowing it down or even knocking it out of the running. Though illegal, when used out of sight of officials these compartments can decide the fate of many a race.

## FOREIGN DEBRIS EJECTOR

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 900 Rarity 2|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/2/23/Profile_Foreign_Debris_Ejector_Adjustment.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 900 Rarity 2

**Models Include:** Various (crews craft them in secret)\
**Base Modifiers:** Encumbrance Capacity -1.

Take a Maneuver to eject a cloud of hazardous debris in the air. Any racer located 1 Pole behind must succeed a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Piloting - Planetary (Ag)|Piloting (Planetary) check]]** in order to dodge. Failure reduces the Speed and Top Speed of the afflicted podracer(s) by 1, and an additional 1 per additional net [[Narrative Dice#Failure|failure]] result, for 1 Round. Two [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results can be spent to extend the effect by 1 additional Round, and three [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result can inflict a Critical Hit. Anyone trying to notice the ejector adds three [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice to any [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] or [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]] check.

Can hold 1 charge of ENC 1, 10 credits and Rarity 1. Other charges can be carried inside the podracer’s holds.\
**Modification Options:** None.
