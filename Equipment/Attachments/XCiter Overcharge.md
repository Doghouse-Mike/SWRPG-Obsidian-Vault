An XCiter overcharge allows an individual to empty a blaster's energy cell in a single, extremely powerful shot.

## XCITER OVERCHARGE

Source: [^1][^2]

![Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price (R) 1,100 Rarity 7|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/4/49/Profile_XCiter_Overcharge.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price (R) 1,100 Rarity 7

While typically applied to holdout blasters, this attachment can be applied to any personal blaster weapon.

**Models Include:** BlasTech "Nova" Overcharge System.\
**Base Modifiers:** As an incidental, shooters may overcharge the XCiter on their blaster. This gives the weapon the [[Item Qualities#Prepare (Active)|Prepare]] 1 item quality and increases its damage on the next attack by 4. Once it is fired, the weapon runs out of ammo and must be recharged. GMs may spend three [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result from any combat check involving an overcharged shot with this weapon to cause the weapon to explode and deal its base damage to the shooter.\
**Modification Options:** None.
