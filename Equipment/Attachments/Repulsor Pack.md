While lacking the range and power of a true jetpack, a repulsor pack provides a reliable—and less terrifying—alternative for those new to personal flight devices.

> More information about the attachment available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Repulsor_pack).

## REPULSOR PACK

Source: [^1][^0-2]

![Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price 2,500 Rarity 4|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/3/30/Profile_Repulsor_Pack.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price 2,500 Rarity 4

**Models Include:** Mitrinomon Transports T-8 Repulsor Pack, Astralor Corp. Mk I Personal Repulsorlift System.[^0-2]\
**Base Modifiers:** Allows the user to function as a speed 1, handling -1, system strain threshold 2 vehicle that can only operate in atmosphere and has the same silhouette as the operator. It has a maximum altitude of seventy-five meters, and the operator's [[Coordination (Ag)|Coordination]] skill is used for any relevant checks.\
**Modification Options:** 1 Increase handling by +1 Mod.
