Those who know what they are doing can sometimes overcharge the power cell of a lightsaber to increase the power of the blade, but these changes make the power cell volatile and potentially unstable.

> More information about the attachment available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Overcharged_power_cell).

## OVERCHARGED POWER CELL

Source: [^1][^2]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 500 Rarity 6|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/8/8b/Profile_Custom_Grip.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 500 Rarity 6

This attachment can only be applied to lightsabers.[^3]

**Models Include:** None; users must custom-make each lightsaber attachment.\
**Base Modifiers:** The wielder of this lightsaber can spend an [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] on a combat check to increase the damage by one, and the GM can spend two [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results to decrease the damage dealt by one.\
**Modification Options:** None.
