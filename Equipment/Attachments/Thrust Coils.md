A popular modification to increase speed performance affixes a series of coils to the engine’s generator, storing energy out of rotor friction and acting as an enabler through magnetic impulses, often very noticeable by the magnetized, loud noise they produce. While injectors directly help with engine power, coils act as force multipliers in the upper rpm rates.

> More information about the attachment available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Thrust_Coil).
