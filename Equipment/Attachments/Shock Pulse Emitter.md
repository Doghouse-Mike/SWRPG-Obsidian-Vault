Shock pulse emitters operate along principles similar to a blaster's Stun setting, but at a much shorter effective range—short enough to make them of little use as standalone.

> More information about the attachment available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Stun_pulse).

## SHOCK PULSE EMITTER

This attachment can be installed on any Melee or Brawl weapon.

**Models Include:** Corellian Personal Defense Stun Pad.\
**Base Modifiers:** Grants the weapon the [[Item Qualities#Stun (Active)|Stun]] (+2) quality.\
**Modification Options:** 1 Item Quality ([[Item Qualities#Disorient (Active)|Disorient]] +1) Mod, 2 Item Quality ([[Item Qualities#Stun (Active)|Stun]] +1) Mods.
