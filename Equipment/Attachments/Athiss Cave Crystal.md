## ATHISS CAVE CRYSTAL

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price (R) 7,000 Rarity 8|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/1/15/Profile_Athiss_Cave_Crystal.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price (R) 7,000 Rarity 8

**Models Include:** N/A.\
**Base Modifiers:** Installing this crystal changes a lightsaber's damage to 6 and its critical rating to 3, and the lightsaber gains the [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] 1 and [[Item Qualities#Sunder (Active)|Sunder]] weapon qualities. If the crystal is ever removed, the lightsaber loses these qualities, and reverts to its previous base damage and critical rating.\
**Modification Options:** 2 Damage +1 Mods, 1 Decrease the weapon's critical rating by 1 to a minimum of 1 Mod, 1 Weapon Quality ([[Item Qualities#Vicious (Passive)|Vicious]] +1) Mod.
