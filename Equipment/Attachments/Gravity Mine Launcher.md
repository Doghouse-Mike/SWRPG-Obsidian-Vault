Gravity mine launchers, and their accompanying gravity mines, are small anti-vehicle mines produced by a number of arms manufacturers.

> More information about the attachment is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gravity_mine_launcher).

## GRAVITY MINE LAUNCHER

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 3,500 Rarity 6|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/8/8f/Profile_Gravity_Mine_Launcher.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 3,500 Rarity 6

The character piloting or operating the weapons on a vehicle equipped with a gravity mine launcher can deploy a single gravity mine anywhere on the ground within close range by spending an action. Once the mine is deployed, it automatically attaches to any other starship or vehicle using repulsorlifts that comes within close range of it. For every 1 mine attached to a vehicle that uses the [[Piloting - Planetary (Ag)|Piloting (Planetary)]] skill, that vehicle reduces its speed by 1. For every 2 mines attached to a starship that uses the [[Piloting - Space (Ag)|Piloting (Space)]] skill, that starship reduces its speed by 1. An attached mine can be removed with an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics check]]**.

**Models Include:** SoroSuub X80a UltraMass Gravity Pulse Generator, Taim & Bak Model 19 micro-gravity well mines.\
**Base Modifiers:** Allows the vehicle to deploy gravity mines anywhere one the ground within close range.\
**Modification Options:** 1 Increase deployable range to short Mod.
