When some hot heads once came with the idea to use actual coaxium fuel in its depleted form as a supplement to the Boost system, and managed to get spectacular results as much in performance as in pyrotechnics, a new fad was born. Not only did it fit perfectly to the high gambling, risk-taking ideals behind podracing, but it also offered tenfold more entertainment. Albeit expensive and volatile, it is possible to use depleted coaxium or coaxium refined byproducts to generate a multiplicating force on the Boost. This usually generates a lot more strain on the engines, and has to be experimented first since all podracers have unique propulsion systems.

## COAXIUM CELL INJECTOR

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 1,900 Rarity 3|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/9/9a/Profile_Coaxium_Cell_Injector.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 1,900 Rarity 3

**Models Include:** Vorsoon Carbide Power Injector, Lightning Tech C1 Cell Derivative Booster, RayTek Magnetic Booster.\
**Base Modifiers:** Encumbrance -1.

The user must first test the Attachment’s viability on the podracer it is installed into by making a [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics]] check during a test run (any mechanic can do that check, not just the pilot), where the Difficulty pool is composed of a number of [[Narrative Dice#Challenge (Red)|challenge]] dice equal to the Boost Rating of the podracer. Failure means that the test is inconclusive, but can be attempted again. Any [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result on top of its normal effects in-flight will generate an explosion explosion of an amount of vehicle scale damage equal to the Boost Rating (including the rating gained from the coaxium pellet).

During a race, may take a Maneuver and inject a coaxium pellet into the Boost and suffer an additional System Strain, doubling the Boost rating for the whole Boost duration. Any Collision occurring while the coaxium is active, will generate an explosion of an amount of Hull Trauma damage equal to the unmodified Boost Rating, that also deals half the amount rounded up to anything at Close range (sharing the same Pole).

Can hold 1 cell of ENC 1, 500 credits and rarity 6. Other cells can be carried inside the podracer’s holds and loaded by spending one Maneuver.\
**Modification Options:** None.
