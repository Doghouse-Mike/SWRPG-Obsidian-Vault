A magnetic weapon tether is a relatively simple modification that consists of a small magnetic device built into the weapon.

## MAGNETIC WEAPON TETHER

Source: [^1][^2]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 250 Rarity 2|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/5/5a/Profile_Magnetic_Weapon_Tether.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 250 Rarity 2

This attachment can be applied to any weapon wielded in one hand that could logically benefit from a weapon tether.

**Models Include:** Czerka "At Hand" Mag-Tether.\
**Base Modifiers:** During their turn, the wielder may recover the weapon as an incidental if they are engaged with it.\
**Modification Options:** 1 Innate Talent ([[Quick Draw talent|Quick Draw]]) Mod.
