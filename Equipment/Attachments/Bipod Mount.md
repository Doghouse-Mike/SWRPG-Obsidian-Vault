Little more than a pair of folding or collapsible legs mounted beneath the barrel of a long arm, bipods are used to stabilize awkward or heavy weapons to improve their firing characteristics.

> More information about the attachment available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bipod).

## BIPOD MOUNT

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance – Price 100 Rarity 1|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/0/07/Profile_Bipod_Mount.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance – Price 100 Rarity 1

This attachment can only be applied to rifles, carbines, and light repeating blaster, and it takes one preparation maneuver to set up.

**Models Include:** Galactic Arms XA Classic Bipod, Czerka AA20 Marksman Bipod.\
**Base Modifiers:** Decrease the weapon's [[Item Qualities#Cumbersome (Passive)|Cumbersome]] rating by 2 when firing from a prone or crouched position (or can otherwise brace the bipod on something).\
**Modification Options:** None.
