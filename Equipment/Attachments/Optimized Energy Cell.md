## OPTIMIZED ENERGY CELL

Source: [^1][^2]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 100 Rarity 5|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/4/4a/Profile_Optimized_Energy_Cell.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 100 Rarity 5

This attachment can applied to any energy weapon.

**Models Include:** Czerka Arms Model 4 Expanded Gas Reservoir, BlasTech X-3 Extended Energy Cell.\
**Base Modifiers:** When the GM would spend a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result or a number of [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results to cause the weapon to run out of ammunition, it requires an additional [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] to have that effect apply to this weapon.\
**Modification Options:** None.
