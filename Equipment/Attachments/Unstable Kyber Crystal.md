There are many opportunities between mining and use for a crystal to become unstable.

## UNSTABLE KYBER CRYSTAL

Source: [^0-1][^1-2]

![Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price (R) 16,000 Rarity 10|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/b/b2/Profile_Unstable_Kyber_Crystal.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price (R) 16,000 Rarity 10

**Base Modifiers:** Installing this crystal changes a lightsaber's base damage to 6, its critical rating to 2, and the lightsaber gains the [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] 1 and [[Item Qualities#Sunder (Passive)|Sunder]] item qualities. After installing this crystal, a character may spend a single maneuver communing with the crystal and attempting to harness its power. If they do, they increase their current Force rating by one. This increase persists until they use the extra [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]][^0-1] *or* [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force die]][^1-2] to fuel a Force talent or Force power. However, until the end of the encounter, the Game Master may spend four [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result generated by the character's checks to have the fault cause the lightsaber to sputter and fail. In this case, the lightsaber deactivates and may not be ignited for one hour. If the crystal is ever removed, the lightsaber loses these qualities and abilities and reverts to its previous base damage and critical rating. \
**Modification Options:** None.
