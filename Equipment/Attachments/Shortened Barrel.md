Shortened barrels are a handy modification used by spies, plainclothes law enforcement officers, and others who wish to conceal the fact that they're carrying a weapon.

> More information about the attachment available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shortened_barrel).

## SHORTENED BARREL

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 250 Rarity 4|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/f/ff/Profile_Shortened_Barrel.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 250 Rarity 4

This attachment can only be applied to Ranged (Light) pistols.

**Models Include:** Arakyd SB4 "Stubber," SoroSuub SBSS Reduced Length Barrel.\
**Base Modifiers:** Reduces the difficulty of checks made to conceal this weapon by one. Reduces range by one range band, to a minimum of short. If already short, add a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die to attack checks.\
**Modification Options:** 1 Innate Talent ([[Quick Draw talent|Quick Draw]]) Mod.
