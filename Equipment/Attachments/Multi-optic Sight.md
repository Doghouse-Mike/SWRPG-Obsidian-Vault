This attachment can be fitted to any ranged weapon.

**Models Include:** Galactic Arms Multi-Spectral Optical Array, Merr-Sonn 480.z1 "Beholder" Multi-Optic Sight, Atlas Corp MULTI Omni-Sight, Galactic Arms Model 44 Combat Optical Enhancer, SoroSuub XAD Omnisight.\
**Base Modifiers:** Removes up to two [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice on any checks to use this weapon due to smoke, darkness, or other environmental conditions that obstruct vision.\
**Modification Options:** 2 Skill ([[Perception (Cun)|Perception]]) Mods.
