This rigging essentially doubles the amount of injector fuel pumps attached to the engines, and uses a magnetized feed and a custom combustion chamber which improves engine output at the expense of durability as it tends to generate a noticeable amount of additional heat and consumes more fuel. Some variants just forego the magnetic field for even more injectors
