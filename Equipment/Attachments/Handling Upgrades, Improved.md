Using an extensive amount of additional and finer circuits linked to the transmission, this type of custom control coupling brings up a much higher granularity in any mechanical shift between components, making piloting much smoother as a result. The added mechanical intricacies tend to unfortunately get in the way of auto diagnosis and make repairs more cumbersome.

This is a homebrew adjustment of an existing [[Handling Upgrades|attachment]] for use with a community-produced homebrew sourcebook!
