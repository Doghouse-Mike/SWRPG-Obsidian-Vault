Produced by numerous starship manufacturers, these armor plates are made from a complex lattice of carbon nanotubes embeded with spun threads of durasteel.

## ENHANCED CARBON-DURASTEEL ARMOR

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

> [!quote] Image text

This modification may be applied to all starships and vehicles.

**Models Include:** Ferro-Magnesium Armor Plate, Cerro-Ablative Sheets.\
**Base Modifiers:** Adds one point to starship armor and and reduces starship handling by one.\
**Modification Options:** None.
