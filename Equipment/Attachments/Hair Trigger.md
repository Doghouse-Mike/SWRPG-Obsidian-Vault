Hair triggers can only be installed on Ranged (Light) pistols that lack the [[Item Qualities#Auto-fire (Active)|Auto-fire]] quality.

**Models Include:** Morellian MWC-HS Hyper Trigger, BlasTech Industries 009 Fastshot.\
**Base Modifiers:** Grants the pistol the [[Item Qualities#Linked (Active)|Linked]] 1 and [[Item Qualities#Inaccurate (Passive)|Inaccurate]] 2 item qualities.\
**Modification Options:** 1 Item Quality ([[Item Qualities#Inaccurate (Passive)|Inaccurate]] –1) Mod.
