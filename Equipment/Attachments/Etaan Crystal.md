## ETAAN CRYSTAL

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance — Price (R) 12,000 Rarity 9|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/8/82/Profile_Etaan_Crystal.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance — Price (R) 12,000 Rarity 9

**Models Include:** N/A.\
**Base Modifiers:** Installing this crystal changes a lightsaber's base damage to 6 and critical rating to 2, and the lightsaber gains the [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] 1 and [[Item Qualities#Sunder (Active)|Sunder]] weapon qualities. If the crystal is ever removed, the lightsaber loses these qualities and reverts to its previous base damage and critical rating. In addition, while the Etaan crystal is installed in the lightsaber, if the character wielding the lightsaber uses the [[Reflect talent#Improved|Improved Reflect talent]] to inflict an automatic hit, increase the damage dealt by that hit by 2.\
**Modification Options:** 1 Item Quality ([[Item Qualities#Vicious (Passive)|Vicious]] +1) Mod, 2 Damage +1 Mods, 1 Decrease the weapon's critical rating by 1 to a minimum of 1 Mod, 1 Innate Talent ([[Parry talent|Parry]]) Mod, 1 Innate Talent ([[Reflect talent|Reflect]]) Mod.
