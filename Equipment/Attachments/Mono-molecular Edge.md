## MONO-MOLECULAR EDGE

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

> [!quote] Image text

This modification may be applied to Melee weapons that use a cutting edge.

**Models Include:** None.\
**Base Modifiers:** Decreases the weapon's crit rating by 1 to a minimum of 1.\
**Modification Options:** 2 Item Quality ([[Item Qualities#Pierce (Passive)|Pierce]] +1) Mods.
