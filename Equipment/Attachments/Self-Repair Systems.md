> More information about the attachment available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Self-repair_system).

## SELF-REPAIR SYSTEMS

Source: [^1][^2]

![Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price 3,000 Rarity 6|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/3/3b/Profile_Self-Repair_Systems.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price 3,000 Rarity 6

This attachment can be applied to any armor with melee or ranged defense of 1 or higher.

**Models Include:** Industrial Automaton Model 3 Autorepair Suite, AccuTronics Advanced Repair Unit.\
**Base Modifiers:** Once per encounter, as a maneuver, the wearer can have the armor attempt to repair itself or provide assistance to a character on a check to repair it; it counts as having an Intellect of 2 and 2 ranks in the [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics]] skill for the check. In addition, there is no cost to repair the armor, whether utilizing only the automatic repair functionality or performing the repair personally.\
**Modification Options:** None.
