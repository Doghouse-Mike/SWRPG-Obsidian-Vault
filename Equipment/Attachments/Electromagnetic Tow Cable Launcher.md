Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 2,000 Rarity 2|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/1/11/Profile_Electromagnetic_Tow_Cable_Launcher.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 2,000 Rarity 2

**Models Include:** Prax Arms "Hangman" Harpoon Cannon, Merr-Sonn GX-3 Tow Cable Launcher.\
**Base Modifiers:** See table below. If the [[Item Qualities#Ensnare (Active)|Ensnare]] quality activates, a character on the target vehicle must make an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]]** **Piloting ([[Piloting - Planetary (Ag)|Planetary]]** or **[[Piloting - Space (Ag)|Space]],** depending on the vehicle**) check** to free the vehicle (instead of an Athletics check as normal for the Ensnare quality). If the target is at least 1 silhouette value lower than the attack and two [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results is generated on a failed check, the target is drawn 1 range band closer to the attacker.\
**Modification Options:** None.
