Mobile listening posts are a common modification to vehicles used by law enforcement and intelligence agencies.

## MOBILE LISTENING POST

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance — Price 5,500 Rarity 5|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/f/f4/Profile_Mobile_Listening_Post.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance — Price 5,500 Rarity 5

This attachment can be applied to any enclosed vehicle of silhouette 3 or larger.

**Models Include:** Various models.\
**Base Modifiers:** Increases a vehicle's passive and active sensor range by one range band. Provides extra audio, video, and holographic capabilities. \
**Modification Options:** None.
