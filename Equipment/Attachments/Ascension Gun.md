## ASCENSION GUN

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance 1 Price 500 Rarity 4|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/0/02/Profile_Ascension_Gun.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance 1 Price 500 Rarity 4

**Models Include:** SoroSuub S-A Grappling Launcher.\
**Base Modifiers:** The user can fire the ascension gun at an anchor point within medium range with an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Easy difficulty]] [[Ranged - Light (Ag)|Ranged (Light)]] or [[Coordination (Ag)|Coordination check]]**. At the GM's discretion, aiming for a precise placement or attaching a grapnel to certain surfaces might add one or more [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice to the check. Once the grapnel is in place, the wearer can activate the repulsor field to ascend one range band toward it every round.\
**Modification Options:** 1 Add a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to checks to fire the ascension gun Mod, 1 Increase ascension speed by one range band Mod.

[^1]: [[Category - Clone Wars Begin|Rise of the Separatists]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 61)
