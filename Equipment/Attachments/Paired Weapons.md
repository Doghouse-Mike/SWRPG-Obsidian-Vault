Warriors have a long history of employing two weapons at one time in a variety of combat engagements.

## PAIRED WEAPONS

Source: [^1][^2]

![Hard Points Required 1/per Encumbrance - Price 300/pair Rarity 3|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/5/52/Profile_Paired_Weapons.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1/per Encumbrance - Price 300/pair Rarity 3

This attachment can only be applied to one-handed weapons.

**Models Include:** Custom variants.\
**Base Modifiers:** Must be applied to two weapons at the same time. Reduces [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result required to hit with the secondary weapon when two-weapon fighting with this pair of weapons by 1.\
**Modification Options:** None.
