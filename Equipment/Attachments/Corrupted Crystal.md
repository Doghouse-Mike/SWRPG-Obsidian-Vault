\[Dark side\] Force users bend the kyber crystals in their lightsabers to their will, leading to the crimson hue of blades produced by these corrupted crystals.

## CORRUPTED CRYSTAL

Source: [^1][^0-2]

![Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price Not for Sale Rarity 9|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/b/bd/Profile_Corrupted_Crystal.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price Not for Sale Rarity 9

**Models Include:** None.\
**Base Modifiers:** Installing this crystal changes a lightsaber's base damage to 6, its critical rating to 2, and the lightsaber gains the [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] 1, [[Item Qualities#Sunder (Passive)|Sunder]], and [[Item Qualities#Vicious (Passive)|Vicious]] 2 item qualities. When a character wielding this lightsaber makes a Force power check, they add a [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|dark point]] to the check. When the Force-sensitive character's Morality rises to 70 or more, this crystal is reclaimed; it no longer provides a [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|dark point]] and it loses the [[Item Qualities#Vicious (Passive)|Vicious]] item quality, though it continues to function as above in all other respects. If the crystal is ever removed, the lightsaber loses these qualities and reverts to its previous base damage and critical rating.\
**Modification Options:** 2 Damage +1 Mods, 1 Decrease weapon's critical rating by 1 to a minimum of 1 Mod, 1 Item Quality ([[Item Qualities#Vicious (Passive)|Vicious]] +1) Mod.

### RECLAIMING A CORRUPTED CRYSTAL

Source: [^0-2]

Characters whose Morality reaches 70 meet the requirements to reclaim the crystal, but doing so should be treated as a major story moment. In addition, GMs may wish to require a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Discipline (Will)|Discipline check]]** with appropriate modifiers as a final challenge to fully purge the crystal in order to represent the crystal's final resistance to being claimed.
