## SECONDARY TRANSPONDER

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price (R) 20,000 Rarity 7|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/5/5c/Profile_Secondary_Transponder.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price (R) 20,000 Rarity 7

This attachment can be applied to any starship.

**Models Include:** Various illicit models.\
**Base Modifiers:** The ship's crew can switch over to the secondary transponder with an action and an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Computers (Int)|Computers check]]**. At the time of installation, the players and GM should determine the name and other details of the false identity. Because the ship's engine signature is part of the identifying information, the false identity must be of the same class of ship and preferably of a similar model. An observer who scans the ship can detect discrepancies indicating the presence of a secondary transponder with a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Daunting difficulty]] [[Computers (Int)|Computers check]]**.\
**Modification Options:** 1 Activate secondary transponder as a maneuver Mod, 3 Add one additional ship identity Mods.
