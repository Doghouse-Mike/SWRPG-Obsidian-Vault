> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 4 Encumbrance — Price (R) 29,000 Rarity 9

This attachment can be applied to any starship of silhouette 3 or larger.

**Models Include:** Sienar Fleet Systems Mk III Stygium Cloaking Device, Kerner Corp Multi-Phase Hibridum Stealth System.\
**Base Modifiers:** Provides a star ship with a cloaking device which can be activated or deactivated by spending a maneuver. A character must make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Daunting difficulty]] [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]]** or **[[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance check]]** to spot a cloaked ship.
