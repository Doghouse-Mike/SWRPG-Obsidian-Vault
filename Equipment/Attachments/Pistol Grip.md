A pistol grip replaces the stock or other grip on a long gun with one like that of a pistol, allowing it to be fired one-handed.

> More information about the attachment available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/H9_pistol_grip).

## PISTOL GRIP

Source: [^1][^2]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 400 Rarity 3|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/8/87/Profile_Pistol_Grip.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 400 Rarity 3

This attachment can be used with any Ranged (Heavy) blaster rifle, blaster carbine, or slugthrower that does not possess the [[Item Qualities#Cumbersome (Passive)|Cumbersome]] quality.

**Models Include:** N/A.\
**Base Modifiers:** The weapon's skill changes to [[Ranged - Light (Ag)|Ranged (Light)]], but use adds a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die to any combat check made while firing the weapon one-handed. The weapon's range is reduced to medium if longer.\
**Modification Options:** None.
