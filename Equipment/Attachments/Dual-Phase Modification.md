Considered antiquated by the time of the Clone Wars, the dual-phase modification is an artifact of an older, more violent time.

> More information about the attachment available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dual-phase_lightsaber).

## DUAL-PHASE MODIFICATION

Source: [^1][^2]

![Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price 4,500 Rarity 6|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/e/e3/Profile_Dual-Phase_Modification.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price 4,500 Rarity 6

This attachment can only be applied to lightsabers.[^3]

**Models Include:** None.\
**Base Modifiers:** The wielder may change the blade's length once per encounter as an incidental. The next attack the wielder makes that turn using this weapon ignores the target's melee defense.\
**Modification Options:** None.
