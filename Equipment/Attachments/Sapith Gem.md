Said to originate from a strange and mysterious world called Lwhekk in the Unknown Regions, sapith gems are similar to krayt dragon pearls in that they were formed when rocks containing a kyber crystal were consumed by a great beast.

> More information about the crystal available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sapith).

## SAPITH GEM

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price (R) 18,000 Rarity 10|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/e/ea/Profile_Sapith_Gem.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price (R) 18,000 Rarity 10

**Models Include:** None. \
**Base Modifiers:** Installing this crystal changes a lightsaber's damage to 7 and its critical rating to 2, and the lightsaber gains the [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] 1 and [[Item Qualities#Sunder (Active)|Sunder]] item qualities. If the crystal is ever removed, the lightsaber loses these qualities and reverts to its previous base damage and critical rating. \
**Modification Options:** 1 Item Quality ([[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] +1) Mod, 2 Damage +1 Mods, 1 Decrease the weapon's critical rating by 1 to a minimum of 1 Mod.
