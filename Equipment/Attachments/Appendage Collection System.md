Some freelance scavengers have taken to outfitting their ships with several droid brain controlled appendage arms. Using the ship's passive sensors, the droid brain is able to detect floating space debris that fits the criteria for mass or volume that the captain desires. The droid arms deliver the materials into apertures in the hull leading to the cargo bay or collection area.
