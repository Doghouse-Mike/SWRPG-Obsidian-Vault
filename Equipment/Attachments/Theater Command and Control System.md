\[A\] theater command and control system (TCCS) grants a commanding officer and staff incredible control over large-scale conflicts.

Fitted to ships or vehicles of silhouette 4 or larger\[.\]

**Base Modifiers:** While active, an active force commander on the bridge of the starship upgrades the ability of all Mass Combat checks \[they\] make twice.\
**Modification Options:** 2 Gain an additional [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result on Mass Combat checks Mods, 1 Remove a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die from Mass Combat checks Mod.
