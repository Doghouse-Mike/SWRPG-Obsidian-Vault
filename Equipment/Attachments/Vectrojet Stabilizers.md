In a similar fashion to retro-thrusters, vectrojets can be added at specific points on the podracer’s frame in order to produce a precisely regulated additional thrust in lateral directions with the aim to help the craft in negotiating turns. Pretty sensitive to impacts, and finicky to tune properly, a good part of the price also often comes from its software package.

> More information about the attachment available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Stabilizer).
