The pommel cap is a small, weighted knob or similar piece attached to the base of a lightsaber's hilt.

> More information about the attachment available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pommel_cap).

## POMMEL CAP

Source: [^1][^2]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 3,000 Rarity 2|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/b/bf/Profile_Pommel_Cap.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 3,000 Rarity 2

This attachment can only be applied to lightsabers or any Melee weapon with a hilt.

**Models Include:** Custom variants.\
**Base Modifiers:** When attached to a lightsaber hilt, this weapon gains the [[Item Qualities#Stun (Active)|Stun]] 2 item quality. In addition, a single small item with an encumbrance value of zero may be stored in the pommel cap, with Game Master approval.\
**Modification Options:** 2 Item Quality ([[Item Qualities#Stun (Active)|Stun]] +1) Mods.
