## GALVEN PATTERN RESEQUENCING

Source: [^1][^2]

![Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price 1,000 Rarity 5|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/9/97/Profile_Galven_Pattern_Resequencing.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price 1,000 Rarity 5

This attachment can only be used with blaster pistols and heavy blaster pistols.

**Models Include:** None.\
**Base Modifiers:** Increases weapon damage by 1. Adds two automatic [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results to all combat checks made using this weapon.\
**Modification Options:** 2 Damage +1 Mods, 2 Item Quality ([[Item Qualities#Pierce (Passive)|Pierce]] +1) Mods.
