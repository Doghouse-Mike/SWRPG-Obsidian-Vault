Turbochargers are one of the most effective technologies that dramatically increase thrust to dereasonable levels by collecting exhaust plasma and gases and compressing them before reinjecting them into the boost system. This comes with some unpleasantness on the controls by introducing a noticeable amount of lag between command input and responses. Turbochargers come in all shapes and forms in order to accomodate for varied engine designs
