> [!quote] Image text

**Models Include:** N/A.\
**Base Modifiers:** Installing this crystal changes a lightsaber's base damage to 8 and critical rating to 2, and the lightsaber gains the [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] 1, [[Item Qualities#Ion (Passive)|Ion]] and [[Item Qualities#Sunder (Active)|Sunder]] qualities. If they crystal is ever removed, the lightsaber loses these qualities and reverts to its previous base damage and critical rating.\
**Modification Options:** 4 Damage +1 Mods.
