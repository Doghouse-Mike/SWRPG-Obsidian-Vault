Low-friction coatings work by lowering the coefficient of friction against solid armor plates.

## LOW-FRICTION COATING

Source: [^1][^2]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 750 Rarity 5|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/d/d9/Profile_Low-Friction_Coating.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 750 Rarity 5

This attachment can only be applied to armor with hard surfaces, such as heavy battle armor, powered armor, and other suits the GM deems appropriate.

**Models Include:** Creshaldyne Industries SL-17 Armor Coating, Brennkeyes Syndicate Serpentskin.\
**Base Modifiers:** Increases the armor's melee defense by +1. Adds two [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice to [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics]] checks to modify or maintain the armor.\
**Modification Options:** 1 Skill ([[Coordination (Ag)|Coordination]] +1) Mod.
