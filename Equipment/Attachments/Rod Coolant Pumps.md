Specifically hot environments can be taxing on podracers. Some racers also feel like their cooling system doesn’t deliver quite as much as they’d have wished. Whatever the case, cooling pumps can usually be added with long superconductive rods leading excess heat directly outside, and one of the perks of such modifications is that they are generally free of any crippling drawback.
