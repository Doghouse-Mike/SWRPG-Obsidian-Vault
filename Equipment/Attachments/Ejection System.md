## EJECTION SYSTEM

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 400 Rarity 4|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/5/51/Profile_Ejection_System.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 400 Rarity 4

\[This\] mod may only be installed on Podracers.

**Models Include:** Incom ACE Ejection Seat, Koensayr Np-K36 Ejection Seat, Sienar Fleet Systems Mk GT8 Deluxe Ejection Pod.\
**Base Modifiers:** Equips a Podracer with an ejection system. To eject, a pilot makes an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Piloting - Planetary (Ag)|Piloting (Planetary) check]]** as an out of turn incidental in response to \[their\] Podracer being destroyed. On a successful check, the pilot is ejected from the crashing vehicle unharmed. On a failed check, the pilot suffers a number of strain equal to the vehicle's speed at the time of the crash, plus 1 Critical Injury. Game Masters can spend two [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result to deal wounds instead of strain.\
**Modification Options:** None.
