A time-consuming and labor-intensive process, reloading a bowcaster is made much easier by use of the automatic re-cocker.

## BOWCASTER AUTOMATIC RE-COCKER

Source: [^1][^2]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price See Entry Rarity 3|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/7/75/Profile_Bowcaster_Automatic_Re-cocker.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price See Entry Rarity 3

This attachment is only for use on [[Wookiee]] [[Bowcaster|bowcasters]] and cannot be purchased; it must be built either by the owner of the bowcaster with an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics check]]** or by the weaponsmith who built the weapon for them.

This item is not available for sale, but parts may be bought for 500 credits.

**Models Include:** None.\
**Base Modifiers:** Removes reloading maneuver requirement from bowcaster.\
**Modification Options:** 1 Item Quality ([[Item Qualities#Pierce (Passive)|Auto-fire]]) Mod.
