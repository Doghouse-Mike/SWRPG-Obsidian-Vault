Water reclamation systems are popular with scouts and hermits, especially those living or working on arid worlds.

## WATER RECLAMATION SYSTEM

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance – Price 500 Rarity 4|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/0/0a/Profile_Water_Reclamation_System.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance – Price 500 Rarity 4

This attachment can be applied to any armor that could logically integrate the necessary pouches and tubing.

**Models Include:** Survival Equipment Inc. Waste Distillation System, Gandorthral Atmospherics "Vaporator II" WRS.\
**Base Modifiers:** Upgrades the ability of any [[Resilience (Br)|Resilience]] and [[Survival (Cun)|Survival]] checks made in dry or hot environments due to scarcity of water twice.\
**Modification Options:** None.
