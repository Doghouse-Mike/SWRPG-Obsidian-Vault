A skilled tinkerer can tailor a weapon to perfectly fits its wielder's hand.

## CUSTOM GRIP

Source: [^1][^2]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 500 Rarity 6|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/8/8b/Profile_Custom_Grip.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 500 Rarity 6

This attachment can be applied to any weapon with a handle.

**Models Include:** None.\
**Base Modifiers:** Remove a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die from all combat checks the weapon's owner makes using this weapon. Anyone other than the owner instead adds two [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice to all combat checks made using it.\
**Modification Options:** 1 Item Quality ([[Item Qualities#Accurate (Passive)|Accurate]] +1) Mod.
