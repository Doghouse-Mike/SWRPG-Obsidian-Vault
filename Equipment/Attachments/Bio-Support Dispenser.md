## BIO-SUPPORT DISPENSER

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance — Price 4,000 Rarity 6|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/c/ca/Profile_Bio-Support_Dispenser.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance — Price 4,000 Rarity 6

This attachment may only be installed on a fully enclosed suit of armor, such as laminate, clone trooper, or heavy battle armor.

**Models Include:** Athakam Medtech Smartmed 1000 Integrated Systems, Arakyd Biosteady Smartmonitor.\
**Base Modifiers:** When the wearer heals strain at the end of an encounter, they also heal 1 wound if they suffered any wounds during the encounter. In addition, the first time in an encounter that the character's current wounds exceed half their wound threshold, the armor automatically applies a [[Stimpack|stimpack]] to the character.\
**Modification Options:** 1 Heal 1 additional wound at the end of an encounter Mod.
