The blade drain attachment works by creating a small power drain when the lightsaber is in contact with another lightsaber blade.

> More information about the attachment available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blade_drain).

## BLADE DRAIN

Source: [^1][^2]

![Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price (R) 4,000 Rarity 9|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/3/3a/Profile_Blade_Drain.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price (R) 4,000 Rarity 9

This attachment can only be applied to lightsabers.

**Models Include:** Custom variants.\
**Base Modifiers:** When making a combat check targeting an opponent with a lightsaber, the character may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result to reduce the opponent's lightsaber's base damage by 1 for the remainder of the encounter. If the combat check generates three [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results, the blade collapses as the power supply fails and may not be reignited until the character's next turn.\
**Modification Options:** 2 Item Quality ([[Item Qualities#Defensive (Passive)|Defensive]] +1) Mods.

[^2]: [[Category - Consular|Disciples of Harmony]]: *A Sourcebook for Consulars* (Page 50)
