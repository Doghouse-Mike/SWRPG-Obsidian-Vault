## INTEGRATED SCANNER

Source: [^1][^2]

![Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price 675 Rarity 5|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/a/a7/Profile_Integrated_Scanner.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price 675 Rarity 5

This attachment can be applied to any Ranged (Heavy) weapon.

**Models Include:** Carbanti United Electronics Mk. II Omni-Scope, CryonCorp "Heads-Up" Scanner Suite.\
**Base Modifiers:** The weapon incorporates a [[General Purpose Scanner|general purpose scanner]] that the wielder can use while the weapon is drawn. The wielder adds two automatic [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results to Initiative checks while the weapon is drawn.\
**Modification Options:** 2 Remove a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die from Initiative checks Mods.
