This missile launcher attachment was made by SFS to improve versatility of missile weapon systems. It carries up to six missiles and a rotating warhead selector serves as a magazine holding up to six shells of one or more shell types. SFS developed six different types of shells to show the Empire the potential of such a versatile weapon system. From somewhat weaker concussive shells, to accurate and overwhelming cluster shells, to jamming and decoy firing shells, an ion shell and an experimental shell firing a tracer, serving as a homing beacon.

The weapon system can carry a number of different types of missiles up to its [[Item Qualities#Limited Ammo (Passive)|limited ammo]] rating, however to switch between different missile types requires on maneuver as per the [[Item Qualities#Prepare (Passive)|Prepare]] 1 quality. Therefore most pilots prefer to limit the missile selection to one, two, or three different types, depending on mission requirements.

**Models Include:** N/A.\
**Base Modifiers:** Once installed, it becomes a weapon using the profile of the missile type prepared. In addition the weapon profile gains the following qualities: [[Item Qualities#Limited Ammo (Passive)|Limited ammo]] 6 and [[Item Qualities#Prepare (Passive)|Prepare]] 1. Changing between missiles types (and weapon profile) requires one maneuver represented by [[Item Qualities#Prepare (Passive)|Prepare]] 1).\
**Modification Options:** 2 increase [[Item Qualities#Limited Ammo (Passive)|limited ammo]] +1 mods.\
**Hard Points Required:** 0 if replacing an existing weapon system. 1 if adding a new weapon system.

### CUSTOMIZABLE MISSILES

The Sienar Fleet Systems Missile Launcher carries up to 6 different missile shells, allowing it to have six different types of missiles or some other combination. The pilot/gunner can ready each missile type into the firing mechanism as a maneuver (see Prepare 1). The SFS missile launcher can carry a wide range of missiles from smaller, but still quite powerful concussive shells to even smaller, but more accurate cluster shells. SFS has also developed an ion shell, decoy and jammer shells, and the rare tracer shell.

#### XX-23 S-THREAD TRACER

Source: [^2]

This projectile is one of the options that can be launched by the SFS Missile Launcher. On a successful hit the tracer attaches itself to the target and activates. The firing vessel, or allied vessel with the correct frequency, can now track the ship anywhere within a sector with an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Easy difficulty]] [[Computers (Int)|Computers check]]**, or while either ship is in hyperspace as an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Computers (Int)|Computers check]]**. Anyone else trying to detect, locate and track the ship must identify this frequency, which requires a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Computers (Int)|Computers check]]**.
