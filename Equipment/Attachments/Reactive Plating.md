This attachment can only be applied to armor with a soak of 2 or higher.

**Models Include:** Koromondian Reactive Plates, Pacnorval Defense Systems XII Fittings.\
**Base Modifiers:** Increase the armor's encumbrance by 4. The first time the wearer of this armor is hit by an attack each encounter, reduce the damage \[they\] suffer by 10; the armor suffers minor damage and loses this benefit until fully repaired.\
**Modification Options:** 3 Reduce the armor's encumbrance by 1 Mods.
