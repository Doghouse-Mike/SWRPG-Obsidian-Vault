## STANDBY POWER RESERVE

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 1 or 2 Encumbrance – Price 2,500 Rarity -|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/3/36/Profile_Standby_Power_Reserve.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 or 2 Encumbrance – Price 2,500 Rarity -

Only 1 HP is required for the base attachment and its first modification, the second HP is required for the final two modifications.

**Models Include:** Girodyne Standby Power Reservoir.\
**Base Modifiers:** Allows the ship to remain powered for up to 6 hours of standby time without the sublight engines running.\
**Modification Options:** 3 six additional hour Mods.

[^1]: [[Category - Ship Operating|Operational Costs]]: *A Guide for Ship Operators* (Page 11)
