## INSULATED FIRING MECHANISMS

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance — Price 1,000 Rarity 7|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/1/1b/Profile_Insulated_Firing_Mechanisms.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance — Price 1,000 Rarity 7

This attachment can be installed on any personal-scale blaster weapon.

**Models Include:** Czerka Parts Replacement Kit NX3-4r, BlasTech "My First Blaster" Safety Kit.\
**Base Modifiers:** Increase the [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] or [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] result cost of any effect that damages the weapon (such as the [[Item Qualities#Sunder (Active)|Sunder]] quality) by one [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] or [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] result (as applicable). The weapon gains the [[Item Qualities#Prepare (Passive)|Prepare]] 1 quality, or increases its existing [[Item Qualities#Prepare (Passive)|Prepare]] quality by 1. Repairs to the weapon when damaged cost half as much.\
**Modification Options:** 1 Reduce [[Item Qualities#Prepare (Passive)|Prepare]] quality by 1 Mod.
