Whistling Birds are a Mandalorian weapon designed as a defense against multiple adversaries. A user designates targets and activates the weapon all through a helmet HUD, allowing the miniature guided missiles to be fired discreetly. This weapon can only be produced by Mandalorian armor smiths and is generally integrated into a vambrace or gauntlet.
