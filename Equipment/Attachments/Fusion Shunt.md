## FUSION SHUNT

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance — Price 2,000 Rarity 6|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/3/37/Profile_Fusion_Shunt.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance — Price 2,000 Rarity 6

**Models Include:** None.\
**Base Modifiers:** Activating or deactivating the fusion shunt requires a maneuver. If the lightsaber is used as a weapon while the shunt is active, all combat checks suffer three [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results, but the weapon's [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] quality increases by 1. While the fusion shunt is active, the lightsaber can slice through obstacles with ease, destroying or cutting through barriers and other inanimate targets at twice the normal rate. Exact details are up to GM's discretion, but in general, hardened security doors and reinforced walls should withstand two or three rounds of cutting, with other obstacles being dealt with in a corresponding time frame.\
**Modification Options:** None.
