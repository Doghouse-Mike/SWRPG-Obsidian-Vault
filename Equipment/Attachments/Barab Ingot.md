Barab ingots are a rare kyber crystal variants from the heavily irradiated world of Barab I, home of the saurian Barabel people.

> More information about the crystal available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Barab_ingot).

## BARAB INGOT

Source: [^1][^2]

![Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price (R) 15,000 Rarity 8|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/b/b9/Profile_Barab_Ingot.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price (R) 15,000 Rarity 8

**Base Modifiers:** Installing this crystal changes a lightsaber's base damage to 8 and its critical rating to 3. In addition, the lightsaber gains the [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] 1, [[Item Qualities#Burn (Active)|Burn]] 1, and [[Item Qualities#Sunder (Passive)|Sunder]] item qualities. If the crystal is ever removed, the lightsaber loses these qualities and reverts to its previous base damage and critical rating.\
**Modification Options:** 2 Item Quality ([[Item Qualities#Burn (Active)|Burn]] +1) Mods, 2 Item Quality ([[Item Qualities#Vicious (Passive)|Vicious]] +1) Mods.
