Through a delicate system of motion sensors and miniaturized motors built into a lightsaber hilt, a craftsperson can alter the lightsaber to respond to adjustments in grip and position with greater responsiveness.

## REFLEX GRIP

Source: [^1][^2]

![Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price 4,000 Rarity 8|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/b/bb/Profile_Reflex_Grip.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price 4,000 Rarity 8

This attachment can only be applied to lightsabers.[^3]

**Models Include:** None; users must custom-make each lightsaber attachment.\
**Base Modifiers:** When a character uses a [[Parry talent|Parry]] or [[Reflect talent|Reflect]] incidental with a lightsaber that has a reflex grip installed, the character suffers 1 additional strain, but counts their ranks in [[Parry talent|Parry]] and [[Reflect talent|Reflect]] as 1 higher.\
**Modification Options:** 1 Item Quality ([[Item Qualities#Defensive (Passive)|Defensive]] +1) Mod, 1 Item Quality ([[Item Qualities#Deflection (Passive)|Deflection]] +1) Mod.
