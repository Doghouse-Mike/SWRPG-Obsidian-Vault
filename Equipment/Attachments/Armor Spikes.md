With the addition of spikes, blades, and other protrusions on areas covering shoulders, knuckles, feet, knees, or elbows, armor can be turned into a deadly weapon of last resort.

## ARMOR SPIKES

Source: [^0-1][^1-2]

![Hard Points Required 3 Encumbrance +2 Price 500 Rarity 2|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/d/d9/Profile_Armor_Spikes.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 3 Encumbrance +2 Price 500 Rarity 2

This attachment can be applied to any armor that covers the arms or legs.

**Models Include:** Creshaldyne Industries SO-9 Razor-Spines, LaserHone "Last Defense" Armor Hacks.\
**Base Modifiers:** Increases the armor's encumbrance by +1[^0-1] *or* +2[^1-2]. When the wearer suffers a hit from a Melee, Brawl, or Lightsaber check, after the attack is resolved, the character may spend three [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result to automatically inflict one hit for 6 damage on the attacker.\
**Modification Options:** 3 Increase the damage attackers suffer by +1 Mods, 1 Innate Talent ([[Intimidating talent|Intimidating]]) Mod.
