Armor inserts, also known as trauma plates, are thin, flexible, plasteel and ballistic gel plates that can be sewn into clothing to give the wearer concealed armor protection.

## ARMOR INSERT

Source: [^1][^2]

![Hard Points Required 0 Encumbrance - Price 450 Rarity 3|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/4/47/Profile_Armor_Insert.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 0 Encumbrance - Price 450 Rarity 3

This attachment can be applied to any clothing or armor with a defense no greater than 0 and a soak of 1 or less.

**Models Include:** Creshaldyne "Slimline" Armor Plates.\
**Base Modifiers:** Turns any article of clothing into [[Armored Clothing|armored clothing]]. Character can notice the inclusion of armor inserts in clothing by making an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Perception (Cun)|Perception check]]**.\
**Modification Options:** 1 Increase soak by +1 Mod, 1 Increase defense by +1 Mod, 1 Increase difficulty of check to notice inclusion to **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]]** Mod.
