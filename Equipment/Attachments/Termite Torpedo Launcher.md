## TERMITE TORPEDO LAUNCHER

Source: [^0-1]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price (R) 10,000 Rarity 8|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/7/72/Profile_Termite_Torpedo_Launcher.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price (R) 10,000 Rarity 8

Additional termite torpedoes cost 900 credits for a set of three.

**Models Include:** Arakyd Industries Dismantler Torpedo, Baktoid BT-30 Termite Droid Launcher.\
**Base Modifiers:** See table below. Additionally, after a ship is hit by a termite torpedo (whether or not it inflicts any hull trauma), for the remainder of the encounter, whenever the crew perform a check while in the ship, the attacker can spend two [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results to have the ship suffer 2 hull trauma or system strain, and spend a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result to inflict a Critical Hit on the ship. As an action, a character aboard the hit ship can perform a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Daunting difficulty]] [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics check]]** to repress the effects of termite droids for 1 round. At the GM's discretion other skill checks can be used instead.\
**Modification Options:** None.
