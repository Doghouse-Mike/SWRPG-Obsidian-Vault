The kyber crystals found deep within the caves of the icy planet Ilum were prized by the Jedi as some of the best crystals for constructing a lightsaber.

> More information about the crystal available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ilum_crystal).

The [Rubat](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rubat) crystals found on [Phemis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Phemis) function identically, as far as game mechanics are concerned.[^1]

**Models Include:** None.\
**Base Modifiers:** Installing this crystal changes a lightsaber's base damage to 6, critical rating to 2, and the lightsaber gains the [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] 1 and [[Item Qualities#Sunder (Passive)|Sunder]] weapon qualities. If the crystal is ever removed, the lightsaber loses these qualities and reverts to its previous base damage and critical rating. \
**Modification Options:** 4 Damage +1 Mods, 1 Decrease the weapon's critical rating by 1 to a minimum of 1 Mod, 2 Item Quality ([[Item Qualities#Vicious (Passive)|Vicious]] +1) Mods.
