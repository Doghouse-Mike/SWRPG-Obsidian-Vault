## AUGMENTED SPIN BARREL

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

![Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price 1,750 Rarity 4|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/c/c4/Profile_Augmented_Spin_Barrel.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price 1,750 Rarity 4

This attachment can only be used with blaster rifles and heavy blaster rifles.

**Models Include:** Dolmax 242X "Penetrator" Augmented Spin Barrel, Arakyd HH780 HotShot Barrel, BlasTech Model 213 Perforator Barrel, Dolmax 243X "Penetrator" Augmented Spin Barrel.\
**Base Modifiers:** Increase weapon damage by 1. Adds a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die to all [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics]] checks when performing maintenance on this weapon.\
**Modification Options:** 2 Damage +1 Mods, 1 Item Quality ([[Item Qualities#Accurate (Passive)|Accurate]] +1) Mod, 1 Item Quality ([[Item Qualities#Pierce (Passive)|Pierce]] +1) Mod.\
