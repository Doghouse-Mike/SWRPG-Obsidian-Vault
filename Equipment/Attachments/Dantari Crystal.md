As their name suggests, Dantari crystals are found on the Outer Rim world of Dantooine.

> More information about the crystal available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dantari_crystal).

## DANTARI CRYSTAL

Source: [^1][^2]

![Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price (R) 12,000 Rarity 9|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/3/38/Profile_Dantari_Crystal.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price (R) 12,000 Rarity 9

**Base Modifiers:** Installing this crystal changes a lightsaber's base damage to 7 and critical rating to 2, and the lightsaber gains the [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] 1 and [[Item Qualities#Sunder (Passive)|Sunder]] weapon qualities. When making a Force power check as part of a combat check, a character using a lightsaber with a Dantari crystal may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]] to recover 2 strain. If the crystal is ever removed, the lightsaber loses these qualities and abilities, and it reverts to its previous base damage and critical rating.\
**Modification Options:** 2 Damage +1 Mods, 1 Decrease the weapon's critical rating by 1 to a minimum of 1 Mod.
