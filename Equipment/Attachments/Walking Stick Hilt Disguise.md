## WALKING STICK HILT DISGUISE

Source: [^1][^2]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 2,500 Rarity 6|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/f/fa/Profile_Walking_Stick_Hilt_Disguise.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 2,500 Rarity 6

This attachment can only be applied to lightsabers.[^3]

**Models Include:** N/A.\
**Base Modifiers:** Unless ignited, this weapon is completely indistinguishable from a regular walking stick, and a character must succeed on a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Formidable difficulty]] [[Perception (Cun)|Perception check]]** or **[[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance check]]** to notice that it is not. The weapon also gains the [[Item Qualities#Inaccurate (Passive)|Inaccurate]] 1 item quality. If the weapon already has the [[Item Qualities#Inaccurate (Passive)|Inaccurate]] quality, its [[Item Qualities#Inaccurate (Passive)|Inaccurate]] quality increases by 1.\
**Modification Options:** 1 Decrease the value of the weapon's [[Item Qualities#Inaccurate (Passive)|Inaccurate]] quality by 1.
