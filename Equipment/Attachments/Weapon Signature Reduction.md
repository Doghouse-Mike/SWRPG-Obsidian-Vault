## WEAPON SIGNATURE REDUCTION

Source: [^1][^2]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 1,000 Rarity 6|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/8/8d/Profile_Weapon_Signature_Reduction.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 1,000 Rarity 6

Weapon signature reduction is only effective on weapons with an encumbrance rating of 3 or less.

**Models Include:** Proprietary models.\
**Base Modifiers:** Increase the difficulty of checks using scanners to search for this weapon by 2. Due to the masking used on various weapon components, this modification adds a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die to all [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics]] checks when performing maintenance on this weapon.\
**Modification Options:** None.
