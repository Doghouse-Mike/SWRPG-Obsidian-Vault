Mandalorians often upgrade their helmet with a general purpose optic suite. Those with a focus on bounty hunting may take these upgrades a step further and install programs that highlight footprints and trail markers, greatly increasing their tracking abilities. This grants all the bonuses of an enhanced optic suite in addition to the trail marker system.

## ADVANCED HUNTING SYSTEM

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 2,250 Rarity 4|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/7/77/Profile_Advanced_Hunting_System.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 2,250 Rarity 4

This can only be applied to armor with a helmet.

**Models Include:** N/A\
**Base Modifiers:** Remove up to two [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice imposed due to darkness, smoke, or other vision obscuring environmental effects. If the environment lends itself to tracking (i.e. Sand, snow, mud, dense foliage, thick branches) add two [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] dice to checks to pick up and follow a trail. In urban environments the software adds no boosts but makes a tracking check possible by scanning for bootprints or disturbed refuse.\
**Modification Options:** 1x "[[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]] Skill +1", 1x "[[Survival (Cun)|Survival]] skill +1".
