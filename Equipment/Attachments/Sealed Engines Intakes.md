Racetracks can prove to be relatively hazardous with random flying debris, thick dust or sand, rocks, or other projections coming either from the environment or other pilots. Those kind of projectiles, small or big, tend to exert a real strain on engines, lowering their lifespan significantly or outright causing damage, sometimes critical. While most podracers rely on atmospheric engines to function, some racetracks like Oovo IV are known to run in the cold vacuum of space. Machines need to be specifically prepared for such runs so that alternative sources of working fuels and combustives, where air has to be replaced by accelerated gas, plasma, or pure ion propulsion. This modification tends to be extensive but few pilots would dare skip it. Equipping a sealed vacuum suit also advised.
