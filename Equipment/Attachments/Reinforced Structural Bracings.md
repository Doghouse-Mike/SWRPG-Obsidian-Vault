Sometimes, one just feels like a little more protection wouldn’t go amiss. Or sometimes, one just feels like being especially bumpy to other fellow competitors and would just need the extra edge... With that in mind, most podracer engines can easily be reinforced with additional bracings, increasing their structural integrity at the expense of inertia. Extremely varied and sometimes creative techniques can be used, relying on strengthening the core structure of the craft.
