This mod reduces both the weight and bulk of ranged weapons to make them easier to handle and conceal.

## LIGHTWEIGHT FRAME

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 650 Rarity 3|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/6/6a/Profile_Lightweight_Frame.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 650 Rarity 3

This attachment can be applied to any weapon.

**Models Include:** BlasTech "Featherlite" blaster frame.\
**Base Modifiers:** Reduces a weapon's encumbrance by 1 to a minimum of 1 Mod.\
**Modification Options:** 1 Reduce a weapon's encumbrance by 1 to a minimum of 1 Mod.
