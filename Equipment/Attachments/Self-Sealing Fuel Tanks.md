One of the big hazards in races is fire, especially when the engines are running hot and feeding on fuel. It is possible to shut down the engines’ fuel intake, which racers do in such emergencies, but it also starves the engines and shuts down most of their power, leaving them flying on their own inertia. Some systems or specific riggings exist however to prevent that from happening, namely fuel tanks that self seal to prevent internal fires, and provide additional insulated and derivative feeds that can keep the engines running, albeit at a reduced throttle while repairs are being made. Most podracing pilots are often too cocky to consider it, and this makes this one of the little modifications that often go overlooked, but one that can also salvage a race, or just save a podracer from vaporizing into a big cloud of smoke and fire due to a fire that goes out of control.
