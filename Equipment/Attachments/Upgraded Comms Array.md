Upgraded comms systems are powerful subspace comms arrays that can be fitted to most ships in the galaxy.

**Models Include:** Chedak Communications Frequency Agility Subspace Transceiver (FAST). SFS Ranger Long-Range Transceiver, Fabritech 44Q/r Multi-Band Transceiver.\
