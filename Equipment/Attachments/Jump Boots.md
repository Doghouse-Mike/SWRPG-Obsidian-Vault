Jump boots are, essentially, a single-use jetpack built into a thick boots and allows a user to leap over tall obstacles and even fly for a very long time.

> More information about the attachment available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jump_boot).

## JUMP BOOTS

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance — Price 1,200 Rarity 6|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/a/a6/Profile_Jump_Boots.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance — Price 1,200 Rarity 6

This attachment can be applied to any armor with an encumbrance of 4 or less. It is good for only one use and cannot be used again until the rockets are refueled, which costs 100 credits and takes 30 minutes.

**Models Include:** Zim Systems LO-44.a micro-rockets.\
**Base Modifiers:** Reduces the difficulty of all [[Athletics (Br)|Athletics]] checks to jump up or over obstacles by 1.\
**Modification Options:** 1 Reduce difficulty of all [[Athletics (Br)|Athletics]] checks to jump up or over obstacles by 1 Mod, 1 Allow the wearer to fly for a total of one minute or 3 rounds Mod.

[^1]: [[Category - Spy|Cyphers and Masks]]: *A Sourcebook for Spies* (Page 58)
