This weapon attachment can be applied to any grenade or explosive device.

**Models Include:** Merr-Sonn Model 8 Smart Trigger, SoroSuub Mk III Proximity Detonator.[^0-4][^1-3]\
**Base Modifiers:** \[As an action, the user can set this setting.\][^0-4] The weapon detonates when a being or creature of silhouette 1 or larger comes within short or engaged range, as determined by the user when placed. At the GM's discretion, other sources of movement within range may cause the device to detonate.\
**Modification Options:** None.
