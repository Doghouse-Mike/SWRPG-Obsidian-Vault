Drifting has always been part of the maneuvers that pod pilots undertake in sharp turns in order to get faster in cornering. Entering a curve with excess speed and oversteering once negotiating the turn before using the thrust power of the engines to propel the craft out of harm is a delicate art of balance between too much and too little. It is possible to assist the pilot in so doing by fixating the exhausts of the engine into a variable, rotating nozzle. That nozzle is able to slightly change the orientation of the exhaust plume in certain directions, which can apply additional thrust force in order to push the craft inward of the curve, thus assisting the airbrakes and steering controls during drifts. It is not unheard of racers using this system to have literally drifted sideways during extreme hairpins at breakneck speeds. It is a dangerous maneuver however since it tilts the podracer away from the track enough for airbrakes to stop working properly.
