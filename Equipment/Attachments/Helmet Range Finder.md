Mandalorians tend to prefer antenna mounted range finders over hand held versions to keep their hands free. These devices provide enhanced magnification, targeting data, and can even track heat signatures through cover.

## HELMET RANGE FINDER

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 1,500 Rarity 4|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/0/0a/Profile_Helmet_Range_Finder.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 1,500 Rarity 4

These can only be installed on armor sets that include a helmet.

**Models Include:** N/A\
**Base Modifiers:** Grants 1 rank of Innate [[Sniper Shot talent|Sniper Shot]] talent: As a maneuver increases the maximum range of a ranged weapon up to one range band per rank. For each rank beyond the normal maximum of the weapon, upgrade the difficulty of the check by one (this is in addition to the increased difficulty of the shot due to longer range).\
**Modification Options:** 1x "Innate [[Sniper Shot talent|Sniper Shot]] +1", 1x "Heat signature recognition" (Designate a target you can see, as long as they remain within extreme range of the rangefinder, their location is relayed to the helmet HUD)
