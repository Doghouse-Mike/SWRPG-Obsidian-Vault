Built from scavenged blaster parts and the occasional piece from a repulsorlift generator, the bowcaster accelerator enhancement is the second most common modification to Wookiee bowcasters after the automatic re-cocker.

## BOWCASTER ACCELERATOR ENHANCEMENT

Source: [^1][^2]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price See Entry Rarity 4|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/0/05/Profile_Bowcaster_Accelerator_Enhancement.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price See Entry Rarity 4

This attachment is only for use on [[Wookiee]] [[Bowcaster|bowcasters]] and cannot be purchased; it must be built either by the owner of the bowcaster with an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics check]]** or by the weaponsmith who built the weapon for them.

This item is not available for sale, but parts may be bought for 250 credits.

**Models Include:** None.\
**Base Modifiers:** Increases weapon damage by 1.\
**Modification Options:** 2 Damage +1 Mods, 2 Item Quality ([[Item Qualities#Pierce (Passive)|Pierce]] +1) Mods.
