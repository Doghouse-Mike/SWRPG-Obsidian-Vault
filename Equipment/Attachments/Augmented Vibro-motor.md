The cutting edge of a vibro weapon's blade is made many times more dangerous than that of a standard blade by the power of the weapon's rapid vibro-motor, which turns the wielder's slightest motion into a powerful and potentially lethal stroke.

## AUGMENTED VIBRO-MOTOR

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance — Price 500 Rarity 4|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/e/e2/Profile_Augmented_Vibro-motor.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text

This attachment can be installed on any vibro weapon.

**Models Include:** Various types.\
**Base Modifiers:** Increases the weapon's base damage by 1. Grants the weapon the [[Item Qualities#Cumbersome (Passive)|Cumbersome]] 3 quality or increases its existing [[Item Qualities#Cumbersome (Passive)|Cumbersome]] rating by 1.\
**Modification Options:** 1 Damage +1 Mod, 1 Item Quality ([[Item Qualities#Vicious (Passive)|Vicious]] +1) Mod, 1 Decrease the weapon's [[Item Qualities#Cumbersome (Passive)|Cumbersome]] quality by 1 Mod.
