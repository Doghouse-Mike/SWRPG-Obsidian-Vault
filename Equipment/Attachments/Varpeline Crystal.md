Varpeline crystals are hard, super-dense minerals found on the Outer Rim world of Mearalis II.

> More information about the crystal available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Varpeline_crystal).

## VARPELINE CRYSTAL

Source: [^1][^2]

![Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price (R) 14,000 Rarity 9|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/3/3b/Profile_Varpeline_Crystal.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price (R) 14,000 Rarity 9

**Base Modifiers:** Installing a varpeline crystal changes a lightsaber's base damage to 8 and its critical rating to 3. In addition, the lightsaber gains the [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] 1, [[Item Qualities#Vicious (Passive)|Vicious]] 1, and [[Item Qualities#Sunder (Passive)|Sunder]] item qualities. When spending a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result to inflict a Critical Injury, the character may spend an additional [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result. If they do so, they may automatically inflict the Maimed Critical Injury on their target instead of the Critical Injury they would otherwise have inflicted.\
**Modification Options:** 1 Damage +1 Mod, 2 Item Quality ([[Item Qualities#Vicious (Passive)|Vicious]] +1) Mods.
