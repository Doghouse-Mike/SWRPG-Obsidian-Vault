While a lot of podracers have a summary control of the ratio between air and fuel on their machine, for those that don’t, their users generally just tune it properly before every race to adjust for the local environmental parameters. It is however possible to install more advanced regulators in order to adjust that ratio on the fly. Usually very handy for tracks that have a lot of altitude variations, this type of modification can give a savvy modder a guaranteed edge.
