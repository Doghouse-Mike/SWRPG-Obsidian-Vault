Though some salvagers prefer to hand pick the pieces they bring on board, others prefer a catch-all system where any piece of debris is drawn into their collector, and the value of the items is sorted out later. Gravitational scoops are a favored method by those who utilize the mass collection method. Grav-scoops operate on similar methods as grav plates installed in the decks of almost any standard starship in the galaxy. Being little more than large apertures lined on all sides with rows of narrow but strong grav plates, the gravitational scoops draw in space debris, and then fire in series to pass them through to a collection area. This method allows the user to make slow passes through fields of small debris making sizable collections on each pass.
