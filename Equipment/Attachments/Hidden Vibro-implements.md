Additional airbrakes are relatively commonly found as a popular customization added to podracers. As simple metal blades attached to the hull with basic to complex regulators and controls, they come relatively cheap, and any savvy mechanic or modder will know where to affix them so that they can produce additional braking, helping in producing enough drag to slow down the craft, or help in intense turns, but at the expense of speed performance.

## HIDDEN VIBRO-IMPLEMENTS

Source: [^1]

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price 2,000 Rarity 5

**Models Include:** Vibro-blades, vibro-saws, vibro serrated edges, plasma drills, plasma cutters.\
**Base Modifiers:** Whenever the user attempts a Collision, may instead use the weapon with the following characteristics: 10 Damage (personal scale), Crit 2, [[Item Qualities#Vicious (Passive)|Vicious]] 1, Close Range (same Pole Rating). Take an Action to align the podracer and activate the weapon by making a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Piloting - Planetary (Ag)|Piloting (Planetary) check]]**. Cannot be used in complement with the Sharpened Edges Attachment (they require an actual collision). Anyone trying to notice the weapon adds two [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice to any [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] or [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]] check. \
**Modification Options:** 3x Vicious 1 (+10 Crit).
