An in-helmet scanner provides the usefulness of a general purpose scanner in a heads-up display splashed across the inside of the user's helmet.

## IN-HELMET SCANNER

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 750 Rarity 4|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/c/c3/Profile_In-Helmet_Scanner.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 750 Rarity 4

In-helmet scanners can be added to any helmet.

**Models Include:** CryonCorp GoScan, CUE Optical Information System (Mobile), Chedak Mk. 7 HUD-INFO.\
**Base Modifiers:** Adds a [[General Purpose Scanner|general purpose scanner]] to the user's helmet. The scanner allows the user to remove a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die imposed on any checks due to darkness or environmental conditions such as smoke or fog. It also allows the user to detect heat sources and motion within medium range.\
**Modification Options:** 1 Install a hands-free [[Comlink|comlink]] within the user's helmet Mod.

[^1]: [[Category - Way of the Force|***Star Wars*** **Force and Destiny**]] Roleplaying Game: *Core Rulebook* (Page 201)
