## CEREMONIAL ADORNMENT

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 0 Encumbrance — Price 2,500 Rarity 4|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/e/e9/Profile_Ceremonial_Adornment.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 0 Encumbrance — Price 2,500 Rarity 4

This attachment may be installed on any armor, although the GM may alter its effects for particularly intimidating or heavy armor.

**Models Include:** None.\
**Base Modifiers:** Removes two [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice from Social skill checks due to wearing armor in socially inappropriate situations. Adds a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die to [[Coordination (Ag)|Coordination]] checks.\
**Modification Options:** 1 Remove [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] from [[Coordination (Ag)|Coordination]] checks Mod.
