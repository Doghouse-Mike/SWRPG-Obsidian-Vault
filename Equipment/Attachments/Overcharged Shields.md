Most shield arrays have the potential to generate a stronger field.

## OVERCHARGED SHIELDS

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 4,000 Rarity 4|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/0/0b/Profile_Overcharged_Shields.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 4,000 Rarity 4

**Models Include:** Kuat Drive Yards Shield Boosters, Outlaw Tech Ray Shield Enhancement.\
**Base Modifiers:** Once per encounter as an action, a character can attempt an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]]** **[[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics check]]** to overcharge the ship's shields, adding two points of defense to all defense zones for one round, plus one round for each [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result on the check. However, if the check generates two [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result, then the overloaded shields immediately burn out and cease functioning until repaired.\
**Modification Options:** None.

[^1]: [[Category - Engineer|Fully Operational]]: *A Sourcebook for Engineers* (Page 61)
