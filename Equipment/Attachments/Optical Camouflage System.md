Popular with both military scouts and assassins, optical camouflage systems are fragile but effective pieces of modern stealth technology.

> More information about the attachment available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Optical_camouflage_system).

## OPTICAL CAMOUFLAGE SYSTEM

Source: [^1][^2][^0-3][^1-4]

![Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price 5,500 Rarity 6|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/f/ff/Profile_Optical_Camouflage_System.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price 5,500 Rarity 6

This attachment can be equipped on any type of armor, but it is nearly useless on heavier armors (laminate and plastoid heavy armor)[^0-3][^1-4], which are too bulky and loud to properly mask.

**Models Include:** Atlas Corp "Smokescreen" Stealth System, Merr-Sonn MS90 Optical Stealth Suite, BioTech "Flicker" Displacement Field, Merr-Sonn "Ghost" Optical Camouflage System.\
**Base Modifiers:** Upgrades the ability of all [[Stealth (Ag)|Stealth]] checks twice while wearing this armor.\
**Modification Options:** Innate Talent ([[Master Of Shadows talent|Master of Shadows]]) Mod, 1 Skill ([[Stealth (Ag)|Stealth]] +1) Mod.
