Most dedicated warships, especially those in service with the Imperial Navy, have special compartments set aside for the briefing and debriefing of fighter, gunboat, and shuttle pilots, ground vehicle drivers, and ground combat forces.

## BRIEFING AUDITORIUM

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 2,500 Rarity 4|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/0/02/Profile_Briefing_Auditorium.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 2,500 Rarity 4

Ships of silhouette 5 or larger can be fitted with a briefing auditorium\[.\]

**Models Include:** None.\
**Base Modifiers:** Characters briefed in this auditorium prior to a mission remove a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die due to known mission conditions from skill checks for the duration of the mission.\
**Modification Options:** 2 Add an [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result to [[Leadership (Pr)|Leadership]] checks made by characters within the briefing auditorium Mods.
