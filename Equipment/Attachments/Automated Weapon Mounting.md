Used by elite special forces across the galaxy—and the occasional bounty hunter—an automated weapon mounting is a sophisticated pseudocybernetic that allows the user to fire a blaster or other handheld weapon while keeping both hands free, possibly for additional weapons..

## AUTOMATED WEAPON MOUNTING

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price (R) 3,000 Rarity 5|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/7/7e/Profile_Under-barrel_Flame_Projector.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price (R) 3,000 Rarity 5

A weapon must also be modified in order to be armor-mounted, requiring one hard point.

**Models Include:** Mitrinomon Transports "Quicker Draw" Hands-Free Trigger, Neuro-Saav Technologies Mk II Self-Defense Harness.\
**Base Modifiers:** Allows the character to mount a single [[Ranged - Light (Ag)|Ranged (Light)]], [[Ranged - Heavy (Ag)|Ranged (Heavy)]], or [[Melee (Br)|Melee]] weapon of encumbrance 3 or less (subject to the GM's discretion) on the character's armor. Once per encounter, the character can spend a maneuver to make a combat action with this weapon without using \[their\] hands or arms. The difficulty of combat checks made with the mounted weapon are upgraded once.\
**Modification Options:** 1 Add a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die to combat checks instead of increasing difficulty Mod, 2 Increase allowable weapon encumbrance by 1 Mods.
