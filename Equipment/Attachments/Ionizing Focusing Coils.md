## IONIZING FOCUSING COILS

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance — Price 800 Rarity 5|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/2/29/Profile_Ionizing_Focusing_Coils.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance — Price 800 Rarity 5

This attachment can be installed on any personal-scale blaster weapon.

**Models Include:** BlasTech "Sparker" Ionization Coils.\
**Base Modifiers:** Increase weapon damage by 2 versus droids. Opponents may spend two [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result on combat checks using the weapon to damage the weapon one step.\
**Modification Options:** 2 Damage +1 versus droids Mods, 1 Decrease weapon's critical rating versus droids by 1 to a minimum of 1 Mod, 1 Increase [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] result required to backfire by 1 Mod.

[^1]: [[Category - Clone Wars Begin|Rise of the Separatists]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 62-63)
