Most races and circuits forbid the use of weapons of any kind in their podracing events, some enforcing it with more zeal than others, while some lawless pirate events held in the seediest parts of the galaxy just do not care or actually encourage their use. In short, while not every event or racer will actually be fielding some mounted blaster weaponry on their craft, it is also not unheard of, even though most racers would consider it to be abhorrent. This kind of implement consists of a single or twin autoblaster usually mounted somewhere on the engines and used in a frontal arc, although some can also be designed to fire backward.

## HIDDEN AUTOCANNON

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price 3,500 Rarity 7 (R)|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/5/5e/Profile_Hidden_Autocannon.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price 3,500 Rarity 7 (R)

**Models Include:** Czerka Arms Model 7 Automatic Kinetic Cannon, Loronar Corporation Outback Mk II Autoblaster, BlasTech Industries SE-14 Mounted Repeater.\
**Base Modifiers:** Affixes an automatic weapon to the podracer with the following characteristics: 15 Damage (personal scale), Crit 3, Forward OR Aft Arc (choose one). It can open fire on any opponent by 1 Pole directly ahead (or behind if mounted Aft). Take an Action to input a target to the targeting computer and make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Gunnery (Ag)|Gunnery check]]**. Anyone trying to notice the weapon (only when hidden) adds two [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice to any [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] or [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]] check.\
**Modification Options:** For 1750 additional credits and System Strain Threshold -1, gain the [[Item Qualities#Linked (Active)|Linked]] 1 quality ; for 3500 additional credits, and System Strain Threshold -2, gain the [[Item Qualities#Auto-fire (Active)|Autofire]] quality ; for 2000 credits and 1 additional HP requirement, mount the weapon on a turret (fire arc: all, can open fire on opponents 1 Pole behind, 1 Pole ahead, or same Pole).
