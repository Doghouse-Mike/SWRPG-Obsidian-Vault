By its very definition, by allowing the machine to go further than its physical limits, the Boost in podracing causes a lot of heat and strain to the engines, lowering their life expectancy and potentially leading to catastrophic failures during races. Some consider cooling as a necessary bandaid, but a bandaid at best, and strive to design boosters that generate the least strain possible while retaining most of their performances. Obviously, the Boost still requires energy for activation and doesn’t run on magic and glitter alone, but the energy expenditures and heat generation once the system is activated and running are relatively negligible, and the efficiency cycle of the boosters is noticeably improved.
