## MICRO-ROCKET ARMOR MOUNTING

Source: [^1][^0-2][^3]

![Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance 3 Price (R) 800 Rarity 6|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/4/4c/Profile_Micro-Rocket_Armor_Mounting.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance 3 Price (R) 800 Rarity 6

This attachment can be installed on any armor.

**Models Include:** Corellian Arms Forearm Missiles, SoroSuub 88K Rocket Mount, custom variants, BlasTech Mag-Clip Micro-Rocket Mounting, Goseia HIC "Last Word" Launch/Targeter System, countless custom variants.\
**Base Modifiers:** Enables user to fire one loaded [[Category - Micro-Rocket|micro-rocket]] without needing to perform a maneuver to draw. Increases encumbrance by +3. As micro-rockets are [[Item Qualities#Limited Ammo (Passive)|Limited Ammo]] 1 weapons, the launcher has the [[Item Qualities#Limited Ammo (Passive)|Limited Ammo]] 1 quality and thus must be reloaded with a new micro-rocket to be fired again.\
**Modification Options:** 5 Mount one additional micro-rocket Mods, 1 Remove a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die from checks to fire micro-rockets from this attachment Mod.
