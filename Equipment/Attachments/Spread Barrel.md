Designed with the kind of close-quarters combat found in buildings and starship corridors in mind, a spread barrel replaces a blaster's stock barrel with one that is shorter, bulkier, and equipped with a built-in flash-hider.

> More information about the attachment available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Spread_barrel).

## SPREAD BARREL

Source: [^0-1][^1-2][^2-3]

![Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price 1,725 Rarity 4|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/f/f9/Profile_Spread_Barrel.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price 1,725 Rarity 4

This attachment can only be applied to blaster rifles and blaster carbines.

**Models Include:** BlasTech 8405 "Scattergun" Spread Barrel, SoroSuub 800 "Blunderbuss" Barrel, Arakyd Model 70 "Chain Lightning" Spread Barrel.\
**Base Modifiers:** Grants weapon the [[Narrative Dice#Blast (Active)|Blast]] (+4) item quality[^0-1] *or* grants weapon quality [[Narrative Dice#Blast (Active)|Blast]] (4)[^1-2][^2-3]. Reduces weapon's range by one range band, to a minimum of engaged.\
**Modification Options:** 2 Item Quality ([[Narrative Dice#Blast (Active)|Blast]] +1) Mods.
