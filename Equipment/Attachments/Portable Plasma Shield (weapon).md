Heavy infantry was fairly vulnerable due to the weight of their weapons and a need to stand out in the open to get the best use out of the spray. Using similar technology as found in energy bucklers or the armour bracer mounted plasma shield, blaster companies and aftermarket modification providers have designed emitters capable of being mounted on a weapon. Due to its distance from the user, it isn’t as effective as a held shield and its positioning prevents the user from aiming as effectively while maintaining the defensive matrix. While the base model is typically only a small torso-sized shield, it can be extended to protect legs and widen it a little.
