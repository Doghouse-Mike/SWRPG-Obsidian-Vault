## PORTABLE PLASMA SHIELD

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance — Price 2,500 Rarity 4|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/2/27/Profile_Portable_Plasma_Shield.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance — Price 2,500 Rarity 4

**Models Include:** Mili-Corp PS-8C Plasma Buckler, Krail 99 Guardian System.\
**Base Modifiers:** The small plasma-shield generator attached to the armor's forearm armor and can be activated or deactivated as an incidental. When the shield is active, the user cannot wield a two-handed weapon. The plasma shield cannot be used to attack but grants the [[Item Qualities#Defensive (Passive)|Defensive]] 1 item quality.\
**Modification Options:** 2 Item Quality ([[Item Qualities#Defensive (Passive)|Defensive]] +1) Mods, 3 Item Quality ([[Item Qualities#Deflection (Passive)|Deflection]] +1) Mods.

[^1]: [[Category - Clone Wars End|Collapse of the Republic]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 63)
