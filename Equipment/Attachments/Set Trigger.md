Commonly known as a "hair-trigger," this weapon retooling allows a blaster or slugthrower to fire with barely a touch of the trigger.

## SET TRIGGER

Source: [^0-1][^1-2]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 450 Rarity 4|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/f/ff/Profile_Set_Trigger.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 450 Rarity 4

This attachment can be applied to any Ranged (Light) or Ranged (Heavy) weapon.

**Models Include:** Custom variants.\
**Base Modifiers:** Adds an automatic [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] and [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] result to the first combat check made with this weapon each encounter.\
**Modification Options:** 1 Add an automatic [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]][^0-1] *or* [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]][^1-2] result to the first combat check made with this weapon each encounter Mod.
