## FABRITECH FOGGER SMOKE GENERATOR

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 2,750 Rarity 2|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/7/7e/Profile_Fabritech_Fogger_Smoke_Generator.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 2,750 Rarity 2

This may only be installed on ground vehicles, landspeeders, or airspeeders, and only works on planets with an atmosphere.

**Models Include:** N/A.\
**Base Modifiers:** The pilot may activate this device as an incidental, creating a smoke screen up to 10 meters in diameter, which trails behind the vessels. The smoke provides concealment from all adversaries in the vehicle's rear arc. The concealment adds three [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice as long as the device remains active. Deactivating requires an incidental; the smoke persists until the end of the pilot's following turn.\
**Modification Options:** None.
