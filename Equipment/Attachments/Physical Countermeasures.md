Like electronic countermeasures, physical countermeasures are designed to fool scanners and make a ship or vehicle harder to hit.

## PHYSICAL COUNTERMEASURES

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 2,200 Rarity 4|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/c/ca/Profile_Physical_Countermeasures.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 2,200 Rarity 4

While any ship equipped with physical countermeasures carries enough to get them through most encounters, engaging in a long, drawn-out fight can quickly deplete a launcher's store of countermeasures. Game Masters may spend two [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result on [[Gunnery (Ag)|Gunnery]] checks made using missiles, rockets, or torpedoes targeting the starships to cause a countermeasure launcher to run out of its load of flares, chaff, or anti-warhead missiles, effectively disabling it until the end of the encounter.

**Models Include:** Loronar Defense Industries A4M "Pepperbox" anti-warhead micro-missile pack, Oriolanis Defense Systems XM-130 chaff dispenser, Conner Ship Systems MJU-9G "Phoenix" Flare Dispenser.\
**Base Modifiers:** Adds a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die to all [[Gunnery (Ag)|Gunnery]] checks made using missiles, rockets, or torpedoes targeting the starship.\
**Modification Options:** 1 Add a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die to all [[Gunnery (Ag)|Gunnery]] checks made using missiles, rockets, or torpedoes targeting the starship Mod. 1 Increase difficulty by 1 when making checks triggered by the [[Item Qualities#Guided (Active)|Guided]] quality Mod.
