## SEEKER CRYSTAL

Source: [^1][^2]

![Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price (R) 16,000 Rarity 9|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/4/46/Profile_Seeker_Crystal.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price (R) 16,000 Rarity 9

**Models Include:** None.\
**Base Modifiers:** Installing this crystal changes a lightsaber's base damage to 7, its critical rating to 2, and the lightsaber gains the [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] 1 and [[Item Qualities#Sunder (Passive)|Sunder]] item qualities. Force-sensitive characters making a [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] or [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]] check may add two [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results to the check for purposes of detecting whether a hidden being is present within short range. If the crystal is ever removed, the lightsaber loses these qualities and reverts to its previous base damage and critical rating. \
**Modification Options:** 1 Damage +1 Mod, 1 Increase check range to medium Mod.
