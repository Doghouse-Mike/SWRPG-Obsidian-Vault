Designed for permanent installation, a containment field generator consists of two flat, cylindrical generators—one bolted to the floor, the other to the ceiling—that hold a single individual captive in a powerful stasis field.

> More information about the attachment available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Containment_field).

## CONTAINMENT FIELD

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price (R) 7,500 Rarity 9|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/3/3e/Profile_Containment_Field.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price (R) 7,500 Rarity 9

This attachment can be installed on any vessel of silhouette 4 or higher.

**Models Include:** Gordarl Weaponsmiths A1 Containment Field.\
**Base Modifiers:** A containment field can hold one silhouette 1 creature. An individual held within a containment field is completely unable to move, although \[they\] can still hear, see, and speak. Escape under these circumstances is incredibly difficult, and thus can only be attempted at the GM's discretion. \
As an action, a character operating the field can use it to inflict harm on the prisoner; the character imprisoned inside must make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Resilience (Br)|Resilience check]]** and suffers 1 damage per [[Narrative Dice#Failure|failure]] result on the check. The operator may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] result to inflict 1 strain on the prisoner.\
**Modification Options:** None.
