## HILT-MASKING KIT

Source: [^1][^2]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance * Price Variable Rarity *|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/0/08/Profile_Hilt-masking_Kit.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance * Price Variable Rarity *

This attachment can only be applied to lightsabers.[^3]

The cost, rarity, and encumbrance of a hilt-masking kit are the same as those of the item it is meant to look like.

**Models Include:** Custom variants.\
**Base Modifiers:** When attached to a lightsaber, this attachment adds two [[Narrative Dice#Failure|failure]] results to any attempts by other characters to find the lightsaber or to identify it as a lightsaber.\
**Modification Options:** 2 Increase the number of [[Narrative Dice#Failure|failure]] results added by 1 Mods.
