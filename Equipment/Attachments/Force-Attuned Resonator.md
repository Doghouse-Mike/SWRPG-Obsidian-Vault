This attachment can be applied to any Ranged (Light) or Ranged (Heavy) weapon that has a trigger.

**Models Include:** Terebin's Site-145 replica.\
**Base Modifiers:** A character must possess a Force rating of 1 or higher and knowledge of how the resonator mechanism works in order to operate this weapon.\
**Modification Options:** 1 Innate Talent ([[Prescient Shot talent|Prescient Shot]]) Mod.
