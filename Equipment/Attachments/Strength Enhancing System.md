## STRENGTH ENHANCING SYSTEM

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

![Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price 5,500 Rarity 4|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/3/34/Profile_Strength_Enhancing_System.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price 5,500 Rarity 4

This attachment can only be used with full-coverage armor with at least some hard plates, at the GM's discretion.

**Models Include:** Atlas Corp Myomer Strength Enhancing System, Industrial Automaton Xr5 Power Assist, BioTech "Brute" Strength Enhancer.\
**Base Modifiers:** Increases the wearer's [[Brawn]] by one point while wearing armor. This does not increase soak or wound threshold.\
**Modification Options:** 2 Skill ([[Athletics (Br)|Athletics]] +1) Mods, 2 Innate Talent ([[Brace talent|Brace]]) Mods.
