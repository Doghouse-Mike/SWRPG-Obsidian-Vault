Many assassins who use melee weapons choose to improve the lethality of their blades by adding a poison reservoir that releases a toxin through a channel in the blade at the push of a button in the hilt.

> More information about the attachment available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Poison_reservoir).

## POISON RESERVOIR

Source: [^0-1]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance – Price (R) 400 Rarity 5|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/d/d6/Profile_Poison_Reservoir_table.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance – Price (R) 400 Rarity 5

This attachment can only be used with Brawl and Melee weapons.

**Models Include:** Numerous variants.\
**Base Modifiers:** Allows the user to store in the weapon one dose of [[Synthetic Neurotoxin|neurotoxin]], [[Synthetic Anesthetic|anesthetic]], or [[Synthetic Neuroparalytic|neuroparalytic]] or any other [[Category - Poisons|poison]] the GM deems suitable.

As a maneuver, the weapon's wielder may douse the blade in the poison. Until the end of \[their\] next turn, whenever the wielder successfully attacks a target with the weapon (whether or not \[they\] deal damage), the target also suffers the effects of being exposed to the poison.\
**Modification Options:** 4 Increase the number of doses of a poison the reservoir can hold by 1 Mods.
