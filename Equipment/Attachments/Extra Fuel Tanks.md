## EXTRA FUEL TANKS

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 1 or 2 Encumbrance – Price 1,500 or 3,000 Rarity -|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/5/5d/Profile_Extra_Fuel_Tanks.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 or 2 Encumbrance – Price 1,500 or 3,000 Rarity -

This may be applied to all Freighters, but not to Starfighters. To fit the additional fuel tanks, the ship does not necessarily need to have free Hard Points. The crew can sacrifice 50 or 100 points of Encumbrance capacity. If the ship does have free hard points, they do not need to sacrifice cargo space.

**Models Include:** None.\
**Base Modifiers:** Increase the fuel cells of the vessel by 15 or 30.\
**Modification Options:** None.
