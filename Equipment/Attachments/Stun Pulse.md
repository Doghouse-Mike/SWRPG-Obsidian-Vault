## STUN PULSE

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance – Price 250 Rarity 4|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/e/e2/Profile_Stun_Pulse.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance – Price 250 Rarity 4

A stun pulse can be attached to any bludgeoning Melee or Brawl weapon.

**Models Include:** CoroTech EV45 Pulse Coil, SoroSuub "Enforcer" Stunner.\
**Base Modifiers:** The weapon gains the [[Item Qualities#Stun (Active)|Stun]] 2 quality.\
**Modification Options:** 3 Item Quality ([[Item Qualities#Stun (Active)|Stun]] +1) Mods.
