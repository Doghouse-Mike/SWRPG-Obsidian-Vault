A staple of spies and infiltrators, this simply mechanical apparatus deploys and retracts a weapon from a hidden spot aboard a ship or vehicle.

## RETRACTABLE WEAPON SYSTEM

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance — Price 1,000 Rarity 6|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/b/bb/Profile_Retractable_Weapon_System.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance — Price 1,000 Rarity 6

This attachment can be applied to any starship or vehicle.

**Models Include:** Various models.\
**Base Modifiers:** Provides a vehicle with a hidden compartment and a device for deploying a weapon. Deploying or retracting a weapon requires a maneuver. Discovering a retractable weapon system requires a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Perception (Cun)|Perception check]]** and a thorough search of the ship or vehicle in question. The GM has final say if a weapon is suitable for the retraction system based on the size of the ship or vehicle.\
**Modification Options:** None.

[^1]: [[Category - Spy|Cyphers and Masks]]: *A Sourcebook for Spies* (Page 63)
