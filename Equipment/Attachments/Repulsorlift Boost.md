## REPULSORLIFT BOOST

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 1,000/silhouette Rarity 4|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/d/db/Profile_Repulsorlift_Booster.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 1,000/silhouette Rarity 4

**Models Include:** Numerous custom variants.\
**Base Modifiers:** Once per encounter as an incidental, the pilot may have the vehicle suffer 2 system strain in order to double its maximum altitude (or gain a maximum altitude of 100 meters, whichever is lower) and increase its maximum speed by 1 for 1 round. This can be used only on vehicles of silhouette 4 or less.\
**Modification Options:** 2 Increase duration by 1 round Mods.
