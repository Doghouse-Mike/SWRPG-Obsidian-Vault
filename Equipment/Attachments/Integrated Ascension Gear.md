Incorporating a grapnel launcher, line, clips, and other climbing and rappelling equipment directly into a set of armor ensures that the user is always prepared to ascend or descend sheet surfaces in a hurry.

## INTEGRATED ASCENSION GEAR

Source: [^1][^2]

![Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price 350 Rarity 3|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/b/b9/Profile_Integrated_Ascension_Gear.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price 350 Rarity 3

This attachment can be applied to any armor.

**Models Include:** TaggeCo. Integrated Ascension Unit, VargeCorp ClimbAll Armor.\
**Base Modifiers:** As an action, the character may make an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Ranged - Light (Ag)|Ranged (Light) check]]** to secure the grappling hook to an object within medium range. Upon success, as an action, they may reel in the cord, pulling themselves to the object (or, if the object is unsecured and lighter than they are, pulling it to them). The character may use the grappling hook to pull another character aloft with them; if they do, they must make an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Easy difficulty]] [[Athletics (Br)|Athletics check]]** to avoid losing their grip on their partner.\
**Modification Options:** None.
