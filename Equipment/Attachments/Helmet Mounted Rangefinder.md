A fairly common piece of equipment for citizens often performing surveillance or scouting, the helmet mounted rangefinder packs all of the functionality of the more common hand-held electrobinoculars into a hands free device. When activated, the rangefinder allows its wearer to zoom in and view distant targets.

## HELMET MOUNTED RANGEFINDER

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 1,600 Rarity 4|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/c/cc/Profile_Helmet_Mounted_Rangefinder.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 1,600 Rarity 4

Data about objects viewed through the rangefinder is shown on a heads up display installed into the helmet as part of the modification. This requires a round of observation, or a Maneuver in structured gameplay. The information received will be basic, generally the make and model of the item or vehicle being viewed, although more detailed information may be available at the discretion of the GM. Standard modes in most models include night vision and environmental condition filtering. Using the rangefinder allows the character to see normally in poor lighting and to mitigate other vision impairing conditions. The rangefinder also contains a small holorecorder which may record for up to 30 minutes and may be played back through any available standard holo-playback device.

Upgraded and customized versions include additional features such as a further view range, additional data processing and identification of items targeted by the sight, and assisted targeting for long-range sniper shots.

This attachment can be applied to any armor that includes a helmet.

**Models Include:** Galactic Electronics V22 "SneakPeek", MandalTech Predview.\
**Base Modifiers:** Removes up to one [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die caused by darkness, smoke, or other conditions that obstruct vision on any Perception or Vigilance checks.\
**Modification Options:** 3 additional [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die reduction, 1 automatic [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result on successful Perception checks, 1 add a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die on any [[Ranged - Heavy (Ag)|Ranged (Heavy)]] attacks made at Long and Extreme range when the character performs an Aim maneuver.
