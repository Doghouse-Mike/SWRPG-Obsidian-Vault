**Models Include:** Pretormin Environmental Mk II Thermal Auger, Ghelrar Consortium "Worldsplitter" Plasma Drill.\
**Base Modifiers:** The vehicle can tunnel through solid rock and most metals at a speed of 1, but its handling is permanently reduced by 2 due to the substantial additional weight and bulk. The vehicle's operator can make a [[Piloting - Planetary (Ag)|Piloting (Planetary)]] check to employ the plasma drill as a weapon with the following profile: Fire Arc Forward; Damage 9; Critical 4; Range (Close); [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] 3, [[Item Qualities#Inaccurate (Passive)|Inaccurate]] 2, [[Item Qualities#Slow-Firing (Passive)|Slow-Firing]] 1, [[Item Qualities#Vicious (Passive)|Vicious]] 1.\
