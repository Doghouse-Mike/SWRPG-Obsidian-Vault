**Models Include:** N/A.\
**Base Modifiers:** Firing the beam is a ranged attack using the [[Gunnery (Ag)|Gunnery]] skill, and may target one ship at close range. If the attack hits, the attacking pilot must use a maneuver to maintain a lock and continue to follow the craft. Enemy craft caught in the tractor beam add two [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice to all [[Piloting - Planetary (Ag)|Piloting]] [[Piloting - Space (Ag)|checks]] and attacks, and suffer 1 system strain when performing the Accelerate/Decelerate, Fly/Drive, or Punch It maneuvers.\
**Modification Options:** N/A.\
**Hard Points Required:** 0 if replacing an existing weapon system, 1 if adding a new weapon system.
