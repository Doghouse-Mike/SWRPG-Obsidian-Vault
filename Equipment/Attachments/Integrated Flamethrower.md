Infamously used by professional and ruthless racer Sebulba in the pre Clone Wars era on his Plug-F Mammoth, any flamethrower, like most weapons, is usually highly illegal on racetracks of all sorts. And like all weapons, this doesn’t prevent some to still hide them as much as they can, and wait for the most inconspicuous moments during races in order to use them incognito. The flamethrower is less likely to utterly destroy the structure of an opponent’s podracer, but is highly effective at lighting an engine fire... which can lead to the same result, with the added benefit of making it look like the result of a catastrophic overheat.

**Models Include:** BlasTech Industries BT X-42 Heavy Flame Projector, Czerka Arms CZ-Heavy Mounted Flame Projector, Heavy Duty ZX Flame Projector, Merr-Sonn Munitions XR14 Flame Projector.\
**Base Modifiers:** Affixes a retractable flamethrower to the podracer with the following characteristics: 10 Damage (personal scale), Crit 2, Close Range (same Pole Rating). Take an Action to align the podracer and activate the weapon by making a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Piloting - Planetary (Ag)|Piloting (Planetary) check]]**. Anyone trying to notice the weapon adds two [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice to any [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] or [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]] check. Inflict 1 additional System Strain to vehicles per [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result spent.\
**Modification Options:** None.
