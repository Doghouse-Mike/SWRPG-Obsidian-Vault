The ability to jam repulsors is a major tactical advantage in a galaxy that heavily relies on repulsorlift technology for most vehicles. The First Order has implemented respulsor jamming to great effect, and has also developed alternative options for when it is inevitably applied against them.

## REPULSOR JAMMERS

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price (R) 50,000 Rarity 9|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/4/41/Profile_Repulsor_Jammers.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price (R) 50,000 Rarity 9

May only be used on ships or vehicles with a silhouette of 5 or more. First Order ships of silhouette 7 or greater come standard with this equipment.

**Models Include:** N/A.\
**Base Modifiers:** As a maneuver, a crewmember may activate the jammer. All repulsors on ships and vehicles at least 1 silhouette smaller than the jamming vehicle within short range cease functioning. Speeders and other vehicles entirely dependant on repulsors are immobilized, and vehicles partially dependant on repulsors have their handling reduced by 3. Most starships are unable to take off or land in this state.\
**Modification Options:** 1 Increase range to Medium mod
