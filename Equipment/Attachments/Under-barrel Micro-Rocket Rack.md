## UNDER-BARREL MICRO-ROCKET RACK

Source: [^1][^2]

![Hard Points Required 3 Encumbrance 3 Price (R) 1,200 Rarity 6|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/b/bc/Profile_Under-barrel_Micro-Rocket_Rack.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 3 Encumbrance 3 Price (R) 1,200 Rarity 6

This attachment can be installed on any ranged weapon.

**Models Include:** BlasTech "Finisher" Rocket Rack, TaggeCo Under-Barrel Rocket Battery.\
**Base Modifiers:** Enables weapon to fire one loaded [[Category - Micro-Rocket|micro-rocket]]. Adds the [[Item Qualities#Cumbersome (Passive)|Cumbersome]] (+1) quality to weapon and increases encumbrance by 3. As micro-rockets are [[Item Qualities#Limited Ammo (Passive)|Limited Ammo]] 1 weapons, the launcher has the [[Item Qualities#Limited Ammo (Passive)|Limited Ammo]] 1 quality, and thus must be reloaded with a new micro-rocket to be fired again.\
**Modification Options:** 2 Mount one additional micro-rocket Mods, 2 Remove a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die from checks to fire micro-rockets from this attachment Mods.
