## HOLONET PIRATE ARRAY

> Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance – Price (R) 25,000 Rarity 8

**Models Include:** N/A.\
**Base Modifiers:** A ship equipped with a HoloNet pirate array gains unique communications and monitoring capabilities. Transmissions sent by a ship so equipped can reach any facility that has access to interstellar communications. Such transmissions arrive without delay, and cannot be intercepted unless the pirate array is being tracked by another HoloNet user, who must already be aware of the pirate array. A HoloNet user, whether equipped with a pirate array or a legitimate transceiver, can intercept transmissions with a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Computers (Int)|Computers check]]**, provided \[they\] are aware of the sender or recipient. At the GM's discretion, \[they\] may upgrade the difficulty of the check to reflect particularly impressive security protocols. A [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result on a check to intercept a transmission can be spent by the GM to reveal the attempted interception and its source.\
**Modification Options:** 2 add a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die to enemy checks to intercept outgoing transmission Mods, 2 add a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to intercept HoloNet traffic Mods.
