**Models Include:** BlasTech "Personal Armory" Gear.\
**Base Modifiers:** Increases encumbrance threshold by 4.[^0-1] Increases encumbrance by 4.[^1-2] The character may holster up to two weapons of encumbrance 3 or lower in the armor. While holstered, these weapons do not count toward the character's encumbrance value.\
**Modification Options:** 2 Add a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die to other characters' [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] checks to find weapons stored in the armor Mods, 3 Holster up to one additional weapon of encumbrance 3 or lower in the armor Mods, 1 Innate Talent ([[Quick Draw talent|Quick Draw]]) Mod.
