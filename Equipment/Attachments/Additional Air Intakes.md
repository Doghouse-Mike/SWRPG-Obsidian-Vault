Much as the name already tells, this modification consists in adding multiple air entries to the engines in order to increase air flow and cooling significantly. This comes at the cost of maneuverability by adding unproductive competition to external flaps and air flow, however. But the gain in cheap, efficient cooling, is unparalleled.
