Installed in starships, especially freighters and transports, for the transportation of contraband, smuggling compartments are used to hide goods (and occasionally, people) from the prying eyes of planetary customs agents and Imperial law enforcement.

> More information about the attachment available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Smuggling_compartment).

## SMUGGLING COMPARTMENTS

Source: [^1][^2]

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance – Price (R) 1,200 Rarity 1

**Models Include:** None,\
**Base Modifiers:** Stores items of up to 25 encumbrance per compartment. This still counts as part of the total encumbrance threshold of a ship. Increases difficulty of checks made to find compartments by +2.\
**Modification Options:** 2 Additional storage (25 encumbrance) Mods.
