## SIENAR T9 STINGER ASSAULT BOARDING TUBE

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 3 Encumbrance - Price (R) 1,000 x silh. Rarity 4|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/7/73/Profile_Sienar_T9_Stinger_Assault_Boarding_Tube.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 3 Encumbrance - Price (R) 1,000 x silh. Rarity 4

This mechanism may be installed on a craft of silhouette 3 or larger.

**Models Include:** N/A.\
**Base Modifiers:** Ships equipped with this attachment may attempt to cut a hole in a docked craft's hull or airlock. The cutting mechanism typically includes high-power plasma torches or similar mechanisms. A silhouette 1-sized hole is typically cut within 5 rounds of activation. Military or armored craft may take two to three times as long to cut.\
**Modification Options:** 4 Decrease time taken to cut through hull by 1 round Mods.
