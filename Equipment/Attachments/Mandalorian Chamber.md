A relatively recent innovation commonly known as the Mandalorian chamber \[...\] enables the blaster to simultaneously apply characteristics of both the deadly and stun settings.

## MANDALORIAN CHAMBER

This attachment can only be installed on blasters.

**Models Include:** None.\
**Base Modifiers:** Grants the weapon the [[Item Qualities#Stun (Active)|Stun]] 3 item quality and reduces its range by one range band.\
**Modification Options:** 3 Item Quality ([[Item Qualities#Disorient (Active)|Disorient]] +1) Mods, 2 Item Quality ([[Item Qualities#Stun (Active)|Stun]]) +1 Mods, 1 Increase Range by One Range Band Mod.
