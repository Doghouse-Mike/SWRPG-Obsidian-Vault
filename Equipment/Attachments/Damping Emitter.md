## DAMPING EMITTER

Source: [^1][^2]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 2,000 Rarity 7|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/a/af/Profile_Damping_Emitter.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 2,000 Rarity 7

This attachment can only be applied to lightsabers.[^3]

**Models Include:** None; users must custom-make each lightsaber attachment.\
**Base Modifiers:** As a maneuver, the wielder of the lightsaber may adjust the device to deal [[Item Qualities#Stun damage (Passive)|Stun damage]]. While the lightsaber is set to deal [[Item Qualities#Stun damage (Passive)|Stun damage]], it decreases the damage it deals by two, increases its critical rating by one, and loses the [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] quality. Adjusting the lightsaber to normal settings requires another maneuver.\
**Modification Options:** 2 Item Quality ([[Item Qualities#Stun damage (Passive)|Stun Damage]] +1) Mods.
