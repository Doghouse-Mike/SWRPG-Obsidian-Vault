## TAINTED NIGHTSISTER CRYSTAL

Source: [^1]

**Models Include:** N/A.\
**Base Modifiers:** Installing this crystal changes a lightsaber's base damage to 6 and critical rating to 2, and the lightsaber gains the [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] 1, [[Item Qualities#Sunder (Active)|Sunder]], [[Item Qualities#Disorient (Active)|Disorient]] 3, and [[Item Qualities#Vicious (Passive)|Vicious]] 4 weapon qualities. If the crystal is ever removed, the lightsaber loses these qualities and abilities, and reverts to its previous base damage and critical rating.\
**Modification Options:** 1 Damage +1 Mod, 1 Item Quality ([[Item Qualities#Disorient (Active)|Disorient]] +1) Mod.
