## ROCKET GUIDANCE SYSTEM

Source: [^1][^2]

This attachment can be installed on any [[Category - Micro-Rocket|micro-rocket]].

**Models Include:** Pharr Co. 'Vornskr" Guidance Package, Merr-Sonn "Outreach" Target Sensor.\
**Base Modifiers:** Grants the weapon the [[Item Qualities#Guided (Active)|Guided]] (+2) quality.\
**Modification Options:** 3 Item Quality ([[Item Qualities#Guided (Active)|Guided]] +1) Mods, 2 Item Quality ([[Item Qualities#Accurate (Passive)|Accurate]] +1) Mods.
