A common modification to holdout blasters, along with removing their serial numbers, is filing down the weapon's front sight.

## FILED FRONT SIGHT

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 25 Rarity 0|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/7/70/Profile_Filed_Front_Sight.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 25 Rarity 0

This modification may only be applied to pistol-sized weapons. Doing the work oneself requires an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulties]] [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics check]]**. The listed price is used when paying someone else to perform the work.

**Models Include:** None.\
**Base Modifiers:** Innate Talent ([[Quick Draw talent|Quick Draw]]). Increases difficulty of combat checks to hit targets at ranges beyond short range by 1.\
**Modification Options:** 1 Decreases the difficulty of checks to conceal the weapon by 1 Mod.
