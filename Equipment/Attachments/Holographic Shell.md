Holographic shells use a combination of optical camouflage and high-resolution holography to create a disguised exterior for a ship or vehicle.

> More information about the attachment is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Holographic_shell).

## HOLOGRAPHIC SHELL

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance — Price (R) 5,500 Rarity 7|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/0/07/Profile_Holographic_Shell.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance — Price (R) 5,500 Rarity 7

This attachment can be applied to any starship or vehicle.

**Models Include:** Various models.\
**Base Modifiers:** Provides a ship or vehicle with a holographic disguise. A **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Daunting difficulty]] [[Perception (Cun)|Perception check]]** is required to see through the disguise at a distance. Physical examination immediately detects the disguise. \
**Modification Options:** 2 Add one additional disguise to the database Mods.
