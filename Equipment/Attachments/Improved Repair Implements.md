The repair systems installed on podracers tend to be crude, automated diagnostics with emergency mechanical procedures that the pilot has to carefully control through the system’s interface. Naturally, some of those systems can perform to very varied degrees, from low grade pumps to sophisticated automated systems accompanying the pilot in their repairs. Some hi-tech systems come with semi intelli gent terminals applying viscous plasteel repair paste to the damaged parts they identify. Obviously, no foolproof repair system exists on the market, but for a very cautious racer, that type of modification is a mother lode of tech options
