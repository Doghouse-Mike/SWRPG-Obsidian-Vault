## DROID TARGETING SYSTEM

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 3 Encumbrance - Price 3,200 Rarity 5|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/6/66/Profile_Droid_Targeting_System.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 3 Encumbrance - Price 3,200 Rarity 5

This attachment can be applied to any personal ranged weapon.

**Models Include:** Baktoid Industries T-80 Droid Targeter, GoCorp "Surefire" Enhancement System.\
**Base Modifiers:** Upgrade the ability on Ranged combat checks made with this weapon once.\
**Modification Options:** 1 Add a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to one Ranged combat check per encounter Mod, 1 Suffer 3 strain to remove a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result from Ranged combat check Mod.
