## THONTIIN CRYSTAL

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance — Price 9,000 Rarity 9|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/4/4d/Profile_Ilum_Crystal_CotR.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance — Price 9,000 Rarity 9

**Models Include:** None.\
**Base Modifiers:** Installing this crystal changes lightsaber's base damage to 6 and critical rating to 2, and the lightsaber gains the [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] 1 and [[Item Qualities#Sunder (Active)|Sunder]] item qualities. If the crystal is ever removed, the lightsaber loses these qualities and reverts to its previous base damage and critical rating. In addition, once per encounter, the wielder may ignore the effect of a single Critical Injury of [[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|**Easy** **difficult**]] Severity (the character still suffers the Critical Injury).\
**Modification Options:** 3 Damage +1 Mods, 1 Decrease weapon's critical rating by 1 Mod.

[^1]: [[Category - Clone Wars Begin|Rise of the Separatists]]: *An Era Sourcebook* (Page 63-64)
