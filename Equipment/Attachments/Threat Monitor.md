A threat monitor is a suite of motion detectors, electroreceptors, and photoreceptors that constantly monitors the armor wearer's vicinity and, through directional video, auditory, or electrical cues, warns the wearer of imminent threats and incoming attacks.

> More information about the attachment available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Threat_monitor).
