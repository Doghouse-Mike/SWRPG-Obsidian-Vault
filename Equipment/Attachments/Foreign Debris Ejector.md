## FOREIGN DEBRIS EJECTOR

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 900 Rarity 3|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/1/15/Profile_Foreign_Debris_Ejector.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 900 Rarity 3

\[This\] mod may only be installed on Podracers.

**Models Include:** Various (pilots and their crew typically craft them secretly).\
**Base Modifiers:** Equips the Podracer with an aft-mounted compartment filled with hazardous debris. The compartment can be activated as a maneuver to eject the debris into the air. If there is a Podracer within close range and directly behind the vehicle, that Podracer's pilot must make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Piloting - Planetary (Ag)|Piloting (Planetary) check]]** to avoid the ejected debris. Failure reduces the target Podracer's speed by 1 for 2 rounds, plus 1 additional round for each [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] result. Three [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result can be spent to inflict a Critical Hit on the targeted vehicle. Once used, this modification cannot be used again until the next race.\
**Modification Options:** None.
