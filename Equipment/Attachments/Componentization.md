This attachment allows a weapon to be broken down into a number of innocuous-looking pieces so that it can be smuggled into and out of places where weapons are not allowed or are highly regulated.

It takes two maneuvers to assemble or disassemble a weapon with this mod. \[...\] This attachment can be applied to any weapon with an encumbrance of 2 or less.

**Models Include:** Various models.\
**Base Modifiers:** Allows a weapon to be broken into pieces and disguised as other items. A character must make a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Formidable difficulty]] [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics]]** or **[[Skulduggery (Cun)|Skulduggery check]]** to deduce the items are weapon parts.\
**Modification Options:** None.
