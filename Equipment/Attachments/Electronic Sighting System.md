By adding a small electronic device to the top of a blaster or other ranged weapon, a small holographic crosshair is projected a meter out from the weapon's barrel.

> More information about the attachment available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Electronic_sighting_system).

## ELECTRONIC SIGHTING SYSTEM

Source: [^1][^2]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 500 Rarity 4|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/c/c4/Profile_Blaster_Actuating_Module.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 500 Rarity 4

This attachment can be used on any ranged weapon that could logically benefit from the addition of a sight.

**Models Include:** SoroSuub True-Site System.\
**Base Modifiers:** Allows the shooter to aim as an incidental once per round at short range. Decrease the difficulty of [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] checks to locate the shooter in low light conditions by 1.\
**Modification Options:** 1 Item Quality ([[Item Qualities#Accurate (Passive)|Accurate]] +1) Mod.
