By removing or deactivating weapon safeties and other features, a gun can more easily be drawn and readied for combat.

## REMOVED SAFETY FEATURES

Source: [^1][^2]

![Hard Points Required 0 Encumbrance - Price (R) 250 Rarity 2|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/9/95/Profile_Removed_Safety_Features.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 0 Encumbrance - Price (R) 250 Rarity 2

This attachment can be applied to any [[Ranged - Light (Ag)|Ranged (Light)]] or [[Ranged - Heavy (Ag)|Ranged (Heavy)]] weapon that could logically have such elements to remove.

**Models Include:** None.\
**Base Modifiers:** Adds a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to the first combat check made with the weapon during an encounter. The Game Master may spend two [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] result from any combat check to have the weapon misfire, running out of ammunition and inflicting 4 strain on the wielder.\
**Modification Options:** None.

[^2]: [[Category - Technician|Special Modifications]]: *A Sourcebook for Technicians* (Page 55)
