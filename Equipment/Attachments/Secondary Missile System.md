Arms manufacturers are constantly inventing new weapon systems to allow for heavy, portable firepower.

## SECONDARY MISSILE SYSTEM

Source: [^1][^2]

![Hard Points Required 3 Encumbrance 3 Price 3,000 Rarity 6|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/d/db/Profile_Secondary_Missile_System.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 3 Encumbrance 3 Price 3,000 Rarity 6

This attachment can be used with any rifle that uses the [[Ranged - Heavy (Ag)|Ranged (Heavy)]] skill.

**Models Include:** N/A.\
**Base Modifiers:** Enables the weapon to fire rockets or missiles. This uses the missile's profile (including Range) and requires the [[Gunnery (Ag)|Gunnery]] skill to use. Adds [[Item Qualities#Cumbersome (Passive)|Cumbersome]] (+2) quality to the weapon and increases encumbrance by +3. Missiles have [[Item Qualities#Limited Ammo (Passive)|Limited Ammo]] 1, so the launcher must be reloaded.\
**Modification Options:** None.
