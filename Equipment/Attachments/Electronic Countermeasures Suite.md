Designed to blind and deafen enemy ships, an electronic countermeasures suite transmits a dense stream of white noise and junk data that fogs sensors and jams comms traffic within close range of the broadcasting ship.

## ELECTRONIC COUNTERMEASURES SUITE

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance – Price 3,000 Rarity 6|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/b/be/Profile_Electronic_Countermeasures_Suite.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance – Price 3,000 Rarity 6

This may be applied to all starships and vehicles.

**Models Include:** Fabritech 995 Stormcloud ECM Suite, Miradyne 4x-Phantom Short-Range Sensor Jammer (starfighters only), KDY 220-SIG Tactical Combat Jammer (capital ships only).\
**Base Modifiers:** Blocks all sensors within range, counting the ship or vehicle's silhouette as one step smaller when being fired at. Immediately notifies all ships in a star system, or within 100 kilometers if planetside, of the ship or vehicle's existence.\
**Modification Options:** 1 Increase difficulty by 1 when making subsequent attacks with the [[Item Qualities#Guided (Active)|Guided]] quality Mod.
