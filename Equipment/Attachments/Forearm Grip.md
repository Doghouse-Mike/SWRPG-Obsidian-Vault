Little more than a vertical handgrip affixed below the barrel of a rifle-sized weapon, the forearm grip is a very popular attachment among mercenaries and professional soldiers across the galaxy.

> More information about the attachment available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Forearm_grip).

## FOREARM GRIP

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 250 Rarity 1|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/7/71/Profile_Forearm_Grip.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 250 Rarity 1

This attachment can only be used on rifle and carbine-sized weapons (but not heavy rifles).

**Models Include:** Dolmax 14A Detachable Pistol Grip, Galactic Arms Model 9 Forearm Grip, Merr-Sonn AG44S Ergonomic Tactical Foregrip.\
**Base Modifiers:** Decreases the additional difficulty of making [[Ranged - Heavy (Ag)|Ranged (Heavy)]] checks with this weapon while engaged to an additional [[Narrative Dice#Difficulty (Purple)|difficulty]] die (rather than two [[Narrative Dice#Difficulty (Purple)|difficulty]] dice).\
**Modification Options:** 1 Innate Talent ([[Point Blank talent|Point Blank]] rank +1) Mod, 1 Item Quality ([[Item Qualities#Accurate (Passive)|Accurate]] +1) Mod.
