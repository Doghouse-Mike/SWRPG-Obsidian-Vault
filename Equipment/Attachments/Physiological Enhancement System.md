## PHYSIOLOGICAL ENHANCEMENT SYSTEM

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance — Price 3,000 (clones)/10,000 (other) Rarity 7|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/4/42/Profile_Physiological_Enhancement_System.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance — Price 3,000 (clones)/10,000 (other) Rarity 7

\[T\]he first cost listed is for clones, while the second, higher price is for a custom support system. This attachment may only be installed on fully enclosed suits of armor, such as laminate, clone trooper, or heavy battle armor.

**Models Include:** Creshaldyne Industries LV-2 "Bio-Strong" Support Suite.\
**Base Modifiers:** Whenever the character suffers strain involuntarily, reduce the amount of strain suffered by 1, to a minimum of 1. The wearer adds a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to all [[Athletics (Br)|Athletics]], [[Coordination (Ag)|Coordination]], and [[Resilience (Br)|Resilience]] checks.\
**Modification Options:** 1 Skill ([[Athletics (Br)|Athletics]]) Mod, 1 Skill ([[Coordination (Ag)|Coordination]]) Mod, 1 Skill ([[Resilience (Br)|Resilience]]) Mod.
