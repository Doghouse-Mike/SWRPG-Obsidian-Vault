## REPULSOR-ASSISTED LIFTING

Source: [^1][^2]

> [!quote] Image text

This attachment can only be used with laminate armor and other full-body hard armor suits.

**Models Include:** Guldak Heavy Industries Personal Repulsor Fitting.\
**Base Modifiers:** Adds +5 to a wearer's [[Brawn]] characteristic for the purposes of determining encumbrance threshold.\
**Modification Options:** 2 Add +1 to a wearer's Brawn characteristic for the purposes of determining encumbrance threshold Mods, 2 Skill ([[Athletics (Br)|Athletics]] +1) Mods.
