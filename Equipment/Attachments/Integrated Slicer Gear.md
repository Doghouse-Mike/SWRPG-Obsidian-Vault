Slicers often conduct their work under strict time constrains and less-than-ideal conditions.

## INTEGRATED SLICER GEAR

Source: [^1][^2]

![Hard Points Required 3 Encumbrance - Price 750 Rarity 6|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/8/8e/Profile_Integrated_Slicer_Gear.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 3 Encumbrance - Price 750 Rarity 6

This attachment can be applied to any armor.

**Models Include:** MerenData Mobile XR-1, numerous custom variants.\
**Base Modifiers:** The wearer counts as having [[Slicer Gear]], which can be controlled simply by gesturing or vocalizing. Adds a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die to all checks made by others to correctly identify that the character is slicing.\
**Modification Options:** 1 Innate Talent ([[Technical Aptitude talent|Technical Aptitude]]) Mod.
