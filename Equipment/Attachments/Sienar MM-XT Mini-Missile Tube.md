The mini-missile tube is a single-shot compact missile launcher designed for use on smaller vehicles, such as speeder bikes, landspeeders and smaller airspeeders. It may carry a variety of missile types, which are typically about a half-meter or less in length. The tube faces a single firing arc, though a turret mount is also available.

> More information about this attachment available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/MM-XT_Mini-Missile_Tube).

It may be installed on any non-starship vehicle of silhouette size 2 or greater.

**Models Include:** N/A.\
**Base Modifiers:** Once this is installed, it becomes a weapon that can fire a single [[Concussion Missile|concussion missile]] or warhead from the table below. The weapon always has the [[Item Qualities#Limited Ammo (Passive)|Limited Ammo]] 1 quality.\
**Modification Options:** 1 Change the Fire Arc to "All" Mod.\
**Hard Points Required:** 0 if replacing an existing weapon system, 1 if adding a new weapon system.

**Decoy:** This decoy missile, once launched, broadcasts an electromagnetic signature that draws off enemy missiles. To fire a decoy, make an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Easy difficulty]] [[Computers (Int)|Computers check]]**. If successful, any attacks against the ship firing the decoy using weapons with the [[Item Qualities#Guided (Active)|Guided]] quality upgrade their difficulty by one (plus an additional upgrade for every additional two [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results) while the decoy is active (one round per [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] result). If a weapon with the [[Item Qualities#Guided (Active)|Guided]] quality misses the target ship but generates three [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results or a [[Narrative Dice#Triumph|triumph]] result, they hit the decoy instead; ending the effect.

**Jammer:** This countermeasure follows but does not attempt to impact the target vehicle. Instead, it fires a [[Sienar Grappler 213 Tactical Tractor Beam|jamming beam]] at the vehicle. If the initial combat check is successful, the missile follows the target at close range for one round per [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] result. The missile adds two [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice to the target's **[[Gunnery (Ag)|Gunnery]], [[Piloting - Planetary (Ag)|Pilo]][[Piloting - Space (Ag)|ting]], and [[Computers (Int)|Computers]] check** (when the pilot is scanning with sensors). The missile's limited fuel means it self-destructs after 3 rounds. The missile can be shot down, but it has a silhouette of 1. Any damage destroys it.

**Mini-Missile and Mini-Rocket:** A scaled-down missile for use in the mini-missile tube and banks mounted to vehicles of silhouette 2 or 3.

**Unguided Rocket:** A cheap, simple rocket with minimal guidance and stabilization systems. They are installed more often on vehicles than starships. Attackers add a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die to their **[[Gunnery (Ag)|Gunnery skill checks]]** when using unguided rockets.
