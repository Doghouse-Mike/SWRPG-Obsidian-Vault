Additional airbrakes are relatively commonly found as a popular customization added to podracers. As simple metal blades attached to the hull with basic to complex regulators and controls, they come relatively cheap, and any savvy mechanic or modder will know where to affix them so that they can produce additional braking, helping in producing enough drag to slow down the craft, or help in intense turns, but at the expense of speed performance.

> More information about the attachment available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Air_brake).
