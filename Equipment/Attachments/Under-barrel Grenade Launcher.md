Under-barrel grenade launchers are designed to give single soldiers or squads heavy anti-infantry or light anti-vehicle capabilities in a small package.

This attachment can only be applied to rifle-sized Ranged (Heavy) weapons.

**Models Include:** Czerka "Thumper" Under-Barrel Grenade Launcher, SoroSuub XA203 Auxiliary Grenade Launcher, Zone Control "Viper" Under-Barrel Grenade Launcher.\
**Base Modifiers:** Enables weapon to fire grenades. This uses the grenade's profile, but it uses the [[Ranged - Heavy (Ag)|Ranged (Heavy)]] skill to fire and has a range of medium. Adds [[Item Qualities#Cumbersome (Passive)|Cumbersome]] (+1) to weapon and increases encumbrance by 2. Grenades are [[Item Qualities#Limited Ammo (Passive)|Limited Ammo]] 1 weapons, and thus the launcher is [[Item Qualities#Limited Ammo (Passive)|Limited Ammo]] 1.\
**Modification Options:** 5 Item Quality ([[Item Qualities#Limited Ammo (Passive)|Limited Ammo]] +1) Mods.
