Kimber stones are a type of kyber crystal produced by violent volcanic activity on the moon of Y'ness in the N'zoth system.

> More information about the crystal available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kimber_stone).

## KIMBER STONE

Source: [^1][^2]

![Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price (R) 6,500 Rarity 8|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/f/f6/Profile_Kimber_Stone.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price (R) 6,500 Rarity 8

**Base Modifiers:** Installing a kimber stone changes a lightsaber's base damage to 9 and its critical rating to –. The lightsaber gains the [[Item Qualities#Stun damage(Passive)|Stun Damage]] quality. If the crystal is ever removed, the lightsaber loses these qualities and reverts to its previous base damage and critical rating.\
**Modification Options:** 2 Damage +1 Mods, 1 Item Quality ([[Item Qualities#Concussive (Active)|Concussive]] +1) Mod, 2 Item Quality ([[Item Qualities#Disorient (Active)|Disorient]] +1 ) Mods.
