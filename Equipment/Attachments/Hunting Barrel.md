Designed to increase range and accuracy, a hunting barrel can turn any blaster rifle into a dedicated hunting weapon.

## HUNTING BARREL

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 3 Encumbrance +1 Price (R) 1,200 Rarity 4|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/1/18/Profile_Hunting_Barrel.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 3 Encumbrance +1 Price (R) 1,200 Rarity 4

A hunting barrel can only be used on a blaster rifle.

**Models Include:** Czerka Model 2 "Huntsman" Precision Barrel, SoroSuub WQ60 "Sure-shot" Barrel, Arakyd 3100 "Deadeye" Barrel.\
**Base Modifiers:** Increases weapon's range by one range band. Adds [[Narrative Dice#Cumbersome (Passive)|Cumbersome]] (+2) quality to the weapon, and increases encumbrance by +1.\
**Modification Options:** 2 Item Quality ([[Item Qualities#Accurate (Passive)|Accurate]] +1) Mods, 1 Innate Talent ([[Lethal Blows talent|Lethal Blows]]) Mod.

[^1]: [[Category - Seeker|Savage Spirits]]: *A Sourcebook for Seekers* (Page 50)
