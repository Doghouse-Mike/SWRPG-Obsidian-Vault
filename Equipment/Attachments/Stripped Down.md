By removing redundant components and stripping down the body, it is possible to significantly reduce the weight and profile of a blaster or other weapon.

## STRIPPED DOWN

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 3 Encumbrance -1 Price 250 Rarity 4|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/0/0f/Profile_Stripped_Down.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 3 Encumbrance -1 Price 250 Rarity 4

This attachment can be used with any [[Ranged - Light (Ag)|Ranged (Light)]] or [[Ranged - Heavy (Ag)|Ranged (Heavy)]] weapon.

**Models Include:** None.\
**Base Modifiers:** Decrease the weapon's encumbrance by 1 (to a minimum of 1). Add a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die to a character's [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] checks to find the weapon when it is hidden.\
**Modification Options:** 2 Decrease encumbrance by 1 (to a minimum of 1) Mods.

[^1]: [[Category - Technician|Special Modifications]]: *A Sourcebook for Technicians* (Page 56)
