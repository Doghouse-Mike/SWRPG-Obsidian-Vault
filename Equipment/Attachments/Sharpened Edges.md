Another one of the wide panel of less than legal, deadly instruments that can often find their way into podracing, sharpening the edges of one’s own engines is often overlooked by most officials considering how murky it can be to actually prove that some parts of a podracer are purposely used as weapon implements. Indeed, there is very little standing between reinforcing the edges of an engine with titanium alloys, and sharpening said edges into spikes and other dangerous protrusions that can wreck havoc on unarmoured hulls full of delicate and volatile components. It is however advised to field a podracer able to take such hits, since this type of modification comes with the idea that collisions are going to happen in the first place.
