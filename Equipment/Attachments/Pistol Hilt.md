## PISTOL HILT

Source: [^1][^2]

![Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price 750 Rarity 5|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/3/3b/Profile_Pistol_Hilt.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price 750 Rarity 5

This attachment can only be applied to lightsabers.[^3]

The user must spend a maneuver to switch from one weapon mode to the other (from lightsaber function to pistol function, for example).

**Models Include:** None; users must custom-make each lightsaber attachment.\
**Base Modifiers:** When the lightsaber is not ignited, the wielder may fire the pistol using the following profile: ([[Ranged - Light (Ag)|Ranged (Light)]]; Damage 6; Critical 3; Range \[Short\]; [[Item Qualities#Stun damage (Passive)|Stun Damage]]).\
**Modification Options:** None.
