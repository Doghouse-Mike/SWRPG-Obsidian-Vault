## ALL-TERRAIN LEGS

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price 3,000 x Silhouette Rarity 5|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/0/0c/Profile_All-Terrain_Legs.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price 3,000 x Silhouette Rarity 5

This attachment can be applied to any airspeeder or starship that can land on a planet.

**Models Include:** Kuat Drive Yards KR-2 "Crab" Auxiliary Locomotion Unit, Alto Industries Variable Combat Kit.\
**Base Modifiers:** As a maneuver, the pilot may shift the vehicle into or out of walker mode. While in walker mode, the vehicle operates as a walker (and thus uses the [[Piloting - Planetary (Ag)|Piloting (Planetary)]] skill if it did not already), and the vehicle's maximum speed is reduced to 2 if it was higher. Walker mode can only be employed in appropriate terrain, as determined by the GM.\
**Modification Options:** 2 Remove a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die from [[Piloting - Planetary (Ag)|Piloting (Planetary)]] checks due to difficult terrain Mods, 2 Remove a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die from [[Gunnery (Ag)|Gunnery]] checks due to cover or terrain Mods.
