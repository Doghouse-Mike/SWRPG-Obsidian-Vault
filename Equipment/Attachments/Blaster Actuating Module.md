Actuating modules are blaster modifications popular with mercenaries, bounty hunters, gunslingers, and anyone who needs to make the first strike count.

> More information about the attachment available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Actuating_module).

## BLASTER ACTUATING MODULE

Source: [^1][^2][^3]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 500 Rarity 4|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/c/c4/Profile_Blaster_Actuating_Module.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 500 Rarity 4

This attachment is only for use on Ranged (Light) blaster pistols.

**Models Include:** Dolmax Heavy Blaster Actuator, Arakyd 97R Enhanced Blaster Actuating Module, SoroSuub X440 "Hammer" Blaster Actuating Module.\
**Base Modifiers:** Increases weapon damage by 1. Adds a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die to all checks made when using this weapon.\
**Modification Options:** 2 Damage +1 Mods, 2 Item Quality ([[Item Qualities#Pierce (Passive)|Pierce]] +1) Mods.\
