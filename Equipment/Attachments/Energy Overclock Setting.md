> More information about the attachment available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Energy_overclock_setting).

## ENERGY OVERCLOCK SETTING

Source: [^1][^2]

![Hard Points Required 3 Encumbrance - Price 500 Rarity 5|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/f/fb/Profile_Energy_Overclock_Setting.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 3 Encumbrance - Price 500 Rarity 5

This attachment can be applied to any vibro weapon or any other powered Melee or Brawl weapon.

**Models Include:** SoroSuub Hyperblade.\
**Base Modifiers:** Once per turn as a maneuver, the wielder may activate the overclock setting to grant the weapon [[Item Qualities#Vicious (Passive)|Vicious]] 3 (or increase the value of its [[Item Qualities#Vicious (Passive)|Vicious]] quality by +3) until the end of the next round. If the wielder generates three [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results or [[Narrative Dice#Despair|despair]] on a combat check with the weapon before the end of the next round, the weapon overheats or shorts out and ceases to function entirely until the end of the encounter.\
**Modification Options:** 1 Add an automatic [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result to combat checks with this weapon Mod, 1 Activate attachment as an incidental Mod.\
