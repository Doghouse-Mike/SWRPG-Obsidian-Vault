A cheap way to increase the power output of the Boost is to mount small auxiliary boosters on the engines, usually placed in clever ways to maximize the thrust without breaking the engines’ physics, and directly connected to the primary afterburner. Z-series is actually an obscure misnomer from podracing lingo, as most of those boosters are very much homebrewed.
