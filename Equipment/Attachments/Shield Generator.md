Most Podracers, whether custom-built racers or one of the many factory customs, mount a small, low-output shield generator in the cockpit.

> More information about the attachment available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Deflector_shield_generator).

## SHIELD GENERATOR

\[This\] mod may only be installed on Podracers.[^2]

**Models Include:** Novaldex GA21 Shield Generator, Chempat CED8089Y Shields, Keizar-Volvec Mk VIII Safety Shield, BGRE "Bug Smasher" Shield.\
**Base Modifiers:** Grants the Podracer defense 1 in its forward defensive arc.\
**Modification Options:** 1 One additional point of defense to one defense zone of choice Mod.
