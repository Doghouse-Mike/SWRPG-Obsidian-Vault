A somewhat less eloquent relative of Whistling Birds, these single fire rocket dart launchers are generally only utilized as a last resort. The launcher housings physically take up very little space on a suit of armor and an untrained observer may not even recognize them as armaments. Most Mandalorians opt to install these dumb-fire launchers on their knee pads, but they can be fitted to any solid material.

> More information about the attachment available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kneepad_rocket_dart_launcher).
