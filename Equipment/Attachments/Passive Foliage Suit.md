## PASSIVE FOLIAGE SUIT

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance – Price 75 Rarity 2|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/a/ab/Profile_Passive_Foliage_Suit.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance – Price 75 Rarity 2

This attachment can be equipped on any armor that could logically integrate the necessary patches and tie-lines.

**Models Include:** Various types.\
**Base Modifiers:** Adds a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die to any [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] or [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]] checks made to detect a character in appropriate environments who is wearing this armor.\
**Modification Options:** None.
