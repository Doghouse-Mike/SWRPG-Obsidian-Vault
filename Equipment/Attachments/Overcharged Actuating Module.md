## OVERCHARGED ACTUATING MODULE

Source: [^1][^2]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 650 Rarity 7|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/4/47/Profile_Overcharged_Actuating_Module.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 650 Rarity 7

This attachment can be applied to any blaster pistol or carbine.

**Models Include:** BlasTech Mk. II Actuating Module, SoroSuub BlastPro.\
**Base Modifiers:** Increases weapon damage by +1. The Game Master may spend two [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] results from any combat check made with this weapon to have it become damaged one step.\
**Modification Options:** 1 Damage +1 Mod.
