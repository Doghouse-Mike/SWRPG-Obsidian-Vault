For those sentients without the evolutionary adaptation of gills, specialized equipment is necessary for surviving in a liquid environment.

## AMPHIBIOUS MODIFICATION

Source: [^1][^2]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 500 Rarity 4|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/c/c4/Profile_Blaster_Actuating_Module.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 500 Rarity 4

This attachment can be applied to any armor that could logically have an air tank attached.

**Models Include:** Gandorthral Atmospherics Submersible 9, BioTech Mk. II Air Supply.\
**Base Modifiers:** The armor provides enough oxygen (or applicable gas) for up to five hours of submersion or in any airless environment (although it does not protect against vacuum). In addition, the user removes a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die from physical checks due to underwater conditions.\
**Modification Options:** 2 Increase oxygen supply by two hours Mods.
