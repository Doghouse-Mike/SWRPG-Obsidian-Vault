## RADIATION SHIELDING

Source: [^0-1][^1-2]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 500 Rarity 3|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/0/0a/Profile_Radiation_Shielding.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 500 Rarity 3

This attachment can only be applied to armor with hard plates that can be fully sealed, at the GM's discretion.[^0-1]

This attachment can only be applied to laminate armor, heavy battle armor, and any other armor that can be fully sealed.[^1-2]

**Models Include:** Loronar Model 4 Hostile Environment Treatment, Neuro-Saav Radiological Hazard Shielding.\
**Base Modifiers:** Adds two [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] dice to [[Resilience (Br)|Resilience]] checks to resist radiation.\
**Modification Options:** 2 Increase armor's soak by +1 against damage caused by radiation Mods.
