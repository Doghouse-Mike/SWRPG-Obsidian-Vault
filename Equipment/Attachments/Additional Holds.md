While usually not designed for it, and shaving off useless space or cramming it with as much as machinery as possible is the end goal before building them, podracers can often have a little room left for holds, notably in the pod itself next to the pilot. It is possible to convert parts of the podracer for extra empty holds, or to directly affix casings in places where it does not hamper performances too much. What the racer makes of those holds, however, it better left at their discretion...
