## MARKSMAN BARREL

Source: [^1][^2]

![Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price 1,200 Rarity 4|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/e/e8/Profile_Marksman_Barrel.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price 1,200 Rarity 4

This attachment may only be applied on blaster rifles.

**Models Include:** Merr-Sonn KX756 Marksman Barrel, SoroSuub WQ80 "Long-shot" Barrel, Arakyd 3300 Hawkeye Sharpshooter's Barrel.\
**Base Modifiers:** Increases weapon's range by one range band. The weapon gains the [[Item Qualities#Cumbersome (Passive)|Cumbersome]] 2 quality.\
**Modification Options:** 2 Weapon Quality ([[Item Qualities#Accurate (Passive)|Accurate]] +1) Mods, 1 Innate Talent ([[Sniper Shot talent|Sniper Shot]]) Mod.
