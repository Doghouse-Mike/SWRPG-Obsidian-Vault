As rare as this type of kyber crystal is, some examples do find their way into the hands of Force-sensitives highly attuned to the light side of the Force.

> More information about the crystal available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Solari_crystal).

## SOLARI CRYSTAL

Source: [^1][^2]

![Hard Points Required 3 Encumbrance - Price (R) 16,000 Rarity 9|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/b/b1/Profile_Solari_Crystal.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 3 Encumbrance - Price (R) 16,000 Rarity 9

**Models Include:** None. \
**Base Modifiers:** Installing this crystal changes a lightsaber's base damage to 7 and its critical rating to 2, and the lightsaber gains the [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] 1, [[Item Qualities#Defensive (Passive)|Defensive]] 1, and [[Item Qualities#Sunder (Passive)|Sunder]] item qualities. In addition, a Force-sensitive character may reduce the amount of strain suffered when taking the [[Reflect talent|Reflect]] incidental by 1. Should a Force-sensitive user's Morality ever fall below 50, or if the crystal is ever removed, the lightsaber loses these qualities and reverts to its previous base damage and critical rating. \
**Modification Options:** 1 Damage +1 Mod, 1 Item Quality ([[Item Qualities#Defensive (Passive)|Defensive]] +1) Mod, 1 Reduce cost of [[Reflect talent#Improved|Improved Reflect]] by one [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] result Mod.
