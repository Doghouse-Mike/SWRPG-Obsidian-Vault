## NISHALORITE STONE

Source: [^1][^2]

![Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price (R) 12,500 Rarity 8|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/4/4b/Profile_Nishalorite_Stone.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price (R) 12,500 Rarity 8

**Base Modifiers:** Installing a nishalorite stone changes a lightsaber's damage to 7 and its critical rating to 3. In addition, the lightsaber gains the [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] 1 and [[Item Qualities#Sunder (Passive)|Sunder]] item qualities. Additionally, a Force-sensitive character with this lightsaber in their possession adds an automatic [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result to any of their checks made to determine their position, navigate terrain, or find power sources or active technology. If the crystal is ever removed, the lightsaber loses these qualities and reverts to its previous base damage and critical rating. \
**Modification Options:** 1 Innate Talent ([[Planet Mapper talent|Planet Mapper]]) Mod, 2 Item Quality ([[Item Qualities#Disorient (Active)|Disorient]] +1) Mod, 1 Item Quality ([[Item Qualities#Vicious (Passive)|Vicious]] +1) Mod.
