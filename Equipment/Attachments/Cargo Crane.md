This attachment adds a crane of varying size to a vehicle. This could be a small crane attached to a speeder to allow it to pick up small objects or a larger crane for moving massive cargo containers.

## CARGO CRANE

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 500 Rarity 2|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/e/ef/Profile_Cargo_Crane.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 500 Rarity 2

Only one object may be picked up. The initial attachment grants a smaller crane while a Modification Option expands into larger versions. It is only available on silhouette 3 or larger vehicles. If used in an attempt to tow an object (the craned vehicle moves), it does so at -1 speed and -1 handling.

**Models Include:** N/A.\
**Base Modifiers:** Light crane which may move vehicle silhouette 2 or larger objects. Qualifies as Light Lifting Equipment. \
**Modification Options:** 3 Increase Object Size by 1 Silhouette Mods. Object size may not be more than 1 less then vehicle silhouette. If object size increased to 4+, crane qualifies as Heavy Lifting Equipment.
