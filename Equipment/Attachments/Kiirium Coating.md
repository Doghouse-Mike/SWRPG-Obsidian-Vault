An obsolete shielding material once processed in abundance in the Si'Klaata Cluster, kiirium was at one time vital to the manufacture of heavy weapons and starships.

## KIIRIUM COATING

Source: [^1][^2]

![Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance 1 Price 2,500 Rarity 7|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/0/0b/Profile_Kiirium_Coating.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance 1 Price 2,500 Rarity 7

This attachment can only be applied to armor that has solid surface plates that the alloy can coat, at the GM's discretion.

**Models Include:** Numerous Variants.\
**Base Modifiers:** Adds +1 soak to armor against blaster and flame attacks.\
**Modification Options:** 2 Decrease the difficulty of [[Resilience (Br)|Resilience]] checks made to resist the effects of fire and extreme heat by 1 Mods.
