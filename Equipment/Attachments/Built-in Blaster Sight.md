A typical modification used by the Rebellion's Spec-Force troops, this attachment allows a harmless beam of energy to trickle out of the barrel on a half-trigger pull to "paint" the target, letting the wielder see exactly where \[they are\] aiming.

> More information about the attachment available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Built-in_Blaster_Sight).

## BUILT-IN BLASTER SIGHT

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance – Price 150 Rarity 4|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/c/c4/Profile_Built-in_Blaster_Sight.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text

This attachment can be applied to energy ranged weapons.

**Models Include:** Neuro-Savv "Deadeye" Sight, Tana Ire y-30Z Targeting Beam.\
**Base Modifiers:** Remove a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die added by the Called Shot use of the Aim maneuver from [[Ranged - Light (Ag)|Ranged (Light)]] and [[Ranged - Heavy (Ag)|Ranged (Heavy)]] checks.\
**Modification Options:** None.
