Practitioners of Form V sometimes use a reverse grip on their lightsabers to assist in many of the techniques common to that form.

> More information about the attachment available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shien_reverse_grip_customization).

## SHIEN REVERSE GRIP CUSTOMIZATION

Source: [^1][^2]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 100 Rarity 6|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/a/a8/Profile_Shien_Reverse_Grip_Customization.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 100 Rarity 6

This attachment can only be applied to lightsabers.[^3]

**Models Include:** None; users must custom-make each lightsaber attachment.\
**Base Modifiers:** The wielder adds an [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result to all [[Shien Technique talent|Lightsaber (Cunning)]] checks made with this lightsaber but adds a [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] result to all other [[Lightsaber (Br)|Lightsaber]] checks.\
**Modification Options:** None.
