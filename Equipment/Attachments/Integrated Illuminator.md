Operating in darkness is never ideal, but there are several ways to compensate for a lack of night vision.

## INTEGRATED ILLUMINATOR

Source: [^1][^2]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance 1 Price 200 Rarity 4|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/5/53/Profile_Integrated_Illuminator.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance 1 Price 200 Rarity 4

This attachment can be applied to any ranged weapon that could logically benefit from a spotlight.

**Models Include:** CSA "Nightfighter" Weapon Lamp, Loronar L09 Lamp.\
**Base Modifiers:** Removes up to two [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice due to darkness on any checks to use this weapon against targets within short range.\
**Modification Options:** 1 Increase spotlight range to medium Mod, 2 Item Quality ([[Item Qualities#Disorient (Active)|Disorient]] +1) Mods.
