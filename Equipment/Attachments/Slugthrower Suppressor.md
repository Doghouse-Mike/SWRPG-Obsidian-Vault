Suppressors are used by assassins and sharpshooters to make their weapons quieter and more difficult to detect when firing.

## SLUGTHROWER SUPPRESSOR

Source: [^1][^2]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance 1 Price (R) 100 Rarity 4|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/c/c6/Profile_Slugthrower_Suppressor.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance 1 Price (R) 100 Rarity 4

Suppressors can only be used on [[Ranged - Light (Ag)|Ranged (Light)]] and [[Ranged - Heavy (Ag)|Ranged (Heavy)]] slugthrower weapons.

**Models Include:** Czerka Mk. I Suppressor, SoroSuub "Whisperflite" Slugthrower Silencer.\
**Base Modifiers:** Adds two [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice to any [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] or [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]] checks made to locate a concealed shooter firing the silenced slugthrower.\
**Modification Options:** None.
