## BLASTER ENERGY DAMPENER

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance – Price (R) 750 Rarity 4|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/6/6c/Profile_Blaster_Energy_Dampener.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance – Price (R) 750 Rarity 4

This attachment is only for use on Ranged (Light) and Ranged (Heavy) blaster weapons.

**Models Include:** Czerka B78 Energy Dampener.\
**Base Modifiers:** Increase the difficulty of checks made to detect this weapon's fire by 1. Decrease weapon damage by 1.\
**Modification Options:** 1 Add a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die to checks made to detect this weapon's fire Mod.
