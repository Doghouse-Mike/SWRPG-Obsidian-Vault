Stygium is used as a key component in cloaking technology. The crystals itself are very expensive and sought after, but their structure and particular make allow not only channeling a lightsaber blade, but also infer their cloaking capabilities to the wielder, through the force.

**Models Include:** N/A.\
**Base Modifiers:** Installing this crystal changes a lightsaber's base damage to 6 and critical rating to 2, and the lightsaber gains the [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] 1 and [[Item Qualities#Sunder (Active)|Sunder]] qualities. While wielding the lightsaber, its owner adds two [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results to any [[Stealth (Ag)|Stealth]] checks they make. If the crystal is ever removed, the lightsaber loses these qualities and reverts to its previous base damage and critical rating.\
**Modification Options:** 2 Damage +1 Mods, 2 add a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to [[Stealth (Ag)|Stealth]] checks Mod.
