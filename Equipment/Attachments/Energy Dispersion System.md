This minute metallic wiring is designed to distribute electric shocks across the armor while grounding the user against energy, reducing the potency of such attacks.

## ENERGY DISPERSION SYSTEM

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 500 Rarity 4|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/0/02/Profile_Energy_Dispersion_System.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 500 Rarity 4

This attachment can be installed on any armor.

**Models Include:** Atlas Corp Model 88 Electric Shield, Merr-Sonn AEGIS Nanoarray.\
**Base Modifiers:** While using the wearer's soak to reduce damage dealt to the wearer's strain threshold, count the soak value as 2 higher.\
**Modification Options:** None.
