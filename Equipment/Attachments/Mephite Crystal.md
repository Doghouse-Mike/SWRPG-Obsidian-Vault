Mephite crystals are rare, lustrous kyber crystals found in a handful of places in and around the Adega system.

> More information about the crystal available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mephite_crystal).

## MEPHITE CRYSTAL

Source: [^1][^2]

![Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price (R) 10,000 Rarity 10|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/4/44/Profile_Mephite_Crystal.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 2 Encumbrance - Price (R) 10,000 Rarity 10

**Base Modifiers:** Installing this crystal changes the lightsaber's damage to 8, its critical rating to 2, and the lightsaber gains the [[Item Qualities#Breach (Passive)|Breach]] 1 and [[Item Qualities#Sunder (Passive)|Sunder]] item qualities. Force-sensitive characters using Force powers to sense their surroundings automatically detect a lightsaber using a mephite crystal if it is in range of the power. If the crystal is ever removed, the lightsaber loses these qualities and reverts to its previous base damage and critical rating. \
**Modification Options:** 3 Damage +1 Mods, 1 Decrease the weapon's critical rating by 1 to a minimum of 1 Mod, 1 Item Quality ([[Item Qualities#Vicious (Passive)|Vicious]] +1) Mod.
