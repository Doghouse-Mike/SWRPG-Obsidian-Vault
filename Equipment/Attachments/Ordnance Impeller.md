## ORDNANCE IMPELLER

Source: [^1][^0-2]

![Hard Points Required 0 Encumbrance - Price 500 Rarity 4|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/e/ea/Profile_Ordnance_Impeller.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 0 Encumbrance - Price 500 Rarity 4

This attachment can be applied to any grenade or explosive device with an encumbrance of 3 or less.

**Models Include:** Ghelrar Consortium Multipurpose Repulsor Unit, Rebaxan Columni MSE-6 Repair Droid (modified).[^0-2]\
**Base Modifiers:** The explosive is able to move overland at roughly the rate of a character on foot. The listed price includes a holocam and remote-control unit, allowing for operation and detonation of the mobile explosive from up to extreme range, although intervening materials may decrease this distance. Any skill checks to operate the mobile explosive use [[Computers (Int)|Computers]] or [[Piloting - Planetary (Ag)|Piloting (Planetary)]] (character's choice).\
**Modification Options:** 1 Add ten-meter maximum altitude Mod.
