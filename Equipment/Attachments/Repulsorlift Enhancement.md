A repulsorlift enhancement increases the output of a standard airspeeder's repulsorlift engine considerably.

## REPULSORLIFT ENHANCEMENT

Source: [^1]

![Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 2,500 Rarity -|400](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/star-wars-rpg-ffg/images/4/4b/Profile_Repulsorlift_Enhancement.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/800)

> [!quote] Image text
> Hard Points Required 1 Encumbrance - Price 2,500 Rarity -

Installing this attachment requires an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics check]]**. Repulsorlift enhancements may only be attached to vehicles of silhouette 2 or lower.

**Models Include:** None.\
**Base Modifiers:** As a maneuver, suffer 1 system strain to add +1 maximum speed for one round.\
**Modification Options:** None.
