### VOICE EMULATORS

Source: [^0-1]

Commando droids sent on infiltration and other spec ops missions made use of vocal emulators to appear as clones and other individuals over comms. When doing so, the droid can imitate a voice with few lines of heard speech and a **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Hard difficulty]] [[Computers (Int)|Computers check]]**. Due to the large amount of clone speech patterns available for analysis in their database, the difficulty for imitating this voice is reduced twice to **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Easy difficulty]]** instead.
