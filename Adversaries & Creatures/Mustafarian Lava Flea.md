Perhaps one of the most unusual mounts in the galaxy, the Mustafarian lava flea is a giant arthropod that evolved for life on the volcanic environment of its homeworld.

> More information about the beast available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lava_flea).
