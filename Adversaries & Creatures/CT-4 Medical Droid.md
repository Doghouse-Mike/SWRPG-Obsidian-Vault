Source: [^0-2]

> [!quote] Image text
> Brawn 1 Agility 1 Intellect 3 Cunning 1 Willpower 2 Presence 1 Soak Value 2 Wound Threshold 8 Melee Defense 0 Ranged Defense 0

**Skills:** [[Computers (Int)|Computers]] 1, [[Discipline (Will)|Discipline]] 1, [[Xenology (Int)|Knowledge (Xenology)]] 3, [[Medicine (Int)|Medicine]] 3, [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] 2.\
**Talents:** [[Bacta Specialist talent|Bacta Specialist]] 2 (patients heal 2 additional wounds when they heal wounds via bacta tanks or long-term care), [[Surgeon talent|Surgeon]] 1 (when making a [[Medicine (Int)|Medicine]] check to help a character heal wounds, the target heals 1 additional wound).\
**Abilities:** Droid (does not need to breathe, eat, or drink and can survive in vacuum and underwater; immune to poisons and toxins).\
**Equipment:** Built-in diagnostic and surgical tools (counts as [[Medpac|medpac]] as well as [[Stimpack|stimpack]] that can be used once per session), synthflesh applicator (can administer application of [[Synthskin - Synthflesh|synthflesh]] five times per session).
