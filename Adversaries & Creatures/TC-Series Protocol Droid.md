**Talents:** None.\
**Abilities:** Droid (does not need to breathe, eat, or drink and can survive in vacuum and underwater; immune to poisons and toxins), etiquette and protocol (TC-series protocol droids allow allies to add a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to any [[Negotiation (Pr)|Negotiation]] checks or other checks made to negotiate or mediate), advanced language module (when encountering a new species or language not in its databases, a TC-series protocol droid may make an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Easy difficulty]] [[Education (Int)|Knowledge (Education) check]]** to learn enough of the new dialect to communicate).\
**Equipment:** None.
