BD units are companion droids designed to assist with operations in remote and dangerous locations across the galaxy; programmed to be the ideal assistant to researchers or explorers operating alone in the field, they are able to traverse all types of terrain. As a result of their manufacturer collapsing, BD units are a relatively rare sight in the galaxy.

> More information about the droid available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BD_explorer_droid).
