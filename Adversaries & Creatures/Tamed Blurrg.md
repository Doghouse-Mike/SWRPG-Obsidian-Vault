Blurrg are stocky two-legged reptiles that can be found on a number of planets across the Outer Rim. Even when raised in captivity they are naturally surly creatures. A potential jockey must prove themself worthy of the beast’s respect before attempting to ride it.

> More information about the beast is available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Blurrg).
