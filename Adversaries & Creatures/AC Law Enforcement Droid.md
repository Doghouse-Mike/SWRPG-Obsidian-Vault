The Rim Securities AC Law Enforcement Droids are a relatively new entry to the field of police automata, envisioned as direct competitors to both Cybot Galactica's HXZ-1 series and SoroSuub's recently released 501-Z series LEO droid.

> More information about the droid available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/AC_law_enforcement_droid).
