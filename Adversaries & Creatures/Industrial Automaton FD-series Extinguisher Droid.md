A highly specialized maintenance droid, fire suppression droids perform the singular function of putting out fires.

> More information about the droid available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/FD-series_extinguisher_droid).

Source: [^2]

**Skills (group only):** [[Athletics (Br)|Athletics]].\
**Talents:** None.\
**Abilities:** Droid (does not need to breathe, eat, or drink and can survive in vacuum or underwater. Immune to poisons and toxins), Firefighter (may make an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Athletics (Br)|Athletics check]]** to extinguish a fire; success extinguishes the fire within a number of rounds determined by the GM, and additional successes may reduce the time needed to extinguish the fire. Particularly large or dangerous fires may require more difficult checks at the GM's discretion), Fire Resistant (immune to fire damage or damage from weapons with the [[Item Qualities#Burn (Active)|Burn]] quality).\
**Equipment:** Fire suppression equipment.
