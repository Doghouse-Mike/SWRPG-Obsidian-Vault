The basic model retriever droid is small and simple, but adept at performing its limited range of tasks. Specifically programmed for release in larger debris fields, it will locate materials of size and composition within the parameters designated by its master. Given the relatively small size of the droid, it is adept at working its way through smaller crevices in breeched hulls, and bringing back smaller materials. Though when released en masse, they can quickly clear debris fields of smaller flotsam, though their small size and limited thrust prevents them from acquiring larger sections of recyclable materials.
