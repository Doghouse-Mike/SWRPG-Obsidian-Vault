Seeing a weakness in the general run of protocol droids, namely the almost universal humanoid appearance of most models, the Rseikharhl Droid Group began the development of a droid who would specialize in assisting negotiators and diplomats working specifically with non-humanoid species.

> More information about the droid available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/ED-V8-series_envoy_droid).
