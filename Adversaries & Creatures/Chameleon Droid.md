**Talents:** Stalker 2 (add two [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] dice to [[Coordination (Ag)|Coordination]] and [[Stealth (Ag)|Stealth]] checks), Cunning Ambusher 2 (opponents add two [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice to Initiative checks using [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]]).\

**Equipment:** Blaster carbine ([[Ranged - Heavy (Ag)|Ranged (Heavy)]]; Damage 9; Critical 3; Range \[Medium\]; [[Item Qualities#Stun setting (Active)|Stun setting]]), minelayer with 24 anti-personnel mines (drop one mine as a maneuver, detonates when another character moves to engage with the mine, dealing 12 damage to each character engaged with the mine).
