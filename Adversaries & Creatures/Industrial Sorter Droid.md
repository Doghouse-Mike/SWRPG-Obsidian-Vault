Though similar to menial labor droids found throughout the galaxy, sorter droids come with a distinct advantage to other models. Passive salvage brings in vast amounts of materials. Some of the debris is actually functional or repairable. Other items are fit for no more than recycling. Sorter droids are able to make assessments of the condition of materials and sort them based on their relative value. Able to stand the tedium of long shifts with little recharge time, sorter droids are found in deep-space passive salvage vessels throughout the galaxy. They become particularly attractive to captains who see no need to environmentally seal their sorting bays.
