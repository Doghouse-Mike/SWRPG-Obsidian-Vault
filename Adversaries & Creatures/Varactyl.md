The Utai species of Utapau has long used varactyls as mounts, favoring the reptavian creatures for their incredible speed, as well as for their ability to defeat osbtacles by climbing using their clinging feet or even gliding over them using their feathered limbs.

> More information about the beast available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Varactyl).
