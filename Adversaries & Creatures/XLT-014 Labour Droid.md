Relative to most labour droids, the XLT-014 is a rather new model to the market. Produced by the renowned labour droid manufacturer Publictechnic, it was fitted with repulsorlifts in a humanoid appearance, allowing it to reach high areas while conserving power working on surfaces. Unlike most simple labour droids, the XLT-014 contains somewhat better programmed and makes fewer mistakes. This sometimes develops into a quirk of superiority, where the droid will start to make mistakes due to an overeager attempt to get through work faster.
