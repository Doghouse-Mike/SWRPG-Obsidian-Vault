The IP-series Interests Protection droid, known colloquially as the “Imperial Police” droid, was produced by Cybot Galactica and released in the last weeks of the Clone Wars. Intended to be sold to corporate clients for security purposes, early stocks were quickly bought out by the new Galactic Empire to replace the old GU-series police droids with the more intimidating and ruthless models which did not come pre-loaded with protocol and threat reduction systems.

> More information about the droid available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Police_Droid).
