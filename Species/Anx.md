- **Wound Threshold:** 13 + Brawn
- **Strain Threshold:** 10 + Willpower
- **Starting Experience:** 85 XP
- **Special Abilities:** Anx begin the game with one rank in the [[Kill With Kindness talent|Kill with Kindness talent]].
- **Anatomical Knowledge:** Anx have an innate grasp of how bodies are put together and are able to intuit the anatomical vulnerabilities of almost any species. Anx gain one rank of the [[Lethal Blows talent]].
- **Mood Indicator:** If an Anx's head-crest is not covered, the character upgrades the difficulty of [[Deception (Cun)|Deception]] checks once.
