- **Wound Threshold:** 10 + Brawn
- **Strain Threshold:** 11 + Willpower
- **Starting Experience:** 100 XP
- **Special Abilities:** Twi'leks begin the game with one rank in either [[Charm (Pr)|Charm]] or [[Deception (Cun)|Deception]]. They still may not train [[Charm (Pr)|Charm]] or [[Deception (Cun)|Deception]] above rank 2 during character creation.
- **Desert Dwellers:** When making skill checks, Twi'leks may remove a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die imposed due to arid or hot environmental conditions.
