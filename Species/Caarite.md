Caarites are sly, ingratiating beings whose penchant for crooked dealings is cloaked by their friendly demanors. While they suffer from a rather poor reputation, it is difficult for anyone around a Caarite for an extended period of time to believe it is capable of anything truly malicious. They wear broad grins, bearing a double row of wide flat perfectly white teeth. Taking advantage of their frail, friendly appearance, they seize the upper hand in negotiations, exuding an almost childlike charm when dealing directly with large groups of individuals.

**Physiology:** Caarites are about a meter tall, with features that are vaguely porcine. While basically humanoid, they have slightly elongated snouts turned delicately upward at their tips. Their broad, open faces make their open smiles look even friendlier. Their flesh is pale pink, and they are completely hairless.

**Homeworld:** The Caarite homeworld is a warm, sauna-like planet with great humidity located in the Outer Rim dubbed Caarimon. The cities of the planet are built in the clouds as an asteroid strike millennia ago rendered the Caarite’s original environment toxic.

**Languages:** Caarites speak Caarimala and virtually all learn Basic. Given their proclivity to enter into the world of trade, many tend to learn more languages to better enable this.

**Life Beyond the Metatheran Cartel:** Caarites who seek adventure do so for personal gain. They favor non-violent means when face-to-face with an adversary, but they nonetheless carry a number of tools and ranged weapons. Caarites favor work as smugglers, entrepreneurs, and fringers. Because they are averse to physical combat, they do not typically enter martial lifestyles. Some are Force-Sensitive, but their inherent greed prevents them from truly embracing the way of the Jedi.

- **Special Abilities:** Caarites begin the game with one rank in [[Negotiation (Pr)|Negotiation]] or [[Streetwise (Cun)|Streetwise]]. They still may not train [[Negotiation (Pr)|Negotiation]] or [[Streetwise (Cun)|Streetwise]] above rank 2 during character creation. Caarites are smaller than average and count as silhouette 0.
- **Sauna Life:** When making skill checks, Caarites may remove a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die imposed due to hot environmental conditions. However, inexperience with cooler temperatures mean that any cold environment imposes a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die to the character’s checks. With their GM’s approval a player with a Caarite character may spend 5 XP during creation to remove this disadvantage from their character.
