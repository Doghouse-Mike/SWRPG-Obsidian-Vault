- **Wound Threshold:** 11 + Brawn
- **Strain Threshold:** 10 + Willpower
- **Starting Experience:** 80 XP
- **Special Abilities:** Selonians begin the game with one rank in [[Coordination (Ag)|Coordination]]. They still may not train [[Coordination (Ag)|Coordination]] above rank 2 during character creation. The structure of a Selonian's eyes allows \[them\] to see in near total darkness. \[They\] may remove all [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice added to checks due to darkness, though \[they\] still cannot see in absolute darkness.
- **Tail:** A Selonian's tail may be used as a Brawl weapon. When used to attack, it uses the Brawl skill combined with the Agility characteristic, but like most Brawl weapons, adds its weapon damage to the character's Brawn for total damage. It always counts as equipped, and has the following weapon profile ([[Brawl (Br)|Brawl]]; Damage +1; Critical 5; Range \[Engaged\]; [[Item Qualities#Disorient (Active)|Disorient]] 2, [[Item Qualities#Knockdown (Active)|Knockdown]]). The Selonian may trigger [[Item Qualities#Knockdown (Active)|Knockdown]] with an [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result, but still must spend additional [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results to knock down targets with a higher silhouette as per the [[Item Qualities#Knockdown (Active)|Knockdown]] quality rules.
