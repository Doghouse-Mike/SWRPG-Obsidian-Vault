The Aqualish are a triad of semi-aquatic sub-species evolved from aquatic mammals on the planet Ando.

- **Wound Threshold:** 11 + Brawn
- **Strain Threshold:** 8 + Willpower
- **Starting Experience:** 90 XP
- **Special Abilities:** All Aqualish are capable of breathing underwater. In addition, they begin the game with one rank in [[Brawl (Br)|Brawl]]. Characters cannot train [[Brawl (Br)|Brawl]] above rank 2 during character creation.
- **Sub-Species Options:** A player choosing to play an Aqualish character must choose one of the following sub-species. No matter which sub-species is chosen, no skills may be trained above rank 2 during character creation.
  - **Aquala:** Aquala begin the game with one rank in [[Resilience (Br)|Resilience]]. Furthermore, Aquala may remove a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die imposed due to any cold or wet conditions.
  - **Ualaq:** Ualaq begin the game with one rank in [[Survival (Cun)|Survival]] or one rank in [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]]. Furthermore, Ualaq may remove a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die imposed due to dark conditions, but suffer a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die to [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] checks in brightly lit environments.
  - **Quara:** Quara begin the game with one rank in [[Athletics (Br)|Athletics]] or one rank in [[Coercion (Will)|Coercion]]. Furthermore, Quara may remove a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die when attempting to track anything through a natural environment.
