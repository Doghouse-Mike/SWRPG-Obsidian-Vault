The Yahk-Tosh are a slug-like sentient species which hail from Xolu, located within Hutt Space. They are pledged to the Hutts and are favoured by the Jahibakti clan as enforcers, rather than the more common Gamorrean or Nikto.

> More information about the species available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yahk-Tosh).

- **Wound Threshold:** 12 + Brawn
- **Strain Threshold:** 10 + Willpower
- **Starting Experience:** 75 XP
- **Special Abilities:** Yahk-Tosh begin the game with one rank in [[Athletics (Br)|Athletics]] or [[Discipline (Will)|Discipline]]. They still may not train these skills above rank 2 during character creation.
- **Additional Limbs:** Yahk-Tosh have multiple sets of arms. As a result, they gain an additional free maneuver per turn, though still may not perform more than two maneuvers per turn.
- **Droid Telepathy:** The mild telepathic abilities of the Yahk-Tosh allows them to take control of droids with their minds. Yahk-Tosh begin the game with one rank of [[Speaks Binary talent|Speaks Binary]] and can direct droids without audible speech when within medium range of them.
