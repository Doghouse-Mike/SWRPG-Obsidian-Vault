Hailing from the planet Yar Togna, the mysterious Tognaths are tall, quiet humanoids adorned with cybernetic enhancements and life-support equipment..

> More information about the species available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tognath).

#### EGG MATES

Source: [^1]

Sometimes Tognath eggs fuse in pairs or trios within their suspension jelly before hatching. \[...\]

If Tognath characters have an egg-mate, they may spend a Destiny Point as an incidental to learn the general direction of that egg mate, as long as the egg mate is within extreme range of their location. They also learn the current emotional state of the egg mate as well.

- **Wound Threshold:** 9 + Brawn
- **Strain Threshold:** 9 + Willpower
- **Starting Experience:** 95 XP
- **Special Abilities:** Cybernetic implants and specialized lenses for their compound eyes grant Tognath characters one free rank each in [[Coordination (Ag)|Coordination]] and [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]]. They may not train either skill beyond rank 2 at character creation. This set of cybernetics counts as 1 implant against a Tognath's limit on cybernetic implants.
- **Primitive Nerves:** Add two [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice to [[Coercion (Will)|Coercion]] checks targeting Tognath characters.
- **Atmospheric Requirement:** Tognaths must wear a specialized mask or breathing apparatus to breathe outside their native atmosphere. Tognath characters start the game with a [[Breath Mask and Respirator|breath mask]] and treat oxygen as a corrosive atmosphere with rating 5. However, they can survive in vacuum for up to five minutes before suffering its effects.
