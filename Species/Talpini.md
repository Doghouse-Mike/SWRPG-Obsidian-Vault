Diminutive humanoids native to Tal Pi, the Talpini are just one of the species that have benefited greatly from the High Republic’s expansionary works. The opening of a new and more direct hyperspace route from Tal Pi to Kashyyyk has crated new opportunities for the Talpini to venture further into the galaxy and become a more common sight across the stars.

**Physiology:** Talpini stand between 1.2- 1.5 metres tall and posses a fine a coat of fur over their skin. Skin which ranges in colour from dark browns to light oranges. They also have a wide down- turned mouth that contains between one and three rows of needle sharp teeth and a pair of small, beady eyes.

The Talpini’s small size allows them to easily blend in amongst the wider galaxy, slipping through large crowds with ease.

**Society:** The harsh and rocky moon of Tal Pi is a dangerous and unforgiving place. A place where only the most powerful survive. The Talpini were far from the strongest or the fastest species on their homeworld. They instead relied on ingenuity and cunning to climb to the top of the food chain on Tal Pi, forging tools and equipment that compensated for their physical inferiority.

Talpini society is centred around a caste system, but one that allows wide room for mobility. Families rise and fall through the ranks of Talpini society with regularity. The species long history of ingenuity and cunning leads to a culture of backstabbing and underhanded behaviour where only the smartest and most crafty members rise to the top. This hunger for power through cunning has carried into the wider galaxy. With Talpini’s relatively recent easier connection to the rest of the High Republic, the species seeks to make its mark and carve out a powerful place in the galaxy, just as they made their mark and carved out a place on their homeworld of Tal Pi.

**Homeworld:** Located in the Jorax Sector of the Outer Rim, Tal Pi is the only moon surrounding the gas giant Tal. The planet’s surface is harsh and barren and frequently bombarded by days- long dust storms. The Talpini make their homes in caverns and burrows deep beneath Tal Pi’s surface, living in a state of near darkness. Tal Pi ‘s seemingly inhospitable surface is surprisingly home to countless types of flora, including many of the large and hostile variety. Korak squalls, Chansata and Rackcrabs are just some of the predators the Talpini have fought back over the millennia.

**Language:** Talp is the spoken and written language of the Talpini. A complicated language, with dozens of variants on every letter, some sociologists believe this is to dissuade any non Talpini from reading any of the species documents. Most Talpini also learn Basic as a matter of course, speaking the language with a thick, growling accent.

#### TALPINI TECH

Talpini tamed their homeworld through the use of power suits. Talpini engineers are masters of interfacing their bodies and minds with technology. If anyone in the wider galaxy is aware of the Talpini at all, it is because of their legendary mastery of technology to enhance their position.
