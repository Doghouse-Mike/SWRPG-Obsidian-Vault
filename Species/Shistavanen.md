The isolationist Shistavanens are fur-covered bipeds with sharp teeth and claws.

> More information about the species available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shistavanen).

- **Wound Threshold:** 12 + Brawn
- **Strain Threshold:** 10 + Willpower
- **Starting Experience:** 80 XP
- **Special Abilities:** Shistavanens begin the game with one rank in either [[Brawl (Br)|Brawl]] or [[Survival (Cun)|Survival]]. They still may not train [[Brawl (Br)|Brawl]] or [[Survival (Cun)|Survival]] above rank 2 during character creation.
- **Hunter's Instincts:** When making a check for initiative, a Shistavanen may make a [[Survival (Cun)|Survival]] check instead of a [[Discipline (Will)|Discipline]] or [[Cool (Pr)|Cool]] check.
