Zabrak resemble humans in skin tone and physique, but they are easily distinguished by their vestigial horns.

- **Wound Threshold:** 10 + Brawn
- **Strain Threshold:** 10 + Willpower
- **Starting Experience:** 100 XP
- **Special Abilities:** Zabrak begin the game with one rank in [[Survival (Cun)|Survival]]. They still may not train [[Survival (Cun)|Survival]] above rank 2 during character creation.
- **Fearsome Countenance:** Zabrak add an automatic [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result to all [[Coercion (Will)|Coercion]] checks they make.
