- **Wound Threshold:** 9 + Brawn
- **Strain Threshold:** 8 + Willpower
- **Starting Experience:** 90 XP
- **Special Abilities:** Dugs begin the game with one rank in [[Brawl (Br)|Brawl]]. They still may not train [[Brawl (Br)|Brawl]] above rank 2 during character creation.
- **Born to Ride:** Whether it be atop dangerous beasts or screaming repulsorcraft, Dugs are particularly adept riders. They begin the game with one rank of the [[Defensive Driving talent|Defensive Driving]] talent.
