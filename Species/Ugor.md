For many people in the galaxy, Ugor are some of strangest and most alien species one could encounter. Originally from the edges of the Outer Rim, the High Republic’s expansionary works have connected the Ugor to the wider galaxy.

**Physiology:** Ugor are large, sentient, unicellular organisms. Members of the species are able to change their physical appearance and create pseudopodia to perform thefunctions of organs and simple tools. The highly efficient Ugor brain, located inside the nucleus, is capable of controlling up to 30 pseudopodia at once. In their naturalform, they ooze about as blobs, but many Ugor choose to literally pour themselves into environment suits and adopt a humanoid morphology when dealing with non- Ugor. The The highly unique physiology of the Ugor, make them an oddity even among the vast array of species inhabiting the galaxy.

**Society:** To many outsiders, Ugor culture can seem chaotic and obnoxious. Ugor society revolves around trading and scavenging; they are famed for providing equipment to nascent civilisations and for salvaging wreckage from failed ones. They excel at those skills that are useful in their pursuit of rubbish collection, but their obsession with garbage has a much deeper connection. They worship it. The Ugor destroyed their once lush homeworld with over pollution overcrowding and now millennia later, they see eating, recycling and reusing every single item that they come across as the best way to stop what happened to their homeworld happening to any other planet. This zealous devotion leads Ugors to fanatically and aggressively collect all garbage they encounter. The Ugor’s new home system of Paradise, is constantly visited by beings from all corners of the galaxy searching for older equipment or technology, and although Ugor demand a hefty price for their goods, they are often the only resource for certain archaic parts.

Many intolerant or close-minded Core Worlders find members Ugor obnoxious or even disgusting and unfortunately the species has developed a reputation as unpleasant to deal with and, all things considered, best avoided. Critics of Chancellor Soh’s expansion project of this era point to Ugor as exactly the kind of Outer Rim species that the Republic has no need to connect closer with.

**Homeworld:** Now native to the Outer Rim’s Paradise system, the actual homeworld of the Ugor was long ago ruined beyond repair and abandoned. This caused a deep shame amongst the Ugor people and even to this day, all Ugor refuse to speak of their unnamed homeworld. They now make their home amongst the various asteroids of the Paradise system and employ a a series of massive salvaged gravity well projectors to corral the seas of space debris floating around the system.

Ugor that live outside of the Paradise system, are often most comfortable aboard salvage ships, loaded up with recycled junk and travelling and collecting across the galaxy. While merchant, engineer and mechanic are common jobs for many Ugor, some put themselves to other uses. Some use their obsessive nature to become archivists and researchers, applying the same trash hunting dedication to pursuit of knowledge and truth.

**Language:** The language of the Ugor, named Ugor, is unpronounceable to all other species, but many Ugor also fluently speak Basic. Ugor who work in trade and sales, also learn a trade language such as Bocce or Sy Bisti. Ugors can speak these languages by creating the appropriate communication apparatus, although their pronunciation often had a watery or slimy tinge due to their fluid nature.

- **Wound Threshold:** 11 + Brawn
- **Strain Threshold:** 11 + Willpower
- **Starting Experience:** 150 XP
- **Special Abilities:** Ugor begin the game with one rank in [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics]]. They still may not train Mechanics above rank 2 during character creation.
- **Bad Reputation:** Unfortunately, Ugor have a poor reputation in certain close-minded areas of the Core Worlds. All Ugor must add an additional [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die to any Social checks in the Core Worlds.
- **Highly Adaptable:** Because of their unique biology, Ugor are able to take on two strain in exchange for temporarily boosting any skill for one round. An Ugor may temporarily grow pseudopodia into a bunch of eyestalks to increase their [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] or grow extra limbs to increase their [[Athletics (Br)|Athletics]] skill. However, no attribute may be boosted higher than rank 3.
