- **Strain Threshold:** 11 + Willpower

- **Special Abilities:** Gossams begin the game with one rank in [[Deception (Cun)|Deception]]. They still may not train [[Deception (Cun)|Deception]] above rank 2 during character creation.
- **Small:** Gossams are smaller than average and count as silhouette 0.
