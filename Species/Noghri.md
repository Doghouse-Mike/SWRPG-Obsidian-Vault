Extremely skilled assassins due to their abilities in stealth and hand-to-hand combat, the Noghri can be an incredibly dangerous species. They are efficient killing machines, with claws, fangs, and a sense of smell so acute, they could smell one's bloodline. They are much like the Wookiee species culturally, in that they respected honour above all else.

**Phsiology:** Noghri are blueish grey-skinned bipeds who are heavily muscled as well as sinewy and possess great agility which is only enhanced by their faster-than-normal reflexes. While not tall, their small size belies their ruthless nature and effective skills.

**Society:** Noghri possess a clan-based society that is made up of close-knit family groupings that have many different forms of customs and rituals. Individual Noghri were able to determine one of their kind's clan by their clan-scent. Noghri are an exceptionally rare sight throughout the galaxy, with the vast majority of other species completely unable to place the strange, dangerous hunters.

**Homeworld:** The Noghri are native only to the decaying and poisoned world of Honoghr, deep in the Outer Rim. A world very few have even heard of, and those few that have know enough to stay away.

**Language:** The Noghri's first language is Honoghran, a guttural and scratchy language, comparable to the Trandoshan language of Dosh. However, Noghri are capable of speaking Basic, albeit with a heavy and growling accent.

**In the Galaxy:** Noghri are a rare sight in the galaxy, If they are seen at all, it is as elite hunters, bodyguards and assassins. The species' strength, agility and clan based system of loyalty and pledged service make them excellent warriors for a variety of causes.
