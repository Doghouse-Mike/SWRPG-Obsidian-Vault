Descended from a semi-amphibious stock, Kallerans are a lesser-known species, native to an isolated world in the Outer Rim.

> More information about the species available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kalleran).

- **Wound Threshold:** 8 + Brawn
- **Strain Threshold:** 12 + Willpower
- **Starting Experience:** 90 XP
- **Special Abilities:** Kallerans begin the game with one rank in [[Streetwise (Cun)|Streetwise]]. They still may not train [[Streetwise (Cun)|Streetwise]] above rank 2 during character creation.
- **Hypersensitive Antennae:** Kallerans begin the game with the [[Heightened Awareness talent]].
