The Chevin are solidly built and hard to overlook, being taller than humans and nearly twice as wide.

> More information about the species available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chevin).

- **Wound Threshold:** 11 + Brawn
- **Strain Threshold:** 11 + Willpower
- **Starting Experience:** 80 XP
- **Special Abilities:** Chevin begin the game with one rank in [[Negotiation (Pr)|Negotiation]]. They still may not train [[Negotiation (Pr)|Negotiation]] above rank 2 during character creation.
- **Advanced Olfaction:** Though it originally developed to track prey, a Chevin's keen sense of smell can be valuable in many situations. Add a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] checks involving the sense of smell.
- **Thick Hide:** A Chevin's hide is thick and tough enough to absorb some damage, so they start the game with one rank in the [[Durable talent]].
