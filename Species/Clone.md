Clones are physically identical, based on the genetic template of the bounty hunter Jango Fett.

> More information about the species available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clone_trooper).

- **Wound Threshold:** 11 + Brawn
- **Strain Threshold:** 11 + Willpower
- **Starting Experience:** 100 XP
- **Special Abilities:** Clones start the game with one rank in [[Warfare (Int)|Knowledge (Warfare)]] and one rank in [[Resilience (Br)|Resilience]]. They still may not train these skills above rank 2 during character creation.
- **Kamino Training:** Clones start with one rank in the [[Physical Training talent]].
