- **Wound Threshold:** 11 + Brawn
- **Strain Threshold:** 10 + Willpower
- **Starting Experience:** 90 XP
- **Special Abilities:** Chagrians begin the game with one rank in [[Resilience (Br)|Resilience]]. They still may not train [[Resilience (Br)|Resilience]] above rank 2 during character creation.
- **Amphibious:** Chagrians can breathe underwater without penalty and never suffer movement penalties for traveling through water.
- **Knowledge Specialization:** Chagrians start with one rank in the [[Knowledge Specialization talent|Knowledge Specialization]] talent.
