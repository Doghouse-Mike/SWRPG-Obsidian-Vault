The Lannik are a diminutive species native to the Mid Rim planet of the same name.

> More information about the species available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lannik_(species%29).

- **Wound Threshold:** 8 + Brawn
- **Strain Threshold:** 12 + Willpower
- **Starting Experience:** 100 XP
- **Special Abilities:** Lanniks begin the game with one rank in [[Streetwise (Cun)|Streetwise]]. They still may not train [[Streetwise (Cun)|Streetwise]] above rank 2 during character creation.
- **Indomitable:** A Lannik removes up to two [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die caused by Critical Injuries, fear, or the disoriented condition from all checks.

```
While Lead by Example: A Sourcebook for Commanders shows "95XP" and "removes up to one setback die", the Errata (Page 4) corrects this to "100XP" and "removes up to two setback dice".
```
