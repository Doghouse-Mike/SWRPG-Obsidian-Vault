Infamous across the galaxy for their association with the illegal spice trade, the Pykes as a species consider themselves to be one large extended family; unfortunately for the rest of the galaxy, that family happens to be one of the biggest criminal syndicates of the Clone Wars era.

> More information about the species available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pyke).
