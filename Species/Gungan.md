- **Wound Threshold:** 10 + Brawn
- **Strain Threshold:** 10 + Willpower
- **Starting Experience:** 100 XP
- **Special Abilities:** Gungans begin the game with one rank in [[Athletics (Br)|Athletics]]. They still may not train [[Athletics (Br)|Athletics]] above rank 2 during character creation.
- **Amphibious:** Gungans may breathe underwater without penalty and never suffer movement penalties for traveling through water.
