The Quermian species is an offshoot of the Xexto, produced through genetic tampering long ago by Arkanian scientitsts.

> More information about the species available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Quermian).

- **Wound Threshold:** 10 + Brawn
- **Strain Threshold:** 10 + Willpower
- **Starting Experience:** 85 XP
- **Special Abilities:** Quermians begin the game with one rank in [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]]. They still may not train [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] above rank 2 during character creation.
- **Additional Limbs:** Quermians have six limbs: two legs and four arms. As a result, they gain an additional free maneuver per turn, although they still may not perform more than two maneuvers per turn.
