Native to the planet Zolan in the Mid Rim, Clawdites are primarily known across the rest of the galaxy for their innate ability to transform their appearance. This ability can be improved using a [[Saline Pump]].

> More information about the species available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clawdite).

- **Wound Threshold:** 9 + Brawn
- **Strain Threshold:** 9 + Willpower
- **Starting Experience:** 95 XP
- **Special Abilities:** Clawdites begin the game with one rank in [[Resilience (Br)|Resilience]]. They still may not train [[Resilience (Br)|Resilience]] above rank 2 during character creation.
- **Changeling:** As an action, a Clawdite may suffer 3 strain and make an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]]** **[[Resilience (Br)|Resilience check]]**. If the Clawdite succeeds, \[they\] change \[their\] appearance to match that of a silhouette 1 character whom \[they\] have observed before. An observing character must make opposed **[[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] vs [[Deception (Cun)|Deception]] check** to detect that something is amiss with the impersonated character's likeness. As always, the GM can add a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die or [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die for situational effects that might affect the check, such as a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die if the Clawdite's garb does not match expectations or a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die if the Clawdite has studied the impersonated individual's mannerisms closely.
