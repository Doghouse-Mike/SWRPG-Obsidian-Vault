Known for their history as explorers and their striking (and often intimidating) looks, Devaronians are a species familiar to many galactic travelers.

> More information about the species available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Devaronian).

- **Wound Threshold:** 11 + Brawn
- **Strain Threshold:** 10 + Willpower
- **Starting Experience:** 95 XP
- **Special Abilities:** Devaronians begin the game with one rank in [[Survival (Cun)|Survival]] or [[Deception (Cun)|Deception]]. They still may not train [[Survival (Cun)|Survival]] or [[Deception (Cun)|Deception]] above rank 2 during character creation.
- **Resilient Metabolism:** Devaronian physiology is naturally hardy and resistant to toxins. Members of the species add an automatic [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] result to all [[Resilience (Br)|Resilience]] checks they make.
