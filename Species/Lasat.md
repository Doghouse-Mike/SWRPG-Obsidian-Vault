- **Strain Threshold:** 10 + Willpower
- **Starting Experience:** 90 XP
- **Special Abilities:** Lasats begin the game with one rank in either [[Brawl (Br)|Brawl]] or [[Lore (Int)|Knowledge (Lore)]]. They still may not train either skill above rank 2 during character creation.
- **Prehensile Feet:** Lasats can move along a vertical surface as if the surface is horizontal.
