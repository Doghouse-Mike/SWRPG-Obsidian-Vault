- **Strain Threshold:** 10 + Willpower
- **Starting Experience:** 105 XP
- **Special Abilities:** Mandalorians begin the game with their choice of either one rank in a [[Category - Combat Skills|combat skill]] of their choice, or one rank each in two [[Category - Knowledge Skills|knowledge skills]] of their choice. They still may not train these skills above rank 2 during character creation.
