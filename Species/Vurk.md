- **Wound Threshold:** 11 + Brawn
- **Strain Threshold:** 11 + Willpower
- **Starting Experience:** 100 XP
- **Special Abilities:** Vurks begin the game with one rank in [[Negotiation (Pr)|Negotiation]]. They still may not train [[Negotiation (Pr)|Negotiation]] above rank 2 during character creation.
- **Amphibious:** Vurks can breathe underwater without penalty and never suffer movement penalties when traveling through water.
- **Cold Blooded:** Vurks add a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die to all checks they make while in cold environments (in addition to any other penalties).
