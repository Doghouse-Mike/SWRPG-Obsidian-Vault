- **Wound Threshold:** 10 + Brawn
- **Strain Threshold:** 10 + Willpower
- **Starting Experience:** 110 XP
- **Special Abilities:** Ganks begin the game with one rank in [[Coercion (Will)|Coercion]] or [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]]. They still may not train [[Coercion (Will)|Coercion]] or [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]] above rank 2 during character creation.
- **Cyborg:** All Ganks are cyborgs, and each one often posseses several different [[Category - Cybernetics|cybernetic]] implants. A Gank begins play with up to two cybernetics that cost a total of up to 5,000 credits. If a character takes on additional Obligation for credits at character creation, \[they\] may apply these extra funds to the 5,000 credit budget to purchase cybernetics. Ganks have a cybernetic implant cap of 3 plus Brawn rating.
