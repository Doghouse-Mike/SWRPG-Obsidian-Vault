Gaunt, ghoulish, and towering over most other sentient species of the galaxy, Pau'ans are easily recognizable by their intimidating presence.

- **Wound Threshold:** 12 + Brawn
- **Strain Threshold:** 8 + Willpower
- **Starting Experience:** 95 XP
- **Special Abilities:** Pau'ans begin the game with one rank in [[Negotiation (Pr)|Negotiation]] or [[Coercion (Will)|Coercion]]. They still cannot train [[Negotiation (Pr)|Negotiation]] or [[Coercion (Will)|Coercion]] above rank 2 during character creation.
- **Sensitive Hearing:** Pau'ans add a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to all [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] and [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]] checks they make as long as they are wearing their protective earpieces. If they are not wearing these earpieces, they do not gain the bonus, and they add a [[Narrative Dice#Threat|threat]] result to [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] and [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]] checks they make instead.
