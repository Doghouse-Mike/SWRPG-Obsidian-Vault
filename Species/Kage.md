The Kage are pale-skinned humanoids native to Qarzite. Because much of their world's surface is uninhabitable, the Kage are almost always in conflict with their fellow natives, the Belugans. The frequent wars have led to the Kage developing a strong warrior culture focused on unarmed combat and martial prowess..
