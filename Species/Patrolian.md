An amphibious people, most Patrolians prefer the aquatic comforts of their homeworld Patrolia to traveling the galaxy. This has given them an unfair reputation as criminals and thugs, as the only Patrolians desperate enough to leave their home's oceans are those who have fallen on the wrong side of the law.

- **Wound Threshold:** 9 + Brawn
- **Strain Threshold:** 11 + Willpower
- **Starting Experience:** 90 XP
- **Special Abilities:** Patrolians are smaller than average and count as Silhouette 0. Patrolians begin the game with one rank in either [[Computers (Int)|Computers]] or [[Skulduggery (Cun)|Skulduggery]]. They still may not train these skills above rank 2 during character creation.
- **Amphibious:** Patrolians can breathe underwater without penalty and never suffer movement penalties for traveling through water.
- **Slicer By Nature:** Patrolians being the game with one rank in the [[Bypass Security talent|Bypass Security]] talent.
