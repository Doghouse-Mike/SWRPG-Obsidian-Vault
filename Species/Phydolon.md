Only newly introduced to the galactic stage, their world having recently been discovered by galactic explorers, the Phydolons are an enigmatic species whose provincial ways seem quaint and even naive to the more jaded travelers they meet.

> More information about the species available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Phydolon).

- **Wound Threshold:** 10 + Brawn
- **Strain Threshold:** 10 + Willpower
- **Starting Experience:** 100 XP
- **Special Abilities:** Phydolons begin the game with one rank in [[Cool (Pr)|Cool]] or [[Discipline (Will)|Discipline]]. They still may not train [[Cool (Pr)|Cool]] or [[Discipline (Will)|Discipline]] above rank 2 during character creation.
- **Symbiotic Resilience:** When recovering wounds naturally, Phydolons heal 1 extra wound per night's rest. In addition, a Phydolon may attempt to recover naturally from one Critical Injury after a full night's rest, instead of after a full week.
- **Symbiont Isolation:** If no other Phydolons are present when a Phydolon recovers strain at the end of an encounter, they recover 1 less strain to a minimum of 1.
