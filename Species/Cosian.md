- **Wound Threshold:** 10 + Brawn
- **Strain Threshold:** 11 + Willpower
- **Starting Experience:** 95 XP
- **Special Abilities:** Cosians begin the game with one rank in [[Lore (Int)|Knowledge (Lore)]]. They still cannot train [[Lore (Int)|Knowledge (Lore)]] above rank 2 during character creation.
- **Strong Backed:** All Cosians have an encumbrance threshold of 10 + Brawn.
