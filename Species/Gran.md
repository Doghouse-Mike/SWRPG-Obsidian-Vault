- **Wound Threshold:** 10 + Brawn
- **Strain Threshold:** 9 + Willpower
- **Starting Experience:** 100 XP
- **Special Abilities:** Gran begin the game with one rank in [[Charm (Pr)|Charm]] or [[Negotiation (Pr)|Negotiation]]. They still may not train [[Charm (Pr)|Charm]] or [[Negotiation (Pr)|Negotiation]] above rank 2 in character creation.
- **Enhanced Vision:** When making ranged combat or [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] checks, Gran remove up to two [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice imposed due to environmental conditions or concealment (but not defense).
