- **Wound Threshold:** 10 + Brawn
- **Strain Threshold:** 13 + Willpower
- **Starting Experience:** 90 XP
- **Special Abilities:** Cereans begin the game with one rank in [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]]. They still may not train [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]] above rank 2 during character creation.
- **Binary Processing:** Cereans treat all [[Category - Knowledge Skills|Knowledge skills]] as career skills.
