- **Wound Threshold:** 8 + Brawn
- **Strain Threshold:** 12 + Willpower
- **Starting Experience:** 90 XP
- **Special Abilities:** Drall begin the game with one rank in [[Education (Int)|Knowledge (Education)]]. They still may not train [[Education (Int)|Knowledge (Education)]] above rank 2 during character creation. Drall have a mind for problems and solutions. In addition to using \[their\] skill or characteristic rating, a Drall adds a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to the dice pool when providing skilled assistance.
