- **Wound Threshold:** 8 + Brawn
- **Strain Threshold:** 8 + Willpower
- **Starting Experience:** 120 XP
- **Special Abilities:** Jawas begin the game with one rank in [[Negotiation (Pr)|Negotiation]] or [[Skulduggery (Cun)|Skulduggery]]. They still may not train [[Negotiation (Pr)|Negotiation]] or [[Skulduggery (Cun)|Skulduggery]] above rank 2 during character creation.
- **Clever Negotiators:** Jawas may use Cunning instead of Presence when making [[Negotiation (Pr)|Negotiation]] checks.
