> The harch is an upright arachnid-like humanoid with many arms, this one presenting a white fur and red eyes.

- **Wound Threshold:** 11 + Brawn
- **Strain Threshold:** 11 + Willpower
- **Starting Experience:** 75 XP
- **Special Abilities:** Harches begin the game with one rank in [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]]. They still may not train this skill above rank 2 during character creation.
- **Additional Limbs:** Harches have multiple sets of arms. As a result, they gain an additional free maneuver per turn, though still may not perform more than two maneuvers per turn.
- **Venomous Fangs:** When a Harch makes a [[Brawl (Br)|Brawl]] check to deal damage to an opponent, the attack gains the [[Item Qualities#Stun (Active)|Stun]] 3 item quality.
