Hailing from the ocean planet Manaan, the Selkath are a peaceful people with a keen proclivity for the sciences. In centuries past, the Selkath wielded considerable influence as the producers of the healing substance known as kolto, but since the rise of bacta their impact on the galactic stage has lessened considerably.
