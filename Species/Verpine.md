The Verpine are a hermaphroditic insectoid species whose affinity for technology is recognized far and wide.

- **Wound Threshold:** 9 + Brawn
- **Strain Threshold:** 12 + Willpower
- **Starting Experience:** 80 XP
- **Special Abilities:** Verpine begin the game with one rank in [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics]]. They still may not train [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics]] above rank 2 during character creation.
- **Microvision:** The compound eyes of a Verpine are capable of seeing tiny, even microscopic, details. While closely examining an object, Verpine characters add a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to their [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] checks.
- **Radio-wave Communication:** Verpine can produce and hear radio waves, which allows them to silently communicate with other Verpine and specially tuned comlinks within a 100-kilometer range.
