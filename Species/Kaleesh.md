A fierce species, the Kaleesh have long been feared by other worlds. Their culture revolves around war, as they compete to perform great deeds in battle. This devotion to war even extends to their appearance, as Kaleesh are rarely seen without the tribal masks that identify their clan, bloodline and historical victories.

> More information about the species available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kaleesh).
