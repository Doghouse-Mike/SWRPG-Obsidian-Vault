The Miraluka are a near-human species with a strong natural connection to the force. They are born without eyes, yet this connection allows them to still navigate and perceive objects and people around them.

**Physiology:** Miraluka are anatomically very similar to humans, sharing typical body types and appearances, though with a slightly lower lifespan. They lack eyes, with skin covering the vestigial eye-sockets of their skull. As a result, they recognize their surroundings through their connection to the force, which allows them to “see”, though not in a manner comparable to humans. Any Miraluka is force sensitive to a degree, though not all of them chose to follow a path that has them capitalize on this connection.

**Society:** : Miraluka are thoughtful, cautious, and deliberative species. Most of them show little interest in personal gain or glory, believing in strength in unity and living as community instead. They commonly refer to other Miraluka as “sister” or “brother”, treating any of their species as parts of a large family - a gesture rarely extended to outsiders as well. For social reasons, Miraluka often hide their unusual appearance by covering their missing eyes with a blindfold, hood, helmet or other headwear.

Their natural receptiveness to the force, while a powerful ally, also leaves Miraluka open to emotional distress. Strong events in the force, such as the destruction of civilizations or planets could be felt by most members of their species. Additionally, encountering a lifeform not connected to the force can prove rather shocking and confusing to Miraluka, as they are unable to sense and perceive them as they normally would.

Miralukan religion is founded in the ancient philosophies of the Greater Force. Those dedicated to this belief worship Ashla and Bogan, deities representing the light and dark side of the force, though they interpret them not as good and evil, but rather life and death, constants that must be kept in balance for the sake of the galaxy.eyes.

**Homeworld:** The Miraluka have made their home on Alpheridies, adopting this world after their original homeworld was rendered uninhabitable by its depleted atmosphere. With its position not near any of the central trade routes, the Miraluka were mostly undisturbed until the Mandlorians invaded their world during the Great Sith War. They were freed from their conquerors by the Republic, after which a Jedi academy was erected on the world to test and train the many Miraluka gifted in the Force. In the following conflicts, the world has switched allegiance many times, often as a result of military invasion by the Sith Empire and ensuing liberation by the Republic.

**Language:** Though the native tongue of Miralukese exists, all Miraluka children grow up learning basic. With quite a few of them being considered for the Jedi academy and subsequent service in the order, this bilingual upbringing is helpful in integrating into the galactic society more easily.

**Life during the War:** Due to the abrupt way the Miraluka first entered into the conflicts between Jedi and Sith, and the subsequent fights over their homeworld, they have more often found themselves drawn to the side of the Republic. Most of them perceive the Jedi as liberators of their world from its various cases of occupation, and the ability to join the order is a driving goal for many young Miraluka looking to explore the galaxy. Never the less, their natural Force-sensitivity also makes them susceptible to the temptation of the dark side.

- **Wound Threshold:** 9 + Brawn
- **Strain Threshold:** 10 + Willpower
- **Starting Experience:** 100 XP
- **Special Abilities:** Miraluka begin the game with one rank in [[Discipline (Will)|Discipline]]. They still may not train [[Discipline (Will)|Discipline]] above rank 2 during character creation.
- **Force Sight:** Miraluka are blind from birth but rely on the Force to perceive their environment. They remove any [[Narrative Dice#Setback|setback]] die added to their checks as a result of smoke, darkness or concealment, but add two [[Narrative Dice#Setback|setback]] dice to any checks that require precise investigation. They may never benefit from equipment that relies on the user's vision. Should a Miraluka become disconnected from the Force, they lose any benefits of Force Sight and incur all penalties of the "Blinded" Critical Injury.
