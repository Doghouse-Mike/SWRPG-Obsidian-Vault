### KAJAIN'SA'NIKTOS (RED NIKTOS)

Having evolved in the blasted expanse of the Endless Wastes, Red Niktos are adapted for this arid environment.

**Special Abilities:** Kajain'sa'Niktos begin the game with one rank in [[Resilience (Br)|Resilience]]. They still may not train [[Resilience (Br)|Resilience]] above rank 2 during character creation. When making skill checks, Red Niktos may remove a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die imposed due to arid or hot environmental conditions.

### KADAS'SA'NIKTOS (GREEN NIKTOS)

Originating in the forests and temperate coastal regions of Kintan, the Kadas'sa'Niktos have scaly green skin and a fringe of bony ridges terminating in small horns around the eyes and chin.

**Special Abilities:** Kadas'sa'Niktos begin the game with one rank in [[Coordination (Ag)|Coordination]]. They still may not train [[Coordination (Ag)|Coordination]] above rank 2 during character creation. They also gain a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to [[Athletics (Br)|Athletics]] checks made to climb trees and other surfaces their claws can pierce.

**Claws:** When a Green Nikto makes [[Brawl (Br)|Brawl]] checks to deal damage to an opponent, \[they\] deal +1 damage and have a Critical Rating of 3.

### ESRAL'SA'NIKTOS (MOUNTAIN NIKTOS)

The bluish-gray Esral'sa'Niktos, or Mountain Niktos, evolved in the mountainous hinterlands of Kintan.

**Special Abilities:** Esral'sa'Niktos begin the game with one rank in [[Survival (Cun)|Survival]]. They still may not train [[Survival (Cun)|Survival]] above rank 2 during character creation. They also gain the [[Natural Outdoorsman talent]] (once per game session, a Mountain Nikto may reroll any one [[Resilience (Br)|Resilience]] or [[Survival (Cun)|Survival]] check).

### GLUSS'SA'NIKTOS (PALE NIKTOS)

Native to the rocky Gluss'elta Islands of their homeworld, Gluss'sa'Niktos, or Pale Niktos, have white-gray skin, tiny horns around their eyes, and small facial fins.

**Special Abilities:** Gluss'sa'Niktos begin the game with one rank in [[Athletics (Br)|Athletics]]. They still may not train [[Athletics (Br)|Athletics]] above rank 2 during character creation.

**Swimmer:** A Pale Nikto never suffers movement penalties for traveling through water and can hold \[their\] breath for a number of rounds equal to twice \[their\] Brawn rating before beginning to drown.

### M'SHENTO'SU'NIKTOS (SOUTHERN NIKTOS)

The Southern Niktos have cream, white, yellow, or orange skin and lack the horns and prominent facial ridges common to other Niktos.

**Special Abilities:** M'shento'su'Niktos begin the game with one rank in [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]]. They still may not train [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] above rank 2 during character creation. Southern Niktos may add a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] and [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]] checks to detect sounds.
