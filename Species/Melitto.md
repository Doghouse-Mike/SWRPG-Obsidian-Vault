Melittos are insectile, chitin-plated bipeds who hail from the volcanic Inner Rim planet of Li-Toran.

> More information about the species available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Melitto).

- **Wound Threshold:** 9 + Brawn
- **Strain Threshold:** 11 + Willpower
- **Starting Experience:** 100 XP
- **Special Abilities:** Melittos begin the game with one rank in [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]]. They still may not train [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] above rank 2 during character creation.
- **Sightless Vision:** Melittos can perceive their surroundings without needing light, and they never suffer penalties on combat or [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] checks due to darkness or other environmental factors that affect vision.
- **Reliant on Toxins:** Melittos require the toxins of their native atmosphere to live; offworld, they gain free special respirators to emulate the conditions of their homeworld. They treat standard planetary air composition as a corrosive atmosphere with rating 6.
