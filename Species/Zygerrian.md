A proud species that once ran a galactic slaving empire, the entirety of Zygerrian society revolves around the restoration of the slave trade and their rightful place at its head, though some members of the species spurn this view. These rogues often find themselves exiled, in line with the Zygerrian principle of survival of the fittest.

> More information about the species available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zygerrian).
