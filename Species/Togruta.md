A carnivorous humanoid species from the planet Shili, Togrutas are easily recognized by the combination of their head-tails and hornlike montrals.

> More information about the species available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Togruta).

- **Wound Threshold:** 10 + Brawn
- **Strain Threshold:** 10 + Willpower
- **Starting Experience:** 100 XP
- **Special Abilities:** Togrutas begin the game with one rank in [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]]. They still may not train [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] above rank 2 during character creation.
- **Pack Instincts:** When performing the assist maneuver, Togrutas grant two [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] dice instead of one [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die.
