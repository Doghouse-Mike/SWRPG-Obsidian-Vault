- **Wound Threshold:** 13 + Brawn
- **Strain Threshold:** 11 + Willpower
- **Starting Experience:** 70 XP
- **Special Abilities:** Hutts begin the game with one rank in the [[Enduring talent]] and one rank in the [[Nobody's Fool talent|Nobody's Fool talents]]. Hutts begin the game with one rank in [[Coercion (Will)|Coercion]] or [[Discipline (Will)|Discipline]]. They still may not train [[Coercion (Will)|Coercion]] or [[Discipline (Will)|Discipline]] above rank 2 during character creation.
- **Ponderous:** A Hutt can never spend more than one maneuver moving per turn.
