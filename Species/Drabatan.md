Music-obsessed amphibious humanoids, Drabatans come from the freshwater swamps of Pipada.

> More information about the species available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Drabatan).

- **Wound Threshold:** 10 + Brawn
- **Strain Threshold:** 10 + Willpower
- **Starting Experience:** 100 XP
- **Special Abilities:** Drabatans begin the game with one rank in either [[Charm (Pr)|Charm]] or [[Leadership (Pr)|Leadership]]. They still may not train either skill above rank 2 during character creation.
- **Big Noise:** When making a [[Leadership (Pr)|Leadership]] or [[Coercion (Will)|Coercion]] check, Drabatans can spend two [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results to affect one additional character not originally targeted by the check or affect targets at one range band further than normal.
