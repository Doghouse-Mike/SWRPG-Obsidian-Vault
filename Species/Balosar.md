- **Wound Threshold:** 10 + Brawn
- **Strain Threshold:** 10 + Willpower
- **Starting Experience:** 100 XP
- **Special Abilities:** Balosars begin the game with one free ranks of [[Streetwise (Cun)|Streetwise]]. They still may not train [[Streetwise (Cun)|Streetwise]] above rank 2 during character creation.
- **Antennapalps:** Balosars add two automatic [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] results to [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]] checks they make.
