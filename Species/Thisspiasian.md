- **Wound Threshold:** 10 + Brawn
- **Strain Threshold:** 8 + Willpower
- **Starting Experience:** 100 XP
- **Special Abilities:** Thisspiasians begin the game with one rank in [[Discipline (Will)|Discipline]]. They still may not train [[Discipline (Will)|Discipline]] above rank 2 during character creation.
- **Rest and Meditation:** Thisspiassians need very little sleep to function. They only need to sleep for two hours to heal all strain they are suffering from, instead of for a full night sleep (they cannot sleep multiple times a day however!). In addition, once per session Thisspiasians may spend ten minutes meditating; if they do so, they heal strain equal to twice their ranks in [[Discipline (Will)|Discipline]].
