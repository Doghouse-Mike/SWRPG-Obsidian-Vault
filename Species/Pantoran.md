These blue-skinned near-humans come from a cold climate and were vibrant participants in galactic society during the time of the Republic.

> More information about the species available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pantoran).

- **Wound Threshold:** 10 + Brawn
- **Strain Threshold:** 11 + Willpower
- **Starting Experience:** 110 XP
- **Special Abilities:** Pantorans begin the game with one rank in either [[Cool (Pr)|Cool]] or [[Negotiation (Pr)|Negotiation]]. They still may not train [[Cool (Pr)|Cool]] or [[Negotiation (Pr)|Negotiation]] above rank 2 during character creation.
- **Tundra Dwellers:** When making skill checks, Pantorans may remove a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die imposed due to cold environmental conditions.
