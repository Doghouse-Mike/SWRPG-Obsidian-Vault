Best known as a kindly and soft-spoken species, Kel Dors are most easily identified by the protective eyewear and rebreather masks that they must wear outside their native environments.

> More information about the species available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kel_Dor).

- **Wound Threshold:** 10 + Brawn
- **Strain Threshold:** 10 + Willpower
- **Starting Experience:** 100 XP
- **Special Abilities:** Kel Dors begin the game with one rank in [[Education (Int)|Knowledge (Education)]]. They still may not train [[Education (Int)|Knowledge (Education)]] above rank 2 during character creation.
- **Dark Vision:** When making skill checks, Kel Dors remove up to two [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice imposed due to darkness.
- **Atmospheric Requirement:** Kel Dors must wear a specialized mask to breathe and see outside of their native atmosphere. A Kel Dor character starts the game with an antitox breath mask and treats oxygen as a dangerous atmosphere with Rating 8. However, Kel Dors may survive in vacuum for up to five minutes before suffering its effects.
