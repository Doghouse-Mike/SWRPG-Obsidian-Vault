Sometimes referred to by the nickname "Hammerheads" (although not by choice), Ithorians are easily recognizable thanks to their unusual head structure and vocal apparatus.

> More information about the species available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ithorian).

- **Wound Threshold:** 9 + Brawn
- **Strain Threshold:** 12 + Willpower
- **Starting Experience:** 90 XP
- **Special Abilities:** Ithorians begin the game with one rank in [[Survival (Cun)|Survival]]. They still may not train [[Survival (Cun)|Survival]] above rank 2 during character creation.
- **Ithorian Bellow:** With two mouths and four throats, Ithorians have a unique natural weapon they can call upon when threatened ([[Resilience (Br)|Resilience]]; Damage 6; Critical 4; Range \[Short\]; [[Item Qualities#Blast (Active)|Blast]] 3, [[Item Qualities#Concussive (Active)|Concussive]] 1, [[Item Qualities#Slow-Firing (Passive)|Slow-Firing]] 2, [[Item Qualities#Stun Damage (Passive)|Stun Damage]]). Each time they use this ability, they suffer 3 strain.
