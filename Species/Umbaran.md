Umbarans are near-humans with pale skin that has a faint tint of blue or purple to it.

> More information about the species available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Umbaran).

- **Wound Threshold:** 10 + Brawn
- **Strain Threshold:** 10 + Willpower
- **Starting Experience:** 100 XP
- **Special Abilities:** Umbarans begin the game with one rank in [[Deception (Cun)|Deception]]. They still may not train [[Deception (Cun)|Deception]] above rank 2 during character creation.
- **Entrancing Gaze:** Umbarans add a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to [[Charm (Pr)|Charm]], [[Deception (Cun)|Deception]], and [[Negotiation (Pr)|Negotiation]] checks.
- **Shadow Dweller:** When making skill checks, Umbarans remove up to two [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice imposed due to darkness. Umbarans add a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die to all checks they make while in bright natural light, such as direct sunlight.
