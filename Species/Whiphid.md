- **Wound Threshold:** 12 + Brawn
- **Strain Threshold:** 10 + Willpower
- **Starting Experience:** 80 XP
- **Special Abilities:** Whiphids begin the game with one rank in [[Survival (Cun)|Survival]]. They still may not train [[Survival (Cun)|Survival]] above rank 2 during character creation.
- **Hardy Survivalist:** A Whiphid adds an automatic [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] to [[Survival (Cun)|Survival]] checks and can survive for several weeks without food thanks to reserve blubber.
- **Tusks:** When a Whiphid makes a [[Brawl (Br)|Brawl]] check to deal damage to an opponent, \[they\] deal +2 damage, has a Critical Rating of 4, and the [[Item Qualities#Vicious (Passive)|Vicious]] 1 quality.
