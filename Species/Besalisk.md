The four-armed Besalisks are one of the larger species galactic travelers are likely to encounter on a semi-regular basis.

> More information about the species available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Besalisk).

- **Wound Threshold:** 12 + Brawn
- **Strain Threshold:** 7 + Willpower
- **Starting Experience:** 85 XP
- **Special Abilities:** Besalisks begin the game with one rank in [[Resilience (Br)|Resilience]]. They still may not train [[Resilience (Br)|Resilience]] above rank 2 during character creation.
- **Additional Limbs:** Besalisks have multiple sets of arms. As a result, they gain an additional free maneuver per turn, though still may not perform more than two maneuvers per turn.
