- **Wound Threshold:** 12 + Brawn
- **Strain Threshold:** 10 + Willpower
- **Starting Experience:** 100 XP
- **Special Abilities:** Gigorans begin the game with one rank in [[Brawl (Br)|Brawl]]. They still may not train [[Brawl (Br)|Brawl]] above rank 2 during character creations.
- **Adapted to the Cold:** Gigorans remove a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die imposed due to cold or snow-filled conditions.
- **Low-Tech:** Gigorans upgrade the difficulty of [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics]] checks involving advanced technology once.
