Mikkians are a diverse species characterized by a variety of bright skin tones and elaborate crests of head-tendrils.

> More information about the species available on the [Wookieepedia article](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mikkian).

- **Wound Threshold:** 10 + Brawn
- **Strain Threshold:** 11 + Willpower
- **Starting Experience:** 100 XP
- **Special Abilities:** Mikkians begin the game with one rank in [[Discipline (Will)|Discipline]]. They still may not train [[Discipline (Will)|Discipline]] above rank 2 during character creation.
- **Sensory Tendrils:** Mikkians add a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to all [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] checks they make.
