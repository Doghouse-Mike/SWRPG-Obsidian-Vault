- **Wound Threshold:** 9 + Brawn
- **Strain Threshold:** 11 + Willpower
- **Starting Experience:** 90 XP
- **Special Abilities:** Chadra-Fan are smaller than average and count as silhouette 0. Chadra-Fan begin the game with one rank in [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics]]. They still may not train [[Mechanics (Int)|Mechanics]] above rank 2 during character creation.
- **Acute Senses:** Chadra-Fan hearing, vision, and olfactory senses are all far superior to most humanoids. They remove up to two [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] dice from their [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] checks.
