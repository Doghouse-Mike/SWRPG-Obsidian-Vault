Enhance power tree

Those strong in the Force can allow it to flow through them, responding to their own intent and movement in order to enhance the actions of their body.

> More information can be found on page 46 of **[[Category - Clone Wars Begin|Rise of the Separatists]]:** *An Era Sourcebook* or on page 288 of **[[Category - Way of the Force|*Star Wars* Force and Destiny]]** Roleplaying Game: *Core Rulebook* or on page 298 of ***[[Category - Rebel Ranks|Star Wars]]*** **[[Category - Rebel Ranks|Age of Rebellion]]** Roleplaying Game: *Core Rulebook*. In the **Age of Rebellion** setting, replace all instances of [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]] (◑) with [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Light point]].

### BASIC POWER

Enhance allows Force users to perform athletic feats beyond the original scope of their natural abilities. The basic power has one way of spending Force points:

- When making an [[Athletics (Br)|Athletics]] check, the user may roll an **Enhance power check** as part of the pool. The user may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]] to gain a [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] or [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result (their choice) on the check. (Remember, this counts as a normal Force power check in every way—it is simply combined with the overall skill check.)

### UPGRADES

**Control Upgrade:** The user gains the ability to use Enhance with the [[Coordination (Ag)|Coordination]] skill.

**Control Upgrade:** Enhance can be used with the [[Piloting - Planetary (Ag)|Piloting (Planetary)]] skill.

**Control Upgrade:** Enhance can be used with the [[Piloting - Space (Ag)|Piloting (Space)]] skill.

**Control Upgrade:** This power gains the ongoing effect: Commit a [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] die. The Force user increases their [[Agility]] characteristic by 1 (to a maximum of 6).

**Control Upgrade:** Enhance can be used with the [[Resilience (Br)|Resilience]] skill.

**Control Upgrade:** Enhance can be used with the [[Brawl (Br)|Brawl]] skill.

**Control Upgrade:** This power gains the ongoing effect: Commit a [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] die. The Force user increases their [[Brawn]] characteristic by 1 (to a maximum of 6).

**Control Upgrade:** The user can take a Force Leap action to make an **Enhance power check**. They may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]] to jump horizontally to any location within short range. They can use this to leap over obstacles or impassible terrain but cannot leap directly vertically. The user may not activate this multiple times.

**Control Upgrade:** When taking a Force leap action, the user can leap to any location in range vertically as well as horizontally.

**Control Upgrade:** The user only needs to use a maneuver to perform a Force Leap, instead of an action.

**Range Upgrade:** Spend a [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]] to increase the maximum range the user can jump by the number of Range upgrades purchased. The user may not activate this multiple times. Remember that the user must still spend Force points to activate the power's actual effects.
