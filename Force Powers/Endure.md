Endure power tree

Those strong in the Force, and who learn to control that gift, can achieve great, even seemingly impossible feats of physical prowess.

> More information can be found on page 36 of **[[Category - Warrior|Knights of Fate]]:** *A Sourcebook for Warriors*.

### BASIC POWER

The basic power of Endure allows a Force user to temporarily ignore the effects of injury. The basic power has one way to commit Force dice:

- When the character suffers a Critical Injury with a severity no greater than **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Easy difficulty]]**, the character may activate Endure as an out-of-turn incidental, committing a [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] die. While the [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] die remains committed, the character ignores the effects of that Critical Injury. This means they do not apply any results from the Critical Injury or add +10 to further rolls on the Critical Injury Result table while the [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] die remains committed. (The injury can still be healed.) When this ongoing effect ends, the character suffers all effects of the Critical Injury (unless it has been treated).

### UPGRADES

**Control Upgrade:** The character can commit additional [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] dice to temporarily ignore one additional Critical Injury per Control upgrade purchased.

**Duration Upgrade:** As an incidental, the character can activate Endure to affect a Critical Injury the character is already suffering. The character commits a [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] die and ignores all effects of the Critical Injury while the [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] die remains committed, just as if the power were activated upon first receiving the injury. The Critical Injury must be of a severity that Endure could affect normally.

**Magnitude Upgrade:** The character can use Endure to affect allies at short range. The character must commit a [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] die as normal, and the Critical Injury must be of a severity that Endure could affect normally. The ongoing effect ends if the character stops committing a [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] die or if the distance between the character and the ally increases beyond short range for any reason.

**Mastery Upgrade:** When the character activates Endure, they may make an Endure power check instead of committing a [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] die as normal. An Endure power check is a [[Discipline (Will)|Discipline]] check with a difficulty equal to the severity of the Critical Injury, to which the character may add [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] dice up to their Force rating. If the character succeeds on the check and generates [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|points]] equal to the number of [[Narrative Dice#Difficulty (Purple)|difficulty]] dice in the severity of the Critical Injury, the Critical Injury is not suffered.

An Endure power check cannot prevent a Critical Injury result of Dead.

**Range Upgrade:** The range at which the character can use Endure to affect allies increases to medium. The ongoing effect ends if the distance between characters increases beyond medium for any reason.

**Strength Upgrade:** The character increases the severity of Critical Injuries they can affect using Endure by one per Strength upgrade purchased.

**Strength Upgrade:** The character can use Endure to affect a Critical Injury of Dead until the end of the current encounter (or until they stop committing a [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] die). A Critical Injury of Dead cannot be treated or healed.
