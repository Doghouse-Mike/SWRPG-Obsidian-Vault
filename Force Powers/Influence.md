Influence power tree

The ability to influence the minds of others is not something to be taken lightly.

> More information is available in **[[Category - Way of the Force|*Star Wars* Force and Destiny]]** Roleplaying Game: *Core Rulebook* (page 294) or **[[Category - On the Fringe|*Star Wars* Edge of the Empire]]** Roleplaying Game: *Core Rulebook* (page 281). In the **Edge of the Empire** setting, replace all instances of [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]] (◑) with [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Light point]].

### BASIC POWER

The most basic form of Influence does not allow the Force user to guide or shape the thoughts of others. \[They\] can merely strain their mind, inflicting stress and exhaustion. The basic power has one effect that can be triggered multiple times on the same or different targets:

- The user spends a [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]] (◑) to stress the mind of one living target \[they\] are engaged with, inflicting 1 strain. The user may activate this multiple times, increasing the strain inflicted by one each time.

### UPGRADES

**Control Upgrade:** To gain the ability to alter the thoughts and emotions of a living target with whom \[they\] are engaged, the user makes an **[[Discipline (Will)|opposed Discipline check]]** against the target as part of the pool to activate the power. The user must spend a [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]] (◑) *and* \[they\] must succeed on the check to force the target to adopt an emotional state such as fear, friendliness, or hatred, or to believe something untrue ("these are not the droids you are looking for"). The effect lasts for roughly five minutes, or one round of combat. If the Force user has the ability to affect multiple minds with this power (such as with the Magnitude upgrade), the [[Discipline (Will)|Discipline]] check either must be opposed by the mind with the highest ranks in [[Discipline (Will)|Discipline]], or its difficulty must be set statically based on the number of minds (at the GM's discretion).

**Control Upgrade:** The user gains the ability to enhance \[their\] arguments and charisma via the Force. When making a Coercion, Charm, Deception, Leadership, or Negotiation check, the user may roll an **Influence power check** as part of the pool. \[They\] may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]] (◑) to gain a [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] result or [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result (their choice) per point on the check.

**Strength Upgrade:** When stressing the mind of a target, the Force user inflicts 2 strain instead of 1.

**Duration Upgrade:** The user may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]] (◑) to increase the time this effect lasts by a number of minutes or rounds in combat equal to the number of Duration upgrades purchased. The user may activate this multiple times, increasing the duration multiple times.

**Range Upgrade:** The user may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]] (◑) to increase the range at which the character can tough minds by a number of range bands equal to the number of Range upgrades purchased. The user may activate this multiple times, increasing the range by this number each time.

**Magnitude Upgrade:** The user may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]] (◑) to increase the number of minds being affected equal to the number of Magnitude upgrades purchased. The user may activate this multiple times, increasing the number of targets multiple times.

### INFLUENCE SPECIAL RULE

When Influence is used to guide and shape thoughts and emotions, checks using a [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|Dark point]] to generate [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|points]] (◑) may be used to create negative emotions such as rage, fear, and hatred, but may not be used to evoke positive emotions. Checks using a [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Light point]] to generate [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|points]] (◑) may create positive emotions such as peace, tranquility, and friendliness, but may not be used to generate negative emotions. More neutral emotions such as confusion can be elicited regardless of whether the check used [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Light points]] or [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|Dark points]] to generate [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|points]] (◑). This means that to generate negative emotions, the average player aligned with the light side must first roll a [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|Dark point]] result, then flip a Destiny Point and suffer strain to use it to generate at least one [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]] (◑) to spend.
