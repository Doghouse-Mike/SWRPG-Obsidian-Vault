Imbue power tree

Those who count the Force as their ally can use the Force to imbue their allies in turn.

> More information can be found on page 36 of **[[Category - Consular|Disciples of Harmony]]:** *A Sourcebook for Consulars*.

**Prerequisites:** Force Rating 2 +

### BASIC POWER

The basic power of Imbue can be used in one way: to boost the characteristics of one nearby ally. The basic power has one way to spend Force points:

- The user may spend two [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|points]] to increase one of another engaged character's characteristics by 1 (to a maximum of 6) until the end of the Force user's next turn. This can only be used once per target per encounter. If the user used a [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|dark point]] to generate a [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]], the target increases a second characteristic by 1 (to a maximum of 6) until the end of the user's next turn, but both the Force user and the target suffer 3 strain.

### UPGRADES

**Control:** The Force user may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]] to allow the target to count as having ranks in a single skill equal to the ranks the Force user has in that skill while the target is affected by this power. The skill must be linked to the characteristic improved by this power.

**Duration:** After using this power, the Force user may commit two [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] dice to sustain the effects of this power as long as the target remains in range.

**Duration:** After using this power, the Force user may commit a [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] die to sustain the effects of this power as long as the target remains in range (instead of two [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] dice).

**Mastery:** The Force user increases characteristics boosted by this power by 2 (to a maximum of 7) instead of 1 (to a maximum of 6).

**Range:** The Force user may spend two [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|points]] to increase the range of this power by a number of range bands equal to the number of Range upgrades purchased. The Force user may activate this multiple times.

**Strength:** If no [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|dark points]] were used to generate a [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]], the Force user decreases all Critical Injuries that the target suffers and the target inflicts by 10 per Strength upgrade purchased. If no [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|light points]] were used to generate a [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]], the Force user increases all Critical Injuries that the target suffers and the target inflicts by 10 per Strength upgrade purchased.
