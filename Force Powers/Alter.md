Alter power tree

Many Force users have the ability to sense the life force of the environment around them.

> More information can be found on page 42 of **[[Category - Mystic|Unlimited Power]]:** *A Sourcebook for Mystics*.

### BASIC POWER

The basic power of Alter allows the user to tap into the Living Force of their surroundings, manipulating the nearby environs. The basic power has two uses, both of which can only be used in natural terrain that can change form easily, such as dirt, sand, mud, and thick vegetation.

- The Force user may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]] to make all terrain currently within short range difficult terrain until the end of their next turn. The Force user must be touching the ground to manifest this power. The user may not activate this multiple times.
- The Force user may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]] to make all terrain currently within short range normal terrain until the end of their next turn. The Force user must be touching the ground to manifest this power. The user may not activate this multiple times.

### UPGRADES

**Control Upgrade:** The Force user may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]] to make an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] [[Survival (Cun)|Survival check]]**. Success allows the Force user to locate food, water, and other critical supplies within extreme range. In some cases, the GM may determine that there are no such supplies available (for example, if the Force user is on the surface of an airless moon). The user may not activate this multiple times.

**Control Upgrade:** The Force user may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]] to give all characters within short range of the Force user concealment, adding a [[Narrative Dice#Setback (Black)|setback]] die or [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to appropriate checks. This lasts for the duration of the power. The user may not activate this multiple times.

**Control Upgrade:** The Force user may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]] to share the senses of non-sentient animals and similar creatures within range of their power. The Force user adds a [[Narrative Dice#Boost (Blue)|boost]] die to [[Perception (Cun)|Perception]] and [[Vigilance (Will)|Vigilance]] checks, and at the GM's discretion, may be able to learn or notice things they could not normally perceive. This lasts for the duration of the power. The user may not activate this multiple times.

**Control Upgrade:** This power can affect firm natural terrain, such as packed earth, stone, or ice.

**Control Upgrade:** The Force user may spend two [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|points]] to make a small patch of terrain within the power's area of effect impassable terrain. This patch is no more than 3 meters across. The nature of the impassible terrain is up to the Force user; it could be a mass of solid rock, an impenetrable morass of twisting underbrush, or a bubbling pit of steam and superheated water. This lasts for the duration of the power. The user may activate this multiple times, choosing a new patch of terrain to affect each time.

**Control Upgrade:** The Force user may spend two [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|points]] to create a corrosive atmosphere in a small area within the power's area of effect. This area is no more than 3 meters across. The rating of the corrosive atmosphere is equal to the user's current Force rating. This lasts for the duration of the power. The user may activate this multiple times, choosing a new patch of terrain to affect each time.

**Control Upgrade:** The Force user may spend two [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|points]] to upgrade the difficulty of all checks made by opponents while in the power's area of effect once. This lasts for the duration of the power.

**Duration Upgrade:** The power gains the ongoing effect: Commit a [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] die after successfully activating the basic power to sustain its effects as long as the user remains in the area affected.

**Range Upgrade:** The Force user may spend two [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|points]] to increase the range of the affected area by a number of range bands equal to Range upgrades purchased. The Force user may activate this multiple times, increasing the range by this number each time.

**Mastery Upgrade:** When the user activates this power without spending [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|points]] generated from [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|dark point]] results, add a [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|light point]] to all other Force power checks made within this power's area of effect for the duration of the power.

When the user activates this power without spending [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|points]] generated from [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|light point]] results, add a [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|dark point]] to all other Force power checks made within this power's area of effect for the duration of the power.

**Strength Upgrade:** Spend a [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]] to select a number of targets equal to the number of Strength upgrades purchased; these targets ignore the power's effects. The Force user may activate this multiple times, increasing the number of targets that can ignore this power's effects by this number each time.
