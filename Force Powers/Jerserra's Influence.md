Jerserra's Influence power tree

One of the more infamous uses of the Force is the manipulate of emotional states.

> More information can be found on page 12 of **[[Category - Story - Ghosts|Ghosts of Dathomir]]:** *Adventure Module*.

**Prerequisites:** Force Rating 2 +

### BASIC POWER

Jerserra combined her Inquisitor's strict training with her guidance from her shard of the Nardithi Cluster and found a way to project fear onto her foes. Other Force users, though encountering Jerserra and her power, can learn to do the same.

- The user may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]] to target one character within short range. That character must immediately make an **[[Narrative Dice#Standard Difficulties|Average difficulty]] fear check** as an out of turn incidental. The user may not activate this multiple times.

### UPGRADES

**Control Upgrade:** When making a fear check, the Force user can roll a **Jerserra's Influence power check** as part of the dice pool. The user may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]] to gain a [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] result or [[Narrative Dice#Advantage|advantage]] result (user's choice) on the check.

**Control Upgrade:** Ongoing effect: Commit a [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] die. The Force user increases \[their\] Willpower characteristic by 1 (to a maximum of 6).

**Control Upgrade:** If the target suffers strain as a result of Jerserra's Influence, the Force user recovers an equal amount of strain.

**Mastery Upgrade:** The Force user may target one character within short range and make an **[[Discipline (Will)|opposed Discipline vs. Discipline check]]** combined with a **Jerserra's Influence power check**. If the user spends a [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]] and succeeds on the check, \[they\] may commit a [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] die and force the target to also commit a [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] die. Both dice remain committed until the Force user uncommits that [[Narrative Dice#Force (White)|Force]] die or the encounter ends.

**Mastery Upgrade:** Once per session, after a target fails a fear check caused by this power, the Force user may have the target perform one action of the Force user's choice as an out of turn incidental. The Force user gains 3 Conflict.

**Range Upgrade:** The Force user may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]] to increase the power's range by a number of range bands equal to Range upgrades purchased. This cannot be activated multiple times.

**Magnitude Upgrade:** The Force user may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]] to increase the number of targets affected by a number equal to Magnitude upgrades purchased. This cannot be activated multiple times.

**Strength Upgrade:** The Force user may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]] to increase the difficulty of the fear check by a number of levels equal to Strength upgrades purchased. This cannot be activated multiple times.
