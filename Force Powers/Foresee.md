Foresee power tree

Force users often have precognitive flashes, experiencing waking dreams or visions about people and situations through their abilities.

> More information is available in **[[Category - Way of the Force|*Star Wars* Force and Destiny]]** Roleplaying Game: *Core Rulebook* (page 290) or **[[Category - Rebel Ranks|*Star Wars* Age of Rebellion]]** Roleplaying Game: *Core Rulebook* (page 300). In the **Age of Rebellion** setting, replace all instances of [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]] (◑) with [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Light point]].

### BASIC POWER

The most basic form of Foresee allows the character to look into the Force to see vague hints of \[their\] near future. These hints may be blurry visual images, brief samples of sound, or simple emotions. The basic power has one way of spending Force points:

- The user may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]] (◑) to gain vague hints of events to come in the next day of \[their\] own, personal future. The basic power cannot see further than one day.

### UPGRADES

**Control Upgrade:** When making a skill check to determine Initiative, the user may roll a **Foresee power check** as part of the pool. \[They\] may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]] (◑) to gain a [[Narrative Dice#Success|success]] result on \[their\] check, and may activate this multiple times.

**Control Upgrade:** When performing a Foresee power check as part of an Initiative check, the user may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]] (◑) to allow all affected targets to take one free maneuver before the first round of combat begins. The user may not activate this multiple times.

**Control Upgrade:** When performing a Foresee power check as part of an Initiative check, all targets affected by the power increase their ranged and melee defense by 2 for the first round of combat.

**Duration Upgrade:** The user may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]] (◑) to see a number of extra days into the future equal to the number of Duration upgrades purchased. The user may activate this multiple times, increasing the number of days by this number each time.

**Magnitude Upgrade:** The user may spend a [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]] (◑) to affect engaged allies with the power as well as \[themself\]. This increases the number of people affected by the number of Magnitude upgrades purchased. The user may activate this multiple times, increasing the number of targets by this number each time.

**Range Upgrade:** The user may spend a a [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]] (◑) to increase the range at which \[they\] can affect \[their\] allies with this power by a number of range bands equal to the number of Range upgrades purchased. The user may activate this multiple times, increasing the range by this number each time.

**Strength Upgrade:** The Force user can spend a [[Narrative Dice#Light Point|Force]] [[Narrative Dice#Dark Point|point]] (◑) to pick out a number of specific details from \[their\] vision equal to the number of Strength upgrades purchased. The use may activate this multiple times, recalling more details each time.
